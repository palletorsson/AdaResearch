**Melencolia**
The Paralysis of Perfect Forms

In 1514, Albrecht Dürer engraved an image that has haunted geometry ever since: a winged figure sits motionless, head resting on hand, surrounded by all the tools of mathematical knowledge. Compass. Ruler. Sphere. Polyhedron. Magic square. Hourglass. Bell.

Everything needed to measure, calculate, and build - yet paralyzed by melancholy.

We have reached the same point. We have the primitives.
