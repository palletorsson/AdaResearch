# Unit Circle & Trigonometry
The Foundation of Wave Functions

The unit circle is a circle with radius 1 centered at the origin (0,0).

Any point on the unit circle can be represented by (cos θ, sin θ), where θ is the angle measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis.

Geometric Properties:
- Radius: Always 1 (unit length)
- Circumference: 2π ≈ 6.28 units
- Any point (x, y) satisfies: x² + y² = 1

---

## Sine Function

sin(θ) = y-coordinate on the unit circle

Properties:
- Range: Always between -1 and 1
- Period: Repeats every 2π radians (360°)
- Maximum at θ = π/2 (90°)
- Minimum at θ = 3π/2 (270°)

Symmetry:
Sine is an odd function: sin(-θ) = -sin(θ)

Applications:
Sound waves, light waves, ocean tides, alternating current, pendulum motion.

---

## Cosine Function

cos(θ) = x-coordinate on the unit circle

Properties:
- Range: Always between -1 and 1
- Period: Repeats every 2π radians (360°)
- Starts at 1 when θ = 0
- Minimum at θ = π (180°)

Relationship to Sine:
cos(θ) = sin(θ + π/2)
sin(θ) = cos(θ - π/2)

Symmetry:
Cosine is an even function: cos(-θ) = cos(θ)

---

## Tangent Function

tan(θ) = sin(θ) / cos(θ)

Geometric Meaning:
- Slope of the line from origin to the point
- Ratio of vertical to horizontal displacement

Properties:
- Range: From -∞ to +∞
- Period: Repeats every π radians (180°)
- Undefined when cos(θ) = 0

Applications:
Calculating slopes and angles, navigation, optics, engineering.
