**0. Point Zero**

You arrive late. Before you see anything, a lineage has already survived: physics, bodies, tools, code, and the long history that makes "now" possible. Heidegger called this thrownness — we begin inside conditions we did not choose.
In this world, what is "already there" is the renderer, the headset body, the frame-loop, and the coordinate system that holds time as motion. Vector3(0,0,0) is not just a point. It is the fact of beginning inside an inheritance.

The origin is not an object but a prerequisite. It is not a visible marker but an implicit agreement between body, coordinate system, and time — an abstraction sustained by the simulation itself. Before any point, line, or measurement can appear, invisible scaffolds must already be in place: rendering pipelines, reference frames, memory, temporal update loops. The origin is infrastructural, not foundational — an effect of system stability, not its ground.

This framework — Godot, the XR toolkit, the history of CGI, the gift of open source — is itself a thing of endless descent, always partly ignored. There are things we cannot explain from the beginning. Things we must return to when time permits. Inherent backgrounds. A folded void.

And there is another inheritance. I am thrown too — into a body, a position, a history that lets me build this. How can we not speak from privilege? To be here, now, making — is that not already a condition? I do not believe in stable identity; I think it is a construction. But the ability to say so is unevenly distributed. This too is part of the infrastructure. The future is queer, is desiring-machinery. But I can only say that from where I stand.
