[b]QFEP: Queer Free Energy Principle[/b]

[i]The formula that unifies everything.[/i]

[color=cyan][b]QFE = F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t)[/b][/color]

[b]The Terms:[/b]

[color=yellow]F[/color] = Free Energy
The prediction error. How surprised the system is.
Systems seek to minimize F — to find patterns, reduce surprise.

[color=red]E(S)[/color] = Entropy  
The space of possibilities. Freedom from fixed patterns.
High entropy = many possible states = openness to becoming.

[color=green]λ[/color] = Lambda (0 → 1)
The entropy drive. How much the system values disorder.
λ=0: pure order (crystal, frozen, dead)
λ=1: pure chaos (dissolved, random, dead)
λ≈0.4: edge of chaos (alive, adaptive, complex)

[color=magenta]φ[/color] = Phi (-1 → +1)
Rate sensitivity. How the system responds to change.
φ<0: resist change (conservative)
φ>0: embrace change (queer signature)

[b]The Insight:[/b]
Life exists at the edge of chaos.
Not frozen order. Not dissolved chaos.
The oscillation between them IS the living.
