[b]QFEP: The Queer Reading[/b]

[i]Why the formula has a queer signature.[/i]

The standard Free Energy Principle (Friston) is:
[color=gray]F = prediction error. Minimize it.[/color]

QFEP adds:
[color=magenta]−λE(S) + φΔE(S,t)[/color]

[b]What's Queer About It?[/b]

1. [color=cyan]The Dark Room Problem[/color]
Pure F-minimization leads to stasis.
A brain that only minimizes surprise would:
- Stay in a dark room
- Never explore
- Never change
- Die

Normativity works like this too.
"Minimize deviance. Stay predictable. Don't surprise us."

2. [color=green]λE(S) — The Entropy Drive[/color]
QFEP says: systems don't just minimize surprise.
They also VALUE entropy — openness, freedom, possibility.

This is the queer move:
[color=magenta]Disorder is not failure. It's generative.[/color]

3. [color=yellow]φΔE(S,t) — The Rate Term[/color]
How do you respond to CHANGE in entropy?

φ < 0: Resist change. Conserve. Preserve.
φ = 0: Neutral.
φ > 0: Embrace change. Welcome becoming.

[color=magenta]Positive φ is the queer signature.[/color]

Not just tolerating change.
VALUING the rate of transformation.
Finding joy in becoming-other.

[b]The Formula as Politics:[/b]
Normative systems: λ = 0.2, φ = -0.3
  → Prefer order, resist change

Queer systems: λ = 0.5, φ = 0.4
  → Value diversity, embrace becoming

Both are adaptive strategies.
The question is: which world do you want?
