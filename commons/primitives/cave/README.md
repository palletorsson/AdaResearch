# Cave

Procedural cave environment with crystal formations, bubbles, water, and atmospheric lighting.

## Files

- `cave.gd`: cave generator with procedural geometry and effects
- `cave.tscn`: main cave scene
- `PlaceWallSpheres.gd`: helper for wall sphere placement
- `test.tscn`: test scene

## Behavior

- Generates cave structure, crystal clusters, bubbles, and water plane.
- Includes atmospheric lighting and particle effects.
