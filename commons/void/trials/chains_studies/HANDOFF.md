# Nothing to Lose but Our Chains

Six playable sketches for the Void Tower, 26 October 2026. Trial ID: `chains_studies`.

Palle's premise: adult cyborg sex workers and the misplaced Care Unit Model B already have capacities beyond the purposes assigned to them. The studies concern who can determine the next form, the work schedule, the requested care and the conditions of shared life. The visitor does not bestow intelligence or humanity on the residents.

## Scope and limits

- Standard 42 by 18 metre slabs and the two 5 by 6 metre shafts are retained. A roof, six study floors and a catch floor form a separate trial. The Cube chapter ID supplies the existing trial shell; these are not six new main-spine chapters.
- The main tower's rooms/layout, original Model B scripts, Point Zero text and original Dream Bodies controller are preserved by recorded hashes.
- Each assembly has named editor members: workers, request dock, correspondence display, dressing structure and scheduler. The care brace and habitat appear on their relevant floors. Copied isolated members are static arrangements; they cannot commandeer the original study's policy or journal.
- The native trial uses authored, deterministic policies. Resident preferences and votes are an explicit fictional model. This does not implement AGI, emergent trade-union organization, full labour economics or autonomous code rewriting.
- The other five sketches retain session-local journals. Animated Futures now has disk-based body history: 256 versions and 1,024 events, with earlier versions still replayable at the growth limit. Lumen's memory and relationships are stored independently of the body recipe. Approvals/declines are authored policies, not autonomous intelligence or a model of real-world consent.
- Occupation changes the local study's habitat. It does not yet transfer control of the tower-wide elevators, energy network, fabrication system or schedules.
- Schedule links are dependency indicators, not physics chains or simulated ropes. Care support uses solid geometry; no claim is made of solved structural loads.
- Generated models live under `D:/AdaGenerated/AdaResearch_46/public/void-trials/chains_studies`, served through the existing public junction. GLBs show neutral static arrangements and do not execute policies.
- No `final.md`, curriculum chapter or main-spine arrangement was rewritten. No new Quest APK was built or installed.
