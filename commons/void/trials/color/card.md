# Colour / a celebration of 23 columns

**Bring:** five primitive forms, their transforms, and an array of parts.

**Encounter:** four 4.4 m columns stand together. Their geometry is identical.
One coat, shape, height and the neighbour below give four different assignments.
Use RULE to change the assignment without moving a vertex. PART + selects an
address, from 0 at the foot to 4 at the top. PAINT changes that part's material.
Compare the unchanged neighbour. RESET restores that station's starting rule.

**Carry:** a palette, a rule assigning colours to indexed parts, and independent
material resources. The same parts can become another grouping.

**Space:** begin with the four rules, then walk among three shape alphabets,
four cut-shape poses, four composed stacks and eight gallery columns. All 23
configurations come from the original museum. The larger compositions reach
7.4 metres; this isolated trial raises the canopy by three metres to provide
nine metres of headroom. The 42 × 18 m floor, both shafts, and the catch floor
stay in place. The main tower retains its current dimensions.

Each column is an independent lab-editor placement. The opening four retain
their controls and matched white lamps. The other nineteen use their original
scripts and share neutral illumination. The path loops around the shafts,
through the west collection and the taller east gallery. Colour begins as a
comparison and becomes something to walk among.

**Pass:** check the four rules against their expected colours; operate the real
desktop and XR pointer controls; repaint one part while every other material,
mesh, transform and collider stays unchanged; repeat, restore, relocate, copy,
save/reload and retry. Check all 23 configurations against the museum, preserve
the gallery heights, and walk the full loop with the actual desktop and XR
bodies. Physical headset readability remains a playtest.

The source map, final.md and tutorial.md remain the reference. This card records
the playable trial; it does not rewrite the chapter.
