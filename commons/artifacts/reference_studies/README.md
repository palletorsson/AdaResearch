# Reference studies

Five independent Node3D artifacts, each with a 0–1 `amount` and `set_amount(value)` method. They do not add a camera, player, global environment or input handler. Shader clock control is explicit. Geometry/material helpers are supplied by `build.gd`.

The complete desktop viewer, exercises and native capture harness are in `doc/book/studies/reference-rooms/`. Its `prepare.py` copies these sources into that portable project and records byte hashes.

These scenes are chapter companions awaiting a deliberate placement pass, not installed museum exhibits. Suspension needs roughly 5.4 × 5.4 m and 5 m vertical clearance; the passage needs a 5.2 m wall span plus approach and exit; the landscape and pattern garden need approximately 12 × 10 m; the plural body needs 6 × 5 m with 4.5 m headroom. They should not be automatically seated on a 1 m plinth.

Only the passage wall has collision inside its artifact. The desktop viewer supplies the room shell and visitor. No headset or performance claim is made. Attribution, reference uncertainty, representation choices and critical questions are in the portable project's README and MAKING files.
