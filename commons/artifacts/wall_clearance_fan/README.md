# Wall-Clearance Fan

A four-blade ceiling fan is mounted closer to a wall than its radius permits. The
fan can complete its cycle only because an elliptical opening has been removed
from the wall. A translucent disk shows the union of every blade phase, while an
orange outline marks where that swept volume has rewritten the architecture.

The opening is calculated from the fan's swept radius and its distance from the
wall. For sweep radius `R` and wall offset `d`, the intersection chord is:

```text
chord = 2 * sqrt(R^2 - d^2)
```

The visitor can change frequency, pause the fan, reset its phase, and hide or
show the swept-volume explanation.

QFEP connection: regular periodic order does not remain inside the rotating
object. Repetition imposes a persistent negative form on its environment. The
wall opening is the accumulated spatial consequence of every possible phase.
