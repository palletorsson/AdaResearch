# Kapoor void studies

An independent Godot chapter companion, not installed in a spine map. It stages three original forms after distinct Kapoor operations:

- a deep circular aperture in a contained plinth, after *Descent into Limbo*;
- six hollow blocks, after *Void Field*;
- a wall swell beside a deep recess, after the convex and concave wall forms discussed in the De Pont essay, including *When I Am Pregnant*.

These are studies, not reconstructions. The near-black material is a small authored Godot shader with a faint edge gradient. It is neither Vantablack nor Kapoor's pigment. Press **B** to replace it with chalk and inspect the concavity; press **L** to compare the form under the studio lights. Mouse-drag orbits, the wheel changes distance, **1–3** focus the studies, and **4/R** restore the wide view.

Open `study.tscn` for the desktop viewer. `kapoor_void_studies.tscn` is the reusable exhibit root. The scene does not invite walking into an unbounded pit: the main opening is a contained object, not a missing section of the museum floor.

References: [Anish Kapoor, *Descent into Limbo* conversation](https://anishkapoor.com/180/in-conversation-with-john-tusa-2); [Museo Reina Sofía, *Void Field*](https://ladigitaldelreina.sofia.es/idurl/5/17289) (1989, sandstone and pigment; six blocks); [De Pont essay on Kapoor](https://anishkapoor.com/9275/on-the-sublime-in-the-work-of-anish-kapoor-by-camile-van-winkle).
