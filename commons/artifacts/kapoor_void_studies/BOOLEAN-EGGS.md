# Boolean eggs

This is an original companion study for the Kapoor surface-and-depth sequence. Three egg-like volumes make Boolean operations visible as sculptural decisions: union joins bodies, intersection keeps their shared volume, and subtraction opens a cavity. Deep plum, indigo, and teal carry the forms out of the chalk-white museum register; a dim ring of light suggests an almost-halo without turning the objects into spectacle.

The cavity is still geometry, and its dark surface is an ordinary Godot material. It is not Kapoor's pigment or Vantablack. The forms respond to his broader investigation of void and surface, but do not reconstruct a specific artwork.
