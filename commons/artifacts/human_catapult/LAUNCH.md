# Board, count down, launch

2026-10-07 correction. Walk up the ramp and into the level cup. The actual player body's feet must be inside its boarding footprint. The visible countdown runs 3–2–1, then launches once. Stepping out cancels it; reboarding starts a fresh three seconds. Desktop layer 1 and XR layer 20 are detected through Area3D overlaps. Angle and speed can be set before boarding; they remain fixed during countdown and release. FIRE is an alternative way to start the countdown while boarded.

Local -Z is the throwing direction over the axle, away from the boarding approach. `launch_velocity_world()` is shared by preview and release. The red arc samples world gravity and converts back with `to_local`, so rotating the artifact does not offset or reverse the path. Solid 44 mm tube segments make the arc visible in VR. Preview origin follows a boarded player's feet and freezes at actual release; it does not chase the moving arm afterward. This is an ideal unobstructed ballistic prediction: collisions may shorten the flight.

The real player moves through the existing `player_launch.gd` receiver and desktop/XR movement owners. The arm swing is an animation indicating release; it does not physically calculate the impulse. Its basket floor is disabled during the swing so it cannot collide back into the launched player. The cup is level when boarding, with an open front and a shallow overlapping ramp. FIRE sits beside the cup; its collider previously blocked the entrance. No tracked-head rotation is applied. The existing receiver stops on landing or its five-second flight limit.

The earlier selection test injected `_armed` and called FIRE directly. That could pass while boarding was broken. New tests use actual collision shapes and overlap detection, with countdown, exit cancellation, reboarding, movement and rotated-preview assertions. Museum checks walk a standing capsule up the ramp without teleporting into the cup and follow the resulting flight. Physical headset comfort remains unverified.
