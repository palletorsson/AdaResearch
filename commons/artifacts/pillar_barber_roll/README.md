# Pillar / pink red white barber roll

A 4.8 m striped sleeve rotates around an existing 0.9 m square structural pillar.
Two copies frame the eastern bay of floor59, turning in opposite directions.
The square pillar remains fixed and keeps its original collision.

Reuses the museum barber pole's generated helix image, replacing blue with pink.
Unlike that original's UV scrolling, this sleeve rotates its actual geometry.
The diagonal bands seem to climb; their height never changes. A stationary white
witness and a rotating pink notch make the distinction visible. No flashing.

The sleeve and bearing cuffs are independently editable parts. The 0.74 m sleeve
radius clears the pillar's corners by approximately 10 cm. Bounds overlap the
pillar intentionally; this is a sleeve, not a misplaced floor-standing object.
Native spin uses elapsed time; the web editor receives the real textured geometry
frozen at phase zero. Reposition a sleeve to a pillar center when reusing it.
