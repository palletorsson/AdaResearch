# Museum: The Wings

The first map with interior walls. Until today the grid knew floors, heights, and void; now it knows rooms.

Four wings open off a central street — Fractal, Chance, Motion, Life — each one a dense pocket from the census, walkable behind real walls with real doorways. The doors are decisions: from the street you read all four wings at once; choosing one forecloses the others until you come back. At the street's north end, where the axis resolves, hangs the anchor: the Mamma Mönster gallery — the origin patterns presiding over the whole collection, the way a temple presides over its court.

The walls themselves are the exhibit here, if you know to look. Each is a segment declared on a cell edge in the map's new walls layer — the same JSON that holds the floors — and the pathfinder proves they mean it: seal a wing's doors and its treasures become unreachable. Architecture, in this game, is now data like everything else.
