# Logic — either path and interruption

Two works on the centre line. `matter_spring_or` releases a ball down each of two parallel lanes toward one shared paddle, so either supported lane can produce the arrival without a hidden selector. Two cells further, `matter_spring_not` runs a continuous track with one lowered blocker: an impact on the blocker records zero, an impact on the end paddle records one. Same slope, same pink stop, same X/Y cubes and ten-second rest reset as in Logic_AND.

The harder lesson is what not to count. A ball that stalls or is picked up is an accident, not a truth value, and the readout leaves it inconclusive. Interruption becomes a rule only when the construction makes it repeatable. Logic_Assemblage next hands the parts over.
