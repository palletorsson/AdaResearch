# What a moving boundary permits

On this room's level floor, an obstacle can approach without a pit beneath it. There is time to stand aside. The existing way around matters: it gives the visitor a choice about how to encounter a moving boundary. A successful exit is not evidence that the visitor has entered every lane, and a detour is not a failed lesson.

The translation blocks repeat their paths with different pauses. The turning slabs occupy different widths as they change orientation. The growers repeat a common tempo. These distinctions can be watched before they are walked. They offer conditions under which an opening becomes usable, rather than one dramatic consequence for getting it wrong.

An invariant of an object does not decide that relation. The translating block keeps its dimensions, but the available clearance depends on the body beside it. A rotating slab retains its internal distances while changing the width left along a route. Uniformly growing cubes preserve their proportions while the spaces between them contract.

Which body do those gaps fit? A collision capsule has a radius and height; a visitor also has a pace, a viewpoint, a way of controlling movement and a tolerance for moving surroundings. These do not collapse into a single number called passable. A gap can be physically open and awkward to approach, or comfortable to observe and unwelcome to cross.

The three growers share a period. Repetition makes the rhythm easier to perceive, but it does not make every visitor ready at the same moment. The clock offers another opening; the way around offers another route. Keeping both available gives the study more than one legitimate form of participation.

This is the critical force of the room as it now stands. It does not stage irreversible death or demonstrate that transformation must become violence. It lets us inspect how programmed motion distributes room, timing and choice. The mathematics remains exact about the operation. The encounter asks what else must be described before we can say what that operation permits.

The final door turns the question towards hospitality. Its leaves move, turn and shrink when someone approaches. The mechanism makes room, but the welcome still has parameters: a detection area, an opening size, a waiting time. These can be inspected and changed. The same vocabulary that displaced the visitor can support their passage.
