# Uffizi Footprint Pilot

## Overview

This pilot corrects the order of composition used by the first 3D court. It starts with AdaResearch's existing `uffizi-spine-ordered` formula and places artifact footprints over that architectural pattern.

## Spatial layout

The visitor enters at the head of the east spine. A three-metre clear lane runs south beside a one-metre display band. Three side rooms open from aligned doorway bands, while a second set of aligned doors creates an enfilade inside the rooms. At the south end, the route turns west past an axial marker and reaches the exit.

The original fifteen-metre Uffizi template offered side rooms approximately eight metres wide. The derived pilot grows only the room side to twenty-one metres overall, producing fourteen-metre-wide bays while the spine remains unchanged.

## Artifact negotiations

- **Shannon Entropy Meter** receives a 3 × 3 m wall-reading zone in the north room without changing the architecture.
- **Pattern Loom** receives a 5 × 8 m productive zone in the middle room. One cross-wall moves south by one metre; doorway alignment and corridor width remain unchanged.
- **Neural Network Visualization** receives a 14 × 6 × 11 m environment in the south room. The room expands six metres away from the spine and the vertical envelope rises to eleven metres.

The colored `footprints` layer is evidence, not runtime geometry. It records which cells each artifact reserves while the standard structure, utilities, and interactables layers remain compatible with GridSystem.
