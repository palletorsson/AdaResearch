# Two flocks, one volume — the swarm hive and the politics of decentralised encounter

Chamber_Swarm stages an encounter between two self-organising systems. The learner's catalyst produces a flock of eight boids rather than a single projectile. The creature — a swarm_hive — runs its own flock with its own parameter set. When the two flocks share a volume, their behaviours couple, and neither side controls the emergent pattern the coupling produces.

Pierre Clastres's political anthropology argued that some societies actively resist the emergence of centralised authority. Stateless societies are not pre-state societies waiting to develop a state; they are societies organised to prevent the state from forming. Clastres's reading is politically charged and has been contested, but its generative move — treating decentralisation as a positive programme rather than a developmental lack — maps onto the chamber's two-flock architecture.

The swarm_hive creature's body is a cloud of small agents. Each agent follows Reynolds' three rules — separation, alignment, and cohesion — with parameters tuned for the hive's behavioural signature. The learner's catalyst produces a second flock on each activation: eight boids with their own Reynolds parameters. When the two flocks enter the same volume, their behaviours couple. The learner's boids align with the hive's average direction at the meeting boundary; the hive's outer agents drift toward the learner's centroid.

A miura_crawler drifts in from a corner and tests the shared airspace, curious whether another swarm now operates in its territory. Its presence is a third flock — a very small one, just one agent — and its curiosity is a behaviour rather than an alignment. The crawler does not join either flock; it samples the meeting and reports back by its own behavioural response.

A science screen on the wall scatters the encounter across alignment and cohesion axes. The trails of the two flocks braid through each other on the plot, and the chamber's thesis is visible as the overlap of their statistical footprints rather than as any single event. The plot does not distinguish the learner's flock from the hive; both are present as clouds of points, and the reader has to look at the braiding to recover which cloud is which.

Clastres's resistance-to-centralisation lands on the chamber's reward structure. A centralised encounter would reward the learner for directing their flock to destroy the hive. This chamber rewards the production of a coherent meeting. The science screen's scatter is richer when the two flocks actually engage with each other — when their boids respond to each other rather than passing through — and the richness is the chamber's success condition.

Neither flock can dominate. The learner's flock is smaller (eight boids versus the hive's many). The hive is slower and more distributed. Domination would require one side's parameters to overwrite the other's, and the Reynolds rules do not support overwriting. They support response. The chamber's decentralised politics are enforced by the physics rather than claimed as ideology, and the enforcement is the chamber's quiet argument about what sorts of encounters are actually possible between self-organised populations.

The swarm catalyst is the learner's side of the mutuality. The swarm_hive is the creature's side. Neither is independently swarming; both are structurally swarming because the chamber's rules enforce Reynolds dynamics on both. The enforcement is political: a chamber that let one side be a swarm and the other be a point target would be a chamber about hunting a swarm. This chamber is about two swarms meeting, and the two-swarm framing is what the chamber's design is for.

Within the sequence, Chamber_Swarm converts the sequence's argument from demonstration into meeting. Local rules produce global coordination, and when two rule systems share a volume the global coordination becomes shared. The chamber hands the learner back to the Lab with the swarm catalyst in their kit and with a body-level sense that decentralised intelligence is a mode of encounter as well as a mode of internal organisation.
