# Chamber Swarm — Summary

Chamber_Swarm is the catalyst chamber for the Swarm Intelligence sequence. It stages collective behaviour meeting collective behaviour: the learner's catalyst is not one projectile but a flock of eight boids, and the creature is a swarm running the same kind of rules.

The chamber holds a swarm_hive creature whose body is a cloud of small agents. Each agent follows Reynolds' three rules — separation, alignment, and cohesion — with parameters tuned for the hive's behavioural signature. The learner's catalyst produces a second flock of eight boids on each activation. The learner's flock follows the same three rules with different parameters.

When the two flocks enter the same volume, their behaviours couple. The learner's boids align with the hive's average direction at the meeting boundary; the hive's outer agents drift toward the learner's centroid. Neither flock controls the other; both are controlled by the rules they share, and the intersection belongs to the collective rather than to either side.

A miura_crawler drifts in from a corner and tests the shared airspace, curious whether another swarm now operates in its territory. A science screen on the wall scatters the encounter across alignment and cohesion axes; the trails of the two flocks braid through each other on the plot, and the chamber's thesis is visible as the overlap of their statistical footprints rather than as any single event.

Within the sequence, Chamber_Swarm converts the sequence's argument from demonstration into meeting. Local rules produce global coordination, and when two rule systems share a volume, the global coordination becomes shared. The chamber hands the learner back to the Lab with the swarm catalyst in their kit.
