## The walk

The standard chamber, and after seven rooms of crowds it feels pointedly single. The becoming_catalyst waits on its pedestal, the catalyst_target marks its spot, the proximity_spawner keys the room to your approach. One walker, one bracelet, the smallest possible colony.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to leave a trail**. Every swarm in the thread coordinated through the marks it left in the world — pheromone, tube, flow field — and the catalyst hands the walker that exact power: the blocks you place are deposits, and the room reads them the way an ant reads a path. You become one agent depositing into a shared environment, and if the next walker follows or avoids your marks, the chamber has made its point without a second body in the room — stigmergy needs no crowd, only a world that remembers. The chapter that proved intelligence needs no leader ends by making you the smallest possible member of a colony that includes everyone who walks here after you.
