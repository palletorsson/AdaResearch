# Chamber Fractals — Summary

Chamber_Fractals is the catalyst chamber for the Fractals sequence. It stages self-similarity as a relationship rather than a pattern: the learner and the creature are both running recursion, just with different base cases and different branching factors.

The chamber is small and dark. A fractal_hydra creature occupies the centre of the room. The learner holds the fractal catalyst, which emits projectiles that branch into four offshoots on impact. Each offshoot can branch again, so a single shot grows into a small tree of hits by the time it reaches the creature. The hydra, in turn, grows new heads every time the learner severs one, branching by a factor of two at each cut.

The exchange does not converge. Both systems are self-referential, and neither has a stopping condition that the other can exploit. The science screen scatters the events across a depth axis so the learner can watch the recursion deepen without resolving. Panels on the wall annotate the exchange: the branching factors, the current depth, the projected step at which each system would stabilise if the other were not also recursing.

Within the sequence, Chamber_Fractals turns infinite regress from a mathematical curiosity into a combat problem, and then turns that combat problem into a relationship the learner cannot win by finishing. The chamber hands the learner back to the Lab with the fractal catalyst in their kit and with a body-level sense that self-similarity is the engine of the sequence's argument.
