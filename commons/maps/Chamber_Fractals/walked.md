## The walk

The chamber holds the chapter's standard apparatus at its calmest: the becoming_catalyst on its pedestal, a catalyst_target waiting, the proximity_spawner keyed to your approach, the science_screen framing the verb. After eight rooms of coastlines, trees, and complex-plane lace, the room asks for the smallest possible act: place a block. Then place one beside it. Then step back and see what you have started.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to nest**. Every fractal in the thread was a rule applied to its own output — and the catalyst hands you that structure at body scale: each block you place changes the room that decides where the next block makes sense, your construction becoming the context of its own continuation. The curriculum nests too, and this room says so: a map inside a sequence inside a spine inside a project that studies maps — self-similarity not as an exhibit but as the shape of the thing you are standing in. The chapter that measured dimensions between integers plants the between itself: build a little, and notice the build recursing.
