Concept: Pheromone systems demonstrate stigmergy — indirect coordination through environmental modification — where individually random agents produce collectively ordered behavior. Each ant walks randomly, but the pheromone trail it deposits biases subsequent walkers, converting individual chaos into colony-level intelligence.
Sequence role: Eleventh map in Randomness sequence. Follows Randomness_Examples_of_Randomness (randomness surveyed across domains) and introduces emergence — the sequence's late-stage thesis that randomness at one scale produces order at another. Precedes Random_Space, the sequence finale. The pheromone system synthesizes lessons from Random_Walk (individual stochastic paths) and Random_Gaussian (population-level patterns from individual randomness) into a single dynamic system.
Technical angle: Agent-based pheromone simulation — each agent moves with random perturbation, deposits pheromone at its position, and biases its direction toward higher pheromone concentration. Pheromone diffuses and evaporates over time (2D texture with blur and decay per frame). pheromone_terrain implements this as a heightfield or color-mapped surface. GDScript implementation: array of agent positions updated per frame, pheromone grid as Image or float array, Gaussian blur for diffusion, linear decay for evaporation.
Critical angle: Stigmergy is communication without language — the environment itself becomes the message. This is a radical model of collective intelligence: no agent knows the plan, no agent communicates directly, yet paths emerge, resources are found, problems are solved. The clipboard#queer_energy artifact suggests a reading of pheromone systems through queer theory — non-normative, decentralized, emergent organization that resists hierarchical structure. The sequence approaches its conclusion by revealing that randomness is not the opposite of order but its precondition: without random exploration, the pheromone system would never discover new paths.
Key artifacts: pheromone_terrain is the core simulation — agent-deposited pheromone creating emergent trails on a responsive surface; clipboard#pheromone_axioms provides the formal rules of the system; clipboard#queer_energy offers a critical-theory reading of emergent organization; dark_sphere anchors.
Gap: No artifact lets the learner adjust the balance between random exploration and pheromone-following exploitation — a slider controlling this ratio would make the exploration-exploitation tradeoff (the sequence's deepest insight) directly manipulable.
