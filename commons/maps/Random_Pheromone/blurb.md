Randomness with memory, trails that decay. Pheromone systems bridge individual chaos and collective order — each agent random, the swarm intelligent. Here randomness becomes stigmergy: communication through environment.

The `pheromone_terrain` simulates it end-to-end: each agent moves with random perturbation, deposits pheromone at its position, biases its direction toward higher concentration. The chemical diffuses and evaporates; paths emerge where walks cross, stabilize, intensify. `clipboard#pheromone_axioms` states the rules formally; `clipboard#queer_energy` reads the same system through queer theory — non-normative, decentralized, emergent organization that resists hierarchical structure. Stigmergy is communication without language — the environment itself becomes the message. No agent knows the plan, none speak, yet paths form. Randomness is not the opposite of order; it is the exploration without which order cannot be discovered.
