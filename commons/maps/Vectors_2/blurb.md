Two vectors meet. The dot product measures their agreement — a single number that captures how much one vector *goes along with* another. Parallel vectors return maximum. Perpendicular vectors return zero. Opposed vectors go negative. Alignment reduced to arithmetic.

Subtraction finds the gap. Vector A minus Vector B produces the arrow *from* B *to* A — the difference made geometric. Not what each vector is, but what lies between them. Every relationship encoded as direction and magnitude.

Scalar multiplication stretches or compresses without changing course. The vector keeps its heading but gains or loses force. Identity preserved under transformation.

Three operations, three ways of asking: how alike are we, what separates us, and how much of myself do I keep when the world scales me?
