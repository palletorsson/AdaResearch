## The walk

The primitive is a circle — the one that secretly writes every wave. The unit_circle_advanced turns, and sine and cosine fall out of it as shadows of a radius; the OscillationCurve catches them and unrolls them into the familiar snake. Then the SimpleOscillatingBridge makes you *trust* the mathematics with your weight: a walkway riding the very curve the circle just drew.

## What it teaches

One turning radius, read twice, is the entire alphabet: sine from the height, cosine from the reach. The chapter's every later extravagance — chords, timbres, harmonics, Fourier's whole claim — is this circle, multiplied. The room keeps it small on purpose; the primitive should fit in one glance and one crossing.
