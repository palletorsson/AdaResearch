# ISO_Caves — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (critical slot, isosurfaces thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

mc_inside_cave · mc_portal_landscape · queer_marching_cave

## The walk

The chapter goes inside its own surface. The mc_inside_cave wraps the boundary around the walker — the cave is where the field says *in*, and you are standing in it, geometry experienced from the wrong side of the skin. The mc_portal_landscape joins two regimes of the same field through a doorway, the level-set constant working as architect of where you can pass. And the queer_marching_cave marches the *same field* under a shifted, blended, contested threshold — and gets another world.

## The turn (critical)

The critical room says the quiet part: if inside and outside are just a number's relation to a cutoff, then the cutoff is a politics. Every isosurface is a decision about where the line falls, and the queer cave demonstrates that the decision was never forced by the data — move the threshold and different bodies of space count as solid, as habitable, as real. The chapter's geometry lesson arrives at the project's oldest question wearing new clothes: who set the iso-value, and what world would the same field yield under another one?
