# PG: Tessellating Portals

A room ends. Another begins. The seam is invisible. Tessellating portals tile space seamlessly — each threshold a gate into a procedurally generated neighbor, identical in structure, infinite in extent. Step through and the room behind you is already gone, replaced by the next iteration. Non-Euclidean space faked through perfect cuts.

Portal rendering cheats perception. The camera sees through the doorway into another world, stitched flush against this one. No loading screen, no fade. Just continuous space that was never continuous — a lie of adjacency. Infinite rooms from finite geometry.

This is the final projection. Higher dimensions collapsed not into shadow but into *passage*. The tesseract doesn't rotate here — it opens. Walk through the face of a cube and arrive somewhere that shouldn't exist. Space as hallway. Geometry as door.
