A wave function maps probability across space. Spherical harmonics are its angular shape — the standing patterns that emerge when oscillation wraps around a sphere. Lobes, nodes, rings. Each solution labeled by quantum numbers that count symmetries the eye can almost trace.

The dark sphere pulses at the center. Purple emission breathes in and out — amplitude made visible, period made tactile. Rotation slow enough to watch the phase shift. This is not decoration. This is the function performing itself.

What oscillates is not matter. What oscillates is the likelihood of finding matter there. The curve does not describe a thing moving. It describes where a thing might become real. Probability has geometry. Uncertainty has shape.
