Marching cubes for destructible geometry (destructible_marching_cube), sphere splitting for volumetric fracture. Critical angle: Destruction is the limit case of force — the moment where continuous deformation becomes discontinuous fragmentation. Every object has a breaking point; forces below it produce elastic response (springs from Forces_3), forces above it produce irreversible change. This is the entropy boundary in the QFEP framework: below threshold, the system returns to equilibrium; above it, structure is permanently lost.
