## The cast

## The walk

The primitive gets personal: the derivative being taken is *yours*. The player_trace records where you have been; the velocity_arrow differentiates it live, pointing where your walking is currently going and stretching with how hard you mean it. The derivative_pair returns from the threshold so the abstract case stands beside the embodied one.

## What it teaches

Velocity is the derivative you already own. Every step you have taken in this project was a rate of change of position; the room just makes the bookkeeping visible. When the arrow shrinks as you hesitate, the epsilon-delta story arrives as proprioception — the limit is what your body does when it stops.
