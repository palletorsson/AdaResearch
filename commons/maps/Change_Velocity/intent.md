Sequence role: Second map of Change, in the foundation cluster. Converts the abstract tangent of the opening map into motion the learner can feel. Reuses player_trace from Primitives so the carry-forward is explicit: a path is a function of time.

Critical angle: Change as embodied. The previous map's derivative was a number on a static curve; here it is the speed of a thing in motion, the rate you would feel as acceleration if it changed. This sets up forces (F = ma) as calculus made physical, the sequence's stated carry-forward.
