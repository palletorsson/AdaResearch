# Bracelet Zoo — Summary

## Overview
Exhibition space for all 10 catalyst crystal forms and the Capacity Bracelet system. Each catalyst is themed to its sequence — the visual shape tells you what it does before you pick it up.

## Spatial Layout
- Dimensions: 15×11 grid
- Two rows of 5 catalysts on raised platforms, plus 3 practice targets on the right side
- Open firing lanes between rows

## Catalysts (Row 1 — Early Arc)
- **Primitives** (2,3) — Clean octahedron, pale lavender
- **Transformation** (4,3) — Twisted double-pyramid, purple echo
- **Chromatic** (6,3) — Triangular prism, rainbow particles
- **Forces** (8,3) — Dense golden sphere, orbiting debris ring
- **Waveform** (10,3) — Teal double-torus, flowing particles

## Catalysts (Row 2 — Advanced Arc)
- **Chaos** (2,7) — Fractured shards, electric sparks
- **Cellular** (4,7) — Dark cube, glowing CA grid
- **Fractal** (6,7) — Recursive blue sub-crystals at 3 scales
- **Branching** (8,7) — Y-shaped green tree with leaf glow
- **Swarm** (10,7) — Cluster of 8 golden boid spheres

## Targets
- **Static target** (12,3) — Fixed floating sphere with rings
- **Moving target** (13,5) — Patrols side to side
- **Orbiting target** (12,7) — Circular patrol path

## Design Intent
The zoo lets you see and compare every catalyst identity at a glance. Each crystal's shape, color, and particle effects communicate its mathematical domain before any interaction. The practice targets give immediate feedback for testing projectile modes.

## Connection to Curriculum
Spans the full QFEP arc — from primitives (order) through chaos (entropy) to swarm (emergence). The bracelet system ties it together: pick up any crystal, and your wrist shows what you've become.
