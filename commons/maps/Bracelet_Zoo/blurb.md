Ten crystals, ten identities. The Bracelet Zoo is a gallery of becoming — each catalyst shaped by the mathematics it carries. The lavender octahedron knows only geometry. The fractured magenta shard crackles with chaos. The green tree branches toward something it hasn't grown yet.

Watch how each sequence transforms differently — prisms scatter rainbows, golden spheres drag orbiting debris, swarms flock forward as one.

This is the armory and the museum. Every tool on display, every identity available to try.
