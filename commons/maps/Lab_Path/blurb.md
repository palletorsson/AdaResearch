# Lab Path

The corridor back. Every sequence ends here — nine maps of noise, and now the return. A dark sphere pulses in purple light, slow rotation, breathing emission. Not a lesson. A threshold.

All paths converge on this point. The same 5×5 grid, the same low ceiling, the same glowing marker. What changes is what you carry through it. Before noise, randomness was coin flips and dice — memoryless, discontinuous. Now it remembers. Perlin's gradients, Simplex's simplices, octaves layered into terrain and cloud and skin. Entropy learned continuity and became a building material.

The sphere rotates. The corridor waits. On the other side: procedural generation, morphogenesis — worlds assembled from structured chaos. The transition is the proof. You crossed from disorder into coherence, and the passage itself is unremarkable. That's the point. Becoming doesn't announce itself.
