No destination, no plan — only the next step. The random walker drifts through possibility space, accumulating history without purpose, tracing paths that look intentional but aren't. Brownian motion made visible.

The `random_walk_128` traces 128 independent steps, each chosen without memory of the path. `random_walk_leash` constrains the walk within boundaries — absorbing or reflecting — to show how constraint interacts with stochasticity. `random_walk_terrarium` lets many walkers coexist in a bounded ecosystem; `pixel_cloud` maps their endpoints into spatial density. Einstein's 1905 paper on Brownian motion proved the jittering of pollen grains was evidence of molecular reality. The walk's displacement grows with the square root of steps — randomness is slower than purpose but never stationary. Free but not unconstrained: each step independent, the path always continuous.
