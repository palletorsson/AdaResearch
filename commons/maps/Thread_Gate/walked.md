## The walk

**Measurement and its limit, grown as an organism — and entered as a story.** You do not spawn inside the gate; you arrive at it. A two-wide corridor between tall flanks is the whole world for five cells — compression — and the first instrument stands in it with you: the prologue, the line, measurement at its smallest, met before there is any room to stand back in. Then the walls end and the ring is everywhere at once — release. Blob chambers sit as petals on the court, one per instrument, in crescendo — each room larger than the last as you round the ring, so the order is legible as a spiral of growth before it is legible as objects. The hero's beam is aimed along the walk, not walled into a hall: its fifty metres are reach, and the room it stands in is sized to its body — the REACH law, legible as architecture. Halfway round, the ring rises: the procession half lifts two heights and wraps the branching cascade at its seam. At the yard, the story's last move: three cells climb to a parapet — it stands west here, found by search, not fixed by habit — behind a wall lowered from four to three, and you look over — down into the quarantine where the mold blooms against its void moat, the one instrument you have only ever seen from above, the growth that exceeds measurement. Past it, the teleporter. Compression, prologue, release, rhythm, ascent, overlook, exit: the arc v3 argued by hand, now grown by the grammar — and there is still no dead cell in this map.

## What it gates, and what it learned

Three versions, each correcting the last's blindness. v1 proved the instruments and had no reason for anything. v2 added reasons — concept, hero, gaze setbacks, the moated anti — and remained a plane: layout, not space. v3 spends the grid's real vocabulary: heights as walls, void as moat, wp ramps as ascent, rooms with doors, compression and release, the overview from height. Composing it taught the composer the floor's hard contracts: the pathfinder's spawn token is exactly `s` (v1/v2's all-floor planes masked the fallback-to-(0,0) bug; the first walled map exposed it); climbing requires `wp` on the step cells; a teleporter stands on void; a door is only a door if it opens onto floor. And the one warning that remains is the design speaking: the mold is *correctly* unreachable — the only artifact in the gate you may see but never stand beside.

The gate's standard, third revision: the instruments must agree (ov 0, ti 0, voices 8/8, pathfinder clean, warm capture); the map must say why each thing stands where it stands (map_info.design); and the space itself must argue — compression, release, proportion, height, enclosure. If this map is ever imperfect, a tool broke; if it is ever mute, the unfold broke; if it is ever flat, the composer forgot what a room is.

**v4 — the promotion.** Four research rounds ended with a machine composition holding the gate, and the lesson of the last round is the one worth keeping: the round-3 runway was never a layout mistake, it was an unenforced law. laser_measure's fifty-metre envelope had been inflating every layout since round 1; the wizard engine enforced REACH (body-sized rooms, reach aimed along the walk) and the ribbon died in all ninety variants at once — median compact 0.52 against the 0.5 target, the trim lever never once firing. A law enforced beats a lever added. What v4 gives up is v3's narrative arc — the compression corridor, the measuring hall with doors read in sequence — and that is now the named edge for round 5: arrival as story, not just as metric. The gate is regenerable from its recipe (wizard_recipes/Thread_Gate.json): the standing gate is no longer only a map, it is a reproducible sentence in the composition grammar. v3 is preserved in git (gate-v3-architecture) as the hand-composed argument the machine still has to answer.

**v5 — the answer.** Round 5 took v3's last exclusive claim — the arc — and made it three operations in the grammar: a compression threshold carved after the rooms (carved before them it sealed the courtyard spawn: one cell reachable of 249, a failure the pathfinder's verdict called "OK" and only the reach score caught — trust the number that counts, not the label that summarises), a prologue artifact standing in the corridor, a parapet at the yard with the wall lowered four-to-three. Then the experiment: the same geometry scored 0.613 bare and 0.793 with its story — the arc is worth +0.180 under a rubric that also pays for tightness, and full-story variants swept the entire top sixteen. The machine composition now carries everything v3 argued: the measured rooms, the enforced laws, and the entry that means something. What remains hand-only is nothing structural — it is taste, and taste now edits weights in a canon file instead of moving cells by hand. The gate's standard, fifth revision, unchanged in spirit: if it is ever imperfect, a tool broke; if it is ever mute, the unfold broke; if it is ever flat, someone turned the story off.

**v6 — the found parapet.** Round 6 changed no weights and no strategies; it only kept a promise. The parapet, which round 5 placed by habit on the yard's south side and lost whenever a room happened to stand there, now searches its sides — south, west, north — and places all-or-nothing, approaching only from floor that already connects. Fifty-four variants asked for an overlook; fifty-four got one. And the guarantee re-ranked the field: the crescendo courtyard, which had been losing to a position accident, was the better gate all along — 0.800, the order readable as a spiral. The lesson joins the canon beside its siblings: a law enforced beats a lever added, and a guarantee is not a repair — it is a re-ranking.
