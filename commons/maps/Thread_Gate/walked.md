## The walk

**Measurement and its limit, built as rooms.** You enter a two-wide corridor between walls — compression — past the line held live between two points. The corridor opens into the measuring hall, and the hero's fifty-metre beam runs its length: the instrument is the room's spine. Along the north wall, five doorways. Each chamber behind them is sized from its artifact's measured envelope — a closet for the helicoid, a cell for the crystal, a room for the carved box, a bay for the dome, a chamber for the grown branching — **the room is the measurement**, and you read the crescendo as architecture, door by door, before you read it as objects. At the hall's end, ramp steps climb to a mezzanine. From its parapet you look down into the walled yard: the mold blooming against a void moat inside four-high walls — the growth that exceeds its own measurement, quarantined twice, watched from above. You descend the far steps and walk the yard's outer wall to the exit.

## What it gates, and what it learned

Three versions, each correcting the last's blindness. v1 proved the instruments and had no reason for anything. v2 added reasons — concept, hero, gaze setbacks, the moated anti — and remained a plane: layout, not space. v3 spends the grid's real vocabulary: heights as walls, void as moat, wp ramps as ascent, rooms with doors, compression and release, the overview from height. Composing it taught the composer the floor's hard contracts: the pathfinder's spawn token is exactly `s` (v1/v2's all-floor planes masked the fallback-to-(0,0) bug; the first walled map exposed it); climbing requires `wp` on the step cells; a teleporter stands on void; a door is only a door if it opens onto floor. And the one warning that remains is the design speaking: the mold is *correctly* unreachable — the only artifact in the gate you may see but never stand beside.

The gate's standard, third revision: the instruments must agree (ov 0, ti 0, voices 8/8, pathfinder clean, warm capture); the map must say why each thing stands where it stands (map_info.design); and the space itself must argue — compression, release, proportion, height, enclosure. If this map is ever imperfect, a tool broke; if it is ever mute, the unfold broke; if it is ever flat, the composer forgot what a room is.
