The chapter's synthesis beat and its dark spot in one hall. Eight bodies at architectural scale, four of which had never been placed anywhere in the museum. The hall is built to ask whether a carved negative becomes inhabitable when it gets large, and the finding — arrived at by walking, not by argument — is that it does not: twelve of the chapter's thirteen artifacts contain no collision construct of any kind.

Do not treat that as a punch list. The absence is the teaching. In `Boolean_Verbs` the missing interior is generative, because a boundary model stores no inside and difference therefore has to *invent* the cavity wall out of the outside. Here the same absence has gone sterile: the claim grew from *this surface is manufactured* to *this is a room*, and nothing underneath it grew to match. `recursive_boolean_cube` is the hinge — a Menger sponge that performs no boolean operation at all, getting its holes by declining to build cells, and it is the only fully reproducible body in the chapter.

The last argument is about the apparatus rather than the objects: the still camera that grades whether a variant is worth keeping rates `csg_architecture_cavity`'s step from one wall to a four-walled room at 4.4% of pixels, because a camera standing outside cannot see the difference between having an inside and not having one. The one property this chapter is about is the one property the instrument is built to miss.

Curriculum role: B3 SYNTHESIZE in the boolean_surfaces sequence — CSG at room scale, and the honest account of what the grammar cannot deliver.
