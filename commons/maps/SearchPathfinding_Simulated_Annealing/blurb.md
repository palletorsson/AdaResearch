A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 region at its center where randomness performs. Walkers drift without destination. Gaussian distributions sculpt terrain into peaks no one designed. Cubes appear, rotate, vanish — selected by coin flip, not intention. Height fields mutate. Profiles generate themselves from noise.

Every algorithm here replaces decision with probability. The random walk doesn't search — it stumbles, and stumbling covers ground. The gaussian doesn't choose a center — it collapses around one. Remove a cube at random and the structure that remains is the structure that was never planned.

Randomness is not the absence of method. It is method without author. The grid doesn't know what it will become — and that unknowing is the engine.
