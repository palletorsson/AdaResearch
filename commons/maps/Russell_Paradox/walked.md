> ghost-drafted from the working map (walk slot, foundationscrisis thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

russell_set_box · barber_paradox · liar_loop · self_membership_set

## The walk

The crisis moves from geometry to the ground under it: sets, the stuff mathematics decided everything was made of. The russell_set_box poses the fatal question as an object — the set of all sets that do not contain themselves, a box that belongs inside itself exactly when it doesn't. The barber_paradox tells the same wound as a story; the liar_loop runs it as a sentence chasing its own tail; the self_membership_set lets you try the operation by hand and feel the moment it bites.

## What it fixes

Self-reference is the room's lesson and its weapon. Naïve set theory didn't fail at the edges — it failed at the center, the instant a definition was allowed to mention itself. Every artifact here is the same trap in different clothes, and walking among them you learn to recognize the shape of the trap: a system rich enough to describe itself has just enough rope.
