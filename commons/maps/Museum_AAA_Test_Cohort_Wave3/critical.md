# Infrastructure is also an argument

The museum usually presents safety, surveillance, containment, and explanation as neutral background. Wave 3 makes those operations explicit. A camera reserves a field of observation. An extinguisher reserves emergency reach. A vitrine decides what may be touched. An information board determines which side contains meaning.

Treating these as contracts does not make them neutral; it makes their claims inspectable. The particle field pushes the lesson further: what matters is not always what a mesh measurement can see. Behavioral extent, institutional power, and visitor consequence all exceed the visible object.
