# Escher_Impossible — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (turn slot, foundationscrisis thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

escher_staircase · penrose_triangle · impossible_trident · magritte_pipe

## The walk

The turn hands the crisis to the artists, who had been expecting it. The escher_staircase climbs forever and arrives back; the penrose_triangle is solid from exactly one honest angle and a lie from every other; the impossible_trident has three prongs or two depending on which end you trust. And the magritte_pipe states the room's rule in paint: *ceci n'est pas une pipe* — the representation is not the thing, and never was.

## The turn

What mathematics experienced as catastrophe, art had been performing as delight. The impossible objects are Gödel sentences for the eye — locally consistent, globally unbuildable — and walking around them you do with your body what logicians did with proofs: discover that every view is partial and the whole does not have to agree with itself. The crisis turns here from a wound into a *medium*.
