Concept: A 3D spring-mass lattice with low stiffness collapses and spreads under its own weight rather than settling as a coherent solid. Same grid as sb07, opposite stiffness regime. The map is a gallery study; the player walks around the single soft-body marker.

Sequence role: Floppy jelly study (sb08) in the imported soft-body gallery cluster within the Soft Bodies sequence (13th spine, integration phase). Forms a contrast pair with sb07 (tall stiff column). The pair completes a four-step stiffness ladder across the jelly family — sb05 minimum-topology cube, sb06 wide low-stiffness grid settles, sb07 tall stiff stands, sb08 lattice flows. The stiffness coefficient is staged as a single dial sweeping through behavioral regimes.

Technical angle: Same lattice topology as sb07 (structural + shear + body-diagonal springs) but spring stiffness k reduced by roughly an order of magnitude. The system's natural frequencies drop below the disturbance frequencies (gravity, impact); the springs cannot restore deformations within a single integration step; deformations accumulate; the lattice flows. Damping helps suppress unstable oscillation but cannot recover the shape because the equilibrium constraint itself is too weak. Verlet/PBD integration over ~150 timesteps with substepping to prevent numerical explosion in the under-damped low-k regime.

Critical angle: Sb07 and sb08 together stage a phase transition by continuous parameter — a phenomenon that crops up everywhere matter is described by spring networks (rubber elasticity, geological viscoplasticity, polymer rheology, dough). The same equations describe both the standing column and the spreading puddle. The transition is not at a sharp k value but a soft band: between the two regimes is a third behavior (settling-but-misshapen) that the gallery does not exhibit but the parameter sweep would reveal. The cluster makes visible that "soft body" is not a single behavior; it is a parameter family.

Key artifacts: gallery_marker_soft-body carries the sb08_jelly_grid_floppy configuration. The collapsed pancake is the artifact — failure-to-stand staged as a study object rather than as a bug.

Gap: A live mass dial alongside the stiffness slider would let learners explore the (k, m, g) parameter cube, since increasing mass or gravity at fixed stiffness produces the same regime transition. The current 8-room gallery samples four points; an interactive control plate would let learners walk the entire surface.
