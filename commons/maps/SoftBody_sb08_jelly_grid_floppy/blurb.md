The same lattice as sb07 — but stiffness turned far down. Drop it. The grid does not wobble and does not settle. It spreads. The springs are too weak to hold the cube against its own weight; the corners outrun the center; the shape that was a column becomes a thin pancake puddled across the floor.

Each spring still does its small job, pulling back toward its rest length whenever stretched. The job has been quieted: the restoring force is so small that gravity wins every local argument before the springs can finish making it. By the time the integration loop catches up, the lattice has flowed.

This is what makes jelly jelly and not gel. Below a certain stiffness threshold, the material stops behaving like a solid that deforms and starts behaving like a viscous fluid that briefly remembers its shape. The numerical scheme is the same. The springs are the same. The qualitative regime has crossed a line.
