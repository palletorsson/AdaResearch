# Mixing

Seventy-two coloured bodies in a glass jar, and four ways of looking at them: where
they lie, the six counts an instrument keeps, the number those counts make, and a
shape grown from that number. Each way of looking is a terrace, and each terrace
stands a metre below the one before it. You can walk down. You cannot walk back.

Take your time on the top floor — it is the only place where the jars still differ.
Two of them are as unlike each other as anything in the room, and you will meet them
again downstairs as one picture, and again in the doorway as one number.
