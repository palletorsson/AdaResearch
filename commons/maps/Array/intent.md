Concept: The index room. A single walkable hall that carries the whole array grammar in one ascent of dimension — the unit you can pick up, the row along X, the column along Z, the 4x4 plane, the 4x4x4 volume, and finally arrays that do work instead of standing still. The claim is that an array is not a container but an addressing scheme: before a value can be stored anywhere, somewhere has to be nameable.

Sequence role: Opening map of the Color sequence, and the map that absorbed the retired array_tutorial sequence in the 2026-08-24 fold (c47be1717, "Array IS the index room — the twin retires"). It seats the learner in addressing before Pattern_Foundry manufactures structure and the color maps thread hue through it — index first, pattern second, color third. Prerequisite is primitives; nothing here needs to be solved, only walked and counted. The map declares `museum.gate = false` (304fe0305), so as a museum hall it refuses its sliding door and opens directly onto its axis rather than through an antechamber.

Technical angle: Twenty by twenty-four, five heights, thirteen placed artifacts across three basin pools ([4,2,6,1], [5,9,1,4], [3,16,5,5]). The dimensional ladder is literal placement, not metaphor: `xyz_coordinates` and `pick_up_cube` at the entrance give the addressing scheme and the unit; `row_3_x` places three cubes along one axis and `column_3_z` three along the other; `grid_2d_4x4` lays sixteen across the floor; `grid_3d_4x4x4` stacks sixty-four into a lattice you circle. The last pool switches substrate — `bar_array` and `bar_array_bubble_sort` are MultiMesh bars whose heights ARE the values, `grid3d` is the same lattice traversed by BFS, and `sorting_algorithm_race` runs four sorts on the same sixteen bars at once. `dark_sphere` and `pulsar_visualizer` stand as invariant references between the pools. Two teleporters and a wedge are the only utilities; the scale and rotate cubes that came in with the tutorial map were removed (5cb5a03d1) as a transformation lesson standing one rung early in a room about addressing.

Critical angle: The room argues that indexing is prior to content and that this priority is a decision, not a fact of the world. Walking the ladder makes the cost visible — each added dimension is another number you must carry to say where you are, and by the 4x4x4 lattice the learner is doing in their body what a nested loop does on paper. Then the last basin turns the tables: the same row of bars that was passive furniture in the first pool begins comparing and swapping itself, and the array stops being a place things are kept and becomes a thing that acts. Putting sorting at the end of an addressing room says that algorithms are what addresses are FOR — that the grid is not neutral storage but the precondition for any operation at all. The bubble sort is kept deliberately slow and honest about its O(n²) rather than hidden behind a faster one.

Key artifacts: `xyz_coordinates` lays the addressing scheme bare — three numbers that turn empty space into an array of positions; `pick_up_cube` isolates the unit as carried displacement, position rewritten by the player's own movement; `row_3_x` and `column_3_z` are the same cube counted along each axis; `grid_2d_4x4` and `grid_3d_4x4x4` raise it to plane and volume; `bar_array` stands the array up so a list of numbers is a shape; `sorting_algorithm_race` runs Bubble, Insertion, Selection and Merge on identical data so strategy is felt as difference in motion; `grid3d` traverses the lattice by BFS; `dark_sphere` stays simple so the changes around it stay legible.

Gap: The ladder is walked but not authored — nothing lets the learner set an index and see which cube answers, which is the one interaction that would turn addressing from a claim into a demonstration. The dimensional jump from the 2D plane to the 3D lattice is the map's hardest moment and currently has no scaffold between the two pools. `bar_array` and `bar_array_bubble_sort` are the same substrate at two settings placed side by side, which reads as duplication rather than as one object under two cartridges. Not yet walked in VR (the whole sequence is at stage 6), so whether the ascent lands bodily is untested.
