# PG Sculpted Forms

Geometry from accumulation. A cube lands on a cube lands on a cube — the mound rises not from design but from repeated deposit. The dome arcs overhead through incremental angular steps, each segment placed by the same rule rotated forward. Membranes layer and fold, thin surfaces stacking into thickness.

Three artifacts. Three ways to build form without sculpting it. The mound piles upward like sediment. The dome sweeps like a compass arm. The membrane wraps like skin forming over absence. None require a blueprint. All require a rule and a willingness to repeat.

This is construction at the threshold — deterministic enough to hold shape, accumulative enough to surprise. The form isn't drawn. It precipitates.
