## The walk

The chapter opens with the oldest learning algorithm on Earth, and it has no mind at all. The 9_3_smart_rockets_vr fire toward a target, and the ones that fly truest breed the next generation; the 9_5_evolving_bloops and evolved_creatures do the same with bodies, fitness culling the clumsy and keeping the lucky. The evolvingflowers bloom under selection; the non_teleological_evolution piece states the hard truth beside them — nothing here is *aiming*, there is no goal in the system, only differential survival that looks like purpose from outside. In the corner, a gradient_descent_visualization waits, the chapter's other engine, quieter.

## What it opens

The threshold's move is to establish learning without a learner. Evolution optimizes — it climbs fitness landscapes — but it holds no model, forms no intention, and cannot see where it is going; it only keeps what worked. This is the chapter's founding provocation and its truth in embryo: *learning is optimization in a landscape you can't fully see*, and the first optimizer needed no eyes at all. Everything that follows — descent, networks, attention — is this same blind hill-climbing, given memory and made fast.
