A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 region at its center. Boolean patterns flip cells on or off — true or false, present or absent. The arena reduces procedural generation to its smallest decision: yes or no.

Random processes fill the grid. Each algorithm produces a different texture from the same binary alphabet. Some cluster. Some scatter. Some form structures that look intentional but aren't. The learner watches patterns emerge from coin-flip logic, manipulates the rules, sees how constraint shapes chaos into something that resembles design.

Every procedural system begins here — not with complexity, but with a single bit. One or zero. The rest is just asking the question more times, in more interesting arrangements.
