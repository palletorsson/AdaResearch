Twenty-four objects in six rows across a wide room, and eighteen of them are things this museum has never shown. Every artifact in the boolean chapter is a family: each declares a named dial with four settings, and every placement anywhere else in the building shows you exactly one of them, the one it shipped with. Here the other three stand up — one family per band, two values on your left and two on your right, so you cross the floor to finish the sentence.

The four settings are not in the object. They are in a file, and a second file, and the two do not always agree. That makes this room usable for something other than looking: a band is a published claim standing next to the thing it is about, so you can check it. Three of the six bands here walk their ladder in a different order than their code does. Two match exactly. One checks itself at runtime, which nothing else in the chapter does.
