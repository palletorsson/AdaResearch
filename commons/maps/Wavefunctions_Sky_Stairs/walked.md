## The walk

The world room is one gesture, scaled until it becomes a place: a staircase wound on a cylinder, each step set by the sine — you climb the function. The recent unpacking cleared its crowding (the stairs breathe now), and the climb reads as what it is: period underfoot, amplitude in the legs, the wave experienced as effort and return. The math_objects shelf waits at the base like a legend on a map.

## What the world says

To inhabit a wave is different from watching one. Halfway up, the body learns what the graph never quite taught — that a wave is a promise of coming back down, that crest and trough are the same news twice. The chapter's world is this: mathematics load-bearing enough to climb.
