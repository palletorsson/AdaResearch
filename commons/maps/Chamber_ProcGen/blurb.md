The golem rebuilds from whatever is nearby. You cannot destroy it. Every piece you knock off becomes material for the next version.

This is the catalyst chamber for Procedural Generation — where destruction feeds reconstruction. The `bricoleur_golem` has no fixed form. It samples the environment at every tick and reassembles itself from available geometry. The catalyst mode is none because there is nothing to project; the fight is already over as a combat proposition. The Science Screen renders the golem's current assembly as a grid so you can track what it just integrated. Bricolage, in Lévi-Strauss's sense: the maker working from whatever is to hand. Procedural generation at its truest — form without predetermined parts, structure emerging from constraints the moment constraints change.
