## The cast

proximity_spawner (bricoleur_golem) · catalyst_target ×4 · science_screen

## The walk

The chapter's authorless growth comes at you as a golem. The proximity_spawner releases a bricoleur_golem that rebuilds itself from whatever is nearby — you cannot destroy it, because every piece you knock off becomes material for the next version of it, the room's own rubble reassembled into a new body. You place catalyst blocks on the four targets, and even those become its bricolage, folded into the golem's endless self-repair.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to grow**. The whole chapter insisted a world needs no author — that rules and randomness are enough — and the golem is that thesis made adversarial: a form with no designer and no fixed shape, only a rule for turning nearby matter into more of itself. You cannot win by subtraction because subtraction is its food; the only way to change it is to change its *environment*, to place blocks that shift what it finds to build from. This is the chapter's verb handed to your hands: you do not author the golem, you gardener it, editing the field of materials and letting the rule do the growing. The room plants procedural generation as a stance toward making — that the powerful move is never to sculpt the thing directly but to seed the conditions and let form arrive on its own.
