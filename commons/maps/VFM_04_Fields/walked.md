## The walk

This is the chapter as a world: force detached from any single pusher and spread across space itself. Every cell holds a direction; standing somewhere *means* being pushed somehow. The visualizers show the geography plainly; the flow_field_painter hands you the brush, so you can author weather and then walk through your own authorship. The magnetic_simulation and the vortex are found fields — nature's handwriting — while noc_5_04's agents demonstrate obedience: drop a walker anywhere and it becomes the field's biography. The interactive_point_origin_force is the room's best joke and its thesis: pick up the origin itself and it pulses, pushing things — the reference point revealed as one more actor.

## What the world says

Force as place. The chapter began with pushes you felt and ends its worldbuilding with an atmosphere of tendency — you stop asking *who pushed* and start asking *where am I standing*. That shift, from agent to field, is the widest thing the chapter knows.
