Twelve algorithms. One grid. Each one reshapes the same 8×8 field through a different species of randomness. Gaussian distributions cluster cubes into bell-curve peaks. Random walks trace drunken paths, raising terrain wherever they stumble. Height maps roll dice per cell. Profiles carve silhouettes from noise. Removal algorithms dissolve structure — column by column, row by row, or all at once.

Every algorithm sounds. Pitch tracks height. Rhythm follows the walk. The grid becomes a score, and the score becomes architecture. What separates a landscape from a composition is only the axis you're listening along.

Randomness is not the absence of pattern. It is pattern before selection has arrived.
