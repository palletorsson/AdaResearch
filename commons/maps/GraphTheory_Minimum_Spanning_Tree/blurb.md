A graph connects. An edge weighs. The minimum spanning tree finds the cheapest way to hold everything together — maximum connectivity, minimum cost. Kruskal sorts edges and grows a forest. Prim expands from a single root. Both arrive at the same skeleton.

Watch algorithms compete on an 8×8 grid. Edges appear, get tested, get kept or discarded. The spanning tree emerges not by seeing the whole — but by making greedy local decisions that turn out to be globally optimal. No planning. No backtracking. Just the right rule applied at the right scale.

Every network hides a tree. Every tree is the leanest version of belonging — the minimum structure required for nothing to be left out.
