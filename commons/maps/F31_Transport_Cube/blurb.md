Platforms float over voids, then move. Step onto one and it carries you — upward along the y-axis, sideways along x, forward along z. The room itself is the vehicle. Stand still and the architecture relocates you.

The transport cube breaks the contract between floor and ground. Floors are supposed to stay put. These do not. Movement becomes passive, environmental — you are not walking, you are being carried. Elevators, conveyor belts, escalators: all are rooms that move. The space is the machine.
