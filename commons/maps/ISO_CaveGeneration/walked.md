## The walk

The algorithm graduates to land-maker. The marchingcave runs generation at world scale — corridors and chambers emerging wherever the noise dips below the line, a cave *system* nobody drew, its floor plan an accident of a field. The marchingcubes_cave opens the same machine as an instrument: field in, fifteen cases per cell, mesh out — the chapter's whole pipeline run end to end while you watch it think.

## The world

This is how modern worlds get made. Every procedurally generated cave, every voxel game's diggable ground, every medical scan turned into a printable organ — the same pipeline: sample a field, march the cells, trust the table. The world-lesson is about authorship at scale: the designer of these caves wrote no cave; they wrote a *distribution*, and the field did the drafting. Generation is the field's authorship — the human moves one level up, from shaping places to shaping the odds of places.
