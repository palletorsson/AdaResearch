## The cast

proximity_spawner (gridagent) · catalyst_target ×4 · science_screen

## The walk

No new weapon here — instead, a study. The proximity_spawner releases a gridagent that traverses, copies, and patterns across the floor, stepping cell to cell the way an index walks an array. You place obstacles with the bracelet on the four targets, and the agent adapts, routing around your blocks, copying itself along the rows, filling the space by rule. The science_screen holds the grid steady beneath the whole demonstration.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to index**. The array chapter taught that a grid is not a picture but an addressing scheme — every cell reachable by its coordinates, every element one step from its neighbors — and here the gridagent makes that abstraction move. It traverses because it can name where it is; it copies because a pattern is just an operation repeated across indices; it adapts to your obstacles because a good traversal reads the grid's current state, not a fixed plan. You plant the verb by shaping the field it walks: place a block and you change the addresses available, and the agent's path rewrites itself around your edit. The room leaves you understanding, in the body, that to index is to make space computable — to turn a floor into a thing an algorithm can think with.
