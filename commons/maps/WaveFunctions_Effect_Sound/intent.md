Concept: One oscillation entering another's rule — phase modulation heard before it is named. A baseline tone, one parameter changed, the two compared back to back, and the readout naming what differed.

Sequence role: The pivot of Wavefunctions from waves you see and walk to a wave you hear. Intro distinguished position from state, Pendulum distinguished motion from its selected record, Sine Space distinguished a visible boundary from the route and measurement assigned to it; Effect Sound asks what another sampling system — synthesis at 44,100 samples a second, a buffer, the air, the ear — makes audible. On the active route AirMusic follows, with a struck resonance; Bernini and Cage lie further along the chapter.

Technical angle: `DualBallFMController` — carrier (frequency, attack, decay) and modulator (ratio, index, modulator decay) as two grabbable balls in 0.5 m cages, staged on two reading desks at hand height (`#stand:desks`). The modulator m(t) = I·e^(−t/τm)·sin(2π·R·fc·t) enters the carrier's phase; a linear attack and an exponential decay shape the note; a tanh keeps every sample within half of full scale; a note lasts 1.2 × the decay, three seconds at most, and is synthesised on the main thread when a ball moves (one note per 0.3 s at most) or a button is pressed. The evidence scope (`#evidence:longhand`) draws modulator, carrier, the rule and the output from the generator's own sample function. An AUDITION panel (BASELINE / HOLD / PLAY / COMPARE) and a cased readout make the one-parameter comparison an instrument rather than a memory: HOLD keeps the synthesised note itself as A.

Critical angle: The stack between the expression and the ear is not hidden. The readout prints how long the note took to synthesise, the scope shows what is being computed, and the racks, the monitor and the cables stand for the wider infrastructure of sound. A comparison you can replay is a small politics of listening: the reference is held, not remembered, so a preference has to survive hearing A again.
