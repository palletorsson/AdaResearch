Sound is oscillation made audible. Waveforms become bleeps and bloops — the 8-bit aesthetic of classic games built from square waves and frequency sweeps. Play with the building blocks of chiptune music, hear mathematics sing.

The synth racks — `Rack303Acid`, `RackDX7Piano`, `RackMoogBass`, `RackSineBasic` — cover genre-spanning synthesis from acid-squelch to FM-piano. The `VRAudioMonitor` renders your output as moving waveform; the `chord_tension_spring` lets you feel harmonic intervals as physics; the `cable_builder` wires everything modularly. Sound is the most direct experience of oscillation — you do not observe it, you feel it. Latency makes it auditory perception's reaction to the past. Quantization noise makes digital audio an approximation the ear usually forgives. The chiptune aesthetic makes the approximation audible on purpose.
