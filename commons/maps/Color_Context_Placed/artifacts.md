## Primary families

`chroma_stack` carries the initial comparisons and four richer compositions. `color_dna_stack` loads actual gallery JSON snapshots through the existing primitive_stack renderer. Seven source configurations make eight studies. The final two combine multiple configurations. These are static constructions, with convex collision per gallery mesh, not a balance simulation.

The two chroma_translation placements (four copies each) have been removed from this hall at Palle's request. Their script remains available; their map positions and prose are in the before/ archive of `doc/space/color-dna-columns-2026-09-17/`.

Gallery source IDs and hashes are in that folder's references.json. The art-labelled studies are adaptations, not replicas of historical paintings or furniture.
