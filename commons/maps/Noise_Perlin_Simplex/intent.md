Concept: A fair comparison of two noise bases. Two fields of half-metre cubes stand on one floor — one placed with generator:perlin, one drawing simplex — with the same seed, the same octave count, frequency and gain, the same sampling coordinates and the same colour ramp. Each has a reachable panel (FREQ, OCTAVES, REGEN, REPLAY) and a readout that names the basis from the generator itself and prints the field's value at two fixed coordinates. Only the basis differs, and the room asks what had to stay the same for that sentence to be true.
Sequence role: Closes the Noise sequence on the current spine, after Noise_6_Wall. The spine puts cellularautomata straight after noise and the museum's own passage carries the walk there, so the route continues to CA_Introduction; the noise sequence's own JSON does not encode that — its maps array ends at Lab_Path and its unlocks name proceduralgeneration and morphogenesis. The portal to Lab_Path is an optional extension and, as of 2026-09-13, inert: its destination does not resolve. Hands on the distinction between sampling a field and updating a neighbourhood of stored states.
Technical angle: FastNoiseLite with noise_type chosen by NoiseVisualizer._noise_type_for; both visualizers sample get_noise_2d(x·frequency, z·frequency) on a 0.5 m grid; declared seed (seed_value, -1 keeps randi()), shared ramp constants, field size in cells (size:8 → a 4 m field of the same cubes), fractal_gain declared on both. Placed as `simplex_noise:0:1.1#seed:20260910#size:8#ramp:shared#panel:front` and `perlin_noise:0:1.1#generator:perlin#seed:20260910#size:8#ramp:shared#panel:front`.
Critical angle: The display named Perlin drew simplex for a year because the match statement's default was simplex and nobody passed the word. A name on a scene is a claim; the readout now reports the basis from the generator, not from the label. The same integer in two bases is not the same field twice — repeatability is per basis, correspondence is not promised — and the lattice each basis hangs its gradients on signs the output whether or not a visitor can see it at this scale.
Key artifacts: simplex_noise and perlin_noise are the primary pair; perlin_noise_terrain shows the same sampling turned into ground as a secondary; configurable_portal#dest_map:Lab_Path is the optional side room; dark_sphere anchors. noise_terrain (a 100 m footprint that used to be the room's floor) was removed from this hall on 2026-09-10 when the basin was floored; it is archived in doc/space/waves-chance-noise-pilots-2026-09-10.
