## The walk

The room's namesake engine spins the grid: cubes rotated by three independent draws, one per axis — position holding perfect order while orientation surrenders completely. RotateGridCubes shows the disciplined version beside it. And then the room's true hero: hardware_entropy_decay, radioactive-style decay events harvested as true randomness, each tick an unrepeatable fact of the world, feeding the random_decay_multimesh where a population of forms crumbles stochastically — no two runs of the room ever the same room.

## What it fixes

This is where the threshold's trial verdict gets embodied. The rotations could run on a PRNG and you'd never see the difference — but the decay pieces *cannot*: their disorder is imported from physics, not computed. The room's quiet structure — order in position, freedom in orientation — is the chapter's design thesis in one glance: controlled entropy, the grid holding one register steady so that wildness in another register becomes legible as wildness.
