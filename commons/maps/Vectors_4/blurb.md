A vector carries magnitude and direction. Projection decomposes it — drops one vector's shadow onto another, extracting the component that aligns. Reflection flips what remains. Two operations, one object split into parallel and perpendicular. The geometry of agreement and refusal.

Motion vectors encode change as direction. Velocity is a vector. Acceleration is a vector acting on a vector. Position updates frame by frame — not because objects "move" but because vectors accumulate. The dark sphere pulses in its own slow orbit, a body governed by the same arithmetic.

Projection asks: how much of me fits along you? Reflection answers: what's left keeps going. Every spatial transformation is a negotiation between alignment and deviation. The vector doesn't choose — it resolves.
