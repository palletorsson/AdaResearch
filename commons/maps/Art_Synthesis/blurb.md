# Art Synthesis

Escher drew staircases that climb forever. Magritte painted a pipe and wrote "this is not a pipe." Malevich hung a black square and called it supreme. Three acts of sabotage against representation — each one a proof by other means.

Locally coherent, globally impossible. The Penrose staircase works at every step but fails as a whole — visual Gödel, incompleteness you can walk. Magritte's pipe splits sign from signified with six words. The Minimalists evacuated meaning from the object and meaning rushed back in — the dark room paradox made gallery-sized.

Art doesn't illustrate mathematics. It arrives first. The impossible staircase existed before Gödel's theorem had a name. The treachery of images preceded semiotics. Artists see the fracture in formal systems and make it visible — hang it on a wall, dare you to climb it.

*Ceci n'est pas une preuve.*
