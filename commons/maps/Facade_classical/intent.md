Concept: Facade_Classical is the *canonical actualization* of the facade assembly principle. Where the principle map names six compositional moves — column orders, bay rhythm, hierarchy, fenestration, rustication, cornice/base — this map applies them as cleanly as the grammar allows: symmetric composition, full column order, three-zone vertical hierarchy (base / piano nobile / attic), regular five-bay horizontal rhythm, smooth ashlar surface, cornice and base framing the whole. No syncopation, no hybridity, no critique. The principle is the subject; the facade is the principle answering "what does my cleanest expression look like?"

Actualizes: Facade_Assembly_Principle, in the *canonical* mode. The map takes a position in the virtual field where every axis is at its rule-respecting value: column order Doric, rhythm regular, hierarchy explicit, fenestration symmetric, rustication smooth, cornice/base both present. The expression maps that follow (Baroque, Venetian Gothic, Rustication, NYC Tenement, Critique) each deviate from this canonical position along one or more axes. This map is the *baseline against which deviation will be legible*.

Sequence role: Second map of the facade_assembly branch sequence (after Facade_Assembly_Principle). The player walks from the principle's six labelled exemplar tiles to standing in front of an actual facade where those six exemplars are integrated into one composition. Establishes the reference body so subsequent expression maps can be read as variations from it. Leads forward to Facade_Baroque (the principle dramatized).

Technical angle: A five-bay Renaissance-canonical facade rendered as a wall composition along the front edge of the room. Three storeys: a rusticated base (heavier blocks at ground), a piano nobile (taller storey with paired pilasters between bays), an attic (lighter, shorter, with reduced fenestration). The base is broader at the ground and steps inward subtly. The cornice projects horizontally to frame the composition at top. Bays are regular: window-pilaster-window-pilaster... across the five-bay width. Pass-2 build: the room walks today as an empty 10x6 with spawn and teleporter; the facade composition's architectural primitives (column_order_doric, bay_rhythm_marker, fenestration_row, rustication_smooth, cornice_horizontal) are queued for pass-3 artifact authoring. The room is shaped for the facade to land on the front edge when those artifacts exist.

Critical angle: The canonical actualization is *not* a neutral baseline. The Renaissance grammar carries assumptions — that hierarchy is good, that symmetry equals authority, that the piano nobile *deserves* the privileged storey. To call this "the principle clean" is already to take a position. The map presents the canonical so the deviations that follow can be read as legible refusals (Memphis Totem refuses hierarchy) or critiques (Superstudio's continuous monument refuses bay rhythm). But the canonical itself is a refusal too — of, say, vernacular spontaneity, of irregular addition, of the asymmetry of actual lived buildings. The player should leave this map with the rule installed *and* aware that having a rule was itself a choice.
