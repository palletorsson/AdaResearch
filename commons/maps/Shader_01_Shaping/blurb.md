A gradient runs from black to white. Left to right. Linear. Boring. Now bend it.

`step()` is a knife — everything below the threshold is zero, everything above is one. No transition. Binary. `smoothstep()` softens the cut, easing between states with a cubic curve that has zero derivative at both ends. Arrival without impact. `sin()` breathes — oscillation as primitive, the wave that never resolves. `pow()` redistributes: raise the curve and shadows deepen, lower it and midtones bloom. Four functions. Four ways to redistribute intensity across a gradient.

A dark sphere pulses in the space, its emission shaped by the same math. Every function here is a policy — a decision about how value distributes across a range. Shaping is not decoration. It is the politics of interpolation.
