# The face and the space

The facade makes a recognisable promise of depth. Here a particular room follows it, and the positive counterpart lets the visitor recognise that room from outside. The same doorway profile that admitted the body becomes a surface that stops it. Appearance alone does not settle which relation is being offered.

Rachel Whiteread's Ghost and House hold the traces of particular domestic places. This invented digital room has no prior inhabitants and should not borrow their history. Its value is an encounter with positive and negative space that can open a more grounded discussion of memory, preservation, privacy and loss. See the primary references in final.md. An irregular interior may exceed an exterior's expectation without becoming a universal image of queer freedom.
