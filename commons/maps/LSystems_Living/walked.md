## The cast

genetic_tree_sculptor · branching_coral · lsystem_tree

## The walk

The grammar meets its gardener. The genetic_tree_sculptor breeds trees instead of writing them — populations of grammars mutated and selected by your taste, evolution steered by pointing at the offspring you prefer. The branching_coral and lsystem_tree stand as the poles of the room's argument: the coral's shape settled by environment, the classic tree's by rule.

## The turn (critical)

The critical room asks who the author of a living form is, and refuses the easy answers. Not the grammar alone — the sculptor shows a thousand grammars nobody wrote, discovered by drift and preference. Not the breeder alone — your taste can only choose among what mutation offers. Not the environment alone — the coral's current shapes but does not draft. Authorship smears across rule, chance, selection, and world, which is Lindenmayer's formalism arriving at Darwin's conclusion: design without a designer is not the absence of authorship but its *distribution*. The room leaves the question on you: when you point at the tree you prefer, whose preference is that — yours, or everything that grew you?
