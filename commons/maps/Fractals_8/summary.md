# Fractals_8 - Cantor Contemplation

## Overview
Fractals_8 provides a contemplative space for comparing the Cantor set (1D) with cube subdivision (3D). Where Fractals_7 introduced the dimensional extension, this map invites sustained observation of both forms together, allowing players to internalize the principle that connects them.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 7×8 grid
- **Architecture**: Flat platform, minimal structure to focus attention
- **Height**: Single level

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **Cantor Set** (3,3) - 1D Cantor visualization
- **Cube Subdivision** (1,6) - 3D Menger principle demonstration
- **Dark Sphere** (3,2) - Ambient lighting element

### Utilities
- **Teleporter** (5,6) - Exit to Fractals_9

## Atmosphere
- **Background**: Sky blue [0.2, 0.3, 0.7]
- **Lighting**: Warm directional with cool ambient
- **Mood**: Contemplative, comparative, meditative

## Learning Sequence
1. Player enters the quiet space
2. Observes both forms simultaneously
3. Notices parallels: removal pattern, self-similarity, fractal dust
4. Spends time with the comparison
5. Internalizes: same principle, different dimensions
6. Exits with deeper understanding of dimensional invariance
7. Proceeds to Fibonacci and Sierpinski

## Design Intent
This map is a **pause for integration**. After the conceptual intensity of dimensional extension, players need space to consolidate understanding. The minimal design focuses attention on the mathematical relationship rather than new complexity.

## Connection to Sequence
- **Position in fractals sequence**: 8/14
- **Precedes**: Fractals_9 (Fibonacci and Sierpinski)
- **Follows**: Fractals_7 (3D Cantor Variations)
- **Establishes**: Consolidation of the deletion principle across dimensions
