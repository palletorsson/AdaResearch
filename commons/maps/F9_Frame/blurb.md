Tall walls surround you, but two gaps — north and south — cut through to open sky. The walls do not block the view; they select it. Step to one side and the gap frames a different slice of the world beyond.

A frame is a wall that chooses what you see. Architecture becomes a viewport, editing reality into a composition. The opening is more powerful than the wall. What the frame excludes matters as much as what it reveals. Seeing is always partial.
