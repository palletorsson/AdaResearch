## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to settle**. The chapter taught that a form is the answer to a minimization problem — the hanging chain, the soap film, the balanced mobile, none of them drawn, each the shape that spends the least energy. Here you make by *relaxing* rather than carving: you set the constraints, the anchor points and the loads, and then the room computes the rest by letting it fall into equilibrium. You cannot dictate the curve; you can only choose what hangs from where and trust the physics to find the beauty. To settle is to author form by authoring its conditions and stepping back — nature computes shape by being lazy, and this chamber hands you the laziness as a craft.
