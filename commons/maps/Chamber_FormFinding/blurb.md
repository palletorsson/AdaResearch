The chamber's verb is *to settle*.

The room is set to forces. You place a block on the target not as a fixed thing but as a hung point — and then you let go. The form relaxes toward the shape that costs the least. No line drawn. A load released, and a geometry found.

The chapter taught that form is the answer to a minimization problem: the hanging chain, the soap film, the balanced mobile, none of them drawn. Here you make by relaxing rather than carving. You choose what hangs from where and trust the physics to find the curve. Nature computes shape by being lazy; this chamber hands you the laziness as a craft.
