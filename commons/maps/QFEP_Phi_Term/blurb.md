# Phi Term: The Queer Signature

*φ = sensitivity to the rate of change.*

Lambda tells you where you are on the spectrum.
Phi tells you how you respond to MOVEMENT along it.

φ < 0: Resist change. Conservative. Preserve the current state.
φ = 0: Neutral. Neither resist nor embrace.
φ > 0: Embrace change. Queer. Welcome becoming.

Here you will:
- Experience both zones: conservative and becoming
- Move the φ slider and feel the difference
- Discover your own signature

Positive φ is not reckless. It's the capacity to find the edge of chaos and STAY there — surfing the wave of transformation rather than fighting it.

*This is the queer move in the formula.*
*Not just tolerating change. Valuing it.*
