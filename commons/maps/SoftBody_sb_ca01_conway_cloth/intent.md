Concept: Conway's Game of Life is iterated on a 2D grid for 8 steps; the resulting alive-cell pattern becomes a spring-mass cloth. Each alive cell at the final step is a mass point; each pair of alive cells in a 4-neighborhood relation is a structural spring. The top row of alive cells is pinned to the world. The cloth hangs under gravity as a tattered lattice. The map is a gallery study; the player walks around the single soft-body marker.

Sequence role: First CA→soft-body bridge (sb_ca01) in the imported soft-body gallery cluster within the Soft Bodies sequence (13th spine, integration phase). Contrast pair with sb_ca02 (Life-without-Death rule, dense filled mosaic). The pair shows that a CA rule is also a tapestry-weaver: the rule's behavior determines the topology of the spring network, which determines the cloth's structural integrity, which determines whether the result hangs as a coherent fabric or as a sparse lacework.

Technical angle: Conway's rule (B3/S23) iterated 8 generations on a ~20×20 toroidal or bounded grid with a seeded initial population. Mass points placed at the world coordinates of alive cells; springs created between alive cells whose 4-neighbor relation persisted to the final step. Top-row alive cells pinned; remaining vertices integrate under gravity with light damping. Verlet integration. Because Conway's dynamics tends toward sparse final populations (gliders, blocks, blinkers, scattered debris), the resulting spring graph is often disconnected or near-critical — some hangs cohere, others tear locally. The simulation is deterministic in the rule but stochastic in outcome via seed choice.

Critical angle: A cellular automaton is a function from grid states to grid states. A spring-mass cloth is a function from positions to forces. The DNA bridge insists they can be the same object — once a CA pattern exists, you can read it as the seed of a hanging surface. This is the same move L-systems make from grammar to plant body. The general claim of the soft-body gallery is that any rule that produces a discrete topology can be embodied as a physical structure, and the choice of rule shows up as the structural signature of the body. Conway's rule writes sparse, fragile bodies. Life-without-Death (sb_ca02) writes dense, woven bodies. The rule is the loom.

Key artifacts: gallery_marker_soft-body holds the sb_ca01_conway_cloth configuration. The tattered hanging lattice is the artifact and the demonstration of Conway-as-weaver.

Gap: A seed-selector control plate (preset gliders, blinkers, R-pentomino, random) would let learners see how the same Conway rule writes radically different cloths from different seeds — staging the dynamical-systems point that the rule and the initial condition together determine the orbit, not the rule alone.
