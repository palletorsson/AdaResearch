## The cast

becoming_catalyst · catalyst_target · science_screen

## The walk

The room runs a field beneath your feet, the science_screen glowing in field-mode. You take the bracelet from the becoming_catalyst and place a block on the catalyst_target — and where you place it, the field crosses its threshold, air becoming stone, the surface finding itself around your hand. Below the line is nothing; above it, world.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to threshold**. The chapter taught that inside and outside are not properties of space but a number's relation to a cutoff — and here you hold the cutoff. Every block you place raises the field past its level-set, and a skin appears at exactly the boundary you chose; move your intent and the surface redraws, the same field yielding a different world. The queer cave two rooms back argued that the cutoff is a politics; this chamber hands you that politics as a tool. To threshold is to decide where in becomes out, and the room plants the chapter's whole geometry lesson in a single act: you do not carve the surface, you *name the line*, and the line does the rest.
