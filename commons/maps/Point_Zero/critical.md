# Arriving inside inherited work

The first light is a first sight for this visitor, not the beginning of the world. The counter includes frames they did not witness. The room borrows code, labour, historical conditions and the visitor’s time. A chosen origin gives them somewhere to count from without settling that inheritance.

The preface now asks whether technical acceleration makes room for play or consumes the time for it. Point Zero stays with falling into a running room, unread inherited code and a chosen reference in space. “Death drive” enters as the author’s question about the desire to push past limits, not a diagnosis of AI or a scientific account of a render loop. Alice offers curiosity and risk together. Keep that invitation open.
