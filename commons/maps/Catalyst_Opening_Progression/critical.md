# What the ability is for

An ability first lets you make and keep something. The same point later lets you interrupt an approaching body. This sequence makes defence a changed use of a familiar capacity, rather than the first explanation of what power means.

The burning head waits before it moves. Its appearance does not by itself designate an enemy. The encounter becomes threatening through a direction, a warning and a movement towards the visitor. An interruption leaves the head present; a passage opens. No relationship of friendship or hostility is automatically settled by a hit.

The head's growing repertoire is authored: translation, then rotation, then scale. It is not autonomous learning. The trial asks whether sharing these operations makes the encounter legible, and whether opening a way is a sufficient incentive to fire. Those questions require play, beyond a passing engine test.
