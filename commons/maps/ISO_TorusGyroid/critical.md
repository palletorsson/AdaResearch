# The wall a picture forgot

A recurring opening is not necessarily an invitation to a particular body. The gyroid gives a repeatable relation in three coordinates; extraction, rendering and collision give different accounts of that relation. The capsule probe makes one small disagreement inspectable.

Increasing the capsule radius changes no visible surface. It changes what can pass along the same test. A blocked straight segment leaves winding routes undecided. Conversely, an omitted collider is not an opening: it is a missing rule in the simulation. Calling either event freedom would confuse an experience with its cause.

The existing specimen's noise can now be removed and restored. Formula is not an innocent origin: it still has a level, sampling density, finite domain and body scale. Keeping those choices visible lets the noise become an alteration we can discuss rather than a general sign for organic life or queer possibility.

Hold the earlier surface. Track a fold through the new one. What does this distortion permit, and what does its visual complexity prevent you from seeing? The question of possible bodies includes both the body meeting the opening and the implementation deciding which encounter counts.
