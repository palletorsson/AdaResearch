# Landscape research: a recurring experiment

Question: **When does repetition direct or obstruct a body?**

Implemented 12 September 2026: `sine_flow_tray` occupies its own forecourt and is the first of two primary book encounters. The corridor remains primary after it, with its wall collision deliberately off. The article's four-tray workshop remains unchanged.

The tray tests stationary sampled contact geometry, sphere size and relief under an explicit deadline. The corridor tests phase, amplitude, viewpoint and the limits of a visible boundary. Both now have text tied to their actual controls.

Next: headset reach and reading review when available; then carry a deliberately specified physical receiver into the random/noise encounters. Player walkability, concealment and optimisation remain separate questions.
