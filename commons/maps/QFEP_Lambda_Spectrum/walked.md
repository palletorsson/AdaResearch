## The walk

This map has one mechanic and it is the best one the lab owns: **your position is the λ value**. The room runs a spectrum from one end to the other, and walking it *is* setting the parameter. At the near end, λ = 0: `crystal_formation`, `ordered_grid` — frozen, exact, dead. At the far end, λ = 1: `chaos_particles`, `dissolving_form` — boiling, formless, differently dead. The world changes continuously as you move; particles shift regime, colors transform, and the `bifurcation_walkway` under your feet is the bifurcation diagram made floor — period-doubling opening beneath you as you walk toward chaos. The tutorial is a search task: somewhere past the middle, around λ ≈ 0.3–0.5, find the green zone. `edge_particles` and `complexity_pattern` mark it — structure that moves, pattern that renews itself without repeating and without dissolving. Walk past it into chaos, come back; overshoot into order, return. Calibrate. Your body is the probe, and by the third pass you can feel the transition coming before you see it.

## The turn (critical)

The room is the curriculum, compressed. Low λ is where the book began — primitives, the grid, pure structure; high λ is the randomness arc; and the places where the book came *alive* — the automata, the fractals, the L-systems, the reaction-diffusion leopard — were always in the green zone, though nothing said so at the time. Walking this spectrum is re-walking twenty sequences in forty meters, which is the map's argument for why λ deserves a room of its own: it is not one parameter among many, it is the *axis the whole book was secretly plotted on*. And the numbers are not decoration. Langton found that cellular automata compute only in a narrow band of his λ; Turing's morphogenesis lives at the same criticality; the claim "life exists at λ ≈ 0.3–0.5" is a measurable property of dynamical systems, not a vibe. What the map adds — what only this medium can add — is that the edge is *findable by feel*. You located it with your legs, overshot it, corrected. That matters against both failure modes the lab has staged: the point was never to admire the edge from a diagram (that is F's way of loving it, pinned and finished) nor to celebrate the whole spectrum indiscriminately (E's way). The point is the skill — knowing where the edge is from inside, so it can be found again in systems that do not come with a walkway.
