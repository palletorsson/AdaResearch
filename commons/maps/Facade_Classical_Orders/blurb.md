Tuscan is plain. Doric adds fluting. Ionic curls the capital. Corinthian erupts in acanthus leaves. Four proportional systems that evolved over two thousand years, each encoding a different relationship between column height, diameter, entablature weight, and ornamental density.

The orders are not decorative styles — they are parametric grammars. Change the height-to-diameter ratio and you move from stocky Tuscan to slender Corinthian. The same column, different parameters, different meaning.
