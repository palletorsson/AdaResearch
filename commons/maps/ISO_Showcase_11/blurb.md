# ISO Showcase 11 — Sculpture Gallery + Cave Explorer

Seven sculptures stand in a row. Each one computed from the same algorithm, different parameters — sphere, torus, strange attractors made solid. A control panel lets you scroll through preset caves, drag sliders for noise scale, iso level, chunk density. The geometry rebuilds in real time. Turn one knob and the cave breathes open. Turn another and it collapses into stone.

Marching cubes finds the boundary where a scalar field crosses threshold. Inside becomes outside becomes mesh. But the cave explorer does something stranger — it lets you sit at the threshold itself, adjusting where "inside" begins. The gallery traces each sculpture into islands, separating fused geometry into discrete bodies. What looked like one form was always many, held together by proximity alone.

The surface is a decision. The cave is what happens when you keep revising it.
