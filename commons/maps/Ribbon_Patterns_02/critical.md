# The carrier participates

The first comparison holds the targets' assigned values while changing their neighbours. The second holds a source texture while changing its carrier and a stated shading condition. The apparent similarity between these two experiments should not erase their difference: one invites a perceptual judgement about unchanged target values, while the other deliberately changes rendered values through a shader.

The experiment earns its argument by identifying which relation it changed. A word such as material, light or metamerism cannot substitute for that account. The earlier wave diagram computed no spectra; here that limit stays visible. Nor does a matte directional factor stand for all the ways cloth catches light or a dressed body is read.

What bodies are possible depends partly on which surface and appearance rules are available. Reusing a mark on a curved carrier does not make it an unchanged original in transit: spacing, projection and shading participate. Keeping the source fixed offers a comparison, not a privileged source outside mediation.

A garment and its wearer would introduce further relations. This room prepares tools for asking about them; the mirror and fabric transfer still require their own implemented encounter.
