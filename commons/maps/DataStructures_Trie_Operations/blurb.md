# Trie Fan-Out Room

One root. Twenty-six children. Each child, twenty-six more. The trie stores strings not as wholes but as shared paths — every word that begins with "un" walks the same two nodes before diverging. Compression through common prefix. The data structure that reads your mind mid-keystroke.

The room narrows at the entrance. A single node. Then it fans wide — branches splitting, splitting again, the geometry of possibility expanding with each character position. Walk the prefix tree and watch the space open. Randomized terrain rises and falls around each branch, procedural landscapes generated along divergent paths. Every fork is a letter. Every leaf is a completed word. The architecture *is* the autocomplete.

Tries don't search. They descend. O(k) for a key of length k — independent of how many words exist. The cost of finding is proportional not to the size of the collection but to the length of the question. A structure where knowing what you're looking for is the only thing that matters.
