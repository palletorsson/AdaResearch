A chasm with no floor and a far ledge you have to reach. On the near platform a machine lets you dial a force field that hovers over the void. Throw a cube in first and watch where the field takes it — point it down and the cube falls, aim it up-and-across and it sails to the other side. Then trust your own body to the same vector and step in. Falling is not a property of the void; it is a property of the field over it — and the field is a vector you can turn. Cross on a force you set yourself, and the far teleporter returns you to where the whole field began: Act I. The field is a circle.
