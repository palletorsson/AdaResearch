# The player becomes the receiver

Primary: `force_capability_lab`.

Extend the familiar point into a thruster. While held and activated, it reports an acceleration in the direction of your hand. The small station script creates two of these points.

Carry: The point now gives you a capacity in the world. Direction, acceleration, resistance and contact are things you can use together. The racer and the larger experiments give you places to try.

Check: Aim one hand up and one down. Before pressing, predict that the two thrusts cancel while gravity still acts. Then aim both upward: their 22 m/s² request exceeds the court’s 9.8 m/s² gravity. Release the triggers and watch how velocity continues to change.
