# Which body hears the field?

The field's strongest claim is also where it can fail. A common vector is offered to different bodies, but an offer is not yet an effect. The loose cube integrates acceleration. The desktop walker previously replaced that result with its own locomotion output. The crab commands its position through another controller. The shared computational medium gives us access to these differences; it does not dissolve them.

This hall makes two receiver paths explicit. It does not upgrade every body in the project into a universal field receiver. Within the opted-in volume, visitor locomotion gives its place to an inertial update. Outside, ordinary steering reclaims it. That boundary determines which history persists, and which movement is allowed to speak next.

There is a queer possibility in a crossing becoming available through a changed parameter. There is also a design decision in the word available. A field pointed upward can carry one implementation and leave another untouched. The question is not whether all bodies secretly were identical, but which translations could admit bodies the current rule cannot hold.

Zero acceleration is a useful interruption of expectation: motion can continue. The cube's damping and the visitor's undamped receiver complicate that apparent sameness. Keep the plank beside them. It is the history of an access repair and an ongoing comparison, rather than an embarrassing support to erase once the new demonstration works.
