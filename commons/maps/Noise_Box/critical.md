# What fits between samples?

A box assigns one answer to every position it contains. The finite interval makes the field buildable, but it can erase a distinction that mattered to a body: a narrow gap becomes solid, or a thin divider disappears.

The two rooms retain wide passages and a level floor. These are design decisions, not properties guaranteed by noise. In the QFEP reading, ask what this representation keeps available and what its resolution withholds; a rougher wall is not itself a measure of greater freedom.
