Concept: A spherical soft body is pinned at a small top ring and allowed to settle under stronger gravity, lower spring stiffness, and a longer 380-step simulation than gl01's bulb. The result is a vertically elongated bottle — same algorithm as gl01, different parameter trajectory, different ancestor. The map is a gallery study presenting one vessel from the glass-blowing series.

Sequence role: Second glass-blowing study (gl02) in the imported soft-body gallery cluster within the Soft Bodies sequence (13th spine, integration phase). Follows gl01 (classic bulb) and precedes gl03 (wide bowl), gl04 (lopsided vase), gl05 (collapsed sag). The cluster argues that a small family of physics knobs — pin-ring size, pressure, stiffness, gravity, steps — generates the entire typology of blown vessels, and that vessel identity is process-determined, not gesture-determined.

Technical angle: Spring-mass sphere mesh with a tightened top pin ring (fewer vertices fixed than gl01). Internal pressure as outward face-normal force; gravity as a uniform downward acceleration with magnitude greater than gl01. Spring stiffness k lowered so the membrane stretches more readily under load. Integration via small-step Verlet for stability. The vessel shape at step 380 is the snapshot of an ongoing flow — the equilibrium between outward pressure and downward gravity has not yet been reached at that step count, and the bottle is the still-stretching configuration captured mid-flight.

Critical angle: Gl01 reads as a steady-state object: pressure balances gravity inside the elastic envelope, equilibrium is reached, the bulb is what equilibrium looks like. Gl02 stages the opposite — vessel as transient, vessel as time-arrest of an unfinished process. "Frozen at the moment chosen by steps param" is the title's own phrasing, and it is the critical claim: the simulation has no preferred end state when stiffness is low and gravity high enough to keep stretching the body. Choice of when to stop the integration becomes an aesthetic and technical decision indistinguishable from the glassblower's choice of when to set the piece down.

Key artifacts: gallery_marker_soft-body presents the gl02_long_bottle configuration. The drooping vessel and its parameter trajectory together compose the lesson.

Gap: A steps-slider artifact would let learners scrub the simulation timeline forwards and backwards, replaying the same parameters and seeing the family of vessels each step value produces. The aesthetic choice of when to stop becomes a visible, single-control gesture rather than a JSON value.
