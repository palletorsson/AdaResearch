Particles search for optima. Each remembers its best position; all know the global best. Move toward both, add some randomness. The swarm explores and exploits simultaneously. Function optimization as social behavior — finding peaks through collective wandering.

`PSOVisualization` renders the fitness landscape as terrain viewed from the elevated observation deck. Each particle has position, velocity, personal best, and knows the global best. The velocity update combines inertia, cognitive pull toward personal best, and social pull toward global best — three forces balancing exploration against exploitation. Kennedy and Eberhart's PSO replaces the environment-as-memory (ACO pheromones) with agents-as-memory — each particle carries its own history and shares only its best result. Not stigmergy but social learning. The swarm converges, overshoots, re-converges, and finds the valley together.
