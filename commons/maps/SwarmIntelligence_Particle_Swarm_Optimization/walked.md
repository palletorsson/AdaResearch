## The walk

The swarm goes looking for something. Particle swarm optimization releases agents across a fitness_landscape_politics — a terrain of good and bad solutions — each particle pulled two ways at once: toward the best spot it has personally found, and toward the best spot the swarm has found together. The self_organizing_patterns station holds the theory beside it: stigmergy fields, phase transitions, attractor basins — the mathematics of how local pulls settle into global order.

## What it fixes

The second walk turns the flock into a *method*. The same three-rule sociality that made birds turn together, aimed at a problem instead of the sky, becomes search: the swarm converges on optima no member could locate alone. And the map's own name — fitness *landscape politics* — refuses to let the metaphor stay innocent. A fitness landscape encodes what counts as better, and whoever draws it rules the swarm as surely as any leader; the particles are free, obedient, and optimizing toward a value they did not choose. The room teaches optimization and indicts it in the same gesture.
