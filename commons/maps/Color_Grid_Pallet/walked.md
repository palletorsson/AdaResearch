# Color_Grid_Pallet — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (second walk slot, color thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

gridcolorizer · spectrum_forest · science_screen · dark_sphere

## The walk

Two scales of the same act. The gridcolorizer paints the map's own floor — the room you stand in becomes the palette, cell by cell, the grid confessing it was always a color field waiting for values. And the spectrum_forest grows the palette into a walkable stand of hue-trees, the gradient at ecosystem scale. The science_screen flattens whichever you face into a readable pane.

## What it fixes

The walk's second lesson is *address*: color lives somewhere. A palette is not a cloud of hues but an assignment — this cell, this value; this tree, this band. When the floor itself takes color, the difference between the room and its palette quietly disappears, and the chapter banks the idea it will spend at the walls later: surfaces are just palettes that hardened.
