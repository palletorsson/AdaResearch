# A direction for a body

The blade rotates around its middle. Neither bank owns the hinge. As one end rises the other descends; the same operation changes the relation with both landings. A higher grid surface can become reachable without moving the centre of the rotating object. This is an argument made by a particular length, axle height and pair of banks, not a property bestowed by the word rotation.

Beyond the landing, the angle is distributed through an array. The row supplies the amount, the band supplies the axis, and the visitor supplies a direction of travel. The x ridges cut across forward movement; the z ridges extend along it. Y changes the horizontal footprint. These distinctions can be inspected in matching cube centres, visible edges and physical surfaces.

The narrow and wide capsules receive the same forward instruction, without jumping or choosing a detour. Their stopping points belong to their dimensions and movement rules. In the z band, the wider body can slide sideways and continue where the narrow body stops: its arrival does not erase the change of route. Neither trial proves the array impassable for every body. A different approach, floor-angle setting or jump may find a remainder these tests omitted. The still aisles make it possible to inspect the difficult row without completing its route.

Rigid rotation preserves the topology of each solid. Traversal depends on how solids meet each other and a body. Maintaining that distinction lets the installation test a claim about connectivity without claiming to have changed the topology of a cube.

Increasing a parameter is not a promise of increasing difficulty forever. This score continues to ninety degrees, where a cube's geometry can repeat an axis-aligned orientation. The range selected for the experiment makes the progression legible, and also leaves something outside it. Keep the range visible beside the array.

The first court gives a twelve-second pause for a climb or boarding a level end-platform. The ride exposes an additional rule: the platform is kept upright while its position follows the rotating blade. Without that rule a standing body loses support as the blade becomes vertical. X offers forward displacement, while Z demands a sideways destination. The second holds the geometry still. These are two different distributions of time and attention. Neither should be accepted as adequate for a group until people have walked the revised hall.


The last court keeps a floor under your feet while the tunnel's frames turn around the passage. Beside it, the carousels distribute different rates through equal layer heights. The same code can be read as a constraint to cross, a rhythm to follow or an enclosure to inhabit. These possibilities need their specific operations named: subtraction makes a hole; a turn orients it; repetition supplies neighbours; time lets their relations move.
