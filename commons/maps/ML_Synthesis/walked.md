## The walk

The chapter closes by asking its machines to account for themselves. The anomaly_detection station learns what "normal" looks like and flags what departs from it — the quiet workhorse behind fraud alerts and fault warnings, a model whose whole competence is noticing the unfamiliar. The explainable_ai_xai_vr holds the instruments that interrogate a black box — SHAP, LIME, Grad-CAM — each one asking a trained model *why* and getting a partial, honest, contestable answer. The joint_learn_walk gathers the thread's engines into one last shared floor.

## The close

The close is about accountability, and it is the right ending for a chapter whose truth is that learning works without understanding. After evolution's blind breeding, descent's local slope, the network that no layer comprehends, and the GAN's two ignorances making a convincing lie — the last room insists the machine show its reasons. Not because the reasons are the truth; a saliency map is not a mind explaining itself, only a trace of where the pressure fell. But a decision no one can question is a decision no one can refuse, and a curriculum that teaches these systems owes its walkers the tools to talk back. The chapter that began with rockets learning by dying ends with a demand: if the landscape cannot be fully seen, then at least let the climber be asked to point at where it thinks it stands.
