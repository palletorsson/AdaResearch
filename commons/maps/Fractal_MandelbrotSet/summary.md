# Fractals_9 - Fibonacci and Sierpinski

## Overview
Fractals_9 juxtaposes two fundamental mathematical structures: the Fibonacci sequence (additive growth) and the Sierpinski triangle (subtractive fractal). Both connect to the golden ratio and self-similarity, but through different mechanisms—one builds up, one carves down.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 7×8 grid
- **Architecture**: Raised center platform with viewing positions
- **Height**: Up to level 2

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **Fibonacci Sequences** (3,3) - Visualization of Fibonacci growth patterns
- **Sierpinski Triangle** (1,6) - Classic triangular fractal through deletion
- **Dark Sphere** (3,2) - Ambient lighting element

### Utilities
- **Teleporter** (5,6) - Exit to Fractals_10

## Atmosphere
- **Background**: Sky blue [0.2, 0.3, 0.7]
- **Lighting**: Warm directional with cool ambient
- **Mood**: Mathematical harmony, golden proportions, natural growth

## Learning Sequence
1. Player enters and observes Fibonacci visualization
2. Notes the additive pattern: each term is sum of previous two
3. Examines the Sierpinski triangle—sees triangular deletion
4. Recognizes: both relate to self-similarity
5. Discovers: Fibonacci → golden ratio; Sierpinski → dimension 1.585
6. Understands: addition and deletion both produce fractal structure
7. Exits to explore Julia sets

## Design Intent
The map pairs **additive and subtractive** paths to complexity. Fibonacci numbers grow by addition, approaching the golden ratio (φ ≈ 1.618). Sierpinski triangles shrink by deletion, producing dimension log(3)/log(2) ≈ 1.585. Both are self-referential, both appear in nature.
