Concept: The primitives concept map made walkable. `tools/mindmap_to_map.py` takes the 2D mind-map arrangement of the 21 primitive concepts (Point through Other primitives) and projects it onto a floor — the diagram's layout becomes the level's layout, and the diagram's reading order becomes the spawn-to-teleporter walk. Distance on the map is a projection across channels (concept distance, spatial distance, walk order), so the level you stand in is a measurable image of how the vocabulary hangs together.
Sequence role: A meta-instrument, not a curriculum step. Undeclared in any sequence by design — it sits beside the Primitives spine as the projector's output: the whole 21-concept neighbourhood compressed into one walkable view. Spawn at `Point` (the curriculum's true origin), teleporter at `Other primitives` (the taxonomy's open edge). One representative artifact per concept keeps the map legible at 737 placed objects.
Technical angle: Generated map_data.json (3 layers) from the concept map via mindmap_to_map.py with weighting w=0.00 (pure concept-graph layout, no spatial smoothing). 737 artifacts, all reachable, single teleporter — pathfinder-clean. The placed-channel loop (placed_channel.py) measures the built level back against the source diagram, closing the 2D→3D→2D feedback so the arrangement can be scored, not just rendered.
Critical angle: A taxonomy is usually a list pretending to be neutral. Laying it out as a space exposes its choices — what sits next to what, what is central, what is exiled to "Other." Walking the map turns classification into terrain: the learner doesn't read the order, they enact it, and the gaps between concepts become literal distances to cross. The map argues that the primitives are a neighbourhood, not an index.
Key artifacts: One representative artifact per concept node — `Point` anchors the spawn; the solid family (Cube, Sphere, Cylinder, Torus, Platonic) forms the dense centre; `Assembly / SDF / boolean` and `Interactive tool` mark where primitives stop being given and start being made; `Other primitives` holds the teleporter and the unclassified remainder.

Gap: Generated meta-map — concepts are represented by a single heuristic-picked artifact each, so the choice of representative is only as good as the concept-map scorer. No subtitle truth-beats embedded yet; the walk currently teaches by adjacency alone.
