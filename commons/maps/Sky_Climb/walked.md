## The cast

calder_mobile · calder_mobile_primaries · calder_object_mobile · force_pad · return_launcher

## The walk

You arrive from below, and the first forces you meet are the ones already at peace: three Calder mobiles hanging overhead — the painted primaries, the abstract one, and the one hung with the project's own objects, point and sphere and arch swaying as counterweights. Every arm obeys τ = w·d, but nothing about them looks like an equation; they look like things that have settled their argument with gravity. The force_pad throws you upward — your first acceleration, felt in the body before it is named — and the return_launcher brings you back for another pass. Climbing here is the lesson: weight pulls, the pad pushes, and between the two you travel.

## What it opens

The chapter's truth arrives as sensation before notation: *acceleration is the only thing you feel.* The mobiles say the other half quietly — that balance is not the absence of force but forces agreeing, for now. Everything after this room is the unpacking of that agreement.
