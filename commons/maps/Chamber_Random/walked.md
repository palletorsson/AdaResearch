## The cast

becoming_catalyst · catalyst_target · proximity_spawner · science_screen

## The walk

A small room that does almost nothing until you move. The proximity_spawner waits — creation triggered by approach, the room's population an event of your walking. The becoming_catalyst rests on its pedestal with the catalyst_target nearby: the bracelet that lets a walker place and remove blocks, chance's opposite handed to you at the end of chance's chapter.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to harvest**. The whole thread taught that real entropy cannot be computed, only gathered from unrepeatable events — and in this room *you* are the unrepeatable event: your path, your timing, your approach are the seed the spawner draws from. The catalyst completes the thought: after seven rooms of the dice acting on the world, the walker becomes the source the room samples. Entropy is freedom; the chamber plants it in the person.
