# BG Crack Propagation

Stress accumulates. Material holds — until it doesn't. A crack begins where the structure is weakest, then propagates along grain and stress gradient, each fracture point redistributing force to its neighbors. The cellular automaton doesn't decide where to break. It reads local tension and yields.

The simulation runs on a wall-mounted display in a compact gallery. Watch fracture networks branch and fork — not random, not planned. Each crack follows the path of least resistance through the material's own memory. Grain orientation, stress concentration, boundary conditions — the material's history written in its failure patterns.

Destruction as information. Every fracture line is a map of forces the structure absorbed before it couldn't. Cracks don't break rules — they reveal where the rules were already breaking.
