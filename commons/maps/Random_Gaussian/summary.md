# Random Gaussian

Pause new draws, let flight finish, then compare GAUSS and UNIFORM at three batches of one hundred. The pale frames show ideal-model expected counts at the current N; the orange curve is a separately scaled shape. BINS regroups the same retained values and can change the binned mean and deviation. Clipping parks an out-of-range value at an edge, losing its overshoot. The seed makes the procedure repeatable. Carry the distinction between a value, its grouping and its consequences into Random Mushrooms.
