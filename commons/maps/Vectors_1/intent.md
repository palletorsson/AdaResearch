Technical angle: a + b = (a.x+b.x, a.y+b.y, a.z+b.z), geometric parallelogram rule, tip-to-tail construction, commutativity (a+b = b+a). Addition is how forces combine into net force, how velocity accumulates displacement, how multiple influences produce a single result. The basis_vectors_rig decomposes the sum back into components.
Critical angle: Addition is superposition — the principle that effects combine linearly. This is the deepest assumption in Newtonian physics and the reason vector math works for forces at all. When superposition breaks (nonlinear dynamics, chaos), addition stops being sufficient. But for now, at the beginner level, linearity holds and addition is king.
