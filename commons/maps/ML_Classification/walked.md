## The cast

## The walk

The machine learns to divide the world. The enhanced_kmeans gathers scattered points into clusters nobody labelled — drawing groups out of pure proximity, deciding where one kind ends and another begins. The svm_visualization finds the single cleanest cut between two classes, the widest street separating them. The random_forest_visualization asks a crowd of small dumb decision trees and takes their vote, accuracy assembled from a mob of weak opinions.

## What it fixes

The walk is about the boundary, and boundaries are never innocent. Each of these algorithms carves a decision surface through data and then calls everything on one side *this* and everything on the other *that* — and the whole apparatus of consequential machine learning, from credit scores to medical triage, lives on exactly this line. The room shows the drawing as a craft (the widest margin, the honest vote) and lets the disquiet stand underneath: a classifier is a machine for producing categories, and categories, once a system enforces them, stop feeling like choices and start feeling like facts about the world. Learning to divide is learning to rule.
