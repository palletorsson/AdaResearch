# Classification Boundaries

Three algorithms. Three ways to carve space into regions where things belong. K-means drops centroids and iterates — points migrate toward their nearest center until the system settles, variance minimized, clusters stable. SVM finds the widest gap between classes, a hyperplane balanced on the few points that matter most. Random forest grows a hundred crooked trees, each one wrong in its own way, and lets them vote.

Walk the ridges between zones. The narrow passes are decision boundaries — the places where one category ends and another begins. Every classifier draws lines. K-means draws circles. SVM draws margins. Random forest draws jagged territories through majority rule.

The question is never whether to partition. The question is what violence the partition does to the points that live near the edge.
