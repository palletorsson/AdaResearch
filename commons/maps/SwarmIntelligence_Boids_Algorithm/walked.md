## The walk

The chapter's hero room, and the most famous three rules in generative art. The boid_flocking piece runs Craig Reynolds' 1986 commandments — separate from your crowded neighbors, align with their heading, steer toward their center — and out of nothing else a flock is born, turning as one body it does not know it has. The boids_aquarium and boids_2d_in_3d give the same law two enclosures; the boid_manager exposes the sliders, so you can weaken cohesion and watch the flock fray, or starve separation and watch it clump into a panicked knot.

## What it fixes

The walk fixes the chapter's astonishment into something you can operate. Flocking looks like a single intention and is provably the absence of one — every bird sees only its handful of neighbors, and the murmuration is what those local glances add up to. The slider room is the honest part: the emergent whole is *tunable*, three numbers deciding between a flock, a swarm, and a stampede. Coordination is not commanded here; it is parameterized, and the gap between those two words is the whole subject.
