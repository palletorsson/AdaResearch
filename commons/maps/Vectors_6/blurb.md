# Vectors: Steering and Attraction

Steering and attraction. Two exercises that close the loop between intention and physics.

Accelerate toward a point. Decelerate before arrival. The dark sphere pulses at the center — not a target but a field source, warping the space around it with inverse-square force. Get close and the pull strengthens. Pull away and it fades. Distance squared sits in the denominator, which means proximity is nonlinear. Twice as close, four times the force. The relationship between bodies is never proportional to the gap between them.

Attraction magnitude is not a property of the object. It emerges from relation — mass, distance, direction. The vector that pulls you toward something is computed fresh every frame. Desire as dynamics: not a fixed state but a continuous recalculation of where you are relative to what moves you.
