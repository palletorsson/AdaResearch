Concept: Steering behavior and attraction — closing the loop between intention and physics, where desired-minus-current produces a steering force and inverse-square law governs gravitational pull.
Sequence role: Tenth map in Forces; the final exercise map before VectorWorkbench consolidates everything. Steering introduces agency (an object that wants to go somewhere) and attraction introduces the force law that governs planets and particles. These are the two patterns that recur through every Forces sub-sequence map; follows Vectors_5.
Technical angle: steering = desired_velocity - current_velocity as a force, attraction magnitude ∝ 1/r² (inverse square law), acceleration/deceleration profiles. The exercise artifacts provide hands-on practice: accelerate toward a point, decelerate on approach, respond to attraction at varying distances.
Critical angle: Steering reveals that forces can encode intention — not just physical law but designed behavior. Game AI, flocking, path-following all reduce to steering forces. Attraction reveals the simplest force law that produces complex orbits. Together they show that the same vector math serves both simulation and design.
Key artifacts: exercise_1_5 (accelerate/decelerate) demonstrates steering with approach behavior; exercise_1_8 (attraction magnitude) demonstrates inverse-square pull; dark_sphere acts as the attractor/target at the center.
Gap: A steering_vs_attraction side-by-side showing how steering produces smooth approach while raw attraction produces oscillation — making the difference between designed behavior and physical law visible.
