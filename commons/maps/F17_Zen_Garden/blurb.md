Scattered elements at odd intervals. A raised block here, a tall column there — nothing centered, nothing symmetrical. The room has no focal point. Your eye wanders, rests, wanders again.

Asymmetry done well is harder than symmetry. The zen garden uses deliberate imbalance to create contemplative tension — each element placed to hold the composition without resolving it. The mind searches for a pattern and finds only relationships. Stillness through incompleteness.
