> ghost-drafted from the working map (seed slot, forces thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

becoming_catalyst · catalyst_target · science_screen · proximity_spawner

## The walk

The catalyst chamber that closes the chapter. The becoming_catalyst waits on its pedestal; the targets stand ready to take whatever mode it grants; the science_screen flattens the room's state into a readable panel. Nothing here lectures. The chamber's rule is that the chapter's content becomes an *ability*: force is no longer something demonstrated at you but something you carry out of the room. The proximity_spawner keeps the population honest — bodies appear because you came close, consequence answering presence.

## The seed

What germinates here is the chapter's verb. Every earlier room conjugated force in the third person — it pulls, it resists, it balances. The chamber hands it over in the first person, and the sequence ends the way all of them try to: with the lesson installed in the hand, not the wall. What exact power the catalyst grants in the forces register is a design decision still open on the bench — the chamber states the form of the gift before its content.
