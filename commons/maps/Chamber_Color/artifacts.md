# Chamber Colour — artifacts

`becoming_catalyst` is primary: its mounted firing bench is the book's encounter. `catalyst_target` is the secondary receiver, configured as a framed cube that disappears after three hits and returns. The experiment compares target tint with nearby grid colour and visual transforms, while grid collision remains unchanged.

`proximity_spawner` and `science_screen` remain secondary. Their presence does not establish a colour-dependent friend transition; that requires its own receiving-code and runtime investigation.
