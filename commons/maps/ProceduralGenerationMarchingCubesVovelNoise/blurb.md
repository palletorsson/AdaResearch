Sample noise in three dimensions. Each point in the voxel grid holds a density value — not solid or empty, but a gradient. A threshold decides what counts as surface. Marching cubes walks the grid, eight corners at a time, and interpolates where the boundary falls. 256 possible configurations per cube. The mesh emerges from local decisions about inside and outside.

The dark sphere pulses at the center — a catalyst station where the field becomes visible. Noise generates the terrain; the algorithm extracts the skin between presence and absence. No modeling, no sculpting. Geometry precipitates from a field of probabilities, the way frost crystallizes on glass.

Every surface is an argument about where something stops being itself.
