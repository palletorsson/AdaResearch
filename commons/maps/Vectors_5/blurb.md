# Advanced Vector Fields

Torque is the cross product with leverage. A force applied at a distance from a pivot — the farther out, the more twist. Every wrench, every spinning top, every planet turning on its axis: r × F. The vector points perpendicular to the plane of rotation. Direction encodes the axis. Magnitude encodes the force of becoming.

Vector fields assign a vector to every point in space. Step into the field and read the flow — not a single arrow but a continuous landscape of direction. Particles stream through, tracing fieldlines that curl, diverge, converge. No trajectory is planned. Each agent reads only its local instruction and moves.

Torque rotates. Fields direct. Together they describe a space that doesn't just exist — it acts. The room has an opinion about which way you should spin.
