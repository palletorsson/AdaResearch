# NonEuclidean_Spaces — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (primitive slot, foundationscrisis thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

curvature_slider · hyperbolic_surface · elliptic_surface · poincare_disk · riemann_sphere

## The walk

The promise breaks, and nothing collapses — that is the shock. The curvature_slider bends space itself: negative, and the hyperbolic_surface blooms with room to spare, parallels diverging like estranged friends; positive, and the elliptic_surface closes, parallels meeting like conspirators. The poincare_disk holds infinity inside a circle; the riemann_sphere wraps the plane onto a ball. Every one of these worlds is consistent. None of them is Euclid's.

## What it teaches

The primitive of the crisis: an axiom is a *choice*. Deny the fifth postulate and geometry does not die — it multiplies. The room teaches the century's hardest lesson gently, with a slider: consistency was never the same thing as truth, and the word "the" in "the geometry of space" was doing more work than anyone had admitted.
