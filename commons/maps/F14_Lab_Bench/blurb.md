A long raised surface runs the width of the room, specimens placed at regular intervals. Platonic solids, a cube, a color study, a rainbow — arranged for examination, not display. The bench is at working height. This is where you lean in.

The lab bench is intimate architecture. It brings objects close and arranges them in a row for serial comparison. Not a pedestal's reverence, not a gallery's distance — the bench says: pick it up, turn it over, look again. Knowledge begins at arm's length.
