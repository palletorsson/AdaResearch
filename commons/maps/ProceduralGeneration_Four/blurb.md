A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 region at its center. Algorithms made visible — not as abstract logic but as spatial events unfolding in real time.

Fibonacci sequences spiral through the arena. Each number the sum of its two predecessors — a rule so simple it generates sunflower heads, branching patterns, the golden ratio. The sequence doesn't know what it will become. It only knows what came before.

Watch the 8×8 region populate. Randomness and structure negotiate on the same surface. Some processes converge toward pattern. Others resist it. The grid doesn't care which. It holds both — order accumulating from addition, chaos from the gaps between predictions. Every algorithm is a bet that the next step follows from the last. Fibonacci keeps winning that bet, and the winnings look like nature.
