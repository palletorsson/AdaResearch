## The cast

line_network_ca · grid_substrate_runner · persian_rug · dark_sphere

## The walk

You arrive and the floor is already thinking. The grid_substrate_runner plays rule-patterns through the map's own tiles, so the room's ground is the first automaton you meet — not an exhibit about cells, but cells underfoot. The line_network_ca stands history upright: one-dimensional generations stacked into strata, time as geology. And the persian_rug makes the chapter's oldest argument without a word — weavers found these patterns centuries before the mathematics did, neighborhood rules knotted by hand.

## What it opens

The threshold installs the chapter's whole grammar in one glance: a cell, its neighbors, a rule, and the shock that this is enough. Worlds from almost nothing. The rest of the sequence only turns up the *almost*.
