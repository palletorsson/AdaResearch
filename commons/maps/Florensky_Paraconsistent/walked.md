> ghost-drafted from the working map (world slot, foundationscrisis thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

florensky_sphere · both_true_gate · schrodinger_box · superposition_display

## The walk

The world room asks what living inside contradiction actually looks like, and answers with a priest and a physicist. The florensky_sphere carries Pavel Florensky's antinomy — truth held as *both at once*, a sphere whose inside and outside refuse to settle — from a man who wrote logic in a labor camp and meant it. The both_true_gate is the room's threshold made literal: it opens only for P *and* not-P together, a door classical logic says cannot exist, standing there existing. The schrodinger_box and superposition_display bring the physics: a state that is genuinely both until asked, contradiction not as failure but as the world's resting condition.

## The world

Paraconsistent logic is the crisis grown up: instead of one contradiction exploding the whole system (ex falso quodlibet — from falsehood, everything), these logics contain the fire and keep reasoning. Theology and quantum mechanics arrived at it independently, which is the room's quiet argument: the demand that everything be one thing or the other was never a law of thought — it was a habit. Some truths come in pairs.
