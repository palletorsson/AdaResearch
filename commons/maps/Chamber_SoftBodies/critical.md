# Soft contact is negotiation — the spring hopper, the absent catalyst, and the politics of yielding

Chamber_SoftBodies removes the projection catalyst entirely. No projectile is fired; no hit is registered; no score accumulates. The interaction is physical push, and the push's effect is a travelling wave of displacement through a mass-spring body. The chamber's argument is that when both sides of a contact are soft, the boundary between actor and receiver blurs, and the blur is not a limitation but the contact's actual mode.

Catriona Mortimer-Sandilands writes about what she calls queer ecology — the argument that the rigidity of categories like nature and culture, native and invasive, pure and compromised, is itself a political construction, and that letting the categories soften is not a loss but an opening. The spring_hopper stages the argument at the scale of a single body. Its rigidity is tunable. At high stiffness, it rebounds sharply; at low stiffness, it deforms deeply and rebounds slowly. The chamber lets the learner move through these regimes by varying their push.

The spring_hopper is built from a mass-spring lattice. Every push produces a local deformation that propagates through the whole body. The learner walks up to the hopper and pushes; the contact region compresses; the surrounding springs pull on their neighbours; the deformation travels through the body as a slow wave. The hopper rebounds softly, redistributing the force across its structure. No single frame names a point of impact; the whole body is participating at once.

A science screen on the wall renders the contact as a field rather than as a scatter. Displacement is drawn as a continuous surface in space, with colour tracking magnitude. A second display traces the energy of the system over time: incoming impulse, stored spring potential, damping loss, restitution. The traces are the contact's biography, and the biography is a distributed phenomenon rather than a punctual event.

Mortimer-Sandilands's queer ecology lands on the absence of a hit moment. A rigid-body collision has a hit moment: the frame at which impact occurs and damage is registered. A soft-body contact does not. The collision is continuous. Energy enters, propagates, dissipates; the process takes time; nothing is cleanly lost or gained. The soft-body contact has no hit moment because the contact is an ongoing redistribution rather than a discrete event, and the ongoingness is what makes it queer in the sense Mortimer-Sandilands uses.

The chamber's empty catalyst slot is itself part of the teaching. A learner accustomed to catalyst chambers reaches for the projection tool and finds nothing. The absence is deliberate: the chamber argues that catalyst practice can be a practice of contact rather than of emission, and the argument lands most cleanly when the projection option is not available at all.

The politics of soft-body contact are in the refusal of the rigid collision as paradigm. A rigid collision treats each body as a self-contained object with boundaries that meet and separate. A soft collision treats the bodies as mutually penetrating elastic media whose distinction is always temporary. The shift has consequences beyond physics simulation: it suggests that collision-as-paradigm — the idea that encounters are discrete events between separate parties — is a simplification that softness reveals as incomplete.

The spring_hopper's recovery curve is the chamber's closing argument. After each push, the hopper oscillates slowly back to its rest shape. The rest shape is not a ground truth; it is the configuration the springs produce at equilibrium, and different spring constants produce different rest shapes. The chamber's rest shape is a convention the hopper's designer chose, and the hopper's recovery is a recovery toward that chosen convention rather than toward an external standard of wholeness.

Within the sequence, Chamber_SoftBodies closes the Soft Bodies sequence by converting rigidity from an assumption into a design choice. The chamber hands the learner back to the Lab with no catalyst added to their kit, because the catalyst was never the point. The point was the felt experience of contact as negotiation, and the experience persists after the chamber does.
