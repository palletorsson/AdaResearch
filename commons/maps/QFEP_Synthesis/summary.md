# QFEP Synthesis - Summary

## Overview
The eighth and final QFEP Laboratory map closes the sequence with integration rather than novelty. All four QFEP terms return as grabbable spheres — F, E, lambda, phi — arranged symmetrically around the complete formula floating at scale 2.0 on the central platform. The queer_morphology_specimen, the sequence's thesis artifact, demonstrates a living form at the edge of chaos. The teleporter returns the learner to a transformed lab.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 12x12 grid with terraced elevation
- **Architecture**: Height 1 walkways, height 2 walls, height 3 central platform (4x4 block); exit gap at south (height 0)
- **Symmetry**: F and E spheres in the upper pair, lambda and phi in the lower pair, formula at center

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **grab_sphere_F** (2,2) and **grab_sphere_E** (8,2) — Structure and entropy terms, scale 0.4
- **grab_sphere_lambda** (2,8) and **grab_sphere_phi** (8,8) — Balance and disposition terms, scale 0.4
- **qfep_formula_3d** (5,5) — Complete equation at scale 2.0, large enough to inhabit
- **queer_morphology_specimen** (5,9) — Living form: vertices wander under noise (amplitude 0.15) while coherence restoration (strength 0.8) maintains topology; the edge of chaos embodied in a mesh

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at (1,1), **elevator** at (5,5), **teleporter** "Return to Lab" leading to post_qfeplaboratory hub variant; secondary exits for synthesis and application paths

## Learning Sequence
1. Enter the neutral-lit space — no colored regime, no environmental rhetoric
2. Revisit the four grab spheres — same objects from the Introduction, now familiar
3. Ascend to the formula at center — the same equation, now understood from eight maps of embodied experience
4. Encounter the queer_morphology_specimen — a mesh that breathes, deforms, and maintains coherence without freezing
5. Recognize: the formula describes the specimen, the specimen embodies the formula
6. Return through the teleporter to a lab that has changed because the learner has

## Design Intent
The Synthesis introduces nothing new. Every artifact appeared in earlier maps. The absence of novelty is the content: synthesis is recognition, not discovery. The neutral lighting trusts the learner to interpret without environmental cues. The queer_morphology_specimen is the curriculum's thesis in one object — adaptive systems at the edge of chaos with positive phi. The sequence ends with companionship, not mastery.
