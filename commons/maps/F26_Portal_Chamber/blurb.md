Walls three levels high enclose the room completely. One narrow gap in the south wall is the only way in. Inside, a crystal ball floats at the center, waiting. Crossing the threshold feels deliberate — you chose to enter.

The portal chamber makes entry an event. A single dramatic opening transforms passage from movement into decision. The room beyond is enclosed, private, set apart. Every temple, vault, and sanctuary uses this geometry: constrict the entrance to amplify the arrival. The threshold is the architecture.
