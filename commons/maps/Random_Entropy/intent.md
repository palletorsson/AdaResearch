The room's question: which differences disappear when a sequence becomes one number?
Sequence role: Active hall 2 in randomness, after Random_Definition (a repeatable arrangement from a seed) and before Random_Remove (the eligible set made spatial). Technical angle: `shannon_entropy_meter` draws `sequence_length` = 200 symbols over `num_symbols` = 10 from a seeded local generator, tallies them and computes H = −Σ p log₂ p over the counts, zero-count terms omitted, against the ceiling log₂(10). Critical angle: the gauge is an instrument whose authority is the account it gives of its own reading — the disclosure ladder makes that visible rung by rung — and its number is a decision about what counts as the same: two sequences with the same tallies are the same to it. The room does not claim that the meter tells true randomness from pseudo-randomness (the secondaries stand beside it as examples of harvested and grown randomness, not as evidence); it claims only what the loop computes.
