# Two entropy halls, two kinds of work

Decision, 16 October 2026: keep both maps, condense repeated explanation, and give each a distinct task. Stable map IDs and sequence order remain:

**Random Definition → Entropy Types (`Random_Entropies`) → Entropy Workshop (`Random_Entropy`) → Random Remove.**

| | Entropy Types | Entropy Workshop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guiding question | Which question does this entropy answer? | What can change that this reading does not register? |
| Encounter | Prepared versions, visible together | Change a condition, compare, preserve and reconstruct |
| Text | Situate the object; introduce the necessary distinction briefly | Follow a consequence, a failed prediction or an inadequate account |
| Tutorial | Build one small calculation and its visible comparison | Change an apparatus and examine its limits |
| Completion evidence | A visitor can name the outcome and condition behind a reading | An experiment produces an observable difference, useful null result or a reason to change the apparatus |

The transition is: **“We can now name the measurement. What can happen that it fails to register?”**

## Distribution of the collection

The seven ordinary-object comparisons stay in Types. The full-size chair on its entrance wall establishes the common specimen. The final comparison now keeps one colour question across three equal-ball inventories, avoiding the earlier changes between identity, colour and weighted tickets. Neither the jar bank nor the prefab laboratory is added to this hall.

The Workshop keeps the ledger, live jar, two-compartment apparatus and prefab investigation. Four glass columns near its entrance begin with different arrangements. Initially, lavender-core/coral-shell leaves colour uncertain under a height record and predictable under a core/rim record. After an eight-second inspection pause, all four continue mixing under the same bounded random-walk rule. PAUSE holds an observation; RESTART restores the starts and their seeded paths. The columns are four independent evolving preparations, not four successive frames. The hand-sized live jar permits a separate shaking intervention. Neither model calculates heat exchange.

The prefab bench remains here with its seed changes, KEEP, gallery and replay. Separation and recombination can produce a meaningful hybrid without producing a thermodynamic entropy value. This is a productive boundary for the research. Source mesh parts share a computational representation; that does not make their original objects physically the same material or their hybrids functional mechanisms.

Not every optional artifact needs a paragraph in the final. The existing workshop route remains available; a reader or visitor can choose one research branch. Condensation should remove duplicate explanation before removing an apparatus that enables a different action.

## The next trial

1. In Types, choose the best colour guess in the three trays before reading the bits. Explain which chances were counted.
2. Enter the Workshop carrying one question. At the jar bank, predict which observation distinguishes the core/shell arrangement.
3. Change one condition in the live jar, ledger, compartments or prefab bench. Name what was held fixed. Keep a receipt where that apparatus supports it; otherwise record parameters, observations and source revision explicitly.
4. Reconstruct the result independently and propose a further change. A simulation's repeatability is not evidence of fresh physical randomness.
5. Record whether the second hall demanded a new action or merely repeated the first. If no new question arose, shorten or move that explanation. If an optional artifact creates a stronger next question, promote it on evidence.

The automated checks establish calculations, scene loading and clear approaches. They do not establish understanding. Human reading, VR comfort and independent reconstruction remain open. The 48-value seed bench remains the first research pilot.
