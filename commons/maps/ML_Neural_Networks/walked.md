## The walk

The chapter builds its central instrument and lets you walk inside it. The neural_networks_vr hangs the network in space — weights as visible thicknesses, activations flaring as signal passes, and backpropagation running the error backward through every layer like a correction travelling upstream to its source. The neural_network_visualization gives the diagram; the learn_world_stacked piles model upon model, each layer learning from the one below, until depth itself becomes the method.

## What it fixes

The second walk fixes what a neural network actually is, against everything the word "neural" suggests. There is no brain here, no understanding, no homunculus reading the picture — only a very deep landscape of numbers that adjusts itself by measuring how wrong it just was and nudging every weight a little downhill. Backpropagation is gradient descent from the primitive room, run through a million dials at once. The astonishment the chapter wants you to hold is that this suffices: stack enough blind adjustment deep enough, feed it enough of the world, and competence emerges that no one designed and no layer comprehends. The stack knows; nothing in it does.
