# Museum AAA Origin — Critical reflection

## The core tension

An origin looks natural only after its choice has been forgotten. Coordinate systems turn a negotiated reference into an apparently neutral centre.

## The body and zero

Walking around the pedestal reveals that the origin does not move, but the visitor’s relation to it does. The empty orbit is part of the lesson: position is always position-from.

## QFEP connection

The origin is maximal formal order, yet the cycling names and visible beam keep contingency present. The room does not abolish zero; it prevents zero from pretending it arrived without an institution.
