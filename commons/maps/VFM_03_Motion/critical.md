# A boundary can recognise one body

The first anomaly is a command that has ended while its effect keeps crossing the room. COAST clears a request; BRAKE spends time changing a velocity. RESET alone has permission to erase that state immediately. Ask the visitor to compare those operations before treating them as synonyms for stopping.

This is an inquiry into which bodies a model permits. The cube and visitor occupy one visible room, but an amber perimeter reverses only the cube. A collision mask decides which encounters exist. Height and rotation locks keep this body in a horizontal model. Mass changes its response without changing its silhouette. The engine's exclusions are inspectable construction choices.

The claim is methodological: simulation lets us vary a rule and observe the forms of movement it permits. The mask is not evidence that human difference is reducible to a bit field. It gives a concrete place to ask who or what an arrangement recognises, and what another arrangement might make possible. The visitor can cross the line precisely because this apparatus has assigned the two bodies different relations to it.

The trace gives another limit. It retains 120 samples while velocity carries the current result of earlier influences. Once old samples leave the drawing, the stored state is insufficient to reconstruct every event that produced it. The chapter can make that loss discoverable without claiming that the body remembers its full past.

The ramp offers a sharper cut. Continuous changes in angle or friction cross a conditional, permitting or withholding sliding. Yet the STATIC branch also zeros existing velocity immediately. That is a programmed abbreviation, exposed beside a chamber that brakes through force over time. We can value the threshold demonstration and still ask for a richer account of the transition.

No entropy measure is computed here. The concrete questions are about retained state, eligibility for contact, temporal resolution and the rules granting a body movement. These are useful footholds for the later investigation of fields and possible forms of existence.
