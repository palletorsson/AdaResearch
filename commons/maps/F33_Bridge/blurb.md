Green translucent grids span the gaps between solid platforms. Below them, nothing — void, open air, the drop. The bridge is visible, walkable, and see-through. You trust it with your weight while watching the emptiness beneath your feet.

The bridge is a span over absence. It connects two solid places by passing through an unsupported one. Walking a bridge is an act of faith in structure — the material holds, the gap does not swallow you, the other side is reachable. To bridge is to make a path where there is no ground.
