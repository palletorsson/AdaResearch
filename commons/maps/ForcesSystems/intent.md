Concept: ForcesSystems arranges four force systems in a clockwise circuit of islands: vector fields and attractors on one, force field superposition on another, springs and oscillators on the third, particle systems and emergent behavior on the fourth. Jump pads connect the loop, and each island shows a different way that forces organize matter over time — attraction creating orbits, fields creating flow, springs creating oscillation, and particles creating statistical patterns from individual randomness.

Sequence role: Map 7 of 9 in Vectors & Forces: From Direction to Dynamics. The learner arrives from ForcesComposition carrying superposition, work-energy, and momentum. This map widens the lens from single forces to force systems — collections of forces that create qualitatively different behaviors depending on their mathematical structure. Attraction produces orbits, springs produce oscillation, fields produce flow, particles produce emergence. It feeds into ForcesChaos (n-body dynamics and strange attractors), where these systems interact to produce unpredictable behavior. In QFEP terms, this is the exploration phase: the learner encounters multiple instances of temporal evolution (different force laws producing different dynamics) and begins to see that the specific form of F determines the character of the evolution.

Technical angle: vector_fields and force_field_visualizer render 3D vector fields as arrow glyphs with direction and magnitude at grid points, using color coding for field strength. force_fields implements superposition of gravitational, electric, and magnetic fields with configurable parameters and scale. particle_systems launches many independent bodies under shared force rules, demonstrating how statistical patterns emerge from individual stochastic trajectories. mass_spring_damper implements the damped harmonic oscillator: F = -kx - bv, where k is spring stiffness, b is damping coefficient, and the system oscillates with exponentially decaying amplitude. spring_system extends this to coupled springs, showing how energy transfers between connected oscillators. firework_launcher combines particle systems with timed forces — an upward launch impulse, gravity, drag, and an explosion impulse at peak height. example_3_2_forces_with_arbitrary_angular_motion_vr demonstrates rotational dynamics under non-centroidal forces. example_3_3_pointing_in_the_direction_of_motion_vr implements velocity-aligned orientation — the object always faces where it is going, using the velocity vector to set rotation.

Critical angle: Each island demonstrates a different category of force law, and the resulting dynamics differ qualitatively — not just quantitatively. Inverse-square attraction (gravity) produces closed orbits and escape trajectories. Linear restoring force (springs) produces oscillation with a natural frequency. Superposed fields produce flow patterns that a particle follows without memory or plan. Stochastic forces on many particles produce statistical regularities (bell curves, diffusion) from individual randomness. The QFEP insight here is that the form of the force function F determines the topology of possible behaviors, not just their speed or direction. The sequence truth — "force is what changes states" — gets its fullest expression: different force laws create different categories of change. Springs oscillate because F = -kx always pulls back toward center. Gravity orbits because F falls off as 1/r^2 and the tangential velocity prevents collapse. Particles diffuse because each force impulse is random and independent. The firework launcher synthesizes all of these — launch force, gravity, drag, explosion — into a single object that transitions through multiple dynamic regimes in seconds. The pointing-in-direction-of-motion artifact quietly demonstrates that velocity is not just a number but a direction, tying back to the foundational vector truth that direction and magnitude are inseparable.

Key artifacts: vector_fields renders 3D force fields as arrow glyphs showing direction and magnitude at every grid point. force_field_visualizer adds interactive color-coded field strength overlays. force_fields implements configurable superposition of multiple field types. particle_systems launches many bodies under shared forces to demonstrate statistical emergence. mass_spring_damper implements F = -kx - bv (damped harmonic oscillation) with tunable stiffness and damping. spring_system extends oscillation to coupled springs showing energy transfer. firework_launcher combines launch impulse, gravity, drag, and explosion into a single trajectory that transitions through multiple force regimes. example_3_2_forces_with_arbitrary_angular_motion_vr demonstrates rotational dynamics under non-centroidal forces. example_3_3_pointing_in_the_direction_of_motion_vr orients objects along their velocity vector. VectorBasics and basis_vectors_rig anchor the spatial frame. catalyst_target and proximity_spawner provide combat context.

Gap: The map lacks a resonance demonstration showing how a driven oscillator's amplitude peaks when the driving frequency matches the natural frequency — this would connect springs to wave phenomena and foreshadow the wave function sequence. A phase portrait tool plotting position vs. velocity for the spring system would make the state-space topology visible (circles for undamped, spirals for damped). The particle_systems artifact would benefit from a histogram overlay showing the real-time distribution of particle positions, making the statistical emergence quantitatively visible rather than just visually apparent.
