## The walk

The field learns to move, and then to obey. The fountain_demo re-extracts its surface every frame as the underlying function changes — geometry flowing like water because it *is* recomputation, not motion; nothing travels, everything is re-decided. The mc_sculpt_vr hands the field to you: your hand adds and subtracts density, and the skin follows as a consequence. Digital clay — but the truth of the piece lives in the numbers underneath, and the visible surface is only their running summary.

## What it fixes

The second walk closes the loop between the chapter's two halves: fields are not just found (noise, equations) — they are *authored*, continuously, by hands and by time. Sculpting by field-editing means you never touch the mesh at all; you argue with the data and the boundary redraws itself. It is the chapter's most intimate version of its truth: change what inside means, and the shape of the world follows.
