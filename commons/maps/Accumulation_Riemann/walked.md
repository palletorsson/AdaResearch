## The walk

The turn is a machine hall. The riemann_pump does integration the honest industrial way — slice, stack, sum, repeat — rectangles marching under a curve until the error is thinner than the eye. Beside it, riemann_pi runs the same discipline at a legend: the circle's area approached slice by slice, π earned the slow way rather than quoted. The integral_area from last room stands as the smooth ideal the rectangles are reaching for.

## The turn

Here the chapter admits its method: the exact is approached, not possessed. Every integral the project will ever take is a promise about what infinitely many rectangles *would* do. That the promise works — that the stack converges on truth — is the quiet miracle the pump performs on a loop, and the first place a walker might ask who audits the promise. (The room's two bodies overlap by a hair; whether the pump and its π sibling should touch is a bench call.)
