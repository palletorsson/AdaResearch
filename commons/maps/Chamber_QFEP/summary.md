# Chamber QFEP — Summary

Chamber_QFEP is the culminating catalyst chamber of the QFEP Laboratory sequence. Every creature the learner has befriended across the curriculum is present — the miura, the kaleidocycle, the hydra, the branching vine, the swarm hive, and the rest — and every catalyst mode the learner has collected is available at once.

At the centre of the chamber stands the qfep_calibrator, a creature that does not respond to any single catalyst projection. It reads all catalyst modes simultaneously and responds to their coherence rather than to any one of them. Firing one mode at a time produces no reaction; firing several modes in a deliberate combination makes the calibrator hum, rise, and extend toward the learner.

The science screen on the wall renders the composite as a single wave. The waveform is the learner's current combination of catalyst modes, collapsed into one readout. The QFEP formula — QFE = F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t) — is spelled out across the top of the screen, and each term lights up in turn as the learner activates the catalyst mode associated with it. The formula stops being studied term-by-term and becomes something the learner operates.

Within the sequence, Chamber_QFEP is the close. The completion the chamber rewards is not defeating a final creature; it is recognising that every creature was already on the learner's side, and that the formula is a mode of relationship rather than a tool.
