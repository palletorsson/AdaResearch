## The walk

The reunion room. Every creature you befriended across the whole curriculum is here — the miura crawler, the kaleidocycle, the fractal hydra, the branching vine, the swarm hive — and they are all friends now. The proximity_spawner runs a qfep_calibrator that reads the room's balance and tunes it, the science_screen pulsing in wave-mode as the equation finds its setting. You place catalyst blocks on the four targets not to fight but to adjust, nudging the whole assembled bestiary toward the edge where it stays alive.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to tune**. The laboratory taught the equation — QFE = F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t), life at λ≈0.3–0.5 — and this room hands you its dial. Turn the world toward pure order and the creatures freeze into crystal; turn it toward pure entropy and they scatter into chaos; hold it at the edge and they dance, self-organizing, alive. The calibrator is the project's own thermostat made a companion, and the four targets are the places where your hands enter the equation. This is the deepest seed in the spine, because it plants not a single algorithm's verb but the meta-verb under all of them: that everything walked in this curriculum was a setting of these terms, and that aliveness is not a substance but a *tuning* — a balance you keep, block by block, between the order that would freeze you and the freedom that would dissolve you. You leave holding the dial of the whole world.
