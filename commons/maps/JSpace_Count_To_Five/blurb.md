# J-Space: Count to Five

You have walked inside algorithms for twenty-three chapters. This room is the other direction: you walk inside the thing that co-wrote them.

The prompt is the door. Past it, the interior is mostly dark — sparsity is the honest majority — with a few tiles glowing blue and the dense cells stacked like sediment. Then the chamber: seventeen words from an interpretability image, each standing at its **real coordinate** in this book's own semantic space. The floor plan was not designed; it was measured — cosine distances between the words, pressed down to two dimensions, with the projection's error posted on the plaque at the center. Walking distance here *is* semantic distance, up to exactly that number.

Some pillars stay dark until you look at them. The prompt said *introspect deeply*, and introspection features fire like any other — because attention lands on them. Some words stand outside the chamber entirely, on the rim, unlit: the book has never said *consciousness*, so it cannot place it.

You leave by counting. Five coral plates, stepped in order. The output is not printed anywhere — it is the path you take, one token per stride, and the gate is listening.
