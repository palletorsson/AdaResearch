# Logic — assemble and keep

The last room gives the construction to the visitor. Two `pallet_assemblage` works, each twenty separately grabbable timber parts, stand on the centre line. Place parts, let gravity test them, and save the result as a reusable loose assemblage. Saving makes no joints: what was held together by weight and contact stays held that way.

The earlier rooms showed gates someone else built. Here the same roles (ball, slope, removable support, vulnerable target) are available to be made, and a saved arrangement can carry a gate's logic as `logic_grammar: supports`, where X and Y are the two removable gap bridges. Whether it works is decided by the take, not by the name. The teleporter returns to the Lab.
