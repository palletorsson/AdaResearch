## The walk

The turn is a single dial and a sudden event. The percolationnetwork_ca fills space with open and closed cells and lets you raise the fraction that are open — and for a long while nothing much happens, disconnected pockets forming and staying apart. Then, at one sharp value, a path snaps into being from one edge to the other, and the whole space is suddenly *connected*. Nudge the dial back below it and the world falls apart again.

## The turn

The turn is from accumulation to *threshold*. Every room before this grew structure gradually, more rule making more form; percolation breaks that expectation and teaches the chapter's harder physics — that connectivity is not gradual, it is a phase change. There is a critical density below which a world is rubble and above which it is a network, and the transition is instantaneous, a property of the whole that no local cell can see coming. Forest fires cross firebreaks, fluids break through porous rock, rumors saturate a crowd — all the same snap. Procedural generation stops being about making stuff here and becomes about the moment stuff becomes *linked*, which is the moment a pile becomes a place.
