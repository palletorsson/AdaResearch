Concept: Connectivity thresholds in random grids. Each cell is occupied with some probability, and at the critical threshold of approximately 59.27%, a spanning cluster suddenly forms — a connected path crosses the entire grid. Percolation is the physics of connectivity, the moment randomness becomes structure.
Sequence role: Third map in the Procedural Generation sequence (12th spine sequence, lambda_edge phase). Shifts from growth algorithms (Genetic_Evolution, Space_Colonization) to connectivity as a generative phenomenon. Advanced difficulty — the learner must grasp phase transitions, critical thresholds, and the relationship between local probability and global structure. Connects backward to the Cellular Automata sequence where grid-based computation was established, forward to PG_Branching_Growth where explicit growth algorithms return, and laterally to graph theory sequences where connectivity was studied as structure rather than emergence; follows PG_Space_Colonization.
Technical angle: Site percolation on a 2D grid — each cell is independently occupied with probability p. Clusters are connected components of occupied cells (using von Neumann or Moore neighborhood). At the critical probability p_c approximately 0.5927 for site percolation on a square lattice, the largest cluster undergoes a phase transition from finite to infinite (spanning). The percolationnetwork_ca artifact visualizes cluster formation, cluster size distribution, and the spanning-cluster detection. Finite-size scaling effects are visible in the grid resolution.
Critical angle: Percolation is the canonical example of a phase transition in discrete systems — a tiny change in probability near the critical threshold produces a qualitative change in global structure. This is emergence in its purest mathematical form: no rule tells the grid to connect; connectivity appears as a statistical consequence of density. The lambda_edge truth resonates: a world doesn't need an author, and at the right density, randomness alone creates pathways.
Key artifacts: percolationnetwork_ca implements site percolation with cluster detection, spanning-cluster identification, and probability-sweep visualization; dark_sphere anchors the research space.
Gap: Needs a probability slider with real-time cluster visualization that lets the learner sweep through the critical threshold and experience the phase transition interactively, with cluster-size distribution plotted alongside the grid.
