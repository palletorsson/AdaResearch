## The walk

The chapter opens on the smallest honest question: how fast, right now? The slope_tangent_demo holds a curve and lets a tangent line kiss it point by point — the whole of differential calculus is that kiss, repeated. The derivative_pair stands a function beside its rate like a parent beside a child with the same face; you learn to read one from the other. The code_display keeps the notation in the margin where it belongs at a threshold: present, not in charge.

## What it opens

*Things change* — but the room's real gift is the second sentence hiding inside the first: change has a size, and the size has a geometry. Once slope means speed, every hill in the project becomes a graph you can stand on.
