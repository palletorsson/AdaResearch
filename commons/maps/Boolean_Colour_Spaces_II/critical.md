# What remains makes a route

The core does not move and gives no instruction, yet its presence turns the walk. At the crossing, another direction exists before we choose it. Inclined walls need not imply an inclined floor. These differences let appearance, construction and bodily movement speak together without treating the most intricate room as the freest.

The material can make a hard boundary seem soft or molten. That discrepancy is part of the encounter. Where could such an ambiguity invite exploration, and where would it mislead someone who needed a reliable route? The question should stay with the actual opening and the body using it.
