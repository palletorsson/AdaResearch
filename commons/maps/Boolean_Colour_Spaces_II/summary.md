# Three further constructions

- Ring: a torus removed from a block leaves a core and two ways around it.
- Cross: intersecting cylindrical cuts meet beneath a diagonal upper opening and a sky shaft; side passages are traversable.
- Fold: three alternately tilted box cuts produce inclined planar walls and ceilings. A slit admits sky.

Each shares a level floor and the four finishes PEARL, AMBER, CHROME and CHALK. DRIFT changes only the shader phase.
