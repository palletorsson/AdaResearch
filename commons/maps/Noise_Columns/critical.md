# What can a column remain?

Recognizing a column brings an expectation of support. These columns carry that expectation into a scene where they hold nothing up. Their simulation concerns appearance and generated geometry. The discrepancy can be an encounter in its own right: what makes us read a body as architecture before asking what it can carry?

The earlier text imagined erosion, an entrance slider, terrain and reversible damage. Those are worthwhile possible projects, but they were not this implementation. Keeping the question of form's apparent permanence means grounding it in the actual moving shafts, their materials and the held comparison. We do not need an invented erosion system for a column to become strange.

The fixed reference is already twisted, ribbed and partly lowered. Its stability is a choice to hold a parameter, not evidence of an original body recovered beneath distortion. A reusable mesh recipe allows several states to be produced again. That reproducibility does not establish that deformation is invertible, nor that changing geometry measures entropy.

The comparison makes one difference easier to attend to by holding others similar. It also leaves things outside its frame. FREEZE has a local scope. Before review, accessory shaders continued to read the engine clock while the shaft clock stopped: a literal remainder of the claim that the column was frozen. The repaired control reaches those shaders. The independent orb still continues, and its continued activity makes the boundary of our attention perceptible.

MARBLE removes the shaft's veining, letting the silhouette be examined without that pattern. This is an analytical action, not a demand for an undecorated truth. The material can return. A surface may invite desire, patience or distraction as well as convey a measurement. Understanding its construction should enlarge those possibilities.

The orb also complicates the category of decoration. It was called a neutral anchor, although it rotates, pulses and spreads a ring beneath itself. Its quieter activity can become foreground when we let it. The synthesis stand carries the same family into a smaller, distributed body: six members and an empty centre. The family connects these works while leaving their different presences available.

A saved metric selected the hush/swarm variant. The plaque makes that choice visible, but a score cannot settle what a visitor will value. The categorical choice of orb versus swarm also qualifies the idea that a common computational substrate makes every transformation gradual. Some capabilities are continuous parameters; others select different constructions. Code gives us both, and transitions between those constructions would have to be designed.

This is a methodological connection to the project's QFEP inquiry: ask what kinds of body and relation a set of operations admits, which alternatives its defaults suppress, and what a change in those operations enables. No entropy estimate or universal threshold is measured here. We can carry the question forward through specific encounters before claiming a general answer.
