A room. Three cubes. An exit.

One cube rotates — constant angular velocity, eternal spin. One oscillates — sinusoidal displacement, back and forth along a single axis. The third combines both: θ = A·sin(ωt) applied to rotation, a pendulum that never decays. Same equation, three different mappings. Position, orientation, both at once.

This is the first question any simulation asks: what moves, and how? Before physics, before collision, before anything touches anything else — there is a body in space and a rule that governs it. The cube doesn't know it's performing trigonometry. It just exists according to its parameters.

Walk to the teleporter. The world continues on the other side. Every transition is a small death of the room you were just in.
