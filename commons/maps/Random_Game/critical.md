# What the floor lets you know

The mushroom bed permitted certain differences through its recipes. This room asks when a difference becomes available to the body that must act on it.

The crossing's next state is specified. Its next wait is sampled. During the current wait, the duration has already been recorded, and the tablet exposes what colour alone does not. Uncertainty belongs partly to the procedure and partly to the visitor's access to its state. Calling both random loses the distinction that makes the experiment possible.

Pressing CUE does not make the machine more generous with its standing time. It changes the notice on its stones. An advance ring permits a different preparation from a beacon during movement. Both are partial: the ring requires attention to the stone; the tablet requires attention elsewhere; neither announces the next unsampled duration. Removing the rings leaves the tablet intact, so this is not a comparison between complete knowledge and its complete absence.

That specificity matters before turning a missed step into a judgment about a person. The result depends on what could be read, when it could be read, movement capabilities, prior experience and the available return route. The state machine stores none of that as an account of the visitor. It can report its own state without explaining why someone fell.

The east corridor and the pit's recovery ramp make different ways of continuing possible. A person can study the stones, attempt the crossing, return through the bed or walk around. These are useful terms for an artistic research question about which bodies an arrangement accommodates. A desktop traversal establishes some geometry and mechanics; it does not settle that question for seated or standing headset users.

The falling field adds a second experiment. Stopping its source leaves earlier emissions moving. Their separate generators continue selecting velocities. A boundary around starting positions cannot promise that trajectories remain inside it, and a repeated launch seed cannot promise repeated contact with a visitor. The object's past is distributed across more state than its label records.

The other creatures complicate the encounter further. A hit cannot automatically be attributed to the field's sampling law, and relocation is not proof of separation from the crossing. The room should eventually make that comparison controllable. For now, the text names the distinction and the review records this limitation instead of describing an isolated or harmless experiment.

QFEP enters here as a research question about what a system keeps fixed, what it permits to vary and what another body can do with those variations. This room does not calculate entropy, a free-energy value or a universal threshold. Its argument is executable in narrower terms: change a cue without changing a deadline; stop a source without recalling its emissions; repeat initial conditions while locating the state that still differs.

Noise Types will change the relation among samples. Before asking whether a landscape is smooth or hostile, we have learned to ask which rule produced its differences and which relation our description left out.
