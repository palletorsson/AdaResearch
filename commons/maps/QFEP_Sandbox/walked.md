## The walk

Every slider unlocked. The `qfep_reactor` drives a field of `reactive_particles`, and both parameters are now yours at once: λ tunes the order–chaos balance, φ sets the disposition toward change, and the world answers in real time — crystallization, emergence, dissolution, sliding continuously into one another as you work the two axes. The `qfep_formula_3d` hangs overhead updating live, the equation and its consequences finally in the same room at the same time. The task is stated once and then the map goes quiet: *find the configurations that feel alive.* Not λ = 0.4 because the previous room said so — the reactor's edge is not quite the walkway's edge, and the difference is the lesson. Search for it. Overshoot, correct, bracket it from both sides the way you bracketed the green zone with your legs. Then hold it — notice that holding is active, that the aliveness drifts and wants re-tuning, that φ decides how much drift you can use rather than fight. What is being tested is not knowledge of the formula. It is whether your hands know what λ = 0.35 feels like versus 0.45 before the display tells you.

## The turn (critical)

The sandbox is where the lab's pedagogy states its philosophy: understanding here is **operational, not declarative** — you understand QFEP when you can *tune* it, the way you understand a bicycle. A learner who can recite the formula but cannot find the living regime does not yet have it; a learner who can find it by feel has it even if they stumble on the notation. This is the map's answer to a question that has shadowed the whole laboratory — what kind of thing is a formula that claims to describe life? Not a fact to be believed, the sandbox says: a *capacity to be acquired*, closer to balance than to doctrine. The intent's own metaphor is "the force," and it earns the melodrama by cashing it out precisely: a force is exactly the kind of thing you cannot have declaratively. And there is a final, quiet turn in the phrase the blurb chooses — find *your* edge of chaos. The definite article has been dissolving all through the lab: Langton's edge, the walkway's edge, the reactor's edge — same physics, different systems, different addresses. Yours too: your own working balance of order and openness, your own φ signature, is a configuration this room lets you locate experimentally, without ever saying the word *therapy*. There are no wrong answers here, the map says — which is true, and carefully put, because there are still *dead* ones, and by now you can feel the difference. That feeling is the curriculum's actual diploma.
