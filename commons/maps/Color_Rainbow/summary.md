# Every Body / More Colour

Color_Rainbow follows the salon and precedes Color_Pillar. A room-scale double arch, candy steps, pearl-collared mushrooms, striped pride banners, stars and confetti turn the existing rainbow generator into a queer platform-game celebration.

The primary remains `rainbow`. Its opt-in `stage:celebration` adds two real controls on a plinth. RE-DIVIDE cycles through 6, 7, 48 and 2 bands while retaining the arch's total colour width and the walking route. CELEBRATE repeats a bounded confetti burst; entering the centre welcome patch triggers one too. There is no score requirement or room transition.

The 15 by 26 metre room retains its enclosing walls and level floor. Optional left-hand steps rise by 0.18 m to 0.9 m. A level centre path remains available. The mushrooms and arches are visual scenery; the steps and control plinth have collision. A pickup cube and catalyst pedestal remain at the far side. The old dark-sphere/MarioCube reveal and automatic catalyst vent are archived with the prior layout.

The lesson is about authored colour partitions on geometry, not wavelength optics. The mode called continuous is a finite 48-band HSV sweep. The book lets the visitor notice changing boundaries before explaining the list and thickness rule, then carries sampling into the pillar field.
