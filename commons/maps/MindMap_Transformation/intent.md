Concept: The transformation concept map made walkable. `tools/mindmap_to_map.py` projects the 2D mind-map of the 12 transformation concepts (Three rigid motions through Curved space) onto a floor — diagram layout becomes level layout, diagram order becomes the spawn-to-teleporter walk. The progression is one of loosening invariants: rigid motions preserve everything, then each step gives up one more guarantee until non-Euclidean space surrenders flatness itself.
Sequence role: A meta-instrument beside the Transformation spine, not a curriculum step. Undeclared by design — the projector's output, the whole 12-concept structure compressed into one walkable view. Spawn at `Three rigid motions` (the invariant-preserving root), teleporter at `Curved space (non-Euclidean)` (where the premises fail). One representative artifact per concept.
Technical angle: Generated map_data.json (3 layers) from the concept map via mindmap_to_map.py with weighting w=0.00 (pure concept-graph layout). 440 artifacts, all reachable, single teleporter — pathfinder-clean. The placed-channel loop (placed_channel.py) measures the built level back against the source diagram, scoring how faithfully the walk reproduces the concept arrangement.
Critical angle: The conventional story of transformation is "apply an operation, get a result." Laying the operations out as terrain reframes them as a hierarchy of what-must-hold: rigid → similar → affine → projective → curved is a staircase of relaxed constraints, and walking it makes the relaxation bodily. Matrix/homogeneous sits where the geometry becomes notation; the learner crosses the line where moving a shape and multiplying a number become the same act.
Key artifacts: One representative artifact per concept node — `Three rigid motions` anchors the spawn; `Translation`, `Rotation`, `Scale`, `Shear` form the affine spine; `Composition` and `Matrix / homogeneous` mark the shift from gesture to algebra; `Field / flow` makes transformation continuous; `Curved space (non-Euclidean)` holds the teleporter and the broken assumption.
