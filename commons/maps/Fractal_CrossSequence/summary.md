# Fractals_14 - Synthesis: Stochastic Trees

## Overview
Fractals_14 concludes the fractals sequence by returning to stochastic trees—now understood in the full context of fractals, self-similarity, dimension, and emergence. What was introduced as "trees with randomness" in Fractals_2 can now be understood as the QFEP in botanical form: deterministic rules (F) + entropy injection (λE(S)) + temporal unfolding (φΔE(S,t)).

## Learning Sequence
1. Player enters having completed the fractals journey
2. Encounters stochastic trees again—now with deeper understanding
3. Recognizes: the F term (branching rule), the λE(S) term (random variation), the φΔE(S,t) (growth over iterations)
4. Sees the tree as QFEP embodied
5. Understands: fractals are not just shapes but principles
6. Completes the sequence with synthesis achieved
7. Exits with integrated understanding of fractal emergence

## Design Intent
The map closes the loop—returning to material first seen in Fractals_2, but now comprehensible in the full framework. The stochastic tree is not just "pretty" but **meaningful**: it embodies the principle that adaptive systems oscillate between order and chaos.
