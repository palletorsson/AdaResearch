## The walk

The laws get named here, in their own room. The newtons_laws artifact states them; the NOC examples stage them one assumption at a time — force applied, gravity scaled honestly by mass, resistance proportional to speed. The basis_vectors_rig underneath it all is the quiet foundation: before any force can be written, someone chose the axes it is written in.

## The turn (critical)

The critical move is to notice what the law's neatness costs. F = ma is exact for bodies that are rigid, isolated, and measured from axes someone already agreed on — the rig makes that agreement visible and therefore questionable. Gravity "scaled by mass" is democratic in the equation and not in the room: mass decides who moves whom. And the proximity_spawner adds the observation the physics textbook leaves out — here, things come into being because you approached. The laws are true; the critical question the room keeps open is *true inside which frame, granted by whom.* That question is the door the chapter's seam artifact wants to stand in.
