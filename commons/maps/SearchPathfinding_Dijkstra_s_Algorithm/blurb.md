A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 region in the center where algorithms perform. Gaussian distributions raise terrain into bell curves. Random walks leave trails across the surface — drunk movement that somehow, given enough steps, covers everything. Cubes spawn, rotate, dissolve. Height maps randomize. Profiles generate. The grid is a stage for stochastic processes made visible and tangible.

Randomness is not the absence of structure. It is structure's raw material. A random walk looks aimless but obeys statistical laws. A Gaussian distribution looks chaotic but converges to a curve. Remove elements at random and pattern emerges from the gaps. Every algorithm here takes noise as input and produces form as output — not despite the randomness but through it.

Order is not imposed. It precipitates.
