# Diffusion Limited Aggregation

A seed sits still. Particles drift — random walks through empty space, bouncing off nothing until they touch something solid. Then they stick. One particle becomes two, two become a branch, branches become a tree that no one designed.

DLA grows by accident. Each particle's path is pure noise, but the aggregate is structured — dendritic, fractal, self-similar at every scale. The pattern emerges not from the walkers but from the boundary. What's already stuck determines what sticks next. History shapes geometry.

The room branches around you. Irregular walls fork and cluster, void patches open where no particle ever landed. This is growth without blueprint, form without intention. The random walk is the brush. The aggregation surface is the memory. Every crystal, every lightning bolt, every mineral vein — diffusion finds the edge and holds.
