# Chamber_Boolean — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (seed slot, boolean_surfaces thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

becoming_catalyst · catalyst_target · science_screen

## The walk

A workshop after the wall of cavities. The becoming_catalyst holds the bracelet; you place a block on the catalyst_target, and then another, and the room asks the only question the chapter cares about — join, share, or remove? The science_screen holds the grid as your two solids become one, or only their overlap, or the first minus the second, depending on the verb you choose.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to compose**. The chapter taught that composition is not addition — that solids have an algebra of three operations, and any form is a sentence in them — and here you speak that sentence with your hands. Place, place, and choose: union fuses, dissolving the seam; intersection keeps only the shared interior; difference carves a hollow shaped by absence. You are not stacking blocks but *composing* them, and the order of your verbs matters the way it does in arithmetic. It is the fitting seed for a chapter that reduced building to a grammar: the room hands you the three words and lets you discover that with them you can say any shape at all. To compose is to make by lawful combination — and to feel, block by block, that a wall is just a difference and a joint is just an intersection, all the way down.

## The chapter's missing middle

This chamber closes the seed, but boolean_surfaces still lacks its critical, world, and close rooms — it remains a focused five-room treatment of the CSG operators plus this seed. Whether it should grow the full arc, or stay a deliberately tight chapter, is a design call left open on the bench.
