# Narrative Generation

A Markov chain has no memory. It knows only where it is — never where it's been. From each state, a weighted die rolls, and the next word falls. Transition probabilities replace intention. Grammar emerges from arithmetic.

The corridor is narrow and moves one direction. States float as nodes in space, arcs between them weighted and glowing. Watch the die tumble, the arc light up, the next node catch. A sequence accumulates — not chosen, not random, but probabilistically inevitable. The stationary distribution settles like sediment: given enough time, every chain finds its equilibrium without trying.

Text generated this way reads almost right. Subject, verb, object — the syntax of sense without the burden of meaning. The chain doesn't know what it's saying. It doesn't need to. Structure was never about understanding — it was about the weight of what comes next.
