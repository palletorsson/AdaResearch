## The cast

shader_noise_space · dark_sphere

## The walk

The room stops containing exhibits and becomes one. The shader_noise_space evaluates noise per-pixel, per-frame, on the surfaces around you — procedural texture with no texture stored, the GPU as noise organ. Nothing here is a picture; everything is a function, sampled where you look, re-decided as you move.

## The turn

The turn is architectural: for five rooms noise was *in front of* the walker — galleries, plinths, terrains under glass. Here it is the enclosure. When all six walls run the function, the distinction between exhibit and room dissolves, and you understand what the chapter has been quietly claiming: noise is not content, it is *material*. The next rooms take the consequence seriously — if noise can be the walls, it can be the inside, and then it can be the world.
