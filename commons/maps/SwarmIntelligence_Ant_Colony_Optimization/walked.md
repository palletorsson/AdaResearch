## The walk

The colony returns, upgraded and interrogated. The AntColonyV2 runs the pheromone economy at full detail — evaporation gradients, multi-source foraging, routes weighted by quality — the same stigmergic computer as the turn room, now dense enough to study rather than admire.

## The turn (critical)

The critical room asks what the beautiful emergence costs. Stigmergy's gift — no leader, no central memory, robustness through redundancy — is also its trap: a colony can lock onto a *good enough* path and pave it with pheromone until no ant explores the better one nearby. Evaporation is the only defense against this tyranny of the trail, forgetting deliberately engineered so the swarm can change its mind. The politics are unavoidable and the map's kin knows it: a system that stores its memory in the shared environment is a system where the majority's habits become everyone's only options. The ant colony optimizes and ossifies by the same mechanism, and the room leaves you weighing the two — the wisdom of the trail against the dead weight of the path already taken.
