Ants leave pheromones as they walk. Paths with more pheromone attract more ants. Paths with shorter distances get walked more often. Positive feedback loops find optimal routes through mazes. Dumb agents, smart swarm. Stigmergy: communication through environment.

`AntColony` implements Dorigo's ACO on the branching corridor network: edge-selection probability weighs pheromone intensity against heuristic desirability, and an evaporation rate prevents premature convergence. Short paths accumulate pheromone faster because they are traversed more often per unit time — the positive feedback loop is mathematical, not metaphorical. Optimization does not require an optimizer; it requires a population of mediocre agents and a medium that remembers. Pheromone is external memory distributed across space, owned by no individual. Combinatorial optimization made walkable.
