Concept: Ants solve the traveling salesman problem without knowing it exists — pheromone trails accumulate on shorter paths, evaporate on longer ones, and the colony converges on near-optimal routes through a branching corridor network. Ant colony optimization is stigmergy as algorithm: the environment is the shared memory, and the pheromone gradient is the message.
Sequence role: Fifth map in Swarm Intelligence (14th spine sequence, integration phase), intermediate difficulty. After the general ABM framework, this map applies agent-based principles to a specific NP-hard optimization problem. The branching corridor network gives the ants a physical graph to traverse, making the pheromone dynamics spatially legible. Connects forward to SwarmIntelligence_Particle_Swarm_Optimization where optimization continues but the mechanism shifts from stigmergy to social learning; follows SwarmIntelligence_Agent_Based_Modeling_ABM.
Technical angle: Dorigo's Ant Colony Optimization (ACO): artificial ants traverse a graph, depositing pheromone proportional to solution quality (inverse path length). Edge selection probability is a function of pheromone intensity and heuristic desirability (inverse distance), controlled by parameters alpha (pheromone weight) and beta (heuristic weight). Pheromone evaporation rate rho prevents convergence to local optima. The branching corridor network maps directly to a graph structure where corridors are edges and junctions are nodes.
Critical angle: ACO demonstrates that optimization does not require an optimizer — it requires a population of mediocre agents and a medium that remembers. The pheromone trail is external memory, distributed across the environment, owned by no individual. This is Dorigo's formalization of the swarm intelligence thesis: local rules (follow strong pheromone, deposit more on short paths) produce global intelligence (near-optimal TSP solutions). The branching corridors literalize the graph abstraction, making combinatorial optimization walkable.
Key artifacts: AntColony implements Dorigo's ACO algorithm within the branching corridor network, visualizing pheromone deposition, evaporation, and path convergence in real time.
Gap: Needs a pheromone heatmap with decay visualization — an artifact that shows pheromone concentration as a color gradient on the corridor network and animates the evaporation process, making the tension between reinforcement and decay visible as the colony searches.
