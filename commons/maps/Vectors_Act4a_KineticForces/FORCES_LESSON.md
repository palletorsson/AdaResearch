# Let motion meet resistance

Primary: `force_play_liquids`.

Keep the sphere and enable gravity. Add resistance while its centre lies between heights zero and two metres. The small study uses a height test; it does not build a liquid surface.

Carry: Drag resists motion. A spring adds a reason to move back toward an attachment.

Check: Increase `coefficient` from 0.6 to 3.0 and restart. At the same speed the drag is five times stronger, until capped. Compare the falling speeds over the available drop; the short fall may end before either reaches a steady speed.
