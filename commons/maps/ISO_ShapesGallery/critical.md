# A chair is a proposal

The field makes no categorical distinction between a chair, a cup and a human figure. Their numerical descriptions can enter the same extractor. Yet this shared substrate does not erase differences in what their bodies can do. A chair-shaped mesh is not a demonstrated support for a sitting body.

Hold the chair before changing the level. Its label can remain while a leg disappears. Recognition, connectivity and use need different evidence. The held mesh lets a reader locate the difference without having to trust a memory of the previous frame.

The apparently neutral global adjustment distributes loss unevenly: a narrow handle and a broad seat meet the same threshold with different consequences. The sampling grid adds another selection. What is absent from the visible mesh may still be specified in the field. Here the question “what bodies are possible?” includes the implementation's budget and the category we bring to its result.

A different repertoire offers more than another organic coating. It exposes composition as a tool we can alter. To exceed the household vocabulary, we can eventually rewrite the ingredients and their relations; the current desk begins with the smaller, inspectable act of comparing their extracted boundaries.
