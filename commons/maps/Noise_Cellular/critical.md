# A cell need not remain a room

Nearest-point assignment gives a precise answer to a limited question: which site is closest? Turning that answer into a wall adds another rule. The wall has thickness; an opening has clearance; a body may fail to pass even where the mathematical regions meet.

The centre here becomes shared because we delete partitions. The strands make a further choice: a boundary stays visible without stopping passage. Neither result follows automatically from the distance rule. QFEP offers a way to ask which relations the enclosure supports and which it withholds, without pretending these rooms measure social freedom or free energy.
