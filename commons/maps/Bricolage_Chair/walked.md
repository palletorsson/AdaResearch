## The cast

chair_parts_inventory · chair_builder · chair_assembled · dark_sphere

## The walk

The first real build, and it is the most ordinary thing in the world: a chair. The chair_parts_inventory lays out the stock — the same primitives from the first room, now cut and counted for a purpose. The chair_builder catches assembly mid-act, some members joined and some still loose. And chair_assembled stands finished at the far end, a thing you could sit on, made of nothing the inventory did not already hold.

## The turn (critical)

The critical room is deliberately humble. After the abstractions — affordances, arrays, constraints — the chapter builds the most vernacular object it can find, because the chair is where bricolage lives for real. Nobody designs a kitchen chair from first principles; it accretes, part by part, each joint a small satisfaction of gravity and balance and triangulation, until the thing holds a body. The map's own words: the chair *emerges* from inventory through constraint satisfaction — not drawn, not engineered, discovered. This is the sharp point of the whole sequence: that the difference between a pile of primitives and a chair is not a blueprint but a series of constraints met, one at a time, with what was on hand. The engineer would find this beneath them. The bricoleur knows it is the entire art.
