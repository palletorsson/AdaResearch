# Trans_Composition - Assembly Puzzles

## Overview

This map presents transformation as **composition**—the combination of scale, rotation, and translation to create complex forms from simple primitives. Five assembly stations explore different facets of building, from templates to pure physics.

## Layout

- **9×9 platform** with raised edges
- **Assembly line** (north) - conveyor belt production
- **Chair puzzle** (center) - Judd-inspired furniture
- **Biederman relief** (west) - constructivist composition
- **Lab equipment** (east) - Half-Life style builds
- **Balance puzzle** (south) - no templates, pure physics, the sublime

## Key Elements

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| `assembly_line_puzzle` | Conveyor belt house building from cubes + pyramids |
| `chair_assembly_puzzle` | 6-piece furniture from scaled cubes |
| `biederman_relief` | Relief composition with colored bars |
| `lab_assembly_puzzle` | Half-Life lab equipment (health station, camera, specimen jar) |
| `balance_puzzle` | Stack freely, reach height threshold, creation transforms and walks |
| `dark_sphere` | Ambient lighting |

## Interaction

### Assembly Line
- Shapes arrive on conveyor
- Match cube/pyramid combinations to ghost targets
- Completed houses drop into container
- Rewards spawn as grabbable objects

### Chair Assembly
1. **Grab** a cube from the workbench
2. **Scale** using two-handed grip (VR) or scroll wheel (desktop)
3. **Rotate** by twisting while held
4. **Position** to match the ghost guide
5. When all pieces match → chair assembles and grows to real size

### Relief Puzzles
- Scale cubes to match target dimensions
- Position on backing to create relief composition
- Excess cubes removed on completion

### Lab Equipment
- Same mechanics as house assembly
- Half-Life themed: health chargers, cameras, specimen jars

### Balance Puzzle (QFEP)
- **No templates** - stack freely on the platform
- **Height threshold** - reach the required height
- **Stability check** - structure must balance
- **Transformation** - when stable at height, pieces reorganize into a walking creature
- **The sublime** - watch your creation come alive and walk away

## Learning Objectives

- Transform composition (T × R × S)
- Scale as form-giving operation
- Primitive combination strategies
- Assembly sequencing
- Relief and spatial construction
- **Emergent form through physics** - truth without templates

## Design Philosophy

Inspired by Donald Judd's furniture and Half-Life's modular lab aesthetic: minimal forms, honest materials, systematic variation. Complex objects are just simple shapes with the right transforms applied.

The balance puzzle introduces QFEP: the Queer Feminist Energy Principle. No single correct answer. Multiple stable configurations exist. The "truth" emerges from physics itself, not from prescription. And the reward is the sublime—your creation transforming, animating, departing.
