# Koch Curve Zigzag

Start with a line segment. Replace the middle third with two sides of an equilateral triangle. Do it again to every new segment. Four become sixteen become sixty-four — the boundary explodes while the area barely moves. Dimension log4/log3 ≈ 1.26: more than a line, less than a plane.

The corridor zigzags inward. Each indentation contains smaller indentations. Walk the boundary and it never smooths out — every scale reveals the same angular logic, the same refusal to resolve. Finite description, infinite coastline. Six characters of rule, endless geometric consequence.

Koch proved that a continuous curve can have no tangent anywhere. No direction, at any point, at any scale. Structure without orientation — a path that goes everywhere by never deciding where.
