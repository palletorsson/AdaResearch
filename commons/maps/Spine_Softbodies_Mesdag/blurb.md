Mesdag — softbodies, as a hypostyle.

the Mezquita: the columns make the rhythm, so things come in pairs between them

The plan is a window of `mesdag-panorama-drum` — 15x19, walled, odd on both axes. 6 artifacts from softbodies stand here, which is 50% of what the room could take. The empty slots are not unfinished: a room filled to capacity reads as everything that fit, and a room at 50% reads as chosen.

Placed by measurement rather than by hand — the slots were measured from the floor, the artifacts matched by grid footprint, and the tightest fit won. Nothing here was walked before it was written.
