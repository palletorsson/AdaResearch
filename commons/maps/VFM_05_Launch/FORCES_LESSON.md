# Give the point a beginning

Primary: `launch_arc`.

Continue the sphere as a marker. Give it an initial velocity and calculate its position as time passes. Freeze its rigid body and turn collision off so this first flight follows the calculation alone.

Carry: You have calculated where the point should be. Next, let the physics engine keep its velocity while you supply a push.

Check: Keep the upward component at 4 m/s. Double the horizontal component from 3 to 6 m/s. Predict what changes before running: the return still takes 0.8 seconds, while horizontal range grows from 2.4 to 4.8 metres.

Back in the hall, try PAIR. Two complementary launch angles at the same speed can reach the same distance in different times.
