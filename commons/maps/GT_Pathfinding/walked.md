# GT_Pathfinding — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (turn slot, graphtheory thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

pathfinding3d

## The walk

One artifact, and it is a mirror. The pathfinding3d runs the classic search — frontier expanding, candidates weighed, the shortest route crystallizing out of the fog of possible ones — through a space you can walk around while it thinks.

## The turn

The turn is autobiographical: this curriculum runs on the thing in this room. Every map you have walked was checked, before you entered it, by a pathfinder crawling the grid — spawn to teleporter, cell by cell, a graph search certifying that the room you're standing in can be survived. The floor under your feet passed this exam. The exhibit is the examiner. Graph theory stops being a subject here and is revealed as the *infrastructure* — the quiet mathematics that decides, for every space in this world, whether it counts as a place at all.
