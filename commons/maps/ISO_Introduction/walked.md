## The walk

The chapter opens on data with no shape. The voxel_noise_demo fills a lattice with numbers — each sample holding one answer to *how inside am I here?* — and shows the field honestly, as a fog of values, before any surface has been asked of it. The science_screen frames the question the next seven rooms will answer: somewhere in this fog there is a boundary, the set of points where the field crosses a chosen line, and that boundary can be *built*.

## What it opens

The threshold separates two things every later room depends on keeping apart: the field (continuous, invisible, everywhere) and the surface (the single skin we extract from it). Inside and outside are not properties of space — they are a number's relation to a cutoff. The chapter starts by showing you the number.
