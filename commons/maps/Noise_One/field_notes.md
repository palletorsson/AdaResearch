# Noise_One — field notes

## 2026-09-12 — N3: is the restless thing a field, a surface or our way of reading it (Fable, the third hall of Astra's noise arc, after Noise_Columns; Astra's card: "make a matched colour-only and relief reading of the same field on the same support; offer a local time freeze and a sample marker that reveals coordinate/value, freezing both displacement sampling and independent hue motion; expose relief amplitude separately from frequency; preserve the ring opening over the beginner range; show whether extreme deformation changes only appearance or actual collision")

**The ruling.** The ring already answered half the question by itself and nobody had noticed: `noiseTorus.gdshader` samples one value per point and spends it twice — once as displacement in the vertex stage, once as brightness in the fragment stage, at the same coordinate, from the same expression. So the staging puts the two spendings side by side: two rings of the same size on one bench, the same shader, the same noise scale, one allowed to show only relief and the other only colour, with a marker standing on the same coordinate of both and a plate naming the value there. The shader takes two new uniforms, `show_relief` and `show_colour`, both 1.0 by default, so the shipped picture is unchanged.

**The finding the room is built on.** The two readings are not equivalent, and the one that loses is the one people trust. The field runs from −1 to +1. The relief spends all of it, pushing outward for the positive half and inward for the negative. The colour cannot: `ALBEDO = rainbow_color * noise_value` has no negative, so everywhere the field is below zero the ring is black — and black however far below zero it goes. Half the field is invisible in colour and perfectly legible in relief. The probe measures this at the marked coordinate: value −0.562 gives relief −0.011 m and colour 0.000.

**A freeze has to cover every clock, and this shader has two.** Astra's card says a material parameter at zero does not prove all time inputs are frozen, so the probe reads the shader source rather than trusting a number: it collects every line containing `TIME`, and for each one either the line multiplies by a speed uniform or it assigns a name whose every later use does. The shipped file does the second — `wrapped_time` takes TIME bare and is spent only as `wrapped_time * noise_speed` — and the first version of the check called that unfrozen, which is the opposite of true. The corrected check follows the variable. With both speeds at zero the sampled value holds across a second and a quarter, measured.

**Amplitude is a reading; frequency is the field.** Two buttons that look alike and are not. Pressing AMPLITUDE changes what the surface does with a value and leaves the value at the coordinate untouched (measured: 0.20 → 0.35 amplitude, value −0.3514 both before and after). Pressing FREQUENCY changes which value is at the coordinate at all (3.575 → 7.0, value −0.3514 → −0.4753). The plate names which of the two was touched last, in those words.

**The collision contract, said out loud.** The displacement lives in the vertex stage, where no collider can see it, and neither ring carries a collision shape. The probe checks the ring subtrees specifically, apart from the bench, which does have one because it is furniture. The plate's last line says it: the relief is not a surface you can hit.

**The shipped numbers are for the shipped ring, again.** `height_multiplier` 0.2 and `noise_scale` 3.575 were set for a torus of radius ~1.8. On the pair's 0.46 m rings that amplitude is a third of the tube radius (the ring folds through itself and photographs as crumpled paper) and that frequency puts barely one noise cell across the whole ring. Three iterations: scale the amplitude, widen the tube from 0.10 to 0.14, and then lower the frequency again because the relief is sampled per VERTEX and thirteen noise cells across forty-eight segments alias into spikes. The mesh is 96 × 40 now and the field coarse enough for it. This is the same fault the trio in Noise_Columns paid a run for, in a different currency.

**The map.** The ring stood at (5,5), in the middle of the hall's own five-by-five hole, where no visitor can stand near it. It stands at (5,2) on the north strip now, turned to face the door, with the bench inside a clear rect. The description promised octave summation and a VR brush, neither of which this hall contains; it says what is there now, including the collision contract. The hall's other two artifacts (`noiselayers`, `dark_sphere`) are still over the hole and are not this pass's to move.

**Exactness decisions.** The sample walks six fixed coordinates round the ring rather than following a pointer, so the reading is reproducible and can be checked. The plate's value is the room's own port of the shader's `hash`/`noise`, ported line for line — the GPU computes it in 32-bit and this in 64-bit, so the last digits of a very chaotic hash differ; what the plate claims is the field's value at a coordinate, which is a definition, not a screenshot. The rings' captions are stood up on the bench, not laid flat, for the reason N1 found.

**Rejected.** Colour and relief on one ring with a button to switch: it makes the comparison a memory test, which is what this room is trying to escape. Rejected too: raising the amplitude until the corrugation is dramatic — it stops being a reading of the field and becomes a different object, and Astra's card asks to preserve the ring opening over the beginner range.

**Pending.** A headset walk; a person stepping sideways to separate a viewpoint change from a field change, which is the one thing a probe cannot do for this room; Astra's review.
