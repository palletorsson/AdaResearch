Concept: Randomness as subtraction from a set a rule chose first. The bench separates three decisions that the word "random" folds together — the filter that decides which cubes are candidates (Range, Row, Column, All), the even draw among the remaining candidates, and the deletion — and makes the first one readable and changeable while the draw's seed is named and replayable.
Sequence role: Active hall 3 in randomness. Random_Definition made an arrangement repeatable from a seed; Random_Entropy showed a number keep the shares of a sample and lose its order; this room shows chance acting only within an eligible set decided before it runs, and hands the changing set to Random_Walk, where a walker's reachable places are decided one draw at a time. The off-route 10 PRINT room is not required.
Technical angle: `RemoveRandom.gd` with `#local_grid:true` builds its own bench (`remove_random_fixture.gd`): a pedestal, a board, sixty-four cubes in one private MultiMesh, permanent slot plates, numerals, a status and a last-removed label, and a panel — RANGE · ROW · COLUMN · ALL / REMOVE ONE · RESET · NEW SEED. `_should_include_instance` is one `match` over the cube's own grid coordinates; `find_all_instances` skips removed cubes and colours the rest amber or grey; `remove_one` draws `randi_range(0, remaining − 1)` from a private RNG, highlights, then scales the cube's transform to zero; `reset_and_find_instances` restores every transform and, on the bench, re-seeds the RNG with the run's seed (`replay_on_reset`, R1b); `new_seed` names another. `get_state` carries the counts, the seed, the removal log and the initial eligible set.
Critical angle: fairness inside a candidate set is not fairness to what the set excluded. The draw is even over the amber cubes and blind to the grey; the boundary was a rule, legible and changeable, and neither the seed nor the evenness of the draw can justify where it fell. The permanent slot plates keep the address of every absence without the order it was taken in — a record of what is missing, not of how.
Key artifacts: remove_random is the primary and the book's hero; hazards_demo and dark_sphere stand a metre off, company rather than evidence.

Gap: physical absence is not claimed by the map and not built (a removed cube is a scaled transform on a board); the removal timer (`start_removal`) exists on the remover but is not on the panel — REMOVE ONE is the visitor's step; a heat map of many runs is not built.
