> ghost-drafted from the working map (primitive slot, randomness thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

remove_random · hazards_demo · dark_sphere

## The walk

The chapter's first act of chance is subtraction. The remove_random engine deletes cubes from the grid by draw — no plan, no pattern, the room thinning around you into a negative sculpture of the dice. Nearby the hazards_demo lets the danger fauna out of their cages: plasma critters and crawlers wandering an arena where chance has consequences, the first room in the thread where a draw can cost you something.

## What it teaches

The primitive is entropy as an *editor*. Every other builder in this world adds; this one takes away, and what remains is shaped by absence. The pairing with live hazards is the honest version of the lesson: freedom from pattern cuts both ways — the same wilderness that makes the butterflies possible makes the floor uncertain. Randomness enters this curriculum not as decoration but as the first force the walker cannot negotiate with.
