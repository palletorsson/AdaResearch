# Borrowed outlines — intent

After Columns, before Portals. Extend the distinction between source profile and construction rule through six supplied glyph forms. Keep the flat O, B, C, I and colon profiles beside their bodies so the borrowed boundary remains inspectable.

The geometry builder, font extraction, triangulation and frame recipes are supplied. Select one disclosed dimension or route choice for a later reduced study; do not turn this encounter into a general topology curriculum or claim a general CAD system.

Keep practical limits explicit: the upright O has a sill; the vessel lacks a bottom; B retains separate wells; C's open channel is not automatically a handle; I gains an opening by closing its route; the colon remains disconnected. Static/source checks do not establish accessible museum passage or VR comfort.
