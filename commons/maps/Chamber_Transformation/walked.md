# Chamber_Transformation — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (seed slot, transformation thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

becoming_catalyst · catalyst_target · proximity_spawner · science_screen

## The walk

The standard chamber, and after a chapter about what survives change it feels aptly poised. The becoming_catalyst waits on its pedestal, the catalyst_target marks a cell, the proximity_spawner keys the room to your approach. You take the bracelet — and the bracelet is itself a machine of transformation, its three stones switching the block you place between cube, wedge, and nothing, a rotation of the wrist changing the world's next word.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to transform**. The whole chapter asked what stays the same when everything changes — and the catalyst hands you the *changing*: rotate the bracelet and the operation changes, place a block and then place its transformed twin, and feel for the thing that survived the move. Position shifts, orientation flips, mode toggles, and something — adjacency, count, the fact of your having placed it — persists underneath. You become the transformation, and the invariant is whatever your hand cannot help but preserve. The chapter that named identity-under-change ends by making you perform the change and keep the identity in the same gesture.

## The close (missing)

The thread has no close between world and this seed — the transformations never gather into one room that names their common invariant before the chamber plants the verb. The same gap as randomness, lsystems, and swarm; it joins the one bench call.
