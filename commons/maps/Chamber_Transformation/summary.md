# Chamber Transformation — Summary

Chamber_Transformation is the catalyst chamber for the Transformation sequence. It is the learner's first creature encounter in the curriculum, and the chamber frames transformation as non-destructive change rather than as damage.

The chamber is small. A miura_crawler paces the floor, the same folding primitive the learner studied as an object earlier in the sequence, now present as a creature with its own behaviour. The learner holds the transformation catalyst. Firing the catalyst does not injure the crawler; it folds. Its panels compress along the Miura crease pattern, the creature reduces to a flat disc, and it sits on the ground waiting for the fold to release. It has not died. Its state has changed.

A science screen on the wall logs each fold as a scatter-plot event. Axes track compression ratio and fold depth over time; the record reads as a small dataset about the transformation rather than as a combat log. A second panel names the move explicitly: folded is not less than unfolded, it is a different state of the same being, and the catalyst's job is to induce the state change rather than to end the creature.

Within the sequence, Chamber_Transformation turns the algebra of rotation, translation, and scale into encounter. The chamber is where mathematics becomes a language the learner negotiates with something else, and it hands the learner back to the Lab with the transformation catalyst in their kit and the crawler in the kind of folded rest the learner induced rather than took.
