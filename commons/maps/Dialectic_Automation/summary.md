# Dialectic Automation - Map Summary

## Overview
A walkable argument about automation and autonomy. The player moves forward through Z-space, encountering a series of panels that present the dialectic as a fractal structure: each position generates its counter, each counter faces critique, and the process continues.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 7×30 grid (narrow corridor, long depth)
- **Architecture**: Linear path with panels arranged along Z-axis
- **Navigation**: Walk forward to progress through argument levels

## Key Elements

### The Dialectic Structure (9 levels)
1. **Thesis**: "Automation Takes Away Autonomy"
2. **Counter**: "Cognitive Offloading is Liberation"
3. **Critique**: "Opting Out is a Privilege"
4. **Pragmatic**: "At Every Level There is a λ to Adjust"
5. **Reality Check**: "Who Actually Has Access to the Dials?"
6. **Hacker Response**: "We Can Make the Dials Visible"
7. **Limit**: "Not Everyone Can Read Code"
8. **Collective**: "We Don't Have to Do It Alone"
9. **Recursion**: "The Dialectic Never Resolves"

### Interactables
- **criticalinfo:automation_dialectic** - Spawns the panel system at row 3
- **dark_sphere** - Ambient darkness for contemplative atmosphere

### Utilities
- **Spawn point** (0,0) - Player starts facing the dialectic
- **Floating text** (3,2) - "The Dialectic"
- **Teleporter** (3,28) - Exit at the end

## Atmosphere
- **Background**: Deep dark blue [0.05, 0.05, 0.1]
- **Audio**: "contemplative_drift" preset at -8dB
- **Mood**: Philosophical, contemplative, recursive

## Learning Sequence
1. Player spawns at entrance
2. Sees title "The Dialectic"
3. Walks forward, encountering first panel (Thesis)
4. Each step deeper reveals the next level
5. Panels include: title, position statement, narrative, code example, QFEP connection
6. Final panel acknowledges the recursive structure
7. Exit teleporter returns to sequence

## Design Intent
The spatial metaphor: **argument as journey**. You can't see all levels at once. Each position seems complete until you take another step and encounter its counter. The corridor format enforces linearity—you must pass through each stage.

## Connection to QFEP
Each panel explicitly connects to the Queer Free Energy Principle:
- F (order) vs E(S) (entropy)
- λ as the dial between them
- φΔE(S,t) as embrace of becoming
- The dialectic itself embodies positive φ: valuing change over stasis
