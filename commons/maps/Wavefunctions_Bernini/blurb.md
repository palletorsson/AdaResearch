Baroque columns twist like frozen waves. Bernini's solomonic spirals are sine functions wrapped around cylinders — architecture as helical oscillation, stone as solidified motion. The wave captured mid-dance, held for centuries.

The `bernini_columns` artifact implements the technique as vertex displacement: rotate each vertex's horizontal position by an angle proportional to its height, add layered noise for organic erosion, and a straight cylinder becomes a twisting column. `ElphabaDress` extends the same wave-driven deformation to fabric; `vowel_sound_board` couples oscillation to vocal resonance. Baroque was a glitch in Renaissance order — excess, movement, emotion disrupting classical restraint. Procedural geometry is the new Baroque: favoring the variable and mutating over the static and ideal. Stone can still dance if you write the shader.
