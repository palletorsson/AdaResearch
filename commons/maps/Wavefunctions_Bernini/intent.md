Concept: Baroque columns as frozen waves — Bernini's solomonic spirals are sine functions wrapped around cylinders. Vertex displacement via rotation matrices transforms rigid geometry into twisting organic forms. Procedural geometry as the new Baroque.
Sequence role: Seventh map in Wavefunctions; the first art-historical map. After five maps of wave mechanics and one of sound, this map applies oscillation to sculptural form. The advanced difficulty signals that the learner must compose techniques: vertex displacement, rotation matrices, noise layering. Connects to Primitives' geometric heritage while radically deforming it; follows WaveFunctions_Effect_Sound; leads to WaveFunctions_John_Cage.
Technical angle: Vertex displacement: VERTEX.xz = rotate(VERTEX.xz, VERTEX.y * twist_rate) in shader, rotation matrix around Y-axis with angle proportional to height. Adding noise for organic erosion: VERTEX += NORMAL * noise(VERTEX). Animating the twist parameter creates breathing, living columns. The bernini_columns artifact implements the full technique.
Critical angle: Baroque was a glitch in Renaissance order — excess, movement, and emotion disrupting classical restraint. Bernini's twisted columns encode oscillation in stone, freezing a wave mid-dance. Procedural geometry is the new Baroque: favoring the variable, mutating, and complex over the static and ideal. The ElphabaDress artifact extends this to fabric — another medium where wave-driven displacement creates form.
Key artifacts: bernini_columns implements the solomonic spiral via vertex displacement; ElphabaDress extends wave-driven deformation to fabric; vowel_sound_board connects oscillation to vocal resonance; dark_sphere anchors the space.
