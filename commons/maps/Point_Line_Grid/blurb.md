What lets us return to the same place? A snapping sphere gives retained positions integer addresses; a walking recorder lets you move inside an interval or give its boundary a rhythm. Your released drawing returns at another scale. On a five-by-five plan, two indices can describe where a cube might stand.

A shared address can help pieces meet and people find one another. It does not decide what should be built, who may enter, or whether a line will carry a body. Those possibilities need further work.
