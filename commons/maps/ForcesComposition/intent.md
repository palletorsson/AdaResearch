Concept: Universal sandbox that computes all vector operations simultaneously on two draggable vectors — addition, subtraction, dot product, cross product, projection, reflection visible at once, revealing the relationships between operations.
Sequence role: Eleventh map in Forces; the consolidation map that closes the pure-vector sub-sequence before Forces_1 introduces Newton's laws. Every operation from maps 1–10 appears together, so the learner sees the full toolkit as a unified system rather than isolated techniques; follows Vectors_6.
Technical angle: Two adjustable vectors A and B with live computation of A+B, A-B, A·B (scalar readout), A×B (perpendicular arrow), proj_B(A), and refl(A) across a normal. The intermediate difficulty reflects that the learner must synthesize, not just recognize. The workbench format invites experimentation — drag A, watch everything update.
Critical angle: Seeing all operations simultaneously reveals that they are not independent tools but different lenses on the same pair of vectors. The dot product measures alignment, the cross product measures perpendicularity, addition measures combination, subtraction measures difference — four questions about two arrows. The sandbox format embodies the pedagogical thesis: understanding comes from manipulation, not memorization.
Key artifacts: VectorBasics and basis_vectors_rig provide the coordinate frame within which all operations are computed; dark_sphere anchors the space.
Gap: The workbench description mentions all operations but the map only has 3 artifacts — needs dedicated visual elements for dot product readout, cross product arrow, and projection/reflection lines to match the "all operations computed live" promise.
