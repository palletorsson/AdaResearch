# Ethics in AI

Twelve algorithms. One grid. Every one uses randomness differently — Gaussian distributions cluster around a center, random walks wander without destination, height maps stack chaos into terrain. Cubes rise, rotate, vanish. Profiles generate and regenerate. The 8×8 region becomes a stage where stochastic processes perform their logic in three dimensions.

Randomness is not the absence of structure. It is structure's raw material. A Gaussian curve is a bias disguised as chance. A random walk is a decision process with no memory. Remove enough cubes and pattern emerges from deletion. Add enough height variation and flat ground becomes landscape. The algorithms here do not simulate disorder — they reveal that order was never pure to begin with. Every grid square holds a coin flip that already changed the architecture.
