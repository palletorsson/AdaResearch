A processional hall with four stations and one question: what is a vector, before it is any particular arrow? Stand inside the coordinate frame and read your own address off three axes. Grab a free arrow and slide it anywhere — same direction, same length, same vector. Stretch the lantern and watch its glow grow with √(x²+y²+z²). Then divide a vector by its own length and keep only the which-way. Four ideas walked in order: a where, a which-way, a how-far, and a how-far refused. The diagram is on the wall; the arrow is in your hands.
