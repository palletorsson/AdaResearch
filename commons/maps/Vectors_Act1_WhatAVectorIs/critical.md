# What the vector leaves behind

A free displacement keeps a difference while discarding the two original addresses. Moving both pins can preserve that difference. Applying a force at a particular point is a different question: the point of application can change torque. Reuse has conditions, and this hall should not erase them.

The wall shows two projections of one measured displacement. Each drops a coordinate. The choice of world frame and the drawing window both belong to the representation. Clipping can preserve a direction while losing visible magnitude, so the numbers retain what the window cannot hold. The wall's sampling clock can also lag behind the hand.

Zero is another boundary. Normalisation uses a threshold around it; the preset controls choose a direction when there is none available from the measured vector. That convenience is declared. A useful interface can introduce a convention without disguising it as information contained in its input.

The force sequence will carry these questions into the room: which rule acts on a parcel, a wall or the player, and which capability is genuinely shared between those receivers? Similar arrows do not establish identical dynamics. The gameplay comparison must expose the connection in code and through movement.
