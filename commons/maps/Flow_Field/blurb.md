The slope arrows from the surface map have left the ground. They float now at every point in the space around you, each one saying which way and how hard at exactly its spot. This is a vector field: not a value at a point but a value everywhere, a function whose answer is a direction. Step anywhere and the field has already decided what happens there.

The `vector_field_grid` is the lattice of arrows for f(x,y) = (vx, vy). Dial through the presets and the whole field reorganizes: rotational, where the arrows circle a center; divergent, where they flee a source; wavy, where they ripple. The `science_screen` shows the function generating them. This is change-and-flow defined over space rather than time — the first map where the rule lives in the room itself, not in a moving thing. Nothing is flowing yet. The field is the instruction; the next map releases what obeys it.
