Slopes connect platforms at different heights. No stairs, no jumps — the floor tilts and carries you smoothly from one level to the next. The ramps wind through the room, turning corners, changing direction, always continuous.

The walkpath replaces the discrete step with the continuous slope. Elevation changes become gradual, embodied, felt in the legs rather than counted. Ramps say: the journey between levels matters as much as the levels themselves. Height is not a destination. It is a gradient.
