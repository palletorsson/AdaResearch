The julia_set_explorer allows real-time c-parameter variation, revealing the continuous deformation of fractal boundaries.
Critical angle: The Julia set is the lambda_edge made literal — it is the boundary between convergence and divergence, the frontier where orbits neither settle nor escape. Five characters (z=z^2+c) contain infinite complexity not as metaphor but as mathematical fact: the Julia set's boundary has infinite length, non-integer dimension, and encodes the complete dynamical history of every point in the complex plane. The shift from geometric construction to dynamical emergence marks a phase transition in the sequence's epistemology.
