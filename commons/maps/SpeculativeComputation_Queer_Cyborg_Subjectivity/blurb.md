A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 region inside it where algorithms perform. Gaussian distributions shape terrain. Random walks leave trails across cubes. Height maps ripple with stochastic variation. Objects appear, disappear, rotate — each operation driven by some flavor of chance. The arena stages randomness as material practice. A walker steps north, south, east, west — no plan, no memory, a coin flip repeated 128 times. Profiles generated from noise read like geological cross-sections. Cubes removed at random from rows and columns open negative space that looks composed. The grid receives every intervention without judgment. Randomness is not the absence of pattern. It is the condition under which pattern becomes surprising — even to the system that produced it.
