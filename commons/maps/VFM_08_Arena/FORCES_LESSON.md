# One impact, then the world

Primary: `force_play_bowling`.

Use the same point twice: one striker and one target. Give the striker one impulse, then let their collision unfold. Start without gravity or a floor so the first question is the impact.

Carry: An impulse happens once. In the last hall, hold a trigger to keep adding acceleration to your own movement.

Check: Double the striker’s mass while keeping the impulse. Predict its initial speed, then observe the collision separately. Restart each trial. A prediction about the launch speed does not yet tell you how the whole collision will end.
