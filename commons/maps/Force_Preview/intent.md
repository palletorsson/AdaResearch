Concept: A preview arena that gathers the three consolidated force experiences into one room — Storm Chamber (weather_vector_field), Mortar Range (mortar_vector_siege), and Vector Bounce Hall (reflection_hall) — so a walker can sample what the Forces arc will teach before the arc formally begins.
Sequence role: An early preview/orientation map in the Forces sequence. It shows the headline experiences without their full teaching scaffolding — a trailer for the sequence. The learner arrives with no force vocabulary and leaves with a felt sense of the three modes (field, projectile, reflection) that the later maps unpack rigorously. Low difficulty by design; the point is anticipation, not mastery.
Technical angle: Three station artifacts anchor the room — weather_vector_field (a flowing vector field driving motion), mortar_vector_siege (projectile launch with vector aiming), reflection_hall (vector reflection off surfaces). Supporting force toys fill the aisle: force_pad, force_vortex, human_catapult, stretch_bench, adder_board, agreement_gauge, length_lantern, and the XL laser-drag vector benches (vector_addition_xl_laser, vector_subtraction_xl_laser).
Critical angle: A preview that shows before it explains inverts the usual teach-then-demo order — it lets the body encounter forces as phenomena first and concepts second, matching the sequence truth that "acceleration is the only thing you feel." The risk is the trailer problem: spectacle without structure can leave the learner entertained but ungrounded, which is why the consolidated experiences here must point clearly forward to the maps that formalize them.
Key artifacts: weather_vector_field stages a force field as a storm to walk through; mortar_vector_siege previews projectile motion with vector aiming; reflection_hall previews vector reflection; the XL laser-drag benches (vector_addition_xl_laser, vector_subtraction_xl_laser) preview vector composition with grabbable endpoints; human_catapult and force_pad give immediate, body-scale force feedback.
Gap: As a preview the map deliberately omits explanation, but a single entry sign naming the three stations and the maps each one leads to would turn a sampler into a map of the journey. The dozen supporting toys risk crowding the three headline stations — thinning the aisle to foreground Storm / Mortar / Bounce would sharpen the preview.
