## The walk

Here the vectors come off the chalkboard and into the hands. The adder_board lets you stack displacements head-to-tail and watch the resultant appear; the XL lasers do addition and subtraction at room scale, drag-points on beams of light. The agreement_gauge reduces a whole geometry lecture to one scalar — how much do two directions concur. Then the room turns playful and honest at once: the human_catapult and the mortar_siege are vector addition with consequences, the force_vortex is a field you can lean into, the weather_vector_field is the sky doing the same mathematics without asking permission. The stretch_bench holds the quiet end — force as elongation, read off a rail.

## What it fixes

After this room, arrow diagrams are memories of things the body did. Addition is a walk you took twice; subtraction is the way back. The chapter banks the intuition here and spends it on laws next door.
