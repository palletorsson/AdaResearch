## The walk

The chapter opens with an organism that has no brain and solves mazes anyway. The PhysarumColony grows slime-mold tubes across the room — reinforcing the paths that carry flow, starving the ones that don't — and the network it settles into is, famously, the one a transit engineer would have drawn. The science_screen names the miracle without dispelling it: no cell here is in charge, and yet the colony *computes*.

## What it opens

The threshold plants the chapter's whole thesis in a single yellow blob: intelligence without a head. Physarum keeps no map — it keeps *the world*, thickening the tubes that work until the surviving network is the answer. This is stigmergy at the door: the environment is the message, memory stored outside the agent, in the trails it leaves. Every room after this one is a variation on the shock of watching coordination happen with nobody coordinating.
