# Color_Paint - Map Summary

## Overview
Color_Paint introduces color as physical action through a ball painting demonstration. Players interact with paint physics, applying color through thrown projectiles rather than menu selection. Color becomes gestural - the result of motion, force, and impact.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 11×13 grid workspace
- **Architecture**: Open floor with corner lighting
- **Height**: Single level with transport cube access
- **Structure**: Laboratory space for painting experiments

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **ball_painting_demo:180** (5,6) - Primary paint physics demonstration
- **dark_sphere** (5,5) - Ambient atmosphere dome

### Utilities
- **Extra lights** (el) - Corner workspace illumination (×4)
- **Next cube** (n) - Progression trigger at (5,1)
- **Transport cube** (tc:4:y) - Vertical access at (7,3)
- **Spawn point** (s) - Player start position
- **Teleporter** (t) - Exit at (9,11)

## Atmosphere
- **Lighting**: Cool ambient with warm directional
- **Background**: Sky blue
- **Mood**: Playful, experimental, physical

## Learning Sequence
1. Player enters painting lab
2. Approaches ball painting demo
3. Selects paint colors
4. Throws paint at target balls
5. Observes color accumulation on surfaces
6. Experiments with mixing through layered throws
7. Uses transport cube for different angles
8. Exits via teleporter

## Design Intent
The map transforms color application from **interface operation to physical gesture**. Throwing paint engages the body differently than clicking a color picker. Color becomes the result of action in space - trajectory, force, aim. The painting demo connects digital color to physical intuitions about paint.
