# PG: Delaunay Triangulation

Three points define a triangle. The Delaunay condition demands that no fourth point falls inside any triangle's circumcircle — the unique circle passing through all three vertices. This single constraint, applied everywhere, produces the "best" triangulation of any point set: maximum minimum angles, no sliver triangles, no degenerate edges.

Drop points. Watch triangles form. The circumcircle property enforces a kind of spatial fairness — every triangle as fat as possible, every connection justified by geometric democracy. Flip an edge and the mesh restructures. Add a point and locality ripples outward.

Delaunay is the dual of Voronoi. Territory and connection, the same data read two ways. One divides space into regions of nearest belonging. The other connects neighbors into structure. Ownership and relation — inseparable operations on the same point set.
