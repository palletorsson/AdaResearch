## The walk

The field becomes census. The voxelnoise piece samples 3D noise on a grid and votes each cell into existence or absence by threshold — the invisible correlation structure of the field suddenly standing there as architecture, clumps and caverns where the values run high or low together. The perlin_terrain_sculptor opens the same machinery as an instrument: octaves, persistence, lacunarity, scale — four dials that decide what kind of world the field is willing to be.

## What it fixes

The walk makes the chapter's abstraction countable. Every clump in the voxel field is a *visible act of memory* — cells agreeing with their neighbors — and turning the persistence dial changes how far the agreement reaches. This is also the room where the thread shakes hands with the isosurfaces chapter next door: same fields, same thresholds, blockier honesty. The voxels show the votes; marching cubes will later smooth them into skin.
