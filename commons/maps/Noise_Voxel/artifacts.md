# Noise Voxel — four primary works

All four authored artifacts are retained and have passages in final.md. Their order is also recorded in artifact_roles.json and the noise book data.

## Perlin Terrain Sculptor

`perlin_terrain_sculptor` at (13,7), on a plinth. The 24³ lattice source offers THRESHOLD, CELL, SEED and view-only CUT. The cased readout identifies one field value, vertical bias, threshold, occupancy, component count and largest piece.

## Science Screen

`science_screen` at (18,4), with a floor stand and explicit voxel_slice mode. It reads the actual local source and displays the selected horizontal score layer beside its stored decisions. It does not invent a replacement field when the source is absent.

## Voxel Noise

`voxelnoise` at (16,24), in volume mode. A 28 m glass flight enclosure surrounds a 24 m cube domain, supported by a 24.4 m stage. Its 24³ cells match the model at twenty-five times the physical spacing, with one-metre cells. Exposed cell faces provide both visible geometry and collision. It now shares the bench's orientation. CELL moves a yellow horizontal perimeter and selected-address outline here, matching the screen without rebuilding the volume. Occupied connectivity does not establish player traversal.

## Dark Sphere

`dark_sphere` at (5,8). The original orb retains its independent rotation, sine-driven emission and halo. It lets the chapter compare binary occupancy with an earlier way to receive a varying scalar.

The hall expands to 33 × 44 m. Recovered interior structure and utility tokens are retained with a nine-cell x offset; the old end wall moves to the new end. The model and screen keep their relative positions, while the volume moves into the flight court and the orb remains outside it. Actual rendered images and the review are at `/research/possible-bodies/noise-voxel.html`; the original artifact page is preserved in the dated review snapshot.
