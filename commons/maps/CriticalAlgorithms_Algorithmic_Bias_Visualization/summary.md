# Algorithmic Bias Visualization — Summary

CriticalAlgorithms_Algorithmic_Bias_Visualization opens the Post-Foundations Crisis sequence. It makes algorithmic bias architectural before it is mathematical. The room is split unevenly by a tall wall: the left half is spacious and comfortable, the right half is cramped, and the same population is meant to be housed in both. The learner walks the division as a condition before any artifact explains it.

At the centre of the spacious half stands the bias_visualizer — a rotatable 3D point cloud of word embeddings. Analogy pairs are drawn on the cloud as lines: man-to-doctor paired with woman-to-nurse, white-to-approved paired with black-to-denied. Geometric distance in the embedding encodes statistical co-occurrence in the training data. The lines make the structure of the dataset visible; the wall next to them makes the consequence of that structure spatial.

A waypoint at the midpoint of the room directs the learner's gaze toward the wall. A void at one edge of the wall provides the exit — a gap in the floor where the classification system runs out of ground to stand on. Crossing into the cramped half is optional; the constrained geometry is felt even from outside.

Within the sequence, Bias_Visualization is the diagnosis. The map argues that every classifier has an outside, and that the people misread by these systems are not errors but the constitutive outside. SpeculativeComputation_Rhizome_Network will next offer a different topology.
