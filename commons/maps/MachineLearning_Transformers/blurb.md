# Transformers

A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 region in the center where algorithms perform. Gaussian distributions sculpt terrain into bell curves. Random walks trace drunk paths across cells, raising geometry with each step. Cubes appear, rotate, vanish — selected by dice rolls, not design. Height maps ripple with noise. Profiles generate from nothing but a seed and a range.

Every algorithm here abandons control. The random walk doesn't know where it's going. The Gaussian doesn't choose its peak — sigma does. RemoveRandom erases without preference. The grid becomes a theater of structured unpredictability, where rules constrain but never determine. Kitbashing as computational method: pieces assembled not by intention but by probability.

Randomness isn't the absence of order. It's order's underground — the substrate from which pattern emerges without being commanded.
