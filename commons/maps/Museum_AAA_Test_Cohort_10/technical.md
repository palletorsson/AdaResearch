# Technical contract

Each artifact owns a canonical dressing-room configuration containing its measured body, hard clearance, preferred room, support dependency, circulation mode, and ideal anchor. The floorplan offers space and support; the placement negotiator may accept, move, grow, shrink, rotate, or apply a declared wall exception. It must reject an offer that cannot satisfy the hard zone.

The generated map contains ten rooms on a one-metre grid, one connected visitor route, one spawn, and one exit teleporter. The pathfinder reaches 357 of 415 walkable cells and passes the map. Three raised-platform artifact anchors produce advisory height warnings; their surrounding visitor routes remain connected.

Promotion remains gated by runtime measurement, capture, visual seating, interaction, negotiation, and map reachability. The pickup-cube repair demonstrates the separation: its museum placement disables bobbing through `artifact_config` and corrects the local support offset, while the scene's default behavior remains unchanged elsewhere.
