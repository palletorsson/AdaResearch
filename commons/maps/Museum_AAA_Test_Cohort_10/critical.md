# A standard that can repair failure

A production system becomes untrustworthy when every generated room is described as successful. This gallery exposed a real failure: the Pick Up Cube's room fit, its support type was correct, and its route was clear, yet the body floated above the table. The repair belongs to the artifact–support seam too: the placement config can now request a stable pickup and seat it without rewriting the artifact's behavior everywhere else.

The museum contract therefore separates spatial acceptance from curatorial promotion. Negotiation can prove that a room is possible; capture can still falsify the result; a revised contract can then be tested again. The visitor's body, the artifact's behavior, and the institution's claim of quality must all agree.
