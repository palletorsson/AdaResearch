# Museum AAA Phi — Critical reflection

## The core tension

A control can look self-contained while depending on an institution to decide where the hand may meet it. Phi makes that support visible.

## The body and its host

The rail is not enlarged into false monumentality. Instead, the museum supplies a working surface and accepts responsibility for access, height, and the empty space in front of it.

## QFEP connection

Phi names becoming: a parameter through which forms may drift, intensify, or stabilize. Here becoming is not disembodied. It starts with a situated hand, a host surface, and a negotiated right to occupy space.
