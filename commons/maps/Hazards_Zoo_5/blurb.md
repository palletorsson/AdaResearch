# Hazards Zoo — Physics & Nature

Ten creatures built from laws. A ribbon of boxes rides a sine wave — frequency climbs toward resonance, then fires. Five tethered blocks crash and bounce, impulse arrows tracing momentum's conservation. A 3×3 grid of masses connected by springs deforms under Hooke's law, tension converted to damage. Six limbs demonstrate six joint types: hinge, ball, slider, revolute, prismatic, fixed. Physics made ambulatory.

Then the living systems. A vine grows from axiom "F" through L-system grammar, branching toward the player. A hydra splits on damage — cut one head, two grow back, fractal to depth three. A Fibonacci spiral spawns nodes at the golden angle, each 0.618× the last. Conway's Game of Life tiles a walker's torso as armor. A hive releases boids that flock on three rules.

Every physical law is a body. Every growth pattern is a weapon. The equation doesn't describe the creature — the creature *is* the equation.
