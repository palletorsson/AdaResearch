## The cast

## The walk

The standard chamber, and in the foundational chapter it reads as the plainest room in the whole project. The becoming_catalyst rests on its pedestal; the catalyst_target marks a cell; the proximity_spawner keys the space to your approach. You take the bracelet, and the first thing it lets you do is put a cube where there was nothing.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to place**. Every chapter downstream — the swarm's trail, the L-system's rewrite, the fractal's nesting — is a rule about *what to place next*; this room hands you the act those rules act upon, stripped of any rule at all. A point is position without extension, the chapter's truth says, and everything is built from nothing — so the catalyst gives you the nothing and the position, and asks you to commit the first primitive: a block in empty space, an origin chosen. You become (0,0,0). The whole curriculum is downstream of the gesture this room isolates, which is why it belongs at the base of the spine: before symmetry, before transformation, before generation, someone has to place the first thing, and here that someone is you.
