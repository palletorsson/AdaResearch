Noise controlling noise. The output of one function becomes the input coordinate of another. Warped, distorted, folded space. What was smooth becomes turbulent. What was regular becomes organic. Domain warping: when the map itself is noise-transformed.

The `noisesphere` artifact wraps domain-warped noise onto a sphere, showing how coordinate distortion compounds with surface curvature to produce marble-like, turbulent patterns. Beside it, `dark_sphere` sits un-warped — same topology, no transformation, making the effect legible by contrast. Domain warping is noise reflecting on itself — recursion without base case, distortion without origin. Given the output, you cannot recover the input coordinates. Information is not lost but scrambled beyond practical recovery. Irreversible entropy in the computational sense: structured disorder that remembers its process while concealing its starting point. Nature is the accumulation of transformations whose origins are no longer legible.
