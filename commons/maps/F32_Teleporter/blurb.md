A small room. A void at the center with a glowing pad. Step onto it and you are somewhere else — no corridor, no transition, no in-between. The exit is instantaneous and non-spatial.

The teleporter abolishes distance. It is the one architectural element that violates Euclidean space: two points connected without a path between them. Every map ends here, at the seam between one world and the next. The threshold is not a door. It is a discontinuity.
