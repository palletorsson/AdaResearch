A cathedral-style gallery showcasing all vector mathematics demonstrations.

## Layout

Walk through the central nave, with alcoves on either side displaying vector concepts in pedagogical progression:

### Left Alcoves (Core Operations)
1. **Basics** — Magnitude, direction, normalization
2. **Subtraction** — Relative displacement, adding negatives
3. **Cross Product** — Perpendicular vectors, surface normals
4. **Forces** — Newton's laws, force accumulation
5. **Torque** — Rotational effects, moment arms

### Right Alcoves (Applications)
1. **Addition** — Tip-to-tail, resultant vectors
2. **Dot Product** — Angles, alignment, projection
3. **Projection/Reflection** — Surface interactions
4. **Motion** — Kinematics, velocity/acceleration
5. **Field Flow** — Vector fields, particle advection

## Interaction

Each alcove contains:
- A **teleporter** (step through to enter the full demonstration)
- A **preview artifact** showing the concept at small scale
- A **3D label** identifying the operation

## Design Notes

The exhibition uses the **Progression Line** spatial pattern:
- Central walkway (height 1) for traversal
- Raised alcoves (height 2) for each exhibit
- Walls (height 2-3) creating intimate showcase spaces
- Cathedral proportions — narrow and deep

Entry at north, exit at south. The player descends through increasingly advanced concepts, from basic magnitude to field flows.
