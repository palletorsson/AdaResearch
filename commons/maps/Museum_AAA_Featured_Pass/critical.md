# Museum AAA Featured Pass — Critical reflection

## The core tension

A museum can make difference legible while quietly forcing every object into the same institutional posture. This pass refuses one universal bay.

## Negotiated coexistence

The pedestal, wall console, host-mounted rail, and low stage retain different rules. The corridor connects them without flattening their needs into an average footprint.

## QFEP connection

The exhibition begins with order, disputes calibration, situates becoming on a material host, and ends with plural form. Integration is not sameness: it is a route through differences that remain materially consequential.
