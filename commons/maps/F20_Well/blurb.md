Tall walls surround you on all sides. Five levels high, closing in, with a single low platform at the center. Look up: sky. Look down: the well floor, intimate and enclosed. The space contracts attention inward and downward.

The well is the inverse of the cathedral. Where the cathedral lifts your gaze, the well draws it down into depth. Tall enclosure without escape creates focus through confinement. The oculus effect — a narrow opening above a deep shaft — makes the sky feel precious and the ground feel close. Descent as concentration.
