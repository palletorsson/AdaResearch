A torus is a surface with no edges. Take a flat grid, connect left to right, top to bottom — the rectangle becomes a donut. Every cell has neighbors in every direction. Nothing is boundary. Nothing is corner.

This pad holds the `combine_torus` station at its origin and a dark sphere pulsing slow violet at center-grid. Navigate the 7×10 platform. Move along X, move along Y. Watch how two axes compose a plane, how a plane folds into a closed surface. The sphere breathes in place — geometry held in rotation, dimension made ambient.

Two-dimensional arrays map flat memory into rows and columns. But wrap those edges and flatness disappears. The grid that seemed finite becomes continuous. No wall to hit, no margin to fall off. Structure without boundary — the first topology that refuses to end.
