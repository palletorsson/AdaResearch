## The walk

One artifact, holding a paradox: a graph has no shape. Vertices and edges are pure relation — the same graph can be drawn a thousand ways and remain itself. The force_directed_layout resolves the embarrassment physically: edges become springs, vertices repel like charges, and the network is released to negotiate its own body. It shivers, unfolds, settles. Clusters find each other; bridges stretch thin between them.

## What it teaches

The primitive is the gap between a structure and its picture. Layout is not decoration — it is an *algorithm's opinion* about what the relations mean, and force-direction is the opinion that related things belong near each other. Watching the graph settle, you see abstraction re-entering space: the squint of the threshold room running in reverse, dots and lines growing a geography again.
