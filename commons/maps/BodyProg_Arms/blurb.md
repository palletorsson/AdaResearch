Inverse kinematics connects the dots. Your floating hands now have elbows, shoulders, a chain of bones that bends to reach where you point. TwoBoneIK3D — two bones, one target, one solver.

The demo shows it without VR: two arms trace figure-8 patterns in space, joints bending and flipping as targets move through their reachable envelope. The boundary between self and world is not the skin — it is the maximum reach of the IK chain.
