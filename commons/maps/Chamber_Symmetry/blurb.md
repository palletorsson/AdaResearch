The chamber's verb is *to repeat*.

A grid waits in the screen. You take the bracelet and place one block — and the room answers by repeating it, carrying your motif across the plane by the group's rule until the field is full. You authored one cell; the wallpaper authored the rest.

You cannot place the whole pattern here. Only its seed. That is the lesson planted in the body: the Alhambra's artisans never drew every tile — they chose the one tile that generates everything and trusted the group to finish. To repeat is to author a world by authoring its smallest unrepeated part.
