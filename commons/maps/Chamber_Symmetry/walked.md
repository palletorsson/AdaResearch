## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to repeat**. The chapter taught that a pattern is not a picture but a rule performed — and that there are exactly seventeen ways a flat pattern can fill a plane, a census the Alhambra's artisans exhausted before group theory could count it. Here you enter the rule from the inside: you place the fundamental domain, the one tile that generates everything, and the symmetry operations carry it outward until the plane is full. You cannot place the whole pattern — only the seed of it — and that is the lesson planted in the body: the artisan's power was never to draw every tile but to choose the one, and trust the group. To repeat is to author a world by authoring its smallest unrepeated part.
