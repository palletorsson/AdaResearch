# PG: Branching Growth

A seed point extends. At each step, the line decides — continue or split. Angle, length, probability. Three parameters produce trees, lightning, rivers, lungs. The algorithm is simple: grow forward, sometimes fork. Repeat.

Branching growth is recursion made visible. Each fork carries the memory of every fork before it. A trunk becomes two limbs becomes four branches becomes eight twigs — exponential proliferation from a single rule applied to itself. The tree doesn't know its shape. The shape emerges from the repetition.

Change one parameter and coral becomes bronchi. Tighten the angle and roots become cracks in ice. The same engine of divergence runs through every system that solves space by splitting. Identity that maintains itself not through permanence but through continuous, irreversible decision. Every branch point is a commitment that cannot be taken back.
