# Point Lines — connection, measure and a prescribed route

Fourth hall in Primitives, after Point_Coordinates and before Point_Trace. The 27 × 63-cell map enters at (13, 0) and exits at (13, 62).

1. Twelve small grabbable points in the first corridor. Release two near one another to create a segment; moving either end changes it. More points can enter the same local graph. No barrier is linked to their connection.
2. The four-metre black measurement enclosure stands as a monolith in the larger room at (13, 11). Its doors remain usable; space around it stays open.
3. Ten tape barriers, two in each of five alternating rows, form a zigzag queue.
4. The queue feeds the three-X/three-plus passage. Damage and healing retain their visible and audible consequences. The gallery is reached on its far side.
5. The remaining line studies occupy that gallery: marks, traces, pair relations, perspective, measures, laser and other manipulable examples. WALK THIS LINE is seated on the floor at its new location.

The final text follows this order. The introductory making lesson still isolates two positions, displacement, length and a rendered segment; its new extension introduces the pairing field without requiring a beginner to reconstruct the entire graph manager first.

Installed-museum tests cover desktop/XR pickup, line persistence, a blocked straight shortcut and a complete walked zigzag into the damage/healing passage. Teleportation and jumping remain separate movement capabilities for headset evaluation.
