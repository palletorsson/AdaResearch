# Point Lines — Artifacts
*Primitives: Points Build Worlds · F_order · 20 artifacts*

> A line connects two points. Two lines cross — the X marks intersection. Parallel lines organize direction. Then: perspective, scale, the grid. Lines become measure. Measure becomes pleasure — the satisfaction of knowing distance, the elegance of proportion. Measure is also violence. What doesn't fit the grid is remainder, error, erased. The line inherits this: efficient, relentless, forgetting everything but endpoints.
