A flower is not one thing. It is petals (attract pollinators), stamens (produce pollen), pistil (receive pollen), sepals (protect the bud), and stem (hold it all up). Each part is procedural — generated from parameters, not modeled by hand.

The BotanicalFlower artifact breaks the bloom into its components. Rotate it, zoom in, see how petal count and stamen arrangement follow mathematical patterns. Taxonomy as boundary-making: naming the parts is the first step to generating them.
