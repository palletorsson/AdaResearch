# BSP Tree Partitions

Binary Space Partitioning. One plane bisects a volume. Each half gets bisected again. Recursion produces rooms from nothing — no architect, no blueprint, just repeated division until the space is habitable. The algorithm doesn't build. It *separates*.

Walk through a room that made itself. Walls appear where hyperplanes fell. Random profiles carve elevation; scattered objects populate chambers the tree decided were sufficient. Every doorway is a split that chose axis and position, then forgot why. The geometry is a fossil record of decisions — each partition a binary question answered and buried in structure.

BSP trees don't create space. They destroy unity. Architecture as recursive negation — what remains after enough cuts is called a room. Identity through separation. Shelter through loss.
