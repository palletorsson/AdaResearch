# PG: 16-Cell Net

The tesseract has a dual. Sixteen vertices become eight. Eight cubic cells become sixteen tetrahedral ones. The 16-cell — hexadecachoron — is the 4D analog of the octahedron. Same dimension, opposite geometry. Where the tesseract builds from cubes, the 16-cell builds from tetrahedra. Dual means: swap vertices for cells, cells for vertices. The mirror operation of polytope space.

Here the net is unfolded — sixteen tetrahedra laid flat into 3D, hinged along shared faces. A 4D object dissected into something walkable. Every fold line is a dimension waiting to close.

Duality says: for every structure, another structure encodes it exactly — inside out. Order doesn't have one shape. It has at least two.
