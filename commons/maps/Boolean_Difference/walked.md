## The walk

The chapter's most useful operator, and its most haunted. The csg_difference_demo subtracts a sphere from a box — A−B — and the box is left carrying a perfect spherical hollow, remembering by absence a thing that is no longer there. Walk around it and the cavity reads as a presence, a shaped nothing with an edge as exact as any solid's.

## What it fixes

The walk fixes the chapter's deepest idea: that form can be defined by removal. Difference is the operator of every window, every socket, every carved bowl and drilled hole — subtract precisely and what remains is shaped entirely by what you took away, the void a negative portrait of the tool. This inverts the naive picture of making as accumulation; here creation is *excavation*, and the sculptor's old claim about releasing the figure from the marble becomes a literal Boolean. The room teaches you to see hollows as authored — that an opening is not an accident in a solid but a deliberate subtraction, and that the shape of an absence is a design decision as real as any wall.
