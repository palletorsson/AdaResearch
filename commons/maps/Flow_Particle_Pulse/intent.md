Concept: Particles released into a vector field follow its streamlines. Flow made felt as motion — each particle reads the local direction and moves, and the swarm's paths trace the field's structure.
Sequence role: Seventh map of Change, flow cluster. The payoff of Flow_Field: the static instruction becomes visible behavior. Reuses vector_field_grid and its presets so the same field can be seen empty (prior map) and inhabited (here).
Technical angle: Integrating particle positions through the field over time (each step advances a particle along the local vector), emitting a pulse of particles, and letting the trajectories accumulate into streamlines. This is numerical integration of an ODE, the same machinery forces will use for motion.
Critical angle: Rule revealed by what obeys it. The particles carry no knowledge of the generating function — they respond only to the arrow underfoot — yet collectively they draw the whole field. Local rule, global pattern: the move that runs under swarms, flocking, and noise throughout the curriculum.
Key artifacts: vector_field_grid supplies the field and presets; particle_flow_swarm is the released pulse whose paths trace the streamlines.
