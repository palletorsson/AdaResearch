The field was a room full of instructions with nothing to obey them. Now you release the particles. They drop into the arrows and start to move — not pushed by you, but carried by the field, each one reading the direction at its spot and going. Watch long enough and the paths reveal the streamlines, the hidden grain of the flow that the static arrows only hinted at.

The `vector_field_grid` returns from the last map, still holding its presets. The `particle_flow_swarm` is what you release into it — a pulse of motion that makes the rule visible as behavior. In a rotational field they spiral; in a divergent one they scatter; in a wavy one they undulate. This is flow felt as motion-through-rule. The particles do not know the function. They only know the arrow they are standing on, and that is enough to draw the whole shape of the field with their bodies.
