# Hazards Zoo — Origami & Rollers

A bestiary entrance. Waterbomb tessellations roll across the floor as compressed spheres, then bloom into rigid-foldable flowers — every triangle face holding its shape through the unfolding. Miura-ori sheets lie flat, nearly invisible, until they pop upward and crawl like inchworms through a single degree of freedom. Kresling cylinders twist from disc to sniper tower. Kaleidocycles tumble through themselves in infinite rotation, each face-color triggering a different attack. Scissor linkages extend three times their compact length in a single pounce.

These are not animated models. The geometry is computed from crease patterns and kinematic constraints. Folds are real. Faces stay rigid. Deployment parameters drive the math that drives the mesh.

Every creature here is a compressed form waiting to unfold — and the thing it becomes depends entirely on what meets it.
