# Museum AAA Platonic — Summary

## Overview

A low island stage for the nine-object `platonicsolids` family. The visitor can complete a perimeter orbit and use the ramp to inspect the stage directly.

## Spatial layout

- **Map:** 23 × 17 one-metre cells
- **Dressing room:** 11 × 7 m
- **Measured geometry:** approximately 6.7 × 2.3 × 1.1 m
- **Posture:** low platform with cordon and ramp
- **Variant:** `staging=bare`, `palette=trans`

The artifact’s legacy 10 m rainbow floor and preview lights are disabled. The museum supplies the stage, route, light, and boundary.

## Visitor sequence

The visitor sees the complete family on approach, chooses either orbit direction, may ascend by the walkable prism, and exits toward the synthesis gallery.
