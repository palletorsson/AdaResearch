Columns divide the wall into bays. One column makes a door frame. Two make a window. A row makes a colonnade. Rhythm emerges from repetition — ABABAB or ABCBA — and the facade comes alive through the interplay of solid and void.

Bilateral symmetry centers the composition. The entrance bay is wider, flanked by narrower windows, mirrored left and right. Every classical facade you have ever seen follows this logic: a center, a rhythm, a grammar of spacing.
