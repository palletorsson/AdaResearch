A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 arena at its center. Flowers grow here — not planted, but evolved. Each generation tested, scored, culled. The survivors reproduce. Mutation introduces drift. Selection applies pressure. What emerges was never designed.

Evolutionary algorithms compress deep time into iteration loops. Fitness functions replace environments. Crossover replaces sex. The flowers don't know they're beautiful — beauty is a side effect of optimization against a metric someone chose. Change the metric, change the species.

Watch the population converge. Watch it get stuck. Introduce more randomness and watch it escape local optima — disorder as rescue, not failure. The arena doesn't find the best flower. It finds the flower that survives the arena.
