Concept: Facade_Baroque is the *dramatized actualization* of the facade assembly principle. Where Facade_Classical applies the six moves cleanly at their canonical settings, Baroque applies the same six moves with the dial turned past comfort — syncopated bay rhythm, broken pediments, paired columns clustering toward a projecting central pavilion, surfaces alternating projection and recess, rustication interrupted by smooth carving in the centre. Every axis is pushed; the rule survives the push and becomes legible *because* it's being stressed.

Actualizes: Facade_Assembly_Principle, in the *dramatized* mode. The map takes a position in the virtual field where the rhythm axis is held at "syncopated regular" (a beat that has *almost* a regular cadence but emphasises its central bay through grouped pairs), the hierarchy axis is at "emphasised through depth" (the centre projects forward), and the ornament axis is at "maximum within the grammar." Bernini's St. Peter's colonnade is the canonical reference — the rule held even at its most theatrically stressed application.

Sequence role: Third map of the facade_assembly branch (after Facade_Assembly_Principle and Facade_Classical). The principle's first deviation; sets up the player's reading of *what the grammar accommodates when pushed*. Leads forward to Facade_Venetian_Gothic (hybridized with another grammar entirely) and Facade_Rustication (surface emphasised over ornament).

Technical angle: A seven-bay facade where the centre three bays project forward as a pavilion. The flanking two bays on each side step back. Paired columns flank the projecting bay's openings; single columns at the wings. The central second-storey window receives a broken pediment (a curved/arched cornice that dramatically interrupts itself, allowing a sculptural detail to occupy the broken space). Bay rhythm reads as ABBB-A-BBBA — not a flat ABABABA, not a chaotic randomness, a *grouped* rhythm where the middle is emphasised by clustering. Pass-2 build: room walks today as 11x6x3 with spawn and teleporter; the projecting-bay artifacts (column_paired_corinthian, broken_pediment, projecting_pavilion, rustication_smooth_interrupted) are queued for pass-3 artifact authoring.

Critical angle: Baroque is the principle *demonstrating its theatrical capacity*. The Counter-Reformation Church needed facades that emoted — projected authority, dramatic depth, sculptural urgency. The grammar accommodated. This is important for the player's reading of the curriculum's later refusals: when Memphis Totem refuses hierarchy, it is refusing *what Baroque maximised*. When Superstudio refuses bay rhythm, it is refusing *what Baroque made theatrical*. The dramatized mode is not "rule-breaking dressed as ornamentation" — it is *the rule operated at intensity*. The deep critique Baroque enables is recognizing how much ideological weight architectural grammar can carry without breaking — and asking whether that weight should be there at all.
