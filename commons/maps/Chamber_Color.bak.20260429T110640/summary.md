# Chamber Color — Summary

Chamber_Color is the catalyst chamber for the Color sequence. It is the last map before the learner returns to the Lab. The chamber turns colour from a property the previous maps studied into a channel the learner uses to communicate with a creature.

The chamber is small and lit by coloured bursts rather than by ambient light. A kaleidocycle_enemy paces the floor, cycling through four attack faces: fire, ice, spike, and shield. Each face exposes a different vulnerability. The learner holds the chromatic catalyst, which projects bursts in a chosen hue. Red provokes the fire face; blue settles the shield; green turns the spike aside; yellow enters the ice face and changes the creature's tempo.

A miura_crawler watches from a corner, no longer hostile after earlier sequences. It does not participate in the exchange but its presence frames the chamber as a meeting rather than a fight. The science screen on the wall scatters the events across a chromatic axis so the learner can read the creature's grammar as frequency rather than as personality.

Within the sequence, Chamber_Color completes the arc the Color sequence began. In Color_Flashlight, colour stopped being a property of objects; here it completes its transformation into communication. The chamber hands the learner back to the Lab with the chromatic catalyst in their kit.
