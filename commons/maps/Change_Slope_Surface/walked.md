## The cast

## The walk

One artifact, room-sized: a terrain whose every point knows its two slopes. Walk east and feel ∂/∂x; walk north and feel ∂/∂y; walk any other way and feel the mix you chose. The partial derivative stops being notation and becomes topography — the mountain's opinion about each of your options. The science_screen flattens the surface you're standing on into its graph, so the room and its mathematics face each other.

## What it fixes

Slope was a number; here it becomes a *field of numbers*, two per footfall. The gradient — where the terrain pushes steepest — is learned by the calves before the eyes. The chapter banks this: when flow arrives later, you will already know that space can have an opinion.
