# Color Pillar — field, sample and collection

The primary encounter is `pillarcolorcollection` in its spatial-gradient configuration, followed by the wall-mounted `color_sets_overview`. A coordinate field continues between thirty-five embodied samples; twelve authored palette collections offer another way to decide what belongs together. The question is what the collection makes present and what its gaps leave open.

Keep the field still while the visitor moves: follow increasing saturation, cross into another hue, then look up into increasing value. A full-value palette forms a ceiling at the pillar tops, with a near-black floor below. A hand-scale pickable sampler shows the same selected colour in RGB and HSV, with a marker at the represented field height; a zero-saturation reference explains why value 1 is not always white. Finally carry a named wall swatch into another company. The sampler changes with its position; the wall swatch retains its assigned value.

The existing mixing, spectrum and stick artifacts retain their secondary roles; the dark sphere retains its decoration role. All remain available in the room. Their relevance should be tested through encounters rather than removed to meet a quota. The first guided route remains field → sample → named collection, preparing the writeable address in `Color_Grid_Pallet`.
