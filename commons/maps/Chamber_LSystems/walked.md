## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to rewrite**. An L-system never adds from nothing — it takes what is written and replaces it, generation by generation, and the chamber hands that operation to the walker: every block you place rewrites the room's text, and the rewritten room is the axiom your next act derives from. You are the production rule. The chapter's truth completes itself in the person: a sentence can become a forest because someone keeps applying the rule — and leaving the chamber, you are the rule the curriculum applies to its next room.
