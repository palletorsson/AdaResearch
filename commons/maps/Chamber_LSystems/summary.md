# Chamber LSystems — Summary

Chamber_LSystems is the catalyst chamber for the L-Systems sequence. The chamber stages grammar as a shared language: the learner's projectiles grow according to one L-system, and the creature grows according to another, so the encounter is a botanical dialogue rather than a fight.

The chamber holds a branching_vine creature. Its body is generated by an L-system whose production rules match the vines the learner encountered earlier in the sequence. The learner holds the branching catalyst. Each shot is a short axiom that expands recursively on impact — tendrils that bud, fork, and fork again according to a fixed rewrite rule.

The vine responds by running its own grammar in return. Where the learner's tendrils approach, the vine sprouts new laterals whose branch angles mirror the incoming structure. Where the learner's growth thins out, the vine lets its own growth slow. The two grammars write into the same volume, and the resulting geometry belongs to neither side alone.

A miura_crawler watches from the edge of the chamber, curious rather than hostile. A science screen on the wall traces the rewrite history of both grammars in parallel: production rules applied, depth reached, points where the two lineages intersect. The screen's label names the chamber's mode as syntactic collaboration rather than combat.

Within the sequence, Chamber_LSystems closes L-Systems by converting the formal pipeline of axioms, rules, and interpretation into a relationship with a creature that runs the same kind of pipeline. The chamber hands the learner back to the Lab with the branching catalyst and a small botanical record.
