## The cast

breathing room · cloth flags · trampoline · ball pit

## The walk

Walls breathe. Flags dance. A trampoline catches what falls. Soft bodies do not merely exist in space — they **negotiate** it, and every surface here pushes back, deforms, recovers, or doesn't. The breathing room pulses: corridors whose walls expand and contract on their own rhythm, so passage is a matter of *timing* rather than force. Cloth flags ripple under a wind they cannot see, pinned at one edge and free at every other. Balls drop into a pit and press against each other, each one yielding to its neighbor. You learn the sequence's third verb after deform and rotate: to *meet* — soft matter arriving at hard geometry and working out terms.

## The turn (critical)

Obstacles expose the quietly political dimension of soft-body physics: **who yields?** In rigid-body physics, collision is instantaneous resolution — two facts hit and a rule decides the outcome in a single frame. In soft-body physics, collision is a *negotiation that unfolds over time*: the material wraps, compresses, rebounds, and searches for an equilibrium that neither party fully dictated. The breathing room turns this into an ethic you walk through — the environment itself keeps changing, and the body must continuously re-negotiate its shape to pass, so adaptation stops being a metaphor and becomes the literal condition of movement. This is the integration phase saying, in physics, what the critical chapters will say in theory: hardness resolves by force; softness resolves by relation, and relation takes time.
