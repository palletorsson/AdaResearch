Concept: Walls breathe, flags dance, a trampoline catches what falls — soft bodies negotiate space by deforming around, through, and against rigid obstacles. This is the collision map: the moment soft matter meets hard geometry and the negotiation between compliance and constraint becomes visible.
Sequence role: Third map in Soft Bodies (13th spine sequence, integration phase), beginner difficulty. After deformation (map 1) and rotation (map 2), this map introduces obstacle interaction — soft bodies responding to static and dynamic rigid geometry. Connects forward to SoftBodies_Obsticals_Part2 where the obstacle gallery expands to twelve new configurations; follows SoftBodies_Carusell.
Technical angle: Collision response between soft bodies and rigid surfaces using penalty forces or constraint projection. The breathing_room artifact animates obstacle geometry (walls that expand and contract), forcing the soft body to continuously re-adapt. The flagdancer applies wind force to a cloth constrained at one edge — aerodynamic drag on a spring-mass surface. Trampoline physics involves elastic collision with a deformable surface, where both the falling object and the surface participate in energy exchange.
Critical angle: Obstacles reveal the political dimension of soft bodies: who yields? In rigid body physics, collision is instantaneous resolution. In soft body physics, collision is a negotiation that unfolds over time — the material wraps, compresses, rebounds, finds equilibrium. The breathing room is a metaphor for adaptive systems: the environment changes, and the body must continuously re-negotiate its shape.
Key artifacts: breathing_room demonstrates soft body response to dynamically changing obstacle geometry; flagdancer shows cloth-wind interaction with fixed-edge constraints; grab_long_stick and pick_up_cube provide interactive manipulation within the obstacle field.
Gap: Needs a collision response mode selector — an artifact that toggles between penalty-based and constraint-based collision handling on the same scenario, making the algorithmic choice visible in the body's behavior.
