# Which return is being requested?

The spring's reference is visible: displacement is measured from the line across the floor. Calling its influence restoring gives that reference a direction of preference. This does not make every restoring force a political metaphor. It gives us a particular apparatus in which the reference, response and exclusions can be inspected before extending the question elsewhere.

The force approaches zero at equilibrium; velocity need not. Reaching the favoured place can be a passage through it. A model that records only position loses the distinction between two arrivals carrying opposite velocities. Previous halls gave the body a history through accumulation. The spring makes that history consequential at the place that could have been mistaken for completion.

Damping adds another preference, opposing velocity rather than displacement. Stronger damping can delay the return. The fastest non-crossing return from rest in this linear model is a specified criterion, not a universal measure of a good body. A resonant instrument, a door closer and a springboard could value different responses. This room supplies one rule to investigate; it does not implement all those applications.

The handle also has a policy. It accepts horizontal displacement, discards the other axes, and discards throwing speed at release. Those reductions make a repeatable experiment possible. They also decide which bodily differences can affect the next state. A different release policy could be tested without pretending this one was a neutral transmission of the hand.

The body and wall share instantaneous force samples. That correspondence supports a visual argument: compare the sum and its parts while the body responds. It does not turn every decorative arrow into evidence of an applied force. The supporting tall pendulum prescribes a cosine; the new spring integrates a state. Similar pictures leave that difference unresolved until the code is examined.

The room claims no measured entropy, physical haptic resistance or universal threshold of queerness. Its opening is specific: the preferred place, the retained velocity and the excluded gesture do not say the same thing about what a return can be.
