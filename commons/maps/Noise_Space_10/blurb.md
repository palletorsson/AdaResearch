Expand the canvas. Ten dimensions of parameters: position, time, octaves, persistence, lacunarity, seed. Each dimension adds variation, adds control, adds possibility. The space of all possible noise configurations is vast. Navigate it. Find terrains no one has seen before.

The `noise_space` artifact exposes all ten sliders and regenerates the noise field in real time. Every texture, cloud, and terrain the earlier maps demonstrated is one point in this ten-dimensional space; now you move through the space itself. Ten dimensions cannot be visualized at once — you navigate by projection, seeing 3D slices of a 10D manifold. The seed parameter raises the deepest question: change the seed and the whole landscape transforms while the statistical properties stay identical. Identity in noise is not about features. It is about distributions. Object-thinking has to give way to distribution-thinking.
