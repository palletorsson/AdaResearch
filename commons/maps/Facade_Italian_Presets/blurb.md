Villa San Michele perches above the Amalfi coast. Certosa di San Giacomo stands white against Capri’s blue. Villa Jovis crowns Capri’s eastern cliff. Each is a complete application of the facade grammar — base, columns, openings, cornice — filtered through site, climate, and culture.

The Italian presets are not templates to copy but grammars to study. Each facade solves the same problem differently: how to make a wall that is shelter and symbol, boundary and invitation.
