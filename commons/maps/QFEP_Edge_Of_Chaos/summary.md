# QFEP Edge of Chaos - Summary

## Overview
The sixth QFEP Laboratory map dwells at the sweet spot: lambda locked at 0.4, the narrow band where complexity emerges. Turing patterns ripple on display planes, the edge_core breathes with counterphase oscillation, and ring particles orbit in dynamic equilibrium. The learner is not operator here but witness — the slider is locked, and the map demonstrates what happens when the parameters are right.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 14x14 grid with concentric elevation rings
- **Architecture**: Height 1 perimeter, height 2 first ring, height 3 intermediate ring (Turing patterns and emergence zones), height 4 center (edge_core)
- **Radial design**: The edge_core at center generates the terrain's logic; complexity radiates outward

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **edge_core** — Pulsing green sphere with counterphase inner/outer oscillation, ring particle emitter; pulse speed and amplitude scale with proximity to lambda 0.4
- **turing_pattern** (2 instances) — Vertical display planes rendering reaction-diffusion patterns via shader approximation; presets include Coral, Mitosis, Fingers, Spots, Waves, Maze
- **emergence_zone** (2 instances) — Framing artifacts at height 3 that visualize the narrow critical band
- **lambda_slider** — Locked at 0.4 with `#locked:0.4` modifier; the learner cannot adjust it

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at south perimeter, elevator, teleporter reading "To the Sandbox"

## Learning Sequence
1. Enter at the perimeter and ascend through concentric elevation rings
2. Pass emergence_zones and Turing pattern displays at the intermediate ring
3. Reach the edge_core at the summit — counterphase breathing, green emission, ring particles orbiting
4. Observe the locked slider — the system decides the lambda value, not the learner
5. Watch Turing patterns shift between presets: spots, stripes, labyrinths, all from the same reaction-diffusion substrate
6. Understand: the edge of chaos is where Langton found computation, where Turing found morphogenesis, where the formula's terms balance productively
7. Exit toward the Sandbox, where all controls are unlocked

## Design Intent
The map is the QFEP sequence's phenomenological center. After five maps of parameter manipulation, it removes control and asks the learner to witness emergence at the critical point. The locked slider argues that the edge is not something aimed for through tuning but something found through adaptation. The concentric layout makes criticality a summit: ascend to reach it, descend to leave.
