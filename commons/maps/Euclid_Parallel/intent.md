Concept: The fifth postulate — 2000 years of mathematicians trying to prove that parallel lines never meet, and the discovery that the postulate is independent, not derivable from the other four.
Sequence role: Opens the Foundations Crisis sequence; the gentlest entry point into formalization's limits. Euclid's geometry feels obvious, inevitable — which makes the revelation that its fifth axiom is a choice, not a truth, genuinely destabilizing. Prepares NonEuclidean_Spaces by establishing what will be broken.
Technical angle: Euclid's five postulates, the logical structure of axiomatic systems, attempts at proof by contradiction that accidentally generated non-Euclidean geometries. The parallel_line_demo and euclid_parallel_demo make the postulate interactive — drag the lines and observe.
Critical angle: The fifth postulate's 2000-year resistance to proof is the first hint that formal systems have structural limits. What feels like geometric truth is actually a contingent choice. This is the QFEP F-term at its most seductive: order so compelling it appears necessary rather than constructed.
Key artifacts: euclid_parallel_demo demonstrates the postulate interactively; parallel_line_demo provides the geometric construction.
Gap: A side-by-side showing the same construction with the fifth postulate true vs false would make the independence dramatically visible.
