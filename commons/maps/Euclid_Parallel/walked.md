## The walk

The chapter opens in the most trustworthy room in mathematics. The plaque states the five postulates; the parallel_lines hold their distance with the calm of things that have never been questioned; the angle_sum_triangle adds to 180° every time you check, and you do check, because the room invites the checking. Everything here is certain, and the certainty is the point.

## What it opens

Two thousand years of geometry rested on this furniture — especially the fifth postulate, the one about parallels, which always sounded less like an axiom and more like a promise. The threshold's craft is to make you comfortable. The chapter is about to spend seven rooms taking this one apart, and it wants you to remember exactly how solid the floor felt.
