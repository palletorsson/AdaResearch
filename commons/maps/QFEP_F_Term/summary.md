# QFEP F Term - Summary

## Overview
The second map in the QFEP Laboratory isolates the F term — free energy as prediction error. Lambda locks at zero, collapsing the formula to QFE = F. The learner experiences pure structure: crystal growth from deterministic seeds, snap puzzles that reward pattern completion, and the dark room problem at the corridor's end where total prediction becomes sensory death.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 10x12 grid, corridor from north (satisfaction) to south (stasis)
- **Architecture**: Snap puzzles flanking the entrance, crystal_cluster on a raised central platform (height 2), elevator to height 3 for overview, dark sphere at the descending south end
- **Height gradient**: Rises to 2-3 at center, falls to 0 at south (walking toward order is ascending; toward the dark room, descending)

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **crystal_cluster** — Five hexagonal prisms with deterministic seeding, demonstrating zero-entropy construction
- **snap_cube_puzzle** and **snap_tetra_puzzle** — Pattern-completion puzzles using SnapConnectionManager with adjacency detection; timed reset at 60 seconds
- **lambda_slider** — Locked at 0.0, showing the order-end of the blue-green-red gradient
- **dark_sphere** — Semi-transparent orb with minimal rotation and fixed-frequency pulse; the dark room problem made spatial

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at north, **elevator** at (4,4), **teleporter** at south leading to QFEP_E_Term

## Learning Sequence
1. Enter and encounter the snap puzzles — connect points, form shapes, feel the satisfaction of pattern completion
2. Observe the crystal_cluster — deterministic, beautiful, fully predicted geometry
3. Ride the elevator for an aerial view of the ordered grid
4. Walk south through descending heights toward the dark sphere
5. Recognize the dark room: prediction error at zero means nothing new can arrive
6. Exit through "Escape the Dark Room" teleporter to the entropy term

## Design Intent
The map makes F-minimization feel good before revealing its cost. Crystals are beautiful, puzzles are satisfying, but the dark room at the end demonstrates that pure order is a trap. The corridor from satisfaction to stasis is the spatial argument: structure without entropy produces rigidity, not life.
