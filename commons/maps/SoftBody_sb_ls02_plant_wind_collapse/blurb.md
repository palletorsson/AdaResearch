The same L-system word that grew sb_ls01's standing plant. Same rule, same axiom, same number of turtle steps. The grammar has not changed. What changed is the world the plant stands in: stiffness dropped, diagonal wind running across the room, integration extended to 140 steps. The sapling that stood up there bends here. After a while the trunk gives. The plant comes down.

The same DNA does not produce the same body. Drop the spring constants and the branches lose their argument with gravity. Add wind and the lean accumulates in one direction. Run the simulation long enough and a small lean becomes a fall. The grammar wrote the shape; the dynamics wrote what happened to the shape. They are not the same plant.

A line drawing is forever — the F→F[+F]F[-F]F is what it is. A body in physics is for now. Two different relationships to time, with the same letters in between.
