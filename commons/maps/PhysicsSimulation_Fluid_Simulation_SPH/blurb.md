# Fluid Simulation (SPH)

Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics discretizes the continuous into drops. Each particle carries mass, velocity, pressure — a kernel function smooths their influence across neighbors. The fluid isn't stored on a grid. It *is* the particles. Density computed from overlap. Pressure from density. Force from pressure gradient. The Navier-Stokes equations, solved without a mesh.

Lattice gas automata run beneath — discrete particles on a fixed grid, colliding by simple rules, and somehow reproducing viscosity, turbulence, flow. Microscopic order generating macroscopic chaos. The continuous emerges from the countable.

Pour into the void basins. Watch particles find their level, press against walls, splash and settle. A fluid has no fixed shape — only the constraint of what contains it. Matter that remembers nothing except how to fill available space.
