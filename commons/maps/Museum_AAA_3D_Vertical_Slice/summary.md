# Museum AAA 3D vertical slice

This map is the first embodied proof of the artifact-placement system developed in the dressing-room corpus. It selects three deliberately different Wave 4 artifacts rather than attempting to display the whole cohort at once.

Shannon Entropy Meter is mounted at reading height against a wall and reserves clear floor in front. Pattern Loom is a self-supporting machine whose generated textile makes the space in front of it part of the exhibit. Neural Network Visualization is a room-scale section with reading positions on both sides and no neighboring artifacts inside its preferred zone.

A reusable forty-by-eighteen-metre museum shell wraps the composed one-metre floor grid. The natural biome is disabled for this interior map. Spawn, connected walking surface, and exit remain owned by the grid map, while the shell owns the arcade, roof structure, and architectural rhythm.
