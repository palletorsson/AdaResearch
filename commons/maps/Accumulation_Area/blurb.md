Here the question turns around. The first maps asked how fast — now this one asks how much. A curve sits above a baseline, and the region between them is shaded. Slide the right edge and the shaded area grows or shrinks, the readout tracking the total as it goes. That accumulating number is the integral.

The `integral_area` is the curve and the fill that answers to the slider. The `code_display` shows the integral notation beside the running value, and the `science_screen` plots the area as a function of where you stopped — because the total-so-far is itself a curve, the mirror image of the derivative you started with. Accumulation is addition that never stops being continuous. You are not summing steps; you are sweeping a boundary and letting everything behind it count.
