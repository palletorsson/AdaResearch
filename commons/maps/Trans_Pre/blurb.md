One cube, and then the difference, and a room that climbs to show it. The same block stands twice: here and four cells on and a metre up, which you reach by the ramp between them, and that is translation. Here and turned, and that is rotation. Here and larger, and that is scale. Then all three at once, which one matrix does in a single multiplication. Nothing is crossed yet and nothing moves; the chapter's three verbs are shown as three differences between a thing and its copy, before any of them is put to work.
