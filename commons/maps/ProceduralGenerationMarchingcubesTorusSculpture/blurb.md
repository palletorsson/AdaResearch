A torus defined by distance, not by drawing. The signed distance function measures how far each point in space sits from an invisible ring — negative inside, positive outside, zero at the skin. Marching cubes walks a grid through this field, reading signs, connecting thresholds, extracting surface from pure math.

The torus is topology's first interesting object. One hole. Genus one. A coffee mug and a donut share the same truth. But here the shape never existed as geometry until the algorithm asked the right question at every voxel. The mesh emerges from interrogation.

Implicit modeling inverts the act of making. Instead of placing vertices, you describe a condition. The surface is wherever the condition is met. The sculptor doesn't carve — the sculpture confesses.
