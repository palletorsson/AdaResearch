# What is the world made of, so far?

A room with one large glass cage, open to the sky. Inside it stands the biome of one spine stage — the kingdoms that stage has unlocked, at that stage's density, seeded from one number and dispatched through the same law the grid lane uses. Walk in through the doorway. The floor glows where the organisms have been. The stand screen names the stage, its seeds and its generations. Change the word on the token and the same room shows another stage.
