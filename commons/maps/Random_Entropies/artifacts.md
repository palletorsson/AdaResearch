# Seven encounters, one walking order

All seven artifacts have independent map placements and registry scenes. Tutorial and final use the same order; the first two are a side-by-side comparison across a low divider.

| # | Artifact | What differs between versions | What is kept fixed for the first comparison |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `entropy_chairs_shannon` — Shannon chairs | Colour shares, assignment to sites, prepared inventory | Eight equally likely IDs; record colour |
| 2 | `entropy_chairs_boltzmann` — Boltzmann chairs | Pink count on the left, individual assignment | Eight marked sites, four pink/four black, same-colour chairs indistinguishable |
| 3 | `entropy_chairs_description` — Description wall | Grid, edit, motif and seeded scatter; recipes always visible | Six chairs per group and shared generating language |
| 4 | `entropy_door_dynamics` — Two starts, two rules | STEP/RESET/GAP; contracting and expanding pairs with retained histories | Binary observation partition, 1,024 equally likely midpoint starts; centre stays open |
| 5 | `entropy_cooling_cup` — Cooling cup | Five mixing stages and five labelled heat times | Prescribed 25% milk; separate heat model with two finite bodies and conserved energy |
| 6 | `entropy_frosted_chair` — Partial chair | Five frost levels; identity/colour+arms/colour/constant record, same displayed chair | Eight equiprobable identities |
| 7 | `entropy_ball_pit` — One guess (chairs) | Types 8/8, 8/4/4, 12/4, 16/0; guess/draw, change one, physical, stackable all-pink ending | Sixteen IDs; selection with replacement; categories defined by colour and arms |

Versions remain visible together. Doors and the last pyramids have small controls for the specific interventions described above. A compact wall explanation names their assumptions and readings. Floor letters identify the versions.
