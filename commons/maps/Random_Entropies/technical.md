# Implementation and measurement contracts

The executable models, reconstruction steps, parameter ranges and save format are documented in [tutorial.md](tutorial.md). Seven wrappers in `commons/artifacts/ordinary_entropies/` remain independent registry entries and placements. Five use the prepared comparison host; doors use `door_study.gd` and the last pyramids use `one_guess.gd`, `guess_table.gd` and `guess_model.gd`.

The runtime is deterministic at a stored seed and state. It uses no network entropy, global RNG, rigid-body scattering or physical steam solver. The cup has an energy-conserving two-body heat model. The hinge has explicitly chosen mathematical update rules. The glass's numerical record channel is separate from its rendered surface. The last comparison uniformly selects a chair ID with replacement using a private seed-435 generator. Categories include colour and arms; their distribution determines both Shannon and min-entropy. Position and rotation are separately retained.

See [native verification and room evidence](../../../doc/book/iterations/2026-10-16-doors-and-one-guess/README.md) and [sources](../../../doc/book/iterations/2026-10-16-ordinary-entropies/sources.md). Scene loading, passage clearance, wall attachment and simultaneous state composition and model correctness are checked separately from the pending learner/headset trial.

The research wall composes the existing dry-marker canvas, XRTools pickup lifecycle and marker body. Notes carry their own local canvases and pin transforms. Ink and positions persist as one local JSON record with PNG buffers under `user://research_info_walls/`. The illustration is replaceable independently; see `commons/artifacts/research_info_wall/ILLUSTRATION_SLOT.md`. No shared multiplayer storage or cloud synchronization is implemented.

Five hollow cups share one 3.6 × 1.1 m table. A lathed open ceramic profile leaves the liquid visible from above. `coffee.gdshader` shears an angular milk region into ribbons and blends it toward its mean: each radial annulus retains the prescribed milk fraction. The display clock animates the ribbons independently of the five fixed `Model.thermal()` times. This is a controlled visual illustration, not a solver for milk diffusion or physical mixing entropy.

`frost.gdshader` averages sixteen neighbouring screen samples, with increasing radius and light tint; the last pane is opaque. Its visual obscurity is independent of the eight-identity record model. The new colour-plus-arms channel leaves one bit unresolved. C and D retain colour and therefore both leave two bits unresolved despite different blur. The local Compatibility renderer is tested; headset rendering and performance need a device trial.

`guess_table.gd` builds four sixteen-chair pyramids with 9 + 4 + 2 + 1 chairs. All are XR pickables with compound colliders, released under gravity; the desktop museum supplies a bounded, horizontal walking-contact force through `pushable_walk.gd`. VR uses the existing player-body rigid-body push. A/B compare two versus three types at the same 50% best guess; C is 12/4; D has sixteen pink chairs. Group membership stays with the labelled ID, independent of location. Spatial disturbance leaves the category distribution unchanged. CHANGE ONE changes a category; RESET restores the baseline and generator. `one-guess-floor/2` retains group/category IDs, full generator state, 3D poses, velocities and sleeping flags. Held bodies block reset/restore. Historical `one-guess-table/1` records require the old miniature mode. Physics continuations are not claimed bit-reproducible. Older `best_guess.gd` and `encounter.gd` receipt formats remain reconstructible through their original classes, not through the new table. The doors’ own partition fills the wall around three openings; the old grid wall at row 24 is removed to avoid blocking them. The centre opening remains clear at steps 0–15. The initial observation is included, so step 7 contains eight symbols. Ensemble record entropy uses 1,024 equally likely midpoint starts and caps at 10 bits. Individual pair separation is not used to estimate this entropy; the default pair rejoins at step 9. These finite calculations do not measure an asymptotic entropy rate.

Rendering reference: [Godot 4.6 spatial shader modes](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.html). The frosted faces write depth; the final face uses a genuinely opaque unshaded material so later depth effects cannot reveal hidden chair edges.

Physical chair contact is deliberately bounded: horizontal speed at most 1.5 m/s, vertical speed between -8 and +2 m/s, angular speed at most 4 rad/s; the desktop contact force is capped at 60 N and scaled by timestep. This prevents a kinematic avatar from launching furniture across the hall. It is a dissipative interaction model, not a calibrated material or thermodynamic simulation.
