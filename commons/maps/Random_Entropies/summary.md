`Random_Entropies` follows `Random_Definition` and precedes `Random_Entropy` in Randomness. Seven separately registered groups show versions together; the door and best-guess studies also have compact controls. The existing entropy hall and its prefab gallery remain available.

Beyond a wall, four chair arrangements compare candidate recipes. Five glass panes show progressive frosting with explicit record channels. Finally, four full-size chair pyramids compare 8/8, 8/4/4 types, 12/4 and 16/0.
