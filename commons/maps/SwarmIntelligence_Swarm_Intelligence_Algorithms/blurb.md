Nature solved optimization before we did. Bees dance solutions. Ants find shortest paths. Fish schools evade predators. Birds flock efficiently. Swarm intelligence algorithms borrow these strategies: decentralized, robust, adaptive. Evolution debugged these algorithms over millions of years.

`SwarmShowcase` gathers the sequence's algorithms into a single gallery — Physarum, boids, ACO, PSO, firefly, bee colony, bacterial foraging — each in its own alcove under unified visualization. Side by side, the family resemblance becomes obvious: every algorithm answers the same question — how do simple agents coordinate without central control? — with a different mechanism. Stigmergic, social, chemotactic, luminescent. The integration phase thesis reaches its fullest expression here: intelligence emerges from material interaction, not top-down design. Every swarm method was invented by evolution before it was discovered by computer science.
