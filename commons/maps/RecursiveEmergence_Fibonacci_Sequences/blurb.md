Two void pools spiral outward from the center like arms of a nautilus shell. The room's proportions follow the golden ratio — each section the sum of the two before it. 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13.

Addition becomes geometry. The simplest recurrence relation — each term is just the sum of its predecessors — generates spirals found in sunflower heads, pinecone scales, and galaxy arms. Fibonacci is not a formula imposed on nature. It is the shape that growth takes when each step remembers the last two.
