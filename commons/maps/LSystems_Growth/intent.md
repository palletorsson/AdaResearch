Concept: Trees grow generation by generation, and context changes everything. The same axiom planted in a constrained corridor versus an open clearing produces different morphologies — environment shapes grammar. Growth is not just string expansion but temporal unfolding under spatial influence.
Sequence role: Second map in the L-Systems sequence (11th spine sequence, lambda_edge phase). Moves from the static rule-to-shape pipeline of Grammar_Lab into animated, time-stepped growth and context-sensitive rewriting. Intermediate difficulty — the learner now understands basic L-Systems and is ready to see how the mechanism gains biological plausibility through environmental response. Connects forward to Grammars_And_Curves where the formal grammar framework is made rigorous, and backward to Grammar_Lab's static presets; follows LSystems_Grammar_Lab; leads to LSystems_Grammars_And_Curves.
Technical angle: Generation-by-generation animation renders each rewriting step as a visible growth phase, making the temporal structure of L-Systems explicit. Context-sensitive rules allow productions to depend on neighboring symbols in the string — a branch flanked by other branches may grow differently than an isolated one. Parametric L-Systems attach numerical parameters (length, angle, thickness) that can be modulated by environmental variables, introducing continuous variation into the discrete rewriting process.
Critical angle: Context-sensitivity transforms L-Systems from mechanical string rewriting into something resembling developmental biology — cells that respond to their neighbors, tissues that differentiate based on position. The corridor-vs-clearing comparison makes the philosophical point visceral: identity is not intrinsic to the axiom but emerges through interaction with constraints. Grammar is generative, but context is formative.
Key artifacts: AnimatedTree renders step-by-step growth with visible generation transitions; ContextSensitiveTree demonstrates neighbor-dependent rule application; parametric_lsystem introduces continuous parameters modulated by environment.
Gap: Needs an environmental parameter dashboard that lets the learner adjust light direction, gravity strength, and spatial boundaries in real time while the tree grows, making the grammar-environment coupling interactive rather than preset.
