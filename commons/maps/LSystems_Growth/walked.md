> ghost-drafted from the working map (primitive slot, lsystems thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

lsystem_tree · AnimatedTree · ContextSensitiveTree · parametric_lsystem

## The walk

Four trees, four upgrades to the same sentence. The lsystem_tree holds the baseline — deterministic rules, the branching you can predict from the page. The AnimatedTree adds time: derivation as visible growth, each rewriting step a season. The ContextSensitiveTree lets a symbol's neighbors change its fate — a branch that grows differently because of what grows beside it, grammar acquiring ecology. And the parametric_lsystem gives every symbol a number to carry — lengths and widths flowing through the rules — arithmetic entering the grammar's bloodstream.

## What it teaches

The primitive is expressiveness as a ladder. Lindenmayer's insight was not one trick but a *family* of grammars, each rung adding a capacity real plants demonstrably have: development in time, sensitivity to neighborhood, continuous quantity. Climbing the four trees in one room, you watch a formalism grow toward biology without ever stopping being text — the chapter's claim in miniature, that the distance between a sentence and an organism is a sequence of small, legible amendments.
