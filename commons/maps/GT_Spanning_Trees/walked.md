## The walk

The mst_visualization takes a rich graph — redundant, loopy, many ways between any two points — and prunes it to the minimum spanning tree: every vertex still reachable, total edge cost as low as it can go, not one connection to spare. The extraction is beautiful to watch, edge by cheapest edge, the network thinning into a skeleton.

## The turn (critical)

The critical room asks what the pruning costs. A spanning tree is efficiency's portrait of a network: connected, cheap, and *fragile* — cut any edge and it falls in two, force any two leaves to talk and they must route through the hierarchy. The loops the algorithm discarded were not waste; they were resilience, alternative routes, the possibility of going around. Every infrastructure argument — roads, fiber, org charts — replays this trade: the tree is what a network looks like after an accountant has loved it. The rhizome two rooms back is what it looked like before.
