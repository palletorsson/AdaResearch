Concept: No instructions, no objectives — just a field of soft things waiting to be touched. The playground is the integration map where all prior soft body techniques converge into open-ended exploration, proving that the best way to understand compliance is through unstructured play.
Sequence role: Sixth map in Soft Bodies (13th spine sequence, integration phase), intermediate difficulty. After five maps of structured lessons (deformation, rotation, obstacles, cloth), this map removes all scaffolding. The learner brings their accumulated intuition into a sandbox. Connects forward to SoftBodies_Affect_Theory_Visualization where soft bodies become expressive rather than physical; follows SoftBodies_Cloth_Physics.
Technical angle: The branching_growth_algorithm introduces L-system or space-colonization branching applied to soft body structures — organic forms that grow and deform simultaneously. The gridagent suggests agent-based interaction with a soft body grid, where autonomous agents push, pull, or perturb the material. The rounded_softbody_test is a geometry variant — rounded rather than cubic, testing how curvature affects deformation behavior and collision stability.
Critical angle: The playground is a deliberate pedagogical stance: that haptic intuition precedes formal understanding. By removing objectives, the map forces the learner to generate their own questions — what happens if I push here? What if two soft bodies collide? The branching growth algorithm is the most radical artifact: it merges soft body dynamics with procedural generation, suggesting that growth and deformation are the same process at different timescales.
Key artifacts: branching_growth_algorithm demonstrates organic branching structures with soft body dynamics; gridagent provides agent-based interaction with soft body grids; rounded_softbody_test explores curvature effects on soft body deformation.
Gap: Needs a recording and replay tool — an artifact that captures the learner's interactions and plays them back, allowing comparison of different manipulation strategies on the same soft body configuration.
