A wall becomes architecture the moment you divide it. Base, wall, cornice — three horizontal zones that every building negotiates, from Roman insulae to Manhattan brownstones. The grammar is simple: heavy at bottom, light at top, a cap to finish. Everything else is refinement.

The facade grammar demo shows these zones as adjustable bands. Slide the proportions and watch the wall’s character shift — a tall base feels defensive, a minimal cornice feels modern, equal thirds feel classical. The same three elements, infinitely tuned.
