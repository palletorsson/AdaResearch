# Rotation Spectacle — two primary families

`boolean_tunnel`: one full-scale fourteen-segment corridor on the museum gallery's central axis. Ten degrees per segment, with its hollow seated for the museum floor.

`carousel_cake`: six full-scale instances, three on either side, using the existing cake, ziggurat, column, spindle and flare profiles; the original cake is repeated as a reference. Every instance uses the same eight-layer speed rule and identifies its profile with a label. Gallery instances use one live stack with matching visible collision.

The two families are the book's two primary encounters. The former rotation/transport obstacles and supporting cast are preserved in `doc/space/rotation-spectacle-gallery-2026-09-09/before`, outside the current exhibition layout.
