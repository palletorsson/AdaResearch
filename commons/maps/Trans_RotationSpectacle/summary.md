# Trans_Rotation_2 - Map Summary

## Overview
Trans_Rotation_2 presents rotation as spectacle through the carousel_cake - a multi-layered structure where each layer rotates at different speeds and directions. The long dual-corridor approach builds anticipation. The boolean_tunnel demonstrates rotation's relationship to spatial carving. This map celebrates rotation as visual phenomenon.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 7×41 grid (very long corridor map)
- **Architecture**: Split parallel corridors leading south to observation platform
- **Height**: Multi-level with walls of height 2 flanking corridors
- **Structure**: Two paths separated by void, converging at carousel

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **boolean_tunnel** (3,3) - Tunnel carved through geometry
- **carousel_cake** (3,40) - Multi-layered rotating structure at 1.2 scale
- **dark_sphere** (3,6) - Ambient atmosphere in north section

### Utilities
- **Transport cube** (3,3) tc:1:y:auto - Entry lift
- **Annotation** (6,0) - Navigation marker
- **Waypoint** (3,39) - Near carousel viewing position
- **Teleporter** (3,39) - Exit
- **pick_up_cube** (2,38) - Collection target

## Atmosphere
- **Lighting**: Cool ambient with warm directional
- **Background**: Sky blue
- **Mood**: Processional, ceremonial, hypnotic

## Learning Sequence
1. Player spawns at north end
2. Uses transport cube to descend
3. Observes boolean_tunnel demonstration
4. Chooses left or right corridor
5. Long walk south through parallel paths
6. Corridors converge at observation platform
7. Views carousel_cake rotation display
8. Collects pickup and exits via teleporter

## Design Intent
The extreme length of the corridors creates a **pilgrimage** to the carousel. The carousel_cake is positioned as destination, reward, attraction. Its layered rotation - each tier spinning independently - demonstrates complex rotation composition.

The boolean_tunnel shows that rotation can carve space: a rotating cutter producing a tunnel through solid geometry.
