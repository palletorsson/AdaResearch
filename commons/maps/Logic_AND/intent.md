# Logic — both supports

AND as a route with two holes in it. `matter_spring_and` sets white balls on a one-metre slope with a 30 cm rise, held by a pink stop. Touch the X and Y cubes to choose whether each falls into its guided slot; lift the stop by hand and gravity does the rest. Both cubes in place carry the ball across both gaps to a hinged paddle that swings when hit. Either missing and the ball falls short.

Keep two tables apart: the one the rear results board expects and the one the balls actually produce. Impacts and falls are recorded as evidence; a stalled or hand-taken ball is inconclusive, not false. After the balls rest ten seconds the take resets, and the observed rows stay. Seeing the full table earns the assembly in the shared prefab inventory. Logic_Alternatives next puts the same decision on two routes.
