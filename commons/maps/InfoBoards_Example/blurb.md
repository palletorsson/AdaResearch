# InfoBoard Gallery

Three boards in a corridor. Point, line, triangle — the first marks on any surface, the minimum vocabulary of form.

An InfoBoard is a knowledge object. It exists in space the way a plaque exists on a wall — you walk up to it, you read it, you carry something away. The syntax is simple: `ib:point`, `ib:line`, `ib:triangle`. A colon and a name. That's how knowledge gets placed in a world.

Point has no dimension. Line has one. Triangle closes space for the first time — creates an inside and an outside. Three primitives, three thresholds. The corridor that holds them is itself a line. Walk it. Each board is a station. Each station is a cut in the continuum where someone decided: this is worth stopping for.
