# Who gets the first turn?

Two equal F grammars take turns on one field. The first receives more from the shared patch. Reverse the order from the same initial state and the allocation reverses. In winter, consumption continues while rewriting waits.

The primary ForestCompetition combines a controlled pair at the front with its original automatic five-strategy forest behind. Coral and the dark sphere remain. Generation count, resources and visible form are distinct records of the process.
