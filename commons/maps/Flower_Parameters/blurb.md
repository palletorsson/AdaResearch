The same flower, now with sliders. Petal count: 5 makes a wild rose, 13 makes a chrysanthemum, 34 makes a dahlia. Petal length: short and round or long and pointed. Curve: flat or cupped or recurved.

Small changes in numbers produce dramatic morphological shifts. The flower is its parameters — everything visible is a consequence of a handful of numeric choices. VR sliders make this tangible: grab, drag, watch the bloom reshape itself.
