Sequence role: Map 3 of 9 in Vectors & Forces: From Direction to Dynamics. The learner arrives from VectorOperations carrying dot product, cross product, and projection. This map applies those tools: the turret targeting uses subtraction (target - turret = direction) and normalization (pure direction without magnitude), the dot product checks whether the target is within the turret's field of view, and the weather vector field demonstrates superposition (R = gravity + wind) in a narrative context. It feeds into VectorAdvanced (map 4), where torque and throwing demand real-time application under pressure. In the QFEP framework, this map transitions from understanding state S to understanding how S evolves when multiple vector fields act simultaneously — the beginning of dynamics.

Technical angle: hl_turret_vectors implements targeting via vector subtraction: direction = (target.position - turret.position).normalized(). The dot product against the turret's forward vector determines whether the target is in range (positive dot = in front, negative = behind). VectorFieldFlow assigns a direction to every point in a 9x9 grid using F(p) = swirl(p) - radial(p), with 81 MultiMesh arrows breathing in unison and a particle tracing the field's will via advection. force_field_visualizer renders gravitational and electromagnetic fields as 3D arrow glyphs with color-coded magnitude. weather_vector_field implements R = A + B where A is gravity and B is wind — 36 grid arrows recolor and reorient as the learner drags VR-grabbable wind handles, rain particles change trajectory, and pressure zones bend the uniform field into curves. VectorForces bridges vector operations to force diagrams. basis_vectors_rig and VectorBasics anchor the scene with the standard orthonormal frame.

Critical angle: A vector field is a set of instructions written in space. The particle has no memory and no plan — it only reads the local instruction and follows. This is a profound computational model: the weather system does not plan trajectories; it evaluates a function at each point and the trajectory emerges. The turret enacts the same principle from the other side — it reads the target's position vector and computes a response. Both are instances of the QFEP insight that behavior emerges from the local evaluation of state, not from global planning. The weather_vector_field's @identity states its truth: "Wind is a vector field. Rain is a particle advected through two fields at once. Weather is superposition you can stand in." This connects directly to the sequence truth that force is what changes states — here, the learner stands inside the changing and feels the wind push particles sideways. The move from abstract dot/cross products to applied targeting and weather is also a move from understanding the grammar to hearing the language spoken, which is the pedagogical arc QFEP demands: formalism earns its meaning through application.
