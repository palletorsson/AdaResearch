## The walk

One artifact, holding the chapter's atom. The FlowFieldMain lays an invisible field of directions across space — every point a little arrow — and releases agents to simply *obey the arrow underneath them*. No agent decides where to go; the ground decides, and the agents pour through it like water finding the grooves of a landscape.

## What it teaches

The primitive is the cleanest separation the chapter offers: the field is the intelligence, the agents are only the readout. Where physarum wrote its field by living, the flow field is authored directly — but the lesson underneath is identical, and it is the one the whole thread turns on: put the coordination in the *environment* and the agents can stay stupid. This is the design inversion behind every swarm — do not make smart agents, make a field worth following. The rooms ahead complicate who writes the field; this room makes sure you can see that a field is what is being written.
