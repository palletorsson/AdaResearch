A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 arena carved from its center. Objects rise at random heights, spin to random angles, and a pipe grows blindly through space — turning, continuing, turning again. Three algorithms, one shared principle: controlled unpredictability.

The dark sphere pulses at the edge. The pipe dreams its way forward segment by segment, deciding at each joint whether to hold course or pivot — a coin flip with architectural consequences. Cubes elevate on vertical dice rolls. Others rotate around Y, each facing a direction no designer chose. Uniform randomness applied to position, rotation, growth. The same function, different axes.

Randomness is not chaos. It is delegation — handing authorship to a distribution and watching structure emerge from what no one intended.
