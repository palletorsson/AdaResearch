Map data alone describes that shell: the launcher mounts `proving_ground.gd` after the hall has built.

The rod extends the existing aligned drawing pen. A held tip must stay inside the 0.23 m field for 0.25 seconds. Existing DrawDot code samples movements of at least 0.015 m, capped at 1,024 positions. Releasing keeps an independent array copy. A fitted MultiMesh of short tubes shows those same intervals on the next plinth. This local record does not enter TraceData.

The existing resolution catalyst sends its existing physical projectile; the head's receiver opens the gate's visual and collision boundary. Crossing completes the trial. Contact records a touch without changing player health. RESET cancels travel, clears local marks and projectiles, restores the head and closes the gate. Released tools return to their mounts; held tools remain in the hand.
