# What travels

The rod takes charge from the sphere's field and samples the visitor's hand movement. Releasing it keeps a copy of that record as a small sculpture in the next room. The head waiting beside the Dürer apparatus is the same instance that later flies into the arena. The familiar sphere still reduces its rings and segments when held; its trigger now supplies the interruption that opens a passage.

This prototype tests making → keeping → defending. It supplies the opening ability and the emitting sphere; it does not yet implement the entire catalyst progression or a companion relationship.
