*Every classifier has an outside.*

Gödel showed us that formal systems can't see their own edges. Algorithmic systems inherit this blind spot and call it objectivity. The people misread by these systems are not errors — they are the outside, the proof that the system is consistent only because it chose what to exclude.

Ethics, here, is not a postprocessing step. It's not a filter you put in front of the model. It's the *shape of the room*: who gets to enter, where the walls run, whose presence the system was designed to hold.

This map is a room designed after Gödel. The walls carve an off-center path. You walk the outside of what the grid would have been.
