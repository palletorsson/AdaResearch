Technical angle: Life-without-Death (B3/S012345678) iterated 12 generations on a ~20×20 grid with a small seeded population. Because no cell ever dies once born, the alive-set grows monotonically; after enough steps the grid fills (modulo the propagation pattern). Mass points placed at all alive cells; springs added between every 4-neighbor pair (and optionally diagonal pairs for shear stability). Two corner masses pinned; remaining vertices integrate under gravity plus a constant lateral wind force; Verlet integration. The resulting spring graph is dense and fully connected — the cloth has more than enough structural integrity to hold its shape and respond to wind as a coherent piece of fabric rather than as Conway's lacework.

Critical angle: The CA cloth pair stages a dynamical-systems point in physical form. The two rules differ only in survival conditions but live in entirely different attractor regimes — Conway in critical-edge, sparse, decaying populations; Life-without-Death in monotonic growth. Embodying both rules as cloths makes the abstract difference between attractor regimes immediately tangible: one rule weaves nets, the other weaves tapestries. The same point lives at the heart of materials science (microstructure determines macroscopic behavior), neural network theory (sparse vs dense connectivity), and epidemiology (immune vs persistent infection). The rule and its death clause together write the body.
