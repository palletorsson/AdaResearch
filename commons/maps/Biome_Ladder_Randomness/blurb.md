# What may chance change, hall by hall?

Glass cages along an aisle, one per hall of the randomness sequence. The work stands in every cage with colour on it; what chance may touch grows hall by hall: a reel of five amounts the seed chose; the grid again with its agreement withdrawn; cubes removed, one protected; a band of slabs that reads as a maze; the point walking by itself; a bell in the count; the first fungus in a fairy ring; Pollock's drips, the dartboard's estimate of pi, a pipe scribbling through the volume; and, last, a body that may not be there when you arrive.

The left column is one work at seed 7 growing through five states in the family its seed drew. The right column holds the four fullest states, each composed by one of the file families written by the four-agent fan-out: the bell in the count on Kandinsky's fan, the fairy ring on Malevich's flight axis, the drips and darts on Klee's square spiral, the absent body on Moholy's bent spine.
