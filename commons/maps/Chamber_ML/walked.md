## The cast

proximity_spawner (gradient_hunter) · catalyst_target ×4 · science_screen

## The walk

The chapter's engine comes hunting. The proximity_spawner releases a gradient_hunter — a creature that learns your patterns and follows the slope of your habits downhill toward you. Move predictably and it finds you; move randomly and it loses the gradient, unable to descend a surface with no reliable slope. You place catalyst blocks on the four targets, and the hunter reads even those, adapting to the structure you leave behind.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to optimize** — and to be optimized against. The whole chapter taught that learning is gradient descent on a landscape, and here you *are* the landscape: your movements are the loss surface the hunter minimizes, your predictability the slope it rides. To escape you must do the one thing descent cannot follow — become genuinely unpredictable, flatten your own gradient into noise. The room plants the chapter's deepest lesson in the body: optimization is powerful exactly where there is structure to exploit, and the only refuge from a perfect learner is a landscape with nothing to learn. You leave having felt both sides of the equation — the hunter's relentless competence and the strange freedom of refusing to be a slope.
