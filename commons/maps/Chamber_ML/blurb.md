The hunter learns your patterns. Move predictably and it finds you. Move randomly and it loses the gradient.

This is the catalyst chamber for Machine Learning — where the creature is the optimizer and you are the training signal. The `gradient_hunter` runs online learning against your movement: it maintains a model of your next position and updates it every time you move, minimizing prediction error step by step. The catalyst mode is none because the interaction itself IS the training loop. The Science Screen scatters your positions and its predictions, letting you see the error shrinking as the creature adapts. Move in a pattern and you become predictable. Move at random and its gradient points nowhere. The chamber argues that learning is adversarial — what the creature gains, you lose, unless you keep changing.
