A gallery of creative chaos — Pollock's drip paintings, pipe dreams, butterflies in flight, extreme randomness pushed to visual limits. Art where the algorithm becomes the artist.

`pollock_painting_in_3d` translates action painting into 3D particle physics: random launch vectors, gravity, paint-splatter collision. `monte_carlo_dartboard` demonstrates randomness as computational method — throw darts at a unit square, count hits inside the inscribed circle, and pi emerges from the ratio. `pipe_dream` runs combinatorial random pipe generation. `extreme_randomness` pushes past Gaussian into heavy-tailed Cauchy and Levy distributions where outliers dominate. Randomness is not one phenomenon. Pollock's drip was deliberate embrace of controlled chaos. Monte Carlo weaponized randomness for computation. Butterflies' Levy flights are optimal foraging. The gallery argues: randomness is a cultural phenomenon, not merely a mathematical one.
