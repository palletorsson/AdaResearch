An elevated walkway rings a central void. You stand three levels above an empty pit, looking down at nothing. The walls keep you at a distance from what you observe. You see without being reachable.

The observation deck separates the viewer from the viewed. It is the architecture of surveillance, of science, of the balcony seat. Elevation plus enclosure equals detachment. From up here you can see the whole, but you cannot touch it. Vantage is always a form of withdrawal.
