Concept: Facade_NYC_Tenement is the *modularized actualization* of the facade assembly principle. Where Facade_Classical applies all six compositional moves canonically, this map strips the grammar to its repeating module: bay × storey, stacked five storeys, repeated four bays wide. The hierarchy is refused (every storey is the same as every other storey), the column order is omitted (no semantic column), the ornament is stripped. What remains is *rhythm at its purest* — one beat per bay per storey, twenty identical beats — and *fire-escape geometry* clinging to the front like an inhabited structural critique.

Actualizes: Facade_Assembly_Principle, in the *modularized* mode. The map takes a position in the virtual field where the rhythm axis is held at "regular repeating," the hierarchy axis is held at "none," the column order axis is held at "omitted," and the rustication axis is held at "minimal." It is the principle reduced to *what minimum survives when you remove the aristocratic ornamentation* — and the answer is rhythm. Twenty windows in a grid. The grammar still speaks; it just refuses to flatter.

Sequence role: Sixth map of the facade_assembly branch sequence (per the curated walk order: principle → classical → baroque → venetian gothic → rustication → tenement → critique → chamber). Paired with Facade_Classical as the *opposite-pole expression* — same principle, opposite axis-positions. Where Classical is the rule applied with maximum semantic load, Tenement is the rule applied with minimum semantic load. The pairing is the lesson: the rule survives both extremes.

Technical angle: A five-storey tenement facade rendered along the front edge of the room. Each storey: four bays wide, one window per bay, no decorative cornice between storeys, simple lintels. A black metal fire-escape clings to the front from the second storey up — externally bolted, structural, vernacular. The window proportions are utilitarian, not classical. No piano nobile; every storey is the same storey, repeated. Pass-2 build: the room walks today as a tall 8x6x5 space with spawn and teleporter; the modular facade primitives (bay_module_tenement, fire_escape_grid, window_lintel_simple, brick_surface) are queued for pass-3 artifact authoring.

Critical angle: The tenement is *the principle in its democratic mode*. It refuses the Renaissance assumption that the second storey deserves more architectural attention than the fifth; it refuses the Baroque assumption that ornament communicates wealth; it refuses the Classical assumption that symmetry equals authority. What it keeps is rhythm — because rhythm is the *minimum required structure*, the floor below which the grammar stops being a grammar. The fire-escape is not decorative addition; it is the building admitting that vertical repetition has a cost (you must be able to get out), and that the admission can be structural. Memphis Totem will later refuse rhythm itself; Tenement keeps rhythm and refuses everything else. This is the principle's *populist* edge — the demonstration that the grammar can speak without flattering power.
