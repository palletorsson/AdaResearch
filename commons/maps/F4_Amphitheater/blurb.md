Stepped tiers descend toward a central floor. Every seat faces the same point. A single light sphere rests at the bottom, glowing in the shared focus of the entire room.

The amphitheater concentrates attention through geometry. Concentric rings of elevation mean every viewer has a sightline to the center and to every other viewer. It is a space designed for convergence — where looking at the same thing together becomes a collective act. Architecture that makes an audience.
