# The Prop Gallery

No diagrams past this line. Every force concept in the sequence stands here as one
surreal applied object: a fountain of museum things paused along their own parabolas,
Calder's lever law hung with fire extinguishers and crates, a ceiling duct raining
boxes onto wedges you can shove, a revolving door that keeps only the aligned part of
every push, the same extinguisher falling forever on three planets, a parlour rug
where furniture orbits a bowling ball, and — on the way out — a field of umbrellas
straining down-wind, so the last thing you do is walk through a vector field wearing
its own weather.

The force is under breath: felt in behaviour, named on small placards, drawn nowhere.
