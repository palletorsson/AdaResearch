# Intent — Forces_PropGallery

The 2026-08-27 brief: remove the abstract vector explanations, one surreal applied
object per concept, props instead of diagrams, Garry's Mod 2040, the Calder mobile
but more beautiful. This map is the gallery form of doc/FORCES_PROP_GALLERY.md.

Five phases along a south-to-north promenade:
1. Entry — spawn faces the paused fountain (motion: velocity, acceleration, g).
2. Rung rooms — arrow (force_cube, length_lantern), operations (revolving_door,
   projection_shadow, the two mobiles in dialogue), forces (three_gravities,
   momentum_cradle, brake_skid, drag_corridor, spring_tower, tug_of_war).
3. Sandbox — prop_spigot: the engine itself as exhibit.
4. Reflection — parlour_orbits: attraction as a living room.
5. Exit — walk THROUGH umbrella_field (force-as-place) to the teleporter.

The 56 abstract tokens this gallery replaces stay in their maps until the casting
is approved; the retire plan is in the design doc.
