The first stable shape you fall into is rarely the best one — you land in a local minimum and stick, a good-enough form with a better one just over the ridge. Annealing shakes the system with heat so it can climb out and settle deeper. But the room holds the chapter's sharpest turn: the deepest well of all is a dead-perfect crystal, frozen, finished, incapable of change. The alive form is the one that stops short of the bottom — resting at the edge, low enough to hold, high enough to still be moved.
