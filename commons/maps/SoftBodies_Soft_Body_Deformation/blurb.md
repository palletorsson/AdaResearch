# Soft Body Deformation Lab

A cube that won't hold its shape. Press it, drop it, watch it recover — or not. The jelly cube is a spring-mass system: particles connected by elastic constraints, each one pulling its neighbors back toward rest. Deformation is the visible argument between force and freedom. The softmill adds rotation, grinding geometry against compliance.

This is matter at λ = 0.5. Halfway between rigid and liquid. The springs encode memory — *this is what I was* — while the physics engine applies pressure — *this is what's happening now*. Too stiff and nothing moves. Too soft and structure dissolves. The interesting things happen in between.

Form is not given. It's negotiated.
