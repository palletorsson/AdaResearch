Concept: The control room where oscillation is first encountered — oscilloscopes display waveforms, interactive cubes teach parameters (amplitude, frequency, phase), and the language of periodic motion is established before the journey begins.
Sequence role: Opens the Wavefunctions sequence; the second oscillation-phase spine sequence (after Forces). Where Forces introduced oscillation through springs and pendulums as physical consequences, this sequence treats oscillation as the primary phenomenon. The four cubes (static, rotating, oscillating, transforming) provide a progression from rest to periodic motion in miniature; leads to WaveFunctions_Pendulum.
Technical angle: x = A·sin(ωt + φ) as the governing equation, amplitude A as height of oscillation, angular frequency ω as speed of cycling, phase φ as starting position. The control_pendulum bridges from Forces' spring dynamics; oscillation_controlled_cube demonstrates parametric control. The rotating_cube_demo and transformation_cube show rotation and combined transformations as oscillatory.
Critical angle: Oscillation is the first non-trivial temporal pattern — not constant, not random, but periodic. The oscilloscope is the instrument that makes time visible (amplitude vs time). The control room metaphor frames the learner as operator, not observer — parameters are adjustable, not given. This is the QFEP oscillation phase made explicit.
Key artifacts: oscillation_controlled_cube demonstrates A·sin(ωt+φ) with adjustable parameters; control_pendulum bridges from Forces; rotating_cube_demo shows rotation as oscillation; y_oscillation_cube isolates vertical periodic motion; mario_cube_time_trace draws the oscillation history; dark_sphere anchors.
