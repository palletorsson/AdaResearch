# Hilbert 3D Curve

A single line visits every point in a cube. No backtracking, no gaps, no waste. The Hilbert curve threads through three-dimensional space by folding itself at right angles, filling volume with a path. One rewriting rule. Infinite coverage.

The grammar fits in a sentence. The geometry fills a world. Each generation quadruples the complexity — what was a simple bend becomes a labyrinth that touches every coordinate in the grid. Step onto the checkerboard. Floor and platform alternate in dense regularity. The curve is underneath, connecting all of it.

A one-dimensional line with a three-dimensional address. Dimension is not a fixed property — it is a measure of how thoroughly something refuses to stay where you put it.
