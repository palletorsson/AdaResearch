# Chamber Foundations — Summary

Chamber_Foundations is the catalyst chamber for the Foundations Crisis sequence. It is the first chamber in the curriculum whose lesson is a limit rather than a method. The chamber embodies Gödel's incompleteness as a creature the learner cannot reliably hit.

The paradox_stalker occupies the chamber in two overlapping ghost states. The two ghosts share the same position, the same silhouette, and the same apparent behaviour. Only one of them inflicts damage on contact; the other passes through without effect. The learner has no catalyst mode — there is no projection that could distinguish the two ghosts, because any test the chamber could host is already inside the formal system that posed the question.

The chamber stages the learner's attempts. A science screen on one wall scatters each attempt and its outcome as points on a plane. The hit rate is plotted as a running average, and the average converges toward exactly one half. Changing strategy does not move the line. The screen's readout labels the phenomenon directly: some questions cannot be answered from within the system that poses them.

Within the sequence, Chamber_Foundations is where mathematics admits its own edges and hands the admission to the learner as an experience rather than a theorem. The chamber does not teach a skill. It teaches a constraint, and it sends the learner back to the Lab holding the constraint in their hands.
