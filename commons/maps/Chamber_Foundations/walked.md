# Chamber_Foundations — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (seed slot, foundationscrisis thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

proximity_spawner (paradox_stalker) · catalyst_target ×4 · science_screen

## The walk

The chamber gives the crisis a body. The proximity_spawner releases a paradox_stalker — two overlapping ghosts, one real and one not, and no test in the room can tell you which. You raise the bracelet you have carried since the first chapters and place catalyst blocks on the four targets, but the stalker cannot be defeated the way a foe is defeated, because you cannot defeat what you cannot distinguish. The science_screen scatters its light over the impasse.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to step outside**. Every room of this chapter proved that a system rich enough to describe itself contains a question it cannot answer from within — and here that proof becomes a creature you meet with your hands. The stalker is the undecidable made flesh: the harder you try to resolve it from inside the room's rules, the more perfectly it doubles. You do not win. You learn the shape of not-winning, which is the chapter's whole gift — that the way past incompleteness is never a better proof but a step to the outside, a larger frame in which the undecidable becomes visible as undecidable. The catalyst lets you build that frame block by block, and the room ends not with a defeated foe but with a walker who has felt, in the body, why every system has a door.
