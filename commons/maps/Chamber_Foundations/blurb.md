Two overlapping ghosts. One is real, one is not. You cannot defeat what you cannot distinguish. Incompleteness.

This is the catalyst chamber for the Foundations Crisis — where Gödel's incompleteness gets embodied as a creature. The `paradox_stalker` exists in two overlapping states; only one inflicts damage, and no test you can run from inside the chamber can tell which. The catalyst mode is none because there is no projection that could distinguish them. The Science Screen scatters your attempts and their outcomes, letting you see that your hit rate hovers at exactly fifty percent no matter how you strategize. Some questions cannot be answered from within the formal system that poses them. This is the first map in the curriculum where the lesson is a limit, not a method.
