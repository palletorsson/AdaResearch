# Museum AAA Test Cohort — Wave 4

This 98 × 23 m connected gallery tests ten further placement contracts. It moves from a hand-operated random-walk post and wall entropy meter through self-supporting cabinets, a harmonic table, a prism specimen, an optimization island, and a working loom before opening into the room-scale Gradient Descent and Neural Network environments.

The cohort makes vertical extent explicit. In museum presentation, the Neural Network Visualization measures only its visible 9.79 × 1.36 × 9.33 m body and requests a 14 × 6 × 11 m hall. Its live telemetry now sits beneath the network while the analysis-only error graph stays out of the museum composition. The composer derives `max_height` from each room's clearance contract instead of imposing a five-metre ceiling. The Pattern Loom separately demonstrates an asymmetric contract: one metre of service space behind the machine and a three-metre protected textile runway in front.
