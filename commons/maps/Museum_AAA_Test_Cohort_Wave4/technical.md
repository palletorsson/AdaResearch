# Technical contract

Wave 4 adds four tested distinctions to the placement language:

- self-supporting cabinets use the floor directly instead of receiving a second museum plinth;
- thin instruments may move to a wall and reserve a frontal reading bay;
- productive machines may declare a directional output runway rather than symmetric clearance;
- room-scale artifacts negotiate vertical clearance as well as their XZ floor envelope.

The map composer now sets its vertical dimension to the ceiling of the greatest declared clearance or measured artifact height, while preserving a five-metre minimum for ordinary galleries. This cohort therefore produces an 11 m envelope for the monumental neural network rather than clipping it to the former fixed height. Runtime measurement ignores hidden geometry, so an analysis-only graph cannot inflate the museum contract after the artifact switches presentation.

The generated 98 × 23 × 11 m map contains ten rooms on the one-metre grid, one connected visitor route, one spawn, and one exit teleporter. Pathfinder validation reaches 487 of 493 walkable cells with zero issues. Fresh staged renders verify all ten contracts, including the integrated neural-network telemetry hall.
