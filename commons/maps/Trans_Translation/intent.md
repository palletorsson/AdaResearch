Concept: Translation as navigational infrastructure — walkways, transport cubes, and axis-constrained paths stage displacement across voids while keeping orientation and scale intact. Pure position change, nothing else.
Sequence role: Second map. After the triptych introduction, this is the first deep dive into a single transformation. Isolates translation from rotation and scale. Establishes displacement as the foundational spatial operation before the axis decomposition in Trans_Translate_2; follows Trans_Intro.
Technical angle: Translation vector addition: P' = P + t. Movement constrained to individual axes (Y and Z transport cubes). Route planning through spatial infrastructure — bridging voids that require specific directional displacement. No rotation or scaling permitted.
Critical angle: Translation as the most democratic transformation — it changes nothing about the object, only where it is. Displacement without deformation. The void is not an absence but a constraint that makes translation necessary and visible. Infrastructure (walkways, cubes) externalizes the translation vector as built environment.
Key artifacts: z_translation_cube and y_translation_cube constrain movement to single axes, making displacement explicit; player_trace records the path taken, turning translation into visible history; pickup_gate#pickups gates progress behind successful displacement; dark_sphere anchors the coordinate reference.
Gap: An artifact demonstrating failed translation — an impassable void where no transport exists — would sharpen the contrast between navigable and non-navigable displacement.
