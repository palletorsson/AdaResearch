## What this map holds (seed)

Walkways extend over void. Transport cubes shuttle across gaps that feet cannot cross. Translation is the first transformation to get a whole map to itself: not rotation's reorientation, not scale's resizing, but pure displacement — here becomes there, and nothing else about you changes.

The `y_translation_cube` and `z_translation_cube` constrain movement to single axes, making displacement explicit. The `player_trace` records the path taken, turning translation into visible history. Seven `pick_up_cube` targets scatter across broken platforms; the `pickup_gate` counts them before it opens the exit. The `transform_composition_workbench` stands at the entry with last map's warning in hand — order matters — and the `dark_sphere` anchors the one point that does not move.

## Why it was built (seed)

Concept: Translation as navigational infrastructure — walkways, transport cubes, and axis-constrained paths stage displacement across voids while keeping orientation and scale intact. Pure position change, nothing else.
Sequence role: Second map. After the triptych introduction, the first deep dive into a single transformation. Isolates translation from rotation and scale; establishes displacement as the foundational spatial operation before Trans_AxisDecomposition anatomizes it.
Critical angle: Translation as the most democratic transformation — it changes nothing about the object, only where it is. The void is not an absence but a constraint that makes translation necessary and visible. Infrastructure externalizes the translation vector as built environment.

Then the ground fails. Voids interrupt the walkable surface, and the map's answer is infrastructure: a transport cube waiting at the edge. You board, and it carries you — Z first, across the gap, past the `z_translation_cube` oscillating on its rail, ghosts stacking only along the one axis it is allowed. Deeper in, the vertical starts. An automatic lift rises on its own schedule — you synchronize to it, not it to you — and deposits you on the upper platforms where the `y_translation_cube` runs its own constrained oscillation and the `dark_sphere` hangs, pulsing, the fixed reference that makes every other motion legible.

## The turn (critical)

Translation is the identity-preserving transformation, and this map presses on what that preservation costs and covers. Position, it turns out, is not essential to identity: the cube is a cube regardless of where it sits; you are you on either side of the void. That is the map's dignity claim, and in the trans chapter it deserves saying straight — *you can be moved entirely and remain yourself*. Displacement without deformation. The change of place that changes nothing about the person.

But the critical file refuses to leave it gentle, and it is right to. Every translation here is recorded — the trace draws your path, the pickups are receipts, the gate's counter is a stamp in an invisible passport. Navigable freedom inside surveilled space: you can go anywhere, and everywhere you go is indexed. And displacement has a shadow vocabulary the map makes room for — refugees are displaced, evicted tenants are displaced, translated populations carry the root word: moved by systems larger than themselves. The transport cube is gentle, recreational; it also rehearses that structure. You board, you are carried, you are deposited. Whether the ride was chosen is the entire political content of the word.

So the turn is double, and both halves are true: translation proves that where you are is contingent to what you are — the most democratic transformation, the one that touches nothing — and in a computed world there is no movement without record. The void remains after you cross it. The bridge does not heal the space; it lets you pass while the absence persists. Someone designed that absence. Someone counts your crossings.
