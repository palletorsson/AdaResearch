The chamber's verb is *to connect*.

A quiet room after the flows and cuts. You place a block and the room reads it as a node — then draws its edges to whatever else you have placed. Place another, and the connections rewire themselves around it.

The whole chapter taught the squint that turns any world into vertices and edges; here you perform the squint with your hands. You are not building objects but adjacencies, and the graph that emerges is authored by nothing but what you chose to place near what. The pathfinder certified every floor in this world by exactly this reading. Now you hold the pen. A graph is not found in the world — it is drawn onto it, one placed relation at a time.
