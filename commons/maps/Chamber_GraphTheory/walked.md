## The cast

becoming_catalyst · catalyst_target · science_screen

## The walk

A quiet room after the flows and cuts. The becoming_catalyst rests on its pedestal; you take the bracelet and place a block on the catalyst_target, and the room reads it as a node — then draws its edges to whatever else you have placed, the science_screen holding the graph steady as it grows. Place another, and the connections rewire themselves around it.

## The seed

The chamber's verb is **to connect**. The whole chapter taught the squint that turns any world into vertices and edges, and here you perform the squint with your hands: every block is a node, and the moment two exist, the room insists on the relation between them. You are not building objects but *adjacencies* — and the graph that emerges is authored by nothing but what you chose to place near what. The pathfinding chapter certified every floor in this world by exactly this reading; now you hold the pen. To connect is to decide what counts as near, and the room plants that decision in the body: a graph is not found in the world, it is *drawn onto* it, one placed relation at a time.
