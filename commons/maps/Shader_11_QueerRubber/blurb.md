Materials are lies about light. PBR models how photons scatter off surfaces — roughness, metallic, subsurface scattering. The GPU fakes physics until your hand reaches out.

Leather without sound. Latex catching highlights too sharp, too wet. Oil slick fracturing the spectrum through thin-film interference. Pop plastic with exaggerated Fresnel. Fur breaking silhouettes — anisotropic shading, every strand a tiny cylinder bending light differently. Rim lighting traces the boundary where object meets void.

Drape each across the dark sphere. Watch it pulse purple beneath new skin.

Every texture here is procedural. No photographs. Pure math pretending to be matter. The question is not how to render realistically — it is which surfaces get rendered at all. Every material shader encodes a claim about which bodies deserve to be touched.
