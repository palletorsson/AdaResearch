# Primitives Columns — map summary

Twenty-five authored columns give profile, height and sampling a room of their own. Five matching bodies at the entrance isolate radial counts 4, 6, 8, 12 and 16. Four later rows vary profile and height as well as resolution.

The supplied builder makes ArrayMesh geometry. The museum specimens are not live parameter editors. The existing reduced column study supplies a smaller place to edit radii, full height and side count.
