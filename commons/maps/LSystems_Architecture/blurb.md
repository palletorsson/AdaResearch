# 04 Architecture

Replace one word and the world changes. Substitute "branch" for "corridor" and a tree becomes a dungeon. Substitute "leaf" for "room" and a forest becomes a city. The grammar never moved. The turtle did.

Walk a procedural street grid generated from the same rewriting rules that grew plants three maps ago. A city generator pipes orthogonal L-system output into buildings and roads. Beside it, a dungeon unfolds on the XZ plane — corridors and chambers emerging from string production, rooms budding where branches once split. Same axiom, same recursion. Different interpreter, different architecture.

The rule system doesn't know what it builds. It rewrites symbols. Meaning arrives at the point of interpretation — when F stops meaning "forward" and starts meaning "floor." Architecture is grammar that forgot it was language.
