Sequence role: Eleventh map in the Fractals sequence (10th spine, lambda_edge phase). Post-synthesis extension into stochastic and reflective territory. The inverted_tree_cloud, seeded in Fractals_2, now becomes the primary artifact — what was a preview becomes a subject. Connects forward to Fractals_12 where individual trees compose into a fractal forest scene; follows Fractals_10.
Technical angle: Stochastic L-system trees with bidirectional growth: the standard tree branches upward with angle and length perturbation, while the inverted tree mirrors the branching rule with negated growth direction, producing root systems. The living_paper artifact applies recursive subdivision to a planar surface, creating a paper-like material whose folds and textures are generated by the same branching grammar as the trees.
Critical angle: Trees grow down as well as up — the root system is the tree's shadow, a mirror-image fractal hidden underground. The inverted_tree_cloud makes this duality visible, challenging the assumption that recursion has a preferred direction. Living paper extends the lambda_edge principle to materiality: if recursive rules can generate trees, they can generate surfaces, textures, and materials. The fractal is no longer an object to be observed but a substrate to be inhabited.
Key artifacts: inverted_tree_cloud demonstrates bidirectional recursive branching — canopy above, roots below; living_paper applies recursive subdivision to material surface generation; recursive_tree serves as the canonical upward-branching reference; dark_sphere anchors.
