# Puzzle 01: Copy - The Duplicator

A 9×9 grid. Gaps in a pattern. One agent that knows one operation: duplicate what's here and place it there.

COPY is the most primitive creative act — not invention, but replication. Capture the grid agent, aim it, watch it read a cube and write an identical one into empty space. The pattern completes itself through directed repetition. No transformation, no novelty. Pure duplication.

But duplication is never pure. Every copy exists in a new position, a new context. The "same" cube in a different cell changes the structure. Identity through repetition — the paradox that drives biology, culture, computation. DNA copies. Printing presses copy. `memcpy` copies. The universe builds complexity not from uniqueness but from the relentless act of making more of what already exists.

First lesson: creation begins with repetition.
