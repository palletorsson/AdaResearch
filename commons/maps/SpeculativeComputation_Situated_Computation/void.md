## 1. One subject, four addresses

Floor 203 opens with four inward-facing `situated_readout` panels that all name the exact same subject — **`SUBJECT #4729`** — while delivering four different authored verdicts and percentages:

- **NW:** `ORDERLY` · `confidence 87%`
- **NE:** `CREATIVE` · `confidence 64%`
- **SW:** `DANGEROUS` · `confidence 92%`
- **SE:** `IRRELEVANT` · `confidence 22%`

Stay with `SW` (`DANGEROUS 92%`) and test its address controls. Switching `footnote` to `erasure` (**COVER**) drops an angled slab of overpaint across the footer; switching to `witness` bolts a steel column, a floor cross-plate, a height scale, and a glowing symbolic eye on a mast above the frame. Neither action changes `DANGEROUS`, neither alters `92%`, and neither earns a single new observation of `#4729`. Removing an address performs the *god-trick* (the view from nowhere); giving a readout a dramatic body performs authority — yet **an address makes a claim traceable, not true**.

## 2. Four stations, one walk — separating evidence from definition

Across the hall stands `four-stations`, the room's earned next experiment: a six-metre square field with four corner stations (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE`). Each station only sees what passes inside its `3.4 m` sight arc, and each holds its own definition of `DANGEROUS`:

- **NW:** more than `0.5 m` off the centre line
- **NE:** faster than `2.0 m/s`
- **SW:** within `1.5 m` of the station
- **SE:** inside its sight arc longer than `3 s`

On the builder's `6.43 s` walk (`2.4 m/s` sprint up the south line, then a slow `0.6 m/s` swing `1.4 m` west), `1.88 s` passes in grey dots **unseen by any corner**. Toggle the two floor plates to separate the causes of disagreement:

1. **As found (`own`):** `1` station says `DANGEROUS` (`NW`), `3` say `NOT DANGEROUS` — sight and meaning are tangled together.
2. **ONE DEFINITION (`speed > 2.0 m/s`):** the four still split `2 vs 2` (`SW`/`SE` saw the south sprint; `NW`/`NE` only saw the slow swing) — isolating disagreement caused by **what each saw**.
3. **ONE RECORD (all four given the full `6.43 s` walk):** the four still split `3 vs 1` (`SW` stayed `>= 2.35 m` away) — isolating disagreement caused by **what each means**.
4. **BOTH (`ONE DEFINITION` + `ONE RECORD`):** all four agree (`4/4 DANGEROUS`).

*A small event can make the next one possible.* — Read all four SUBJECT #4729 panels, toggle COVER/WITNESS on DANGEROUS 92%, then compare ONE DEFINITION vs ONE RECORD at Four Stations to separate missing evidence from different rules.

## 3. Cameras that arrive late

Beside the readouts stand the five monitors of `museum_witnesses`: portable cameras `A` and `B`, fixed camera `C`, delayed feed `D`, and held frame `E`. Even when every screen films the exact same hall, holding or delaying a frame separates *where* a view was taken from *when* it arrives. Objectivity here is never the absence of a standpoint — it is the **careful accounting of the position, instrument, definition, and timestamp you are speaking from**.
