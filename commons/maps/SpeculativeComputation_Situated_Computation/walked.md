## The cast

three viewing platforms · situated_readout · annotation bench · standpoint classifier

## The walk

One dataset, three platforms. Climb the ground-level stand, then the aerial one, then the slanted local-community vantage: the `situated_readout` renders the same data differently from each, though every underlying coordinate, height and distance is constant. Same input, different verdicts — and the map's tutorial discipline is that the difference is not an error to be reconciled but a *measurement to be recorded*. That is what the annotation bench is for: at each platform you mark what this view shows and what it hides, and the annotations accumulate into a running account that reads as partial and located rather than universal — a log of perspectives instead of a claim from nowhere. The second station sharpens it into machine learning: train a small classifier from a chosen standpoint and the standpoint surfaces in the output labels, visibly, rather than laundering itself into "the model says." The skill being taught is genuinely technical: how to *account for* a vantage point — declare it, instrument it, ship it with the result — instead of subtracting it and calling the subtraction objectivity.

## The turn (critical)

Haraway named the fantasy this map dismantles: the **god-trick**, the view from everywhere and nowhere, objectivity imagined as a perspective so total it stops being one. Computation inherited the trick wholesale — the algorithm runs "the same" anywhere, temperature-invariant, body-invariant, nowhere-shaped — and the map's patient answer is that a recommender in Lagos is not the same object as a recommender in San Francisco even when the code is byte-identical, because training data, priors, latency, expectation and the power grid all silently relocate it. Her alternative was never relativism, and the bench is built to prove it: *situated knowledges* — partial, locatable, critical — hold each other accountable precisely because each states where it stands; the view from a body can be interrogated, while the view from nowhere cannot even be found. So objectivity gets redefined operationally: not the absence of a perspective but the **careful accounting of the one you're in**. Note what this does to the sequence's arc: paraconsistency taught systems to carry contradiction; situatedness teaches them to carry an address. Both are the same post-crisis gesture — stop demanding that knowledge arrive purified, and build the instruments that let it arrive honest. The gap between the three platforms is not a bug in the readout. It is the knowledge telling you, for once, where it comes from.
