A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 arena carved inside it. The border holds; the center doesn't.

Walkers move without destination — each step chosen by coin flip, cardinal direction, pure chance. They raise the ground where they pass, leaving trails in displaced vertices. The flat plane remembers. Terrain forms not from design but from accumulated accident. A dark sphere pulses at the edge, marking nothing, watching everything.

Random displacement lifts vertices by fractions — ±0.5 at most. The mesh breathes. What was flat becomes undulating, organic, unpredictable. No two runs produce the same surface. The algorithm doesn't sculpt; it wanders, and the wandering *is* the sculpture.

Randomness isn't the absence of pattern. It's what pattern looks like before you've decided what counts as order.
