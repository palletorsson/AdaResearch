# Tutorial 3D

Two dimensions flatten. Three dimensions inhabit. The grid gains a vertical axis — X, Y, and now Z. Height enters the data structure. What was a spreadsheet becomes a volume.

Stepped platforms rise along the north and west edges. Lifts carry the body upward through layers of the same array. A dark sphere pulses at the periphery, slow purple light marking the threshold. At the center, a 4×4×4 cube — sixty-four cells suspended in space, each one addressable, each one exact. `grid[x][y][z]`. Three indices to name a point in volume.

Every element still sits at a known address. The order holds. But the body now moves through it differently — climbing, descending, occupying the structure from inside. The grid doesn't change. The relationship to it does.
