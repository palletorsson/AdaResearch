## The turn (critical)

The critique cuts at the chapter's own foundation. Fourier says every signal is a sum of sines — but who decided what counts as *signal*? Cage's answer: the border between sound and noise, signal and silence, is drawn by attention and convention, not by the waveform. Asawa's answer: the wave's dignity does not require an oscilloscope; a woven basket knows it. The chapter's most confident theorem meets the two artists who show that its words — signal, noise, silence — were value judgments all along.
