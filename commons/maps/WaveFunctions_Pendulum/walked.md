## The walk

The second walk slows everything down until oscillation becomes observable. The PendulumWave is the room's spell — a row of pendulums, each a little detuned, drifting in and out of every possible pattern, order and chaos taking turns without a rule change. The foucault_pendulum swings the largest possible frame: its slow precession is the *planet* oscillating under the room. The seismograph and the draw-dot both do the chapter's recording gesture — motion smeared into a trace — and the WavePaintings hang the results as what they always were: drawings the world makes of itself.

## What it fixes

Period, phase, and the trace. After the sound room's speed, this room teaches patience: a wave is also a thing that takes its time, and the instruments that know it best — pendulum, seismograph — are the ones that just watch.
