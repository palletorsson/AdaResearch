Concept: Where a movement puts its past. One pendulum, integrated on the fixed physics step, and a finite record of its positions laid out behind it by age, so that a returning motion becomes a series of distinct marks and the record's two limits (300 marks, 10 seconds) can be read in the room.
Sequence role: Second active hall in Wavefunctions. The Intro gave a returning bob two velocities at one position and kept only a count of its crossings; this hall gives the return a history with a depth axis and shows what a finite, sampled record keeps and loses. Follows WaveFunctions_Intro; leads to WaveFunctions_Sine_Space, where the same rising and falling is laid along a passage in space.
Technical angle: `PendulumWave.gd`: motion in `_physics_process` (symplectic Euler, angular acceleration -(g/L)·sin θ, fixed 60 Hz step, no damping so the amplitude and period hold); a tick-quantized sampler pushing (x, y, t) samples with targets FINE 25 ms, COARSE 200 ms or STROBE = the period measured from two successive upward crossings of the centre; two eviction rules, count over 300 and age over 10 s; depth = age × 0.6 m per second; a cased readout printing count, span kept, target interval, actual timestamp gaps, depth rate, period and step; RESET returns this experiment's swing, clock and list to their start and nothing else.
Critical angle: A record is a list with its own rules, separate from the rule that moves the thing recorded. The fine sampler spends its memory on resolution and loses span; the coarse one keeps the span and loses the shape; a sampler given the rhythm of its subject makes the motion disappear. What a record keeps was decided before the reader arrived, and the reader can change it here.
