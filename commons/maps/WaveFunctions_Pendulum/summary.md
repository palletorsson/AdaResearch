# WaveFunctions_Pendulum — Summary

A pendulum swings under a gallows frame at the head of the hall's open south half, its bob at hand height. Behind it, a line of small marks recedes six metres down the hall toward the exit. Each mark is a sample of the bob's position, kept together with the experiment time it was taken at and drawn at a depth of 0.6 metres per second of age. Walking south beside the record, the visitor sees the swing side-on as a wave that the bob itself never drew.

The record has two limits. At most three hundred marks are kept, and nothing older than ten seconds. A cased readout three metres down the trail prints the count, the span kept, the sampling interval, the depth rate, the measured period and the physics step. A four-button panel on the east post switches the sampler. FINE targets 25 ms, recorded on alternating 16.7/33.3 ms physics-tick gaps at 60 Hz; three hundred entries retain about 7.5 seconds. COARSE targets 200 ms and retains around fifty entries under the age limit. The readout distinguishes the target from measured timestamp gaps. STROBE uses the measured period (or a small-angle estimate until available), producing near-aligned samples with some tick-scale spread. RESET returns this experiment's swing, clock and list to their start and touches nothing else in the room.

The motion runs on the fixed physics step, so the record does not depend on how often the headset redraws it. On the west walkway a seismograph keeps a comparable record on a drum, where time is an angle. The north half keeps the Foucault pendulum, the double-pendulum paintings and the dot plotter as other pendulums and other recorders.

Within the sequence this is the second active hall. The Intro kept a count of a bob's crossings; this hall keeps a history and shows what a finite, sampled record loses. Sine Space, next, lays the same rising and falling along a passage in space.


## Measured (2026-09-13)

Every button on the panel now answers a real press. The record does not care how fast the room is drawn: at thirty frames a second and at sixty it keeps the same 120 marks over the same three seconds. Past its limit of 300 marks it keeps losing its oldest as it gains the newest, and nothing grows while it does.
