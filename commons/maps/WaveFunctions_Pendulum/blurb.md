Gravity creates rhythm. A weight on a string swings, tracing time with its body. The pendulum is sine made physical — restoring force proportional to displacement, perpetual return toward equilibrium that momentum always overshoots.

The `PendulumWave` runs several lengths side by side so you can see phase separate them: identical forces, different periods, the ensemble rippling in and out of alignment. The `foucault_pendulum` writes the Earth's rotation onto the laboratory floor — the swing does not precess, the ground does. The `seismograph` and `draw_dot_time_domain` record the motion as trace you can read after it stops. Oscillation is not designed. Any system displaced from equilibrium with a restoring force oscillates. The pendulum just makes the inevitability visible.
