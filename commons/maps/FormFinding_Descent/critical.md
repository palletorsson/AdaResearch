# Who supplied the cost?

Two starting points, one rule, two accepted endings. The lower valley remains visible from the other. That is the encounter's critical opening: the visitor's overview contains a possibility the update has no means to pursue.

SMALL SLOPE is an operational judgment. Its tolerance is chosen, its samples are finite, and its endpoint need not be globally best. LEFT FRAME and STEP LIMIT retain different reasons for stopping. Keeping those names visible prevents a technical ending from quietly becoming a claim of completeness.

The queer possibility here belongs to what changing a beginning, rule or threshold makes inhabitable. It does not require calling every minimum death or every departure freedom. A cost function can omit relationships we care about. Changing that function changes what the search values; improving its answer need not repair the omission.

The plotted landscape and the sampled function are also different constructions. Triangles give the visitor a surface; finite differences give the update a local measurement. Neither provides unencoded access to an original. Their disagreement is something we can inspect and work with.

This hall does not establish that organisms, learning systems and hanging chains all minimise the same quantity. It prepares a methodological question for each subsequent model: what is variable, what is held fixed, which measure organises change, and who decides when enough has happened?
