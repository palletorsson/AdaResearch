# Remainder

Eight readings of the same bench, in a room that closes as the readings seal.

You arrive on open floor. Two benches stand apart in it. One shows nine bars of light with gaps between them. The other shows nine bars that touch, at half the brightness, and a dozen definite little marks scattered along its margins. The same amount of light, differently sorted. Something out here is holding a record.

The only way on is a single gap in a wall, with the two halves of that wall standing beside it. On your left a closed instrument with a seal across it. On your right the face is off and two lamps are lit. Look up: both screens are the same. Reading the record changed nothing, because it was the record existing that did the work.

Past the gap there are two lanes with no door between them, and a cell with one door. Go into the cell last. Whatever you decide about the two things in there, you will have to decide it somewhere else.
