## The walk

The walk is a corridor of mutual attraction. Two bodies first — a clean ellipse, Newton keeping his promise. Then three, and the promise wavers: the three_body_problem runs the same law with one more participant and the orbit stops rhyming. By n bodies the room is a weather of pulls, every mass a cause and a consequence at once. The spring_system and particle_systems answer from the side — constrained force, force en masse — and the chaos_attractor at depth traces where all of this ends: determinism that cannot be predicted, only watched. The graph laid out by forcedirected3d makes the joke structural — even *data* settles by pretending its edges are springs.

## The turn inside the walk

F = ma never breaks in this room. That is the unsettling part. Every unpredictable curl is the law obeyed exactly; chaos is not the law failing but the law compounding. The chapter's cleanest sentence — force is what changes states — survives with its confidence removed.
