## The cast

network_analysis · networkflow3d · rhizomatic_structure

## The walk

The graph starts doing work. The network_analysis station measures what a network quietly knows about its members — degree, centrality, who is a hub and who a periphery; the networkflow3d sends traffic through the structure and watches where it thickens. And between the two instruments grows the rhizomatic_structure: Deleuze and Guattari's counter-image, a network with no center to measure, any point connectable to any other, spreading like couch grass through the analysts' garden.

## What it fixes

The walk pairs measurement with its critique in one room. Centrality metrics are real — networks do have chokepoints, and finding them is power — but the rhizome stands there insisting that "who is central" is a question some structures refuse to answer. Analysis reveals the shape of a network; it also *imposes* one. The room lets you hold both instruments and feel which one the structure under your hands actually fits.
