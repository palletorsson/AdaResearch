## The walk

The color chapter opens, deliberately, without color theory — it opens with a foundry. Four pattern machines, same idea in four bodies, stamping ornament the way industry stamps anything: repeatably, indifferently, at scale. You arrive among machines that make *surface*, because that is where color will live for the rest of the chapter — on surfaces, produced, chosen, applied.

## What it opens

The threshold's quiet claim: before color is perception it is *production*. Someone mixes it, prints it, ships it. The four machines running their patterns are the chapter clearing its throat — everything you are about to see was made by something like these.
