# Where can a difference remain?

The colour chamber ended with one message and several receiving operations. Here one source supplies many marks. Shared authorship is useful: an edit can travel further than the hand that made it. The same connection makes a local exception hard to keep.

Try to change just one repeated mark at the tile station. The source cell changes, and its other appearances change with it. This is a limit of this instrument's representation. It does not establish that repetition must exclude exceptions, or that a less regular pattern is necessarily freer.

The loom's held curve supplies a small alternative. It declines the next source image while its flat neighbour accepts it. The difference is a relation between two receivers and two moments. No randomness was needed to produce it. But its unit of independence is still a whole image. A visitor wanting one red mark to remain inside an otherwise changing field needs a finer editor.

The bent sheet also interrupts an easy identification of sameness. Its colour addresses match those of the flat sheet, and its surface width matches along the arc. Their appearances from one standing position differ. Moving lets the visitor discover a contribution that was already present: the carrier and the viewpoint participate in what the pattern becomes. Neither sheet is the original to which the other must be corrected.

For Ada's question, what bodies are possible, the implemented answer now includes the room's inner wall. Its larger field uses the same cell spacing as the sheets. Giving it this pattern alters neither its collider nor its capacity to block a body. The common source lets us compare receivers without making all their capacities identical. Dressing the avatar or giving cloth physical softness still requires other receiving operations.

The queer possibility here is an invitation to author which relations stay shared and which can separate. It does not reside in a prescribed palette or a machine-certified degree of disorder. Selecting WALL and holding it makes a piece of the background able to retain a different moment from the foreground samples. UNDO LINK makes that separation revisable.

The ALL FIVE label also names a boundary. Its apparent totality depends on which receivers were registered. It leaves the carpet, other machines, outer museum walls and the visitor's outfit outside its scope. As we extend the panel, that selection must remain legible: a promise that the whole hall can change should be tested against the actual surfaces included.

Changing repeat size makes another distinction available. A mark can occupy more of a surface without gaining another source cell. The same small motif becomes harder to overlook as its copies grow. A held wall and a live sheet can carry different scales of that motif at the same time. This is a change in the space given to a decision, without a change in the decision's stored detail. The one-centimetre render sampling remains a separate limit; enlarging the motif does not recover information that was never recorded.

ARCHITECTURE now groups three inner-wall faces. Holding this group leaves the foreground specimens free to change; holding EAST alone makes a difference within the background. These are overlapping selections of the same source relationship, rather than permanent categories of matter. The grouping is authored. Its promise should extend only as far as its actual membership, which still excludes the outer museum walls.

Under the starting translation rule, the zero and full-period offsets give the same visible field while storing different address offsets. Appearance alone cannot distinguish those states. This modest ambiguity can be discovered by pressing the control and watching its number, without claiming that every symmetry or every other system has the same periodicity. A held wall can meanwhile retain a different relation between mark and edge.
