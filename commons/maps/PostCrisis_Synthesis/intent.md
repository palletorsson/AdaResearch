Critical angle: The map resists monument. The Foundations Crisis is reframed not as a failure of mathematics but as the moment the discipline admitted its own edges, and the post-crisis practice that follows is characterised by careful, partial, accountable use of formal tools rather than by their abandonment. The closing sentence does the work of a thesis without the volume of one.
