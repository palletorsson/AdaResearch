## The walk

A central raised platform — the ground you earned — ringed by miniatures of the rooms you just walked: the divided room, the case tables, the Florensky sphere, the three platforms, the four-agent commons, the rootless cave, the modest laboratory. Each miniature surfaces a short recap when you approach; the map is a small interactive index of its own sequence, and the walk is a deliberate act of remembering — take the circuit, let each room restate itself in one sentence, and notice that the sentences now compose: ethics as architecture; contradiction held, not avoided; knowledge with an address; a commons instead of a proof; rhizomes instead of trees; molecular design instead of reduction. Above the plinth, `qfep_formula_3d` glows — QFE = F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t) — the same formula the laboratory sequence taught term by term, standing here as the closing mark. The plinth beneath it carries one sentence without ornament. Read it and stand on the platform for a moment. There is no task left in this room. That is the design.

## The turn (critical)

The map resists monument, and the resistance is the content. A triumphant capstone would betray everything the arc established — completion was the fantasy the crisis dissolved — so the synthesis is built as an *index*, the most modest architectural form the sequence owns: small, clickable, revisable, a table of contents rather than a temple. Its reframing is stated plainly: the foundations crisis was not mathematics failing but mathematics **growing up** — learning its own edges, retiring the view from nowhere — and everything after is engineering with that knowledge. The practices in the miniatures are not compromises, not the consolation prizes of a discipline that wanted certainty and settled for less; they are what *becomes possible* when the demand for completeness is withdrawn — you cannot build a paraconsistent database or a situated classifier or a corrigible commons while still pretending a complete system is on its way. And the formula overhead earns its place by what it no longer claims. QFE = F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t) hangs here not as the theory of everything but as a *worked tool* — order, entropy, becoming, held in one line by a curriculum that has spent twenty-two sequences showing where each term lives and what each term costs. The edge is not the limit; the edge is the ground. The book's last room hands the walker forward, not upward — the exit panel names a continuation, not a conclusion, because the only honest ending for a curriculum about incompleteness is the one that does not close.
