# AdvancedLaboratory_Entropy_Visualization

A 12×12 grid. An 8×8 arena carved from its center. Algorithms enter, execute, leave traces. This is the operating theater where randomness gets caught in the act — shuffles, selections, distributions made visible as colored cells flickering across the field. Each algorithm a different strategy for introducing or managing disorder.

The Mandelbrot set watches from the wall. Infinite complexity from z² + c. A reminder that the boundary between order and chaos isn't a line — it's a fractal, infinitely detailed at every scale. The arena runs finite processes; the fractal promises they never exhaust what's possible.

Randomness isn't absence of pattern. It's pattern too dense to name. The grid makes this legible — eight rows, eight columns, every cell a decision point where structure and entropy negotiate.
