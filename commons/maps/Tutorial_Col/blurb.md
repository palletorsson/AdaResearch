A column is a line drawn through depth. Seven rows, three wide — a corridor that only asks one question: forward or back.

The spawn point places you at the origin. Movement along Y means moving *into* the grid, each step a new row index. A dark sphere pulses at the edges, ambient marker, proof that space is already alive before you arrive. The coordinate visualizer at the far end names your position in three numbers — x, y, z — giving language to what your body already knows.

Walk the column. Watch the numbers change. One axis is enough to produce direction, and direction is enough to produce narrative. A single degree of freedom — forward, backward — and already the grid has a story. Every journey begins with the discovery that depth is not given. It is traversed.
