## The cast

fifteen_cases_demo · mc_cave

## The walk

The whole algorithm fits on a plinth. The fifteen_cases_demo shows the trick at its smallest: a cube has eight corners, each either in or out of the field — 256 configurations, which symmetry folds down to fifteen. Fifteen little tile-patterns, and that is the entire vocabulary. Beside it the mc_cave puts the vocabulary to work at room scale — a cavern marched cell by cell out of a scalar field, every square meter of its walls one of the fifteen tiles, looked up and glued.

## What it teaches

The primitive is the lookup table as a complete language. Marching cubes never *understands* the shape it builds; it reads eight bits, consults the table, places the tile, and marches on. Every isosurface in this chapter — terrain, sculpture, cave — is these fifteen cases repeated without comprehension. The room teaches the move this project trusts most: a tiny alphabet, honestly applied everywhere, speaking worlds it cannot see.
