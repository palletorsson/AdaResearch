# Random_Mushrooms — field notes

## 2026-09-12 — R4: which parts of this population were allowed to vary (Fable, the next hall on the route after Random_Gaussian; Astra's card: "retain the meadow's pleasurable variety; add a small specimen/table view linking one template to several instances and a reachable local recipe/seed control; separate template choice, size variation, placement acceptance and explicit ring/cluster construction; one fixed population seed that covers those choices if replay is offered; ground alone is insufficient; keep an accessible margin; check the actual ground collider; cap lights, meshes and spawning")

**The ruling.** The card asks for a station that reveals the meadow's decisions without labelling every specimen, a population seed that covers every draw, the four decisions separable, replay compared on full instance transforms and the ground, requested and accepted counts, bounded lights, and a walk that says what carries a visitor. The primary had the decisions and none of the means: six templates built and duplicated, eighty candidates gated by a noise threshold, a yaw and a size per copy, rings and clusters by their own routines — all on the global `randf()` / `randi()` stream, so no population could be seen twice; `ground_seed` seeded the ground alone; `apply_grid_config` was `pass`, so no token could reach it; and the templates, never entering the tree, outlived the meadow (fifty-one objects leaked at exit).

**What the museum built before anything was touched (the survey run, 09:12).** The dealt lane slid every body off its cell (the meadow from (4,6) to (8,10), the bubbles to the north wall, the reaction-diffusion display and the RAND pages into the west corridor) and shrank the meadow to 65% to reopen the walk; at 65% the ten-metre meadow still ran to z 13.73 in a hall thirteen deep. Its ground undulated ±0.12 around the deck, so the deck cut through it and the floor rays under it hit the museum's collision at 0.0, not the meadow's ground; the walk down the middle stopped at 0.61. 127 mushrooms (94 scattered, 21 ring, 12 cluster), 14 glowing with 15 omni lights, 291 mesh instances, 22 multimeshes. No walk severed. Fifty-one leaked objects at exit.

**What changed, and why.**

1. *The draws (R6).* Every `randf()` / `randi()` of the build became `_rf()` / `_ri()`: with `population_seed` −1 (the shipped default) they fall through to the global calls, same call, same order, so the shipped meadow is byte-for-byte the same population process; under a non-negative seed a private generator makes every draw and the ground's generator takes seed + 1. `regrow()` rebuilds in the build's order. The probe's replay check compares every instance's template, kind, group, position, yaw and scale (a hundred of them) and a hash of the 1369 ground heights, not the seed.
2. *Bookkeeping on every path.* `_candidates_requested`, `_rejected` (the positions the threshold refused), `template` / `kind` / `group` metadata on every instance, a record per ring and per cluster of what it requested and what it placed, glowing instances and lit lights counted through `_note_light`. No returned value changes.
3. *The specimen table (opt-in `stand:specimen`; R4 · R2 · R7).* A 1.5 × 0.5 × 0.9 m body at the bed's +z edge (the token is turned 180° so it faces the north door) with the six templates duplicated onto numbered discs at 0.8 scale (the type specimens), a stencil SPECIMENS · ONE POPULATION, a housed six-line plate leaning back 12° (the seed and REGROW's policy; candidates, accepted, rejected; rings and clusters with what they placed, templates; the SHOW or KIND line with its count; the size rule or SIZE off; glow, lit of the cap, mushrooms), and SHOW · KIND · SIZE / REGROW · NEW SEED beside it at 32°. The highlight is a torus outline wider than a cap on the ground and a pin above each marked instance, one MultiMesh each: orange for every copy of the shown template, blue for the scattered, green for ring members, violet for cluster members, grey for the rejected candidates. `stand:none` builds none of it.
4. *The bed.* Under the stand the ground, the field, the grass, the rocks and the leaves are lifted by the ground's amplitude (0.18 m) so the ground stands on the deck instead of being cut by it, and four boards kerb it at the ground's highest reach. The lights are capped at twelve (the cap keeps its emissive material past the cap).
5. *Map tokens (R8).* `apply_grid_config` reads `size` (the bed's side; whitelisted), `count`, `density`, `seed` and `stand`; the museum's config arrives before `_ready` and is stored, the grid's after and regrows. `mushrooms:180#stand:specimen#size:6` at (6,7): a six-metre bed (x 3.5–9.5, z 4.5–10.5) with the table's face at z 3.1 and the visitor's spot at (6.5, 2.3), 1.8 m in from the north door. At six metres the shipped arithmetic makes `int(6 / 5)` = one ring and `int(6 / 3)` = two clusters; the ten-metre meadow made two and three.
6. *The other bodies.* The dark sphere to (10,2), the bubbles to (10,4) and (10,9), the RAND pages to (10,7) a metre up, the reaction-diffusion display to (9,11): the east nook and the south margin, off the walk. `artifact_placement: "map"`, `sculpture_clear_rects [[3,1,10,12]]` (cells 3–9 × 1–11; the far edge is exclusive), `floor_cells [[8,12]]` for the teleporter's void cell (the grid keeps its `0`).
7. *The leak.* `_exit_tree` frees the templates.

**Rejected.** A ring-count or cluster-count override on the token (the counts are the shipped arithmetic of the bed's size; a token that set them would have the plate report a number the rule did not make). Walking into the bed (the ground's cliff at the kerb stops a walker; the bed is a specimen bed seen from its edge and the table, and the west and south margins carry the walk). A label on every specimen beyond its disc number (the card: "without making every specimen into a label"; the plate names the template by number and SHOW marks its copies). A per-frame anything: the bed is built once and rebuilt on a press.
