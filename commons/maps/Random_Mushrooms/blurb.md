A raised bed of mushrooms and, before it, a specimen table: six bodies on numbered discs, a plate that counts the population, and five buttons. Every mushroom in the bed is a copy of one of the six, turned and sized by a draw; eighty candidates were scattered and a noise threshold refused some; a ring and two clusters were placed on purpose with rules of their own. SHOW rings and pins every copy of one template, KIND marks the scattered, the ringed, the clustered and the refused, SIZE turns one rule off under the same seed, REGROW grows the same population again and NEW SEED another.

The `mushrooms` artifact is the primary. Around it: `reaction_diffusion_intro` (a Gray-Scott field that makes spots from two rates), the bubbles (`bubbles_random`, `bubble_particles`), the RAND Corporation's 1955 digits cascading on `random_number_book_page_collection`, and the dark sphere. The question is which parts of this population were allowed to vary, and the answer is written rule by rule: which of six, which way, how big, where, and whether at all. Nothing here grows; the bed is an arrangement made once and made again on request.
