# Museum AAA Lambda — Technical

The playable map uses:

```text
lambda_slider:0:0#calibration:dispute
```

The console’s old 8 × 8 m size-oracle estimate was rejected. Runtime geometry measures 0.69 × 0.33 × 1.30 m; its canonical contract is one 3 × 3 × 3 m wall bay with the slider as the sole artifact. The rear row is the wall and the front two rows remain clear for approach and XR interaction.

Only rotation `0` is permitted. This preserves the relation between the console face, wall backing, and approach direction.
