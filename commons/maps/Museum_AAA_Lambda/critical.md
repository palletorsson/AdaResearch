# Museum AAA Lambda — Critical reflection

## The core tension

A slider presents a continuous range as though the world had already agreed on what its ends mean. The `dispute` calibration refuses that agreement.

## The body and calibration

The visitor can choose a position, but the wall records several incompatible optima. Agency remains real while authority becomes visible.

## QFEP connection

Lambda names an entropy drive between crystallization and dissolution. This room treats the edge of chaos as a political and epistemic negotiation, not a green sweet spot painted onto a universal ruler.
