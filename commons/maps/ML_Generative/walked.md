## The walk

One arena, two adversaries. The generative_adversarial_networks_gans_vr splits the room into a generator's zone and a discriminator's zone and sets them at each other's throats: the generator forges samples, the discriminator calls the fakes, and every round each one's failure trains the other toward mastery. Walk between them and you are walking a duel that produces, as its by-product, images that never existed and cannot be caught.

## The world

The world register is where the chapter's stakes come home, because this is the machinery now loose in the world — the engine of synthetic faces, deepfakes, generated voices, the flood of the plausibly-real. The GAN's unsettling proof is that *convincing* can be reached without any understanding whatsoever: the generator knows nothing about faces, only about fooling a critic that knows nothing about faces either, and out of two ignorances pressed together comes something that passes. This is learning-as-optimization arriving at its most consequential form — a landscape you can't fully see, climbed by two blind adversaries, whose summit is a world where the difference between the found and the fabricated stops being visible. The room does not moralize. It just lets you stand in the arena and feel how little understanding it took to make the counterfeit indistinguishable.
