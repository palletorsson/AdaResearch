Concept: Generative models as learned dreamers — networks trained on data distributions that can sample new instances from the learned manifold. The model does not memorize examples; it learns the structure that makes them possible, then hallucinates variations.
Sequence role: Seventh map in the Machine Learning sequence (15th spine sequence, integration phase). The creative turn in the arc — after classification (discriminative) and sequence memory, the model now produces rather than categorizes. Connects back to ML_Sequence_Memory (generative models often use recurrence) and forward to ML_Synthesis (where all threads converge). The generative capacity connects laterally to QFEP's edge of chaos: generation happens at the boundary between memorization and noise.
Technical angle: Generative adversarial networks (GANs) with generator-discriminator dynamics as a minimax game. Variational autoencoders (VAEs) with encoder-decoder architecture and latent space regularization via KL divergence. The latent space as a compressed manifold of possibilities. Mode collapse in GANs, posterior collapse in VAEs. Interpolation in latent space as smooth morphing between instances.
Critical angle: A generative model raises the question of originality — if the network produces an image never seen in training, is it creative or merely recombinant? The latent space is a map of possibility, not a library of memory, which makes generation more like imagination than recall. Generative models also expose the training data's biases: the model can only dream within the boundaries of what it was shown. What it generates reveals what it was taught to consider real.
Key artifacts: generative_model provides an interactive generative system with latent space exploration, interpolation between generated instances, and real-time sampling from the learned distribution.
Gap: Needs a latent space atlas — a 2D projection of the latent space with labeled regions showing how the model organizes its learned manifold, making the structure of possibility visible.
