Solid ground gives way to nothing. You walk along a raised ledge and then the floor vanishes — a void opens beneath you, three cells wide and three cells deep. The drop is sudden, architectural, deliberate.

Vertical negative space is a shock the body registers before the mind. A hole in the floor creates drama through absence. The drop reveals what was hidden: depth below, sky above, and the vertiginous awareness that ground is not guaranteed. The reveal is always vertical.
