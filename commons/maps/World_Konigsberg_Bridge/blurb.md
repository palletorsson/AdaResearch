# The court around an impossible walk

You enter above the water, on a pale ring measured in one-metre squares. Inside it, four landmasses and seven old bridges seem to offer countless routes. Two dark tongues lead from the museum into the city. They are access, not evidence: the graph begins only where the named stone bridges begin.

Try to cross every bridge once. When the route fails, stop searching for a better itinerary and read the landmasses instead. All four have odd degree. The impossibility belongs to the structure, not to your performance.
