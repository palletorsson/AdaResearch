# Summary

`World_Konigsberg_Bridge` is the first authored dedicated-world site produced by the endless-museum negotiation pipeline. The placement contract rejected `KonigsbergBridge` from ordinary rooms because its measured 50 x 32 m body exceeds the 40 m bridge-court limit. This map accepts that refusal and gives the artifact a 56 x 38 m site: its full footprint plus a three-metre observation apron on every side.

The court is raised to the 2.5 m walking level of the artifact's landmasses. A continuous route runs from the south entry, across a dark museum-access tongue, through the connected graph, out through a matching northern tongue, and to the return portal. The access pieces are not counted as graph edges and use a different material language from the seven historical stone bridges.

The canonical walk remains explicit: artifact-order index 710 is `KonigsbergBridge`; the return target is index 711, `force_directed_layout`, in `GT_Layout`.
