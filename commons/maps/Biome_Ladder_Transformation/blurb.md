# What did the world learn to do, hall by hall?

Glass cages along an aisle, one per hall of the transformation sequence. The grey garden of the primitives stands in every one; what changes is what it may DO. A cube you can take; movements left running; a surface that carries; a bar that turns about one end; a body that swells and shrinks; a post the height of you; a wall that arrives. Nothing here has a colour yet.
