Concept: An open sandbox collecting the loose force props of the Forces sequence in one room, so each toy can be tried in isolation before it is composed into a teaching map.
Sequence role: A development/sandbox map in the Forces sequence, not a curricular step. It is a staging area for the maintainer to test individual force artifacts and a play space without a designed arc. No difficulty progression and no narrative phases — by intent.
Technical angle: A loose set of force artifacts — force_cube and force_mower (applied-force props), bubble_blaster (projectile/emitter), circle_train (circular motion), wedge_slide (inclined-plane sliding), dot_aligner and vector_add (vector composition props), plus control_console and science_screen for parameter and abstraction display. death_pylon supplies a hazard for testing the death/restart path.
Critical angle: A sandbox names the gap between a part and a place — these artifacts work, but a working artifact is not yet a meaning; meaning is what arranging them into a narrated map produces. Keeping the sandbox honestly labelled as scratch space resists the anti-pattern (noted in FOCUS_VECTOR) of shipping many untested rooms as if they were finished maps.
Key artifacts: force_cube and force_mower demonstrate applied force; bubble_blaster emits projectiles; circle_train shows circular motion; wedge_slide demonstrates sliding on an incline; dot_aligner and vector_add show vector composition; science_screen mirrors the 3D props as 2D abstractions.
Gap: As scratch space this map should live in a tools/sandbox sequence rather than the player-facing Forces journey, or be flagged non-curricular. If it stays, the props should be grouped by force concept (applied / projectile / circular / incline / vector) so even the sandbox teaches by arrangement.
