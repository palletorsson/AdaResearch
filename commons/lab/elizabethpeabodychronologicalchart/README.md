# Elizabeth Peabody Chronological Chart

Procedural recreation of Elizabeth Peabody's 1859 color-coded chronological chart of universal history.

## Behavior

- Builds a grid of colored rectangles representing historical periods
- 10 color categories: ancient civilizations, classical, medieval, renaissance, modern, wars, discoveries, cultural movements, political changes, religious events
- Configurable grid_width, grid_height, cell_size
