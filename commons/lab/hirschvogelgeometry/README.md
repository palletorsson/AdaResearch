# Hirschvogel Geometry

Procedural recreation of Augustin Hirschvogel's 1543 Geometria -- compass-and-straightedge constructions rendered as ink on paper.

## Behavior

- Paper background with ink-style line materials
- Generates geometric constructions (circles, triangles, polygons)
- Triangle fill materials with period-appropriate muted tones
