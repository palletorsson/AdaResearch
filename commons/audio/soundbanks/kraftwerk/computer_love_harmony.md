## 🎵 CHORD PROGRESSION

### Main Progression (Verse/Arpeggio Section)
```
||: F    | Dm   | Bb   | C    :||
    I      vi     IV     V
```

**Harmonic Rhythm:** 1 chord per bar (4 beats each)

### Analysis:
- Classic **I - vi - IV - V** (the "50s progression" / doo-wop changes)
- Creates sense of longing through vi chord (relative minor)
- The IV - V provides gentle lift without resolution tension
- Loops seamlessly - hypnotic, robotic repetition

## 🎹 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### For DAW Programming:

1. **Arpeggio:**
   - Use 16th note quantization
   - Slight velocity variation (±10) for human feel
   - Gate length: 60-70%
   - Sound: Bright, bell-like polysynth

2. **Pad Layer:**
   - Whole note sustained chords
   - Very low in mix (foundation)
   - Slow attack (200ms), long release (1000ms)

3. **Bass:**
   - Quantized exactly on grid
   - No swing
   - Short decay envelope
   - Low-pass filtered around 800Hz

4. **Melody:**
   - Vocoder or formant synth
   - Slight pitch drift for analog feel
   - Delay: 1/8 note, 30% feedback
