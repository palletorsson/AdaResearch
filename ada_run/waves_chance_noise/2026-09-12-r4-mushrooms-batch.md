## One reusable improvement, and its conditions

**The arrangement's bookkeeping, printed and pointed at.** A generated arrangement hides which of its procedures put each thing where it stands, and a readout of counts alone leaves the visitor matching numbers to a picture. Recording, on every path, what was requested, what was accepted, what was refused and what was placed on purpose — with a metadata tag on every instance saying which procedure made it — lets one plate print the decisions and one highlight point at them in the scene, kind by kind, and lets a one-rule switch (SIZE) show what a single decision contributed under the same seed. Conditions: every draw through one seeded generator so the comparison is between the same population with and without the rule; the tags written by the procedures themselves, not inferred from names; the refused candidates kept as positions, not counted away; the highlight visible from where the visitor stands (a ring on the ground alone hid under the caps); and the plate reporting what the rule made, never what a token asked for. Random_Entropy's ledger tiles and Random_Gaussian's ghosts put a sample beside its expectation; this puts an arrangement beside its procedures. A third use would justify extracting it.
