## Design decision

The cabinet is an opt-in `stand` axis on a shared artifact (ten placements), like the terrarium's logbook: `stand:none` builds nothing and the draws, bins and stats are the shipped ones. The readout is housed under the histogram, where the eye goes after the bars (R2); it prints the whole measurement contract — landed, in flight, the cap, the clipped draws, the two edge bins, the bins, the seed and its policy, the cadence (R7) — and the ghosts put the expectation on the bars' own scale so the comparison has two halves (R1). BATCH is what makes "equal N" a gesture rather than a wait; PAUSE lets the sparse picture be read; BINS re-bins the kept values so a change of bins cannot silently resample; NEW SEED is the honest name for the other operation. The density curve keeps its own scale, as the card wanted, and the final says why.
