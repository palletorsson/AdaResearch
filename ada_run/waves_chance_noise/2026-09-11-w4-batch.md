## One reusable improvement, and its conditions

**HOLD with a marker and a readout drawn from the display's own arithmetic.** An instrument whose picture is a pure function of its state and a clock can stop the clock, mark one sample, and print at that sample exactly what it drew — each row, their sum, the total — so "this place on the sum" becomes a comparison a visitor reads across rows without a panel of numbers for the whole curve. Conditions: the display must be a function of (state, clock) with no hidden accumulation; the readout must use the display's own sample index and phase (`display_values(i)`, `phase_at(i)`), never a second implementation; the marker must cross every row it prints. Alongside it, a method note for the live harness: a desktop rig placed within its radius of a collider is pushed after it has aimed, and at 0.65 m an eye that moved 0.11 m misses a button by 8 cm — stand a rig clear of colliders, record the pose at the press, and inspect the control in place before blaming the geometry.
