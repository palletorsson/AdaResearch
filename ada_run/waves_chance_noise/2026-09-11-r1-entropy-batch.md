## One reusable improvement, and its conditions

**A sorted copy under a fixed reading.** An instrument whose number is a function of tallies can carry its whole sample as a row of tiles and regroup a COPY of them on demand, printing the copy's reading beside the live one and counting what moved — so the invariant is seen (the bars and the number standing still while everything on the desk rearranges) before the formula says why. Conditions: the sample must stay on the wall (no resampling behind the sort); the sort must be stable and operate on a copy, with the number recomputed by the same loop; the tiles must wear the histogram's own colours so a block can be matched to a bar; a contrast must change one thing only (here the shares, with the alphabet, N and the seed's provenance printed).
