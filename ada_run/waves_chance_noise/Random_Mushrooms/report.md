## Design decision

The table is an opt-in `stand` axis on a shared artifact (`stand:none` builds nothing and the shipped build makes the same calls in the same order on the global stream), like the cabinet of Random_Gaussian and the logbook of Random_Walk. What is different here is the object of the readout: not a measurement contract over a stream but the bookkeeping of an arrangement — what was requested, what was accepted, what was refused, what was placed on purpose — printed on a plate and pointed at in the bed by the highlight. The type specimens on the discs give the population's six bodies a place to be compared with their copies (the card's sample-to-body mapping, R4); the seed covers every draw and the ground (R6; the card: "ground alone is insufficient"); SIZE is the one-rule control the card asked for; the lights are capped and the bed is built once (R8). The highlight is a ring on the ground and a pin in the air because a flat disc under a cap is invisible from the table (the first captures showed it).
