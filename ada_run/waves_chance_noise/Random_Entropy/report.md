SORT is a stable sort of a copy: the sample, the counts, the histogram and H are never touched, and the readout says so with the sorted copy's H and the moved tiles (R1 controlled comparison; R2 housed readout). CONTRAST is a second fixed sample, not a resample: the card allows new sampling only with the source exposed and a local reset, and the comparison needs the same two hundred draws to stay available — so the contrast has its own seed (the first plus one), is drawn once at build, and CONTRAST again measures the first sample again (R7 measurement contract). DISCLOSE reuses the meter's ladder rather than a second disclosure of my own.
