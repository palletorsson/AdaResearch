## Question and experiment

Who was eligible to disappear? A visitor meets a board of sixty-four cubes with sixteen amber — the 4×4 block a rule admitted — and forty-eight grey. REMOVE ONE highlights and takes one amber cube, its slot plate staying and the line naming its column and row; sixteen presses empty the square in an order the visitor could not know toward a shape they could. RESET restores all sixty-four and replays the same seed's order; NEW SEED starts another. ROW, COLUMN and ALL change the rule before the same grid; the same seed over two rules shows the same draws landing on different cubes. The filter, the draw and the deletion are read apart in the final.

## Design decision

Build on the other hand's bench rather than restage it: the card's tray, border, distinct cell states, counts beside the grid and reachable controls were already there. The one missing instrument was the seed — R6 local seed, R1 controlled comparison: the same order twice, then the same draws over another mask. The replay is opt-in on the remover (`replay_on_reset`, default off — the shipped placements keep their stream across resets) and turned on by the bench. Physical absence is neither claimed by the map nor built (a removed cube is a transform scaled to zero on a board), so it is not tested, as the card allows.
