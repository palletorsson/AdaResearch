## Design decision

The logbook is an opt-in `stand` axis on a shared artifact (twelve placements), like W4's bench and R1's ledger: `stand:none` builds nothing and the stream, the colours and the trail are byte-for-byte the shipped behaviour. The wing hangs on the visitor's left so the keypad's face stays the cabinet's own; the readout is housed (R2), names both clocks and the catch-up cap (R3), and names the seed and what RESET does with it (R6) — the shipped label promised a reset and the button gave nothing; the plate now says `RESET replays it` only when it does. The named five-digit seed follows Random_Remove's pattern: a private generator, re-seeded before the state is restored, short enough to repeat; NEW SEED is the other operation's honest name. ONE dims four beads rather than removing them, so the population is one press away.

"Start/stop" stay the artifact's cadence; the keypad has no pause, and a local pause would not stop the museum's clock.
