The spider

What it is: 56 CSG primitives and nothing else. The root scene carries the script and no mesh; the base class builds a box torso and four skinned tube legs, and both are discarded before the first frame.

Four spheres are the body — a bulge of radius 0.60 rig units, a subtraction sphere at 0.63 hollowing the underside, a beak at 0.135, a tooth at 0.075. The other 52 are legs: thirteen per leg, four legs. Per leg that is six cylinders, six spheres at the bone origins, and one foot sphere on the last bone.

6

Composition

The operation: take one chain, place N copies at angle i·TAU/N about a centre, each copy also rotated by its own angle.

It is not performed by a loop. csg_four_leg_walker.tscn holds four hand-copied blocks of about 106 lines each, differing in exactly three fields: an integer suffix, the leg basis (four sign patterns at 45°/135°/225°/315°, step exactly 90), and the shoulder origin (the four sign combinations of ±1.5556, which is 2.2 × cos 45°). The shoulder ring is transcribed six times across the corpus.

The one genuine i·TAU/N ring in the builder never runs: bulge_only defaults true and the function returns before it. Six of the animal's fourteen shape parameters are dead as a result.

The gait pairs the diagonals, [[0, 2], [1, 3]]. That pairing is the parent class's own stated essence — four legs pair diagonals because geometry demands it — which the parent declares in its identity block and implements nowhere: its own code steps a single worst leg. The deployed spider is the declaration finally built.

The corpus teaches the ring properly one sequence away: pattern_mill in Pattern_Foundry performs it as a loop, and returns fold = 4 for the p4 wallpaper family. The spider's number, in a loop, in a walkable room — but in colour, not in transformation and not in forces.

5

Force

The operation: there is none. No velocity, no acceleration, no gravity, no stiffness anywhere in the chain.

The entire locomotion is two lines: a position write from a unit forward vector times speed times delta, then a hard vertical pin. Height is re-assigned every frame, so falling is impossible by construction. The step is not a physical arc but a normalised bump — and the first normalized() in the whole force path exists only because the root's scale would otherwise shorten the forward vector.

The corpus teaches the real integrator one room earlier: spring_demo in VFM_06_Springs is Hooke minus damping over mass, accumulated into a velocity. The spider stands two rooms further along the same sequence and integrates nothing.

And the very first room already applies a real one. interactive_point_origin_force, placed three times in Point_One, applies inverse-square attraction to nearby physics bodies. Sequence one, room one, exerts a force on the world. Sequence five, the animal, exerts none.

4

Weave and trace

The question: does the walk leave a mark? Measured, not argued — and the first two measurements contradicted each other.

Walking straight, the four foot traces crossed zero times. Walking a circle, they crossed seven times and each foot crossed itself sixty-odd times. Both readings are worthless: a spider walking a circle comes back over its own ground, so those crossings are a fact about the loop, not the gait.

A gait braids when a foot swings across the body's own midline, and that is only visible in the body's frame. Over 401 samples: every foot's local x holds its sign, no two feet come within 5 cm of the same local ground, zero midline crossings. It does not weave. Four traces held apart by the sign of each shoulder, collapsing on a straight walk to two rails 0.58 m apart, dotted every 0.27 m.

The corpus's real weave is one rung below on the same object that hatches the spider. The triangle weaver builds an actual cloth: three fans of fourteen straight chords, each drawn as three cylinders through four nodes, with a three-state depth function returning +0.028 / 0 / −0.028 so the strands read over and under. 42 chords, 126 instances, six seconds to weave, then it stops forever.

And a trace here has a canonical mechanism: sample a moving node, gate on a minimum segment distance, append to a capped array, rebuild a line strip. draw_dot does it in Point_Trace; player_trace applies the identical mechanism to the visitor's own body, and keeps a second unquantised path at four times the budget so the discard stays visible. The corpus can draw a walk. It has never been pointed at the walker it built.

3

Triangle

The operation: three points that do not need to negotiate with falling — the first polygon that stands.

In the deployed spider the triangle survives as arithmetic rather than as geometry. With the diagonals paired, one pair is airborne at a time, so two feet are down — below the tripod minimum. Nothing computes a support polygon or a centre of mass, because the vertical pin makes falling impossible. The stability the triangle exists to teach is bypassed, not solved.

The ladder that would teach it is real, written and placed: seven leg critters from one to eight legs, each with its own truth line. One leg, one contact point — the pogo stick is locomotion reduced to its axiom. Three points define a plane. Two alternating tripods, always three on ground. All seven stand in one row in Hazards_Zoo_3 under a panel reading “From 1 to 8 legs — watch locomotion emerge.” Hazards is not one of the 22 spine sequences, so the ladder is built, placed, and off the path.

2

Line

The operation: a cylinder whose height is the distance between two points, centred at their midpoint, oriented along their difference.

This is the rung that makes the descent worth writing, because the two files compute the same thing. The corpus's line measures the distance between two grab spheres at runtime and sets the cylinder's height to it. The limb builder sets height = 0.92 as a constant — because the bone rest is a constant, one unit from parent to child, with 0.04 left at each end so the joint sphere reads as a joint.

So a line in this corpus is two spheres and a cylinder between them, and the spider is 24 of those cylinders. The only difference is whether the length is measured at runtime from two movable ends or baked from a fixed rest pose.

The radii taper down each chain by a factor of 1 − (b/(n−1)) × 0.62, giving 0.0750 / 0.0657 / 0.0564 / 0.0471 / 0.0378 / 0.0285 — a linear taper that loses 62% of the radius by the tip. Measured on the standing animal, all six.

The one step this repository cannot demonstrate. Searching the whole tree for the IK solver returns sixteen call sites and no definition; no addon provides it. Everything between a marker's world position and six bone poses — the fold, the knee, which chain solves first — is a reading of property names in a scene file. The transition from “a line between two points” to “a limb that reaches” is the unmechanised joint in the whole descent.

Point

The operation: a sphere at a bone's origin, which is exactly its articulation with its parent.

Six per leg, radii 0.1500 / 0.1314 / 0.1128 / 0.0942 / 0.0756 / 0.0570 — the same 62% taper as the shafts, confirmed in the histogram. Plus one foot sphere on the last bone only.

Counted the same way across ten artifacts, the rule holds for the whole corpus: a point is a sphere, a line is a cylinder.

The first room's own blurb denies the point: the origin is not a point but a prerequisite — position without extension. The point you can actually pick up is the one that also pulls, placed three times in a row, plurality authored into the map because no artifact in the corpus has “a set of points” as its subject.

The egg derives which form to hatch from the name of the room it stands in. Four rungs yield: a point, a line of five boxes, the weaver's cloth, the spider. There is no square rung, no grid rung, no trace rung. Across 2,790 map files there are 349 eggs, and 321 of them derive nothing at all — and the room that actually places the spider places no egg.

What is not the case

Three findings that only a boot could settle. Two were defects in the deployment made the same day and are now fixed; one is a design decision, left open.

The small crab was not small

The map token asked for scale 0.11. The placement pipeline applies configuration after the node enters the tree, so the setter changed a number nothing re-read: measured at 0.882 of default size where 0.733 was asked for. Everything derived from scale is now re-derived when a token arrives late.

The feet planted through the floor

Every plant was written to world zero. Both arena crabs stand on structure cells of height 1, seated at 0.5 m — so their feet were half a metre under the deck they were placed on, while four ground probes hung unread beneath the body. The feet now cast down and take the hit.

It cannot touch anything

The registry files it as a hazard. It instantiates no collider, no area and no physics body of any kind: it walks through the visitor and always has. Giving it a hurt is a decision about the museum, not a bug in the animal — the death effect reloads the map, which is wrong for a walker.

What would make the trace walkable

Everything above is argued. These are what it would take to walk it instead, cheapest first.

  1. The leg ladder, on the spine.It already exists as a built room — seven critters, one row, panel text written. It sits in a sequence nobody walks. Four cells beside the spider, or the room promoted.
  2. A foot trace on the animal.The trace mechanism ships and persists across maps. Four instances on the four feet turn the spider into a mark-maker and make the braid question answerable by looking instead of by probe.
  3. The line, performed.One placement putting the corpus line beside one leg segment, both heights driven from the distance between their ends. The load-bearing step of this whole descent currently lives only in the fact that two files compute the same thing.
  4. The missing rungs.Square, grid, trace, and the arithmetic that turns four legs into one gait. The ladder currently jumps from three points to a walking animal.
  5. A stage override that works.The egg documents one and never reads it, so every hatch is chosen by room name alone — and the match happens after the shell is already in nine pieces.
  6. Identity blocks on the four files that ship.Every ancestor has one. The animal, its builder, its body builder and the weaver do not, so the truth-statement tooling cannot see the thing that walks.
