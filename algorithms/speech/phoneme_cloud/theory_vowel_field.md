# Vowel as Field: Ada Research Speech Theory

## 1. Core Concept
In Ada Research, we do not perform "speech synthesis" (symbolic reconstruction). We cultivate **Resonant Fields** (material construction).

**The Vowel is a Field Configuration.**
*   It is not a discrete object.
*   It is a stable spectral emphasis emerging from a harmonic source.
*   It exists *before* language.

## 3. Implementation Logic
Our "Vowel Synthesizer" is a direct material simulation of this theory:

### A. The Point (Source)
*   A **Harmonic-Rich** oscillator (Sawtooth/Pulse).
*   Pure sine waves are forbidden (they have no material to sculpt).
*   Pulse width determines the "nasal" or "pressed" quality of the source material.

### B. The Lines (Constraints)
*   **Parallel Band-Pass Filters**.
*   These do not *produce* sound; they *select* it.
*   They act as architectural constraints on the energy field.
*   **F1** (Jaw) and **F2** (Tongue) are the primary axes of this architecture.

### C. The Trace (Life)
*   Static fields are dead.
*   **Drift**: Parameters must never be frozen. Slight modulation represents the organic instability of the constraint.
*   **Envelope**: Meaning emerges in the *transition* (the trace), not the state.

## 4. Operational Mandate
*   **Do not** aim for "perfect speech".
*   **Do** aim for "distinct, embodied resonance".
*   The artifact is a *controlled resonant field*.
