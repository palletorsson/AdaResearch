# The Wavefunction Curriculum
## A Comprehensive Ground-Up Presentation Plan

This document outlines a structured progression for presenting **ALL** material in `algorithms/wavefunctions`. It organizes every existing example into a coherent 5-Map journey, from basic oscillation to complex systems.

---

### 🗺️ Map 1: The Heartbeat (Oscillation & Cycles)
**Theme:** The fundamental unit of motion. Everything starts with a pulse.
**Environment:** Laboratory / Clean Room.

1.  **The Pulse (Basics)**
	*   `oscillation_driver/OscillationDriver.tscn`: Linear oscillation driving scale.
	*   `sine_oscillation.gd` / `sineoscillation`: Basic sine wave implementation.
	*   `oscillating_wave`: Simple wave motion.
2.  **The Circle (Trigonometry)**
	*   `oscillation_driver/UnitCircleTrig.tscn`: Visualizing Sin/Cos from a circle.
	*   `unit_circle`: Existing unit circle exploration.
	*   `unitcirclecrack`: Variant of unit circle visualization.
	*   `triangle_circle.gd`: Relationship between triangles and circles.
	*   `angular_motion.gd`: Basic rotational physics.
3.  **The Timeline (Time as Space)**
	*   `oscillation_driver/OscillationCurve.tscn`: Visualizing the history of a wave.
	*   `sine_work`: General sine wave experiments.

---

### 🗺️ Map 2: The Flow (Propagation & Space)
**Theme:** Waves moving through the world, creating paths, environments, and architecture.
**Environment:** Abstract Neon / Architectural Void.

1.  **The Path (Navigable Waves)**
	*   `oscillation_driver/TrigWalkingPath.tscn`: Walkable steps generated by Sin/Cos.
	*   `sine_cylinder_staircase`: Vertical spiral navigation.
2.  **The Environment (Immersion)**
	*   `sine_hallway`: Walking through a breathing corridor.
	*   `rainbow_hallway`: Color-mapped wave environment.
	*   `hallway_lines`: Perspective and wave lines.
	*   `sine_wall`: A wall of oscillating blocks.
	*   `sine_space`: A 3D field of oscillating points.
3.  **The Form (Spiral & Helix)**
	*   `oscillation_driver/CircleSpiral.tscn`: Rotational propagation.
	*   `oscillation_driver/DoubleHelix.tscn`: DNA structure.
	*   `doublehelix`: Existing double helix experiments.
	*   `static_cables`: Catenary curves and hanging wave forms.
4.  **The Field (Grid Propagation)**
	*   `wave_propagation_3d`: Ripples spreading through a 3D grid.
	*   `line_builder_3d`: Tool for drawing wave paths.

---

### 🗺️ Map 3: The Structure (Geometry & Form)
**Theme:** Math building reality. From simple shapes to complex organic forms.
**Environment:** Museum / Art Gallery.

1.  **The Shape (Parametric)**
	*   `parametric_shapes`: Mathematical sculptures.
	*   `fourier_transform` / `fouriertransform` / `fouriertransformshape`: Decomposing complex shapes into circles.
	*   `spherical_harmonics`: 3D spherical wave functions (orbitals).
2.  **The Art (Aesthetics)**
	*   `berninicolumns`: Twisted columns (Baroque waves).
	*   `kusamasine`: Polka-dot wave patterns (Yayoi Kusama inspired).
3.  **The Body (Organic)**
	*   `vitruvian_man`: Human geometry and proportion.
	*   `dancing_body`: Wave functions applied to motion capture/animation.

---

### 🗺️ Map 4: The Harmony (Sound & Physics)
**Theme:** When waves meet. Sound, resonance, and interference.
**Environment:** Concert Hall / Acoustic Chamber.

1.  **The Composition (Synthesis)**
	*   `harmonic_builder`: Additive synthesis (stacking sines).
	*   `beat_frequencies`: Interference in time (tuning).
	*   `oscillation_driver/Lissajous3D.tscn`: Complex harmonic motion (X vs Y vs Z).
	*   `lissajous_curves`: 2D/3D Lissajous figures.
	*   `commons/audio/systems/graph_music/CircleOfFifthsTest.tscn`: The Circle of Fifths (Music Theory).
2.  **The Physics (Resonance)**
	*   `wave_interference`: Constructive/Destructive interference in space.
	*   `standing_waves`: Nodes and Antinodes.
	*   `resonance` / `resonancefrequencies`: Cymatics (Sound shaping matter).
3.  **The Ear (Perception)**
	*   `coshlea`: Simulation of the inner ear's frequency analysis.
    *   `spectralanalysis`: Visualizing the frequency spectrum.
    *   `soundtimeline`: Visualizing audio over time.
4.  **The Music (Generative)**
    *   `liturgicalambientgenerator`: Procedural music generation.
    *   `noirsequencer`: Music sequencing logic.
    *   `technoirgameaudio`: Interactive game audio systems.
    *   `commons/audio/systems/discreet_music/DiscreetMusicTest.tscn`: Ambient looping systems (Brian Eno style).

---

### 🗺️ Map 5: The Chaos & Systems (Complexity)
**Theme:** When the pattern breaks. Unpredictability, fluids, and interconnected systems.
**Environment:** Deep Space / Underwater / Simulation Grid.

1.  **The Pendulum (Gravity)**
    *   `oscillation_driver/PendulumWave.tscn`: Simple harmonic motion.
    *   `oscillation_driver/PendulumCircle.tscn`: Compound motion.
    *   `parametric_pendulum_waves`: Multiple pendulums creating patterns.
2.  **The Chaos (Unpredictability)**
    *   `oscillation_driver/DoublePendulum.tscn`: Deterministic chaos.
    *   `double_pendelum.gd`: Existing double pendulum script.
3.  **The Network (Connection)**
    *   `spring_network`: Masses connected by springs (elasticity).
    *   `coupled_oscillator_lattice`: Grid of connected oscillators (emergent waves).
4.  **The Fluid (Simulation)**
    *   `underwater`: Fluid dynamics and buoyancy.
    *   `water_land_ride`: Large-scale water simulation.
    *   `mariocontrol`: Interaction with physics systems.

---

### 📝 Implementation Strategy
To present this, we should create a **"Hub World"** or **"Teleporter Room"** that links to these 5 Maps. Each Map can be a large scene containing portals or walkways to the individual exhibits listed above.
