The `GA-CA Shape Learner` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this resonant atelier, the scene `ShapeLearner.tscn` and its companion script `ShapeLearner.gd` combines rigor with playful improvisation.

Patient tuning across `grid_size`, `population_size`, `mutation_rate`, `generations` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_start_ga()`, `run_ga_loop()` keep the pulse steady. The sandbox holds still just long enough for insight to land.
