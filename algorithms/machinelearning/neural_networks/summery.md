The `Neural Networks` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this resonant atelier, the scene `neural_networks.tscn` and its companion script `NeuralNetworks.gd` combines rigor with playful improvisation.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `create_network()`, `setup_materials()` keep the pulse steady. The experience lands somewhere between experiment and poem.
