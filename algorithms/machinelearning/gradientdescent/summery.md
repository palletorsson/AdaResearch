The `Gradient Descent Sandbox` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this floating promenade, the scene `gradient_descent.tscn` and its companion script `GradientDescentDemo.gd` turns gradients into drift and pulse.

Curious fingertips over `grid_size`, `height_scale`, `noise_frequency`, `noise_lacunarity` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_ready()`, `_init_noise()`, `_generate_terrain()` keep the pulse steady. Every reset becomes another verse in the same idea.
