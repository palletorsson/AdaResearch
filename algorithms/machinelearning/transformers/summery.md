The `Transformers` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this windlit studio, the scene `transformers.tscn` and its companion script `Transformers.gd` invites you to map intuition onto geometry.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Under the hood, loops like `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_input_tokens()` keep the pulse steady. Every reset becomes another verse in the same idea.
