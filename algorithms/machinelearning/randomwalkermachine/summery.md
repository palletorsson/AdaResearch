The `Random Walker Machine` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this floating promenade, the scene `randomwalkermachine.tscn` and its companion script `randomwalkermachine.gd` turns gradients into drift and pulse.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_init()`, `update_position()`, `_ready()` keep the pulse steady. Helper forms like `RandomWalker` hold memory and meaning between beats. What emerges is a living sketch of learning in motion.
