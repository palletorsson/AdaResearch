The `Time Series Analysis` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this floating promenade, the scene `time_series_analysis.tscn` and its companion script `TimeSeriesAnalysis.gd` turns gradients into drift and pulse.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_time_series_particles()` keep the pulse steady. Every reset becomes another verse in the same idea.
