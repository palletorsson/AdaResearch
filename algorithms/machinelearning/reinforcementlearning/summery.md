The `Reinforcement Learning Creature` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this midnight observatory, the scene `joint_learn_walk.tscn` and its companion script `joint_learn_walk.gd` translates equations into choreography.

Careful dial turns on `num_limbs`, `min_limb_length`, `max_limb_length`, `limb_radius` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Backstage, routines like `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_environment()` keep the pulse steady. The experience lands somewhere between experiment and poem.
