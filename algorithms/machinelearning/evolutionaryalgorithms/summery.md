The `Evolving Creatures Simulation` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this resonant atelier, the scene `evolving_creatures.tscn` and its companion script `evolving_creatures.gd` combines rigor with playful improvisation.

Patient tuning across `limb_length`, `limb_radius`, `torque_strength`, `creature_type` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_environment()` keep the pulse steady. The sandbox holds still just long enough for insight to land.
