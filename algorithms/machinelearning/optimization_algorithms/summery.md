The `Optimization Algorithms` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this luminous laboratory, the scene `optimization_algorithms.tscn` and its companion script `OptimizationAlgorithms.gd` lets the mathematics breathe in color and motion.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_landscape_particles()` keep the pulse steady. Together they stage a miniature study in adaptation.
