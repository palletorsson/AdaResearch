The `Reinforcement Learning` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this luminous laboratory, the scene `reinforcement_learning.tscn` and its companion script `ReinforcementLearning.gd` lets the mathematics breathe in color and motion.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_environment_particles()` keep the pulse steady. What emerges is a living sketch of learning in motion.
