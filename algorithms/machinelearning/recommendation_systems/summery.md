The `Recommendation Systems` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this floating promenade, the scene `recommendation_systems.tscn` and its companion script `RecommendationSystems.gd` turns gradients into drift and pulse.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_user_particles()` keep the pulse steady. Every reset becomes another verse in the same idea.
