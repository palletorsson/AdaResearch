The `Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this resonant atelier, the scene `variational_autoencoders_VAEs.tscn` and its companion script `VariationalAutoencodersVAEs.gd` combines rigor with playful improvisation.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_input_particles()` keep the pulse steady. The whole scene leans toward discovery and delight.
