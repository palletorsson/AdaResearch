The `BuildEnv - Grammar Discovery RL Environment` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this chromatic engine room, the scene `diagnostic_test.tscn` and its companion script `LearnWorldStacked.gd` lets hypotheses bloom like constellations.

Careful dial turns on `arena_size_x`, `arena_size_z`, `grid_res`, `settle_time` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_setup_environment()`, `_create_material()` keep the pulse steady. The experience lands somewhere between experiment and poem.
