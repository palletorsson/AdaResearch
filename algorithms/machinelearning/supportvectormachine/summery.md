The `Support Vector Machine Visualization` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this echoing arcade, the scene `svm_visualization.tscn` and its companion script `svm_visualization.gd` braids signal, light, and balance.

Careful dial turns on `kernel_type`, `C_parameter`, `gamma`, `degree` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Backstage, routines like `_init()`, `_ready()`, `setup_ui()` keep the pulse steady. The experience lands somewhere between experiment and poem.
