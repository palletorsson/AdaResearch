The `Attention Mechanisms` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this echoing arcade, the scene `attention_mechanisms.tscn` and its companion script `AttentionMechanisms.gd` braids signal, light, and balance.

Patient tuning across `token_count`, `base_attention`, `attention_gain`, `focus_gain` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Backstage, routines like `_ready()`, `_process()`, `_setup_materials()` keep the pulse steady. The sandbox holds still just long enough for insight to land.
