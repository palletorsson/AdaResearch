The `Dimensionality Reduction` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this echoing arcade, the scene `dimensionality_reduction.tscn` and its companion script `DimensionalityReduction.gd` braids signal, light, and balance.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Backstage, routines like `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_high_dimensional_particles()` keep the pulse steady. The whole scene leans toward discovery and delight.
