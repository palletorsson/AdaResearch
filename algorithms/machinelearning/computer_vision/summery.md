The `Computer Vision` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this windlit studio, the scene `computer_vision.tscn` and its companion script `ComputerVision.gd` invites you to map intuition onto geometry.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Under the hood, loops like `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_image_pixels()` keep the pulse steady. Together they stage a miniature study in adaptation.
