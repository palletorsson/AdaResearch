The `Ensemble Methods` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this windlit studio, the scene `ensemble_methods.tscn` and its companion script `EnsembleMethods.gd` invites you to map intuition onto geometry.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Under the hood, loops like `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_model_particles()` keep the pulse steady. What emerges is a living sketch of learning in motion.
