The `Long Short-Term Memory (LSTMs)` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this resonant atelier, the scene `LSTMs.tscn` and its companion script `LSTMs.gd` combines rigor with playful improvisation.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_process()`, `create_input_tokens()` keep the pulse steady. The experience lands somewhere between experiment and poem.
