The `Neural Network Visualization` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this resonant atelier, the scene `neural_network_visualization.tscn` and its companion script `neural_network_visualization.gd` combines rigor with playful improvisation.

Delicate gestures around `input_layer_size`, `hidden_layer_sizes`, `output_layer_size`, `learning_rate` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_input()`, `build_network()` keep the pulse steady. The whole scene leans toward discovery and delight.
