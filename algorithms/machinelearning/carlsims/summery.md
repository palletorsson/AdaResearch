The `Karl Sims Inspired Evolution` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this windlit studio, the scene `evolved_creatures.tscn` and its companion script `evolvedcreatures.gd` invites you to map intuition onto geometry.

Curious fingertips over `random_seed`, `enable_random_restart`, `enable_debug_prints` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Under the hood, loops like `randomize()`, `duplicate()`, `_ready()` keep the pulse steady. Helper forms like `CreatureGenome`, `CreatureInstance` hold memory and meaning between beats. Every reset becomes another verse in the same idea.
