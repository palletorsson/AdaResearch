The `Clustering Algorithms` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this midnight observatory, the scene `clustering_algorithms.tscn` and its companion script `ClusteringAlgorithms.gd` translates equations into choreography.

Careful dial turns on `dataset_seed`, `algorithm_mode`, `animate_clusters` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Backstage, routines like `_ready()`, `_generate_dataset()`, `_build_point_meshes()` keep the pulse steady. The experience lands somewhere between experiment and poem.
