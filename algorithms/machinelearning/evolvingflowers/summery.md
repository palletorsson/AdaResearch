The `Evolving Flowers` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this floating promenade, the scene `evolvingflowers.tscn` and its companion script `evolvingflowers.gd` turns gradients into drift and pulse.

Adjustable parameters wait for curious hands to sculpt the flow. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_init()`, `randomize_genome()`, `crossover()` keep the pulse steady. Helper forms like `FlowerGenome`, `Flower` hold memory and meaning between beats. Together they stage a miniature study in adaptation.
