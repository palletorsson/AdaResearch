The `Shape Learner 3D` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this chromatic engine room, the scene `ShapeLearner3D.tscn` and its companion script `ShapeLearner3D.gd` lets hypotheses bloom like constellations.

Delicate gestures around `grid_size`, `population_size`, `mutation_rate`, `generations` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. In the control room, calls to `_ready()`, `_process()`, `run_ga_generation()` keep the pulse steady. The whole scene leans toward discovery and delight.
