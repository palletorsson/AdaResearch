The `Principal Component Analysis (PCA) Visualization` sandbox is a small theatre of learning. Within this floating promenade, the scene `pca_visualization.tscn` and its companion script `pca_visualization.gd` turns gradients into drift and pulse.

Sweeping motions through `num_dimensions`, `target_dimensions`, `show_all_components`, `normalize_data` change the rhythm of the system in an instant. Behind the scrim, functions such as `_init()`, `_ready()`, `setup_ui()` keep the pulse steady. What emerges is a living sketch of learning in motion.
