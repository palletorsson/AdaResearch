# Platonic Solids

The five perfect forms. Euclid's geometry, queered.

## QFEP Connection

Platonic solids are **pure F** — maximum symmetry, minimum entropy. But this implementation queers them: pride colors, trans palettes, rainbow environments. The solids remain mathematically perfect while their presentation challenges "neutral" aesthetics. Form is political.

## The Five Platonic Solids

| Solid | Faces | Vertices | Edges | Face Shape |
|-------|-------|----------|-------|------------|
| Tetrahedron | 4 | 4 | 6 | Triangle |
| Cube | 6 | 8 | 12 | Square |
| Octahedron | 8 | 6 | 12 | Triangle |
| Dodecahedron | 12 | 20 | 30 | Pentagon |
| Icosahedron | 20 | 12 | 30 | Triangle |

## Queer Aesthetics

- **Pride colors**: Rainbow gradient (red → purple)
- **Trans colors**: Light blue, pink, white, pink, light blue
- **Rainbow floor**: Pride flag as ground plane
- **Colorful lighting**: Multi-hued light sources

## Additional Forms

Beyond the Platonic five, this module includes:
- Diamond (double pyramid)
- Prism
- Bipyramid
- Truncated Tetrahedron
- Geode
- Crystal Shard
- Rough Rock (organic variation)

## The Golden Ratio

Dodecahedron and icosahedron use φ (phi):
```
φ = (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.618
```

Vertices are computed using φ for precise golden-ratio proportions.
