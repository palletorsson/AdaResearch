MUSEUM MODULARITY — PREDICTION, WRITTEN BEFORE ANY MEASUREMENT
===============================================================
Written 2026-08-13, before running any counting script over
commons/data/template_patterns.json. Nothing below has been measured.

DEFINITION I WILL MEASURE (fixed now, so I cannot move the goalposts later)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Corpus  = the museum-tagged patterns in commons/data/template_patterns.json
          (the ~30 real museums; `bay:`/`lattice:`/`beat:` brushes excluded).
Cell    = one entry of a pattern's `tile`, 1 m x 1 m. Wall cell = "4".
Face    = (wall cell, direction d in N/E/S/W) where the neighbour cell in
          direction d is walkable ("1","1s","2","2s","3s"). A face is a
          square metre of wall a visitor can actually stand in front of.
Run     = maximal chain of faces sharing the same direction d and contiguous
          along the axis perpendicular to d. Length in metres = face count.

HEADLINE PREDICTION
-------------------
  % of museum wall FACES that sit in runs of 4 m or longer:  52%

Reasoning: doc/reports/white_cube.md measured em_detail's own stretches at
27.6% of run length in stretches >= 6 m and 38.7% in the 3-5 m band. If the
3/4/5 m stretches are roughly equinumerous, the 4-5 m part of that band is
about 3/4 of its length, i.e. ~29%, giving 27.6 + 29 = 56.6%. I shade that
down to 52% because tile-level faces should break MORE often than em_detail's
stretches at podium/slot cells my definition does not treat as openings.

SECONDARY PREDICTIONS
---------------------
  museums with at least one run >= 4 m (could host a 4 m panel):  26 of 30
  museums with at least one run >= 8 m:                           14 of 30
  % of wall faces sitting in runs of exactly 1 m:                 14%
  mean run length across the corpus:                              4.4 m

WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG IN A USEFUL WAY
----------------------------------------
If the measured >= 4 m share is well ABOVE 52%, then the corpus already has
long planes and the "59% of walls are 1 m" finding in white_cube.md is an
artefact of em_detail's face-splitting, not of the tiles — which would move
the whole modularity argument from the data to the code.
If it is well BELOW, the tiles genuinely cannot host a 4 m module and any
"museum built from modules" story has to change the tiles, not the dealer.
