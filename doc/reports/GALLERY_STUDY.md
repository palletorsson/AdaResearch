# What the strong galleries do — a study of all 18

Measured 2026-08-15 across `ada_encyclopedia/public/galleries/*.html`. Not an impression:
frame counts, section counts and thesis counts were extracted from the markup.

The drop is not tone or care — waves 8–12 are written as carefully as 2–6. **It is that a wave
stopped being a wave.** Waves 2–6 shipped five or six syntheses that could be read against
each other; 8–12 ship one artifact and two sections of commentary about the instrument. One
object cannot make a comparison, so the late galleries argue about method instead, which is
why they read thinner while looking just as finished.

This is the same finding as `feedback_bodies_not_gauges` (3+ syntheses per wave), arrived at
independently from the markup.

## The four things every strong section has

**1 · An H1 that leads with the retraction, never the achievement.**

> Five more, and the one change I had to take back out.
> Three came back thin. One prediction missed.
> It predicted an equality, not a number.
> It works as an axis and fails as an instrument.
> Three repairs, and the instrument was right twice.
> A null that held to the byte.

Not one of these announces a success. Each names a claim and the place it broke. A headline
that cannot be wrong is a headline about nothing.

**2 · A `fam` line: the family's census in one breath.**

> `removal · 6 members · 1 vocabulary · every member a fractal of subtraction`
> `mutuality · 5 members · 1 vocabulary · two of them are Kosaraju and Tarjan`
> `upkeep · 7 station props · same four values, two orders`

It states scope so scope can be checked. "6 members · 1 vocabulary" is falsifiable; "a family
of removal artifacts" is not.

**3 · A thesis that is a proposition, not a description.**

> What a cut leaves — the exact complement of what a mark keeps.
> Mutual reachability is not the same as being connected.
> Four walls are four theories of what is outside the picture.
> Equipment's life is a loop, not a line.
> The more the machine discloses, the smaller its answer gets.

The test: **could a reasonable person disagree?** "Six sorting algorithms visualised" fails it.
"Six sorts, one array, cut at the same fraction of their own work" passes, because the choice
of *fraction of own work* over *absolute step count* is arguable and the gallery argues it.

**4 · A tail carrying the number AND the miss.**

> It predicted bays-against-line — and it is a cluster of four, not a single pair — weakest
> at three of the four policies.
> The absolutes were low by about 6.5× rather than the 1.5× the note allowed for.
> Left at 0.60% on purpose.

Every strong tail reports a measurement *and* where the prediction was wrong about it. The
prediction that agrees with the sweep is worth nothing; the one that disagrees carries the pass.

## The counter-example worth keeping

`genome.html` (60 frames) and `every-artifact-swept.html` (182 frames) have the highest frame
counts in the set and **zero theses**. They are catalogues. Volume is not the variable — those
two prove it. Frame count matters only because it is the fingerprint of having built enough
objects to compare; it is not the thing itself.

## The recipe for the next wave

1. **Five or six syntheses, not one.** Non-negotiable — it is the whole difference.
2. Each gets: `fam` census · thesis as a disputable proposition · body glossing **every value
   by name** · tail with the measurement and the miss.
3. Pre-register a `predicted_degeneracy` per synthesis and let one be wrong in public.
4. H1 names what had to be taken back.
5. A section on the instrument only when the instrument actually failed — in waves 8–12 that
   section grew to fill the space five missing artifacts left behind.
