## The cheat-code

The prior sixteen sequences each had an API. This one's is subtler and it is the whole
point: **every convenience the engine hands you is a decision somebody already made.**
`get_children()` returns an order nobody voted on. Collision layers are a classification
with an outside. Defaults are opinions shipped as facts. Post-crisis is learning to read
the engine as an argument rather than a floor — so the four inserted rungs are all about
the tool, not the topic:

1. **The default is an argument** — gravity 9.8, `cull_back`, white light, scale 1. The
   engine arrives already having decided.
2. **The classifier's outside** — every classification has things it cannot say, and the
   bin marked *other* is always fuller than the labelled bins.
3. **The comparator you supply** — `sort_custom` refuses to sort until YOU give the rule.
   That refusal is the good moment: the engine forcing values to become explicit.
4. **The view from somewhere** — every render is from a `Camera3D`, which is to say from
   a position. There is no view from nowhere here, and there never was anywhere else.

Then the inherited eight follow: bias as structural incompleteness, ethical design,
paraconsistent engineering, situated computation, the commons, rhizome networks,
molecular design, and **the edge is the ground**. Live at
**localhost:3003/postfoundationscrisis-concepts** — 41 tiles, 12 sections (was 32 tiles
of blurbs).

## The super: the_commons_table — and the spine's closing body

A round table with **no head**. Around it sit partial instruments, each honest about its
own outside:

- a **classifier** whose three labelled bins are outnumbered by the drawer marked OTHER
  — and the counts are computed, not asserted: 18 specimens, most of which no rule
  claims;
- a **sorter** with its comparator printed on a card, because the order is somebody's
  values and not a fact;
- two **contradictory readings** held in one bracket, needles opposed, uncollapsed;
- a **camera** on the table aimed at the table, its cone of sight drawn — and therefore
  its blind spot;
- a **rhizome** of cords with no root, every jack reaching every other by more than one
  path;
- the engine's **defaults** shelved as specimens: 9.8, cull_back, white, scale 1.

And at the centre, where a chairman would sit, a lit ring around **nothing** — the edge
is the ground, and the middle was never the authority. 197 meshes, probe clean first try.

Primitives opened the spine by asking what a thing must have in order to be. This closes
it by asking what we owe the things we make.
