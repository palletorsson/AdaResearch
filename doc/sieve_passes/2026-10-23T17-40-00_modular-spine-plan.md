## Where QFEP fits

`QFE = F − λ·E(S) + φ·dE(S,t)`, and the five pieces are its terms as things one can build with:

| term | piece | why |
|---|---|---|
| **F** — order, prediction | `replicate`, `compose`, `field` | a repetition is a prediction of the next cell; a field is a prediction over space |
| **E(S)** — entropy | `by` with random / noise | the per-part function that spreads parts apart |
| **λ** — the balance | the weight inside every `step` rule | ca rule number, boids cohesion vs separation, spring stiffness vs damping — λ is not a piece, it is the parameter every rule carries; the lab's `dial` turns exactly that |
| **φ·dE(S,t)** — rate of change | the `t` in `by` and the tick of `step` | motion is the same function with time running; φ is how much the tick counts |
| **Q** — relational, non-binary | `connect` | the queer move: a part's state is not its own but depends on another's; a spring, an edge, a weight are the same relation with different rules |

The chapters read the formula left to right: order (1–6), oscillation (7, 9), entropy (10–12),
edge (13–16, 20), integration (17, 22), relation (21), synthesis (23–25). That was true of the
spine already; the compression made the chapters BE the phases instead of inventing seventeen.

## Where the edges and the leaks go

Asked next ("where does leaks, edges go?"). A kernel piece is a rule, and every rule refuses
something: its parameters are its edges, of the thirteen material kinds — `connect` is Coupling
outright, `replicate`'s depth is a Ceiling and its minimum size a Deadband, `by`'s index a
Quantiser, `step`'s tick a Clock and its radius a Ball, `field`'s threshold a Deadband and its
subtraction a Mask. What they refuse is a leak, and the plan now declares, per piece, what
escapes and what is spilled to `LeakData` — named, never counted. The scaffold is the kernel's
first leak in code: `composite.gd` spills the line inside the tape before freeing it. The
conceptual edges A–M are not pieces; they are what the late families are about, and φ·dE is
read as their political term: the bus is where what F leaves out accumulates over t. A twin
that hides the edge its original shows is a lie twin.

## The sixth piece

Asked last ("not only the heroes are relevant, I mean all principles. Are they covered?"). They
were not: of the 420 principles the sequence files state, the grammar's words reached 122. The
uncovered ones are verbs — compare (104), measure (67), procedure (47), retain (45) — and no
construction says them. So `measure` is the sixth piece, on Palle's word: a value read off a
thing, retained across steps, compared under a rule; the instrument half of the grammar, with
its own edges (resolution, what the record forgets, what the rule equates). Eight families stand
on it and coverage moved to 150. The foreclosure to watch: a measure is a number, and the leak
bus's rule — name what was dropped, do not count it — must hold for the readings too, or the
grammar has built the scoreboard the book argues against.

## 1. Does this thicken the cognitive water?

Five pieces are holdable where eighteen were a list. A family with an axis makes its variants
one thought (the kernel's own DNA mechanism applies, unchanged). The twin beside the installed
work puts the learner reconstruction in the same hall as the encounter, which is the voice
decision made physical. The synthesis sequences stop being "no terms" and become the three
things one can do to a grammar — compare, parametrize, rewrite — which hands the crisis halls
a concrete object to break, as the biome pass wanted.

## 2. What is foreclosed?

Compressing twelve engines to `step` with rules makes the difference between boids and verlet
a parameter, and a learner may never meet it as a difference in kind. Naming five pieces
first is the Self-Q's colonial gesture exactly: every new idea will look like one of the five.
And the grammar decides what counts as a construction — 213 documents and 993 kept works are
"what the grammar does not reach", which is a judgement the plan makes about the floors, not
one the floors make about themselves. Guard: the rule names stay as lessons (the plan says so);
the kept column is read as the museum's own voice, never as a backlog.

## 3. What lives in the dark spot?

The untermed: films, plaques, screens, the crisis workbenches. Generative as long as they stand
beside the grammar in their own register and the plan's "done" is 189 twins, not 1448 terms.
Sterilising the day "no term yet" starts to mean "not finished". The second dark spot is
`field`: boolean and isosurface folded into one piece on the strength of distance-field
arithmetic, before anyone opened the boolean halls — the plan marks it "to be checked", and it
must stay marked until someone does.
