## 4. Cross-sequence (the phase as a phase)

The intended arc:
1. **foundationscrisis** — show that formalism breaks (Gödel, Russell, non-Euclidean)
2. **qfeplaboratory** — show what replaces it (QFEP as a generative reframing)
3. **postfoundationscrisis** — show what you build with it (ethics, commons, rhizome)

This is the spine's *thesis*. The whole curriculum is a 7-phase argument toward this triplet:
- crisis (problem) → laboratory (theory) → applied (practice)

When read together, the arc is structurally beautiful. **But the third leg is the lightest.** postfoundationscrisis is supposed to demonstrate *what becomes possible* after the crisis, and it currently does this with 3 artifacts in 8 maps. The thesis arc has its conclusion told but not shown.

Cross-arc artifact reuse is good: qfep_formula_3d, lambda_slider, phi_slider, godel_statement_plaque, russell_set_box, florensky_sphere all appear across multiple maps in the arc. This is **good motif behavior** — the formula and the paradoxes recur, building familiarity.

But there's a chamber-style hollowness at postfoundationscrisis: PostCrisis_Synthesis is the spine's *literal* capstone (last narrative map before graphtheory at 19, if graphtheory stays terminal), and it carries no artifacts.

## 5. Three-question sieve

### Thicken?
- The crisis→lab→applied arc is the spine's strongest macro structure. Reading it end-to-end is the curriculum's payoff.
- qfeplaboratory's interactive sliders are the rarest UI in the spine — explicit parameter manipulation. Strong.
- Cross-arc motif reuse (qfep_formula_3d, sliders) builds *recognition*, which is a key synthesis verb.

### Foreclose?
- Sticking the *thesis landing* in postfoundationscrisis without artifacts forecloses the player feeling the resolution. They walk maps with no objects in them. The thesis arrives as text plaques.
- Naming the phase "synthesis" rather than "thesis" hides what it actually is. *Synthesis* implies combining; the phase is **the curriculum's argument**, not just a recombination.
- Putting postfoundationscrisis at order 18 with `unlocks: ["qfeplaboratory"]` and qfeplaboratory at 17 — these contradict each other in the spine. postfoundation says it unlocks qfep, but qfep is earlier in order. The ordering and the unlock graph disagree.

### Dark spot?
- *Whether the player actually closes the loop.* QFEP names itself "F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t)". The player should *feel* having walked F (forces, primitives), E (entropy, noise), λ (edge of chaos, CA), and Δ(t) (waves, change-rate). The phase has the formula but doesn't *map back* to where each term was first met.
- *Catalyst at synthesis.* By now the player has accumulated catalyst affordances from every prior phase. The bracelet at synthesis should be *full* — every stone glowing — and the player should be able to enact the entire QFEP formula through bracelet gestures. This is the dark spot: the catalyst-as-formula-output is implied but not staged.
- *Why graphtheory comes after this.* If graph at 19 is terminal (as in current spine), then the *last* map is graph theory, not the QFEP synthesis. That's structurally backwards. The integration sieve already flagged this; here it compounds.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6a. Rename phase: synthesis → thesis_arc

The phase's true function is to *land the thesis*. Renaming clarifies. `synthesis` reads as "combine prior material" — what the phase actually does is *argue and resolve*.

Proposed:
- foundationscrisis (16) → phase `crisis`
- qfeplaboratory (17) → phase `formula`
- postfoundationscrisis (18) → phase `application`

OR keep one phase name but with sub-phase tags:
- `thesis_arc.crisis`
- `thesis_arc.formula`
- `thesis_arc.application`

OR keep `synthesis` and just write a strong phase truth statement that names the argument.

**Lightest change preferred:** keep `synthesis` label, write clarifying phase truth (see 6c).

### 6b. Fix the unlock graph

`postfoundationscrisis.unlocks` = `["qfeplaboratory"]` but qfep is at order 17, postfoundation at 18. The unlock edge points **backward**. Fix:
- Option 1: swap orders → qfeplaboratory at 18, postfoundationscrisis at 17 (so post unlocks qfep next)
- Option 2: change unlock → `postfoundationscrisis.unlocks: []` (it's terminal)
- Option 3: `qfeplaboratory.unlocks: ["postfoundationscrisis"]` (which already includes recursiveemergence, speculativecomputation — add post too)

**Option 3 preferred.** Lab teaches the formula, then applied uses it. Spine order: crisis → lab → applied is correct.
