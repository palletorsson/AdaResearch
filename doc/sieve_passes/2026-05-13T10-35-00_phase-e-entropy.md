## 4. Cross-sequence (the phase as a phase)

The arc reads:
- **randomness**: pure E(S) — no constraints, all possibility

- **CA**: emergence from rules — *firmly past* the λ boundary into computational universality.

The phase title `E_entropy` only fits randomness. Noise is already λ-adjacent (its own qfep_connection says *"Noise is the λ parameter tuned for coherence"*). CA is firmly λ_edge (universal computation).

**The phase is mis-bounded.** It collapses three different stages of the QFEP arc into one label.

## 5. Three-question sieve

### Thicken?
- The trio works pedagogically: pure noise → smooth noise → emergent rules. Conceptually the player walks from chaos to structure-in-chaos to chaos-producing-structure.
- Naming Shannon at randomness (today's add) makes the *measure* of entropy explicit.

### Foreclose?
- The phase name `E_entropy` makes the player expect *disorder*. By CA they're seeing **highly ordered emergent structures** — gliders, oscillators, Turing-complete computation. The phase label foreclosed seeing these as ordered.
- Worse: the next phase `lambda_edge` includes fractals and L-systems and procgen — but **CA is already those things**. There's redundancy where the phase boundary should fall.

### Dark spot?
- *Where exactly entropy stops being random and becomes structured.* The transition from random_walk to perlin_noise to Rule 110 is the curriculum's deepest pedagogical move and it lives between sequences, never in a map.
- *Computational universality as the punchline*. CA contains Rule 110 = Turing complete. This is one of the most important results in CS. It's an objective in CA's list; it doesn't get a dedicated map.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6a. Move CA out of E_entropy into λ_edge

`cellularautomata` belongs at the start of `lambda_edge` (between current spine positions 9 and 10). Its truth — *"simple rules → complex behavior, Rule 110 is Turing complete"* — is the λ_edge thesis statement.

Proposed reorder:
- 7 randomness  → stays at E_entropy
- 8 noise       → stays at E_entropy (it's the bridge — keep here as the transition)
- ~~9 CA~~ → **move to 10** under lambda_edge
- Existing fractals/lsystems/etc shift forward in order

This makes E_entropy a 2-sequence phase (randomness + noise) and λ_edge a 6-sequence phase. λ_edge gets denser but its sequences now share a coherent ground: *emergent order from constrained entropy*.

### 6b. Foreground computational universality at CA

A standalone map within CA: `CA_Universal_Computation` (or similar). Houses Rule 110 + a Turing-complete demo + the "rules compute everything" claim. Currently this is one bullet in objectives; the result deserves a dedicated artifact.

- **E_entropy**: 2 sequences, both about possibility-space and its constraints

Load-bearing rule out of this pass:

> **A sequence belongs to the phase whose claim it most strongly carries.** Where pedagogical content drifts across phases (CA fits both E_entropy AND λ_edge), it belongs to the phase that needs it most as a *thesis statement*. λ_edge needs CA as its opening claim; E_entropy can do without it.
