## 2. The argument the spine wants to make

Reading the spine top-down, the implicit argument is:

> Math starts with primitives (points, lines, polygons). It accumulates: transformation, array, color, change. These are *deterministic* — F. Then we meet the geometry of *force* (forces, waves) — F at the boundary of behavior. Then *entropy* (randomness, noise) — pure E(S). Then *the edge* (CA, fractals, L-systems, procgen, swarm) — where F and E together produce form. Then *integration* (softbodies, ML) — systems descending landscapes shaped by both. Then *relation* (graphs) — the substrate that was always under it all. Then the *thesis* (crisis, formula, application) — the foundations break, but here is what doesn't: F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t).

Read this way, every phase has a job. The argument is **build the formula by walking it.**

But the spine *as found* has three structural noise patterns:
- Half-step orders (4.5, 12.5, 12.7) — post-hoc insertions
- Phase labels that disagree with sequence-internal `qfep_term` fields (swarm, isosurfaces, CA)
- Backward unlock edges (post → lab)

These three patterns share a cause: **the spine grew by addition, never by reordering**. Each pass added a sequence at the boundary of where it felt right; nothing got moved later when a better home appeared. The sieve passes have been exactly the *reordering audit* the spine didn't get organically.

## 4. The QFEP formula made walking

The formula `F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t)` should map term-by-term to phase positions in the spine. After reorder, this becomes legible:

Each term has a home. The player walks the formula's parts before meeting it whole. This is the *retroactive recognition* the synthesis sieve flagged.

## 5. Three-question sieve (on the macro)

### Thicken?
- The reordered spine is one continuous argument. Every phase pushes the next. The catalyst arsenal accumulates in lockstep — by synthesis, the bracelet has ~20 affordances, one per major concept.
- The new `relation` mini-phase makes graphtheory's substrate-status legible. The player meets graphs *after* seeing every prior system was secretly a graph; this is the spine's most pedagogically rich moment.
- The QFEP formula has a *walking map* — each term's home is known, each can be revisited.

### Foreclose?
- Tightening the spine to one argument **forecloses pedagogical pluralism**. The player walking out of order, or skipping a phase, loses the argument's force.
- Naming `relation` as its own phase **forecloses** treating graphs as a subject; it becomes a *moment of recognition* rather than a sequence-in-its-own-right.
- Postfoundationscrisis's hollow maps are a foreclosure: the thesis ends with text, not body. The reorder doesn't fix this — phase 2 build does.

### Dark spot?
- *Whether the player can read the spine at all.* The reordered shape is legible to someone who can hold all 19 sequences in mind; for a player walking it for the first time, the argument is in the *experience*, not in the labels. The spine's argument needs a **shadow document** — a one-page map that names each phase's job. (This file *is* that document.)
- *What gets harder after reorder.* isosurfaces + boolean_surfaces at 4.6/4.7 means the player meets marching cubes and CSG **before forces**. That's a pedagogical hard step: complex surface construction without the vectors-as-physics frame. Mitigation: vectors are named in transformation (already), so the substrate is there; but the depth-of-claim ("define a field, extract a surface") might land flat without forces to motivate it.
- *Whether λ_edge can hold 5 sequences without fatigue.* The phase grows from 5 (current, but with isosurfaces + boolean_surfaces wrong) to 5 (CA, fractals, lsystems, procgen, swarm — all coherent). Same count but tighter. The fatigue risk is real: five "emergence" sequences in a row may blur. Mitigation: the bracelet affordances at λ_edge (evolve, split, grow, seed, flock) are distinct *verbs* — the player feels different at each.
