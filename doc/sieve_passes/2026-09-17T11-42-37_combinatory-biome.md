# Sieve pass — the combinatory biome

**Target:** `doc/COMBINATORY_BIOME.md` — the biome as the living record of the spine's
vocabulary: operators unlocked sequence by sequence, bodies as expression trees over
them, a lineage log on disk, and a dream tool that replays the record (stale,
sufficient, unreachable, counterfactual) after the pattern of Dream-RSI
(arXiv:2609.14858). Three gardens per threshold, no number.

**Occasioned by:** Palle, 2026-09-17 — "I dream of having the game, an evolving biome…
combine, dream, next sequence… what is sufficient, what happens if we add cellular
automata and still let it evolve, and then the crises maps. See what I mean."

**Change made during the walk:** building the vocabulary ladder against
`curriculum_spine.json` showed `commons/maps/soft_stages.json` lags the spine (no
tiling, no formfinding; isosurfaces/boolean at 4.6/4.7 vs spine 17/18). The sync
became step 1 of the memo.

---

## 1. Does this thicken the cognitive water?

> What relational handles does it add?
> What ways of moving through become possible?
> What new things does it make thinkable?

It gives the spine a body: a typed operator ladder makes "what the world is made of so
far" a thing that can be composed, and the ring around each hall a population the
visitor can read back to the sequences that made it. It makes history a second
object: a lineage on disk with seeds and fitness names turns every walk into a world
that can be walked again, so stale / sufficient / unreachable are answered, not felt.
It joins three things that were apart — the bracelet memo (tending λ), the fractal
database (a tree at any depth, and its limit), GeneticProgramming (tree genomes in one
hall) — into one loop whose clock is the sequence index, and hands the crisis
sequences a concrete object to break.

## 2. What is foreclosed?

> What thinking becomes harder?
> What habits are suppressed?
> What grammar is installed, at what cost?

The score, deliberately: the replay objective stays with the agents; the rooms show
three gardens and never a figure, and the fitness must not become visitor-visible
either. The cheap ring: composed bodies cost more than parametric ones (the museum
already flags 130 ms builds), so the tree depth bound is a budget and must be named
as one. Walking out of order: "so far" is defined by completed stages, as soft_stages
already does — it must never be re-keyed to spine position. Typed refusals: a grammar
that refuses union(line, line) also refuses the interesting accident; the types are
kept to nine and every refusal is listed, not silently dropped.

## 3. What lives in the dark spot?

> What does the encoding hide?
> Is what it hides a generative habitat or a sterilising seal?

"Never survived" may be a fact about the fitness, not the world — the record carries
the fitness name and the counterfactual runs under any pressure, so the spot stays
open; calling a combination impossible because one fitness culled it would be the
seal. The lineage is not a tree (two parents, cross-kingdom): the paper, the fold and
the three gardens all compute on a projection — generative if the crises show it,
sterilising if the projection is taken for the thing. What the record cannot carry:
the visitor's attention, patina (non-genetic memory of care), other walkers' worlds
(the headset's log lives in user:// and returns only by USB). The dream is of this
walker's world; the tool must say whose history it dreamed.
