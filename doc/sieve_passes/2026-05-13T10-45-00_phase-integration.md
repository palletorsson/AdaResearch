## 4. Cross-sequence (the phase as a phase)

The phase reads:
- softbodies = matter learning shape
- machinelearning = computation learning function
- (swarm) = colony learning coordination
- (graph) = relation made explicit

The two integration-true sequences (softbodies, ML) share a common structure: **a system descends a landscape**. Cloth descends an energy landscape under gravity + spring constraints. Gradient descent descends a loss landscape. **Both are physical systems finding equilibrium on a manifold of states.** This is the *real* integration claim — not just "F↔E," but "the system *settles* in the landscape."

Swarm is different: stigmergy is **lateral**, not landscape-descent. The agents don't descend a loss — they leave trails that change the field for each other. That's a λ_edge mechanism (rules + memory of rules → emergent global pattern), not a landscape problem.

Graph theory is **pre-physics**: nodes/edges with no energy term. The truth "everything is a graph if you squint hard enough" places it as **a substrate-claim**, akin to "everything is a vector" (forces) or "everything is a number" (primitives). It is closer to F_order than to integration.

## 5. Three-question sieve

### Thicken?
- Softbodies + ML pair is genuinely thick: cloth/jelly as physical landscape, NN as computational landscape. Different substrates, same principle. The 33-artifact softbodies sequence is well-stocked.
- Naming **landscape-as-shared-form** would tighten the phase: *"In the integration phase, every system settles."*

### Foreclose?
- Stuffing graphtheory at order 19 hides its *substrate* status. Players reach graphs only after ML, when graphs were silently the structure of every neural network, every flock, every CA neighborhood, every L-system tree. Late-game framing forecloses the *"this was always here"* recognition.
- Putting swarm in integration dilutes the *landscape-descent* claim. Swarm is a field-update algorithm, not a descent algorithm.
- ML's nested vs root `artifact_groups` schema duplication is a maintenance debt — one truth source needed.

### Dark spot?
- *What integration actually is.* The QFEP φΔE(S,t) term is **time-evolution under F↔E pressure**. Both softbodies and ML are time-evolution systems descending landscapes. The phase's true claim is **dynamical descent**, not just "synthesis."
- *Where graph theory really belongs.* If everything is a graph, then graph theory is an *atom* of structure, comparable to vectors. Placing it among the integration arc gives it an unearned position; placing it earlier would let players see graphs in CA neighborhoods, force-bonds, ANT colony pheromone fields, etc.
- *Swarm's natural sibling.* Procedural generation (WFC) already uses local-constraint-propagation; swarm is the same mechanism with agents instead of cells. Both belong in λ_edge.

Beautiful arc. Five flavors of the same claim.

### 6b. Promote graphtheory to a foundation/substrate position

Three options:

**Option A (preferred):** New phase `foundation_relation` between F_order (geometric foundation) and oscillation. Holds graphtheory at an early order (around 4.8, after change). Reads as: *first what things are (primitives, transformation, array, color, change), then how they relate (graph). Then forces, waves, entropy, λ_edge.*

But graphtheory has 8 maps and demands prereqs (transformation, array_tutorial — which is fine, both come first), and pedagogically belongs *after* the player has things to relate. So early-foundation feels right, but the player has only the primitives vocabulary by then. The depth-of-claim ("everything is a graph") might land empty.

**Option B:** Move graphtheory to a meta-position **before synthesis**. Between machinelearning and foundationscrisis. Reads as: *now that you've seen all this, here is the structure they all shared.* This is the *retroactive recognition* placement — naming what was always there.

**Option C:** Leave graphtheory where it is (order 19, terminal in integration) but reframe it as the *bridge to synthesis*. Less structural change, treats it as the "everything connects" reveal before the final QFEP arc.

**Option B recommended.** Place graphtheory at order 18 (between ML and foundationscrisis), in a new mini-phase `relation` (or as a hinge between integration and synthesis). Reframe truth to: *"This was always here. Every cell, force, agent, neuron was a node. Every adjacency, attraction, message, weight was an edge. Now you see it."*
