# Sieve pass — orb-gesture-detector

_Recorded 2026-05-11T10:54:27_

**Target:** the catalyst gun-shot replacement. A two-handed gesture forms an orb between the palms that projects a catalyst field forward; agents bathed in the cone advance one personality step per dose-window.

**Design refinement made during the walk:** *two-handed = sustained continuous dose; one-handed = burst with cooldown.* Accessibility becomes a strategic mode with a real cost, not a fallback. The "gun's continuous problem" (held-trigger without depth) is fixed by binding continuity to commitment — you sustain only by giving up the use of your other hand.

## 1. Does this thicken the cognitive water?

> What relational handles does it add?
> What ways of moving through become possible?
> What new things does it make thinkable?

Six relational handles, none of which the gun-shot frame had:

- **Capability-as-relation.** The orb cannot be possessed. It exists only while the gesture is held. Removed instantly when the palms part.
- **Two-handed as a primitive verb.** Adds *hold-together-forward* to a gesture grammar that already includes bracelet-rotation, grab, place.
- **Sustained contact as conversion.** Replaces *instant zap* with *dose*. Trains thinking-in-times rather than thinking-in-strikes — ayni-shaped: catalyst as time given, not as force applied.
- **Distance-from-head as commitment.** Reach scales with how far the player pushes the orb out from their body. The body becomes a parameter.
- **Audiotactile feedback as state-change channel.** Hum + haptic register conversion before vision does.
- **Choice between root-and-sustain (two-handed) and move-and-burst (one-handed cooldown).** A relational decision the player makes per situation. The catalyst stops reading as a weapon and starts reading as a reaction the body catalyses — two hands as stable vessel, one hand as dispenser with recharge.

## 2. What is foreclosed?

> What thinking becomes harder under this structure?
> What habit does it suppress?
> What cognitive grammar does it install — and at what cost?

Honest foreclosure list:

- **Drive-by interaction.** Cannot catalyst-and-move without committing to one of two modes. *Deliberate.*
- **Long-range targeting / sniper grammar.** Cone is short; far agents require approach. *Deliberate.*
- **Instant feedback.** Dose-window means lag between *I am doing it* and *something changed*. Some players will read this as broken in the first session. *Friction-as-induction.*
- **Weapon muscle memory.** Trigger-click-fire reflex actively suppressed. *Foreclosure-as-feature.*
- **Pointing-as-targeting.** Cone is volumetric; no reticle. *Deliberate.*
- **Free other hand while sustaining.** Two-handed sustain commits both hands; one-handed costs cooldown. *The choice is the depth.*

The accessibility foreclosure flagged earlier in the walk has been **eliminated by the design refinement** — one-handed play is now its own strategic mode with a real cost, not a pity-fallback. The remaining foreclosures are honest costs paid for honest gains.

## 3. What lives in the dark spot?

> What does this encoding hide?
> Is the hiding generative (room for experience to exceed) or sterilising (sealed)?
> Is the surrounding encoding rigorous enough that the dark spot is habitat, not mystification?

What the encoding cannot reach but the system requires:

- **The felt presence of the gesture.** The orb is encoded as midpoint + direction; the gesture is a tension between palms in the player's body. The system needs that felt tension to mean something. It cannot name it.
- **The two felt modes.** Two-handed sustain is a *root*. One-handed cooldown is a *breath* — inhale-recharge, exhale-dose. The cadence lives in the body, not in a timer.
- **What conversion feels like for the agent.** No encoding of pain, fear, recognition, gratitude. The player projects via movement signatures. Glissant's right to opacity, applied to the f-agent.
- **The implicit chord-shift on bracelet mode change.** Audio register read without conscious decoding.
- **The reading of the catalyst as care vs as violence.** Mechanically identical; the player's framing distinguishes. *What does the lichen gain?* is the unanswerable that makes the system work.

**Generative — fragile.** The dark spot stays habitat if and only if we refuse the legibilisation creep:

- no reticle
- no hit confirmation pop
- no damage number
- no accuracy stat
- no "you converted +1 friend" toast

Audiotactile and creature-visual change are the *only* feedback channels. The dark spot seals the moment any one of those UI accretions appears.

The biggest risk for this slice is not building it wrong; it is building it right and then *creeping features* over the next year that fill in the dark spot to make it more legible.

**Recorded design rule (load-bearing):** *No orb-action produces a number on screen or a confirmation toast.*
