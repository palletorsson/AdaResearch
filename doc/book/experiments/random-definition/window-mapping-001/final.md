# A range of possibilities

The windows stand beside one another. Their numbers can agree while their colours differ. On the right, two channels may remain still while one is allowed to vary. Or the same values may occupy a narrower interval. No new surprise has been requested from the source. We have changed where a surprise is allowed to appear.

Elsewhere in the room, a sphere carries a visitor away and brings them back. Its direction and distance vary inside an authored range. Returning is an agreement the program keeps. The window and the journey let us approach the same question through different experiences: what has been left open, and what has already been decided?

The frame monitor adds a complication. Its line can look irregular, but appearance alone does not establish a source of entropy. We must ask what is measured, what is stored, and how the measurement becomes a seed. A seed is already a commitment to a procedure that will use it.

Palle's request joins these questions and changes the apparatus: the colour window now offers an explicit range and assignment comparison. Its arithmetic and replay have been tested. We do not yet have an account of what a visitor notices through it. Does narrowing the colours reveal a relation, make a difference harder to see, or invite another mapping? That account can revise this passage, the tutorial, or the apparatus.
