## Capacity without overload

By the end of Change the student can hold a baseline, inspect a sample and name a discrete step. Forces makes its consequence measurable. Randomness adds uncertainty in sampling. Shaders changes the receiver. CA keeps the past. Later control, inference and fault experiments reuse those capacities. These are returns with new jobs, not repeated introductions.

An early hall may notice a phenomenon without teaching its complete mechanism. The plan must label that encounter honestly and point to the later full lesson. Only one primary artifact reconstructs each new principle; another artifact may challenge or extend the experience without adding another code requirement.
