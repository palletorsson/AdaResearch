# Evidence, literature, decision

Working protocol, 15 October 2026. Based on Palle's supplied proposal and his selection of cellular automata for the third calibration area. The first [prepared handoff](../iterations/2026-10-15-independent-writer/README.md) uses Random Definition. The calibration passages are nominations, not three approved models.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Supplies | Does not decide alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situated researcher | Current apparatus, placement, actions, technical distinctions, source versions, actual observations and open questions. | What the encounter must mean in the final text. |
| Independent literary writer/editor | A complete chapter with its own movement of attention; factual questions; proposed changes of emphasis, relation or sequence. | That an imagined apparatus is already installed, or that a candidate is adopted. |
| Palle | Calibration choices, artistic direction, response to consequences, adoption or further experiment. | A technical claim merely by preferring its wording. Check the claim. |

The final need not explain an artifact's intended meaning. It remains accountable to what the artifact does. A funny, quiet, inconclusive or unexpected reading is available when it arises from that material. Literary disagreement can initiate an artifact revision as well as a prose revision. Tutorial and final retain their ability to initiate and revise experiments.

Independent here describes a deliberately different position of knowledge. It does not certify that another model, another chat or the same model with a different prompt has an independent judgement. Record who wrote and read the work and what they received.

## Prepare three calibration examples

Use three different encounters: embodied, epistemological, and conceptual/poetic. For this first preparation they are the five-row abacus, Geiger study and a cellular-automaton bridge. Preserve the exact passage and source edition; distinguish approval of a voice from approval of every current sentence. The changed abacus requires a fresh assessment even though its predecessor established a working “we”.

For each example Palle can retain, revise or reject it and name the detail that earns attention. Once selected, ask the writer to infer useful principles of spatial precision, rhythm, explanation and withholding. The examples calibrate a practice; they do not impose a common paragraph pattern or a compulsory narrator across halls. Three alternatives to one scene are not three kinds of encounter.

## Prepare the evidence dossier

Give the writer a compact, versioned account of the selected sequence. Describe what is visible or audible, what action changes it, what stays, what is recorded, and what the system actually computes. Include necessary factual limits before writing. Mark implemented code, placed artifact, tested behaviour, reported encounter and proposed experiment separately. A screenshot is a view of a build, not evidence of a visitor's sensation.

Keep proposed gems, five consequence fields, author assignments and the existing literary argument in the internal research record. Do not pass them as a required interpretation. Supply exact technical excerpts or additional evidence if the writer asks. Withholding a theoretical conclusion is useful; withholding a known factual problem is not.

The current Random Definition dossier explicitly records that the saved map removes MOVE / KEEP even though its code and Final-tagged position remain. That gap cannot be silently converted into a witnessed encounter.

## One complete candidate and two readings

An optional form-discovery pass can precede the complete candidate. The [five returned studies](../iterations/2026-10-15-returned-literary-forms/README.md) show why: asking every text to cover the same itinerary can prevent substantive divergence. Short works may select one object, change narrator or use a fictional situation while remaining precise about the actual apparatus. Read these as works before their strategy notes. Develop a form that earns attention into a complete chapter; do not pad every study to the chapter budget. This exploration does not adopt a changed room/final sequence. See [the two-stage method](IDEAS_AND_DIVERGENT_READINGS.md).

1. Palle selects/refines the calibration set. Freeze those passages and the dossier supplied to the writer. Record any additional material requested later.
2. Ask for one complete chapter. Normally allow 600–1,500 prose words according to the gems that develop; neither fill a quota nor optimize each artifact independently. Put factual questions, apparatus proposals and proposed route changes after the chapter.
3. Read the chapter as literature first, without showing its intended effects. Can someone unfamiliar with the game locate the encounters? Where does attention change, where does it become a catalogue, what remains interesting, and what does the reader want next? Record the reader's words and whether they read a passage or the whole chapter. Surprise, confusion and refusal are evidence too.
4. Then conduct a technical reading. Check the actual claims against the retained source/build: visible hand versus body, calculation versus acquisition, source record versus inference, implemented behaviour versus proposal. Classify each problem as an error, an ambiguous claim, a permissible interpretation or a proposed change. Request the smallest correction that resolves a factual error; do not automatically turn the passage back into documentation.
5. Let the writer revise with both readings visible. Preserve disagreement and the prior candidate. Palle can adopt, retain the reference, request another comparison or send a question back to the apparatus. No score or successful test adopts the chapter.

For Random Definition, room and final currently share encounter order. The writer may propose another sequence and explain its consequence. Keep it as a proposal until the matching room change is made and checked; do not silently publish a different order or move magnets on the writer's behalf. Other maps need their own decision.
