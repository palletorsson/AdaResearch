# Teaching choices across the museum

5 October 2026. Read alongside the [whole lineage](index.html) or the [editable hall cards](http://localhost:3003/tutorial-cards).

The question for each hall is: **what can the student do here with what they already know, and what does that make possible next?** The artifact can contain more than the lesson teaches. Reflective writing and exhibits can carry connections without adding a programming exercise.

This pass covers 207 halls in 25 sequences. Short subject lists now appear in all 197 existing `tutorial.md` files; 10 tutorials are missing. After the authorized eight-interaction revision, 65 focuses are supported by their current lesson and 132 require some revision. These are editorial assessments of the writing, not evidence that a learner has mastered the material or that a proposed cross-hall transfer works in the museum.

## The opening connection

Point Zero introduces initialization and repeated updates. Point One gives named values a visible and physical use. Coordinates adds picking up the existing body and reading its position in local and world frames. Lines adds the relation between two positions: displacement and distance. Trace keeps positions in order and traverses neighbouring entries. Grid gives that retained material addresses. Triangle and Polyhedra then distinguish positions from the connections that make faces and bodies.

That is a useful progression. Lines still describes a separate starter. Trace and Grid now follow the museum artifacts and their session handoff: shaped world-position snapshots, display fitting, then XYZ snapping. The original is kept separately, and stopping the game clears the session record. Coordinates keeps the author's agreed focus and now explains release; distance remains in Lines. A hands-on trial remains necessary.

## Where to concentrate

- **Lines → Trace → Grid:** bring Lines into the existing museum next, then try the three lessons together. Trace and Grid now name the real record handoff, preserved source and derived display; their source agreement is not yet learner verification.
- **Primitives → Transformation:** use known forms for composition, then introduce time-dependent change. The three new primitive exploration lessons are still outlines; their labels should not be mistaken for completed instruction.
- **Arrays → Colour:** Array's opening tutorial is absent. Its object references, indexed writes and collection traversal matter to later lessons. Colour's complete returning function and small class are proposed learning steps that still need to be written and connected to a declared receiver.
- **Waves → Randomness → Noise:** keep first encounters distinct from deeper returns. Several current recipes differ from the proposed encounter; some observational companions need a specific learner operation before they can teach the capability named on the card.
- **Shaders and growth:** four shader lessons and Shape Grammars are missing. Teach a small usable operation and make its earlier coordinate, colour or rule connection explicit. A complex renderer need not become the entire lesson.
- **Later computational and reflective rooms:** Graph Flow and ML Synthesis currently teach different algorithms from their storyboards. Some Foundations/QFEP demonstrations overstate what their staged results establish. Resolve the algorithm or claim before expanding the prose.

## How we continue

Use **Learning elements** to agree on the subjects, then **How this lesson fits** to check what the hall inherits, adds and carries. Saved author choices remain authoritative. Changing a choice, source or sequence order makes the relevant connection visible for review; it does not lock the museum into this outline.

Rewrite one agreed tutorial at a time. A revision is ready when its small learner action can be explained, its supplied machinery is named, and the next lesson can actually use the capability it claims to inherit. A practical check can be modest: choose new endpoints, traverse another drawing, or construct another valid face without copying the original values.
