# Random Definition: a first writing trial

**Design example, not an executed reading trial.** The machine-readable [pool, cards and routes](writing_pilot.json) preserve source versions and leave ghost-reader runs, human reviews and selection empty. The [writing specification](WRITING_SPEC.md) defines the method.

The initiating question is: **when the same numbers return, what has returned—and what have our choices allowed to change?** This preserves the 48-value bench as the first pilot while giving `final.md` a purpose beyond explaining its controls.

## A small research pool

| Candidate | Available material | Editorial possibility and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K1 | The same sixteen triples drive five spatial assignments | Begin with a difference the reader can follow; colour uses three channels, spatial views use their mean |
| K2 | Keeping values and a recipe supports replay | Separate return of a configuration from return of a lived encounter; the latter is an interpretation |
| K3 | The current final's RAND passage and its cited primary source | Ask why reusable chance was wanted; recheck the source before carrying its history into a new draft |
| K4 | The current final's falling-number page | Introduce storage versus access; inspect/run this apparatus before asserting present behaviour |
| K5 | The current final's returning sphere | Ask what a return rule preserves; separate a documented rule from the passenger's experience |
| K6 | Equal-sum RGB triples share their mean; the user requested less amplification and fuller trays | Investigate what a mapping omits and how an author's preferences revise it; do not infer general visitor pleasure |

K3–K5 remain useful pool entries even if the first draft does not select them. Existing prose is evidence of a published/drafted claim, not independent evidence that its factual or runtime assertion is correct today. These candidates carry recheck requirements.

## Competing routes through changing questions

**A — consequence first:** K1 → K2 → K6 → K3 → K4 → K5. Start by following one triple across views, then ask what is kept and what the mean loses. History enters once reuse has become a question.

**B — braid the room with history:** K1 → K3 → K4 → K2 → K5 → K6. A local observation opens onto historical storage, returns to the present record, then makes the mapping itself questionable.

**H — candidate hybrid:** K1 → K2 → K3 → K4 → K5 → K6. Preserve A's opening and B's later movement. This is a candidate suggested by the supplied account of an earlier editorial preference. It is not this trial's winning route.

All three use the same candidate pool. Draft lengths should be comparable enough to identify order as the main difference; log any substantial changes in wording or content. The purpose is to compare reasons for continuing, not to force identical sentences into unsuitable transitions. The actual paragraph cards carry information, job, attitude, reader gain, bridge, size and optional critical/experimental fields. They are planning movements; a draft may split or combine them with a versioned reason.

## A grounded critical return

The current spatial recipe uses `u = (a+b+c)/3`. Consider the **constructed arithmetic fixtures** `(0.9, 0.2, 0.1)` and `(0.1, 0.2, 0.9)`. They have equal means and different RGB assignments. They are not samples collected from a visitor or an unreported museum run.

A proposed reading is that one spatial measurement makes a difference unavailable. An alternative is that the shared mean gives us a useful common measure for comparing views. Neither implies that the mean is simply wrong. The technical question is reconstructable; the value of the omission remains open.

One bounded follow-up could hold both triples, geometry and draw order fixed, then change **only** the height rule from mean to first channel. Record the two recipes and outputs, retain/replay them, reconstruct from the tutorial, and ask what became perceptible or harder to compare. The expected numerical difference is a prediction, not an observed encounter. If the change is unhelpful or the common measure is preferred, revise the original critical association instead of treating the participant as having missed the point. This is a proposed branch, not a change made to the live bench by the writing spec.

The same method can later investigate a movement boundary or the lava wall's sample/hash distinction. It need not build those works before testing this chapter's composition.

## Work and acceptance

1. **W006 — inspect and enrich the pool.** Recover and verify selected sources; park attractive but unsupported claims. Keep author feedback distinct from visitor findings.
2. **W007 — develop cards and A/B routes.** State each paragraph's job, changing question, bridge and size. Preserve the hybrid as an alternative, not a default verdict.
3. **W008 — draft and conduct ghost readings.** Preserve exact prefixes and responses in fresh contexts; report technical uncertainty and transition friction. This task can progress while human reconstruction remains open.
4. **W009 — investigate one critical association.** Link an exact operation, rival reading and revision condition to a bounded comparison. Use the existing companion for actual evidence. An unsupported or inconclusive interpretation may be revised or deferred honestly.
5. **W010 — record editorial and human review.** Select, combine, revise or defer the route with a named decision and evidence limits. Review tutorial and final independently and return an unresolved question to the task graph.

W004 remains the later edition review, with W010 added as a prerequisite. No new task is marked complete because this scaffold exists. Ghost-reader reports and human response records remain empty until those encounters occur.
