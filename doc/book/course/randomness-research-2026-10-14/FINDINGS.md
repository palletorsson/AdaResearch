## Reuse before adding more machinery

1. **Seed bench and returning sphere.** The current Definition prose already distinguishes a saved atmospheric seed, a local generator, +1 DRAW and an imagined sleeve that is explicitly unbuilt. Preserve this unusually good bridge between observation and structural change. The proposed record/interpretation adapter extends it; it should not overwrite the existing encounter with an abstract RNG lecture.
2. **Distribution and entropy.** Existing tutorials already retain samples, rebin, compare arrangements and identify limits of a count. The proposal changes when these capabilities are used, so handover paragraphs and prerequisite statements need rewriting after acceptance. Moving Entropy later requires an elementary count at Gaussian, not a hidden dependency on the old order.
3. **Geometry and field receivers.** The Perlin bridge, Noise One's torus pair, voxel slices, cellular/box rooms and physical terrain provide different receiving operations. Reuse them. A new universal “noise wall” would duplicate valuable distinctions.
4. **Related Weather.** Its source has independent/accumulated/filtered signals, `replay()`, retained records and `get_state()`/`record_changed`. This is useful prior art for the primary experiment seam. The moving view slits are not moving holes in the fixed glass collider. Do not turn its readout or raw state into a stronger spectral or collision claim without inspecting the signal kernel and test.
5. **Monte Carlo and pheromone.** The sample-budget study already offers a concrete bounded experiment. The pheromone comparison is a useful transition from a trace that records to a signal that influences subsequent choices. Extend record transfer before inventing another demonstration of either principle.

The randomness revision status records substantial previous lesson and museum checks. Preserve their baseline and current intent, then reuse the relevant tests when an implementation task touches them. This pass has not rerun those historic checks.

The local task schema is already defined. A separate goal file preserves the existing book tasks while linking the new route, particle path, final text and explicit dependencies. Technical claims with uncertain scope are repair tasks; the plan does not convert them into findings of runtime failure without running them.

## References for the conceptual boundaries

Godot's [RNG documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_randomnumbergenerator.html) distinguishes seed and captured state; its [Crypto API](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_crypto.html) provides secure random bytes. Neither is a certificate for a custom timing-jitter source. Engine and procedure versions belong with replay evidence.

Friston's [free-energy review](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2787) concerns a variational bound, and [Schwartenbeck and colleagues](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1423327/1/fpsyg-04-00710.pdf) discuss exploration, novelty and surprise within that research tradition. Preserve the supplied text's distinction between this literature and Ada's proposed QFEP framework. In particular, avoid claiming conventional FEP simply excludes exploration, or that an artistic boundary is automatically a statistical Markov blanket.
