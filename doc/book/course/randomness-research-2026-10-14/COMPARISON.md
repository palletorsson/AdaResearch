## What remains from the committed spec

Artifact, experiment-cycle and specified cross-hall checks remain distinct; loading, stability, performance, headset operation and independent human learning remain separate evidence. Keep explicit fixed ticks, draw roles/order, event state, independent snapshots, boundary policies and dependency versions.
