# Randomness as shared experiment

**14 October 2026 · Recursive book architecture, revised hall proposal and five particle tutorials.**

Ada should develop from explaining known results toward experiments through which author and visitor can learn something not known in advance. The accepted seven-step cycle ends with an observation leading to another change, beyond a successful replay.

Both `tutorial.md` and `final.md` can initiate experiments and be revised by their consequences. The tutorial develops construction and inquiry; the final develops encounter and possible futures. Every selected cycle reviews both texts, allowing reasoned retention or an unresolved question. The user has selected the 48-value seed bench first; subsequent experiments follow its actual evidence.

The [revised final-text draft](FINAL.md) develops dissipated authorship and learning debt in the writing itself.
