**14 October 2026.** The original research text is preserved verbatim in [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md). The current task asks for a proposed order and artifacts, a particle tutorial path, final writing based on that text, and the `to-spec` process for work to undertake. It is a design/specification request; this pass has not implemented the proposed museum changes.

The author explicitly approved the experiment cycle and expanded it as follows:

> The central test should follow a visitor who:
> 1. Establishes a baseline with recorded parameters and seed.
> 2. Changes one condition while keeping the others fixed.
> 3. Observes and compares the consequences.
> 4. Preserves an interesting result, including its configuration and relevant state.
> 5. Replays the result to verify reproducibility.
> 6. Reconstructs the experiment independently from the tutorial.
> 7. Uses the observation to propose or implement a further change to the system.

The author also required three distinct levels: artifact tests, experiment-cycle tests, and cross-hall integration where the design promises that capability. Scene loading, runtime stability, performance and VR interaction are tested separately. Verification documents whether an experiment produces an observable difference, a meaningful question, or a reason to reconstruct the apparatus. Stored-outcome replay and new unpredictable acquisition must be distinguished.
