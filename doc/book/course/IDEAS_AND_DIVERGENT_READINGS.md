# Keeping an idea without fixing its prose

An idea can survive the rejection of a chapter. Keep it in the map's `composition/ideas.json`, with a stable ID, a concise claim or question, its source, the apparatus that supports it, its limits and a next test. Preserve an exact sentence when that sentence matters, but also name what it makes available. A sentence and the idea it carries can have different futures.

Use distinct statuses: a proposal, a reported point of attention, a technical tension, a calibration example from another map, an adopted use, or a retired idea. Record who supplied a response. “This reader noticed it” is evidence of attention; it is not proof of a universal gem. Do not remove a useful failure from the record. Append a disposition and reason.

The first [bank and five full readings](../iterations/2026-10-15-five-randomness-readings/README.md) concern Random Definition. The CA bridge supplies a calibration principle about material consequence; it does not become an artifact in the randomness room.

## Five texts as exploration

The first comparison held the apparatus version and selected encounter order fixed. It changed how a complete text develops: what opens it, what is delayed, what takes time, what kinds of evidence appear, and where it ends. A different adjective or a replaced middle paragraph does not constitute a different full composition.

For this trial the forms are continuous observation, documentary montage, an investigation, a composition of durations, and a speculative constitution. They are written by the same situated assistant. They are alternatives to hand over, not five independent judgements and not imitations of five authors. Because several formal choices change together, this is exploratory comparison rather than a controlled test of one literary device.

Palle's request for these drafts authorizes exploration while the three calibration examples remain provisional. It does not adopt those examples or a chapter. Those five candidates keep the accepted local “we” reference and factual limits. Their 600–1,500-word range concerns complete chapters, not every literary experiment.

## Discover a form before expanding it

The [returned studies](../iterations/2026-10-15-returned-literary-forms/README.md) expose a limit in that design: a shared obligation to introduce every next object can leave all five texts as walkthroughs. A performance, a fictional dispute with the rules, or a close study of one patch of ink can discover something that a complete itinerary suppresses.

During form discovery, allow coverage, selection, order, narrator and length to vary. Keep the apparatus facts accountable to the retained evidence. Mark invented situations as fiction and proposed apparatus changes as proposals. A short work has no minimum word quota and need not visit every object. Author labels name available operations, not a requirement to imitate a style or reproduce a standard structure.

Read the work before its intended-effect notes. Keep a concrete detail or consequence worth developing, including one that calls for changing the instrument. Separate an editorial recommendation from an independent response or Palle's adoption. Studies 3 and 4 are the current proposed contrast: conflict between intention and rule, and close observation of a material trace. Do not merge them merely because each contains a promising passage.

Only then develop a complete chapter, usually 600–1,500 words according to the gems that earn attention. Reconcile the selected encounter order with the room at that stage. A free-form study does not silently revoke the shared room/final order or turn omitted encounters into optional ones. The local “we” remains an available reference, not a compulsory narrator for every study.

## Read before explaining the plan

Read one complete candidate before looking at its strategy notes. Record reading order and previous exposure. Identify a concrete sentence or event worth retaining, where the sequence loses necessity, and what the account makes the reader want to investigate. A preference with no new curiosity is still a useful response. A full chapter can fail even when several paragraphs work.

Read a second candidate with the same questions. Do not require one person to finish five in a sitting. Keep complete-chapter responses separate from snippet responses, and supplied criticism separate from a witnessed visit. Then check technical claims against the retained apparatus. Author self-checks before handoff are not the independent reader's literary or technical verdict.

Let a reader propose new idea-bank entries or challenge existing ones. The portable reading pages can retain notes in that browser and export them as JSON. Exported notes remain unreviewed submissions until deliberately incorporated into the repository bank. Browser storage is not the durable research record; the versioned file and its source links are.

## Revise a whole account

Choose a composition to develop, or keep none. Retain useful ideas from the others with their IDs. Import a sentence only when it works in the new account's movement of attention. A collage of the five favourite paragraphs may destroy the transitions that made each interesting.

For a more controlled next comparison, preserve the selected whole chapter and change one consequential decision: the opening's attention to marks, the placement of the receipt, or how long the ending stays with release. Record the predicted gain and a possible loss, then read the complete revised sequence.

Room and final remain aligned where that is the map's rule. All five current candidates rehearse the selected five-stop route, conditional on resolving the documented MOVE / KEEP placement gap. None authorizes moving magnets, restoring a removed part or changing the canonical final. Either writing track can still initiate an apparatus experiment; the 48-value bench remains the first runtime pilot.

## Handoff with a declared purpose

For a fresh independent chapter, supply the updated factual dossier and selected calibration examples, then record any additional material requested. For critique or revision of these five texts, supply the texts and record that exposure. The idea bank and strategy notes are optional editorial material, not a required interpretation. Preserve both the supplied reading and the new writer's disagreement.
