# Forces: what receives the arrow?

4 October 2026. Thirteen revised teaching proposals, in the existing museum order. This pass changes summaries and their selection of learning. It preserves the full map texts, artifact code, placements and navigation.

## The line through the halls

Change introduced a finite rate, a finite sum and a follower that reads its next direction at its new address. Forces asks what happens when a calculation meets a particular receiver. A displacement, velocity, acceleration and force can use the same vector representation while requiring different operations and units.

The opening returns to positions, displacements and routes. It should not demand new drawing machinery to repeat a known relation. Normalization deserves a short explanation here: its earlier appearance in prose is not evidence that the student can use it independently.

Launch remains before Motion. Supply its constant-acceleration formula, angle conversion and flight duration; let the learner evaluate and vary the prediction. Motion then introduces a force pulse received by an engine-integrated body. Ending the command does not erase the body's velocity.

The central making passage is **Fields → Kinetic Forces → Oscillation**. Fields makes assignment and accumulation visible in two small updates. Kinetic Forces uses current velocity in a resistance force. Oscillation brings displacement into the force as well. Keep the runner and display supplied while the learner predicts a changed beginning and writes the selected calculation.

This is a shared teaching construction, not a claim that the current rooms load one portable learner script. Their actual implementations differ. Agree a reduced edit location in each existing host before expanding the lessons.

## Keep these distinctions

- A world displacement and a reduced drawing of it are different objects. Name the frame and any display clipping or scale.
- Summing displacements retains a destination. Summing lengths retains travelled distance. An instrument's instantaneous reset may sit outside its recorded trip.
- A dot product against a unit axis is a signed scalar. Multiplying that scalar by the axis gives a vector; subtraction gives the remainder. Flipping the axis reverses the scalar without reversing the retained projection vector.
- The launch marker evaluates a supplied position function. The Motion cube delegates integration and contact to Godot. The Fields markers explicitly update state and have no collisions or mass. These are different receivers, not successive claims that one simulator was built.
- Send a force in newtons to the rigid-body engine. In an explicit model, divide force by mass to obtain acceleration, then multiply acceleration by elapsed seconds to change velocity. Applying the same time factor again to an engine force command would change its meaning.
- A restoring force can vanish at the centre while velocity carries the body through it. Reaching equilibrium is different from resting there. A prescribed sine animation remains a useful comparison, not the implementation of this spring.
- The spring's DAMP control selects a ratio. The supplied adapter computes the coefficient as `c = 2 * ratio * sqrt(k * m)`. Hold mass and stiffness fixed when comparing damping; changing either also changes this coefficient.
- A local acceleration field can act on a cube and a visitor through separate adapters. A shared vector does not make their entry tests, damping, collision or exit behavior identical.

## Leave room for comparison

The torque diagram, nominal-work account, conveyor, support plate and creature controller can contribute to the inquiry without becoming five extra required builds. Keep their actual mechanisms visible. A static support test does not teach or validate a gait. A target selector and an event counter do not establish learning or intention. A deliberately reflective hall can ask for a prediction and an explanation without a compulsory script.

Gravity selects one reusable operation: distribute paired interaction contributions to declared recipients. Supply the attraction function and stepping support; do not add a high-accuracy orbit solver as an unexpected prerequisite. Equal opposing forces need not produce equal accelerations when masses differ.

The held-source and mutual-response stations also use different masses and initial states. Their paths cannot isolate the effect of releasing the source. Make that distinction at the two receiver contributions before interpreting the displayed orbits.

Choreography introduces period and phase at its rods, then uses the existing paired-dial edit to halve a rate difference. Oscillation's returning motion provides a connection, but does not establish that those terms have already been taught.
