# Noise 6 Wall — two primary works, three placements

`shader_noise_space:180#stand:panel` at (9,2) is the supported four-patch instrument. LAYERS, BASIS, FREEZE and DRESS control only receivers in this museum hall. The cased, tilted plate states the held/live phase, term counts, weights, gain and finish.

`shader_noise_space#stand:room` at (6,6) is the same primary work at bodily scale. Its five-metre floor bridges the existing central opening. A 4.8 m enclosure has a 2.8 m ceiling and opposing 1.6 m wide, 2.3 m high door openings. The original internal sphere is retained and now receives the material at its combined CSG root. Each wall piece uses its own UV chart. Legacy standalone enclosure geometry remains available in the unchanged scene resource.

`dark_sphere` at (10,10) is the second primary work, moved from the central opening to solid floor near the exit. Its original material, rotation and independent sine-driven pulse remain. FREEZE at the panel does not stop this orb.

Book anchors and primary order are shader_noise_space, dark_sphere. The two shader placements share a passage; the internal sphere is part of that scene, not an additional map token. Recovered structure and utilities are unchanged. See `/research/possible-bodies/noise-six-wall.html` for photographs and evidence.
