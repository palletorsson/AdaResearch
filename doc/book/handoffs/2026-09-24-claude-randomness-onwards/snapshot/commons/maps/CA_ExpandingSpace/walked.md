## The walk

The automaton leaves the plane. A tree accretes in three dimensions, branching where its rule permits — growth as computation's fossil record. The ca_chair_test makes the claim domestic: furniture grown by rule, sat on as proof. The crossway_ca runs an intersection with no traffic authority, lanes of cells negotiating by neighborhood alone. And the decaying_bridge is the turn's dark twin: the same local logic that builds can also un-build, plank by plank, as you cross.

## The turn

Up to here the automata performed on surfaces; now they claim volume, furniture, infrastructure — the *inhabited* world. The turn is the realization that emergence does not stay in the dish. Rules that build chairs and rot bridges are rules with consequences for bodies, and the chapter's cheerful "worlds emerge" acquires its first note of caution: you may be standing in one.
