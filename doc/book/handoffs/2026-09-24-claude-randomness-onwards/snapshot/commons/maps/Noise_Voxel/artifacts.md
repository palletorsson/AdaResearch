# Noise Voxel — four primary works

All four authored artifacts are retained and have passages in final.md. Their order is also recorded in artifact_roles.json and the noise book data.

## Perlin Terrain Sculptor

`perlin_terrain_sculptor` at (4,7), on a plinth. The 24³ lattice source offers THRESHOLD, CELL, SEED and view-only CUT. The cased readout identifies one field value, vertical bias, threshold, occupancy, component count and largest piece.

## Science Screen

`science_screen` at (9,4), with a floor stand and explicit voxel_slice mode. It reads the actual local source and displays the selected horizontal score layer beside its stored decisions. It does not invent a replacement field when the source is absent.

## Voxel Noise

`voxelnoise` at (9,9), in volume mode. A 5.2 m frame contains a 4.8 m sample domain. Its 24³ cells match the model at five times the physical spacing. Exposed cell faces provide both visible geometry and collision. Occupied connectivity does not establish player traversal.

## Dark Sphere

`dark_sphere` at (4,12). The original orb retains its independent rotation, sine-driven emission and halo. It lets the chapter compare binary occupancy with an earlier way to receive a varying scalar.

Recovered map structure and utilities are unchanged. Actual rendered images and the review are at `/research/possible-bodies/noise-voxel.html`; the original artifact page is preserved in the dated review snapshot.
