# A route is a proposal made to a body

The gold line reaches the far posts before the player does. It describes a continuous path across the sampled ground. The moving body adds width, collision, slope rules and time. When the walk stops, the line has not become false; its account has proved insufficient for this encounter.

The fixed field gives the comparison a useful restraint. Raising HEIGHT changes how strongly each value contributes to ground height. The destinations, generator and seed remain. A stalled crossing can then be investigated through a particular operation rather than attributed to noise as an undifferentiated adversary. The terrain resists without needing an enemy to make the resistance meaningful.

The margin is also an operation. A smooth envelope withholds the field's height contribution near the edge so that the patch joins a stable place to stand. This is an authored condition for access and comparison. The work would teach a different lesson if it concealed that intervention and called the entire landscape simply natural or spontaneous.

Route B takes a longer way and, in the measured sections, has a slightly steeper maximum segment too. A detour is not guaranteed to rescue a route. Its value might appear under another criterion: a different view, a place to wait, a chance to pass another body. The current instrument measures section length and sampled grade. It leaves those other values open to further construction and encounter.

The same crease may obstruct one body and shelter another. That is a research question we can make specific by introducing body dimensions and lines of sight later. It does not follow from calling irregularity free or calling a smooth surface orderly. A smooth ground can exclude; a rugged one can enforce a narrow passage. What matters is how a surface, a body and a task meet.

The profile offers a second view while keeping its omissions available. It samples a line through a mesh; the player occupies a volume. The mesh itself stores one height per horizontal position and cannot hold the roof and floor of a cave there. A new representation might support an existence that this one cannot describe. The limitation becomes a place to build from.

RESET returns a local configuration while the orb continues its pulse. The route can be attempted again, but the time spent choosing it has not been returned. This is the hall's contribution to the larger thread: learn enough of the operation to alter it, carry the untested questions forward, and keep the body inside the account of what the algorithm permits.
