# Critical register · Where the Variants Stand

An axis looks like a natural property once it has a slider. Here the slider is a publication: one list in code, another in the registry, and a third order on the floor. The gallery makes those lists stand next to the bodies they claim to describe.

The primary pair holds two kinds of remainder. `csg_difference_demo` makes disclosure gradual—outcome, trace, operands, expression—while `removal_room` checks its own vocabulary and still carries a mistaken explanation of one ratio. Around them, union's ladder is published out of order, intersection gives a wildcard a proper name, and the coincident-face artifact diagrams a failure without allowing the renderer to fail.

This is a small politics of infrastructure. A default is not neutral when it becomes the first thing a visitor sees. A registry is not merely metadata when it determines the path through a room. The gallery lets the reader discover that the museum's apparent certainty is assembled from competing descriptions, each with its own blind spot.
