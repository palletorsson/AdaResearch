# Book instructions and encounter status

Editorial agreement, 17 September 2026, arising from the Color reading review.

**The book may speculate about a future experiment, but when it tells the visitor to walk, press, pick up, compare or look for something, that encounter must exist in the build being documented.** Instructions presented as current must describe placed, reachable encounters with the required controls and behaviour. A registered artifact or a successful standalone test does not establish museum placement or bodily access.

Keep three questions separate:

| Question | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| What does the artifact implement? | Relevant source, configuration and runtime checks. |
| What can the visitor encounter here? | Placement in the current map, approach and exit, connected controls, and traversal checks with the tested input method. |
| What does the accepted text ask the encounter to make possible? | The action and observation that become the placement/design brief, with remaining work stated. |

## Working drafts and future experiments

Accepted wording may remain in a working `final.md` while its encounter is being prepared. Put this visible marker immediately after that artifact's anchor and before its passage:

> **PLANNED ENCOUNTER - approved text; museum placement and traversal pending.**

Keep the marker in reading copies and exports that include the passage. If an export is intended to contain only current encounters, omit the entire pending passage or move it into an explicitly labelled future-experiments section. Removing the marker alone would misrepresent its status. This is an editorial convention; no automatic export filtering is implemented by this document.

Preserve promising prose as a design brief. A missing placement need not force an account of absence into the literary passage. Record the intended configuration and the conditions that must be met before the marker can be removed. Preserve useful artifacts and strong passages while completing that work.

## Advancing an encounter

Record the map/configuration checked, date, evidence and remaining limitations. Confirm that the described objects are placed, the approach and onward route work, and any instructed controls are connected and usable with the tested input method. Then remove the pending marker for that encounter and update its status record.

Desktop or headless checks establish only what they test. They do not certify headset reach, comfort, perception or performance. An invitation to observe an effect can stay open; a guaranteed perceptual result needs appropriate evidence. Clearly distinguish a proposed experiment from one available to the visitor.

## First application: Color

All four revisions in the [Color review](review-packets/color-focused-revisions-2026-09-17.html) are accepted. `Color_Walls` stays one hall; its transition is applied. The Life, filter and drum passages in `Color_Context_Placed` carry the planned-encounter marker. Their [placement brief](review-packets/color-placement-brief-2026-09-17.md) states what must be made available. The accepted wording and original source snapshots remain in the review's JSON record.


Later direction, 17 September: the opening Color hall is now a column gallery combining primitives, static transformation and colour. Its three formerly pending passages were archived for later reuse and are no longer proposed additions to this hall. The visible-status convention remains the rule wherever a working draft contains an unplaced encounter.
