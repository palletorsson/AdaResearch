# The folding future

Author-adopted preface theme · 5 October 2026

**CORE:** Anticipated displacement. Future student changes what we teach now; future teacher changes the author's relation to knowledge and labour. Keep these two ghosts distinct. Expected change already affects practice; its eventual outcome remains unknown.

**WHERE:** [Preface](../preface.md), between its invitation to readers and its account of learning through encounters. QFEP receives the question of which capacities to preserve as the pedagogical relationship changes.

**CLAIM TYPE:** Situated interpretation of the author's practice. “Folding future” is the author's phrase. “Anticipatory hauntology” describes this emphasis, not an attributed Derrida coinage, a claim to a new field, or a forecast that teaching will vanish.

**WHY:** The author began from university courses in programming for artists: learn operations, then build visual work. During Ada's construction, more of their own production happened through instructions to AI. A result could arrive before they understood its construction; inspection, judgment and revision remained necessary. If a student can begin through that route too, the original order between acquiring coding skills and making work becomes uncertain. The ghost concerns a changing pedagogical relationship, not merely a student who has yet to turn up. See the [author's account](../iterations/2026-10-05-preface-folding-future/AUTHOR-CONTEXT.md).

## What each ghost does

- **Ghost student:** an imagined recipient guides sequence, prerequisites, explanation and what must carry between lessons. Their code fixture makes some assumptions executable. A passing fixture is not a person learning.
- **Ghost teacher:** the author anticipates a changed relation to their own knowledge, teaching and labour while still doing that work. This does not simply mean an AI acting as a teacher.

Record a date, concrete decision, inspected artifact and uncertainty when adding further cases. Do not infer that a future event caused an earlier edit: it is the present expectation of that event that is being investigated.

**QFEP QUESTION:** What capacities should this world preserve when the relationship it was built to support is itself changing? Keep this a research question; no quantitative model or inevitable technological destination has been established.

**RETURN TO PEOPLE:** The imagined student must remain revisable by someone who hesitates, disagrees or wants another kind of work. Useful automated checks should make room for that encounter, not stand in for it.
