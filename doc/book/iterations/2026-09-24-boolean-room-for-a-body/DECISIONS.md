## Why this sequence

The 23 September assessment identified this as the clearest example of a visitor's chapter becoming a source audit. A fresh complete reading of the three manuscripts and their intent, critical, tutorial and technical companions confirms that judgement locally. This is not a new ranking from rereading every remaining sequence. Randomness, Noise and Cellular Automata remain in Claude's separate assignment; existing Soft Bodies and ML pilots are protected.

The revision preserves it while reducing file counts, line-number arguments, catalogue bookkeeping and universal claims about what no other artifact does. Complete originals remain in before/. They are dated material, not newly recertified findings.

## Decisions

- **Verbs:** keep membership, disconnected union, sampled versus faceted measurement, the generated cavity surface and reversed subtraction. The bench makes order and composition legible without counting operator spellings. Social names invite inquiry; they are not moral properties inherent in mathematical operators.
- **Gallery:** keep all six families and the actual default-first order. Walk the alternatives, including a route through union that differs from placement order. Disclosure, a named centre, amounts of removal and gone/ghost/scar carry the critique of how a museum constructs an apparent norm. The stable stripe diagram remains distinct from a live depth contest.
- **Works:** keep all nine artifact anchors. The unresponsive workbench, porous visual wall, raised cavern and rack with disabled normal pickup remain unfinished or failed affordances. They are not all repaired to make the manuscript reassuring. The existing inhabitable burrow carries a constructive counterexample: changing a cutter profile accommodates one body's movement. Its boundaries can also offer seclusion; another body can need a different interior.

No new artifact was necessary to establish this reading: prior work already supplied the positive encounter in the burrow. Existing possibilities are now noticed more fully. Rack handling and workbench composition remain explicit future work, not silently implemented capabilities.

## Factual repairs that mattered

- The first hall repeats box and sphere with different sizes/offsets/colours. Its older tutorial's claim that only the verb varies was corrected.
- A boundary representation can describe an interior region without storing interior matter. It does not forbid an inside, continuous change, or reversed operands.
- Older subtraction-suite prose inferred that ordinary back-face culling removes the carved interior. That does not follow from the setting alone: inward-facing cavity walls can face a viewer in the hollow. The revised text distinguishes geometric and rendering choices without claiming an unobserved disappearance.
- The coincidence diagram is not proof that Boolean algebra has no answer, and its stripes are not a live z-fighting measurement.
- Works' companion still said 17 by 36, eight tokens and two primaries. It now reflects the existing 17 by 44 map, nine families and three primaries including the burrow.
- Fallback behavior, historical counts, duplicated constants and old pixel metrics remain recoverable in originals. They are not promoted as freshly quantified evidence.
