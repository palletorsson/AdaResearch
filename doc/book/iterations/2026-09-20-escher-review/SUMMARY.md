## Findings and decisions

- **Strengthen the staircase before rewriting.** `climb_step()` already advanced a marker but had no visitor controls. Add opt-in physical STEP, BACK, FORM and RESET buttons, a stationary smaller model, and a compact side readout. Keep the original five seam constructions. A reparenting check caught and repaired a marker lookup that otherwise stayed at its first tread. Rebuilding forms preserves the controls and external map nodes.
- **Measure what is there.** RETURN SIDE descends; CLOSING DROP has fifteen rising transitions followed by a return. OPEN RUN and SCAFFOLD stop at the last existing tread. TILTED TREADS has level centres with individual inclined surfaces. The readout measures centres, not travel along those surfaces. There are no walkable tread colliders, and the controls never move the player or camera.
- **Repair the triangle's projection.** A z-offset alone did not align its separated ends to the viewing bead. The back endpoint now lies on the ray through the front endpoint from the marked eye. Optional active-camera tracking updates the detector. Exploded and sealed forms cannot falsely announce this broken-joint alignment. The geometry remains a three-beam perspective study, not the classic three-right-angle impossible tribar.
- **Preserve the supporting artifacts.** The pipe stays near the first experiment; the trident remains a comparison. Correct the old reversed description: two round rods at the base, three flat tines above. A count changing from two to three is not itself a contradiction; the chapter asks the reader to follow the connecting edges.
- **Revise the claim, not the desire.** Keep the opening desire to climb the impossible staircase, then let the reader inspect how this finite construction handles it. Remove the false equivalence with Russell/Godel and the assertion that no local measurement could disclose the descent. The exit now asks what counts as making something, preparing Brouwer.
