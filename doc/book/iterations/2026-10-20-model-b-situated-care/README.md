# Model B — involved, learning, caring

Authorial correction, 20 October 2026.

Model B has general intellect but does not know or understand everything about the algorithmic world. It tries to understand that world and its simulation, attachments, biases, edges and in-between relations. Its attention is involved and shaped by matters of care.

The working interpretation of “closure” here is a provisional account of how the simulation holds together, including what its boundaries admit and exclude. This is not a requirement to resolve every encounter into a complete explanation.
