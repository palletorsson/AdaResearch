## An improvement principle to test

Start with an action and change one consequential condition. Remove supports that conceal that difference. Let a geometric relationship alter what a hand or walking body can do. Give the consequence room to be encountered before introducing the next one. Provide pieces with which the visitor can test a different construction.

This is a working method, not a promise that every map needs the same spatial formula. Here it produces different forms: a pass-through comparison, an open connection field, a constrained route, an assembly surface, a completion that falls, and a slope that supports a body.

The triangle pieces remain where released: this is an assembly model, not a test of structural stability. Completed tetrahedra and wedges use gravity and convex collision. Queue barriers constrain ordinary walking; teleportation and jumping are separate movement capabilities to evaluate in the headset.
