# Noise — let the encounter carry the explanation

19 September 2026. **The four proposals below have now been applied to `final.md`, following the author's continuation.** The `before/` and `proposed/` files retain the original comparison. See [application record](applied.json). This follows the author's request to keep the book poetic and accessible, and our agreement to lead with a small number of consequential passage comparisons.

The book needs enough code for a reader to recognise an operation and imagine changing it. A line that keeps writing zero into depth belongs in the encounter. A string of generator settings can often wait until a note. The distinction depends on what the reader can discover with the detail, not on whether the word or number is technical.

I read all nine current chapters and used the online review's reader-experience findings to identify four interventions. Five chapters remain outside this proposal. No artifact or room is redesigned to justify an editorial preference.
