## Findings and decisions

The existing chapter opened with a door you could walk through. Its artifact was a 0.66 by 0.92 m illustration, and Florensky was absent from the authored museum plan. The map put its four works around void cells and a central mound that becomes a blocking wall in the museum. This was an encounter-development problem, not a reason to remove the passage.

- **Keep all four families.** `both_true_gate` and `florensky_sphere` remain primary. `schrodinger_box` and `superposition_display` remain secondary comparisons. No existing map artifact is removed.
- **Give the promise a working encounter.** The gate's opt-in `report_controls` study has a 1.716 m wide, 2.392 m high opening, two translucent leaves and three reachable push buttons. OPEN and CLOSED toggle independently; CLEAR removes both. The unrelated Q line remains NO SUPPORT. That represents absence of support, not a proof of not-Q. This finite report store is not a complete paraconsistent calculus.
- **Separate reports from bodily permission.** Only the frame has collision. A visitor can pass while a closed report remains visible. The final chapter asks who has made that permission possible. It does not claim that two meshes constitute a physical contradiction or that a calm room proves non-explosion.
- **Let attention persist.** In this hall the sphere uses `hold_both:on`. Its colour changes but observation, cycling and state setters cannot collapse the two readings. Its label spells out A AND not-A because the baked font did not render the conjunction symbol. Existing seam geometries and legacy default behavior elsewhere remain.
- **Keep resemblance available for investigation.** The box selects an ordinary random outcome on desktop click and resets. The display cycles opacity; it has no measurement operation. Captions, present-tense source identities and registry descriptions now state those limits. The book no longer calls either object a contradiction with an expiry or claims Florensky supplied one established formal logic.
- **Restore the hall to the route.** A level 13 by 16 map places gate, sphere and rear comparison pair in order. Both museum plans and the authored sequence contain all eight declared maps. Roles, core encounter hashes, hand-authored book lines, both trunks and derived museum/necklace indexes agree. Other seven map files and manuscripts are preserved.

The first runtime inspection found console text clipping into its casing when billboarding and an automatically dealt `dream_bodies` statue obstructing the exit approach. Text is now fixed to the board; this hall excludes that automatic sculpture. This exclusion does not remove any of the four authored artifacts or change other halls.
