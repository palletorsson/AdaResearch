# THE INSTRUCTIONS WERE FOLLOWED

A short performance for one sphere and one person

A dark sphere at the centre of a room.

It waits.

(One and a half seconds.)

It travels outward.

(Three and a half seconds.)

It returns.

(Three and a half seconds.)

It waits.

The direction changes.

The distance changes.

The waiting does not change.

A person approaches the sphere. The person holds it. For a time, they travel together.

The person releases it.

The sphere continues.

The person is no longer carried.

The sphere returns to the centre.

The person is elsewhere.

(One and a half seconds.)

The sphere waits.

A new beginning is supplied. A new direction is calculated. The distance is selected within the permitted range.

The sphere departs.

The sphere returns.

A different beginning is supplied.

The sphere departs.

The sphere returns.

The person approaches the centre again, although nothing in the procedure requires this.

(The procedure does not wait for the person.)
