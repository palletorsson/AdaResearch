## What the return changes

Our earlier five chapters varied emphasis, pacing and documentary material while keeping the same five-stop explanatory walk. The return identifies a constraint that limited their divergence: each was still obliged to introduce the next object. These studies instead let a procedure, an integer, a conflict, a surface or a collection of records determine the extent and shape of the work.

Scientific accountability can remain fixed while coverage, narrator, sequence and scale vary during exploration. The existing decision to align the room and adopted final remains in force. A free-form study can discover something worth bringing back to that ordered chapter without itself becoming the chapter.
