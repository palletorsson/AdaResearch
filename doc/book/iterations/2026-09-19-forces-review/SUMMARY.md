## Missing encounter versus incomplete catalog

The authored maps contain 178 placements, including 29 primary instances representing 27 book encounters, supporting objects, decoration and idea markers. All resolve to an existing scene, directly or through one-hop registry delegation. Three placed force cubes share one book encounter. The already present pendulum model is now explicitly secondary.

Nine placed secondary works had scene files but lacked `map_ready`, `lookup_name` and `include_in_map_data`. The museum's actual liveness gate uses **map_ready**, not the presence of lookup_name: it reads the dictionary key and requires a ready scene. Restoring all three catalog fields makes their intended placement available. This is a repair of omitted works, not the addition of nine new objects to the map.

The tenth omission is different. Arena's `momentum_collision` explicitly has map_ready=false and include_in_map_data=false. Its ten-metre automatic collision rail merits a separate spatial and physics check. Preserve that decision rather than treating every absence as permission to enable a scene. The primary collision_carts comparison remains.

The normalization, torque, ring, gait and field descriptions now name implemented operations. The older Forces overview is retained verbatim in dated curation history. A source/header cleanup does not certify all of an artifact's variants or establish a learner's experience.
