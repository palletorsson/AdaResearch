cycle, editable thirteen-joint body, pin-jointed ragdoll, and Chair/Table/Lamp

shortcuts, which conflict with the Void player controls. The automatic cycle,

lower hemisphere is visible above the slab. It retains 0.45 m cells and extent 6.
