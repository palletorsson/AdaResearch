# Wavefunctions — what returns

Forces gave the point a state that changes. Wavefunctions asks which differences a returning movement keeps: direction, timing, phase, neighbours and the receiver. Reuse the point and earlier constructions; do not make twelve unrelated instruments.

Status: the opening lesson is rewritten. The following eleven rows are the next teaching pass, not completed or newly validated tutorials. The museum order and its artifacts are retained. Form Finding remains a separate, paused rethink.
