A drawing
leaves the plane.

A face made from separate strokes. Its eyes and nose sit in front of its outline. The same marks can become soft colour, transparent spheres or a thin line of light.

One drawing, three appearances

This irregular portrait was replayed through the ordinary mouse input handler: eight pen-down strokes, with pen lifts between them. Its 584 marks occupy four depth layers spanning 0.9 metres. It is a repeatable drawing test; the Mona Lisa remains a future experiment.

Transparent spheres — native Godot capture.

The light points retain the clearest outline. Spheres give each stroke a thicker body; their overlaps brighten. Soft colour blurs the edges. Moving around the drawing reveals the depth we deliberately gave it.

Actual mouse strokes

Three pink drags, then one cyan drag closer to the viewer. This was entered through the desktop window, independently of the portrait replay.

Pen lifts keep strokes separate. Changing materials retains their positions. The wheel moves the next stroke closer or farther away; it can also change depth while drawing.

One problem fixed

Switching away from the window during a stroke could leave the brush drawing. It now ends the stroke when the window loses focus. All 32 targeted checks pass, including separate strokes, depth, material changes, undo, save/load and the focus fix.

Depth still asks for a deliberate gesture with the wheel or a change of viewpoint. This is a working desktop brush. Tracked-hand use in VR remains to be tested.

Test evidence and limits

Findings and controls · 32 passing checks · The two failures before repair · Desktop input session · Code change

Holding still deposits extra marks. At 900 marks the oldest are recycled. These tests support small drawings, not an unlimited painting system. Test saves are separate from the user's F5 save.

Try a drawing

Open the project in Godot 4.6 and run. Press 5, then C for an empty canvas. Press [ two or three times for a narrower brush.

Left-drag draws; releasing lifts the pen. Wheel changes depth, X colour and B material. Shift + left-drag lets you orbit. Z undoes. F5 / F9 save and load locally.
