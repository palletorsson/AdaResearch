# A family the cube can become

Restores the deferred LSystems_Shape_Grammars hall after LSystems_Living and before Assemblage_Same_Desire. Six static specimens are built at runtime by the existing mesh grammar: cube, top-face inset, one inset/extrusion round, three rounds, a changed inset amount, and three rounds on all twelve initial faces. Exact meshes and plinths have collision. A 21×24 m level hall provides routes around both rows. No animated growth, selection interface or physics settling is claimed.

Colour introduces saved compositions; Form Finding tests arrangement and success; Living exposes parameter-to-grammar conversion; this hall opens the face-operation vocabulary; Genetic Evolution later exposes scored selection.
