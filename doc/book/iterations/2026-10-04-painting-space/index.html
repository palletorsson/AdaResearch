A short memory

Moving points leave tails that shrink and darken. The paths lie on a sphere, but no complete surface is ever supplied. Space pauses their motion.

Duration becomes part of the body we perceive.

Construction, evidence and limits

32 native checks passed, including actual mouse-event drawing, ray hits and misses, painting across the longitude seam and pole, recycling old brush marks, pause and glow. The native renderer reported no errors.

The shells use additive colour and a view-dependent edge shader; they do not simulate filled fog or physical glass. The free-space brush holds at most 900 stamps and replaces the oldest after that. The surface painting is stored in a texture. Both drawings last for the current run only.

The live camera flies freely without collision. This is a separate desktop study; it has not yet been placed in a museum hall or tested with tracked hands on Quest.
