Boolean surfaces · companion study

Three eggs, three ways to leave a body.

A Boolean operation is a decision about relation. Join two volumes; keep only where they meet; or let one form cut an inside into another. The egg gives these operations a body we can read at a glance.

Where this sits

This is an original counterpoint to Anish Kapoor's inquiries into void and surface, not a copy of a particular work. In his account of Descent into Limbo, the floor opening reads like a black carpet; writing on the work also describes how a convex wall form can be paired with a concavity. This study borrows those questions of depth and measure, then turns toward Boolean operations. Kapoor in conversation about Descent into Limbo · Camile van Winkle on Kapoor and the sublime.
