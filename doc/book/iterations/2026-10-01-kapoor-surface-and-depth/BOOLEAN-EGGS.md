# Boolean eggs: a deep-colour counterpoint

The egg is a soft body for a hard operation. Three close forms let the Boolean operations register as relations: union joins two volumes, intersection keeps the part they share, and subtraction opens a cavity. Plum, indigo, and teal move the study away from the chalk-white display register. A faint ring of light gives the group a little distance and wonder without making it celestial decoration.

This is an original study in conversation with Anish Kapoor's treatment of void, surface, and the body's uncertain measure. It does not reconstruct a Kapoor work. The dark opening is modeled geometry with an ordinary Godot material, not Vantablack or Kapoor's pigment.

The companion scene has now been rendered in native Godot 4.6 from three viewpoints, without engine errors. It is now installed as `boolean_eggs` in Boolean_Works, after the cavern and before the final comparisons. [See the actual museum capture](../2026-10-01-boolean-eggs-in-the-spine/index.html).
