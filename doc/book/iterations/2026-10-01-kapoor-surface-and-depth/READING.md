# The surface that says depth

## Central work

Anish Kapoor, *Descent into Limbo* (1992), as a question about a real cavity that can be read from above as a dark, almost flat surface. The image does not disclose enough for the eye to settle the body's question: does the floor continue?

## What existing Ada artifacts can carry

- `boolean_colour_chamber` makes traversable passages from CSG subtraction, intersection and union, with collision and a level floor. It teaches an actual void and a rendered surface, but does not establish an optical-depth illusion.
- `facade_room_cast` derives a room and its corresponding positive from one volume. It makes the absence measurable, a useful contrast to Kapoor's uncertain reading.
- `super_drag_mirror` reflects a shared world through a planar camera. It is a live reflection, not a hole or a curved/deep mirror.

## Smallest next study

Hold circle, visible dark value, light and viewpoint fixed. Compare a recessed CSG volume with a dark disc over an intact floor. Inspect from the approach, after a lateral move and from a protected cross-section. Record parallax, edge occlusion and ray hits. Keep the visitor route supported; the purpose is to compare image and geometry, not to invite a step onto an uncertain surface.

The HTML schematic is explanatory, not a Godot capture. A separate Boolean egg component and a camera/light preview scene are now authored in Godot source; they have not been rendered here. The egg component is an original study, not a reconstruction of Kapoor's work and does not assert artistic equivalence.

## Capability placement

Use this first as a looking encounter late in Boolean Surfaces, after actual CSG boundaries and collision are familiar. A later making return in Shaders can change the surface image while leaving geometry fixed. This needs no new foundational ladder rung; its making operation crosses existing Boolean and rendering capacities. It remains a proposal and is not installed in either sequence. The egg scene is a standalone companion component, also not installed in the spine.

## Follow-up: Boolean eggs

The authored chapter companion now has a deep-colour Boolean egg counterpoint: plum union, indigo intersection, and a teal subtraction with a dark concavity and restrained halo. It is an original study after Kapoor's concerns with void and surface, not a reconstruction. The dark material is ordinary Godot shading, not Vantablack or Kapoor's pigment. The component is authored but not runtime-tested or installed in the museum spine. See [the Boolean egg reading page](boolean-eggs.html) and [study note](BOOLEAN-EGGS.md); the component scene is `res://commons/artifacts/kapoor_void_studies/boolean_eggs.tscn`.
