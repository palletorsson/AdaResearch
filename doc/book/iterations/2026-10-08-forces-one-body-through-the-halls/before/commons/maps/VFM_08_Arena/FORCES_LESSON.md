# One push reaches another body

Artifact and lesson contract, 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts.

Tutorial anchor: `force_play_bowling`.

Roll the known sphere into ten real pins along a long banked lane. The impulse initiates motion; contact distributes it. Compare a free and a braced launcher receiving equal opposite impulses. Keep the target bay as a separate question about what is selected. Reconstruct the sphere impulse and observe the pin contacts; pins need not become ten separate tutorials.

Carry onward: An action can redistribute motion beyond its intended recipient.

Independent check: Predict the signs of the two velocities before release. Compare braced and free without calling both systems isolated.

## Selected comparisons

Compare two presentations: Momentum Cradle shows ideal input/output poses; Newton's Cradle integrates pendulum angles with an idealized equal-mass collision exchange. Neither is the Jolt collision solver used by bowling.

The oversized impact club sits beside the bowling ball: a held head transfers motion through contact. The hanging seven-kilogram punching bag beside recoil receives hand, tool and loose-body impacts, then returns under gravity while its joint holds. These add ways to explore impulse and constrained motion; the original bowling sphere remains the tutorial carrier. The hand mitts are kinematic collision proxies, not measurements of muscular force.
