# The push ends. Motion stays.

Artifact and lesson contract, 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts.

Tutorial anchor: `force_play_mass`.

Keep the familiar PointBody, with matched visible radius, height and collision shape. Apply the same one-newton force for one second to 1, 2 and 4 kg spheres; read acceleration and the retained velocity after the push. The loaded trolley and push-box puzzle let the same relation become a task. Reconstruct only the equal-force comparison.

Carry onward: A dynamic point retains velocity after an input ends.

Independent check: Predict the velocity ordering for another mass. Verify that ending the pulse does not command a stop.

## Selected comparisons

The mower turns a 20 × 28 m lawn into a flower trail. Push its held pink handle forward; lateral hand displacement steers with yaw drag. A 14 kg rigid body responds to a bounded model force and rolling resistance. The handle reads model force, drag, distance and signed work; it does not measure muscle force.
