# The source can move too

Artifact and lesson contract, 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts.

Tutorial anchor: `force_play_mutual`.

Let five PointBody spheres attract one another with paired forces, softened at close range. Initial tangential velocity and disabled contact keep this an orbital model; entering adds the visitor as a sixth model mass. Compare a selective, three-second magnet and a hand-held gravity point with ordinary gravity: remove a bottom brick, cross a release threshold, and watch existing support cease to hold. Reconstruct the pairwise relation; the other experiments expose different boundary conditions.

Carry onward: A source can also be a receiver; selecting bodies is part of a force system.

Independent check: Change one initial velocity, preserve the others, and compare paths without claiming every difference proves chaos.

A small stacking table beside the tall tower is a comparison: rearrange nine loose cubes, bricks, cylinders and the familiar point by hand. Change the order and orientation; contact and gravity decide whether it stands. The small stack has its own reset. It does not remotely rearrange the large tower or introduce another required tutorial.
