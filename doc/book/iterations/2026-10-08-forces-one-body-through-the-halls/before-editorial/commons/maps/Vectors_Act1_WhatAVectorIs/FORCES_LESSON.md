# The relation between two points

Primary: `force_point_direction`.

Keep the point class from Lines. This small study returns to two endpoints before adding a force. First read the displacement and its length. Then read direction. Finally inspect the X projection and the remainder in `relation()`; together they reconstruct the same displacement.

Carry: A displacement can begin elsewhere. In Launch, a vector will acquire units of metres per second and become an initial velocity.

Check: Place the endpoints three metres apart along X and four along Y. Predict a five-metre gap. Translate both together; the result should stay unchanged. Then bring them together: the cylinder disappears and direction becomes zero.
