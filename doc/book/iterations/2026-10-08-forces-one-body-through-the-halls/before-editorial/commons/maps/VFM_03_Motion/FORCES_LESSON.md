# When the push ends

Primary: `force_play_mass`.

Use three instances of the same PointBody. They differ in mass, not visible size. The new free_point.gd subclass allows physical release and removes inherited gravity and damping for this controlled comparison. A single button applies one newton for one second.

Carry: A force can end while its consequence continues. The field will choose the next acceleration from the point’s current address.

Check: Change the third mass to 8 kg. The same pulse should add 0.125 m/s. Predict the result, then compare it with the readout. Keep your hands off during this measurement; a grab or collision is another influence.
