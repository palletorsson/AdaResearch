# A force toward home

Artifact and lesson contract, 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts.

Tutorial anchor: `force_play_springs`.

Return the point to a displaced rest length with a spring force; its position evolves through the physics engine. Pull one of seven hanging points and follow the response through neighbour springs. Compare three connected swing pairs with increasing damping, then basket, chain, plank and arm constraints. Reconstruct one spring first; coupling is one return of the same relation. Hinge grip proxies transmit force while preserving the joint.

Carry onward: Restoration and resistance can act together; a periodic appearance can have a dynamic cause.

Independent check: Choose a new displacement and damping. Predict whether the body crosses equilibrium, then test the release.
