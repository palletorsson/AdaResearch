# Color_Grid_Pallet

**Desire.** Leave a colour somewhere, move away, and find it still waiting.

**Route / writing move.** Protect that small successful return before RULE overwrites the exception. The later discrepancy between the console's selectable region and the program's wider reach can then arise from wanting to colour a particular unreachable address.

**Encounter to lean on.** `gridcolorizer` states “Your exception has not followed it.” The moving white outline and fixed written value make address, selection and content visibly distinct.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the drawing-buffer assignment beside the first return, then the modulo rule when the exception is replaced. Keep geometry and support unchanged in this colour experiment. An edit's lack of reload persistence belongs to the record, not to the visitor's memory of choosing.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Pillar offered positions to sample and swatches to carry. Here the visitor can make one persistent local exception. Paint will broaden that act into a shared surface that continues changing while attention moves elsewhere.
