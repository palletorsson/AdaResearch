From minimum to maximum

The vertical audio fader previously used a horizontal driver and an unsupported axis setting. Its track now supplies the rotation. The horizontal audio fader also had an extra offset that put its travel to one side of the slot.

Keep the differences

Smooth, automatic/manual, stepped, and return-to-centre controls stay distinct. The automatic slider yields while you operate it, then resumes. Small pointer movements accumulate across a detent. A second pointer cannot take over a held control.

Turn the knob, keep the plane

The knobs rotate in their face plane and their pickup regions sit on the visible grips. The same local-coordinate approach works when the whole console is inclined or turned in the hall.
