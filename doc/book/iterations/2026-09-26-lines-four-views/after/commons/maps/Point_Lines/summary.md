# Point Lines — from connection to arrangement

Make a segment and open a barrier. Measure between two endpoints. Walk a straight black marking while a trace keeps part of your movement. The room then opens into four sightlines: plus and X; parallels; vertical beams and horizon; grid and depth. Pick up the lines and leave another arrangement. A final floor instruction reaches a solid wall and continues upwards.

The same segment can receive a stick, plank, luminous, graduated or beam-shaped body. The visible body and the pickup envelope are separate choices. This encounter extends the point/sphere distinction from Point One and returns to Trace with a detour worth recording.

The map is now 27 × 37. The original entry and Walk this line basin stay at their source positions. A rear open gallery replaces the partitions; selected earlier works remain on its sides and the four-monitor workshop remains near the exit. The before-layout and parked duplicate placements are preserved in `doc/book/iterations/2026-09-26-lines-four-views/`.
