Primitives: what carries onward

6 October 2026. Current editorial notes for the fifteen-hall sequence. These guide the next writing pass; they do not add requirements to museum runtime or record a learner's achievement.

The opening question is: how does this running world become available for construction? The route develops execution, a point, a relation, a retained record, another representation, connected faces, generated positions, finite construction and an arrangement. The student gains ways to act without being told that those operations decide what should be made.

What we take home

  • Carry recognisable material forward. Distinguish a reused class, an independent instance and a saved record; do not promise that one runtime node persists through every hall.
  • Select the lesson from the encounter and its place in the progression. A room's other artifacts need not become exercises or paragraphs.
  • Name the comparison: what changes, what remains fixed, and what consequence the student should be able to predict. Those choices belong to an experiment, not permanently to a hall.
  • Keep the two texts distinct. The tutorial explains and prints the runnable reconstruction. The reflection develops the bodily, critical and poetic stakes.
  • Distinguish supplied support, explanation, practice and independent understanding. A passed program is evidence about behaviour; a student's explanation and changed construction supply different evidence.
  • Let a closing hall use existing capacity. Melencolia asks for composition and judgement; Transformation can then give familiar constructions motion.

Compact instruction for the next writer

BRING → CHANGE → KEEP → LEARN → TEST → CARRY

BRING names the inherited object, data, skill and working museum host. CHANGE names one chosen operation. KEEP names the specific things held fixed during that comparison. LEARN selects what needs explanation now. TEST names a predicted result and an assertion or observation able to contradict it. CARRY names what the next lesson receives and how. Link the full source and evidence behind the brief; compression must not conceal a dependency.

For reflection-led passages, TEST can be an observable question or comparison. It is not an assertion that the reader must feel a prescribed emotion. This is an editorial guide, not six compulsory headings in every text.

Learning status is separate from code status

Use these terms per concept, not as a single grade for a hall:

Term What would justify it
Supplied The dependency, role and interface are declared; reconstructing its internals is outside this lesson.
Introduced The lesson explains the operation in the current construction and connects action with result.
Practised The lesson provides a meaningful variation or prediction using that operation. This describes the exercise offered, not a completed student attempt.
Independently demonstrated A learner produces a new working use and explains the relevant choices without being given that result. Record the work and observation; do not infer it from an exercise prompt.

For example, Godot Primitives introduces resolution comparisons and offers practice; its held-time controller is supplied support. Grid practises operations learned in Trace while introducing a new representation. Melencolia assigns independent composition. No observed human attempt has established independent mastery in this revision.

The current native evidence checks exact printed programs, source ownership, saved-data transfer, variants and deliberate faults. It does not grade prose, aesthetic judgement, headset comfort or human understanding. See the bounded verification record.

The boundary with Transformation

Bring PointBody and CubeBody recipes, independent instances, local/world frames and the distinction between a node's placement and its construction data. Point Zero supplies an earlier encounter with repeated processing. These are reusable foundations, not permission to assume every student has mastered them.

Transformation should make changes through time legible using these familiar bodies. Name the moving property, the frame and the timing rule. A small supplied sine expression may support periodic motion before its later derivation. Do not turn that early use into a claim that oscillation has been taught in full.

Before expanding the next hall, check its existing artifact, current paragraph plan, printed dependencies and proposed transfer exercise. Keep the order open to evidence; do not force every later sequence to repeat Primitives' fifteen-step structure.
