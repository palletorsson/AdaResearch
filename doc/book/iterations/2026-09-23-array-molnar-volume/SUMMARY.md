## What the object does

Four nested transparent/edge cubes at each of 64 indexed sites. Three compact controls independently vary translation, rotation and uniform scale. Distinct per-frame departures are assigned once using stable seeds. Returning to the same settings restores the same arrangement. Fixed grey markers retain the sites. DEPTH exposes one to four layers; RESET returns the three transformations to order. The visitor can walk around the plinth.

The controls do not individually edit cells. They vary the extent of different stored decisions across the array. The 64 sites and 256 frames are allocated once: DEPTH changes visibility, not allocated memory. This distinction matters to the user's question about computational limits.

The first capture revealed dark edges were illegible in the actual hall; the final frames are pale. A supplied physical walk found a floor drop beside the exhibit. The new 6×6 m, 3 cm thick exhibition floor provides a continuous circuit without changing the source floor grid. The control stem was moved behind its panel after visual inspection.
