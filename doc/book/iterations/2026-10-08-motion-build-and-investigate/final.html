When the push ends

Three black spheres wait above their lanes. They are the same size. Press PUSH 1 s, then leave the controls alone.

The command lasts one second. The points keep going.

There is time to look back at the button. It has finished its small contribution. Something of that contribution is still crossing the room.

The spheres separate. One kilogram, two, four: the labels offer a difference their silhouettes could not tell us. Each has received the same push for the same time. The heaviest acquires the least speed. Equal treatment has produced different movements here; mass is the particular difference this experiment lets us inspect.

The bodies have been kept at one height and along separate lanes. Gravity and linear damping are absent from this comparison. Their easy continuation has been arranged, just as the neighbouring trolley needs an arrangement in which it can be moved. The small study gives us time to notice something that other influences could quickly conceal.

Wait until the pulse has ended, then press again. The new push meets the movement already there. It does not begin a second journey from rest. The latest instruction does not contain the whole movement.

In Launch, the marker occupied a position calculated from its beginning and a clock. These points keep a current velocity. There is no complete curve waiting for them to follow. Catching one, releasing it, or letting it meet another object gives the next event a different beginning.

What the drawing keeps

A coloured trace follows each point. It can show you where to look again, but it keeps only a recent part of the journey. After enough movement, the earliest part leaves the drawing. A contribution to the present velocity can outlast the mark of where it began.

The run has room for a recent history. It has not kept everything that made the present.

We met a trace earlier as something we could carry into another hall. Here the record is continually being shortened. Both are made from positions. What can be asked of them depends on what has been kept.

Nor can a path, however long, tell us every influence that produced it. The trace does not separately record the button, your hand and each contact. You watched the push happen. Someone arriving now sees its consequence.

RESET puts the points back and clears their motion and trails. The apparatus can offer a fresh trial. You still remember the earlier one.

Move your hand toward the control again. One point is farther along its lane than the others. The button waits for the same action; the bodies no longer wait in the same state.
