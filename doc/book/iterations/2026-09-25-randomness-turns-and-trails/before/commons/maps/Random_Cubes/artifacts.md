# Random Cubes — Artifacts
*Randomness: Freedom from Pattern · E_entropy · 5 artifacts*

> An arena of randomized edges — cubes with profiles shaped by chance. The grid imposes order; randomness fractures it. Walk among forms that could have been otherwise, each edge a frozen coin flip.

The map, read through what it holds — its artifacts in the order you meet them:

## Dice Throw
![Dice Throw](/scene-catalog/dice_throw.png)

A VR dice on a felt table. Grab and throw the dice — roll a 6 and 100 balls rain from the sky, roll a 1 and just 1 ball falls. Tracks roll statistics showing convergence to discrete uniform distribution with expected value 3.5.

`dice_throw`

## Coin Toss
![Coin Toss](/scene-catalog/coin_toss.png)

The simplest random experiment. Grab coins from a tray and toss them. A counter tracks the heads/tails ratio converging to 0.5 over many flips. Gold side = heads, silver side = tails.

`coin_toss`
