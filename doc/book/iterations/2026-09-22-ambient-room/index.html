Slow Circuits has become a room. Move between rhythm, tone and space, then return to the centre and listen to what you have changed.

How to enter

In WaveFunctions_Synthesis_Lab, find the Slow Circuits console and press ENTER ROOM. In the room, start at RHYTHM → PLAY / STOP. Walk through the rear doorway or press BACK TO HALL to return to the Synthesis Lab.

Left / Rhythm

Leave a gap.

DENSITY decides how often a proposed note is admitted. RATE speeds or slows the three clocks together. NEW SEED changes the choices that follow.

Ahead / Tone

Change the edge.

WARMTH softens the voices; LEVEL changes their volume. Above you, the wall traces actual audio from the output.

Right / Space

Let the room answer.

SPACE adds echoes and reverb. DRIFT deepens slow modulation. LFO ROUTE switches between the filter and movement of the three sound positions.

Implementation and scope

The room reuses the same two cached synthesised notes, eight-voice pool and native audio effects. It places the three voice families at separate 3D positions. In orbit mode, the sound emitters move with their visible markers, rather than applying one global stereo pan. A short audio preview of the earlier console is linked above; it is not a binaural recording of this room.

The round shell has a solid floor and walls. Repeated timber fins share one mesh instance batch. All previous Synthesis Lab exhibits are retained. Entering saves pending museum edits, stops the console, and requests the room through the existing staging loader. Returning selects WaveFunctions_Synthesis_Lab again.
