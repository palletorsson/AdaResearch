# Recovering the encounter, one map at a time

Author correction, 21 September 2026. This records a bounded comparison and an editorial decision; no `final.md`, artifact, map or role was changed in this pass.

The recent work sometimes treated verifiable operation as sufficient material for a chapter. That produced repeated commands, explanatory caveats and future experiments. The earlier discovery agreement already required an explicitly poetic, investigative voice. The failure was in applying it consistently, not a lack of instructions from the author. A passing test can establish a capability; it cannot establish that the capability has become a compelling encounter or a good paragraph.

The working loop is recorded in [the existing voice agreement](../handoffs/fable-final-discovery-voice.md#agreed-clarification-21-september-2026) and [the handoff](WORKING.md). It begins with current and earlier writing, critical material, and existing artifacts; it produces one concrete investigation and a small passage to read beside its original. If the investigation needs a new immersive artifact, build towards it. If the artistic lead is uncertain, ask the author a specific question. Do not complete the map by defaulting to a control manual.

## Rhizome: what can be recovered

The [older manuscript](../../space/postcrisis-pass-2026-09-15/before/commons/maps/SpeculativeComputation_Rhizome_Network/final.md) contains:

> This separates growth of the connection structure from movement of the whole display. It also exposes a small asymmetry in time: an existing node can receive a new relationship from a later arrival, while the newcomer cannot choose a future node that does not yet exist.

The current chapter retains the mechanism, including the neighbour search before insertion. Recovery should give the relation time to become perceptible rather than repeat both explanations. This also offers a return to Point One: arriving in something already underway acquires a different consequence in a growing network.

The current passage worth protecting is:

> The ring does not spread with the shoots. The machine keeps finding new places inside a boundary it chose before the network began. Something is growing, but its territory has already been given.

These are leads for the next map pass. An immersive version could let a visitor encounter the difference between a changed route and a changed territory. The existing cave and the tabletop grower have not been established as one shared model. Walking, closures and repair must be built and checked before being narrated as available actions.

The author has already supplied the main desire: walk a rhizome. Further questions should concern a concrete artistic choice if one arises—for example, whether its first encounter is shelter, disorientation or another way through—rather than asking the author to restate the whole project.
