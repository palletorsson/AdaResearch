# Primitives Profiles — map summary

Borrowed Outlines shows six bodies from five written signs: O twice, then B, C, I and colon. Each source profile accompanies its transformed form. The visitor can compare the region carried with the route along which it is carried.

The font and geometry builder are supplied. This is a bounded encounter with tested forms, not a general topology lesson or a live glyph editor. The O sill and the vessel's open bottom remain visible limitations rather than silently completed architectural functions.

Sequence: Columns → Borrowed Outlines → Portals. The wider museum retains its existing Biome Ladder interlude after Portals before Melencolia; it does not become an extra required lesson here.
