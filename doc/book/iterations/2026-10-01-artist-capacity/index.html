An artist names a field of questions. An artifact offers particular operations. The useful match is where a question can become something we can change, observe and make.

Capacity belongs to a specific operation, not to an artist’s whole practice.

Three capacities, held apart

The project can

Point to an implementing scene, script and operation. Then distinguish a direct map placement from a registered or standalone component.

The student can

Carry a specific operation from earlier work. The ladder records prerequisites and borrowed apparatus; it does not certify a completed tutorial.

The encounter asks

Give the operation a reason to matter: a body, a history, a choice, an exclusion or a material consequence that the parameter alone cannot supply.

question→operation→one changed relation→observed consequence→something the student can make

What these judgements mean

Existing operation A specific operation already exists in source. Reuse it for a new study; this is not a claim to reproduce the artwork.

Combine / adapt Useful components exist, but their arrangement, input or relation needs to be authored and tested.

New capability needed Available parts do not supply an essential mechanism named in this proposed translation.

Define the encounter first Technical parts exist, but a specific source, situated relation or artistic question must guide their use.

“Define the encounter” is not a lower grade. It means there is no honest automatic conversion from the reference to an algorithm.
