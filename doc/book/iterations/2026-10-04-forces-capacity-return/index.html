Thirteen map summaries · 2026-10-04

Forces · what changes

Thirteen new proposals for review, separately from earlier accepted plans: what changes in each map, and why.

Images show earlier museum encounters, including their existing comparisons. This update changes teaching plans; visuals and runtime are unchanged.

01 Vectors_Act1_WhatAVectorIs

Move the relation without changing it

Before
Write a measurement function, choose display projections and normalize inputs in one opening exercise.
After
Read and predict the existing displacement measurement; use a brief normalization return with its explicit threshold.

Why The learner already has positions and translation. A lighter return establishes what an arrow measures without delaying the later retained-state hinge.

02 VFM_02_Operations

Keep the destination and compare routes

Before
Create separate summation and travelled-distance functions and assume supplied receivers consume their outputs.
After
Use the installed result calculation, compare route lengths, and inspect the existing counter with the same carriage and drawings.

Why The current instrument already makes the distinction tangible. A bounded arithmetic edit avoids adding a second route system before Motion.

03 Vectors_Act2_VectorArithmetic

Keep the component along a direction

Before
Teach dot product, projection and remainder while several adjacent operations compete for attention.
After
Make the connected gap/projection pair the core, with recombination as its check and the other operations as short returns.

Why A single live relation supports reuse and an independent check. The critical questions survive without requiring mastery of every nearby instrument.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

04 VFM_05_Launch

Read a flight from its beginning

Before
Construct a sampled projectile trajectory while introducing acceleration and its quadratic position contribution.
After
Read, evaluate and vary the supplied analytic path, using the existing chamber to check one prediction at a time.

Why The strong spatial encounter can introduce acceleration without consuming the numerical-state lesson that belongs to Motion next.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

05 VFM_03_Motion

End the command and keep the motion

Before
A finite pulse exercise already separates ending force from stopping motion, but its prediction conditions and handoff into the common state study remain implicit.
After
Make one mass-and-duration prediction before contact, keep drag off for the comparison, and carry the force schedule and observed retained velocity into Fields.

Why A controlled beginning makes the new idea available for reuse without asking the learner to rebuild the engine’s integrator or turning the friction ramp into another required lesson.

06 Vectors_Act3_FieldsAndMotion

Give the same field two meanings

Before
Two receiver rules are already explicit, but the authored exercise reads as another isolated pair of markers and lacks a small independent prediction.
After
Name position and velocity as the common study state, predict two uniform-field steps, and keep that update convention for the later spring model.

Why Assignment versus accumulation becomes something the learner can change and predict, while the runner and display remain supplied and museum state stays honestly separate.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

07 Vectors_Act4a_KineticForces

Make resistance depend on motion

Before
Drag is the principal exercise, but work can become a second required lesson and the mass comparison lacks a concrete predicted quantity.
After
Keep one force function and one initial-acceleration prediction, carry it as damping, and leave walking, mower and ring encounters as optional comparisons.

Why The state-update strand gains one reusable force term without turning every artifact into another assignment or conflating engine force, annotated work and prescribed motion.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

08 Vectors_Act4b_Oscillation

Returning does not mean stopping

Before
The spring lesson already combines restoration and damping, but the carried update from Fields and the learner’s independent prediction remain implicit.
After
Return explicitly to the same small study convention, own the force sum and ordered update, and predict a release before comparing damping from equal beginnings.

Why The sequence deepens a usable state model rather than restarting with another animation, while preserving the room’s question of return, rest and motion.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

09 Change_Choreography

Keep an offset, change a rate

Before
Build another short row of phased markers, then compare its drift with the conveyor backlog.
After
Use the existing paired-dial source edit to halve drift; predict the gap and explain why equal rates preserve it.

Why A concrete exercise already exists. It makes the earlier rate and phase skills reusable while leaving rods, conveyor and the artistic reference room to offer different kinds of comparison.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

10 VFM_07_Gravity

Allow the reference to answer

Before
Write a two-body interaction and compare a fixed source with mutual response.
After
Focus the work on two receiver contributions, unequal masses and the source's omitted response; keep the solver supplied.

Why The reusable skill is distributing a shared interaction. Naming the stations' different beginnings prevents a misleading comparison and keeps the task smaller than building an orbital solver.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

11 VFM_09_Legs

Ask what supports a still body

Before
Arrange supplied support pads and use a supplied hull calculation before comparing the walker.
After
Use the existing support controls for one prediction, then compare physical support with the walker's commanded movement.

Why The hall already offers a precise comparison. It can deepen judgement about bodies and models without turning reflection into another geometry or gait-building task.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

12 VFM_08_Arena

Ask what an encounter keeps

Before
Write a nearest-eligible selector and completed-meal counter alongside the creature encounter.
After
Compare the existing selector, event history and authored stopping rule; test one occlusion prediction without building a controller.

Why The encounter can develop a careful reading of agency and retained state. An additional implementation obligation would crowd that work and blur its distinction from the separate collision model.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

13 Vectors_Act5_ForceAsPlace

Declare what receives the field

Before
Compose a cube's field acceleration and compare its receiver with the visitor crossing.
After
Make membership, units and retained velocity explicit, then mark the next hall's deliberate change to descent without velocity.

Why The closing field should prepare a reusable question about receivers, rather than suggest that Form Finding is another force simulation with the same state.
