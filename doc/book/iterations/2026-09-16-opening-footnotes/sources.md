# Sources checked for the opening notes

Checked 16 September 2026. The notes make modest connections to these works; they do not claim that a programmed encounter verifies a philosopher's complete argument. This record identifies what was inspected rather than implying every linked book was read in full during this pass.

| Work | Verification and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Sara Ahmed, *Queer Phenomenology* (Duke, 2006) | [Publisher](https://www.dukeupress.edu/queer-phenomenology) and [excerpt](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5e7e6c1f737abc0444cc838b/t/66708504196a0f16f95511e5/1718650118866/Ahmed-Queer-Phenomonology_INTRO%2BEXCERPT%2B%281%29.pdf). Inspected opening orientation/reach discussion, printed p. 16 on paths and p. 20 on deviation. The PDF contains omissions and editorial annotations; page references refer to the printed book, not PDF indices. Notes paraphrase the author's text. |
| Donna Haraway, “Situated Knowledges” (1988) | [Article scan](https://knowledge4empowerment.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/haraway_1988_situated-knowledges.pdf); stable reference [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/3178066). Inspected the discussion of embodied objectivity, partial perspective and relativism around printed pp. 581–585. |
| Euclid, *Elements*, Book I | David Joyce's edition: [Definition 1](https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/elements/bookI/defI1.html) and [Postulate 1](https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/elements/bookI/post1.html). The latter is a construction postulate, not a renderer specification or a complete modern axiomatization of line uniqueness. |
