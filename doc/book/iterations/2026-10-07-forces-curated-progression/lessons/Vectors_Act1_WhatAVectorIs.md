# The point gives a direction

Curation and next tutorial contract — 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts; this contract controls their next revision.

Tutorial artifact: `force_point_direction`.

Learning elements: Displacement, magnitude, unit direction.

Bring the grabbable PointBody from Coordinates. Move it relative to a fixed origin and read the displacement, its length and its unit direction. The rose unit marker is a separate reading; it never moves the held point for you. The magnitude and scaling benches offer comparisons, not two further reconstructions.

Carry onward: A displacement with a declared origin and a direction separated from length.

Independent check: Move the point to another position; predict which readings change. Explain what zero displacement leaves undefined.
