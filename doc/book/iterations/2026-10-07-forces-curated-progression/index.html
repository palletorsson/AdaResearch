The teaching path

Relation → motion → consequence → capacity

The small learning thread is cumulative. A position becomes a displacement; compatible displacements combine. Velocity then carries a body beyond the moment of input. Fields, resistance, restoration, contacts and other bodies alter that journey. The final court gives those relations back to the visitor as usable powers.
