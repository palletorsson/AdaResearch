## A separate spatial obstruction

The original vault is about ten metres wide and its current placement extends beyond the hall. Its raised ground plate caught the walker on a 3 cm edge. A map height offset was normalized away by the museum seating path, so the final change uses a scoped `#ground_top:0` configuration. The vault's default remains 0.03 m elsewhere. The flush placement hides its duplicate visible ground plate to avoid flickering against the museum floor; its floor collision remains.

The first straight exit attempt also ran into a pod wall. A capsule-clearance survey found a route through the existing openings; the controlled CharacterBody then walked each segment. This preserves the vault rather than shrinking it to get a passing test. Its oversized footprint still needs a separate architectural placement decision. Neither this pass nor its text claims that the vault is fully accommodated by the current room.
