## Gödel

Before a next-budget trial, record a prediction; carry its actual inputs, candidate, outcome and source identity into Synthesis. The text bridges symbol records to arithmetic encoding without claiming the toy constructs incompleteness.

## Brouwer

Then construct a four-row diagonal witness, checking its disagreement at each named position. Keep chosen continuation, finite construction and infinite argument separate.
