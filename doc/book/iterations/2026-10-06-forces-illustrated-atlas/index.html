01 / Proposed teaching route

The same point, more capacity.

Push → launch → fly → brake → enter a vortex → deflect → return → choose a route. Each encounter reuses a known object and adds one relation. This eight-stage arrangement remains a proposal; the current thirteen-hall spine is preserved.

Proposed encounter / 01

The push ends. The point continues.

Finite force · mass · retained velocity

The familiar point becomes a force receiver. Push briefly, release, then repeat with twice the mass. In the isolated comparison, the same force for the same duration gives half the change in velocity.

Proposed encounter / 02

The body becomes the arc.

Launch velocity · gravity · landing

Walk onto a pad and become the moving body. Compare the launch direction with the direction you travel at the top of the arc. Gravity continues there, even when vertical velocity is momentarily zero.

Proposed encounter / 03

Two points can carry you.

Two force contributions · vector addition · continuous thrust

Hold a sphere in each hand. Aim down and hold the triggers to rise; aim back to move forward. Each hand contributes independently to the same player receiver. Release removes thrust while velocity and gravity continue.

Proposed encounter / 04

Release is not a stop.

Gravity · drag · counter-thrust

Release the triggers and keep travelling. Gravity bends the path. Compare passive drag with a deliberate push against your motion, then use what you know to approach a landing.

Proposed encounter / 05

The room turns your path.

Spatial field · inward force · tangent

Enter a field with the velocity you already have. Combine an inward contribution with one tangent to the centre, observe the changing path, then use the held spheres to leave it.

Proposed encounter / 06

Send the push to another body.

Projectile contact · impulse · mass

Keep the catalyst sphere in your hand and send a small form toward another body. One hit delivers one impulse. Compare equal-size receivers with different masses, then divert an approaching body to open a route.

Proposed encounter / 07

Who chose the resting place?

Restoring force · overshoot · damping

Displace a tethered sphere and release it. The spring pulls toward its anchor; the moving sphere can overshoot. Change the damping, then ask what changes when you move the resting place.

Proposed encounter / 08

Take what you made through the room.

Transfer · choosing an ability · combined encounters

Launch, steer, release, escape a swirl and deflect a body. The course asks you to choose among the operations already learned. A ground route remains beside the aerial route.

Beyond / speculative

What can a force permit?

Drawn fields · collective response · movable centres · access · alternative routes

Draw a field, gather a moving shield, change who supplies the centre, or make the same ability available through another control. These panels ask how rules distribute power and access.

Reading key

A picture proposes an encounter.

Continuity. The student, dark sphere, pale museum, wall identity and recurring material language join the plates. The point changes capacity while remaining recognizable.

Arrows. Amber usually marks a force or impulse; cyan marks velocity where labelled. White streams show thrust aim. Curved paths show a journey. In the combined-course plate, cyan lines guide a route.

Exactness. Arrows and architecture are schematic. Numerical ratios, trajectories, collisions and VR comfort require runnable models and tests. Individual plate notes identify the important simplifications.

Scope. Complete coverage here means all thirteen canonical Forces halls plus the eight proposed encounters. The beyond pages select further directions; this is not an exhaustive inventory of every force-related script in the repository.
