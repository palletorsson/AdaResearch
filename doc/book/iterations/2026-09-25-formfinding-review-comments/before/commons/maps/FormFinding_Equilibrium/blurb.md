Where forces cancel, form holds still. A Calder mobile balances every arm by the lever law, τ = w·d, hanging in a stillness that is not rest but perfect opposition. A fractal truss spans the widest gap with the least material. A tensegrity triangle stands under a load that would collapse a loose pile of the same parts — held by a tension you cannot see. Gaudí found his arches by hanging chains and turning the photograph upside down: gravity as co-designer.
