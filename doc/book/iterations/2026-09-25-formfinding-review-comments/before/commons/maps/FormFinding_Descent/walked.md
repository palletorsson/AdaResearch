## The walk

A marble in a bowl. Pick it up, let it go anywhere on the curved inside, and it always ends at the same place — the bottom — and nowhere else. The `descent_marble_toy` is the whole chapter compressed into one object: a form (where the marble rests) is the *answer to a minimization* (lowest point of the bowl). Nothing placed the marble there; the bottom is simply where its energy stops dropping. The `gradient_descent` station makes the rule explicit — a ball on a curved loss surface, following the steepest downhill direction step by step until the slope goes flat — and the `gradient_descent_well` lets you drop marbles into a glowing basin and watch them settle, pulse, and begin again. The gradient is the arrow that always points downhill, and rolling along it is how matter finds its shape, and how every optimizer the machine-learning chapter will meet finds its answer. Same arrow, eight sequences apart.

## The turn (critical)

The map's turn is to name the actor that isn't there. Ask "why does the marble take *this* shape, rest in *this* spot?" and the ordinary answer wants a placer — someone who set it down, a designer who chose. There is none. The bottom of the bowl was not selected; it was *found*, by a body obeying one local rule (go downhill) with no view of the whole and no plan. This is the variational principle, the deepest idea in physics wearing its simplest coat: form is what minimizes, and minimization needs no mind. It dissolves the same author the fractals chapter dissolved (no one drew the depth-4 staircase) and the swarm chapter dissolved (no one leads the flock) — here it is the shape itself that arrives without a shaper. And it sets the chapter's stakes against forces, one map back: forces taught you what *moves* a body; form-finding is where the moving stops, where all that dynamics settles into a shape you could not have drawn but the world computes for free. Hold the small strangeness of the marble: it solves an optimization problem perfectly, instantly, every time, and it has no idea it is doing anything at all.
