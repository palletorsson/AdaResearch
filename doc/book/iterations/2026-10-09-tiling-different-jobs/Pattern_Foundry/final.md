# A mark in the room

In the disco, a choice of colour reached every square carrying the same value. Here you can make the little arrangement that a larger pattern will repeat. One mark, then its many appearances. How far can a decision made within reach travel?

<!-- @pattern_architecture -->

There is already pattern underfoot. It climbs the walls. The loom, kaleidoscope, stamp press and pattern mill can each supply their output to these surfaces. At DRESS THE ARCHITECTURE, choosing another SOURCE changes what the hall wears.

Find a piece of the loom's carpet in the larger field. The carpet keeps a short history of bands; those earlier decisions have reached the wall with it. A room can repeat a record as readily as a single motif. Its floor still supports you, and its walls still stop you. For now, leave this larger image running and go to the small tile. It has its own preview, where the beginning of a pattern is easier to follow.

<!-- @pattern_tile_4x4 -->

Take a coloured cube from the palette and release it over an off-centre cell in the small tile. Watch the repeated preview. Find several places where your mark appears, then locate the one source cell you changed.

Add a second mark beside the first. Predict the repeated pair before looking across the whole field. The size of the result can make the action seem more complicated than it was. Many visible changes have followed one local edit.

The tile stores a finite array of colour choices. To draw the larger field, the repetition rule maps each preview position back to a position in that array. Copies share a source. They do not each contain a separately authored decision.

Now try to make the mark appear only once while the rest of the field stays unchanged. Editing the shared source makes it recur. The instrument makes it easier to change every appearance than to keep just one different. A single stored cell holds all these places together.

<!-- @ -->

A repeated field can be economical, recognisable and pleasurable. It can also make a wanted exception difficult to express. Choose a reason for keeping one copy different: a direction marker, a mistake you want to retain, or a place that should resist the general pattern.

For now, this source cannot grant that exception. The mark you change will return wherever the rule reads it. You have made a small decision whose reach exceeds your hand. What would you want to keep shared, and where would you want another decision to begin?

Leave the larger machines running. In Tutorial Pattern, keep the painted cells as they are and change the way a copy reads them. That is a different action from painting another mark.
