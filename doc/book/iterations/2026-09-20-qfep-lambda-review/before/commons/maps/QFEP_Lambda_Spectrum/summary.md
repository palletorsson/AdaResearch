# QFEP Lambda Spectrum - Summary

## Overview
The fourth QFEP Laboratory map builds the entire lambda gradient as a physical corridor. The learner's z-position maps directly to the lambda parameter: north is lambda zero (crystal), south is lambda one (chaos), and the narrow green zone around lambda 0.4 is where complexity emerges. The body becomes the slider.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 5x18 grid — narrow corridor, long depth axis
- **Architecture**: Crystal_formation at north end, bifurcation_walkway along left wall, edge_particles and complexity_pattern in the green zone (rows 7-11), dissolving_form and chaos_particles at south end
- **Height gradient**: Starts at height 1 (spawn), walls at height 2, drops to 0 at south exit

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **crystal_formation** — Hexagonal prism tower at lambda near zero; deterministic lattice, zero entropy
- **bifurcation_walkway** — Logistic map x_next = r*x*(1-x) rendered as a 3D point cloud; 400 r-values iterated through warmup, showing period-doubling cascade and chaotic band
- **edge_particles** — 120 particles at lambda 0.4; 45-degree spread, mild gravity, partial coherence — neither fountain nor fog
- **complexity_pattern** — 64 coupled cells producing emergent clusters that form, hold, and reform
- **dissolving_form** — Mesh vertices displaced by noise over time; the object melts incrementally
- **chaos_particles** — Full-spread, zero-gravity scatter at lambda 1.0
- **ordered_grid** — Perfect flat lattice near the crystal end

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at north, text labels marking "lambda=0.0 ORDER," "EDGE OF CHAOS," and "lambda=1.0 CHAOS"; teleporter at south exit

## Learning Sequence
1. Spawn at the north (order) end among crystals and the ordered_grid
2. Walk south — the background gradient shifts from blue to red, particles thicken, sound broadens
3. Pass the bifurcation_walkway and see period-doubling unfold laterally
4. Enter the green zone (rows 7-11) — complexity_pattern activates, edge_particles cohere partially
5. Continue south past the dissolving_form into chaos_particles territory
6. Recognize the edge of chaos as a narrow, felt boundary — not a label but a change in the world's character
7. Exit through the teleporter toward QFEP_Phi_Term

## Design Intent
The corridor compresses the entire QFEP dialectic into a single spatial axis. The learner discovers the edge of chaos kinesthetically — by walking to the point where the world feels most alive. Every sensory channel (visual color gradient, audio spectral density, particle behavior, height drop) carries the same lambda information, making the parameter felt rather than computed.
