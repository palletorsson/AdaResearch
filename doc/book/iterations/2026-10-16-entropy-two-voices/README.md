## What the revision tries

The “we” voice stays with things an encounter makes available: looking into a cup, following a neighbour as SORT moves it, lifting a jar, seeing a gap where a support stood. Definitions enter when needed to follow a consequence. This is composed prose, not a report of a completed visitor session.

Types keeps returning to the chair under different questions. The short recipe that reconstructs a scatter prepares the moving door's rule. The cup holds simultaneous stages; the glass changes which details a record retains. The last tray settles a colour guess while leaving many possible arrangements.

Workshop starts by making that last distinction happen. SORT changes neighbours without changing the colour reading. Jars make the choice of region visible. Four cubes make the surviving possibilities countable before sixteen places enlarge the count. A preserved arrangement contains more than the dials report. At the ruin, a change can remove actual support; in the signal room, changed black and white patches leave the floor firm.

The halls are not exclusive subject categories. Both can ask about information, arrangement, memory and observation. Types supplies prepared comparisons; Workshop permits interventions and examines their consequences. The two texts share a question without doing identical work.

## Technical attention

| Encounter | Limit retained in the prose |
| --- | --- |
| Shannon chairs and best guess | Draws are imagined under an explicit uniform-ball or uniform-chair rule, with replacement. The installed comparisons have no live draw controls. Colour probabilities, not visible irregularity, determine the readings. |
| Boltzmann chairs and compartments | The fixed-site colour model has 36 and 4,900 compatible assignments at the stated balances. The displayed quantity is the natural logarithm of multiplicity, not the count itself. These are counting models without temperature. |
| Algorithmic complexity | The generator belongs with the seed. A candidate recipe does not prove a shortest program; [Vitányi's paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.07674) supplies the theoretical qualification. |
| Doors | The apparatus compares LOW/HIGH words from 1,024 equiprobable starting bins over eight steps. It does not measure an asymptotic entropy rate. |
| Coffee | The same quarter share of milk appears in five fixed mixing stages. Their moving swirls are distinct from the five thermal model times. The thermal model accounts for both finite bodies; it does not derive entropy from the shader. |
| Frosted chair | Eight equiprobable identities, with declared records leaving 1, 2, 4, 4 and 8 candidates. Two panes can look different but leave the same two bits unresolved. This is not a measured account of human recognition. |
| Min-entropy | The best colour guess succeeds with probabilities 1/2, 3/4 and 1. [NIST's definition](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/min_entropy) gives 1, about 0.415 and 0 bits. |
| Jars | The large jars retain 40 lavender and 40 coral beads, an eight-second hold and separately seeded slow paths. The hand jar retains its red/blue palette. Neither measurement is heat from shaking. |
| Ruin and signal room | The ruin uses physical contact; the adjacent architectural wall uses composed landings. Repeating the selection seed is not an exact rigid-body replay. Neither a fallen column nor an irregular signal pattern supplies a thermodynamic reading. |
