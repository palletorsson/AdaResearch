Every chapter here is another writer's finished work (its book tasks closed) that the tree carries uncommitted. For the 19 tracked ones the title line was added to the working copy AND to HEAD's copy, and HEAD's copy with the line was staged as a blob, so the commit carries one line per chapter and the other writer's uncommitted rewrites stay in the tree as found. The 3 untracked array chapters (Tutorial_3D, Tutorial_Tiling_Floor, Tutorial_Tiling_Walls) have no HEAD copy; they are landed whole, with the title, and disclosed as another writer's. Figures they reference under `doc/book/figures/` that are themselves untracked stay untracked.

Two titles are derived rather than copied: Trans_Pre takes its own first question ("What survives its movement?") and Trans_Introduction its hall's blurb line ("The far bank is already there"), because neither the map file, the sequence file nor the summary offers one. Trans_Scale's only heading, "A door, at last", was already its title at the wrong level and is promoted.
