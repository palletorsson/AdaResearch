# Does the rest of the spine carry us?

4 October 2026 · Editorial and teaching review of the 24 sequences after Primitives.

**The spine has a convincing intellectual and artistic movement. The continuity of the main writing is ahead of the continuity of the course.** It increasingly teaches a reader to distinguish what an image suggests from what a construction does. It also offers increasingly powerful material for making. What remains uneven is the passage from encountering those powers to possessing a small, reusable version of them.

That is a reason to concentrate the next work, not to rewrite every sequence with the same intensity.

## What is building

Three kinds of capacity develop together, at different speeds:

- **Perceptual capacity:** notice a distinction, compare two cases, follow a consequence through time or space. The existing writing often does this very well.
- **Making capacity:** construct, change, preserve and recombine an operation without following the original example word for word. The teaching plans describe this more consistently than the current tutorials deliver it.
- **Critical capacity:** identify what a rule notices, what it ignores, who chooses its terms, and where its apparent authority comes from. The later sequences build on the earlier material most convincingly when this question changes something a visitor can actually encounter.

None replaces the others. A beautiful encounter need not become a programming lesson. A reflective room can be a place to compose or reconsider known material. A code example needs its own evidence of usefulness; a concept appearing in that code is not evidence that it has been taught.

## The order is mostly earning its place

The early movement is readable: **a constructed body changes → copies acquire addresses → colour depends on its receiver → a rule travels across surfaces → change is measured → a body keeps the effect of a force.** Form Finding then asks what counts as an arrival, and Waves shows that persistence need not mean rest.

One especially good boundary is Waves → Randomness. Synthesis Lab distinguishes retaining a recipe from recovering an instant. Random Definition then asks what must be kept to recover an unfamiliar arrangement. The question changes, while something already understood remains useful.

The middle changes the kind of rule: **a choice → a correlated field → a surface evaluation → a state that receives a previous state → a repeated construction → a rewritten sentence → a generated world subject to selection.** These distinctions give the apparent profusion of forms a structure.

The later route can expand the learner's agency further: yielding relations, extracted boundaries, composed hollows, shared trails, learned parameters and explicit connections. The final sequences ask what warrants the resulting claims and how a model could be revised. This is a change of emphasis, not a requirement that every late hall teach a more difficult algorithm.

**Keep the broad order for now.** Early, usable introductions can precede later deep studies: sine before Waves, bounded random choices before Randomness, adjacency before Graph Theory, a supplied shader before Shaders. State what is borrowed and what the learner can already do. Moving a whole sequence is justified only if this smaller distinction cannot solve a real dependency.

## Where the accumulation is vulnerable

The strongest material continuity is already visible in Trace → Grid: something the learner made returns with another use. The final Assembly → PostCrisis return is another important example: a kept pose and its connections return as a body and ledger. Across much of the intervening book, transitions more often carry a question than an actual learner-made record, rule or construction. Both matter, but they should not be mistaken for each other. These examples also show why the ending should not be flattened into a catalogue of advanced concepts.

Use a few deliberate returns. A palette and addressed list can become a pattern; a sampled path can receive a field; a cost function can return when parameters are learned; a neighbourhood can return with moving bodies. These are **proposed teaching connections**, not claims that the current museum automatically transfers those objects between halls. A shared operation can also be reused in code without requiring global persistence infrastructure.

The next storyboard for a teaching hall should name: **what arrives; one new operation; what the museum supplies; one independent variation; what remains usable afterward.** The independent variation should show understanding, not add a ritual assessment to every artwork. Some halls only need to deepen a question or offer space to compose.

## Concrete gaps, with bounded evidence

- The canonical spine contains **25 sequences and 207 book halls**. Every hall has `final.md`. This report reviews the 24 sequences after Primitives, comprising **192 halls**.
- `tutorial.md` exists for **197 of 207 halls**. Its presence does not establish that a lesson is complete, runnable or cumulative. Selected readings reveal visitor instructions, source walkthroughs, compact making lessons and future teaching briefs under the same filename.
- Missing tutorial files are **Array; Ribbon_Patterns_01, 02 and 03; Shader_01_Shaping, Shader_11_QueerRubber, Shader_09_FBM and Shader_10_ReactionDiffusion; LSystems_Shape_Grammars; ISO_RhizomeBody**. A missing file is not automatically a missing lesson: Rhizome Body may appropriately consolidate earlier abilities. Array and the shader entry deserve greater prerequisite attention.
- The detailed proposed subject register contains **104 subject records**, but its declared introduction coverage stops at Randomness. Its older Point Zero entry still asks the student to create a display; the current storyboard uses the existing counter. Reconcile those planning documents before treating them as instructions for future writing.
- Both saved museum plans agree, but omit **Lab_Path** and **LSystems_Shape_Grammars** as sequence/map pairs. Lab_Path's current intent explicitly calls it an optional coda, which can explain that omission. Shape Grammars remains the more consequential route check because its specimen family is recalled at the beginning of Procedural Generation. Saved-plan absence alone is not a runtime verdict.
- Some descriptive metadata retains older orders and scaffold descriptions. Read current sequence membership and hall sources before concluding that an encounter is absent or that an old prerequisite still governs the route.

## Correctness comes before polish

Several old tutorials need substantive repair before being presented as reliable making instructions. These are findings from the actual files, not conclusions drawn from their lengths:

| Hall | Current mismatch | Most useful repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tutorial_Disco | The tutorial builds a 17×17 floor that records footsteps. The current main text describes a 12-column floor, a separate 16-step score and no footstep recording. | Teach one bounded address/clock operation in the current encounter. Preserve the main text's distinction between moving with a rhythm and being measured by it. |
| WaveFunctions_3D_Wave_Propagation | The tutorial constructs distance-delayed sources and dropped pebbles; the current teaching storyboard and main text concern coupled oscillators. | Choose the intended physical model, then make a small working update around that model. Do not silently equate prescribed waves and neighbour-driven motion. |
| Godel_Incompleteness | The tutorial turns a finite search returning “not found” into “a true statement no proof can reach”. Its main text explicitly explains why that inference is not warranted. | Replace the purported demonstration with a bounded proof-check/search study whose conclusion states exactly what was checked. Keep the theorem and its assumptions distinct from the illustration. |
| GT_Matching | The “Hungarian” function fills an array with −1 and returns it; the actual algorithm is omitted. The conclusion nevertheless claims the student can compute it. | Teach the existing greedy comparison and a fully implemented next step if selected. Keep unimplemented advanced methods as named further possibilities. |
| ML_Generative | The tutorial calls training methods without providing their implementations or a declared usable supplied component, then claims the student can train GANs and VAEs. | Select one small complete construction. State clearly which trained or staged machinery is supplied, and which operation the student has actually learned. |

The detailed reviews include further examples. Repair the highest-consequence contradictions first; then strengthen the early reusable operations that many later lessons depend on. The revised main text should guide that work, rather than being weakened to agree with an older tutorial.

## What not to flatten

Do not turn the whole book into repeated instructions to change a setting and check a result. Do not move all critical thought into footnotes. Do not require a new programming concept from every room. And do not shorten a strong passage merely because an intended encounter is temporarily secondary, missing from a plan or awaiting development.

The working hierarchy remains: **restore or strengthen the encounter → reconsider placement and timing → revise the relation between text and artifact → consider cutting only when the claim itself no longer earns its place.**

The useful next question is not whether all halls are equally finished. It is where one small improvement gives the learner more freedom in several later rooms.

## Scope of this judgment

Parallel readers reviewed the current main texts and authored storyboards in three groups; selected tutorials and relevant supporting sources were read more closely. The coordinator reviewed sequence boundaries, the subject register, source inventory and saved museum plans. The detailed reviews record their own coverage and limitations.

This pass did not run every artifact, test headset pacing, execute tutorial code or observe students. It assesses the current writing and evidence of intended teaching, with structural checks of the files and saved plans. The source manifest records the bytes reviewed; freshness is not proof of correctness. No hall text, tutorial, artifact logic or sequence order was changed for this report.
