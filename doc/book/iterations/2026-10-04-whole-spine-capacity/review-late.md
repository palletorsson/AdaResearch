# Late spine: capacity, encounter and return

Review date: 2026-10-04. Read-only review of eight canonical sequences, their 63 current `final.md` files, 14 complete selected tutorials and all 63 authored storyboards in `doc/book/course/storyboards/later.json`. Exact coverage, missing documents and source excerpts are enumerated in `review-late.json`. No runtime, headset, learner or external-theory verification was performed. “Holds” below judges the authored journey; source inspection supports the final record transfer, not a fresh playtest.

The late spine largely holds as a museum of increasingly consequential distinctions. It is less consistently a course through which someone can construct and retain those distinctions. The finals are often more exact, materially grounded and generous than their corresponding tutorials. Several tutorials still announce mastery after incomplete sketches, or describe a superseded encounter. The storyboards generally recognise this: their learner constructions are proposals with supplied apparatus and unresolved choices. Their “carries” fields must not be counted as existing saved work.

The best late rooms make earlier operations newly consequential: an edge becomes a passage, a preserved report resists deletion, and a coordinate or neighbour chosen by the visitor returns as a body. Those achievements deserve different treatment from an unresponsive sign or a fictitious implementation.

## Isosurfaces — strong representation hinge; uneven middle

This sequence earns its length where the visitor must distinguish a field, its sampled boundary and what a body can enter. `ISO_LookupTable/final.md` holds classifications while endpoint magnitudes move crossings. `ISO_FlatTerrain/final.md` makes the restriction of one height per horizontal address tangible through multiple vertical crossings. `ISO_RhizomeBody/final.md` makes a planned connection answer to an attempted return journey. That last encounter should remain an interior to discover.

The middle risks repeatedly teaching “appearance is insufficient” without adding a new operation. `ISO_CaveGeneration/final.md` admits that the compared recipes change several parameters; `ISO_ImplicitModeling/final.md` relies partly on remembered specimens instead of the same field through two representations. These are specific missing comparisons, not reasons to flatten every room into a controlled bench. The portal landscape's second appearance can earn its place through sustained composition, provided it develops the earlier terrain.

**Capacity:** brings noise, coordinates, interpolation and meshes; adds representation choice, field composition and clearance; carries a basis for inhabited Boolean construction. Existing inspection and sculpting are substantive. A reusable learner recipe and recorded route remain proposed. The lookup tutorial is an encounter guide with a useful crossing calculation; the Rhizome Body tutorial is absent.

**Focused intervention:** give one retained field recipe a controlled second use, then permit a simple route sketch after the walk. Keep extraction supplied. Confidence: high on textual continuity, moderate on embodied availability without a new walk.

## Boolean surfaces — compact arc, rich architectural ending

Three halls move convincingly from operations to alternatives to inhabitation. `Boolean_Gallery/final.md` lets walking reorder an authored family; disclosure itself becomes a choice. `Boolean_Works/final.md` gives subtraction stakes through Boolean Burrow's floor, opening and collision, then keeps darkness, concealment and attraction available in the void studies. These need neither a universal optimisation task nor a slider on every sculpture.

The long Works text sometimes lets the inventory of withheld affordances dominate the built achievements. Its claim that the first hall held operands still also conflicts with `Boolean_Verbs/final.md`, which explicitly describes differing sizes, offsets and colours. The storyboard's matched operand exercise would repair a learning gap; it is not already that first gallery.

**Capacity:** brings composed fields and transforms; adds ordered region operations and separate treatment of geometry, contact and depth cues; carries deliberate openings and sightlines. `Boolean_Works/tutorial.md` offers credible small source changes for material and shared-cutter studies, unlike the fixed compose presets.

**Focused intervention:** finish one matched two-operand making exercise, leading to one inspected opening. Use the existing cut and material studies as two short returns. The transition to swarm changes scale from one inhabited body to many coordinated bodies; it need not imply a direct algorithmic derivation. Confidence: high on the authored arc, moderate on physical encounter conditions.

## Swarm intelligence — coherent account of where coordination lives

The sequence differentiates agent-written trails, supplied directions, neighbours, local state, remembered scores and population support. `SwarmIntelligence_Boids_Algorithm/final.md` is particularly good at making boundary and neighbourhood choices visible without treating a flock as a model of consent. The closing ecosystem's support record gives autonomy a concrete dependency; care is not framed as a contaminant that must be removed.

Automatic tours in Physarum and Flow Fields can change the conditions before a visitor has settled an observation. More seriously, the closing tutorial teaches a generic algorithm interface and sketches comparison around a `pass` placeholder, then claims the gallery can be implemented. It does not teach the support-accounting operation that gives the actual ending its purpose.

**Capacity:** brings grids, steering, state and histories; adds location of information and explicit enabling intervention; carries evaluation and inheritance into ML. This boundary is strong because ML Evolution tests the apparently neutral conditions that pay for survival. Construction of a reusable event account remains a storyboard proposal.

**Focused intervention:** replace the capstone's generic interface lesson with one small event account separating births, losses and replenishment. Let an early automatic comparison hold its conditions long enough to be read. Confidence: high on mechanism distinctions; no population behaviour was remeasured.

## Machine learning — good critical questions, weakest continuity of access

The best moments are specific: loss rules judge the same observations differently, k-means still demands four centres from an unclustered sample, and `ML_Sequence_Memory/final.md` makes two histories with the same last token yield different retained states. The linear, hand-set cell is a legitimate teaching simplification. Its three-metre endpoint, however, limits what a normal reach can set.

The sequence repeatedly asks visitors to investigate missing evidence. `ML_Neural_Networks/final.md` says training finishes without a restart; Perception describes unresponsive interaction promises; Generative clearly identifies scripted losses. That honesty protects the claims, but repeated diagnosis can crowd out the pleasure of making a learner work.

**Capacity:** brings objective-dependent memory and weighted updates; adds distinctions among learned state, activation, input evidence and judgement; carries a disciplined account of representation into graphs. It does not establish broad independent ML implementation. The neural tutorial's backpropagation sketch omits activation derivatives and propagates through weights already changed in the loop. The generative tutorial calls undefined training methods while claiming GAN and VAE capability.

**Focused intervention:** prioritise one restartable tiny learner with a correct supplied update, an editable forward calculation and a held-out case. Keep the generative diagram modest; its storyboard's fixed critic is a better first construction than three model families. Confidence: high on document and code-sketch gaps; moderate on visitor timing claims, taken from the finals.

## Graph theory — increasingly useful questions; revision stops short

The route from visiting order to layout, availability, evidence, return paths, economy, capacity and pairing is strong. `GT_Network_Analysis/final.md` gains credibility from the fictional evidence network: a line has a record and a count survives rearrangement. `GT_Matching/final.md` supplies a memorable controlled contrast: the same ring and spokes yield three or five pairs through ordering.

Yet two endings explicitly stop before revision. Flow is forward-only; matching starts a new greedy scan rather than augmenting an existing one. This makes a productive desire, but the storyboard's “revisable allocations” is future construction. The tutorial disagrees with the encounter: Flow switches to Edmonds–Karp; Matching ranges across several algorithms and its Hungarian function returns an unfilled assignment while claiming capability.

**Capacity:** brings lists, forces and references; adds adjacency independent of drawing, reachability and objective-specific network claims; carries the distinction between a valid local step and a warranted larger conclusion. That prepares Foundations as a question, not a proof that graph limitations imply incompleteness.

**Focused intervention:** teach one explicit reversal—an alternating path or residual return—using the actual small example. Remove unsupported tutorial mastery before adding algorithms. Confidence: high on the inspected textual and sketch mismatch; implementation completeness was not retested.

## Foundations crisis — strong distinctions, a tutorial that reverses them

The current finals avoid the easy collapse of geometry, contradiction, incompleteness, illusion and disagreement into one mystical “limit”. `NonEuclidean_Spaces/final.md` distinguishes geodesics from bowed pictures; Russell tests both membership answers and bounded filtering; Brouwer lets the row wait for a choice. Florensky's permeable ghost leaves preserve an embodied question about passage without claiming that overlapping meshes implement a logic. These are substantial encounters.

The severe weakness is the making material. `Godel_Incompleteness/tutorial.md` converts a bounded “not found” into a verdict of true-but-unprovable; its lossy encoding does not supply the promised construction. This directly undoes the final's central caution. `Crisis_Synthesis/tutorial.md` also presents a complete summit and lambda-driven bifurcation where the final explicitly keeps the logistic parameter independent and QFEP unproved. Its later sentence-authority exercise is much better scoped.

**Capacity:** brings concrete constructions and graph claims; adds a way to name assumptions, witnesses, checks and distinct limits; carries questions that QFEP must answer. It grants no mathematical warrant to QFEP.

**Focused intervention:** replace the Gödel sketch with the storyboard's bounded proof-checking example and preserve the sculpture as sculpture. Bring the synthesis tutorial into agreement with the independent experiments. Confidence: high; these contradictions are explicit in the documents.

## QFEP laboratory — accountable experiments, not yet a measured unified model

This sequence holds when treated as the project's own proposal under examination. The introduction preserves different contributions behind equal totals; F separates source uncertainty from a predictor's mistakes; E distinguishes offered branches from histories. Lambda's light can recommend a blocked route. These are effective returns to earlier operations.

`QFEP_Edge_Of_Chaos/final.md` adds a particularly valuable capacity: compare equal model time with different numerical steps. Sandbox exposes a disconnected control and a receiver that retains its last input. Synthesis separates stiffness, pressure, damping and ongoing motion. The terms are allowed to acquire specified local consequences instead of certifying life or queerness.

**Capacity:** brings probability, records, time stepping and mechanics; adds accountability for measurement, weighting and receiver; carries questions about permitted responses into Post-crisis. However, F is not measured variational free energy, introductory change is fixed, and Phi's readings and the body's deformation trace remain proposed in storyboards. The two inspected closing tutorials are accurate visitor instructions, not coding lessons.

**Focused intervention:** one small writable receiver mapping with a retained response would make the constructive promise concrete. Do not require every hall to become a full QFEP assay. Confidence: high on conceptual scope, moderate on operation without fresh execution.

## Post-crisis — strongest cumulative return; preserve its bodily turn

The first five halls risk another run of “the apparatus has an author”, but the operations differ: compression, arrangement, retained disagreement, located images, and revision with provenance. The witness cameras and commons table give partial accounts somewhere to coexist. They should not be reduced to terminology quizzes.

The decisive acceleration is the last three halls. `SpeculativeComputation_Rhizome_Network/final.md` makes a circuit a return experienced from inside, including a crossing where no turn exists. Assembly distinguishes moved coordinates, changed neighbours and constraints that transmit a pull. `PostCrisis_Synthesis/final.md` then returns the visitor's released pose and connections beside their start. Earlier points, arrays, transforms, graphs, mechanics and records now serve an authored body.

**Capacity:** brings the entire constructive vocabulary; adds revisable relation and durable reconstruction; carries a specific desired next operation. Source inspection confirms the shared record writer, readers and final map's `visitor_record:true`; this is stronger evidence than a storyboard promise, though not a new runtime test. Only the latest pose survives, without identity or gesture history.

**Focused intervention:** update the Assembly and final tutorials first: they still call editable relations future work and describe a fictitious scrolling ledger. Optional two-version storage is a good next lesson, not a condition for the existing ending to hold. Keep walking before the rhizome controls. Confidence: high on the source-supported transfer and documentary drift; physical comfort remains unassessed.

## What the last three together can sustain

Foundations asks what warrants a claim; QFEP asks what its own quantities and connections actually do; Post-crisis asks what someone can revise and meet again. That progression holds without turning the first into a proof of the second or the third into an ethics score. Its cumulative capacity is strongest as careful making, situated comparison and return. Independent algorithm construction is still uneven. The priority is to repair the few tutorials that teach a false conclusion, then complete selected small constructions already identified in the storyboards. More interfaces throughout the museum would not supply that missing continuity.
