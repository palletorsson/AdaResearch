Sound path and implementation

Three clocks (3.7, 6.1 and 10.9 seconds, scaled by RATE) → seeded chance → C D E G A pitch selection → Discreet pad / FM piano → low-pass filter → stereo delay → reverb → panner → limiter. The 29-second LFO modulates the filter or panner.

The artifact reuses Ada’s DiscreetAudioGenerator, FMPianoGenerator and AsyncAudioGenerator. Only two notes are baked on a background worker. Playback transposes those finite samples, so transposition also changes their duration. Excess notes are skipped rather than stealing voices. Arriving is silent; PLAY starts preparation. Leaving a disabled hall stops the instrument.

Native effect settings follow the Godot delay API. Each instrument owns its bus and removes only that bus on teardown.
