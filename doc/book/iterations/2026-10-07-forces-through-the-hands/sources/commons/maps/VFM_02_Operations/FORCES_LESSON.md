Curation and next tutorial contract — 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts; this contract controls their next revision.

Tutorial artifact: `force_point_addition`.

Learning elements: Vector addition and travelled route.

Keep the same point class and introduce a second displacement in the same frame. Place their arrows head to tail and build one result point from a+b. Compare the sum with the two travelled segments. The drafting board and walk-in construction let the same operation be read at two other scales.

Carry onward: A sum can preserve an endpoint without preserving the journey.
