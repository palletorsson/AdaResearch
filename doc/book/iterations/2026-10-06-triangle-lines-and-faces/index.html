Three lines, almost meeting.

The first puzzle has three segments already aligned with the triangle. Each stops short of its corners. Pick up a white endpoint, bring it to a white target ring, and release to snap. When all six ends meet, three shared corners remain. SHOW / HIDE FACE makes filling a separate choice.

One drawing, two scales.

The small triangle begins 80 cm wide. Its face is visible in the larger twin, enlarged 3.3 times. Hold and move a small corner: both figures change while it is in your hand. Release to settle onto the drawing’s 5 cm grid. The surrounding floor remains open on both sides.

A smaller drawing instrument.

The white drawing point rests beside the small triangle. Its centre is the drawing position. The panel sits below the work, with explicit actions instead of timed placement.

  1. Press NEW FACE and hold the drawing point.
  2. Move to a corner and press the controller trigger, or use PLACE POINT. Near an existing corner, the new face shares that point.
  3. After three corners, press CLOSE FACE. The enlarged surface appears immediately.
  4. Use UNDO POINT for an unfinished corner. RESTORE returns the reference triangle and docks the released drawing point.

Moving a shared corner changes every attached face. SHIFT START retains the existing fan construction: for a loop with four or more corners, it changes the fan’s starting corner and internal diagonals.
