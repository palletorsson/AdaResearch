# The view has partitions

Eight furnished cubicles compare visibility with physical passage. A GLASS / OPAQUE console changes forty panes without moving their colliders or doorways. Two standing exhibits then separate colour coordinates from colour interpolation: finite RGB/HSV sample models, and sixteen gradient samples with endpoint and mode controls.

The room follows Paint and leads into Chamber_Color. Its three primaries are color_constellation_office, color_space_navigator and gradient_interpolator.
