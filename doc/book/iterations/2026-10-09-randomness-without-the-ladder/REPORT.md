## Where it holds

**The opening gives chance several distinct jobs.** A seed makes a procedure repeatable; a count discards order; an eligible list excludes possibilities before the draw. These are operations the student can change and compare. “Random” stops being an explanation by itself.

**Cubes → Rotation → Walk is particularly strong.** A number first contributes to a growing record, then changes orientation, then changes position. Assignment and accumulation become visible before the learner meets a moving history. The cube and point return with useful work to do.

**Gaussian → Mushrooms gives the abstraction a body.** A retained number becomes a construction parameter. The SIZE comparison makes a precise intervention possible: change one use of the record while preserving the other choices. Adding a recipe then shows the difference between exploring existing possibilities and authoring another possibility.

**The two tracks often meet through the same experiment.** Sorting without changing entropy, suppressing scale without redrawing, and shortening notice without changing a deadline are especially convincing. The tutorial gives the student an operation. The reflection follows what that operation lets us notice, conceal or demand of someone. Neither text has to do the other's entire job.

**Game earns the ending.** The chapter's distinctions become support, warning, delay and a choice of route. The next event can already be determined while remaining unknown to the player. Removing the ladder leaves that bodily question with the reader, ready for Noise to examine relationships between neighbouring samples.

## Where I would tighten it next

**1. Give Entropy two depths.** Counting, copying and comparing neighbours fit this point in the course. Deriving the logarithmic expression adds a different mathematical demand. Keep the complete function and its zero/uniform checks, but let a first reading establish what the gauge reads before requiring the derivation. The early placement has a purpose; it need not become an early examination in information theory.

**2. Keep the late changes of purpose explicit.** Space Geometry → estimation → feedback → layered fields is not one steadily growing algorithm. It compares different responsibilities of a sample. The current bridges already explain this, but one short reminder at each entrance would help: “neighbours share a construction”; “draws supply evidence”; “visits alter the next choice”; “fields combine without a visit history.” I would test those signposts before moving halls.

**3. Let Pheromone unfold one step at a time.** It combines a grid, a walker, two records, decay, a branch and boundary decisions. Its smallest reconstruction and STEP control are the right scale. The teaching emphasis should remain the order of one update, then the comparison between ATTRACT and AVOID. This is the largest conceptual increase in the latter half.

**4. Reduce reading interruptions while retaining complete code.** Most tutorials have roughly 1,000–1,500 prose words plus 130–210 GDScript lines, including familiar display construction and PointBody. Their central operation is much smaller. Several already fold the unchanged dependency away; apply that treatment consistently and identify the few functions to read first. Keep every runnable file available. This is a presentation edit, not a reason to remove necessary initialization or hide a dependency.

**5. Give the reflective voice more breathing room.** The exactness is valuable, especially where a display or a title makes a claim the mechanism cannot support. But Walk, Entropy and Game sometimes carry several operating instructions inside the encounter. Game is the longest reflection, at roughly 2,100 words. Put some detailed control, timing and display qualifications in marginal notes, preserving the actions and consequences that carry the argument. The last room should leave us with its question as well as its controls.

The lineage is reusable constructions and increasingly discriminating experiments. It is not a claim that one saved object or dataset literally travels through every hall. PointBody often supplies the familiar hand control; the records and their uses change. Keep that distinction explicit so continuity does not become an invented implementation.
