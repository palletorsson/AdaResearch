## What the sequence makes possible

The return to Disco joins two earlier lessons: an address can hold a value, and the value has a colour. Now arrangement, palette and the time reading them can vary separately. Foundry makes a source decision propagate onto other surfaces. Later halls ask what changes when a copy reverses, a surface turns a corner, a source gains depth, or a shader changes the appearance without changing collision.

The garment and runway ending gives this accumulated knowledge another body. A stored recipe can travel while its receivers keep their different shapes, other materials and routes. That is new work for repetition. Removing the return would weaken the sequence's argument.

The existing order holds:

> Arrangement, palette and clock → shared sources and receivers → relations between copies → an addressed environment → bounded symmetry comparisons → volume → appearance → a recipe worn by bodies.
