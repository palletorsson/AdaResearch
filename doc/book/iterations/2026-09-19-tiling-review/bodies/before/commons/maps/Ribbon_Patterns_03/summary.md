# From source to skirt

Paint a source cell and compare its repeats on a wall and a skirt. Inspect UV coordinates, offset and repetition; distinguish fragment cut-outs, material finish and static vertex displacement from physical changes. Save a recipe, deliberately wear it, inspect the actual body in the mirror, and dress four walking performers. Studio undo recovers prior assignments. The book follows these three primary artifacts towards Change_Intro: the next question is how variation proceeds over time.
