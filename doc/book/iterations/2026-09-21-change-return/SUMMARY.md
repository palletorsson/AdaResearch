## Two corrections to the review

**A retired hall is not necessarily a lost artifact.** Change_Intro still places velocity_arrow at grid (23, 10) and partial_derivative_terrain at (6, 25), as secondary works. They can earn a larger role without reopening their old maps. There is no player_trace placement in the two current Change halls.

**The velocity arrow does not measure your walk.** Its implementation derives the arrow from its own puck's track. The difference quotient is with respect to track angle (dP/da), while phase advances by delta times speed. That angular-speed factor is omitted from the arrow calculation. Promoting it as measured temporal velocity first requires a units/behavior repair. Placing a trace beside it would not itself connect the data. The current book makes no such claim; this supporting artifact remains unchanged.
