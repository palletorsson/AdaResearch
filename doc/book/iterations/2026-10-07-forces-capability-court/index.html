A court inside the Endless Museum

The existing field encounter opens into a trial court. The ball, cube, slope and moving platform acquire new relations. Walk around each station; no outcome is compulsory.

The point moves you

The same 28 cm point body is still grabbable. Its trigger now adds thrust opposite your aim. Two held points lift the XR rig. Release stops acceleration from the points, while gravity and existing velocity continue.

The donut cake mortar

An eight-tier base, resolutions 4 through 11, supports an adjustable launcher. Lower, fire, raise: one launch speed, several trajectories. The emitted objects are physical torus rings with open collision holes.

A miss stays in the room

The target posts can receive a ring, but the same collision rules can tip, stack or scatter it. Eight rings are available. A missed ring can be picked up and used; firing does not silently erase earlier attempts.

A familiar platform becomes a destination

Step onto the launch pad. It gives an upward and forward velocity; the earlier moving cube crosses the landing path. Timing changes the encounter, even when the launch stays the same.

A pole becomes a bridge

Pressing RELEASE POLE removes its hold. Gravity turns the rigid body around an actual hinge. The receiving block arrests its fall. The image shows the resulting bridge, not a prescribed animation.

One push reaches another body

The same point body appears twice on a rail. A restoring force pulls each toward its own resting position; their collision transfers motion. Pull a point or press the impulse button and observe the other one.

Drag, then arrival

Five familiar cubes share a drag lane and slope. Switch the drag coefficient and compare equal pushes. Bring the unpowered point from the entrance to the final plinth; the other objects keep their altered positions.

To drag

You drag something with you. The medium exerts drag against its motion. Drag performance introduces another relation between a body and the forms available to it. These meanings touch without becoming the same explanation.

The mechanical question is precise: what force acts, in what direction, on which body? The reflective question stays open: what must a body carry, what resists it, and which capacities become possible when we change its presentation or attachments? The room gives those questions actions and consequences.

What the run establishes

43 checks passed in the production museum. The probe used real rigid bodies and collisions, the production desktop grab/action/drop path, and an actual XRTools player rig with two pickup functions. It supplied input; no physical headset was worn.

Measured launch rise: 1.78 m during the test. XR lift: 2.84 m. In the matched cube trial, travel changed from 0.75 m to 0.51 m when drag was enabled. These are run observations, not general performance guarantees.

Headset reach, comfort and Quest frame time remain to be checked in VR. The engine emits one existing unresolved-UID diagnostic (rwex60pqapc); this run has no script errors. The successful tests do not certify the whole project.
