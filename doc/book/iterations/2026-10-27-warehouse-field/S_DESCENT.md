# S descent / proposed next experiment

A 12-metre descent across two standard 6-metre storeys. This is a design study, not a built or traversal-tested passage.

Treat the passage as a negative volume: sweep a player-clear section along two opposing descending turns, subtract it from a thick mass, then rotate the cut relative to that mass. The inside and outside become one piece of architecture; there is no teleport implied by the portal-like mouth. Keep world gravity and the player's horizon upright.

Start with a bay around 7 × 4 metres, a clear passage at least 1.6 metres wide with 2.2 metres of headroom, and a landing about 1.8 metres deep near the intermediate floor. These are starting dimensions to measure against the actual B capsule and the final curves. At a steep angle the legs act as slides; the flatter turn permits walking and stopping. A 4 × 4 shaft would require a steeper chute or more turns and would not provide the same mix of walking and sliding.

Prototype order: build a collision floor ribbon and the landing, drop a white probe ball through it to find seams/pockets, walk B through it, then subtract the visible tunnel and bake matching collision. Inspect the entrance jump, head clearance through bends, slide-to-landing transition and exit. After that, connect the accepted warehouse exit to the mouth in an isolated multi-storey trial. Main tower floor configuration remains unchanged during this study.

The source sketch shows the intended sequence; it is not the construction mesh or a guarantee of clearance. A plan view is needed to resolve the landing's lateral return and avoid self-intersecting passage volumes.
