# A beginning, then a flight

Curation and next tutorial contract — 7 October 2026. Full tutorial.md and final.md remain separate manuscripts; this contract controls their next revision.

Tutorial artifact: `launch_arc`.

Learning elements: Initial velocity and constant acceleration.

Give the point an initial velocity and observe how gravity changes its journey. Reconstruct the compact analytic trajectory with its stated constant-acceleration assumptions. Then compare the hinged catapult and the open pipe: their release velocities arise from contacts. The same appearance of a curve need not mean the same mechanism.

Carry onward: Initial state and a rule for change; prediction can be compared with an actual release.

Independent check: Change launch speed or angle and predict the landing. Explain why a collision invalidates the simple analytic prediction.
