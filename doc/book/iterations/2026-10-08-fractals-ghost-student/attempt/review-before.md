## What accumulates, and what I supplied

The accumulated object is a way of explaining a construction: state what is stored, how one step transforms it, what ends repetition, and how a reader makes it visible. The point changes jobs coherently: endpoint, branch editor, release request, path reader, cell-address reader, sum request, and recipe selector. The same mesh does not pretend that these are the same operation. The repeated current/next pattern earns its place because intervals, connected vertices and cube centres have different meanings.

I supplied prior knowledge of scope, typed function signatures, recursion stacks, powers/geometric sums, approximate floating-point comparison, radians and rigid rotations. The supplied Point lessons explicitly cover many engine basics; I did not need to treat mesh creation, `add_child`, `preload`, local/world coordinates, array indexing or neighbour loops as unexplained. The packet labels line alignment and nearest-point reading as supplied helpers. They could support later depth, but do not have to be derived here. XR signal connections, weak references and the specific desktop hooks are reusable adapters whose internals I accepted; the exact desktop gesture is not retaught in this packet. Godot's prepared project is an express assumption, so its absence from the packet is not a missing starter project claim.

My optional transfer adds a further reasoning step: sine need not receive time. The terminal-direction prediction uses sums of planar rotations, not an explanation explicitly given by the chapter. I should not attribute that competence to the four-step assignment.

The two tracks work best when read as construction plus a wider invitation, not as two specifications for the same scene. The tutorial sets finite tasks and the reflection asks what their drawings omit or permit. The prose can sometimes sound as though the encounter is already decided—“try”, then immediately what it means—but repeated invitations to preserve a comparison keep room for disagreement. The recurrence of limits, representation and bodies mostly accumulates new distinctions; the surrounding collections sometimes become a roll call I cannot use without being there. I would retain those as detours, not require them as proof that I understood my script.
