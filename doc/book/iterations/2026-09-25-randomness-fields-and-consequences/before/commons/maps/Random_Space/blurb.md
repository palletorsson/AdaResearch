Layers that look like land. On a plinth, the noise mixer adds several layers of smooth noise at different scales: OCT sets how many, LAC how much finer each is, PER how much each counts. The sum is stretched across an earth-toned palette, so it reads as water, grass, rock and snow although nothing was simulated. Step the sliders back and the same picture returns; SEED starts another.

Underfoot is a different kind of randomness: a surface whose every corner height was drawn on its own, breaking through the floor in jagged pale patches. Neighbouring pixels on the plinth agree; neighbouring corners under your feet need not.
