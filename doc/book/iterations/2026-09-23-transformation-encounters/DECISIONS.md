## Implementation discovery

The grid parser initially read `#initial_pair:4` as tutorial shorthand, turning the station by four degrees and passing a boolean in place of the pair number. Adding `initial_pair` to its recognised numeric configuration keys fixes both the initial comparison and the intended 180-degree placement. The workbench itself did not require a rewrite.

## Evidence and limits

The isolated production museum passes 81 focused Rotation checks and 16 Introduction checks, including supplied slider/reset events, rendered composition bases, unchanged cube collisions, mark attachment, approach-floor support and landing movement. Native captures were visually inspected. The bank photographs hold the production bridge at its aligned quarter-turn in both states, so the landing displacement is the only changed crossing parameter. Normal play retains the existing animation.

These checks do not establish headset comfort, physical hand reach for every visitor, route acceptance or learner understanding. The controls and comparison still need a human encounter. The live museum was not restarted, and its saves and live bake were not replaced. Historical full-book and audiobook exports remain snapshots.
