What carries across

The same mechanism handles translation, yaw rotation and uniform scaling. It responds to placement changes and explicit rebuilds, with no idle frame loop. The controls still belong to their artifact, so hiding or unloading the exhibit also hides or frees its station.

Edge of Chaos retains its differences: a withheld fader stays absent and a clamped handle stays clamped. Enclosed furniture keeps its existing restrictions. Other artifacts adopt this arrangement only when they explicitly declare their station.

Evidence, and the next check

192 mounted-fixture checks cover dimensions, physical grabs, pointer rays, button actions, model responses, live rebuilds, visibility and unloading. These desktop fixtures establish the mounting behaviour. They do not certify tracked-hand comfort or standalone Quest performance.
