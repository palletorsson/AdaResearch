## The cast

## The walk

Chance gets its hands on form. The random_transformations_geometric station puts geometry under dice — translate, rotate, scale, each parameter drawn — identity loosened into a family of variations. sculpt_one shapes a form by accepting the dice's suggestions, the sculptor keeping only the veto; env_one kitbashes an entire environment by draw, the chapter's room-scale claim. At the far end the perlin_noise_bridge and curl_noise_particles lean toward the next chapter: randomness that remembers its neighbors, disorder growing a grain.

## The turn (critical)

The critical question: who is the author here? sculpt_one's honest answer — authorship as curation of accidents — indicts most "generative art" politely: the dice propose everything, the artist signs. But the room also defends the practice: the veto is real work, taste applied at volume, and the environments chance furnishes (env_one's rooms, this project's own grown maps) have a quality deliberation cannot fake — they were not *meant*, and the walker can feel it. The critique cuts both ways and the room declines to settle it; it hands you the dice instead.
