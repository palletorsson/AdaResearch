# Intent

The biome was the ring around a grid map; the endless museum has no ring. This room contains the biome instead: a cage in the middle, the visitor outside it or in it, the stage a parameter. It is the first of the demos in doc/COMBINATORY_BIOME.md — the evaluation surface where every stage can be seen, and later queried and dreamed. Placement notes: the cage is 8 x 8 m with 3 m glass and two 2.2 m doorways on the -z/+z ends; the stand screen sits off the front-right post; the token's rotation 180 faces its front doorway toward the spawn.
