19 September 2026. Read against `doc/book/REVIEW_PRINCIPLES.md` and the frozen Randomness review. This opening already has a strong progression: a repeatable procedure, a measurement that forgets order, an eligible set, local marks and their connections, then a physical throw and its accumulated record. ## Decisions by hall

The private inspection generator does not consume exhibit draws. RANDOM chooses among the slider's seed range and need not produce novelty; +1 DRAW is a toggle, not a cumulative counter. **Random Entropy — preserve the chapter and all six placements.** The ledger measures marginal categorical entropy from counts; SORT rearranges a display copy, not the measured sample. CONTRAST switches between two cached samples. The source normalizes the histogram by its current largest count and preserves the two-millimetre floor on its bars. Fresh execution of the actual contrast sampler confirms 200 draws, 109 occurrences of symbol zero, four absent symbols and an entropy rounding to 1.82 bits. The quoted numerical passage is supported. The displacement field is a separate construction; the square token changes its plan and enclosure without turning it into a Shannon calculation. The ruin retains its 116 pieces, selects exposed top pieces, stages the fall and resets the seed. Keep the manuscript's explicit distinction between the ledger, displaced points and architecture. The ledger identity comments now make that distinction too. A stale alternate catalog description calls `entropy_axiom` a Shannon instrument although neither source computes H; a guarded correction is proposed.

The retained **Entropy Jar is a development issue**, not a reason to weaken this chapter. Its `_measure_entropy()` computes a colour-mixing statistic over six vertical bins. Its current display can still say “MAXIMUM ENTROPY” and “Cannot unmix!” at a value above 0.85. Those statements overreach: the scalar is not thermodynamic entropy, the threshold is not the exact maximum, and the source does not establish impossibility of unmixing. Its radial `shelled` configuration is a particularly useful existing counterexample: order may remain while a vertical-bin statistic misses it. A later bounded repair should name the statistic on the jar and compare sorted/shelled/mixed arrangements, retaining the work. This pass does not silently advertise that comparison as a current primary encounter or claim to have corrected the jar's display.

**10 PRINT — targeted implementation repair; preserve the chapter and all twelve authored tokens.** The primary appears three times as the same shipped 100-cell `stroke` scene. The ant is not teleported to the entrance. The next search now admits exactly the unblocked neighbours in the rebuilt raster. The rendering, marks, placement, global random draws, update interval and default field size are unchanged. The navigation remains a coarse raster and a local heuristic search, not proof of a traversable physical maze. A newly drawn wall can occupy the ant's current discrete cell; the ant keeps its position and seeks available neighbouring cells. Full routing success and physical visitor clearance were not established by this repair.
