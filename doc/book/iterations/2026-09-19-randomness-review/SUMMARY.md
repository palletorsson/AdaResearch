## The decision

The sequence already has a useful progression: a draw needs a procedure; a procedure admits some possibilities; accumulated draws become records, movement, dress and fields; a retained mark can then enter a later decision. The closing game makes those distinctions matter to support and timing. Most of the manuscript understands these distinctions more precisely than the older registry descriptions.

Fourteen book chapters now have a continuous declared museum route, with two deliberately retained biome applications. Five authored halls were missing from the previous eleven-space plan: Random Cubes, Rotate Random XYZ, Space Geometry, Pheromone and Random Space. They have been restored. Biome Cage stays after Mushrooms; Biome Ladder Randomness stays after Game. They are museum interludes, not two newly invented chapters.

Nine `final.md` files are byte-preserved. Five receive bounded changes: three route handoffs, the Walk label and preceding-hall sentence, two Mushroom artifact markers, and invitations to use the new Pheromone comparison. [Every proposed replacement](text-changes.json) and [exact diffs](diffs/) are retained. The primary filter no longer hides the arrival wardrobe and couture comparison that the Mushroom chapter was already discussing.

## What stays open

The secondary Entropy Jar still makes stronger entropy/unmixing claims than its vertical-bin statistic supports. It deserves a contrasting sorted/mixed arrangement and a more explicit measurement, rather than cutting the main chapter's careful distinction. An unplaced 10 PRINT tile variant also warrants a separate orientation review. The standalone Nature demo needs adaptation before admission.

The decay hall's retained dark sphere remains visually close to the prism display, and Walk's existing button materials have low contrast. These are further curation/UI questions, not silently certified fixes. Later extreme drift is not constrained by the initial stack fit. Pheromone needs a headset visit and sustained observation of the new controls; its display coordinates are deliberately smaller than its model coordinates.

The missing projectile source was a package omission, not an absent implementation. The source supplement and hashes are retained with the ending evidence; the original review ZIP and reviewer response remain unchanged.

The accepted Pheromone run also reported a `SEVERED77cells` route warning in the neighbouring Random Space hall and a one-cell reservation around the witness sphere. These were not investigated as a complete player crossing. Refreshed indexes report existing source-layer dimension mismatches in Rotate and Space Geometry; five retained Space Geometry companions lie beyond its source structure rectangle. The production museum derivation includes their cells, but that alone does not prove reachable support. These are concrete physical-route follow-ups, before describing the restored sequence as fully walkable. Earlier Wave Functions and Noise Inside Noise dimension warnings remain unchanged.
