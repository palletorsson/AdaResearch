# Random_Gaussian — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (turn slot, randomness thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

galton_board · gaussian_random · random_bell_curve · distribution_comparator · distribution_sampler · GaussianBlurCircle · GaussianBlurShader · GaussianPaintSplatter · dark_sphere

## The walk

The turn is a single machine and its congregation. The galton_board drops beads through a forest of pins — every bounce a fair coin — and the bins fill into the bell, every time, from pure chance. Around it the congregation elaborates: gaussian_random and random_bell_curve hold the curve as source and as object; the distribution_comparator and distribution_sampler let you stand uniform against gaussian and feel the difference between *anywhere* and *near here, mostly*. Then the blur pieces — circle, shader, paint splatter — apply the same curve to sight itself, the bell reappearing as the soft edge of everything.

## The turn

The chapter turns here from freedom to law. Randomness, left alone in quantity, is not formless — it has a favorite shape, and the Galton board manufactures it from nothing but fairness repeated. This is the deepest joke in the thread: perfect disorder, aggregated, is the most predictable object in mathematics. Entropy is freedom, yes — and freedom, in the plural, files into a bell. What the walker does with that (comfort or claustrophobia) is the next room's business.
