What was missing: the seed — `_rng.randomize()` at `_ready` and nothing re-seeding, so "empty the same range twice" gave two orders with no way to replay one, and no way to put the same draws over two masks — and the museum placement (the map had `wall_height` only, and the hall's three tokens were "unplanned" in the plan).

**What changed, and why.**

1. *The replay, opt-in.* `RemoveRandom.gd`: `replay_on_reset` (default false — the shipped behaviour keeps its stream across resets), `run_seed` (the randomized seed at `_ready`, or the value given to `set_random_seed`), `removal_log` (the indices removed this run, cleared on reset), `initial_eligible` (the set at a run's start), `new_seed()` (randomize, name, reset), and these in `get_state()`. With replay on, `reset_and_find_instances` re-seeds the private RNG with `run_seed` before restoring the cubes, so the same set empties in the same order, and the same seed over another mask shows the same draws landing on different cubes (R1 controlled comparison; R6 local seed).
2. *The fixture.* `build()` turns `replay_on_reset` on for the bench; a seventh button, NEW SEED (`Btn_6` → `new_seed()`); the status names the seed (`RANGE: columns 2-5, rows 2-5 · seed N`); the legend says `RESET replays this seed's order; NEW SEED starts another`. Nothing else on the bench moved.
3. *Map.* `remove_random:90#local_grid:true` at (6,7) as it was; `museum.artifact_placement: "map"` and `sculpture_clear_rects: [[3,4,10,11]]` (cells 3–9 × 4–10: the bench, its approach on the east and the route past it on both sides — the rect names one cell beyond each far edge, which the museum's `Rect2` excludes; it began as [[4,5,8,9]] and grew twice under dealt plinths, see the runtime); `wall_height` 3 kept. No structure or utility cell moved.
4. *Not built.* Physical absence: the map claims none (a removed cube is a scaled transform on a board; nothing under it is floor), so none is tested, as the card allows. A "start" that runs the timer (`start_removal`) is on the remover and not on the panel — REMOVE ONE is the visitor's step, one press per cube, which is what the final reads; left as the fixture has it.

**Rejected.** Replacing the fixture with a staging of my own (the bench is right: the board, the plates, the numerals, the panel); re-seeding on reset for every placement (the shipped placements keep their stream; the bench opts in); a heat map of many runs (the card's older "gap" — not this room's question, and the seed comparison does the work with two runs); moving `hazards_demo` (5,9) or `dark_sphere` (5,8), both a metre from the bench and off the route.
