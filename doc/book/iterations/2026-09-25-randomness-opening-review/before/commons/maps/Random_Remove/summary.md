# The set before the choice

The existing 8×8 removal bench separates eligibility, random choice and visible removal. Sixteen amber cubes are admitted by RANGE; forty-eight grey cubes cannot be chosen. The controls stand to the right, with a cased account of the set and last event on the left. One press highlights a candidate, then zero-scales it while its slot plate and original transform remain.

RESET restores the complete model and replays the named seed. ROW and COLUMN admit eight candidates each: the same seed gives equal list offsets but different spatial addresses. RANGE and ALL change the draw bounds as well as the set. NEW SEED can repeat. The full removal history exists in code; the visible plate retains only its last event.

The final text follows a grey cube, observes removal, reads the filter separately from the draw, compares the two eight-choice masks and pauses over an unfinished pattern. A fully emptied range forgets the order in its visible shape. Random_Walk carries the question into successive movement.
