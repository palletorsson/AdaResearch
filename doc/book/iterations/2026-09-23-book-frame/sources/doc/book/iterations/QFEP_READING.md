# What QFEP means in this book

Reading note, 20 September 2026. This gathers the project's own definitions for the Laboratory chapters; it does not introduce a new scientific result.

**QFEP means Queer Free Energy Principle.** Ada proposes a critical, relational extension of the questions surrounding prediction and persistence: whose conditions of living does a model support, what differences can it register, and who can contest its terms? The [project entry](../../ENTRY.md#the-self-q-recursive-qfep) names the Queer-Q and Self-Q. The [QFEP development notes](../../research/qfep/README.md) and [Entropic morality](../../research/qfep/entropic-morality.md) sharpen their ethical stakes.

Q asks that a model remain revisable while what it leaves out retains capacities to live, act and change those terms. Omitting a difference from a representation and destroying its conditions of existence are different acts. A more accurate prediction does not, on its own, settle whether its use is desirable. Neither greater intelligence nor a larger entropy value guarantees care.

**Self-Q returns the question to the maker.** What we build also trains what we notice and imagine next. The project's three questions ask what capacities a structure makes possible, what it forecloses, and what inhabits the part it cannot see. These are questions for judgment, not another score to maximise. They apply to the museum's walls, controls, chapter order and primary-artifact choices as much as to its simulations.

The teaching expression is:

`QFE = F − λE(S) + φΔE(S,t)`

| Contribution | Question to carry into an encounter |
| --- | --- |
| F: modelling or free-energy contribution | Whose expectation is served? Can a better score conceal a loss elsewhere? |
| E: entropy over specified states | Which alternatives and probabilities enter the count? What differences remain outside it? |
| λ: relative weight | Who changes the weight, and whose possibilities are traded? |
| φΔE: weighted change contribution | What kind of change is counted? What can recover, and what loses the capacity to return? |

Q does not add a measured numerical term. A quantitative use must specify states, probabilities, interval, units, normalisation and whether the objective is minimised or maximised. ΔE becomes a rate only when divided by an interval. The console's supplied change value and the reactor's authored preferred band do not establish universal conditions for life.

The current encounters give these questions handles. Introduction keeps two arrangements with a similar total. F Term changes a prediction while its bits remain fixed. E Term prunes branches, displays what the closure removed, and restores the same tree. Each does something inspectable; each also has a boundary. No button yet lets a visitor invent another kind of ending.

More open branches are not automatically better. Shelter, refusal and protective boundaries can sustain possibilities; arbitrary disturbance can destroy them. The research commitment is to attend to those consequences and keep the authority of the model open to challenge. The fan's count cannot make that decision for us.

## Tolerance — author emphasis, 22 September 2026

The author identifies tolerance as a central QFEP word: both a mathematical measure and a condition that must facilitate difference. Carry this through concrete encounters rather than turning it into a universal moral score or a new unexplained formula term.

A numerical tolerance specifies an allowed deviation under a stated measure, reference and purpose. It might decide when an arrival counts, when an approximation is sufficient, or when a solver stops. The bound does not decide its own adequacy. In [Point One](../../../commons/maps/Point_One/final.md), the target accepts a position within 0.45 m of its centre: “A hand has room to arrive.” Its acceptance test does not register the visitor's intention or route. [Primitives Portals](../../../commons/maps/Primitives_Portals/final.md) asks what a perimeter tolerance is sufficient for. These are early material entries into the later QFEP question.

Distinguish accepting a difference from making it disappear in the account. A comparison may label two different states equivalent for one task. Facilitation asks whether their difference can remain perceptible, act, develop and alter the terms of the encounter. A wider permitted band still has a centre and an author. Who may revise the reference, the measure, the boundary or the objective?

Do not conflate numerical error tolerance with mechanical freedom. In Assembly, freely rotating joints permit movement; solver error is the imperfect satisfaction of those joints. Increasing solver error is not a way to demonstrate political generosity. A useful proposed comparison would vary allowed joint motion or compliance while retaining adequate numerical accuracy, then examine which gestures and supports become possible. That comparison is not yet a visitor control.

The ethical commitment follows the project's [entropic morality](../../research/qfep/entropic-morality.md): sustain the conditions in which different lives and capacities can continue and can contest the model's authority. Tolerance therefore needs resources, time, support and room for refusal as well as permissions. Simply allowing more variation does not establish that those conditions have been supplied.

Writing prompt: **What must this system change so that difference can do more than fit?**
