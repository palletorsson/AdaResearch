Write from what happens
# Write from what happens

A curious beginner enters a world they did not build. Code gives a body an address, a surface, a permitted movement, a memory, a neighbour or a route. Our accumulating question is: **what bodies are possible with the tools and combinations we have learned?** The reader may be sitting with the book rather than standing in VR. Give them somewhere particular to begin.

The author wants the writing completely concrete, poetic, technical and critical at once. These are qualities of an encounter, not four headings or four equal portions. A hollow may offer somewhere to hide. A stored position may preserve a movement while losing everything between samples. A rule may let something remain. Explain enough of the operation for its consequence to become intelligible; give that consequence enough room to matter.

## Let the situation lead

Before revising, write one private sentence naming the incitement: what is it about this object, action or relation that makes this passage necessary? Identify the sentences already carrying it. Do not replace mature prose to establish uniformity.

For your own thinking: what is here; what draws attention or desire; what can this body do; what becomes surprising; what operation explains it; what does that operation make possible or demand; where does curiosity go next? These are questions to think with, not a compulsory paragraph order. An opening can begin with a gesture, an image, an address, a memory or a precise question. It need not begin with an instruction or thesis.

Stay specific at sentence level. Who is "we"? What has the reader actually done? What is changing, relative to what? Do not assume a beginner has already programmed something or experienced a feeling. Prefer a particular relation over "the system" when that makes the thought clearer. Let the reader discover something before summarising its meaning.

Useful commands can belong in the prose. A long succession of Choose, Press, Compare, Reset, Notice turns discovery into an examination. Keep the actions that produce the encounter; put exhaustive control recipes, debugging details and verification qualifications in companions or field notes. Do not hide a fact essential to understanding the experience. Compact code can be beautiful and explanatory when its inputs and consequences are clear.

## Keep critical thought close enough to touch

Look for pleasure, desire, attachment, support, labour, dependence, selection, memory, refusal, exposure, shelter or allocation as the encounter warrants. A support is not only a restriction. A boundary can shelter. A repetitive structure can be wanted. Noise, complexity, novelty and instability are not automatically queer, free or alive. Queer inquiry can motivate a new construction without making geometry prove a political conclusion.

The author wants a little awe. Earn it through the scale, strangeness, economy or consequence of what happens, rather than adjective inflation. Poetry can be explicit and technically exact. Preserve a pause or unresolved question when that does more than a final explanation. Avoid giving every hall the same ending about what representation leaves out.

References should do specific work. Use the existing critical/technical drafts and author's remarks to find historical and theoretical leads. Verify a new quotation, attribution or scientific claim against a primary source; do not add names as atmosphere. Do not call roughness, entropy, topology and complexity the same quantity. "Topology" can mean connectivity in a precise argument; terrain shape or navigability sometimes requires a different description. Smooth/striated space is not simply a synonym for a smooth/rough mesh. QFEP, if relevant, means Queer Free Energy Principle, a situated research proposal.

## Let writing and artifact improve each other

Read supporting markdown and current placements, not only final.md or the promoted list. An older draft can contain a better question. A secondary work can supply the missing comparison. A returning artifact can do new work with another body, scale, receiver, context, time or question.

Use this order: **restore or strengthen the encounter → reconsider placement and reveal timing → revise the relation between text and artifact → consider cuts.** The primary list is a reading scaffold, not a one-artifact quota. Do not remove a good argument because a previous curation pass temporarily hid its evidence.

If an essential encounter is absent, define the smallest working addition that lets the question be experienced. First search existing sources, optional configurations and earlier placements. A new panel alone is not an immersive improvement: say what changes in the room, object or available movement. Sometimes a small study is the right scale. Sometimes the player needs to enter the form. Build within ownership when feasible, and verify the consequence that matters.

A missing implementation is not solved by writing as if it exists. Keep the imaginative passage in a clearly labelled candidate until its encounter is supported. If no adequate construction is feasible now, make a specific development task and improve independent supported parts. Do not shrink the project's ambition into the current reduced scene.

## Read aloud, then stop when the chosen gap is resolved

Read the revision aloud and in its neighbours. Does the reader have the information when it is needed? Does the technical sentence answer a question the encounter made available? Is the poetic sentence giving perception time, or hiding a missing connection? Does the critical turn arise from this particular object?

Preserve, target, or develop according to maturity. No word-count quota, compulsory new artifact, or requirement for a large diff. Strong short chapters can stay short. Strong long chapters may need pauses rather than cuts. This pass is complete when the selected gap is resolved and the next hall inherits a useful question.

# The three-sequence assignment

## 1. Randomness — what is left open, and by whom?

Carry forward the toolbox: an array supplies places, a transformation changes a body, colour marks a difference, a force moves it, a wave repeats. Randomness changes how a value or choice is obtained. It does not make the whole construction without conditions.

The trajectory already includes repeatable generation; distribution versus arrangement; removal and eligibility; a binary choice becoming a larger structure; single outcomes and accumulated evidence; perturbation and retained state; a walk and its trace; sampling laws; kinds of body invited by a recipe; neighbourhood correlation; different jobs for chance; feedback; layered terrain; and uncertain availability for a moving body. Make the returns cumulative without announcing this list at every doorway.

**First delivery: Randomness_10_PRINT_Algorithm.** Read the existing ten_print printer, ten_print_structure#form:floor, ten_print_maze_3d placements and composition_stochastique before deciding what the chapter lacks. A character, a diagonal, an array, a visual contour and a usable passage give different consequences to related choices. The existing prose distinguishes contour from route, but repeatedly tells the reader how to compare. Find the strongest actual encounter. Inspect collision and navigation before promising that the visitor can walk the apparent maze. Several works being placed does not establish that they are working.

**Next development candidate: Random_Rotate_Random_XYZ.** The final text repeatedly asks the reader to separate drift, rotation and colour, and finishes by proposing a rotation-only matched-rate study. Source pointers include random_decay_multimesh, hardware_entropy_decay and RotateGridCubes. Determine whether an existing configuration can make the comparison immediate. If a matched study would clarify the question, implement it as a scoped addition. The two stacks' unequal configured rates are not evidence that one shape inherently decays faster. Keep restoration of an arrangement distinct from exact replay of a stochastic future.

**Read with selective pressure:** Random_Cubes, Random_Space_Geometry and Random_Space are other short chapters worth checking for repeated procedural directions, an unrealised comparison, or a weak bridge. Shortness is not the defect. Random_Definition, Random_Entropy, Random_Walk, Random_Gaussian, Random_Mushrooms, Randomness_Examples_of_Randomness and Random_Game already contain developed inquiries; start by identifying what to preserve. Random_Remove and Random_Pheromone also need their own judgement rather than a preset intervention size.

Possible lead: the freedom promised by a random choice depends on which outcomes were admitted. Let particular eligibility, distribution, memory and bodily consequences carry that thought. Do not turn every hall into a correction of the word "random".

## 2. Noise — correlated difference, inhabitable ground, the convenient organic

Randomness does not automatically teach correlation. Make the difference available: nearby queries can give related values; frequency changes where we sample; threshold, displacement, colour and collision spend values differently; octave composition and coordinate warping are different operations. Earlier height and force encounters can return as questions of walking, hiding or seeing.

Protect the author's **blob culture** argument in Noise_Perlin_Simplex. Noise is a powerful, easy way to give an object organic movement, pleasure or queer possibility. Its convenience can also become a mental algorithmic wall: similar answers arrive before different bodies have been explored. This is an inquiry into our own production, not a verdict that blobs are bad, noise is empty, or queer form has one appearance. The need to learn another operation leads into Cellular Automata.

Several Noise chapters are already developed. Noise_Perlin_Simplex has a detailed matched comparison, but its source explanations may be a pacing candidate: keep the distinction and observable consequences while considering which implementation reporting belongs in technical.md. Do not dismantle a working comparison merely to make prose lyrical.

**Noise_Inside_Noise:** the author explicitly wants the visitor enclosed inside the sphere. Check the present scene and preserve that scale of encounter. Do not reduce it to viewing a ball on a plinth. **Noise_Space_10:** the walking body's support is central; visible deformation is not automatically changed collision. **Lab_Path:** preserve a possible reflective pause. Its legacy dark_sphere need not acquire an invented primary lesson. Dark_sphere is usually a secondary remainder across the sequence; frequent placement is not a primary argument.

Do not assert that random land is impossible to optimise or that a named noise basis guarantees walkability. Samples, scale, receiver, collision and route matter. Keep any future machine-learning gradient-descent connection as a situated return, not a claim that walking downhill and optimisation are identical.

## 3. Cellular Automata — a form that has to continue itself

The bridge from Noise is stored state and local dependence. It is not "noise has no rules, CA has rules." Both have rules. A later state can depend on earlier neighbouring states; a record of those states can acquire a spatial body.

The present order moves through local updates, a cell's history, a recorded crossing, apparent travel at fixed addresses, participation by the visitor, continuous or soft states, the difference between resemblance and computation, and the spread of a perturbation. Read current implementations: map names are historical. In particular CA_GameOfLife currently centres a Rule 30 history bridge; its name is not permission to describe it as Conway's two-dimensional Life.

Keep CA_GameOfLife's difference between ornament and a floor that must support a body. The same record has several uses. This already brings the museum and text together and may warrant preservation. Do not force all eight halls into new experiments because the block contains eight halls.

The author's open directions remain valuable: stack generations into a space we can enter; compare a genuine three-dimensional automaton with a history extrusion; let local rules shape walls, growth and relations; explore sine together with CA. These are candidate questions, not facts to paste into the manuscript. If one resolves a demonstrated gap, recover or build a bounded encounter and show what each rule contributes. A sine displacement of cell geometry is different from changing its neighbourhood or update law.

Do not claim that every CA is Turing complete, every apparent circuit computes, or any spatial deformation changes topology. A branching decision rule is not automatically a decision-tree model. Use the specific rule and implementation. Hand over to Fractal_Recursion through the distinction between local evolution, a stored history and a repeated construction.
