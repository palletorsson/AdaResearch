# A small complete delivery for each changed hall

Work under doc/book/iterations/2026-09-24-claude-randomness-onwards/<Map>/.

Save before.md before changing live text; save after.md and an exact unified text.diff afterwards. Add DECISIONS.md with a short account of what was preserved, what needed improvement and why this amount of change was appropriate. Include relevant source/placement paths, what was checked and the limits of that evidence. Keep audit apparatus out of reader-facing prose.

If the artifact changes, retain a source/map diff too. Record the wanted action and observable consequence, actual result, and remaining limitations. Save native captures when useful to the reader or to assessing the spatial change. A new illustration is not a requirement for an editorial-only change.

Use a clearly marked candidate.md for prose that depends on an unbuilt or unapplied capability. Do not silently copy it into final.md. Current supported revisions may go directly into assigned canonical final.md with their before/after record. Preserve deliberate anchors or supply an explicit migration when an encounter change needs one. Do not discard useful mathematical/technical distinctions merely to shorten the chapter.

A preserved hall needs only a short STATUS entry explaining why it already works; no artificial diff. A development gap needs a concrete proposal: existing recovery candidates considered, chosen operation, where it belongs, what a body can do, how to check it, and which prose it supports. Avoid a speculative catalogue of new rooms.

Maintain an aggregate STATUS.md and INTEGRATION.md in your own delivery directory. For shared metadata changes include map name, previous/current text hash, relevant source hashes and exact fields/entries to update. Do not mark a new runtime or learner status merely because text was edited. Astra can integrate aggregate metadata/exports after review without rediscovering your changes.

End-of-block handback:

- Halls preserved, targeted or developed, with the important reasons.
- What works better for reader and visitor, with representative diffs/captures.
- Canonical changes versus candidates and unresolved artistic choices.
- Shared patches waiting to be integrated; consequential checks still missing.
- The closing question inherited by Fractal_Recursion.

There is no need to repeat the entire source audit. The work should be easy to inspect and continue.
