## Primitives: Points Build Worlds

Make a running world available for construction: distinguish execution, position, frame, relation, record, representation, connectivity, generating rule and finite limit; compose with what those distinctions permit.

Sequence demonstration: Offer an independent arrangement using generated positions, a preserved source and altered copy, a valid indexed face and two sphere resolutions. Ask for predictions, execution and an explanation of the choices. The exercise is assigned; independent learner understanding has not been observed.

## Transformation: What Stays the Same When Everything Changes
