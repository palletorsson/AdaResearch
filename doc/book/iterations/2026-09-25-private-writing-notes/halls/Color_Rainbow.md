## Author question — colour, mixture and the boundary (28 September)

The author asks whether queer has a colour, why pastels can become politically charged, and whether a continuous slider could make mixture and the disturbance of categories felt. They propose Kristeva, abjection and Lacan as possible references.

**Writing lead.** Colour acquires queer meanings through bodies, uses and histories; those meanings are not intrinsic RGB values. Avoid treating a fear of pastels as a fact about all conservatives. Ask when the same colour supports an expected order and when it troubles it. Preserve the possibility that named bands can support recognition and solidarity: a smooth field is not inherently more emancipatory than distinct colours.

**Closest reference.** Kristeva's [Powers of Horror, opening chapter](https://ieas-szeged.hu/downtherabbithole/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/Kristeva_Abjection.pdf), especially pp. 1–4, gives the relation between attraction, repulsion and threatened boundaries. A reading of political colour through this account would be our application. Do not make queerness or pastel an inherently abject property, or use the theory to diagnose all political opponents. Give Lacan a specific role only when the encounter needs one; do not collapse the two accounts into a name list.

**History already available.** Gilbert Baker's [memoir excerpt](https://gilbertbaker.com/rainbow-flag-origin-story/) connects the flag to collective visibility, celebration and a chosen symbol. [MoMA's object account](https://www.moma.org/audio/playlist/344/4563) describes assigned meanings and practical changes to the bands. The existing arch is an authored colour construction, not a reconstruction of each historical flag.

**Artifact proposal, not yet implemented.** Keep RE-DIVIDE. Add one continuous control for sharp band boundaries becoming a blended gradient, and a separate control that mixes the display colours toward white so they pass through pastels. These are different operations; a hue sweep at saturation/value 1 cannot perform the second. Affect the arch and its existing path colours together; retain the historical banners as fixed comparisons. Physical handles should remain reachable from the level route. Keep discrete presets recoverable. Do not label either axis as a degree of queerness or claim that finite rendering supplies mathematical continuity. The object should invite stopping anywhere, not require reaching a preferred endpoint.

**Possible passage.** A colour can be welcome on one body and treated as a warning on another. What changed: the colour, the body, or the rule that brought them together?
