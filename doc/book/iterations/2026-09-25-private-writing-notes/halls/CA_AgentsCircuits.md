# CA_AgentsCircuits

**Desire.** Recognise a triangular relation across two objects and find out how far the recognition remains useful.

**Route / writing move.** Keep a remembered patch, including occupied cells, as the thing carried between works. Permit a partial match or a failed one to determine where the prose looks next; avoid announcing that similarity must be misleading.

**Encounter to lean on.** `ca_rule_explorer` supplies “If one part refuses the match, stay with that part”. `sierpinski_pyramid` then gives the comparison depth, overlap and a different copying procedure.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the fixed-size overlapping cubes when they complicate the remembered gap. Protect the joy of seeing a relation. The rule-class survey can remain a brief optional desk return rather than a second chapter competing with the comparison.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Soft Rules moves through time within paired fields; this hall moves attention between different constructions. Edge of Chaos should reverse the question by holding the procedure fixed and following one changed value.
