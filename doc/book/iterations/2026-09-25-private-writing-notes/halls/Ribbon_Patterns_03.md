# Ribbon_Patterns_03

**Desire.** Wear a mark you made and recognise it on other moving bodies.

**Route / writing move.** Preserve the journey from source cell to skirt, mirror and procession. The repeated mark changes carrier, receiver and social situation each time; these returns are the chapter's subject, not redundant illustrations.

**Encounter to lean on.** `super_drag_mirror` says “part of how you meet yourself.” SAVE and WEAR SAVED make that encounter depend on an authored choice, while `drag_catwalk` lets the same recipe meet several silhouettes.

**Explain / linger.** Give wearing and undo instructions clearly. CUT OUT and VERTICES need their distinct collision explanations, but the mirror should get breathing room afterwards. Preserve the pleasure of seeing one's mark travel; the Skirt-only adapter can name a concrete future garment task without becoming the scene's final judgement.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Ribbon Patterns 02 investigated a carrier's contribution. Here the carrier is worn, remembered and shared. Change Intro then begins with two readings that disagree about direction, a quieter numerical opening after the procession.
