# Primitives_Polythedra

**Desire.** Turn a little corner in the hand, then discover what happens when that construction becomes part of the room containing the hand.

**Route / writing move.** Move between scales and receivers. An open trihedron invites looking inside; its physics supplies another boundary; the pink simplex occupies a real room corner. Let this change of role carry the argument before naming a general politics of enclosure.

**Encounter to lean on.** `grab_trihedron`, `grab_tetrahedron` and `polyhedra_boundary_plate` make the boundary comparison explicit. The room-sized counterpart earns “The corner looks cut away because we have filled it.” `prism_block` then turns a face toward the feet.

**Explain / linger.** Separate the regular tetrahedron from the corner-fitting simplex. Give the pink face time to be followed into the walls. A collision hull is source evidence; passage for the particular player remains an embodied check.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Triangle's failed fill already offered a contradiction. Here lead with assembly and inhabitation. Animatedcube can take one familiar solid apart after this move into architecture.
