**Desire.** Recognise a familiar floor, give it another palette, and move with what returns.

**Route / writing move.** Preserve the repeated floor as a changed encounter after Colour. Follow one tile while its value stays fixed, then let the visitor walk into a moving band and finally add a note. The route can become progressively less stationary.

**Encounter to lean on.** `standalone_disco` asks “Can an invitation to move be felt”. The passing colour band supplies that invitation while its supporting floor remains in place. `step_sequencer` adds an independent clock worth hearing on its own terms.

**Explain / linger.** Explain palette lookup through one chosen tile. Keep the instruction to stop both instruments clear when comparison needs stillness. Otherwise leave time for visual and musical phases to disagree pleasurably; the hall need not require synchronisation as success.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Colour made surrounding relations newly noticeable. This return lets that knowledge dance. Foundry will ask the hand to author a source, so end with appetite for making rather than a summary of every control.
