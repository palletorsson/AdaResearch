# FormFinding_Equilibrium

**Desire.** Follow a hanging branch upward and understand how unequal parts can balance.

**Route / writing move.** Let the mobile be a generous success before the triangle and tower complicate appearances. Its crossed mass-distance calculation can feel like a discovered relation. Preserve the sculpture's breadth, discs and nameplates rather than compressing everything into evidence for an implementation audit.

**Encounter to lean on.** `calder_mobile` supplies “The heavier child rides the shorter arm”. `tensegrity_triangle` and `fractal_truss_tower` offer different visual promises; their names and prescribed movement should stay distinct from measured structural behaviour.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the ideal mass model and recursive accumulation. Allow quiet beside the unmoving mobile. Changing masses, comparing a hinged square and loading the tower are proposed experiments needing development, not controls the prose can presently invite.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Relaxation already contrasts live and frozen forms. Here foreground a readable balance relation, not another surprise that a sculpture is still. Annealing will introduce the untroubled teacup; save that comic uncertainty for its own setting.
