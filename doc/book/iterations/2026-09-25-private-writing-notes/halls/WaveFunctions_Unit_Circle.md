# WaveFunctions_Unit_Circle

**Desire.** Predict the next part of a rising line from a point going round.

**Route / writing move.** Let a few successful predictions carry the tutorial. The existing four-reference-position paragraph partly repeats the opening; it could become one compact worked comparison, leaving more room for the moment when the graph stops growing and the point continues.

**Encounter to lean on.** `unit_circle_advanced` supplies “The point goes on turning”. Its projection rods and retained graph already make a complete encounter; the adjacent bridge-like forms need no invented climb to justify their presence.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the graph's angle axis and the difference between one coordinate and two. The return to a repeated height can introduce ambiguity naturally. Radians deserve concrete rim-distance language, while arctangent can remain a useful continuation rather than a second full lesson.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Sine Space gives a broad, moving passage; this hall can be spare and precise. The oscillator lattice next repeats instructions to compare neighbours, so avoid carrying a prolonged observation checklist into both chapters.
