# Change_Choreography

**Desire.** Find a pattern among many repetitions, then watch a successful delivery become a pile.

**Route / writing move.** Preserve the move from one rod to a whole row, and from one parcel to congestion. Those changes of attention suit the material better than an imposed mystery-and-reveal structure. The parcel's awkward arrival can stay funny before it is asked to carry an institutional reading.

**Encounter to lean on.** `phase_rod_array` and `conveyor_backlog` ground “Its schedule cannot collect a promise.” Floor eyes, missed platforms, wrong-destination escalator and mutual sweepers are authored `if_not_exist_create` proposals, not verified encounters to narrate as present.

**Explain / linger.** Distinguish phase offset from frequency drift; keep the 150-second realignment qualified. Allow recovery time to remain visibly longer than a button press. Carrying or climbing the heap would require development.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Oscillation explained restoration; here clock-driven coordination changes the question. Gravity next shifts to reciprocal attraction, so avoid making every relation another version of synchrony or repair.
