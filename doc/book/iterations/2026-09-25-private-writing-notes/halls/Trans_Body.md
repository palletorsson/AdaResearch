# Trans_Body

**Desire.** Continue through a boundary and discover whether it remembers the passage.

**Route / writing move.** Follow the aftermath of three approaches. Turning slats recover, removed cells retain a trench, shrinking blocks return as distance increases. Looking back can become the recurring action while the three consequences remain different.

**Encounter to lean on.** `approach_wall`, `carve_grid` and `approach_scale` make “Two boundaries return; one keeps the cut.” Keep the lattice's actual chest-height, open-topped character; a dramatic enclosed tunnel would require another build.

**Explain / linger.** Explain deletion's reach margin after the first layer gives way. Leave time to inspect the stepped residue and imagine someone following. The successor's fit is a question, not a tested multiplayer claim.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Scale changes the room in a cut; this room responds continuously to approach. Pit can alter that relation by letting clocks continue regardless of proximity, so its timing matters before another general statement about the body recognised by code.
