**Desire.** Recognise how a plain cube becomes the inviting pickup already familiar from games, then collect one.

**Route / writing move.** Accumulate with a small absence. Observe the still cube before taking it; use its remembered shape as movement, turning and pulsing join the other examples. The missing first cube is a consequence of participation, not an inventory mistake to write around.

**Encounter to lean on.** `pick_up_cube` supplies the chain: “The still cube has gone if you collected it; the three moving examples remain.” The small single-operation markers let the visitor separate what the larger objects combine.

**Explain / linger.** Give each new operation its name after a corner or edge has made it noticeable. Explain interpolation economically. Let the finished pickup feel desirable before appending any broader interpretation.
