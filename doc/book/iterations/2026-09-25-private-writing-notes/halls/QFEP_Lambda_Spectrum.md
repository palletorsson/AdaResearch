# QFEP_Lambda_Spectrum

**Desire.** Choose somewhere to stay, then move the light that first recommended it.

**Route / writing move.** Preserve the invitation before naming its argument. A return to the original strip after visiting both ends can make preference a lived comparison, not an automatic allegiance to the middle.

**Encounter to lean on.** `lambda_hall` says “Light has made a recommendation”. The adjustable strip moving beneath a form while rows retain their own colour and spacing makes persuasion visibly different from a cleared passage.

**Explain / linger.** Explain λ's role as an authored spatial analogy after the visitor has found a dependable interval or compelling moving form. Keep the chalked 0.4 as a thesis to investigate, never a universal scientific or ethical value. Give steadiness and variation equal permission to be useful pleasures.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** E counted the offered endings; here their staging attracts attention unequally. Phi will examine direction through time, so this hall can take a slower architectural breath around where a body wants to remain.
