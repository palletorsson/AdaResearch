**Desire.** Join a painting already underway and discover what remains after releasing the ball.

**Route / writing move.** Protect the wait before pickup, the crossing of the automatic line and the continued work after release. Authorship can remain unresolved without forcing every mark into a contest over ownership.

**Encounter to lean on.** `ball_painting_demo` gives “The other painter continues.” `albers_wall_gallery` offers a valuable held neighbour comparison while the canvas keeps changing out of sight; preserve that detour before considering cuts.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the two coordinate routes where both reach drawing. Keep manual brush size and snap resolution distinct from colour. The different frame and elapsed-time clocks matter, but can follow a visible instance of fading rather than interrupt the first gesture.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Grid Pallet kept one current value until an explicit rewrite. Here allow a longer overlapping duration and an incomplete return. Color Walls will then let a stable partition reorganise looking and approach.
