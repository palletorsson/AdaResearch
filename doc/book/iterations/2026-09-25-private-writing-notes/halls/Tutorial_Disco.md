# Tutorial_Disco

**Desire.** Make a beat worth waiting for and move across a pattern without surrendering one's own timing.

**Route / writing move.** Protect the slow arrival of one sequencer note after its preview. Let a second event and a deliberately kept interruption develop a rhythm before the two clocks are separated analytically.

**Encounter to lean on.** `step_sequencer` says “Keep it for a few loops before deciding.” `disco_controls` and `standalone_disco` let sound and visual animation continue independently while a sounding step still flashes a column.

**Explain / linger.** Explain modulo aliasing through steps zero and twelve only after the held floor makes the flash readable. Preserve SUPER TRIANGLE's changing scale of figure. Leave time for listening and moving; footsteps need not become measured data to matter.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Walls paused outside the onward route. This hall can enjoy returning beats without a final aphorism. Color Context then holds several choices still so the visitor, rather than the columns, supplies movement.
