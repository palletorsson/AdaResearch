# VFM_08_Arena

**Desire.** Discover whether this creature wants something from us, and how that interpretation changes when it chooses food.

**Route / writing move.** Keep the feeding pause and the visitor's expectation of a jump. Allow attachment, irritation or amusement before exposing the nearest-visible-candidate rule. The collision carts can then become a deliberate change of pace: a still comparison demanding careful identification of each body.

**Encounter to lean on.** `arena_relation_lab` grounds “arriving is not yet having eaten.” `collision_carts` offers an outcome comparison rather than repeated physical impacts. Other Arena creatures and props should retain their own configurations, not inherit this panel's claims.

**Explain / linger.** Clarify the two-second meal and five-meal state change without calling them learning. Preserve the open questions about trust and friendship. Explain momentum with both carts in view; conservation alone need not determine what a visitor values.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Legs already distinguished programmed gait from support. Spend less time rediscovering that distinction. Force as Place next admits the visitor as a receiver; let choosing YOU prepare that shift through relation and anticipation.
