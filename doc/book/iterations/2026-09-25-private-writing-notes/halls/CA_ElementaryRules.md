# CA_ElementaryRules

**Desire.** Follow a disappearing cell long enough to find out whether its place becomes available immediately.

**Route / writing move.** Keep the small suspense around the green cell with seven neighbours. Its next step can contradict or confirm the visitor's prediction before the state diagram is explained. Avoid treating every disappearance as loss.

**Encounter to lean on.** `structure_growth` makes the relevant discrepancy explicit: “Being present and being counted have parted company.” Preserve the golden address and enlarged neighbourhood; they let attention survive an absent occupant.

**Explain / linger.** Give the fading branch enough space to explain why seven neighbours cannot revive this cell yet. VIEW and DRAWN deserve shorter returns once that event is legible. The current distinction between hidden occupancy and collision is useful practical precision.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Contract the introduction's tour into two or three generations. Game of Life will then broaden a replaced present into a retained history; do not exhaust that spatial-history question here.
