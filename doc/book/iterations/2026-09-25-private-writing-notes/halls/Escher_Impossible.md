# Escher_Impossible

**Desire.** Keep going up, even while looking for the place the height must return.

**Route / writing move.** Preserve desire before explanation. The staircase's five forms can be treated as successive attempts to honour that wish, with the open run and elaborate scaffold offering different kinds of unfinishedness.

**Encounter to lean on.** `escher_staircase` asks “Which one first persuaded you that it might?” The readout, closing drop and absent fourth side make persuasion change with a specific construction.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the closed-loop height sum after one completed circuit. Let the sideways movement at `penrose_triangle` remain a crisp visual event. Keep Magritte's generous recognition and the trident's edge-following as pleasures, not a queue of faulty promises awaiting correction.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Gödel worked through formal reach and self-description. This hall lets movement answer an image. Brouwer then begins by waiting; leave enough appetite for construction that the next row's refusal to grow automatically feels like a new possibility.
