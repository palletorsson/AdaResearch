# QFEP_Sandbox

**Desire.** Send a gesture into another body, then discover what remains when its route is cut.

**Route / writing move.** The retained receiver value is the strongest turn. Begin with an unresponsive receiver, connect it, change one value, cut it, and try to undo the change. This can read as a short consequential episode rather than a tour of controls.

**Encounter to lean on.** `qfep_reactor` says “Disconnection has left a memory of your action.” The separate handle and receiver readings support that surprising persistence.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the authored mapping through one visible change in colour or speed. Preserve REPEAT's fixed animation start as a practical comparison tool. The neighbouring particle field can remain attractive while independent; it need not become one more accusation against the room's appearance.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Edge of Chaos compared calculations sharing a start. Sandbox compares connected and interrupted influence. Synthesis then brings the controls onto one contained body, replacing the route's drama with the slower question of how a changed shape settles.
