**Desire.** Find a hollow that holds something, then move among folds that seem to make a passage.

**Route / writing move.** Keep the tray and corridor as a consequential pair: one surface receives colliding spheres, the other guides attention without collision. SIZE is a good first variation because it makes a landscape relational before changing the landscape itself.

**Encounter to lean on.** `sine_flow_tray` and `sine_wall_corridor` support “The walls keep changing the invitation.” The older sine floor and explanation case can remain companion forms instead of being pruned for not carrying the main argument.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the twelve-second observation window and the chosen x-gap measurement. Linger with the half-turn passage that swerves at constant reported width. Calling a hollow shelter should remain a question requiring more evidence, while following a noncolliding fold can still be a real choice.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Pendulum spatialised a record; this room changes what surfaces do. Unit Circle next supplies explicit construction, so avoid front-loading every sine term before the tray has made contact worth explaining.
