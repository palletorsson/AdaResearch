**Desire.** Recognise the same relation while everything giving it a place drifts.

**Route / writing move.** Let one stretching edge anchor the prose. Follow it as the cube-pattern graph settles, then walk around the drawing and recognise it again without relying on left and right.

**Encounter to lean on.** `force_directed_layout` offers “every vertex in this graph has exactly three neighbours”. The regular graph makes a visually central vertex especially productive: apparent importance can change while that degree remains constant.

**Explain / linger.** Explain spring attraction, repulsion and damping as the forces making this drawing. Leave time for settling to be graceful, not merely suspect. A close encounter between unconnected vertices supplies an exact distinction between proximity and adjacency.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** The two travellers gave a stationary graph temporal rhythms. Here the drawing itself moves while the relations endure. Pathfinding then removes an actual option; this hall should leave the distinction felt clearly enough that the sinking stone changes the stakes.
