# Wavefunctions_Bernini

**Desire.** Follow a ridge upward and feel a turn held in an apparently solid thing.

**Route / writing move.** Begin with the ridge, allowing whole-column rotation to complicate it. The extra paragraph repeating low/high comparisons could be shortened to make room for intervals between shafts and the synthetic voice. Those are already different relations to repetition.

**Encounter to lean on.** `bernini_columns` and the placed `vowel_sound_board` ground “The words return”. The repeating voice can make a particular present strange while the shafts keep turning. It is a separate instrument, not an acoustic property of touching stone.

**Explain / linger.** Distinguish change through height from change through time. Keep the hum's connection to rotation accurate. Let columns, sight lines and repeated speech create an atmosphere before asking what architecture or history the procedural model cannot contain.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Effect Sound is densely adjustable; this hall can be a slower walk around a finished arrangement. John Cage next expands listening to unpredictable entrances, so let the insistent repeated phrase act as a particular bridge, not another general lesson on authorship.
