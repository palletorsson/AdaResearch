# WaveFunctions_AirMusic

**Desire.** Strike once, lift the hand, and hear something continue without further effort.

**Route / writing move.** Keep the same-bar restart and different-bar overlap as the main discovery. They make the number and age of voices meaningful through play. The return of the stick can give the ending a small choreography while the last note fades.

**Encounter to lean on.** `resonating_metallophone` earns “the sum is not a third bar.” The pipe, glass and ambient instruments remain surrounding material, but the current note should not assign them the metallophone's synthesis or input mapping.

**Explain / linger.** Explain chosen frequency, exponential decay and speed-to-level mapping after hearing the contrasts. Preserve the distinction between the never-zero envelope and the finite stored sound. Allow a pair of notes simply to sound good before asking whether the scale could have been otherwise.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** John Cage waits for generated entrances; here the hand chooses them. Sky Stairs next is visually and spatially cautious. Let this hall offer uncomplicated musical agency and listening duration before the question of bodily support returns.
