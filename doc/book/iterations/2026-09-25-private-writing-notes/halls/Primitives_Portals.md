# Primitives_Portals

**Desire.** Decide when an approach has gone far enough, and discover whether the displayed order has been deciding that on our behalf.

**Route / writing move.** Establish an expectation of improvement, then reverse the procession. Follow one chosen corner through the change before moving to Achilles' shrinking gap. The two approaches need not collapse into a single moral about infinity.

**Encounter to lean on.** `combine_portals` permits the ordering experiment: “What the earlier arrangement kept for later has become the entrance.” `achilles_tortoise` supplies a finite staged approach with separate present and target readings.

**Explain / linger.** Clearly separate the planar perimeter calculation from the torus geometry. Explain tolerance when the visitor needs a stopping criterion. Leave some attention for looking through the rings; these portals do not need an invented teleportation function.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Ignorance chose among forms; this hall questions how their sequence guides choosing. Melencolia can finally remain with a correct answer and an unfinished desire, without another numeric test at every turn.
