# VFM_09_Legs

**Desire.** Find out how little has to change for standing to become falling, then watch an apparently capable walker with new attention.

**Route / writing move.** Protect the nearly uneventful RELEASE. The quiet answer from the posts is an encounter, not a deficient spectacle. Follow with one load shift and one missing support before introducing the whole family of leg counts.

**Encounter to lean on.** `support_polygon_lab` and `four_leg_critter` make “That almost is worth watching” concrete. The authored one-, two-, three-, five-, six- and eight-legged companions are worth preserving as differentiated constructions rather than reducing the ecology to one demonstrator.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the initial support margin and finite pad width. Let the walker remain pleasurable while distinguishing a convincing gait from force-supported balance. Wider feet, tails and catching controllers are development possibilities, not available tests.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Gravity's bodies had no contact; this plate can make contact consequential. Arena next foregrounds target selection. Avoid making both creature chapters primarily an exposure of illusion: here stay with planted feet and the suspense of lifting one.
