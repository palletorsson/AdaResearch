# Trans_Rotation

**Desire.** Discover what a turn can carry, obstruct or reveal, and what counts as returning to the same form.

**Route / writing move.** Follow a changing reference: blade end, rider, cube edge, fin, pale L. These give the long hall distinct episodes. Let the static array slow the moving rides; let the tunnel become a sustained walk after the hand-scale comparisons.

**Encounter to lean on.** `rotation_wall_crossings`, `rotation_array_compare`, `transform_composition_workbench` and `axial_sweep` support “What returned depends on what you were following.” The marked cube keeps symmetry from becoming an unsupported assertion that everything is unchanged.

**Explain / linger.** Explain the platform staying upright and the order of two turns where they become consequential. Preserve a stretch simply walking the `boolean_tunnel` and watching `carousel_cake`; not every episode needs another lesson about exclusion.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Translation follows successive rides; avoid recreating that pace throughout. Scale can open with a figure, food and a bottle, giving the reader a narrative invitation after this dense comparative hall.
