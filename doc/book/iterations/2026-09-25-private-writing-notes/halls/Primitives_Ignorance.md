# Primitives_Ignorance

**Desire.** Discover something worth keeping in the allegedly inadequate sphere or capsule, instead of merely selecting the smoothest one.

**Route / writing move.** Let an initially confident judgement become more specific. Choose by eye before revealing counts; vary distance; end with the five-sided capsule whose unseen side refuses an easy prediction. The restriction can produce a desired body.

**Encounter to lean on.** `budget_of_smoothness` pairs a choice with delayed accounting, while `capsule` shifts the question from resolution to symmetry: “What would you miss if you corrected it into the capsule you expected?”

**Explain / linger.** Distinguish mesh divisions, edge overlay and viewing distance. Keep counts subordinate to a proposed use. Allow an unattributed liking for a facet; the visitor does not owe every preference an efficiency argument.

**Rhythm beside its neighbors.** Cube already unsettles a familiar name. Give this hall a curatorial rhythm of choosing and comparing. Portals should then explore the direction and stopping of refinement, rather than repeat the verdict that a finite mesh is imperfect.
