# Possessing an answer, finding a relation to it

Working note, 24 September 2026. The author's supplied draft is preserved verbatim in research-input.md. This note develops a research proposition; it is not evidence for a general historical claim about AI or education.

## The proposition

“AI changes the scarcity structure of knowledge” gives Ada a question to investigate. In this collaboration we can obtain implementations, explanations and variations faster than we can experience their consequences. An object can already work when our understanding of what it does is still beginning.

Possessing something before understanding it is not unique to AI. Instruments, inherited code, engines and tutorials already make this possible. Nor has laborious implementation always guaranteed understanding. The useful proposition is about how the balance can change: what becomes easier to obtain, and where sustained attention is still needed.

The draft's claim that answers become nearly free should remain a provocation, not a premise. Production has costs and uneven conditions of access. The differences between executing, explaining, using, interpreting and revising an algorithm deserve separate attention; conceptual understanding does not simply replace bodily or practical knowing.

## What the Trace work lets us examine

The short-memory recorder worked, but our first screenshot used a sine-generated gesture. It gave the wrong account of the encounter's source at this point in the curriculum. The new head-path artifact samples movement, then makes the loss of height visible through projection.

The desk develops a different question: can an instrument keep a pause? Its old generated handwriting looked expressive, while head movement only modulated its waveform. In this hall the measured mode gives time an axis and records head height. Constant input makes a level mark. Missing input can leave blank paper. A spatial drawing dot supplied with constant position adds no further point.

Source inspection establishes those rules. Controlled input establishes the particular behavioural contrast. Actual rendering added another finding: the tilted paper was partly inside its tabletop. The recorder had passed while its first rows were hidden. The paper is now raised clear. A headset visit still has work to do: whether the comparison can be discovered, whether the writing can be seen comfortably, and what a learner chooses to make with it.

That sequence is a useful answer to the draft's `???`: encounter the result, intervene in it, inspect what changed, and revise the account. There is no requirement that every room follow the same order.

## How to carry this forward

Start from a question already alive in an artifact or passage. Restore or strengthen an existing encounter when that can answer it. Choose a contrast a visitor can actually try. Let observations change both the implementation and the writing. Keep the uncertain part visible in the working record.

Prediction can sometimes help, but it need not become a compulsory question in every room. Play, lingering, bodily difficulty, surprise and deliberate misuse can also open an inquiry. A tutorial gives the reader enough hold on the mechanism to intervene. A critical voice follows the consequences of that intervention; a poetic voice can keep something unresolved without concealing how the artifact works.

Code remains part of the material. `_elapsed += delta` has a consequence here: a wait takes room on a page. Its brevity does not make understanding automatic. We can read it, change it, and compare the changed encounter. The work need not require every visitor to reconstruct the engine before asking a good question of it.

A possible line for the larger project, kept here for editorial consideration:

> We can produce more possible worlds than we have time to inhabit. Artistic research asks what becomes knowable by staying in one long enough to encounter its limits.

The companion political questions remain concrete: who chooses the representation, whose differences it can register, what kinds of participation it requires, and who has time to question its rules. These guide inquiry; a single successful prototype does not settle them.
