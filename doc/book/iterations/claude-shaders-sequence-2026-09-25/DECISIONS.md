# The shaders sequence, spine order 12 (25 September 2026)

Palle, 25 September: "maybe after randomness we should bring in a shader sequence so we can bring in water and other shader material into the book ... it should be introduced with the architecture of liminal spaces. pillar hall ways passage basins etc." Then "lets go".

**Placement.** Order 12, between noise (the field) and cellular automata (the rule given a memory); every sequence from cellular automata on moves up one, and the spine has 25 sequences. Not straight after randomness, because the randomness-to-noise handover is a tight pair and the noise-to-CA one was the loose seam; the new sequence's last hall, a shader given a memory through a buffer, is the natural step into CA.

**Assembled, not built from nothing.** The twelve Book of Shaders halls already existed as maps in the non-spine pattern-generation sequence, with the shader artifacts placed and registered but no chapters and no museum blocks. Six were chosen and ordered through the liminal architecture the museum already builds:

| order | hall | liminal form | museum block |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shader_01_Shaping | the passage: the exit crossing runs 14 rows instead of 7 | `passage.depth 14`, piers off |
| 2 | Shader_11_QueerRubber | the pillar hall: the one hall in the spine that keeps the template colonnade | `piers true`, wall height 4 |
| 3 | Shader_09_FBM | the basin: a 5 by 5 pool sunk 1.2 m under a glass lid, with the new water surface on it | `basin {rects [[1,2,5,5]], depth 1.2, glass true}`, token `basin_water:0:0#width:5#depth:5` at (3,4) |
| 4 | Shader_05_Patterns | the floor returned (tiling as a per-pixel fold) | piers off |
| 5 | Shader_12_PinkExtravaganza | past the intended range; its own wrong water on the floor | piers off, wall height 5 |
| 6 | Shader_10_ReactionDiffusion | a rule given a memory: the seam into cellular automata | piers off |

All six carry `artifact_placement: map` so the museum places what the maps place. The other six shader halls and the arch gallery are deferred in the sequence file with a reason each. Titles are in `map_info.title`; the chapters come next.

**New artifact: basin_water** (`commons/artifacts/basin_water/`, registry `basin_water.json`). One plane displaced in the vertex shader by three directional sines and a value noise, coloured by viewing angle; TIME only, nothing on the CPU after `_ready`; token keys width, depth, amplitude, wavelength, speed, noise_amount, lift, calm. Compile-checked in one boot. Not yet captured or walked.

**Other files.** `commons/maps/sequences/shaders.json` (spine-shaped, like noise.json); `commons/maps/curriculum_spine.json` (entry inserted after noise, orders renumbered, meta v1.5); `commons/maps/soft_stages.json` (a `shaders` stage at 8.5, the noise stage's settings with no foe of its own); `commons/maps/Lab/map_data_post_shaders.json` (a copy of the pattern-generation lab state, renamed); `commons/data/spine_artifact_order.json` regenerated by its tool (25 sequences, 202 maps, 979 artifacts). Pathfinder: all six halls 1 OK, 0 FAIL.

**Disclosure.** `curriculum_spine.json` was already modified in the tree when found (754 lines against HEAD, another writer's, uncommitted); the insertion was made on top of that copy and the file lands whole. `spine_artifact_order.json` is generated and lands as regenerated.
