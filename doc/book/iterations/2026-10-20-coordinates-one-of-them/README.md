## Fiction and limits

Choosing one ball, its particular landing apart from the others, readability from the proposed route, the later near-landing, circling and crouching, memory of an address, attachment and resentment are literary proposals. They have not been observed together in a live tower run. The lifetime and replacement are implemented behaviours, not newly invented events. Model B is not given knowledge of the timer, queue_free, instance IDs or source code. The disappearance is not asserted to be death.

The current task adds no physical capability, ball persistence, retrieval system, scene change or narrative progression system. A particular random landing and the player's actual movement through this space still need encounter observation before the prose can be described as a recorded playthrough.

## Writing process

Our previous method risked preserving an entire demonstration and attaching one unusual incident at its end. That can become another formula. Begin instead with a short record of the apparatus and a few distinctions worth inheriting, then set aside the old paragraph order. Follow one interest until it changes what the narrator does or neglects. The chapter may return to the lesson, skip parts of it, or finish elsewhere.

Draft the scene, then read it once for movement, cadence, desire and surprise without the tutorial beside it. In a separate pass, check action and knowledge against the apparatus. An ending does not need to identify what has been learned. A stray interest does not have to persist across every subsequent chapter. Comparison with the tutorial asks whether their necessary knowledge conflicts, not whether the two texts cover the same stations.

Keep an experiment that changes the scene even if it resists a neat explanation; cut an attractive sentence that merely announces the intended interpretation. For this draft, “It is easy to begin watching this one. I resent that a little” is the sentence to test with a reader: it may earn the contradiction, or may state what the preceding actions should be allowed to leave unstated.
