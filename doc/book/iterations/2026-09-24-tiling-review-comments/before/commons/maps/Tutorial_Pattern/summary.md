# Tutorial Pattern — one source, two rules

The second room in Tiling and Symmetry follows Pattern_Foundry and leads to Array_Patterns. Its primary Pattern Maker stands in an east comparison bay. One editable 4×4 motif feeds a P1 translation panel, a PM reflection panel and a floor that can switch between those rules.

Begin by finding the same asymmetric mark in both panels. Change the floor's rule without changing its cells. Then flip or rotate the source and observe all three fields. Paint a local interruption and watch repetition distribute it. Undo and reset make these comparisons reversible.

The sixteen-cell source, eight-colour palette, 40 cm tile span and fixed reference rules delimit this encounter. The original pattern, weaving and array exhibits remain supporting material. Headset and gesture verification are deferred; the book describes the implemented desktop/pointer controls.
