# Fly into the Third Index - source transfer

Codex retained all three original installations at scale 1, source configurations
and source longitudinal spacing (22 m, then 21 m). The flight volume is the
original 4 x 4 x 4 with 64 addressed pickups, not the customised 6 x 6 x 6 Array
encounter from the earlier trial. It keeps both ground doors and its 12 m glass
enclosure, X/Y/Z index controls and automatic inside/outside flight.

Open Structures keeps five forms, the 5 x 5 arrangement, stacks and alternate
rule. Reference Suspension keeps 75 fragments on three levels with roughness
controls. Their receiving surfaces and control consoles belong to the original
installations, and remain part of each movable artifact.

The whole source row is turned toward the arrival: flight at z20 facing180,
open structures at z-2 facing0, suspension at z-23 facing0. Arrival is z29.
Both shaft centres remain open; the central displays stop short of their edges.

Fit adaptation: this isolated trial uses 14 m storeys and a 72 m deep slab. This
preserves the full-size flight volume and source distances. It does not change
the main tower. Corner landing pads allow the corresponding 14 m trial descent
(20 m/s threshold). Compact arrangement and final tower fit await curation.

No original scripts, map data or chapter texts have been edited. All three
installations are independent saved placements. Native controls, flight and
collection will be checked with desktop and synthetic XR input; headset play is
reported separately.
