# What this scouting test should discover

These are Codex's provisional design candidates and source checks, kept separate from the common agent brief. They are not implementations, runtime results or conclusions from Claude/Google.

## Random Definition — a recovered distribution room

The abacus is bolted into a braced maintenance wall. Its five beads retain the same paths and rate comparisons; ink remains on the backing. Farther along, the falling number page hangs from a service frame. A glass window, the gesture recorder and the four-assignment shelf share a visible connection to the overhead supply. The old instruments seem to have been wired into the unfinished building without anybody fully understanding their purpose.

The architectural opportunity is the original `seed_replay_demo` glass comparison becoming a screen between a working aisle and the open facade. The addition must preserve comparison/readout access. It is not automatically a collision barrier. `random_return` could occupy a contained return bay; adapting its rider movement requires a separate trial before placing it over a fatal shaft.

Tutorial focus stays one retained value assigned to position, size, angle and colour. The final text's encounter route is wider: hand motion, retained digits, calculated clicks, recorded gesture and return. The old trials brief still centres the shelf; that is legitimate as a lesson focus but incomplete as a relocation brief for the whole room.

Known adaptation seams:
- `hall_seed.gd` enables sharing through `config_hall_seed` or `em_map`; it scopes to the nearest ancestor with `em_map`/`hall_seed_scope`. The Void content root already supplies `em_map`. Do not add a nested room scope to each exhibit accidentally. A shared seed does not mean a shared advancing RNG.
- `seed_replay/editor_parts.gd` uses `artifact_map_frame` for stored map-coordinate poses and sets parts top-level. Copying the original long-hall coordinates into a 42 × 18 m floor would be unsafe. Rebase them explicitly or supply trial-local placements.
- `random_return.gd::install_adapter` attaches a movement provider to XRTools player bodies. Inspect detachment, desktop behaviour and floor unload; don't treat matching visuals as input parity.
- The shelf's current program differs from the older `test_random_definition_lesson.py` extraction contract. That script expects seed-picture files, while the current tutorial marks `direct_randomness.gd`. A scout should not report that test as current shelf validation.

## Random Noise Types — a wall that admits positions and views

Keep the two framed point clouds together in an open inspection bay. Rehouse their frames as repaired equipment cages, preserving matched volumes, seeds and controls. Minimum spacing applies when accepting positions; it does not become a continuous repulsion force after the visitor moves a point.

Use `related_weather` as the strong architectural encounter: its three viewing walls become a passage that changes what the visitor can see towards a distant fixed marker or open shaft. Its glass collision stays fixed while the sight slits move. That distinction is the interest; it must not become a promised door that the player can walk through.

The original `geometry_noise` comparison and noise-colour galleries can remain secondary inspections, with their exact configuration and scale evaluated before placement. The tutorial remains rejection sampling and finite attempts. Temporal signal relationships belong to another encounter; placing them nearby does not make them the same concept.

## Noise Columns — strange inserts in a load-bearing frame

A rigid steel transfer frame holds the visible construction above. Beneath it, three existing Bernini shafts deform side by side, with an intentional separation between their capitals and the actual support. Temporary braces, fresh bolts and an old veined surface make the repair visible. Feed the control instrument from the ceiling station; keep all three silhouettes readable from one approach.

The shafts retain fixed/periodic/coherent drivers, local FREEZE, SPIN, MARBLE and delayed REVEAL. They do not carry the floor. An alternative structural study could later drive a separately collidable test panel with the same scalar, but that is new behaviour with separate tests.

The return to Primitives is concrete: rings and triangle faces receive a value. The return to Transformation is whole-body rotation, distinct from deformation. Preserve the common geometry/material/range so staging does not reveal the hidden driver prematurely or confuse the comparison.

## Noise One — one field, two architectural readings

Mount the existing paired rings in a service bay so they can be seen together, like two apertures in an unfinished machine. One carries relief and the other colour. Their probes, FREEZE, REMAP, AMPLITUDE and FREQUENCY remain accessible. Use a shallow construction recess for the existing terrain basin, with inspection access and visible supply routing.

The meaningful structural candidate is the terrain's actual mesh/collider pair. It could inform an inserted patch of ground; enlarging it or making it part of the required walking route is a proposed adapter, not a placement-only change. The torus shader relief cannot support a foot.

Keep the same sampling frame when the instrument moves. A colour remap must change its reading without changing the field. The terrain's LOW/MIDDLE/HIGH controls isolate supplied resources before the common slope-lowering pass; they are not automatically octave bands. The orb's independent pulse can remain a small comparison.

## First build after the scouts

Prefer Random Noise Types if the goal is to test architectural integration quickly: its existing sight-wall encounter already changes space without needing a new power or moving-platform simulation. Prefer Random Definition if the goal is to test the hardest state/identity handoff first. The choice should be made from the scout evidence, not from how impressive its prose sounds.

No layout coordinates in these candidates have been measured in the tower. No floors were added or changed.
