# Review each scout against the same questions

Record **supported / partly supported / unsupported / not assessed**, plus a source or an explanation. Do not turn this into a single percentage that certifies design quality.

1. **Actual inventory:** exact map, artifact, branch/config and source references; no invented IDs. Accounts for the wider field without making every object a tutorial.
2. **Teaching continuity:** names what returns, what changes, what the student can now do and a specific demonstration. Distinguishes whole-map encounter from learner reconstruction.
3. **Architectural use:** explains a staged home and a structural/spatial role. A frame, panel or ground patch has a reason beyond decoration.
4. **Physics truth:** distinguishes mesh, collision, movement and appearance. No supporting columns or walkable ring relief invented.
5. **Supply truth:** uses the four real ceiling stations; identifies current decorative wiring. Any power interaction has a new explicit contract.
6. **Frames and scale:** floor-local coordinates, shaft/pillar/route clearance, measured versus provisional dimensions. Rebases old map-coordinate parts.
7. **State and identity:** seed sharing/local streams, record ownership, copy isolation, held-state guards, unload/reload and death. Doesn't silently reset a kept experiment.
8. **Editor and input:** editable group/member identities, real model export, save/reload and desktop/VR behaviour. Doesn't equate a web model with a functioning interaction.
9. **Falsifiability:** setup/action/expected result; at least one deliberately wrong variant; no invented passes or screenshot claims.
10. **Interpretation:** old alien technology is an authored staging choice; preserves scientific distinctions and leaves the reflective text open.
