# AI: a room that can change

11 October 2026. Author's standpoint adopted from the supplied note. The encounters below are editorial proposals grounded in the existing spine; they are not completed lessons or newly tested exhibits.

## The undertaking

Ada uses AI to make a world in which algorithms can be encountered. That makes its own production part of the inquiry. We want to learn what a system does, experience what it makes possible, and ask how its arrangement could be otherwise. Pleasure, competence, uncertainty and critique can belong to the same encounter.

The critical thread must keep moving between a particular technical operation and the relations that sustain and direct it. Do not make today's failure a permanent definition of AI. Do not let a general account of power or infrastructure replace an explanation of the actual model. A critique should be able to change with its object.

This does not make present failures unimportant. A dated, demonstrated failure remains evidence of what happened under those conditions, including consequences that a later improvement does not undo. Equally, success can change dependence, authority and what people expect from one another. Keep both available for investigation.

Relational does not mean equal power. Name who can change the objective, see the data, retain the records, withdraw access, refuse a decision or bear its consequences. Having an effect in a system does not by itself establish intention, consciousness or moral equivalence. We can investigate a relation without settling those questions first.

## One recurring action

**Encounter → predict → change one condition → inspect what happened → trace who or what enabled it → revise the claim.**

The student carries a small record of this encounter: object/version, examples, rule or objective, prediction, observation, interpretation, dependency and an open question. This is a proposed notebook interface. Initially it can be a short file alongside the working code; it need not add more controls to every room.

Keep types distinct: a measurement is an observation; an implementation explanation needs source evidence; an analogy is an interpretation; a social consequence needs evidence from the people and setting concerned. A small classifier cannot prove a claim about all AI or about a social group.

## Through the existing eight halls

Each row proposes one primary tutorial construction. Other artifacts can remain encounters in the reflective track or in the museum alone.

| Hall | Proposed construction and carried material | Critical encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ML_Evolution | Reuse a small population of familiar bodies; expose inherited parameters, variation and an explicit survival or selection condition. Hold the beginning while changing that condition. | A body becomes successful in an environment. Who arranged the resources? Which capacities become useful when those conditions change? Avoid treating survival as an inherent ranking of worth. |
| ML_Gradient_Landscape | Use recorded point positions as a small authored dataset; fit a bounded straight-line predictor. Show prediction, residual, loss and update. Reserve observations never used in fitting. | The same observations can support different judgements of error. Who chooses what a costly error is? Better fit does not decide whether the objective is appropriate. |
| ML_Classification | Keep an inspectable point collection and implement one clustering procedure. Compare assignments under changed feature scaling or K while keeping the observations. | The partition is produced by data, representation and procedure. A geometric cluster does not arrive with a social name. A more stable partition can still serve an objectionable use. |
| ML_Neural_Networks | Reuse the prediction/evaluation contract in a tiny fully printed trainable network. Distinguish transient activation, persistent weights, training error and held-out error. | Let a genuine improvement be visible. Then ask what that improvement licenses someone to delegate, what it leaves untested, and who controls the examples and targets. |
| ML_Perception | Build one real local image operation with editable input and computed output. Treat supplied recognition labels as a separately identified display, or remove them from the small study. | Change the image and follow which claims change with it. A label must be connected to evidence; an accurate label also has a context of use. The unseen and the unrepresented are not necessarily the same thing. |
| ML_Sequence_Memory | Carry a short input history and a small explicit state update. Keep the hand-set gates as a model one can predict. Name its difference from a trained sequence model. | Compare what is retained, admitted and exposed. Apply this distinction to Ada's compressed instructions: what was kept, who edited it, and what disappeared? Hand-set state is not evidence of learned memory. |
| ML_Generative | Choose one bounded generative mechanism with visible reference examples, actual changing parameters and inspectable candidates. Scope it before writing; do not promise GAN, VAE and diffusion in one lesson. | Explore what becomes possible, including pleasure and unexpected forms. Change the reference set or evaluator and examine whose aesthetic judgement is being reproduced. Trace provenance without treating every resemblance as documented influence. |
| ML_Synthesis | Return to the point collection and build one transparent anomaly score with adjustable reference population and threshold. Use the same point before and after the change. | Keep score, decision and consequence separate. Who can challenge an exclusion? Change the decision policy even when the score is correctly calculated. Close toward Foundations Crisis, not back toward Graph as a next room. |

The proposed point-data transfer must become real before a lesson claims it: specify the record, units, coordinate frame and save/load path. Until then, say that a supplied sample stands in for a visitor's record. The familiar sphere may return as an input or probe without pretending one live object persists across all scenes.

## Two connections the route still needs

**A bridge to the generative AI used in Ada.** The current eight primary encounters do not explain the language-model workflow producing this book. Attention and Transformer artifacts exist in the registry, while dedicated maps are deferred. Inspect those implementations before choosing one concise bridge after Sequence Memory or as a companion encounter. It should distinguish supplied weights from training, context from retained parameters, token generation from checking a claim, and model output from tool execution. A small attention calculation can reveal an operation; it cannot stand in for a complete account of an LLM. The original Transformer paper is a technical reference for that operation, not an explanation of every contemporary system. [Vaswani et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).

**A view of the system that makes the room possible.** Pair one working object with its actual dependencies: source material, authorship and edits, saved files, model/service where known, hardware and access arrangements. Let the player or reader consider changes such as unavailable network access, a different model, exported local records or a changed evaluator. Identify which are actual tests and which are scenarios. An account of energy, labour, licensing or ownership needs sourced evidence; do not manufacture numerical costs or hidden supply-chain details.

These connections should receive enough space to be understood. If each requires a new operation, a dedicated hall may be clearer than adding further load to Memory or Synthesis. This brief proposes that decision; it does not alter the route.

## The thread through the whole museum

Earlier encounters prepare this inquiry without turning every chapter into an AI lecture. Trace asks what a record preserves. Arrays and Classification ask how a representation groups things. Forces exposes consequences beyond intention. Tiling and the loom make instructions, making and labour perceptible. Shaders separates an image from the system that produces it. CA keeps an old state while calculating another. Graphs makes connection, cost and available routes explicit. ML changes some of the rules or parameters through examples and feedback. Foundations can revisit what these formal operations do and do not settle.

At selected returns, ask two questions together: **what capacity did we gain, and what relation now supports or constrains that capacity?** Keep the concrete construction primary in the tutorial. Let final.md follow a particular encounter rather than recite the same theoretical checklist in every hall.

## Ada is evidence, too

Keep a few actual production cases: an AI contribution that enabled a new artifact; an instruction that faded; an apparently complete program with an undeclared dependency; a working example reconstructed by the ghost student; a human correction that changed the curriculum. Preserve what was requested, produced, tested, corrected and accepted, including the model/version when known. Do not backfill unknown metadata.

An AI-generated lesson and an AI reviewer can share an assumption. A fresh-context reader offers useful separation, while executable assertions test specified behaviour. Neither is proof of human learning or an impartial stamp on the project's philosophical claims. A physical encounter and another person's reading can disagree with both. Those disagreements belong in the record.

## Writing and verification contract

`tutorial.md` gives context, the complete smallest working construction and one operation to own. `final.md` develops its reflective encounter, including the relations relevant to that particular object. Production notes record how AI participated. These tracks meet through evidence; they do not substitute for one another.

For each future ML lesson: inspect the current hall; settle one primary construction; write the two texts; reconstruct exact printed code in Godot; test the predicted operation and an independently chosen variation; then read the reflection against what actually happens. Keep failed attempts and identify whether the prediction, test or program failed. If a measured capability improves, revise that claim and revisit which relational questions remain.

First implementation pass: **ML_Evolution**, followed by a complete **ML_Gradient_Landscape** learner/evaluator. The first hall connects to population history; the second establishes the compact prediction/loss/update/evaluation contract carried into later learning. Before expanding the whole sequence, decide where the language-model and dependency encounters belong. No new implementation or ghost-student pass is claimed by this brief.

## Intellectual company, with distinctions intact

Latour's move toward matters of concern supports attending to how a technical object is assembled while retaining the demand for evidence. Applying that to Ada is our interpretation. [Latour, “Why Has Critique Run Out of Steam?”](https://www.bruno-latour.fr/fr/node/165.html).

Andreotti and GTDF explicitly experiment with relational engagement with AI and question inherited separations. Their published exchange includes AI-authored passages. Read it as a situated philosophical experiment; its first-person machine language is not independent evidence of machine consciousness or of measured ecological cost. Ada can learn from its questions without treating its conclusions as prerequisites for participation. [GTDF, “Entangled Relationality in AI Kin-Machine Engagement”](https://decolonialfutures.net/entangled-relationality-artificial-intelligence/).

## Compact instruction for a future AI

Existing Endless Museum. AI critique is a central revisable inquiry. Follow exact operation + enabling relations + consequences; include successful capabilities. Do not universalise a dated failure or dissolve the model into generic power. Name unequal control, dependency and possible alternatives. Tutorial: one real construction, complete code, carried object, original/variation assertions. Final: situated reflection; separate observation, mechanism, analogy and normative judgement. Reuse good writing. ML drafts are not validated complete lessons. Graph → ML → Foundations. Attention/LLM and infrastructure bridges need inspection and placement; no silent reordering. Record Ada's own AI successes, failures, corrections and verification limits. Revise claims when evidence changes.
