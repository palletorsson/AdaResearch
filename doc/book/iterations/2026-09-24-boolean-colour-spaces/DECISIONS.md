# Boolean colour spaces — built study, 24 September 2026

## The encounter

The author's three reference images propose dense curved interiors in pearl pink/blue, molten amber and reflective dark chrome. Built three full-scale constructions with four selectable finishes, using native Godot geometry and shaders. The same native images now appear in the study chapter and review page.

1. **Path.** A box minus a union of seven overlapping spherical cutters, with two higher cuts. This develops the previously located path-carving mechanism: positions along a line leave somewhere to go.
2. **Intersection.** A box minus the region shared by two spheres. Cylindrical entry and exit cuts and an upper light well connect the lens-shaped void to the exterior.
3. **Shell.** Two joined outer spheres minus smaller joined volumes, doorway cylinders, five pores and a bottom trim. The openings distinguish passage from visibility.

All three have a separate level floor. Entrance plinths offer PEARL, AMBER, CHROME and CHALK, plus optional slow surface drift. Finishes also change the local light colours. Material changes do not rebuild geometry. Drift holds its current phase when stopped.

The first amber and chrome renders were too strongly patterned. Reduced their contrast and replaced the rectangular entrances with cylindrical cuts, then reran the full bounded probe. The supplied images are the resulting native renders, not generated concept art.

## What the evidence establishes

`run.py` copies the production artifact and standard XR pointer event class into an isolated Godot project. `probe.gd` checks a 1.8 m tall / 0.30 m radius CharacterBody capsule through each passage in both directions, support at the floor, a solid flank, ray access to the four material controls, pointer selection, unchanged mesh faces after finish selection, and drift activation/deactivation. It records the actual rendered views.

All 70 recorded checks pass. This includes image-write checks and triangle-count records, not 70 independent user scenarios. Boundary triangle counts: path 4,682; lens 1,104; shell 6,246. These counts are not a Quest performance measurement. `manifest.json` verifies that the current production scripts/scenes/shader match the copies used for this run.

The standalone scene can be opened and run with F6. It uses a small desktop study controller. The new map's full museum-loader behavior, actual museum player, tracked VR controller, headset comfort and Quest rendering have not been tested. Pointer-event compatibility is source/runtime evidence, not a headset claim.

## What the surfaces do

Position-based procedural patterns, view-dependent colour, metallic/roughness changes and local lights. No wall displacement, screen-space refraction or physical thin-film simulation is claimed. Reflective appearance depends on the surrounding lighting/environment; the museum may require a lighting adjustment after placement.

The writing stays with the specific experience: the skin suggests one body, contact gives another account. A hole can admit a body, a glance or light. These relations give critical and poetic work to the construction without declaring irregular geometry inherently liberating.
