# Color_Flashlight — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (critical slot, color thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

— none. The room is the artifact.

## The walk

An 11×12 room with no exhibits, and that is the whole argument. The colorizer is enabled; the light is yours. Color here exists only where you point — surfaces hold their hue in the dark the way a tree falls in the empty forest, which is to say: the question is wrong, and this room makes you feel why. You walk a space whose palette is conjured by attention and gone the moment attention leaves.

## The turn (critical)

The chapter's truth — *color is perception, not physics* — delivered with the furniture removed so nothing can soften it. RGB was a lie we agree on; here even the agreement needs your flashlight to exist. The critical room of a chapter about seeing is, correctly, a room about the seer. Whether this emptiness is the finished design or a stage awaiting its one instrument is an open call — but the emptiness *works*, and the text votes to keep it.
