# Find the colour again

Color_Grid_Pallet follows Pillar's spatial samples and precedes Paint's accumulating marks. Its primary encounter is `gridcolorizer`, configured with `#lesson:addresses`.

The original 19 by 21 metre grid remains the receiving surface. A standing console at (7,15), centred at 1.15 m, selects cells with x and z from 8 through 12. X + and Z + cycle independently; COLOUR writes one of four inks into the selected instance. The outlined cell and cased RGB swatch reveal what is selected and stored. Moving the selection does not move its colour.

RULE rewrites the interior floor from `(x + z) % palette.size()`. PALETTE cycles Stonewall Freedom, Starry Night and Mondrian Grid collections through the same rule. Walls, transforms and collision are not changed. Reapplying a rule replaces local edits; the lesson has no undo history or save file.

The old NextCube was removed from this room in favour of local console controls. The science screen moves to (15,15), leaving the approach open. Spectrum forest and the existing transport cube remain secondary context. This is a colour-assignment lesson on the existing floor, not a separate pattern machine.
