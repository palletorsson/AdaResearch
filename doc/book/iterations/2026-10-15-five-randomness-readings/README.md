15 October 2026. Palle asks how to preserve good ideas and produce five very different complete texts for handoff. These five drafts answer that request as an exploratory comparison. They are authored by the same situated assistant, not an independent writer. No chapter or calibration set is adopted.

## The sixth candidate

F was composed on 15 October after a full reading of A–E (reading order: strategy notes first, then A–E, then evidence — a contaminated order, declared). It is a proposal like the others, not an adoption, and it names its sources: D as the base register (durations; its page-weakness of printed pacing becomes a hall-strength where durations are real), C's suspicion as the through-line, B's "the document and the clicks have different jobs" at the Geiger, A's unkept release point at Out / Back, and E's two closing paragraphs as the exit. The choice of register converges with the same-day composition pilot, where the patient (duration-voiced) Geiger passage won on the same grounds — two experiments, one varying a passage and one varying whole forms, pointing at the same voice. The MOVE / KEEP condition applies to F exactly as to A–E.
