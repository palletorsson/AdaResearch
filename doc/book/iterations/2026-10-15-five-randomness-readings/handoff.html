Handoff: six complete alternatives

(F was added after a reading of A–E and names its sources in the README; read it last, since it will spoil the five for a fresh reader.)

Purpose: read or revise five full Random Definition candidates and retain useful ideas without prematurely choosing a house style. The five texts were written by the same situated assistant. Palle requested this exploration; no candidate or calibration set has been adopted.

Start with a complete text before reading the strategy notes. The five share factual constraints, a local “we” voice and the selected encounter order. They change several compositional choices together. This comparison can reveal promising directions but cannot isolate a single device as the cause of a response.

For each complete reading, report:

  1. A sentence or event that remains interesting, with a specific reason.
  2. A place where the next encounter becomes necessary, or fails to.
  3. Something the text makes you want to observe, compare, keep or rebuild.
  4. What the chapter loses through its chosen form.
  5. Reading order, prior exposure and whether you read the complete chapter.

An uninteresting passage, desire to stay, or rejection of the whole account belongs in the record. Do not reward a text for matching its stated intention. Keep literary response separate from factual correction; after the literary reading, check claims against evidence.md. Return both readings without silently repairing the text you evaluated.

The idea bank contains reported points of attention, technical tensions and proposals, with their sources and limits. It is editorial material you can question, not a required argument. Preserve a useful idea even when its draft fails. Propose a coherent next chapter rather than assuming the best paragraphs will assemble themselves.

Known apparatus dependency: MOVE / KEEP is implemented but currently removed from the saved map. These candidates describe a proposed restored five-stop walk. Do not treat them as testimony that the complete route is installed. The current abacus exposes only REPLAY; reconstruction helpers must not become invented public controls.

The full archive includes five Markdown/HTML texts, comparison pages, idea bank, evidence and this brief. Notes entered in the pages are retained locally in that browser; export the JSON to send them back. They are not automatically written into the repository bank.

For a fresh independent composition without exposure to these five interpretations, use evidence-only.zip, which contains the updated dossier and a neutral brief. Select calibration examples separately. Record exactly what was supplied. Nothing in this preparation sends a message to an external writer or another chat.
