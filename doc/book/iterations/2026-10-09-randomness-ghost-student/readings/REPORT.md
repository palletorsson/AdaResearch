## What changed

**Entropy starts with the experiment.** Count, copy, sort and compare neighbours. Two known inputs check H = 2 and H = 0. The full calculation remains in the program and is explained in a second-reading note. It can be understood more deeply after the student has seen what its input excludes.

**Each tutorial points into the relevant code.** The new operation remains visible with its complete implementation. Familiar PointBody code is consistently expandable; Walk already groups its inherited point and line constructions together. Every required file is included. Folding a known construction changes the reading, not the dependency.

**The late changes of purpose are named.** Neighbours share a field; draws supply evidence; visits change later choices; fields combine at an address. These short openings connect the lesson to its predecessor without treating every hall as an extension of the same algorithm.

**Pheromone follows one update.** Fade, deposit, count, choose, move. One completed step gives one departure visit and two recorded positions. The reader establishes that order before batching steps or comparing attraction with avoidance.

**The reflections lose repeated instructions.** Entropy keeps the distinction between an arrangement and its measure; Walk keeps the separation between a trail and a rule that reads it; Game keeps the relation between a decision, its disclosure and its consequence. Numerical and operating qualifications move into notes. Shorter cuts also remove repeated prompts in Space Geometry, Pheromone and Space. All existing artifact markers and images remain.

The accompanying change table gives approximate word counts, including notes and excluding code where indicated. A shorter text is not in itself proof of a better lesson; the review brief asks a reader to judge what can now be made, understood and questioned.

## Checks and limits

All fourteen current printed baseline examples were extracted into fresh lesson folders and run in Godot 4.6: **420 assertions passed**. Every GDScript block, including suggested small edits, matches the text before this pass. These are fresh runs through existing probes, not a new independent learner trial.

The package contains both complete reading tracks, the lesson files, before/after differences, test reports, logs and a brief explaining where Randomness sits in the book. It retains the assumption that the student is working inside the prepared museum. The route and production artifacts are outside this editorial change.

The useful next question for the reviewer is whether the capacity accumulates at a readable pace. We have not added another lesson or required the reflective text to teach every artifact. We have made the chosen path easier to find.
