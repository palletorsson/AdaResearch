Thin coloured lines. Each stroke has its own colour and finite thickness.

The face is an authored example. It is made from separate paths, not an underlying head model. Switching modes retains those paths; the dots are sampled along them by distance.

Make a drawing.

Open the downloadable Godot project and run. Press C to start empty. Left-drag draws; a click leaves one point. Release to lift the pen. B switches between lines and light dots.

X changes the next colour. Right-drag moves around the drawing; Shift + wheel changes drawing depth. [ / ] change size, G toggles glow, and Z undoes. F5 / F9 save and load locally.

The drawing plane is a desktop way of giving a gesture depth. This scene does not yet read a tracked hand or pressure-sensitive pen. The dots emit colour and bloom on screen; they do not light the surrounding room.
