# QFEP Introduction - Summary

## Overview
The opening map of the QFEP Laboratory sequence presents the complete Quantized Free Energy Principle formula — QFE = F - lambda E(S) + phi delta E(S,t) — as a walkable 3D structure at the center of a laboratory space. Five terms rendered as spatial geometry, each colored and positioned so the learner can walk beneath the equation, reach into its components, and begin to understand what every prior sequence was building toward.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 10x12 grid with concentric elevation (height 1 perimeter, height 2 inner zone, height 3 core platform)
- **Architecture**: Laboratory with the formula floating at center, grabbable spheres distributed around it
- **Navigation**: Elevator at (4,4) provides overview; teleporter at south edge leads to QFEP_F_Term

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **qfep_formula_3d** — The equation rendered as navigable geometry, each symbol a physical object
- **grab_sphere_F** — Dense, heavy sphere representing the structure term; triggers memory projections of Primitives and Transformation sequences
- **grab_sphere_E** — Light, drifting sphere representing entropy; triggers projections of Randomness and Noise sequences
- **grab_sphere_lambda** — Balance parameter sphere; triggers projections of edge-of-chaos maps
- **lambda_slider** and **phi_slider** — First interactive parameter controls in the QFEP sequence

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at (1,1), elevator, teleporter to QFEP_F_Term

## Learning Sequence
1. Enter the laboratory and encounter the full formula floating overhead
2. Walk beneath the equation — pass through the minus sign gap, stand under each term
3. Grab individual spheres to isolate terms and trigger memory projections of prior sequences
4. Adjust lambda and phi sliders; watch the formula and environment respond
5. Recognize that every prior sequence corresponds to a term: Primitives are F, Randomness is E, the edge maps are lambda
6. Exit through the teleporter toward the F term deep dive

## Design Intent
The formula is not introduced as new content but as a name for what the learner already knows. Every phenomenon encountered across the curriculum — crystal order, random chaos, oscillation between them — maps to a term. The introduction's power comes from retrospective coherence: recognition rather than novelty. The grabbable spheres democratize the abstraction by making it physical.
