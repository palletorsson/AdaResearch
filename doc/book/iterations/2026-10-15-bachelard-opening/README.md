# Bachelard opening trial · 15 October 2026

[Read the opening](opening.md) · [Strategy collection](../../course/writing-patterns/AUTHOR_STRATEGIES.md) · [Current chapter](../../../../commons/maps/Random_Definition/final.md)

Palle requested a new opening from the moving spheres to the RAND book: scientific, poetic and close to the player's actual encounter. After trying several perspectives he responded to the “we” version: “ok I think that voice works.” The shared voice is now the chosen working voice for this opening, which replaces the chapter's first two encounters in the canonical final. The [previous chapter](before-adoption.md) and earlier opening variants remain available. The six-stop order and tutorial are unchanged.

The Bachelard adaptation is attention to a measured space becoming inhabited, with an exact relation available for questioning. It is original Ada prose, not an imitation or quotation. The described actions propose a possible VR encounter; they are not participant observations. No fear, delight or bodily resistance is attributed to the player.

Palle's revisions first removed direct address, then clarified first person, then tried thought without an identified speaker, and finally chose “we”. The opening uses a shared narrative voice moving through the encounter. This does not assert multiplayer carrying or a participant study, and it leaves emotional responses open. The [addressed version](opening-addressed.md), [external-observer version](opening-observer.md), [first-person version](opening-first-person.md) and [unnamed-voice version](opening-unnamed.md) remain available for comparison. His judgement selects a local working voice; it does not settle every sentence or establish a universal voice for every Ada text.

## Selected movement and reserves

The local route is visible extent → carried viewpoint → controlled variations → repeatable positions → withdrawal → selective retention → historical record → attention to what remains. Detailed generator construction, source provenance, statistical tests and sensor acquisition remain in the wider chapter or tutorial rather than becoming separate branches in this opening.

| Paragraphs | Writing action and intended effect | Bridge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Establish the bare lines, then change the reader's relation through touch. Keep the measured extent legible while the viewpoint moves. | A known extent leaves the next turn open to investigation. |
| 3 | Compare shared positions at two rates, then added height. Distinguish changes of timing and permitted direction. | A comparison raises the practical possibility of returning to a movement. |
| 4–5 | Withdraw from the sphere, then distinguish the unmarked line from positions retained in the program. Briefly define generator and seed before replaying. | The next touch changes another object's behaviour. |
| 6–7 | Describe descending rows, amber selection and persistence after withdrawal. Give the reader time with the group. | Keeping a number opens the historical practice of keeping many for use. |
| 8–9 | Introduce the historical table with its preparation, distinguish the simulation, and return to the selected group. | Further reading can investigate production or selection; the reader may also want to linger. |

## Evidence and limits

- [Sphere record](../../../../commons/artifacts/random_touch/motion_record.gd): shared X positions, rates 0.8 / 1.6 / 0.8, separate Y values; replayable generated targets.
- [Sphere interaction](../../../../commons/artifacts/random_touch/random_touch.gd) and [existing trial](../2026-10-15-touch-lines/README.md): waiting, carrying, withdrawal and return. World collisions can alter the avatar's actual route. The draft promises a return to the sphere's turn, not identical embodied experience.
- [Falling-page implementation](../../../../algorithms/randomness/randomnumbergeneration/scripts/random_number_book_page_1995.gd): row stream, persistent freeze toggle on touch, amber state. The filename's year is a legacy mismatch; the scene and historical reference are 1955.
- [Map settings](../../../../commons/maps/Random_Definition/map_data.json): row stream enabled, cadence 0.4 rows per second. The visible digits are generated locally, not copied from the historical RAND table.
- [RAND introduction and production account](https://www.rand.org/pubs/monograph_reports/MR1418.html): electronic source, observed biases, transformation and published record. The opening paraphrases this briefly.

This writing-only trial uses source inspection and the existing artifact checks. Palle's response establishes that the shared voice works for him at this point. It does not establish headset feel, independent reader understanding or a universal literary model. The transition's remaining uncertainty is whether the reversal of touch earns the move to the page, or whether another reader wants to investigate the spheres further.
