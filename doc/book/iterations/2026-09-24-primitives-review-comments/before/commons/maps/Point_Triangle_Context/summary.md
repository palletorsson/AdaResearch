# Point_Triangle_Context — boundary and face

The first passage uses two primary artifacts. At `triangle_line_puzzle`, close three edges, inspect the empty centre, then toggle the fill. At `draw_triangle_faces`, draw a triangle before comparing two starts of the same concave outline. A boundary and a filling procedure are separate constructions.

## Learning progression

1. Bring Grid's repeatable positions into a closed three-edge boundary.
2. Show and hide its face while retaining the edges; compare views from both sides.
3. Place a new loop with the drawing point and read the distinction between filled loops and triangles.
4. Keep an L-shaped boundary fixed and restart from its next corner. Locate where the first-vertex fan overspills.
5. Carry boundary, face and filling rule into the next question: how do faces enclose a volume?

The side instrument counts completed constructions. Each six-corner L adds one loop and four logical triangles; NEW OUTLINE preserves earlier work. Counts do not certify a correct fill.
