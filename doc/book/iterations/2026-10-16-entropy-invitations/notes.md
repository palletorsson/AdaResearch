## The next human test

Approach an encounter without an instruction to press anything. Record the first thing the visitor wants to inspect or change, whether they find a relevant control, whether they can activate it once and whether they see its consequence. Do not supply the desired next artifact. A wish to stay, a different action, or no action is evidence. Distinguish a control that cannot be reached from a question that does not interest the visitor.

The final narrates one possible investigation. A successful software test cannot turn it into a transcript of an actual walk. Technical operating details remain in the tutorials.
