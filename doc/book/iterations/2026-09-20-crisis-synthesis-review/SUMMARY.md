## Reading and theory

The recurring formula is not a scalar value computed by this display. Its colours and calibration markings are authored interpretations. F needs a model; E needs states/probabilities; a rate needs a time interval; signs need an objective direction. The independent recurrence makes a different kind of claim, with a reproducible update rule and explicitly finite evidence.

The mathematical references in the chapter are [Rutgers' logistic-map teaching notes](https://www.physics.rutgers.edu/grad/509/Logistic%20map.html) and [Feigenbaum's 1978 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01020332). The methodological connection is to Ada's [Entropic Morality](../../../research/qfep/entropic-morality.md). This is an editorial articulation of the project's proposal, not a claim that the cited mathematics entails its ethical position.
