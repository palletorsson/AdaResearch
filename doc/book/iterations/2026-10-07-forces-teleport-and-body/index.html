One body, different directions.

The small LOOK marker follows the head. The arrows represent world directions; their numerical components stay in world X, Y and Z. Turning your head changes how you see a vector, not the vector itself. Release the triggers: thrust becomes zero while velocity continues and gravity changes it.

ReadingWhat the instrument knows
Velocity / m/sThe actual player-body velocity after movement and contacts.
Thrust F/m / m/s²The same acceleration supplied by the held spheres to the movement provider. This player model has no declared physical mass, so the visor does not invent a force in Newtons.
Gravity / m/s²The court's constant downward acceleration, 9.8 m/s². A floor can support you while this input remains present.
Launch noticeThe pad sets an initial velocity. It is distinguished from continuous thrust.

This is a selective instrument. Contact forces, walking control and every part of lived movement are not measured here. Arrow lengths are bounded to fit the visor; the numbers retain the magnitude. The overlay reads the movement system without changing it.
