What this comparison establishes

The source JSON is byte-for-byte extracted from Git. The renderer built every archived structure cube and all 12 artifact placements, with no missing-artifact placeholders. The report includes hashes and the positions produced by the current loader.

For photography, the camera uses the JSON’s ambient and directional lighting and a plain background in its specified colour; artifact lights remain. Player HUD and biome scenery are omitted. Shared materials, artifact geometry, grounding and animation are current. This verifies the recovered layouts, not historical VR behaviour.
