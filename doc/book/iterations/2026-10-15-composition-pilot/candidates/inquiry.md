The Geiger machine is clicking. On its screen:

> seed 435

The colour window displays the same number in large figures. Beside the virtual lamps, a capture also carries seed 435. The opening hand’s path began from it. The number recurs; what it sets in motion changes.

We press RESTART. A click, the same gap, another click.

The returning gap gives us something to recognise. It does not disclose how the gap was made. The screen supplies a number; the sound supplies a sequence. Neither is the calculation itself.

This machine detects no radiation. Its waiting times are calculated. We are listening to the result of an operation that has already taken place.
