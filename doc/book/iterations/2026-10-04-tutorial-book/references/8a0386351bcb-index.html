What the work holds, and what it leaves out

The supplied strokes are authored beginnings. The skin remains visual geometry. Pipe bores are open; cable sag is a drafted shape, not a rope simulation. Shared sockets create logical connections; the fitting meshes are not Boolean-welded plumbing, and no liquid or audio flows through them. Wall attachment fixes an endpoint; crossing alone does not join runs. Up to eight pieces and runs and sixteen undo states live in this hall instance. They are not saved after unloading it.

Sampling retains actual input positions, with a 0.045 m minimum gap and 256-point limit per stroke. The supplied frame transport is available to inspect without becoming the first exercise.
