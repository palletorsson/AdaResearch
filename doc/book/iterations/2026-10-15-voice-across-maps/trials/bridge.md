The tile can still be seen when it can no longer carry us. Below its faint surface lies a shallow basin, ruled with the same grid. The place has kept its address. What it offers a body has changed.

RESTORE gives the bridge its full surface again. With SOURCE set to VISITOR and RUN active, we approach the cell marked W. A gold frame marks the corresponding cell on the upright panels beside the path. The same changing colour appears at foot level and in front of us.

The number on the desk falls as we remain nearby. The instrument calls it health. Below 0.2, the cell stops supplying collision support: the simulation no longer lets that surface hold a body up. Its colour becomes faint, but the tile remains visible. If we are standing there, the basin receives us. On the panels beside us, the gold frame has stayed at the same height.

Our weight has not been measured. The rule reads distance from the tracked body's position. It can respond before a foot reaches the tile. With SOURCE set to PROBE, a pink marker supplies a position instead; no person needs to stand above the cell for its number to fall.

The bridge makes an explanation almost bodily available: a surface gives way beneath someone. The probe lets us question which part of that explanation the apparatus actually uses.

We can withdraw. Recovery depends on neighbouring cells whose values remain high enough to contribute. A completely depleted field has no such neighbours. Leaving it alone is then insufficient. After finding where support ends, there is another boundary to investigate: what must remain for it to return.
