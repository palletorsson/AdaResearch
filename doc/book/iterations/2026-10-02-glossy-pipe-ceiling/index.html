Construction and limits

The widest, densest weave retains separation between crossing surfaces. All 27 offered layout combinations keep the pipes above 5.8 m and within the bay. Geometry rebuilds only when a layout control changes; finish changes reuse it. A freestanding frame carries the hangers.

These are visual hollow pipes. The rib joints share logical endpoints but their skins overlap; they are not welded plumbing or a fluid simulation. The canopy surface is not collidable. The support posts and console are. A once-updated local probe captures the surrounding room, so later moving figures and drawing changes are not reflected live. Settings last for the hall visit.

152 native checks passed, including maximum-density mesh generation, crossing separation, real pointer colliders and dispatch, unchanged drawing/audio state, and both museum aisles. Physical Quest performance and headset comfort still need a hardware pass.
