## The walk

The world room is nearly empty and entirely inhabited: a sierpinski_pyramid — the triangle-in-triangle figure that Rule 90 draws for free when you stack its generations — standing as monument to the chapter's deepest coincidence. You do not run an automaton here; you stand inside one of its theorems. The walls hold the space the way walls do, without comment.

## What the world says

The pyramid is what it looks like when a local rule's output becomes a *place*. Rule 90 was never told about triangles; the triangles are what its neighborhood arithmetic cannot help producing, generation on generation, until geometry appears that no one drew. A world, in this chapter's sense, is exactly that: the shape of a rule's persistence. The room offers it at monument scale and lets you walk the consequence.
