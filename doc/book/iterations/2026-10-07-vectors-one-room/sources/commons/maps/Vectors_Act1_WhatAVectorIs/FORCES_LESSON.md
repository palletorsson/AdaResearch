# Vectors in your hands

Three stations in one map. One reconstruction per principle.

Carry the known grabbable PointBody through three stations in one room. First move one point relative to its local origin: read displacement, length and a separate unit direction. Next move two points and follow a then b to the rose sum; compare the route length with the direct arrival. Finally project onto the signed X rail and retain the perpendicular remainder. Live labels follow the inputs. Thin cylinders give direction a visible body. These are geometric operations; a moving point does not yet imply a force simulation.

Learning elements:

- Displacement and magnitude.
- Unit direction, including zero.
- Addition: head to tail; route versus arrival.
- Signed projection and the perpendicular remainder.

Carry: One point class; a declared local frame; direction, sum and selected component ready for launch and force.

Check: Choose an unlisted displacement, then zero. Predict length and direction. Swap two displacements and cancel them. Move perpendicular to the X rail, then behind its origin: recover a from projection + remainder.
