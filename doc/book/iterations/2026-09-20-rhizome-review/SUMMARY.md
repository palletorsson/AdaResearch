## Findings and decisions

The grower samples positions inside a fixed annulus. It does not expand a frontier. Two initial points have no edge between them; later points connect to earlier neighbours by distance. Birth order and geometry can distribute links unevenly without any explicit root property. The registry's older claim that nodes always link to many and grow sideways was too broad.

The full-size interactive placement now provides six controls: GROW holds or resumes arrivals; STEP adds one point and holds; LINKS switches between one and three nearest earlier neighbours while keeping all positions; NODE selects a birth index and highlights its active links; CUT temporarily removes that node and its incident edges; REPLAY returns to the same two seeds and retains the sequence of sampled positions. At forty nodes, growth holds for inspection. Passive placements keep the smaller automatic display and restart with fresh samples at capacity.

DEGREE counts active undirected neighbours; COMPONENTS counts connected groups, including isolated points. A cut node leaves a small grey ghost. Stored positions and edges are retained, but inactive endpoints are omitted from drawing and measurement. The remaining points do not rewire around a loss. Growth stays held while any node is cut. Restoring a node returns its links under the selected rule; it is not a repair invented by the network.

The comparison is reproducible. In the captured 24-point sample, node 3 has degree 3 under the one-link rule. Removing it changes two components into four and 22 edges into 19. Under three links, the same node has degree 8: its removal changes 65 edges into 57 while the remaining graph stays connected. This is evidence about the displayed construction and chosen removal, not a claim of universal resilience or social equality.

The first readout now counts both seed nodes instead of showing zero. Rendered diagonal edge cylinders meet their node coordinates: length scales the cylinder's local axis after orientation. Rebuilds replace only generated children and preserve the museum's attachments. Node and edge storage are bounded.

## Limits and next work

The probe explicitly grants the real gate access signal and tests selected desktop walking segments. It does not establish continuous gameplay, palm recognition, headset comfort, Quest performance or learner understanding. The retained cave has not received a traversal or architectural-fit test in this pass. The colour gradient supplements the numbered birth index; the close nodes still benefit from selection and highlighted edges.

Distant connections, a changing sampling region, automatic repair and communication between nodes remain possible further experiments. They are not claimed as current controls. The three-link case still costs more edges and can concentrate degree. A more connected diagram does not by itself settle the question of which relations are desirable.

No live restart, live bake replacement or commit was performed. Derived web views can still report stale live-pack ledgers; this private probe does not replace them.
