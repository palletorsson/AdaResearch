# A body given a place

*Proposed passage for Rings of Ash. The gallery is built as a standalone companion; this passage is held outside the map manuscripts until its placement is settled.*

A narrow stem rises beside a swollen body. Farther along, a column opens like a bell. The pale surfaces catch the same light, but their edges break it differently. Each plinth names the profile, height and divisions used to make the body above it.

Four sides can be enough to make a form you want. Sixteen can bring another outline into view. Height changes what you look up to; a wider middle changes what you can see beyond it. These decisions meet in one body without becoming the same decision.

The loop closes each ring. It asks for no ceremony. We gave its result a pedestal, a place among other bodies, a distant light. How much of what holds you here have we built between you?

You can follow the construction and still find something you had not expected. Choose one column to keep looking at. What would you change first, and what has already become worth preserving?
