# Form grammar enters the spine

Restored **LSystems_Shape_Grammars** between **LSystems_Living** and **Assemblage_Same_Desire**. It was a deferred generic arena with env_one, no chapter and several generic exits. Its complete previous map remains in `before/`. The replacement has a true floor spawn, one next-in-sequence exit and one six-specimen encounter. All other hall artifacts remain unchanged.

This is a pedagogical connection across existing systems, not a claim that their data structures or builders are interchangeable. DNA names saved form parameters here. Face grammar operates on a mesh; the facade composer arranges parts; form finding includes physical constraints or search; scored selection evaluates candidates.

## What was built

`form_grammar_family` calls the existing MeshData, MeshSelector, InsetFaceOp and ExtrudeFaceOp. Six static results keep their comparisons available. The seed is one metre across. Pink identifies continuing tips, mint the inset shoulders. A triangular face is the operation's unit, so the cube's apparent square top produces two descendants. There are two rows, three specimens in each.

Three repetitions produce 84 triangles when two seed faces continue, and 444 when all twelve continue. No mesh-generation code was changed. The changed-amount specimen uses 8% inset instead of 28%; the outward extrusion remains 0.4 m. New side faces do not themselves become tips. The last specimen grows down too, so it is seated higher without changing scale.

The actual mesh and its plinth have collision. The floor is separate support. Six fixed recipes make this a walk-around comparison, not an editor, a biological process, an animated morph or a physical settling simulation. A future tool for changing the operations or asking for a joined tip/hole is a development lead, not a present affordance.

## Evidence and limits

Native Godot 4.6 isolated Forward+ run: 47 checks pass, including ten continuous route segments with a 0.6 m capsule, floor support, six mesh-surface ray hits and plinth refusal. Five PNGs are rendered from the actual production artifact. The preview reconstructs the production map's level floor and wall boundary; it is not a screenshot of the full museum loader. Tracked VR, Quest performance and visitor interpretation have not been checked.

Run `commons/artifacts/form_grammar_family/study.tscn` with F6 for desktop walking. Production map and sequence changes take effect when those resources are reloaded. No live museum state, bake or save file was replaced. No commit or frozen full-book rebuild.
