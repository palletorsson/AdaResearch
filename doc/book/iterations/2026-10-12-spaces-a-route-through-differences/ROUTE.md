# Spaces — proposed teaching route

Editable teaching order, not a change to the physical room order. Same live PointBody throughout. Existing plane/sphere runnable tutorial remains the baseline. These are briefs for expansion, not tested student reconstructions.

## 1. Perspective

Entrance: Lobby: left gold doorway.

Learning elements: Perspective, scale, measurement.

Carry the same sphere to the viewpoint, then inspect the tapered corridor from the side. Compare apparent depth with measured construction. The sphere keeps its size; the architecture produces the illusion.

Carry: Keep object size separate from apparent size.

## 2. Overlapping rooms

Entrance: Lobby: Rose and Blue pavilion doors.

Learning elements: Portal transform, object identity, visibility.

Walk around the pavilion before entering Rose and Blue. Carry the same point through a doorway, leave it, and retrieve it. Reconstruct one paired doorway before adding more. Explain the declared source and destination frames and the visibility mask.

Carry: Keep the object; change its spatial connection.

## 3. Around the corner

Entrance: Lobby: right gold floor line.

Learning elements: Generation, stable address, bounded cache.

Round the hidden corner into another numbered room. Four turns restore heading without returning to the same room. Leave the point, continue, then backtrack. Explain stable room addresses and rebuilding empty rooms while protecting the room holding the point.

Carry: Familiar appearance does not establish identity.

## 4. Repeating gallery

Entrance: Rose: rear-left cyan doorway.

Learning elements: Periodic boundary, translation, visual instance.

Cross a tile edge while holding the same point. Leave it and walk a lap to find it again. Reconstruct one wrap translation; distinguish the physical tile and live point from their visual neighbours. Heading and held offset survive the wrap.

Carry: A repeated image is not another live object.

## 5. Labyrinth

Entrance: Blue: rear-right gold doorway.

Learning elements: Connectivity, persistent state, event.

Place the point in A and walk A–B–C–D–A. Retrieve that instance, then touch the seal to open A–C. Reconstruct the connection state and both barriers. The lesson changes a graph through a hand event; it does not change room dimensions.

Carry: A stored object tests a return; an action changes the route.

## 6. Column forest — side encounter

Entrance: Blue: rear-left gold doorway.

Learning elements: Profile, branching, parameter, guide.

Bring the point sideways to change the small study’s branch angle; use its trigger to reveal guides. Reuse the earlier profile and transformation work. Follow shaft, branch and vault construction while keeping geometric resemblance separate from structural proof.

Carry: Earlier geometry becomes architecture; this is not intrinsic curvature.

## 7. Curved paths and metric spaces

Entrance: Return to the lobby console: chambers 3–7.

Learning elements: Parallel transport, geodesic, metric, chart.

Return to the carried triangle and compare plane and sphere before the exact disk, H² × R and H³ views. Teach these in separate small steps. Measure paths and angles under the declared metric, then use geodesic target practice. Distinguish surface curvature, product geometry and full three-dimensional curvature.

Carry: Changing connections and changing a metric are different operations.
