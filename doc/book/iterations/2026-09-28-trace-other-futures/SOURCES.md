# Sources for this addition

The author's supplied hauntology note is the starting point. The prose makes its technical extension explicit rather than attributing it to the sources below.

- Jacques Derrida, [Specters of Marx, publisher preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781040559963_A63760427/preview-9781040559963_A63760427.pdf), Exordium, PDF pages 23–25. Inheritance and responsibility reach beyond those presently living. The application to a tool's other possible uses belongs to this project.
- W. Ross Ashby, [An Introduction to Cybernetics](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf), §§7/6–7/7 and §§11/5–11/9. Distinguishable variety and the limits of regulation, including variation already blocked by an environment. Changing the task or judging whose possibilities should matter is not a conclusion of the law alone.
- Izadi and colleagues, [KinectFusion](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/kinectfusion-real-time-3d-reconstruction-and-interaction-using-a-moving-depth-camera/), 2011. A moving Kinect depth camera supplies data for 3D surface reconstruction. This supports the game-camera-to-scanner example.
- [DIVE IN manual](https://www.pina.si/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/DIVEin_Manual_EN.pdf), §2.3.1, printed pages 24–25. Household equipment, including a microwave, appears among the resources for DIY microbiology. The book mentions this change of context without giving laboratory instructions.
