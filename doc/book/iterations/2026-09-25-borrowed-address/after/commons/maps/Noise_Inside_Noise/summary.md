# Borrow an address

Noise_Inside_Noise develops the existing noisesphere into an opt-in coordinate study, retaining the independent dark_sphere. A ten-metre enclosing sphere surrounds the visitor and the comparison instrument. Its two opposing side openings lead onto a continuous circular floor. A matched pair of smaller spheres, two sampled image plates, a direct/displaced coordinate grid and the enclosing surface share one explicit sampler. WARP selects 0, .35, .8 or 1.4; ZERO returns immediately to direct sampling; SAMPLE selects the small witnesses and a matching hollow ring on the enclosing sphere; RELIEF separately converts the same values into radial mesh displacement.

The base is single-octave FastNoiseLite Value noise (seed 4, frequency 1.2). Two single-octave Perlin fields (seeds 19 and 47, frequency .45) provide the coordinate displacement. The result is N(p + amount × displacement(p)). All comparison state is static between actions. The two sphere geometries remain identical in SKIN mode, and zero warp restores identical field samples and relief geometry.

The instrument stands inside the sphere on a continuous circular floor connected to the museum aprons. The spherical wall and ceiling begin in vertex-shader relief, with collision matching their displaced geometry. Two real openings and the floor seam stay fixed. WARP changes the field used for both colour and relief; RELIEF switches the pair and the enclosing room between round support and displaced surfaces. The enclosure displays colours at gain .65 with lighting from displaced faces and mild emission. All original structure cells and utilities remain; an added east boundary contains the stage, and museum-only floor declarations bridge the approach and exit threshold. One new ground row wraps around the preserved exit wall. The sphere's legacy scene and shader remain available when stand:warp is absent. This stage uses a new explicit CPU sampler, not the original dome shader's field realization.

The primary encounter is noisesphere; the book also retains its brief supporting return to dark_sphere. The hall is reconnected after Noise_6_Wall in the museum plan, matching its already declared place in the book sequence. Noise_Space_10 is the next hall to develop; its museum connection remains part of that later pass.
