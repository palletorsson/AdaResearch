# A body receives an answer from elsewhere

The visitor is inside the noise sphere, able to look back towards the museum through an opening. The calculation has become a surrounding surface; it changes what occupies the visitor's view. The fixed floor still carries the same body.

The yellow point stays on the body while the pink point marks a different address in its source. This is the relation the room makes available to thought. The apparent disturbance on a surface has a particular construction: an answer arrives from somewhere else. A noisy silhouette alone could not establish that account.

The two fields have assigned roles. One supplies the value; two others supply components of a displacement. Their combination can be useful without pretending that the roles were inevitable or equal. We could exchange them, change their relative scales or choose a non-noise source. Those would be different experiments. The present instrument lets us follow one composition closely enough to know what another experiment would alter.

Zero is a return to an explicit agreement. It does not reveal a world before mediation. The baseline still has a seed, frequency, finite samples, a purple-to-mint palette and a projection that omits y. Returning to it makes a changed operation legible; it does not make that baseline ontologically innocent.

There is pleasure in a recognisable patch acquiring another neighbourhood. A contour can become vein-like, crowded or difficult to name as the amount changes. The unfamiliar does not have to become a threat before it matters. Nor does increasing irregularity automatically increase freedom. A maker may want a quiet patch, a sharp gathering, a repeated motif or an awkward seam. Knowing where to intervene makes that desire more specific.

RELIEF introduces another consequence. The same values now alter mesh radii. Input-coordinate displacement and output geometry can be separated, although both may produce a writhing appearance. Neither deforms the player collision here. The enclosure supplies a fixed wall, ceiling and floor while the small comparison bodies remain without collision. Colour changes around the visitor; the openings remain available. Possible bodies depend on which part of the simulation receives the result: the material, the mesh, the collider, an agent's steering rule. The shared computational medium makes transfers possible, but each transfer still needs an implementation.

The sampled coordinate grid prevents the final image from becoming the only account of itself. We retain an address and an operation beside the result. That record is also selective: a finite grid can miss a fold between its lines, and the sphere's two-coordinate projection identifies directions that differ in y. The remainder is not a secret original hidden behind the work. It is a difference another question, representation or body may make relevant.

The study stays still between actions while the familiar orb continues to pulse. Time remains in the room without being required to explain this operation. We can spend a little of it following an address before moving towards the next terrain, where a value may become something that carries or obstructs us.
