## What QFEP is

**Queer Free Energy Principle.** The project documents make Q a practice of keeping the terms and authority of a model revisable while protecting the capacities of what it excludes to live, act and change those terms. **Self-Q** returns this question to the maker: structures shape our own later capacities to think. These are an ethical and methodological proposal, not a theorem derived from entropy.

The definition and Self-Q now appear in the Introduction manuscript, with document footnotes. E Term carries the question into the controls: a visitor can reopen this tree but cannot yet ask it to grow otherwise. Neither more leaves nor a more open boundary automatically means care; some boundaries protect a body's possibilities.

The new [QFEP reading note](../QFEP_READING.md) gathers the terms and sources for later halls.
