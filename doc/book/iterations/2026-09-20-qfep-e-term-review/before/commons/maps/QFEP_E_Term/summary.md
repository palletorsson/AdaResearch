# QFEP E Term - Summary

## Overview
The third QFEP Laboratory map isolates the entropy term E(S) by driving lambda to 1.0 — maximum entropic weighting. The formula tips entirely toward dissolution: particles scatter omnidirectionally, random cubes drift and bounce without settling, and the room itself resists coherence. Where the F term was cold precision, the E term is warm scatter.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 10x12 grid with central raised area
- **Architecture**: Four random_cubes clusters flanking a central particle_chaos, entropy_axioms text display on the eastern edge
- **Height**: Rises inward (1 to 2 to 3), drops to 0 at the south exit gap

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **particle_chaos** — 200 particles, zero direction, 180-degree spread, zero gravity; the visual signature of maximum entropy
- **random_cubes** (4 instances) — 20 cubes each with randomized position, rotation, scale, and color; drift with independent velocity vectors, bounce off boundaries
- **lambda_slider** — Set to 1.0 (red handle, red particles), showing the chaos end of the gradient
- **entropy_axioms** — Tutorial text declaring entropy as possibility space, not just disorder

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at north, **elevator** at (5,5), **teleporter** at south reading "Find the Balance"

## Learning Sequence
1. Enter a red-lit, noise-washed environment — immediate sensory contrast with the F term's cold blue
2. Observe particle_chaos at center — particles refuse to cohere, scatter in every direction
3. Walk among the random_cubes — each cluster is Kolmogorov-incompressible, no two arrangements alike
4. Read the entropy_axioms — entropy as freedom, as the size of the possibility space
5. Experience the lambda slider at maximum — the environment at full dissolution
6. Recognize that pure entropy is not liberation but formlessness; the teleporter invites the search for balance
7. Exit toward QFEP_Lambda_Spectrum

## Design Intent
The E term completes the dialectic pair begun in QFEP_F_Term. Pure order is a trap (the dark room); pure entropy is dissolution (the formless fog). Neither extreme sustains life. The map makes dissolution feel real — exhilarating and unstable — so that the edge of chaos, when reached, carries the weight of both extremes the learner has inhabited.

## Connection to Sequence
- **Position**: 3/8 in qfeplaboratory
- **Follows**: QFEP_F_Term (pure order, the dark room)
- **Leads to**: QFEP_Lambda_Spectrum (walking the full gradient from crystal to chaos)
