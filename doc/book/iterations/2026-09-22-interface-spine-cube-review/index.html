In front of the work

Each builder now has a supported, inclined console in front of it. The readout shrinks from 110 × 96 cm to 76 × 32 cm. Four physical buttons sit 19 cm apart on one backplate. Their captions are flat textures; the readout is a 2D screen in the room.

The console height is independent of the specimen height. The case is excluded from automatic specimen seating, so changing the interface cannot raise the cube. Its readout remains below the intact cube from the tested operating eye position.

The corner must be reachable too

The original upper corners were just over 2 m high. Both builders now stand on 55 cm plinths and retain their one-metre geometry. The visible grips grow from 1.2 to 2.8 cm radius inside the existing grab colliders. The room still contains both builders and its supporting works.

Let go, then replay

Replay keeps the changed vertex positions. Restore returns the original cube. While a corner is held, replay and restore dim and the readout asks for release; the existing owner guards prevent either action from taking the corner out of the hand. Pause becomes a lit RESUME control. The diagonal control becomes available after assembly and lights when the six face diagonals are shown.

The readout counts the visible points, edges and triangle patches. Its displacement number is measured from the original vertices in local metres. Text changes request a screen redraw; an unchanged readout does not redraw continuously.

Keep the comparison

The book keeps its argument: coordinates may move while connections persist. Only its reference to a side instrument changes to a front instrument. The artifact selection and primary roles remain intact.

A pattern for the next halls

Give the specimen and its controls their own working heights. Place a compact control strip in front, keep the sightline open, show which actions are available, and preserve a way back after an experiment. This console reuses the shared cased data surface, baked lettering and native push buttons already used by the earlier interface passes.
