# The comic arrangement, in the real museum

User: apply the approved comic's architecture to the VR Endless Museum too.

- Point One: three equal off-white supports, empty / visible / solid. Preserve
  point positions, 0.28 m diameter and distinct collision conditions.
- Last Point One sphere: existing 1.3 m diameter and collision; black polished
  clearcoat. This is the requested object of the future.
- Shared hall identity: ENDLESS MUSEUM / always another room. Cormorant Garamond,
  bundled with OFL, interprets the generated comic's serif lettering.
- Mount lettering on three contiguous wall faces, clear of passages, voids and
  tall exhibit enclosures. The shared desktop/VR dressing path installs it;
  no per-frame work or additional collision. No suitable wall means no sign.
- Podium lighting belongs to the room. The switch retains the points' dedicated
  sun and fill; the bases no longer inherit that special lighting.

Evidence: actual museum renderer, not generated pictures. Point scene and two
hall reports are linked from index.html. No headset session or APK deployment.
The historical before picture is dated 2026-10-03. Existing tutorials and their
dated, source-pinned tests have not been rewritten or silently recertified.

Typography source and redistribution: commons/font/cormorant-garamond/
provenance.json and OFL.md. API reference for the bundled font variation:
