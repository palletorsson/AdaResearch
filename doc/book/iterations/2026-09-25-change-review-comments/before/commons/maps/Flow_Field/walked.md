# Flow_Field — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (critical slot, change thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

vector_field_grid · science_screen

## The walk

The third sentence arrives: *accumulation flows*. The vector_field_grid fills the room with directions — every cell an instruction, the derivative generalized into weather. Standing in it, you are no longer measuring change or collecting it; you are *inside* it, and the field neither knows nor cares that you arrived.

## The turn (critical)

The critical charge is the field's indifference. A slope was your reading of a hill; an integral was your patience with a curve; but a flow field is change with the observer deleted — direction assigned to space itself, before anyone walks it. The room quietly asks the chapter's political question: who wrote this field? Every cell's arrow was authored, and the author is nowhere visible. Calculus's cleanest abstraction turns out to be the project's oldest concern — a structure that moves everyone and signs nothing.
