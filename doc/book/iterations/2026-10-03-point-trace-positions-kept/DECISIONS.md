# Point Trace: positions kept

3 October 2026 · one tutorial pilot for author review.

## The new work

An ordered `Array[Vector3]` retains positions. `append()` adds one; indexes read it; `size()` counts it. A `for` loop traverses adjacent pairs, reusing the two-position drawing operation from Lines. The loop completes a traversal when a sample is accepted; `_process()` supplies repeated opportunities to sample over time. These two kinds of repetition are distinct.

A small `if` comparison is taught here because admission to the record needs a decision. The planned broad conditions chapter remains in Transformation; do not treat comparisons and branching as wholly new there. `pop_front()` introduces finite capacity through the amber-memory encounter. Copying whole arrays, persistent storage and several simultaneous strokes remain later work. The local lesson contract records what this pilot actually teaches; the older numeric course taxonomy is not declared complete by it.

## The deliberately reduced artifact

The study inherits the frozen, grabbable PointHandle and Segment renderer from Lines. It records the handle centre in Study's coordinate frame whenever it has moved far enough from the last retained position. This is one continuous record, including the displacement when desktop pickup brings the point to the hold position. Release freezes the handle; taking it again continues. Restarting the scene begins a fresh record.

The museum's pens follow their nib, gate recording on the hold, shape values with a spatial grid, and copy strokes on release. Those behaviors are not silently attributed to this smaller study. We retain the source's core relation: distance threshold, append, ordered traversal, bounded capacity. The source is `commons/primitives/point/draw_dot.gd`, especially its recording and trail-rebuild methods. Grid will distinguish a kept position from a position assigned to a cell.

`TraceDrawing.clear()` hides pooled geometry; it does not change the student's array. `join(a, b)` reuses Segment and marks the sample positions. It creates and reuses its own nodes. The student controls the list, threshold, capacity and loop. No teaching object is fetched from a scene by dollar reference or another node path.

The pen variants, rolling ball, whiteboard and detailed telemetry remain available to the museum and critical text. This pass changes `tutorial.md` and its executable companion, not `final.md` or the live hall.

## Evidence and limits

The fresh downloaded package imports cleanly in Godot 4.6. The native harness checks ordered retention, thresholds, Vector3 value storage, exact joined pairs, changed spacing, bounded memory, no timeout deletion, geometry reuse, desktop pickup/release, synthetic XR grip and construction without scene-authored lesson objects. The captures are native desktop renders of a scripted route, allowing a controlled comparison; they are not recordings of a visitor's hand.

A physical headset and a beginner reading trial remain pending. Coordinates still needs its shared-starter rewrite and keeps its place before Lines. The earlier separate `studies/point-trace` project is retained as an older companion; it is not this lesson's download.
