Make the source less distinctive. Does the difference remain visible? A chosen mark can conceal an operation or introduce extra symmetry. The selected button names a construction; it does not prove that the resulting image has exactly that symmetry group.

The cage marks rotational orders one, two, three, four and six, and crosses five and seven. Its restriction concerns rotations compatible with a periodic planar lattice. A fivefold-looking flower may sit inside a repeating cell without making the whole field fivefold symmetric. Look for the boundary of what is being counted.

For ideal patterns repeating in two independent directions on the Euclidean plane, there are seventeen plane-group types. The [IUCr tables](https://it.iucr.org/Ab/) describe their operations. The catalogue closes under particular assumptions. This finite carpet still ends, meets the light, and appears differently from where you stand.

At FOLLOW THE THREAD, cream threads rise and fall. Take the brass shuttle through their opening. Beat the pink thread into place. Follow it back at the edge, then make the returning pass.

Here an instruction says which thread passes over another. Look between the crossings. The rule has helped construct the surface you are examining.

Change the lift draft. The cloth over the front beam keeps its earlier crossings. New passes receive the new instruction. A change has a beginning you can locate in the object. It has not rewritten the work already done.

Stay for several passes. The same gesture returns, but the growing cloth makes each return another part of the work. There is time between choosing a rule and seeing what it becomes.

These thread paths are geometry, held in shape by the program. Yarn tension and friction would require other knowledge. A visible crossing gives us something specific to inspect without completing the account of weaving. Knitting would ask another question again: how does one loop hold through another?

Read this beside Sadie Plant's *Zeros + Ones* and the textile practice described in *Mamma Mönster*. Whose work becomes visible when we call a pattern a program? Which skills have we kept close enough to follow with our hands?

At the atlas plates, read the makers' names. A symmetry group can describe relations between marks without describing the labour, place or desire that brought them together.
