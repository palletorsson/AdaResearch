20 September 2026. Hall: `CriticalAlgorithms_Algorithmic_Bias_Visualization`.

## Findings and decisions

The physical slider supplied its travel in metres to a callback expecting a normalized value. Running the frozen old script confirms that a handle at 1.0 produced a blend of only 0.14. The callback now reads `get_normalized_value()`. Both authored arrangements are available to a visitor.

The earlier movement also brightened the warm group and faded the grey group. Those material changes are removed so the comparison concerns positions and label overlap. Perspective, distance and occlusion still affect visibility: fixed materials are not a claim of equal perceived salience.

The handle is named COMPRESSION. The neutral header names arrangement A, B or the interval between them. READ exposes a short interpretation after looking: both groups were chosen; more room is not a verdict; which distinction matters to you? The optional text does not change the geometry. The code still accepts the old `perspective_blend` configuration key for compatibility. Reconfiguration now regenerates the seeded endpoints when radii change, without duplicating the position arrays.

The study moves from cell (5,2) to (4,6), at full scale and an explicit 1.085 m vertical offset. A narrow support brings its controls to approximately 1.1 m. The panel sits ahead of the initial ring so a word label does not cover its button. Opening the lower divider cell (6,6) and removing its obsolete wedge give the expanded arrangement room. A local sculpture-clear rectangle reserves circulation. The original spawn and the exact placements of `bias_visualizer`, `science_screen` and `situated_readout` remain.

The book pearl previously named the secondary `bias_visualizer` as its hero. The book and both trunk mirrors now agree with the existing primary role: `bias_from_inside`. No role promotion or secondary removal was needed.
