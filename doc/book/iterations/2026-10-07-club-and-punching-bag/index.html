Held tools and local hand mitts are kinematic contact sources. The bag is a rigid capsule on a pin joint, with a visual tether; its padding and rope do not deform. These are models of contact and constrained motion, not measurements of a visitor’s muscular force.

The original bowling sphere remains the tutorial carrier. These objects add play around it. Existing hall order, size, aisle and exit remain; full tutorial.md and final.md were not rewritten.
