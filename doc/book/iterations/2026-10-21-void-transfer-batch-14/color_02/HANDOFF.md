# Colour transfer: Nails and Rainbow

Codex placed all six Nails artifacts. Claude Opus delivered the four Rainbow
placements and a dependency/fit review; Codex checked IDs, source configuration,
geometry bounds and control handlers before merging the fragment.

The original scene files and map texts are unchanged. Both floors keep their
source scale and arrangement through a simple translation. Standard Void
dimensions remain 42 x 32 metres with six-metre storeys. Every original placement
is a separate editor object; the internal parts of a source assembly remain
an assembly.

Two integration adapters keep the Nails scene working under a new parent:
the colour-ball reset uses the case's coordinates, and the RGB controllers find
the current Void hands.
Geometry, source palettes, controls and signals remain inherited from their
museum scripts.

## Deliberately deferred

- The Nails title board meets the ceiling by about 0.19 m. Rainbow's welcome
  board extends above its floor's ceiling and slightly through the slab; its
  outer arch also clips. These are measured fit issues, not grounds for silently
  enlarging the shared tower.
- The original science screen is in field/demo mode, as its source token asks.
- Actual grab edits are checked; persistence across game restarts is outside
  this transfer's acceptance scope.
- Claude identified the optional catalyst's museum sequence dependency:
  `sequence:auto` has no running museum sequence in a fresh Void trial. Its
  bracelet absorption is XR-specific; desktop parity and deterministic sequence
  binding await a shared catalyst integration pass. This is not claimed as a
  fully working desktop catalyst encounter.
- Physical headset play, curation, encounter order, and text revisions remain
  pending. Native screenshots and editor models show the transfer as it is.
