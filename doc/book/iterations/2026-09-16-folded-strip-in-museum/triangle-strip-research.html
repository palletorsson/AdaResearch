Reading the evidence

Same source strip. Different things made visible.

  • Facets: the production red and green double-sided triangles. Its duplicated front and back faces can affect lighting.
  • Cast: a single-colour surface with generated normals. The source uses alternating triangle winding without correcting it before smoothing. The flat control already shows a lighting pattern; do not read that pattern as a geometric fold.
  • Edges: unshaded tubes, 12 mm in radius. They add geometry and stay bright regardless of lighting. Their brightness and thickness are part of this treatment.
  • Thick triangles: each face becomes a separate 60 mm prism. This adds internal rims and can introduce overlaps. It is not a welded or watertight shell.

All candidates retain the original surface connectivity. Folding changes its placement in space; this study does not establish a new abstract topology. The two failure cases show why connectivity alone cannot guarantee a usable surface.

Measurement and capture method

The default vertices were exported by Godot from the production script. A deterministic Python search generated the candidates and checked 18 analytic fixtures. Hinge candidates rotate downstream vertices around shared edges; bend, twist and wave candidates deform the sampled coordinates.

Creases use consistently oriented triangle normals, correcting strip parity for measurement only. Intersection counts concern zero-thickness faces with no shared vertex. Shared-vertex pairs, shell thickness and handle clearance are excluded; zero detected contacts is not a collision-free guarantee. Fully degenerate faces have undefined creases and are excluded from intersection tests.

Each candidate is translated onto the same plinth, without scaling. Every treatment uses the same two orthographic cameras, 6.2 m camera width, lights, 960 × 640 image size and Godot compatibility renderer. Grab handles are hidden and their processing is disabled to isolate the surface. The lowest ideal vertex is held 40 mm above the plinth so the added tubes and triangle thickness are not clipped. Original positions and production source hashes are checked during capture. These are isolated research-stage renders, not screenshots from the hall. There has been no headset or interaction test.

From this candidate to the hall

The single hinge makes the operation legible; the paired fold gives that operation a more surprising body. The hall revision now repairs winding and normals, then lets this existing strip slowly unfold and refold through those two cases. The original gallery keeps the earlier rendering defects visible so we can distinguish a form decision from an engine repair.
