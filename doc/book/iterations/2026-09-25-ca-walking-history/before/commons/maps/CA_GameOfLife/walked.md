# CA_GameOfLife — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (second walk slot, cellularautomata thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

mirrored_cellular_automata · ca_bridge · ca_columns · lab_room · dark_sphere

## The walk

Conway's game gets a room built out of itself. The ca_bridge and ca_columns are architecture whose surfaces run the automaton — you cross a bridge that is computing while it carries you. The mirrored_cellular_automata doubles a world against its reflection, and the eye starts looking for the difference between a pattern and its ghost. The lab_room frames it all with clinical calm, as if gliders were specimens — which, by now, they are.

## What it settles

Life is the chapter's celebrity and this room treats it correctly: not as a toy but as a load-bearing material. When the automaton holds up the bridge you walk on, "simple rules can compute anything" stops being a slogan; it becomes structural engineering.
