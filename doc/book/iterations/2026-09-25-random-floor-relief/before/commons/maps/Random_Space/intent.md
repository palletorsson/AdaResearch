Concept: Correlated against independent randomness. The noise mixer's picture is a sum of octaves of one smooth field, so nearby samples agree and broad features persist as finer layers are added; the random ground is one independent draw per vertex, so neighbours share nothing.
Sequence role: Thirteenth hall of Randomness, after Random_Pheromone (a terrain shaped by visits) and before Random_Game (uncertainty in timing rather than texture). It prepares Noise, where correlation becomes the subject.
Technical angle: noise_mixer:0:-0.5#plinth:0.9 stands on a 0.9 m plinth (the hall builds from its map; noise_mixer.gd guards config that arrives before _ready). Sliders OCT, LAC, PER; SEED re-draws the octave offsets from an undisplayed global value; every picture is normalised to the full nine-colour palette, which is not monotone in lightness (brown rock sits just below the snowy peaks). random_space: 11 x 20 m trimesh ground, seed 1, independent uniform heights about +/-0.12 m, no animation; the museum's walk map treats it as an obstacle and reports the hall severed, though a body walks it. worley_noise: a static cellular field, also normalised to its own maximum. shannon_entropy_meter repeats the seeded sample of Random_Entropy.
Critical angle: A palette of land invites a reading of land. Normalisation hides the raw spread, so two settings can look equally contrasted while their values differ.
Key artifacts: noise_mixer (primary), random_space (the ground), worley_noise; shannon_entropy_meter and dark_sphere are secondary.
