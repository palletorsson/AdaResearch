# Implementation and measurement contracts

The executable models, reconstruction steps, parameter ranges and save format are documented in [tutorial.md](tutorial.md). Seven wrappers in `commons/artifacts/ordinary_entropies/` use the comparison host with control-free encounter copies while remaining independent registry entries and map placements.

The runtime is deterministic at a stored seed and state. It uses no network entropy, global RNG, rigid-body scattering or physical steam solver. The cup has an energy-conserving two-body heat model. The hinge has explicitly chosen mathematical update rules. The glass's numerical record channel is separate from its rendered surface. The ball pit's probabilities come from the selected sampling rule, not mesh volume.

See [native verification and room evidence](../../../doc/book/iterations/2026-10-16-entropy-visible-states/README.md) and [sources](../../../doc/book/iterations/2026-10-16-ordinary-entropies/sources.md). Scene loading, passage clearance, wall attachment and simultaneous state composition and model correctness are checked separately from the pending learner/headset trial.

The research wall composes the existing dry-marker canvas, XRTools pickup lifecycle and marker body. Notes carry their own local canvases and pin transforms. Ink and positions persist as one local JSON record with PNG buffers under `user://research_info_walls/`. The illustration is replaceable independently; see `commons/artifacts/research_info_wall/ILLUSTRATION_SLOT.md`. No shared multiplayer storage or cloud synchronization is implemented.
