# Seven encounters, one walking order

All seven artifacts have independent map placements and registry scenes. Tutorial and final use the same order; the first two are a side-by-side comparison across a low divider.

| # | Artifact | What differs between versions | What is kept fixed for the first comparison |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `entropy_chairs_shannon` — Shannon chairs | Colour shares, assignment to sites, prepared inventory | Eight equally likely IDs; record colour |
| 2 | `entropy_chairs_boltzmann` — Boltzmann chairs | Pink count on the left, individual assignment | Eight marked sites, four pink/four black, same-colour chairs indistinguishable |
| 3 | `entropy_chairs_description` — Description wall | Grid, edit, motif and seeded scatter; recipes always visible | Six chairs per group and shared generating language |
| 4 | `entropy_door_dynamics` — Driven door | Contracting/expanding rule, step, held-open passage | Binary observation partition and 1,024 initial bins |
| 5 | `entropy_cooling_cup` — Cooling cup | Time since initial heat difference | Two finite bodies, capacities and conserved energy |
| 6 | `entropy_frosted_chair` — Partial chair | Identity/colour/constant record, same displayed chair | Eight equiprobable identities |
| 7 | `entropy_ball_pit` — Best guess | ID/colour outcome or explicit large-ball weight | Sixteen objects; large size is not itself a sampling weight |

The versions now remain visible together with no controls. A compact wall explanation names their assumptions and readings. Floor letters identify the versions. The [tutorial](tutorial.md) separates installed comparisons from independent reconstruction, and the [current review](../../../doc/book/iterations/2026-10-16-entropy-visible-states/index.html) records native views. Human comparison and headset acceptance remain open in the [plan](plan.md).
