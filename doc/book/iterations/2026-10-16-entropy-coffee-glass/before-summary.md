# Different entropies, ordinary objects

`Random_Entropies` follows `Random_Definition` and precedes `Random_Entropy` in Randomness. Seven separately registered groups now show versions simultaneously, with no interface. The existing entropy hall and its prefab gallery remain available.

The 19 × 57 m floor enters at the top and exits at the bottom. Four Shannon inventories and three Boltzmann assignments face the entrance across the low divider. Beyond a wall, four chair arrangements compare candidate recipes. Two driven door pairs repeat contracting and expanding rules; a separate central door stays open. Three tables show successive cooling snapshots. Another partition introduces the same chair behind three record channels. Finally, three identical ball pits declare different sampling and recording rules.
