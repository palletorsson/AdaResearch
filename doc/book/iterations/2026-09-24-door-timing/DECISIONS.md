## Decision

Keep the existing fire doors. Their assignment is already made before a button reveals it; calling that a post-choice draw would misdescribe the source. Add three matched walking trials to separate the moments of drawing, choosing, and revealing.

The consequence is a direct passage or a longer route around a wall. This makes the evidence spatial, with no new fire, score or simulated entropy source. The visitor can choose the longer way deliberately even when the answer is shown first. Knowledge changes what is available to action without prescribing desire.

Both shortcut gates have identical closed geometry before choice, including in the preassigned hidden trial. Distinct local seeds prevent the first trial teaching the others' answer. REPLAY intentionally allows memory to outlive concealment. NEW ROUND changes the seed but need not change the binary result.
