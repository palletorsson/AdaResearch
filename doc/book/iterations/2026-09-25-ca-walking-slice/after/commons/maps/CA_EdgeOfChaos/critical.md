# What is allowed to remain different?

The previous hall asked what a cell can receive. Here it receives an average and a disturbance, then meets a further condition: what may be stored? The integer conversion is easy to overlook because it looks like housekeeping. In the uniform experiment it decides whether the intervention survives its first update.

The fractional version distributes a trace widely while weakening it. The integer version can remove it. Neither result tells us in advance which body is preferable. A quiet uncoupled cell retains its changed value. Stillness can keep a memory; smoothing can lose one. Movement and mixture do not automatically amount to freedom.

This matters to the project's question, “what bodies are possible?” The rule admits neighbourhoods and numbers; the storage admits some distinctions; the display admits others. A counter uses a tolerance. A difference image increases its gain as the trace fades. The artist's decisions about visibility take part in what the visitor calls a pattern. The counter and the luminous floor therefore need to be read together.

Matched forcing is also a deliberate experimental arrangement. It excludes one source of divergence so we can follow another. That is useful, but it is not a view of all possible disturbances, bodies or encounters. A result from one beginning and observation window does not settle the capacities of a rule family.

Earlier room text identified an intermediate dial region as computation's only home and treated it as the privileged place for life. That conclusion was assigned rather than measured here. The literature also contains direct re-examinations: [Mitchell, Hraber and Crutchfield (1993)](https://csc.ucdavis.edu/~evca/Papers/rev-edge.html) obtained different results from an evolutionary CA experiment and challenged the earlier interpretation. Their study is a reason to specify the task and evidence, not a proof that every criticality hypothesis is false.

QFEP remains a research lens for asking about persistence, suppression and becoming. This installation does not measure thermodynamic entropy, free energy or a universal queer threshold. Its narrower contribution is executable: show how a difference is carried, attenuated, rounded away, or made visible again by an instrument. Queer possibility can enter through questioning the permitted states and the criteria of a successful outcome. It need not be confined to a band that the interface has already painted green.

The next hall approaches form through recursion. The distinction to carry forward is methodological: where is resemblance produced by local exchange, and where is it explicitly instructed? Similar-looking bodies may have inherited very different conditions of existence.
