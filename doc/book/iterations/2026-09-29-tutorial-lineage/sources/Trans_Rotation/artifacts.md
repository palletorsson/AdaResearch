1. **rotation_wall_crossings** at (19,11): three 8.4 m centre-mounted blades. Y crosses level banks; X/Z reach grid landings 3 m higher. The reversed rotation holds each crossing pose twelve seconds. Grid wedges return to the floor.
2. **rotation_array_compare** at (19,25): four full-size 2 by 6 cube arrays. X/Y/Z/XYZ share eighteen-degree row increments and fixed centres. Aisles preserve a viewing and bypass route.
3. **transform_composition_workbench** at (29,39): starts with X30° and Y45° applied in both orders, with intermediate forms visible. The pair slider also compares rotation with uniform scale. Restored existing artifact, using `initial_pair:4` and `workings:trace`.
4. **axial_sweep** at (14,39): approach the 1.7 m viewing plate, follow a rotating cube edge, then reveal its full-turn boundary; change to the triangular profile and compare the cone. Placed after the arrays and before the tunnel.
5. **boolean_tunnel** at (19,42): five hollow segments, eighteen degrees per segment, fifteen metres of continuous-floor enclosure. Static spatial repetition.
6. **carousel_cake**: cake, column, ziggurat, spindle and flare at 0.4 scale. All five share eight layers and the same angular speeds; profile changes radius, silhouette and rim speed.
