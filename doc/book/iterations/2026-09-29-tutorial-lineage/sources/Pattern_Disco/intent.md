# Pattern_Disco — a familiar floor, another choice

The first hall of tiling returns to the monochrome disco after Colour. Preserve the familiar arrangement so the new palette can be experienced as a changed relation. Let a chosen tile lead into the nested palette lookup, then let the visitor leave the console and walk.

The shared palette is a specific limit: one choice reaches every address carrying the same value, unlike the earlier grid's local colour edit. Five modes and four prepared pairs are enough for the comparison. SUPER TRIANGLE applies that pair inside the square and can retain an unfinished turn while the palette changes.

The body can follow, cross or miss a rhythm without having its footsteps entered into the score. Musical and visual clocks remain distinct; generated musical events can still brighten floor columns. Do not treat synchronisation as the required outcome.

Foundry should follow as an appetite for making a source rather than a summary of all controls. Native desktop checks verify palette/phase, actual geometry and selected walks; they do not establish listening quality, headset comfort or learner response.
