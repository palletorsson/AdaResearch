# The reach of a choice

The palette button makes a shared decision. Every square carrying the same calculated value receives the same new colour. The visitor has a choice, but its reach is already arranged: this console cannot recolour one square independently. The earlier palette grid offered that local exception; the returning floor makes the contrast available to see.

The moving pattern and musical score have separate clocks. Neither requires the body to reproduce it. A visitor can cross a band, miss a pulse or keep another rhythm. The floor's regularity can support a movement that departs from it. This gives the hall's queer opening a concrete place: in the possible relation between an offered order and the body answering it, without declaring a particular colour or shape inherently queer.

Keep this permission distinct from a claim that the visitor is outside the program. The choices, collisions and clocks remain authored. Pattern_Foundry follows by letting the visitor change the source that many copies read. The inquiry moves from selecting among offered arrangements towards making a decision that others will repeat.
