# What the criterion leaves alive

Changing TARGET changes the order without changing the bodies. That consequence locates a small politics of evaluation: an apparently intrinsic quality, being the best, depends on a relation to an authored criterion. A number becomes consequential because the selection routine uses it to decide which descriptions are copied and which receive more chances to become parents.

The ring opens another relation. The builder represents it and the evaluator omits it. An unchanged score does not imply an unchanged body. An elite can carry this uncounted part forward; its survival is not evidence that the criterion selected for every feature it has. These are inspectable consequences of this implementation, not a complete model of biological evolution.

There is pleasure in an opening that the number does not know about. That pleasure can direct a new question without making a software defect into liberation. The estimator should be repairable. The research value is in learning to locate the mismatch, compare its effects and decide what should change. Adding the missing torus formula would address one omission; it would not establish that closeness to target volume exhausts the forms we might desire.

The instrument also limits refusal. HOLD preserves the algorithm's current leader, not any candidate the visitor chooses. It provides a witness against later change, but not yet an alternative selection policy. A future nomination control could keep a rejected candidate with the visitor's reason, without forcing that reason into a new numerical score. That would be a specific extension of agency worth testing.

The floor makes a further distinction bodily. Generated geometry can be visible and highly scored while offering no support. The old platforms can carry a visitor because the museum constructs collision independently of the genome. Selecting for an inhabitable space would require an explicit body, dimensions, support, connectivity and a test of movement. We cannot infer those from a silhouette or a good volume estimate.

The box factor and missing torus are concrete discrepancies; finite search, limited representation and limited measurement are broader constraints that survive their repair. This hall adds no measured entropy or universal threshold. Its contribution to the red thread is methodological: desire can notice a possibility the current evaluation does not register, and code gives us somewhere precise to investigate that difference.
