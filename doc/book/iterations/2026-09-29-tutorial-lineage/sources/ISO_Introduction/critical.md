# The boundary is a decision we can inspect

The field assigns values where the mesh shows nothing. A missing triangle is not an absence of data; it may be the consequence of a classification shared by neighbouring samples. The rendered object makes one selected relation visible.

Holding the field fixed while changing its level makes that relation available to question. We can identify which choice changed without attributing every difference to an unknowable generator. The level is useful precisely because it is explicit. Its usefulness does not make it a natural division between inside and outside.

Sampling adds another condition. A feature too narrow for the lattice may fail to appear, while a coarse approximation may imply an opening the continuous field does not support. The live collider adds a further bodily condition; the held specimen deliberately has none. Similar images can therefore offer different possibilities of contact.

This is where the tutorial and the question of possible bodies meet. Before naming a form free, closed or queer, follow an operation: which comparison made this boundary, which sample could not carry that detail, and which collision permits or refuses contact? The construction gives us a way to revise a limit. It does not establish that greater complexity or a lower threshold constitutes freedom.
