# The room wears the sum

Noise_6_Wall carries the field from the voxel room's occupancy decisions into a material. Two placements of shader_noise_space form one primary encounter: a four-patch panel inside an enterable enclosure with its retained sphere. The independent dark_sphere remains a supporting comparison, with its brief return in the text retained. All three authored placements remain.

FREEZE holds the current local phase, colours and density. LAYERS sends 1, 2, 4 or 6 cumulative cloud terms to the enclosure and sphere. BASIS changes the shader generator. DRESS compares the greyscale cloud sum with the complete pink finish at the same held moment. The four patches retain their fixed counts.

The loop halves amplitude, doubles frequency and folds signed noise through abs before adding it. Weights, sampled values and display encoding jointly determine the image. Each wall piece and the sphere have their own UV mapping. Material variation changes the visible surface without changing its geometry or collision; the separate orb keeps its own clock.
