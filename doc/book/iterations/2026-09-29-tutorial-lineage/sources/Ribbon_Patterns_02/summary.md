# What the surface contributes

Two primary comparisons turn the collection scaffold into a lesson. SimultaneousContrast holds two grey targets fixed while their surrounds and connecting bar change. MetamerismLab is configured as a flat/curved carrier study with one shared pattern, matched metre spacing, repeat boundaries and an explicit directional shading model. The inherited token name does not imply spectral simulation.

Ten supporting exhibits remain. Both primary consoles are manually controlled and support undo/reset. The next room is Ribbon_Patterns_03, the closing shader and fabric studio; garment transfer remains to be built there.
