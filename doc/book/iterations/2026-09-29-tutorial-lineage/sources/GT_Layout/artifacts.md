> Scatter nodes. Connect them with edges. Now let physics do the thinking. Repulsion pushes every node apart — Coulomb's law borrowed from electrostatics. Springs pull connected nodes together — Hooke's law borrowed from mechanics. The system oscillates, overshoots, dampens. Iterates. Eventually settles into something readable. No one designed the layout. It emerged.
