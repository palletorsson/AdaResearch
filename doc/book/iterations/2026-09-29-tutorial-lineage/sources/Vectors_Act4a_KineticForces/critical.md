# What a return leaves behind

The mower returns to its starting address while its lawn remains cut. Its path length increases and its signed nominal work can cancel. These differences are observable because the apparatus keeps distinct records. None is a full account of what happened. The old positive-only work counter concealed this distinction by treating every displacement as more of the same accomplishment.

The revised counter still has a limit: its force is chosen, not measured from the visitor's effort. Naming that choice makes a narrower but more defensible argument. Work against one nominal vector cannot stand for labour, usefulness or every material change. The grass is a counterexample inside the same scene, rather than an abstract objection attached afterward.

The drag corridor asks which body a medium reaches. A rigid probe receives -b v as force, divided by its mass through the solver. A desktop or XR stick request receives a chosen speed factor. A person's tracked physical steps receive neither. All three can inhabit one coloured strip. Shared appearance does not guarantee shared susceptibility, and the mapping can be switched off or bypassed.

The ring reverses the direction of explanation: a path is prescribed, then the acceleration it requires is drawn. That can teach the cost of a turn, provided we do not claim the arrow caused the path. Correcting its independent speed mappings matters because the visual argument depends on the readout agreeing with the movement.

For the research question of possible bodies, the method is to identify the state, the receiving rule and the omissions before generalising from an image. The field chapter separated velocity assignment from accumulation. This hall separates physical response, controller adaptation, annotation and prescribed movement. It offers specific changes to investigate; it computes no entropy threshold and does not equate a particular motion with freedom.
