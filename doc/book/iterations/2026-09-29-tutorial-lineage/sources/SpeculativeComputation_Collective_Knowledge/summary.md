# Contribute, then reconsider

Does more activity mean more voices?

The situated panels preserved separate views. This machine sends contributions from a rim into one shared form. Primary: `peer_production_tool`.

Hold the arrivals, change from order colour to source colour, and follow a landed piece back to its source. Mark it disputed and revise that piece without increasing the pile. The dispute remains until explicitly cleared: a change is not automatically a settlement.

PIECES, SOURCES, EVER, ADDITIONS, PARTICIPATIONS and REVISIONS describe different counts. The earlier CONTRIBUTORS label could make repeated source-version participation look like a growing community; its counting history remains, now named.

Many sources can still feed a single centre. The next machine changes the rule of connection. Supporting tokens remain: `the_fourth_limit`, `wiki_fragment`, `the_commons_table`. Their presence does not imply the separate wiki circuit is complete.
