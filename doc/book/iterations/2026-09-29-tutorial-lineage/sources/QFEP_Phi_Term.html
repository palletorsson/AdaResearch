# Giving change a sign

Can change have a direction without that direction already meaning improvement?

<!-- @phi_slider -->

Move PHI through zero and watch the particles belonging to the slider. Compare a negative setting with a positive setting of similar magnitude. The local feedback changes direction and colour: one side sends particles down, the other sends them up. The handle starts at zero, and nothing is released. Once you have moved it, bringing it back near zero only slows the release to a crawl; watch the speed rather than waiting for silence. The pattern behind the rail keeps moving.

The handle gives you a signed parameter. Its sign distinguishes two directions of weighting, while its distance from zero gives the magnitude. In the project expression, phi multiplies a term associated with change in entropy. A weight, a measured change and the resulting response are three separate quantities, even when a display uses one colour to connect them.

Compare the midpoint with two settings on opposite sides. Crossing zero changes the sign; moving further along the same side changes magnitude without another sign change. These are distinct comparisons you can repeat on the rail. If you describe both simply as “more change”, you lose what the signed control makes available. Name the direction first and then how strongly it is weighted, before asking what a receiving system would do with that value.

After trying both sides, press EARLIER LABELS. RESIST and EMBRACE appear above the same rail. Nothing else has changed. Does the upward movement feel different once it has been called an embrace? You can hide the words again, although you may still carry them into the next movement of your hand.

The slider’s particles make the sign easy to recognise. They are visual feedback for its own value, not an instrument measuring whether a nearby system is becoming more open, more rigid or more alive.

<!-- @transforming_pattern -->

Now watch two cells in the transforming pattern. Compare their rises, sizes and colours over time. They share prescribed waves but meet those waves at different phases, so the whole can keep changing while individual places follow related rhythms.

This pattern currently runs independently of PHI. Moving the nearby handle does not control its waves. The room has two representations of change beside one another; their independence is part of this comparison. Bringing them under one control would be another experiment, with another rule to explain.

You can nevertheless make a careful observation: equal-looking states at two moments need not describe the same trajectory. One cell may be rising through a height while another is falling through it. A snapshot loses that direction unless the representation preserves some history.

Consider a pattern worth protecting from an imposed change. Downward, negative or resistant need not mean bad. Conversely, rapid variation can erase something valuable. The sign of a project parameter cannot decide which history deserves continuity. Before that judgement, someone has to decide what counts as change and whether the resulting score is something to raise or lower.


On the way to the door, eight small bodies stand in a row with λ numbers on their plinths. That rail reviews the previous hall in miniature; it carries no φ control, and nothing on it hears the rail you have just moved.

The next room provides a control connected to a changing pattern. We will keep the parameter, the observed response and the interpretation distinct while comparing them.

# Phi Term

φ is the attitude toward change. Negative φ resists; positive φ welcomes. Build a room where your φ choice reshapes what the world lets you do.

Declare the phi dial.

```gdscript
class_name PhiDial
extends Node3D

signal phi_changed(value: float)

@export var value: float = 0.0

func set_value(v: float) -> void:
    value = clamp(v, -1.0, 1.0)
    phi_changed.emit(value)
```

One exported value, clamped to [-1, 1]. Negative resists, zero is neutral, positive welcomes. The signal is how the world hears it.

Grab the dial.

```gdscript
func _on_dial_grabbed(offset: float) -> void:
    set_value(value + offset * turn_gain)
    dial_label.text = "φ = %+.2f" % value
```

The learner turns a physical dial. Each degree updates φ. The label shows the sign explicitly because the sign is the lesson.

Apply φ to a morphing object.

```gdscript
func _on_phi_changed(v: float) -> void:
    morph_target.blend_shapes = _shape_for(v)
    morph_target.metallic = 0.3 + 0.5 * clamp(v, 0.0, 1.0)
```

The object becomes smoother and more iridescent as φ rises. Negative φ hardens it. Zero holds a neutral pose.

Let the rate of change matter.

```gdscript
func delta_energy(prev_state: float, state: float, dt: float) -> float:
    return (state - prev_state) / max(dt, 0.0001)
```

ΔE/Δt is the instantaneous rate. φ multiplies this rate. The formula's third term is a response to motion, not to position.

Accumulate the φ·ΔE contribution.

```gdscript
func phi_contribution(v: float, prev: float, state: float, dt: float) -> float:
    return v * delta_energy(prev, state, dt)
```

Positive φ amplifies fast change into reward. Negative φ amplifies it into penalty. The same rate becomes two very different forces depending on sign.

Paint the room's floor by φ sign.

```gdscript
func tint_floor(v: float) -> void:
    var mat: StandardMaterial3D = floor_mesh.material_override
    if v < 0.0:
        mat.albedo_color = Color(0.3, 0.2, 0.2)
    else:
        mat.albedo_color = Color(0.2, 0.3, 0.4)
```

Burgundy floor for preservation; cool blue for becoming. The room changes under the learner's feet with the dial.

Display the political reading.

```gdscript
func update_reading(v: float) -> void:
    if v < -0.2: reading.text = "conservative"
    elif v > 0.2: reading.text = "queer"
    else: reading.text = "neutral"
```

The reading names φ without euphemism. The formula is a politics; the dial lets the learner declare one.

You have built the attitude term. The next map, Edge Of Chaos, zooms into where φ-positive systems find their most productive balance.
<<</MAP>>>

Lock the dial if the learner requests it.

```gdscript
func lock_dial(locked: bool) -> void:
    dial.can_turn = not locked
    dial_lock_label.text = "locked" if locked else ""
```

Locking preserves a chosen φ while the learner explores. The dial stays readable but stops accepting input.

# A signed control before its interpretation

The Lambda hall separated a recommendation from its surroundings. Here the PHI rail separates sign, magnitude and the language used to interpret them. The museum uses the existing phi_slider with sign_study:on and starts at zero. Its original XR handle remains full size on a plinth, 1.1 metres above the floor.

The hall-specific feedback uses equal speeds at equal magnitudes, opposite vertical directions and no particle gravity. Zero stops new emission; already emitted particles may survive for their two-second lifetime. The original legacy feedback favoured upward motion and continued emitting at zero. Other placements retain that legacy behaviour.

```gdscript
phi = (position / rail_length) * 2.0 - 1.0
particle_mat.direction = Vector3.UP * signf(phi)
particle_mat.initial_velocity_min = absf(phi) * 0.20
particle_mat.initial_velocity_max = absf(phi) * 0.20
_particles.emitting = not is_zero_approx(phi)
```

NEGATIVE / ZERO / POSITIVE remain on an evenly marked rail. EARLIER LABELS / HIDE reveals or hides the old RESIST / EMBRACE interpretation on a backed sign. That button changes text only: it does not move PHI, alter the wave pattern, or choose a moral classification. Blue and pink, upward and downward remain authored choices, available to question.

The sign study does not join the global qfep_phi_controllers group. The transforming_pattern runs independently from its prescribed wave field; its 6 by 6 cells are enlarged to 0.15 metres for comparison. The preserved pattern, rigid sculpture, fluid form, lambda promenade and two existing secondary teleporter tokens remain placed. The map uses its authored placements, with four display plinths and level circulation.

The QFEP expression in the project documents is QFE = F - lambda E + phi Delta E. A signed weight is not a measurement of change. For Delta E=+0.5, phi=+1 adds +0.5 to the score, while phi=-1 adds -0.5. If the score is minimized, the first increases cost with F and E held fixed. Reversing Delta E reverses those contributions. Zero phi makes this contribution zero, not the world's change zero. This rail does not measure entropy or run an optimizer.

Delta E names a difference between specified observations. It becomes a rate only after division by the elapsed interval. An entropy claim also needs defined states, probabilities, units and normalization. The wave field supplies none of those through its animation alone.

The earlier technical and critical drafts are archived in design-proposals.md. They describe intended connections, not shipped behaviours. Headset operation and what readers learn remain distinct from the recorded desktop signal and route checks.

Concept: φΔE(S,t) — sensitivity to the rate of change. Negative φ resists change (conservative: preserve current patterns). Positive φ embraces becoming (queer: welcome transformation). φ is the political term in the formula.
Sequence role: Fifth map in QFEP Lab; introduces the final and most radical term. After λ set the balance between order and entropy, φ determines the system's relationship to change itself. This is where the QFEP formula becomes explicitly queer: φ > 0 means preferring becoming over being, process over state, transformation over preservation; follows QFEP_Lambda_Spectrum; leads to QFEP_Edge_Of_Chaos.
Technical angle: φΔE(S,t) as the temporal derivative term — how fast entropy is changing, weighted by sensitivity. φ < 0 resists entropy increase (rigid_sculpture, preserved_pattern); φ > 0 embraces it (fluid_form, transforming_pattern). The phi_slider lets the learner feel the difference: same λ, different φ, radically different behavior.
Critical angle: φ is where the formula becomes ethical. Every system, every institution, every identity has a φ value — a disposition toward change. Conservative systems (φ < 0) resist perturbation, return to equilibrium. Queer systems (φ > 0) use perturbation as material for transformation. Neither is inherently better, but the curriculum's thesis — made explicit here — is that life and creativity require φ > 0.
Key artifacts: phi_slider controls the rate-sensitivity parameter; rigid_sculpture and preserved_pattern demonstrate φ < 0 (resistance to change); fluid_form and transforming_pattern demonstrate φ > 0 (embrace of becoming); tt for navigation.

# A word arrives after the movement

The particles have already gone up. Then a word arrives: embrace.

The button changes the inscription while leaving the movement alone. We can ask whether it also changes our account of what happened. Hiding the word gives us the earlier display again; it cannot promise the earlier reading.

The unlabelled experience was already shaped. A rail offers two directions. One rises, one falls. Colours distinguish them. A body brings associations to all of this. The first display is a place from which to examine these choices, rather than an escape from interpretation.

Behind the rail the pattern keeps moving. At zero it still moves. A system's refusal to give change weight does not stop that change from occurring elsewhere. Nor does our ability to move a control establish authority over every object that shares its theme.

A pattern worth protecting can need resistance to imposed change. A damaging arrangement can survive by demanding continuity. The word alone cannot choose between them. In the QFEP documents, keeping models revisable includes keeping open the capacities of those their terms exclude. The question reaches the person setting the terms: what have we already made easier to call an embrace?

This encounter makes the older judgement available to examine. It does not measure the ethics of the visitor or assign a queer identity to a parameter setting.
