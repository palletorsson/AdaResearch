Waves move through space. Ripples spreading from a dropped stone, sound radiating from a speaker, light expanding from a star. Here oscillation gains direction — amplitude at every point, phase shifting with distance, interference where waves meet.

The `wave_propagation_3d` makes all three mechanisms legible in one field: phase changing with distance from source, amplitude falling by inverse-square, two wavefronts summing constructively where aligned and cancelling where opposed. `kusama_sine` stipples the field with polka-dots displaced by the wave so you can read the pattern at a glance. A wave is not an object — it is a disturbance passing through a medium, the medium barely moving while the pattern travels. To exist in a medium is to be distorted by it. This is the QFEP environment term rendered as physics.
