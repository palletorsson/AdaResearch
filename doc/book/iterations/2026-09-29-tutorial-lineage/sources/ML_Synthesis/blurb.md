Everything learned, gathered on floating platforms above a void. Three islands. Three problems. One question: what holds them together.

A creature with random joints thrashes, fails, thrashes again — and walks. Reinforcement learning as bodily negotiation. The agent doesn't know its own shape until it moves. Policy gradient descent through a space of possible gaits, each trial burning a path between chaos and coordination. Reward is just surprise reduction with a direction.

On another platform, anomaly detection draws boundaries. Isolation forests, z-scores, autoencoder reconstruction error — three ways of asking "does this belong?" The answer depends on who built the model of belonging. Every threshold encodes a politics of normalcy.

The synthesis is not combination. It is the moment the tools turn back on themselves — the model that questions its own predictions, the agent that learns it cannot fully see the landscape it descends. Optimization all the way down, and no ground floor.
