## Evidence already available

The actual-scene primary probe passes 23 checks. The fan probe drives the scene with supplied positions and presses its actual NEW OUTLINE button callback. It finds four triangles from each start; the second has two triangle centroids inside the excluded notch. The summed areas are approximately 0.8 and 1.44 square metres, against a boundary area of 0.8. The larger sum includes overlaps and is not a union-area measurement.

These checks establish behavior for this specified input. They do not demonstrate a person's comprehension or ease of drawing. [Implementation and scope](technical.md); [dated evidence record](../../../doc/space/point-triangle-focus-2026-09-16/README.md).

## Next useful observation

When a headset is available, let a learner close the initial triangle without explaining the fill in advance. Then observe whether they can distinguish the boundary, the fill and its visibility. For the L, watch whether the one-second timing, snapping and START indicator let them preserve the intended six corners. A mistake in hand placement is not evidence against the specified fan comparison.

If exact drawing dominates attention, a later improvement could offer the same stored L with an explicit choice of starting corner. That would isolate input order while retaining the free drawing tool. No preset selector or fan-quality warning is installed by this pass.
