## Working loop

Whole → sequence → map → evidence → map → sequence → whole.

Before descending, keep a short position: **question; sequence/map; inherited decisions; why local work matters**. Before leaving, record: **finding or change; reason; status; source; affected neighbours; open question**. Update existing summaries and notes together when a teaching decision changes. Carry back only findings that affect the larger view.

A local discovery may change the sequence. A changed sequence may change the larger argument. Check both directions. Resume from the recorded question; do not reconstruct intent from the latest artifact alone.

Parallel readers can inspect different sequences against one saved state. Return claims and evidence, not automatic edits. A synthesis pass must inspect connections between their sequences. Shared assumptions and agreement are not independent verification.

Pilot: [4 October test](../../iterations/2026-10-04-context-roundtrip-test/index.html) source-checked thirteen frozen inputs; one fresh reader retained 8/8 required distinctions from 402 words. This tests one explicit handoff, not long-term reliability, runtime behavior or curriculum quality. Keep source hashes and claim scope beside compressed findings.

Judge fidelity, teaching progression and artistic force separately. Prefer "exact for this claim" and "sufficient for this encounter" over a permanent "perfect" label. Review mature sequences lightly unless evidence gives a reason to reopen them. A text/artifact gap may call for restoring an encounter; it does not automatically justify cutting text.
