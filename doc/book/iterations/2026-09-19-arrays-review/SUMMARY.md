## What the images establish

The Floor and Walls pairs are fresh actual-museum views from matching cameras and motif states. All six floor image planes were at Y = 0 before the fix and stand 0.025 m above the same floor after it. The striped interference is visibly removed. The bright magenta wireframe becomes subdued. Existing capture-mode height colours remain active in both runs; these images do not prove completely monochrome surroundings. No global capture flag or shader was changed.

The 3D pair uses the real artifact in an isolated scene. It exposed a second problem while testing the invitation: labels set to draw through geometry crossed the opaque instructions. Enabling depth testing alone hid them inside the cubes, so the lab also raises them from 0.6 m to 1.0 m above the cube root. Final images show visible addresses and clear instructions. Ordinary pickup animation continues, so cube phases need not match between the two runs.

The Disco pair captures the actual 800 × 300 screen with the same preset and cell selected. It proves a visible label correction, not how the sound or screen feels to a headset user.
