## Same material, different attention

Both use K1–K9, the same title, a comparable word budget, and the same unbuilt sleeve possibility. R1–R4 remain available. Neither adds a philosopher's name to stand in for an argument.

A explains the discarded draw before travelling historically. B distributes the definition and history across returns. Information, job and attitude are recorded separately for each actual paragraph.

This is an editorial reading, not Palle's verdict or a student-comprehension test. These are adaptations with rewritten bridges, not a blind experiment that changes only paragraph order.

## Provisional editorial reading

B is my provisional choice for Ada's final: the book gets to interrupt the seed bench, and the reader returns to both with a changed expectation. A makes the mechanism easier to follow and may better serve a reader arriving without the tutorial.
