A first bounded automatic investigation

The runner exports the production Godot geometry, sweeps triangle displacement and four-edge folding, then measures endpoint connections and the dimension spanned by the endpoints. These are diagrams of measured geometry, not screenshots or evidence of a headset visit.

The Godot exhibit moves automatically: six seconds to inspect each arrangement, then five seconds of rigid movement. The four-edge fold preserves its joins. The diagrams below are sampled geometry, not a recording of the animation.

Spatial arrangements, encountered as images

Only four monitors now appear in the hall. The animated 3D line sets exist in a private filming world and reach the visitor through a frontal monochrome view, an oblique ink image, a slowly orbiting contour image and an oblique scan-line image. There are no exposed rods, invisible grab targets or buttons.

Move beside a monitor: you see the screen become oblique, but cannot see around the object inside its image. Your viewpoint and the filming camera’s viewpoint have come apart. What suggests depth, and what can this view never establish?

The live source geometry retains its lengths, joins and changes of depth. The screen presents a 2D projection. The monitor itself is a spatial object in VR. These are different roles, even though all are computed.

Feeds use 384 × 288 pixels and at most twelve updates per second while visible. Rendered checks confirm that the source geometry is absent from the museum camera while changes to the animation still appear on its monitor. Filters alter the image, not the geometry. This is separate evidence from the endpoint measurements below.

  • Two skew segments already require three coordinates to describe their full arrangement.
  • The tested triangle closes in a plane. Every tested positive displacement of one whole edge opens its endpoint joins.
  • Every sampled four-edge fold keeps a single closed endpoint loop and 0.6 m side lengths. Positive folds in this sweep leave the plane.
  • This bounded experiment finds a closed nonplanar outline with four segments. It is not an exhaustive search or a proof of every possible arrangement.

What is not measured

  • Endpoint joins only: interior crossings are not treated as graph vertices.
  • Dimensions describe the ideal segment endpoints; visual rods have thickness.
  • No faces, solid interiors, collision clearance, bodily comfort or aesthetic quality are inferred.

How this can travel to another room

Give the experiment one question, an explicit set of permitted changes, quantities to keep fixed, observations to record, and a stopping condition. Run it against the real artifact code. Keep counterexamples and failed candidates beside the attractive ones. Use the report to propose the next encounter; a measured geometric difference still needs an embodied and editorial reading.
