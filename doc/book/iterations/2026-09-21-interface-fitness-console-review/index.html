Something to press. Something to hold.

The interface is a compact physical object. Buttons move into their backing when touched; the fader can be grabbed or dragged with a pointer. Titles, numbers and captions stay flat on surfaces. RESET restores the starting population while keeping the chosen reward and inertia.

The same agents, another reward

The rule changes immediately; the swarm responds over time. Its mathematical behaviour is preserved. The interface makes the choice and its persistence easier to notice.

A pattern other artifacts can use

The shared choice console combines up to four physical choices, one continuous parameter and reset. Its owner controls the selection and readout, so configuration changes and direct interaction tell the same story. It reuses the existing physical buttons and the grammar fader, with an explicit continuous mode.

Shared repairs also ensure that untouched buttons display their configured colour and that a disappearing pointer cannot leave a cap pressed. A hand still touching the button retains its own press.

Evidence and the next step

45 fitness checks exercise physics touch, ray hits, cap travel, XR Tools grab and release, state updates and reset. 46 grammar and 23 Shannon checks protect the earlier shared-control work.

Headset comfort, tracked-hand behaviour and Quest performance remain untested. Next is the edge-of-chaos interface, where different configurations deliberately grant or withhold control. Improving its presentation must preserve that distinction.
