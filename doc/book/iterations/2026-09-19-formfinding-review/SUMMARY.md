## What the local check changes about the received review

See [historical evidence comparison](descent-historical-evidence.json) and [original check](../../../research/possible-bodies/descent-landscape-check.json). **Grid overlap did not establish an overhead placement.** Relaxation's current bench is on the floor, about 5.10 m horizontally from the museum visitor's arrival. Its map placement is preserved. The Q tank was also already on the floor; its original front faced through an outer wall. A local tank/bench relocation clears that approach and reserves its footprint without removing partitions.

**Authored dimensions do not equal installed dimensions.** The museum's route repair still scales Q to 0.5 even after the placement repair. The tank installs at 2.45 m wide, its rim 0.525 m high, its wells 0.475/0.15 m deep and its cube 0.14 m across. The manuscript uses relative depth and a concrete approach instead of repeating unscaled source dimensions. Full-scale installation remains a shared routing question; this pass does not change the museum loader. Earlier artifact-relative camera views were below standing eye height and are retained only as diagnostics.

The final [standing view into the deep well](search/standing/Annealing/q-deep-side.png) shows its cube from the outside edge at world eye height 1.7 m. The front view makes the depth less legible: all mesh normals point up. Better surface shading and the low control's reach belong on the next encounter pass. The museum wall card's displayed line now invites comparison before INTERPRET; its existing editorial note is retained.

**The whole-cloth claim needed a measurement.** At 420 total undriven steps, increasing passes from one to four improves both mean absolute relative spring error (1.409% → 0.359%) and worst error (34.276% → 8.936%). The useful critical question is what a small average conceals. It is not evidence that the worst spring necessarily deteriorates. The live display is continuous; the quoted comparison is a separate finite source experiment.

**Two secondary works were present in the map but excluded by the loader.** The cathedral and end table had valid scene paths and authored positions, but no `map_ready` flag. Their restoration retains existing work. Their recovery does not make the static models physical solvers.
