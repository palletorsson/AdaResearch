# Measurement references and changes to the supplied poster

The user's poster supplies the seven subjects and ordinary-object vocabulary. The requested room order and ball-pit replacement supply the spatial brief. The implementation uses explicit models where the poster's examples alone would not justify a measurement.

- **Shannon and conditional entropy:** Yury Polyanskiy and Yihong Wu, [MIT Information Theory lecture notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-441-information-theory-spring-2016/pages/lecture-notes/). Entropy concerns a declared random variable and its distribution; conditional entropy concerns uncertainty remaining after a specified observation. The implementation's chair colour and identity channels are finite teaching examples.
- **Boltzmann/statistical entropy:** [MIT: A Statistical Definition of Entropy](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node56.html), and [Statistical Mechanics I lecture notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/pages/lecture-notes/). The chair apparatus uses fixed colour totals and marked sites to make a configurational multiplicity finite. A mere scatter with unchanged constraints would not itself establish a change in Ω. Independent enumeration gives 1, 16, 36, 16, 1 assignments for left counts 0–4.
- **Algorithmic complexity:** Peter Grünwald and Paul Vitányi, [Algorithmic information theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.2754). A short seed-based recipe can generate an irregular-looking arrangement. The encounter compares candidate descriptions, with shared source code disclosed; it does not claim to calculate the shortest program or treat visual disorder as a monotonic complexity measure.
- **Dynamical entropy:** [Entropy and Ergodicity of Boole-Type Transformations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8623432/) discusses the doubling map and its generating binary partition. The installed door uses a finite symbolic calculation over 1,024 starting-bin midpoints, at most eight symbols. The counterexample is a contracting rule. Neither a self-closing door nor divergence of two trajectories alone establishes an entropy rate; the door is an interface to these declared update rules.
- **Thermodynamic entropy:** [MIT: Calculation of Entropy Change in Some Basic Processes](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/SPRING/propulsion/notes/node40.html), especially heat exchange between bodies. The model accounts for cup and surroundings separately: C ln(T/T₀) for each constant-capacity body. Cup entropy falls while total entropy rises. Steam is a visual cue, with no phase-change model. Digital RESET is not physical reversal.
- **Min-entropy:** [NIST CSRC glossary](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/min_entropy). H-min = −log₂(max p) concerns the most likely outcome. The large ball makes perceptual salience distinct from probability: equal ID weights, grouped colour outcomes and explicitly changed weights produce different answers without requiring a change in size.
