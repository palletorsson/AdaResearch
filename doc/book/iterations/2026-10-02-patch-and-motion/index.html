The cord has the same kind of skin as the lines we have been making. Here its ends also carry an agreement: this output may reach that input. The drawing has acquired a consequence. What would we connect if a line could give something else permission to move?

After a path acquires a surface

This application stays with the later geometry workbench in LSystems_Grammars_And_Curves, before Architecture. The first exercise still changes the number of vertices around a ring. Patching then adds a use for the surface: named, compatible endpoints form a route.

The kinetic study draws on Tinguely’s mechanisms and Saint Phalle’s rounded coloured bodies. Their collaboration is part of the reference, not an invented pairing. These are newly constructed Ada figures, not copies of a particular sculpture. See Myths & Machines, an exhibition made with the Niki Charitable Art Foundation.

What is working, and what is supplied

The patch_and_motion preset uses the existing UniversalVRAudioController, EurorackRack, ModuleFaceTexture controls, XR pickup and physical lever. Its bounded live audio graph supports one oscillator, one optional low-pass filter, an amplifier/pan stage and a speaker input. Other presets retain their prior sound behavior.

The motor is a gate-controlled kinematic animation, not simulated electrical power or rigid-body machinery. The hand-drawn tube network has no audio or fluid routing. Gate and audio are different socket kinds; crossing geometry alone creates neither connection. Cord meshes rebuild when their ends move. The state lasts for this hall visit.

Desktop pointer/grab, native XR-tools pickup, disconnect cleanup, actual filtered audio, independent motor stopping and museum aisle clearance have automated checks. Physical headset comfort and Quest performance still need a hardware pass.
