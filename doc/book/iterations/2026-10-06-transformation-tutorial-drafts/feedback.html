Why not add visited now?

The distinction is valuable, but history is already part of the student's work. Trace retains positions and saves the drawing for Grid. Pre retains elapsed time. Pit retains opening progress, so its current pose depends on the preceding approach or departure. These are different uses of state; they should not all be called historical records.

Body's selected study recalculates a transform from the current distance. The installed cut gives us a strong comparison with a lasting consequence. A visited flag would add another behaviour to build, explain, show and reset. A one-bit visit record also cannot reconstruct the route or geometry of that cut. Keep it available as an optional future extension when remembering an encounter becomes the lesson's object.

Translation likewise does not need an unused array declaration to suggest a longer route. Arrays were already introduced in Trace. The useful return here is that ordered positions could prescribe movement as well as record it. Traversing that list would need its own complete, tested continuation.

What to carry forward

The tutorial selects a relation the learner can reconstruct and vary. The reflection can meet a wider field of artifacts. Their different coverage is intentional.

Use two editorial questions: what survives this transformation, and what becomes possible because of it? Keep them as a test of the writing; repeating them as headings in every hall would flatten the encounters.

The reviews support architectural readiness. They do not establish human comprehension or headset comfort. A successful learner reconstruction remains separate evidence.
