# Release one; another waits

The stand supplies one fresh ball after release, when HOME is clear. Existing balls and individual traces remain. Reusing an old ball preserves its ink, starts a separate stroke after carrying and does not duplicate the waiting supply. Balls now collide with each other. Escaped bodies are parked in place instead of returned onto the stand.

Only the museum artifact and its registry description changed; the tutorial and reflective texts were not rewritten.

The production scene passed 34 native assertions and two authored placement/registry checks. The test covers real gravity and XR Tools pickup/release with a synthetic hand. Real controller input and prolonged headset use remain manual checks.
