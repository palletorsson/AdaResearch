What should a point have to do to be kept?

With a small sampling distance, the trace follows small turns. With a larger distance, fewer positions survive. Switch to Grid and another choice appears: where is a recorded position allowed to stand?

Draw, then look.

Pick up a Draw Dot pen in the Trace hall. Its moving tip supplies the monitor. Release it to replay that gesture. Look away and the replay holds; look back and it continues. Recording while you draw remains independent of your gaze.

In the desktop preview, hold Space and move the mouse; use the wheel to draw in depth. Release Space to replay. D supplies a labelled demonstration. Right-drag looks away, Home returns, and the arrow keys turn the camera around the three traces.
