An arena of randomized edges — cubes with profiles shaped by chance. The grid imposes order; randomness fractures it. Walk among forms that could have been otherwise, each edge a frozen coin flip.

The `random_edge_profile` is the core artifact: per-edge random decisions determine bevels, extrusions, and profile curves, so every cube is an individual whose form encodes its generation history. Pick up the `coin_toss` for binary random, the `dice_throw` for discrete uniform, and feel the mechanism before seeing its geometric consequence. `random_object_spawner` populates a field of distinct cubes from one algorithm. The cube is the sequence's primitive — maximally regular, six identical faces. Randomizing its edges is controlled violation of symmetry. Identity through entropy: each cube a record of what happened, not of what was designed.
