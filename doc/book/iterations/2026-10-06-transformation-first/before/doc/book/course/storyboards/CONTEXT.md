## Working loop

Whole → sequence → map → evidence → map → sequence → whole.

Before descending, keep a short position: **question; sequence/map; inherited decisions; why local work matters**. Before leaving, record: **finding or change; reason; status; source; affected neighbours; open question**. Update existing summaries and notes together when a teaching decision changes. Carry back only findings that affect the larger view.

A local discovery may change the sequence. A changed sequence may change the larger argument. Check both directions. Resume from the recorded question; do not reconstruct intent from the latest artifact alone.
