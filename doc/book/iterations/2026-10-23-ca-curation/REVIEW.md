# CA in the actual skyscraper

23 October 2026. Main floors 103–110. First functional integration and spatial
curation, following the current artifact-space discussion; not a certification
of the rest of the spine.

The source museum scenes, configurations and studies were still present. The
earlier broad transfer receipts established placement and load coverage. They
did not establish that every imported encounter worked in the main tower.
In particular, the CA trials used 42 × 32 m floors, while the real tower is
42 × 18 m. Some imported positions were outside the slab.

All 43 existing occurrences remain. The long fields now run across the real
floor, with their collecting basins transformed consistently. The layout has
clear operating approaches, bounded end bays, low unfinished partitions,
separate cube/wedge thresholds and work lamps. The original algorithms still
own their output.

Specific changes:

- Original laboratory door wired to its existing proximity sensor, rather than
  waiting for a palm scanner the imported room never supplied.
- Life and walkable-history controls remounted on the safe entry side, on
  concrete supports. Their poses are artifact-local and follow the exhibit.
- Three-state neighbourhood enlarged to 1.35×; its basin follows that scale.
- Paired exchange displays turned toward arrival, exposing their working face.
- SIR keeps all 64³ states and the original synchronous probabilities. An exact
  infectious-frontier evaluation replaces the very slow dense frame budget.
  A seeded comparison with the original dense rule produced identical state.
  Occupied cells are drawn rather than missed by a fixed stride. The display
  cap is 4096, and the readout reports full counts. Placement scale is explicitly
  0.21, height 2.85 m, to fit the full source domain inside this storey.
- Stress/crack rendering shows the original occupied cells, avoiding stride
  loss and unused instances parked far below the floor. STEP/RUN/RESET added
  to those two previously hard-to-operate specimens.
- New 16 m history wall on floor109 reads the same 32 × 24 stored rows as the
  original desk and floor. There is no second solver or clock. It computes the
  first 23 generations through that original source at entry, then follows its
  STEP, RUN, SEED and carried-point interactions. Pink cells, an off-white
  inspected address and steel supports make a local decision legible at room scale.
- Catalyst hatch, real pickup and negative-floor vent emission checked. The
  bracelet's wearing hand now rejects its own wrist handles before grab
  selection, allowing it to pick up the actual lesson point after catalyst pickup.
  Deferred controller restoration and pickup-collision teardown also guard the
  removed-rig case.

Verification is retained in `ada_run/ca-curation/`: 187 inspected passing
per-floor desktop/synthetic-XR checks; 96 geometry/arrival/horizontal-fit checks;
44 actual exported GLBs parsed; production editor move/turn/copy and save
validation on disposable arrangements. The final targeted native run exited
without engine errors. Failed attempts are retained with their diagnoses.
Five additional native checks verify a solid architectural receiver, actual
walking around its left, rear and right sides, and collision following its
moved/rotated placement. The editor was visually inspected with that receiver
selected and its position fields exposed (`ca-editor.jpg`).
The publisher merged only these eight CA floors into the latest saved layout
and verified that the other 197 floors, including the concurrent floor14 work,
were unchanged.

Limits: synthetic VR is not a physical-headset comfort test. Not every control
combination, combat interaction or same-process desktop-to-VR transition was
accepted. Procedural CA exhibits remain whole source-backed editor objects;
their generated cells are not individual editor parts. GLBs are static previews,
not live simulations. The original Clockmaker is a static ornamental work.
Collecting basins and the laboratory's authored lower steps extend below the
slab. These results do not certify Fractals or the other later sequences.

The new wall is the first stronger spatial experiment under Palle's latest
direction. Further rebuilding should be judged one encounter at a time: what
does its principal do, what output deserves architectural scale, where does the
body work, and what makes the room worth traversing? Empty space is a design
decision; unrelated crates are not a substitute for a strong encounter.
