## The walk

The chapter opens by refusing stillness. Every cube in the room is doing something periodic — rotating, scaling, cycling its transforms — and the three pickup cubes stage the lesson in the hand: hold the still one, then the turning one, and feel the difference between zero and frequency. The mario cube drags its own time-trace behind it like a receipt; the control_pendulum swings the oldest clock; the SphericalHarmonics bloom overhead as what waves do when you give them a sphere instead of a line.

## What it opens

*Everything oscillates* is stated here as an observation, not a theorem — the room simply contains no rest. The eye leaves calibrated to period and amplitude before either word has been used.
