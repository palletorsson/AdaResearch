## The turn

Column, dress, vowel: three proofs that the wave was never the property of physics. Culture has been composing in oscillation all along — architecture froze it, costume swings it, speech *is* it. The chapter turns here from demonstrating waves to recognizing them, and once you've seen the sine in a baroque column you will not stop seeing it anywhere cloth hangs or a voice rises.
