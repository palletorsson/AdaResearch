What this review proposed
  1. Make the opening comparison easier to discover. The rug has its own room; the row explorer is across a long divider. A clearer sightline or connection should let the visitor recognise the two as a comparison.
  2. Give the network breathing room. Its neighbouring decorative plinth is only about one metre away at the anchors. The overhead image shows both competing in the same small area. Relocate that secondary object before enlarging this exhibit.
  3. Simplify the lower-left pockets. Much of that part of the footprint is wall or narrow passage, with the showcase tucked into the far corner. Consolidating those pockets could give it a legible destination and a clearer return route.

This panel preserves the initial spatial diagnosis. The linked follow-up applies the changes and verifies them from visitor height and against collision geometry.
