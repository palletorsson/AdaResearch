# Three openings for the moving spheres

15 October 2026 · Random Definition · Comparative drafts for the final text.

Read each column from top to bottom. These are original passages trying three writing operations discussed with Palle, not imitations of an author's prose. First-person actions and responses are imagined narration, not recorded visitor feedback. The main room/final order remains moving spheres → RAND book → lamps → Geiger → MOVE / KEEP → Out / Back.

The references are Calvino's uncertain observer, Ponge's sustained attention to an object, and Lispector's attention to bodily present experience. The comparison holds the implemented encounter fixed: shared sideways targets, twice the rate in the middle, added vertical movement on the right, retained path replay, and the following page's touch-held group. No new artifact behaviour is proposed.

## The same next paragraph for all three

> On a large page, rows of five-digit groups enter from above and descend. Touch one group. It stays amber while the others continue. Here keeping interrupts a disappearance. It may begin with no better reason than that these were the numbers under a finger.

This is the current RAND opening, shown unchanged to test each handoff. Each candidate already anticipates some of it. If one is adopted, revise that overlap so the next paragraph develops the encounter rather than repeating the invitation.

## What changes in each reading

| A | 1: sideways and faster motion; try an explanation; tentative; speed seems sufficient. 2: added vertical motion; unsettle that explanation; questioning; direction matters too. 3: replay; make the surprise inspectable; investigative; a choice of attention is needed. 4: falling page; offer selective retention; curious; a chosen detail can remain. | The reader wants a prediction or generator experiment next, so the page feels like a diversion. |
| B | 1: paths and timing; establish three objects' capacities; exact; conditions are legible. 2: surface and contact; follow the object; attentive; touching does not alter its path. 3: replay without hand history; discover the record's limit; mildly unsettled; attendance and recording separate. 4: the page; offer contact with a visible consequence; curious; touch can change what remains. | The narrator's "permissions" and "mattered" frame the interpretation too soon, restoring the knowing guide. |

## Editorial estimate, before reader response

B currently makes the strongest bridge to RAND: contact that does not enter the retained motion record is followed by contact that visibly selects a group. A makes the clearest developing investigation, but may generate a need for a different next artifact. C is the strongest invitation to remain with the bodily encounter, with a more tentative bridge.

Provisional Ada alignment for B alone: 8/10 (subject/truth 2, learning/agency 1, situated encounter 2, wider possibility 1, voice/desire 2). This is an editorial estimate, not a reader result or a judgement of the full tutorial. Its strength is the concrete change in what touch can do. Its weakness is that it may announce the interpretation before the reader has discovered it. Leave the alternatives separate until a response identifies a useful sentence or transition.
