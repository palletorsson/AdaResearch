The trial changes where things stand, not what they do. `tutorial.md` and `final.md`
are unchanged. These are the sentences that describe the museum hall's geography and
will need a reader's decision once the trial's arrangement is accepted. Line numbers
refer to the files as of 2026-10-20.

## What this list is not

It is not a rewrite and not a claim that the museum hall's text is wrong. The museum
hall keeps its own arrangement; these notes exist so that nobody revises the texts by
accident while the trial is still being judged.
