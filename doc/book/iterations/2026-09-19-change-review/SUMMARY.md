The received critique is useful evidence about a frozen first pass, not a mandate to rewrite two developed chapters. [Paired views and exact text changes](../2026-09-19-review-comparison/index.html#change) accompany this report.

## The red thread holds

A pattern can stay fixed while the address reading it changes. Change begins there: the curve does not move, but different intervals report different rates. A wide secant falls while the analytic derivative at its first point rises. The disagreement gives the local question a purpose.

The same curve then supplies a different account. Sampling rules, rectangle counts and a held picture let a visitor examine how contributions become a total. The reassuring reference is another finite construction. At the bridge, positive and negative rates can recover a zero endpoint difference without erasing the rise and fall that produced it.

Flow makes the next address depend on the previous update. Its particle receives a prescribed direction and speed; it does not carry velocity or negotiate a force. This gives Forces a concrete addition to make: what happens when the body must carry something of its previous movement?

**Keep the sequence order and its mathematical density.** Its critical argument is already inside the comparisons: a correct report can discard the relation needed for another question; a drawn path can imply a crossing that the coordinate update never made.

## What remains open

The new layout suggests an order; it cannot establish how an unassisted learner will browse. A headset trial still needs to check controller reach, small signs and curves, the readability of arrow lag, and whether an individual particle can be followed through the eighty-particle drawing. The bridge retains its equation as a title; only its numerical comparison waits for a request.

Existing startup UID/certificate and renderer/cache diagnostics are kept in the logs. A focused passing probe is not a claim that the whole project is warning-free, that every secondary artifact works, or that a complete headset walk has passed.

The next sequence is Forces. Preserve the question at Flow's exit: what possibilities appear when a body cannot take a new direction immediately?
