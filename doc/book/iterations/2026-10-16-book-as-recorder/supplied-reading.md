# A critical reading of What Chance Is Allowed to Do

Ada Research · Eight-page illustrated edition · 16 October 2026

Having examined all eight pages, including their illustrations, typography, narrative sequence and reference material, I think the project is beginning to work as an artist's book, rather than simply documentation of a virtual installation.

My provisional rating is 8.5/10 as an artist's-book research prototype.

The strongest quality is that text, image and computation increasingly have different responsibilities. The prose lets us encounter the virtual world; the drawings preserve selected aspects of that world; and the numerical record on page 7 lets us inspect what the images themselves cannot establish.

But I also see a significant unrealized possibility: the book itself is performing many of the operations it describes—selecting, filtering, retaining, transforming and leaving things out. That could become a conscious part of its literary and artistic form.

The short sentence “Click. A gap. Another click.” introduces a different kind of encounter. The diagram shows the apparatus and its temporal marks, while the text supplies duration through language. This is one of the most convincing image–text relationships in the booklet.

Pages 5–6 — A gesture becomes a seed; a rule limits a journey

The writing becomes denser and more explanatory. The movement sampler and returning sphere are conceptually important, but neither receives an illustration. The absence creates a conspicuous change in the visual rhythm.

Page 7 — Numbers become verifiable material

The table of ten outputs from seed 435 is an exceptionally useful addition. It shows how one numerical sequence can be consumed differently by the colour window and falling page. This is not merely an appendix: the actual numbers become part of the artistic material.

Page 8 — The illustrations become a proposal

Path. Hold. Interval. offers a more abstract image language, deliberately separating the drawing from direct documentary evidence. It is an interesting direction, although this page currently reads as a studio note appended to the work rather than part of the finished composition.

The progression from spatial movement to retention, sound, bodily action, numerical verification and visual abstraction gives the edition a coherent conceptual structure.&#x20;

## 2. The illustrations: the most promising development

The choice to filter the VR captures into fine monochrome drawings is more consequential than it may first appear.

The images have begun to resemble scientific engravings, diagrams or plates from an older technical publication. Their visual restraint makes the strange computational instruments appear as objects of patient study.

That is appropriate for Ada Research, with its interest in reconstructing the relationship between artistic experimentation and mathematical knowledge.

But there is also a productive tension.

The original simulation contains movement, depth and colour. The printed illustrations retain outlines, selected marks and positions. The book loses information while making certain relationships easier to examine.

That is exactly what the Randomness chapter investigates.

The image is another recorder.

### The abacus drawing — page 2

The five rods, shown after equal numbers of movement steps

The drawing is strong because the repeated horizontal patterns make the relationship between speed and trajectory visible. The fifth row adds vertical displacement.

This image could become even better if we could more clearly distinguish the beads' selected targets from the circles accumulated between them.

That distinction matters scientifically: the density of marks is not necessarily the distribution of selected random destinations.

Currently, the circles are so pale that some of the evidence becomes hard to read at ordinary viewing size.

I would make the contrast between target and accumulated path a little stronger—not by adding decorative detail, but by distinguishing the two operations.

### The falling numbers — page 3

This may be my favourite illustration conceptually.

A page crowded with numbers is an unusual image to place in an artist's book, precisely because no individual number has an obvious reason to stand out.

Then one group is held.

But there is a contradiction: the text speaks about the selected digits becoming amber, while the printed image is monochrome. The selection becomes much less apparent.

I would consider introducing a small bracket or fine rectangular outline around 84288, as you already explore on page 8. That would make the intervention legible without depending on colour.

### The Geiger illustration — page 4

The line drawing removes the colour and visual complexity of the apparatus, while the timing marks remain. It looks like a technical object rather than an illustration of danger or radiation.

That is appropriate, because the detections are simulated.

However, the relation between the short and long gaps in the printed marks and the described audible sequence is not immediately readable. A small, carefully selected segment could provide a much more precise comparison.

The illustration could allow the reader to find one interval, hear it through the prose, and then encounter it again on replay.

The drawing would then do something that the text alone cannot.

## 3. Typography and page architecture

The typography is disciplined and largely successful.

The warm paper colour, generous margins, restrained serif type and small running headers give the edition the feeling of a coherent publication. The illustrations do not compete with the text for attention.

There is also a good contrast between the literary serif text and the more technical presentation of numbers on page 7.

The first four pages establish a rhythm in which the reader alternates between observing and reading. Pages 5 and 6 become much more text-driven.

That could be a deliberate acceleration towards the conclusion, but at present it feels more like the images stop just when the bodily consequences become most important.

The final sphere is arguably the chapter's strongest spatial event. Yet it exists only in prose.

I would test one small illustration of the release event: the sphere continues, the participant remains elsewhere, and the return is compulsory only for the object.

There is no need to render an elaborate VR room. A very simple spatial diagram might be more powerful.

## 4. The literary voice: strong observation, but still an explanatory habit

Three sentences are especially successful.

> The rods have not grown, but there is less time inside the same distance.

This is precisely the kind of writing we were searching for: an exact computational comparison opens an unusual experience of space.

> We have made a small still place in the fall.

This gives the act of keeping a number a bodily and temporal quality without turning it into an abstract philosophical statement.

> An acquired number has become a kept beginning.

This connects the historical receipt with the deterministic apparatus. It is clear and evocative without pretending that the subsequent simulated events contain fresh atmospheric randomness.

All three sentences work because a particular operation produces the strangeness.

Where the voice becomes weaker is in its repeated desire to explain the conceptual significance of those operations.

Page 5, for instance, spends considerable space explaining seeds, hashing and gesture recording. This information is necessary for scientific accountability, but it arrives in a fairly continuous didactic register.

I might preserve the most important technical details and let page 7 carry more of the evidential burden.

### The ending needs attention

The current ending says:

> Here, randomness is domesticated.

I would remove that sentence.

Not because it is wrong as an interpretation, but because it reduces a complex encounter to a conclusion.

The returning sphere has a particularly interesting asymmetry: its excursion has a compulsory return, but the visitor can release it and need not return with it.

That is a stronger situation than the general assertion that randomness has been domesticated.

My preferred final movement would be closer to:

> We release it. The sphere continues without us.
>
> It returns to the centre.

The image and the event can conclude the literary chapter. The broader philosophical reading remains available.

## 5. Page 7: The most important scientific addition

The reference page deserves special attention.

It provides the first ten values generated from seed 435, comparing a value between zero and one with its five-digit representation.

For example:

Generator output, displayed to six decimals

0.498111

First five fractional digits

# 49811

This is a different kind of image from the filtered screenshots.

It makes the numerical transformation itself inspectable. The reader can observe the relationship instead of merely being told that one seed produces different representations.

It also clarifies a distinction the narrative sometimes compresses: a common seed can initialize separate streams, while the programs consuming their draws determine how those values are grouped and represented.

The page's verification note is particularly valuable. It reports testing the generated values and displayed results in Godot 4.6 at several seeds.&#x20;

I would retain this page, although I might place it closer to the main encounter rather than after the literary ending.

And I would consider adding one actual RGB grouping beside the five-digit groups, to demonstrate how three draws become one colour.

That would make the connection between numerical data and visual form immediately graspable.

## 6. Page 8: A different possibility for the entire book

I am very interested in the visual proposal titled Path. Hold. Interval.

Unlike the earlier illustrations, these drawings are not mainly descriptions of complete artifacts. They isolate what the apparatus is doing.

This distinction matters:

| Documentary illustration                                   | Interpretive illustration                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shows the object and selected evidence from the simulation | Investigates a relation through a new visual composition                                |
| Supports verification and orientation                      | Supports speculation and comparison                                                     |
| The image is accountable to a captured state               | The image is accountable to the idea it proposes, with its invented elements identified |

Both can belong in the same publication.

The page correctly distinguishes the speculative drawing from measured experimental data. I would preserve that transparency.&#x20;

But if this becomes a final artist's book, I would move the explanation of image generation and source status into a colophon or research note. The image itself deserves room to operate.

This could develop into a powerful visual language across Ada Research.

The Geometry halls might use constructive diagrams. The Randomness halls might use sampled marks and distributions. The Cellular Automata halls might use grids that visibly change their conditions. Later maps might need images that can no longer be adequately represented from one fixed viewpoint.

In other words, the illustration strategy could evolve along with the computational concepts, just as we discussed allowing the literary narrator to develop.

## 7. The book itself could become another experiment in information

This is the most interesting discovery of my reading.

The publication is already making several different reductions:

- A moving 3D encounter becomes a 2D image.
- Colour becomes monochrome.
- Continuous movement becomes selected frames and accumulated marks.
- Sound becomes printed intervals.
- A procedurally generated sequence becomes ten retained numbers.
- A lived or imagined visitor experience becomes prose.

None of these reductions is neutral.

But none is necessarily a failure either. The reduced representation can make a particular relationship more accessible, inspectable or durable.

The filtered abacus picture may reveal spatial accumulation more clearly than a moving VR encounter. The table may preserve a numerical relationship more exactly than the coloured cubes. The prose may connect encounters that never coexist in the participant's field of view.

So the book is not simply reporting a research problem about information and entropy.

It is another apparatus that selects what survives the transition between media.

I think this deserves to become a conscious principle of the larger publication.

## 8. My editorial priorities

I would not redesign the edition. Its visual identity is worth preserving. Instead, I would make four targeted changes.

1\. Preserve the documentary precision while improving image legibility. Give the abacus traces a little more contrast and make the selected five-digit group unmistakable.

2\. Reconsider the visual silence of pages 5–6. Test one small diagram of the final sphere's release and return. Let the most consequential bodily encounter also have a visual life.

3\. Remove the overdetermined ending. Allow the return to happen without concluding that randomness has been domesticated. Also verify that MOVE / KEEP is present in the intended edition's installation state, since the earlier development handover described it as temporarily removed from the saved room.

4\. Give the evidence and speculative images clear editorial roles. Preserve page 7's verified numbers and page 8's interpretive freedom, but distinguish the literary chapter, scientific reference and visual research proposal as separate forms within the publication.

The strongest achievement is not any individual sentence or diagram. It is the emerging relationship between scientific evidence, spatial encounter and artistic interpretation.

The visual language is restrained enough to make the simulated objects strange. The literature is precise enough to preserve the actual operations. The numerical reference makes the research inspectable.

What remains is to give these three forms a little more independence from one another.

I would not aim to make every page more poetic or every diagram more abstract. The productive tension is precisely that some pages make claims that can be verified, while others invite the reader to imagine something the apparatus has not yet produced.

And I think this suggests something important for the larger Ada Research book:

The book need not be a reproduction of the VR world. It can become a second world, with its own methods of selecting, transforming, preserving and questioning what the first world makes possible.
