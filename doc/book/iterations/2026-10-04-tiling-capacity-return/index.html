Eight map summaries · 2026-10-04

Tiling · what changes

A short comparison for each map: what the plan said, what is proposed now, and why.

Images show earlier museum encounters, including their existing comparisons. This update changes teaching plans; visuals and runtime are unchanged.

01 Pattern_Disco

Keep the pattern, change its palette

Before
The checker rule and palette were separated, but the plan could read as another construction exercise.
After
Reuse the existing floor rule. Change palette and phase separately; keep the triangle shader an optional comparison.

Why Let Colour return through an operation the student already knows.

02 Pattern_Foundry

Author the source others repeat

Before
A four-by-four source, copying and the loom’s curved receivers were bundled into one lesson.
After
Start with one small motif and wrapping lookup. Move copying to a return visit; distinguish the tile, loom and held images.

Why Reduce first-visit load without losing the richer questions of sharing and keeping.

03 Tutorial_Pattern

Transform the lookup or the source

Before
“Reversed source rows” left the direction of the source edit ambiguous.
After
Make horizontal reversal explicit: read columns backwards, or reverse entries within each row. Check which operation actually changes the source.

Why Keep a visually similar result from concealing two different operations.

04 Array_Patterns

Give an address physical dimensions

Before
Physical cell size was included, but transfer and surface conventions stayed open.
After
Use the installed fixed motif and name each face’s directions. Distinguish one cell’s width from the width of the complete repeat.

Why Give the same number an unambiguous unit and meaning.

05 Symmetry_Seventeen

Make a reversal visible

Before
The glide was described, but a source block could still be mistaken for its full repeating unit.
After
Inspect two neighbouring blocks and test the translation period. Keep the seventeen-group claim separate from this bounded comparison.

Why Make the limit of the demonstrated rule as clear as its visible effect.

06 Ribbon_Patterns_01

Let height enter the pattern

Before
The height shift and slicing were clear, but the storage convention was implicit.
After
Name source [z][x] and volume [y][z][x], then predict one shifted lookup. Keep zero-as-colour distinct from earlier occupancy.

Why Reuse the earlier volume knowledge without silently changing what its values mean.

07 Ribbon_Patterns_02

Compare what the carrier contributes

Before
The reflective comparison already worked well.
After
Keep it. Clarify equal surface distance versus apparent width; leave the lighting calculation supplied and require no perceptual answer.

Why Allow the sequence to deepen attention without adding more programming.

Review this map’s proposal ↗

08 Ribbon_Patterns_03

Carry a small recipe onto fabric

Before
A tiny shader was proposed, but its host and what SAVE could carry were left open.
After
Name the existing texture/material host. Teach repeat, offset and sampling only; distinguish saved recipe data from shader code and metre spacing.

Why Give the sequence a small, usable shader ending without implying the whole studio must be rebuilt.
