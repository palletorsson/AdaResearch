# Two clocks decide what survives

Follow a mark until it leaves the record. Nothing happened to gravity at that moment. `PendulumWave.gd` can advance the next swing from its current angle and angular velocity; the disappearing mark belonged to another system, a list with a count limit and an age limit. The motion continues while its accessible past gets shorter.

FINE targets 25 milliseconds and spends three hundred entries on roughly 7.5 seconds. COARSE targets 200 milliseconds and keeps around fifty entries under the ten-second age limit. More detail keeps less duration here because the memory budget is held constant. A different budget would produce a different trade. Before calling one record better, ask what it must let someone discover.

The target has to meet another clock. At sixty physics ticks per second there is no tick exactly 25 milliseconds after the last one. Actual sample gaps alternate around the request, and the readout admits the difference. The continuous cyan line joins positions the simulation retained; its joins are not evidence of positions sampled between them. The recording rule is already being translated into the capabilities of its host.

STROBE gives the sampler the pendulum's measured period. The marks nearly align while the bob keeps moving. Tick-scale spread remains because the measurement of the period is itself discrete. The record makes movement look close to rest without needing to fabricate a single position. What happens between its appointments falls outside its account. Which histories acquire that appearance because of how often someone looks?

The drawing dot in the point rooms asked whether the hand had moved far enough before keeping a position. This recorder asks whether enough simulation time has accumulated. Each can reveal something the other misses. Neither offers an unencoded original to which all other records must answer.

The pendulum is also a model: one angular degree of freedom, a chosen length and gravitational acceleration, a numerical integrator. Its restoring acceleration uses `sin(angle)`; it is only approximately linear for small angles. This instance starts at 45 degrees and has zero damping, so the room does not demonstrate friction settling a body into obedience. That story would require different parameters. The critical work begins with the decisions this body actually carries.

RESET makes one more selection. It clears this experiment's state and record while the museum keeps running. A beginning is made available inside a world that has already begun.
