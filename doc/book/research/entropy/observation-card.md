# Optional entropy / information observation card

Use only the fields needed for the question. “No numerical measure justified” is a valid answer. Copy the short record into the existing writing-wall Research loop; this is a method template, not a new interface or mandatory schema.

## Encounter and question

- Map, artifact, current scene/configuration and source version:
- Status: proposed / source inspected / calculated fixture / native trial / human trial.
- Origin: artifact / tutorial / final / subject. Parent experiment:
- What caught attention? What could make the candidate gem fail?

## Baseline and intervention

- State retained: ordered values, transforms, trace, raw draws, initial conditions, schedules or dependencies as required.
- Record location and provenance; seed and generator version if relevant:
- One intervention: world state / recording procedure / governing rule.
- Held fixed; known confounds:
- Prediction, including a possible unchanged or unhelpful result:

## Measurement contract

- Quantity, formula and units, or why no number is justified:
- Random variable and distribution, or macrostate/ensemble and constraints:
- Population/sample, category definitions, spatial resolution, history length and actual timestamps:
- Exact enumeration, empirical estimate or theoretical bound? Uncertainty, sample-size and estimator limitations:
- What the reading cannot establish:

## Evidence and next decision

- Observation and evidence link. Keep this empty until there is evidence.
- Perceptual/bodily consequence; observer and conditions. Keep interpretation separate.
- Replay: what was restored and what was compared?
- Independent reconstruction: what code/dependencies were supplied, and what was reproduced?
- Next change, retained result or reason to stop; alternative explanation:
- Tutorial consequence / final consequence / spatial consequence:
- Cross-hall transfer, if promised: payload, units, coordinate frame, source version, receiver and round-trip check. Otherwise: not promised.

## Mapping into the existing writing-wall loop

Use **question** for the encounter; **baseline** for retained state and the measurement contract; **one change / prediction** for the intervention; **observation / evidence** for actual results; **replay / reconstruction** for distinct checks; **interpretation** for inference and its limits; and **decision / next question** for the proposed revision. Attach this card's path as evidence if the full contract would overwhelm those fields.

A wall snapshot preserves an editorial state, not the Godot simulation state. KEEP records also differ between artifacts: check their actual payload. Saving a seed, saving the outcome and acquiring a new physical input are different acts.

Verify artifact correctness, the experiment cycle and promised cross-hall integration separately. Scene loading, runtime, performance and physical VR comfort also remain separate. A completed card alone is not evidence of learning or artistic value.
