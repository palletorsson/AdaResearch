ARTIST INDEX — SPATIAL / MATERIAL OPERATIONS

Purpose
A working atlas for translating artistic operations into 3D / VR. Categories describe what the work does to space, material, bodies, images, or systems rather than conventional movements. Artists can belong to more than one category.
