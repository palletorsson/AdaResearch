# Artist operations × existing capacity

Capacity belongs to a specific operation, not to an artist’s whole practice.

100 entries · 99 distinct artist labels · 12 categories · 24 operation families · 37 candidate artifacts.

Artist descriptions are supplied prompts. Judgements are editorial matches checked against repository sources. No fresh Godot runtime tests were performed. No map or spine order was changed.

## How to use this

Choose an encounter → name what remains fixed → change one relation → observe a consequence → identify the borrowed apparatus and the student’s required capabilities → retain the question the mechanism cannot answer.

Full artifact source links, actual direct spine placements and prerequisite gates are in [the review atlas](../iterations/2026-10-01-artist-capacity/index.html) and [artifact-capacities.json](artifact-capacities.json).

- **Existing operation: 10.** A specific operation already exists in source. Reuse it for a new study; this is not a claim to reproduce the artwork.
- **Combine / adapt: 38.** Useful components exist, but their arrangement, input or relation needs to be authored and tested.
- **New capability needed: 32.** Available parts do not supply an essential mechanism named in this proposed translation.
- **Define the encounter first: 20.** Technical parts exist, but a specific source, situated relation or artistic question must guide their use.
