## Revisions, in order

1. **Flow_Field, after the first code block: name the clock.**
   > You have seen this shape before. At the accumulation bench the loop added a height times a width. Here it adds a direction times a speed times `delta`, a rate times a small interval of time. This is the clock the previous hall kept refusing. Each frame is one rectangle, and the particle's position is the running total. What is new is that the total chooses where the next sample is taken.
2. **Change_Intro, the bridge: name the return to the second room.**
   > In the second room, `b - a` gave the same answer for a direct walk and a detour. The bridge makes that point with a slope. Add every rise and every fall and you recover only the difference between the ends. The turns are still there in the strips, and the total cannot show them.
3. **Change_Intro, first paragraph.** Cut "The fabric can come with you", or give it one concrete handle.
4. **The clock disclaimers.** Keep the first, about speed not being velocity. Cut the next two, or turn the second into a forward pointer to Flow_Field.
5. **The walking-controller sentence** reads as an audit. The second reader's version opens a possibility instead:

6. **Seed acceleration for Forces.** At the plot of slopes along the curve, ask one question: how is the slope itself changing? Forces needs a rate of a rate, and the plot already shows one.
