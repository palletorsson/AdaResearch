# Translation: seven journeys

This 15 x 55 m hall merges the former Translation and AxisDecomposition maps. Seven transport cubes carry the visitor through depth, sideways movement, ascent, a high crossing, reverse sideways movement, descent and a final diagonal. Thirteen pickups wait along their paths. The route climbs three metres and returns to ground level before the exit.

The carriers do not rotate or scale. Pickups rise and fall without spinning or pulsing in size until collected. Entrance markers make x, y and z explicit; the transport and pickup objects carry the book's primary thread. At the final terrace, two grabbable door mechanisms compare the same destination under sequential axis constraints and free combined movement. These are a third primary encounter. The next active hall is Trans_Rotation.

Original maps and texts are preserved in doc/space/translation-merge-2026-09-09/before. The active Trans_AxisDecomposition stop was removed from the sequence, not deleted from the repository.
