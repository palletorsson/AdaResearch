## Place in the spine

formfinding → **wavefunctions** → randomness

Editorial intention (a question to test against the material): Let periodic rules organise movement, sound and terrain; feel how phase and superposition can alter the space a body encounters.

Arriving from: Follow a body's descent or relaxation and discover how an objective, constraints and stopping rule decide what counts as an arrived-at form.

Preparing for: Ask how a draw, a distribution and a sampling rule produce different kinds of uncertainty, opportunity and difficult ground.

Declared objectives (source claims, not proof of completion):
- Rotation creates oscillation (unit circle â†’ sine)
- Parameters of oscillation: amplitude, frequency, phase
- Fourier synthesis: any signal is a sum of sines
- Interference and resonance: when waves meet
- Beat frequencies: slow modulation from fast waves
- From periodic to chaotic: the double pendulum transition
- Exponential form: rotation in the complex plane is e^(iθ); sine is its imaginary part. Fourier synthesis is integration in the limit — same accumulation move as Riemann sums at change (seq 4.5), generalised to a continuous sum of sines.
