Beginning → **primitives** → transformation

Editorial intention (a question to test against the material): From an unseen point to lines, traces, grids and faces: what must be encoded before a body can appear, move or be recognised?

Arriving from: No prior chapter is assumed.

Preparing for: Translate, rotate and scale the familiar body; discover what remains the same and how a changed relation opens or closes a passage.

Declared objectives (source claims, not proof of completion):
- Understanding Vector3 as position in 3D space
- Point visualization: existence without extension
- Line topology: connection between two positions
- Triangle rasterization: the GPU's atomic unit
- From primitives to meshes: building complexity from simplicity
