# QFEP Phi Term - Summary

## Overview
The fifth QFEP Laboratory map introduces the final and most radical term: phi delta E(S,t), sensitivity to the rate of entropic change. The map splits into two zones — conservative (phi < 0, left) and becoming (phi > 0, right) — with the phi slider at center. Negative phi resists change; positive phi embraces it. This is where the formula becomes explicitly dispositional and political.

## Spatial Layout
- **Dimensions**: 10x12 grid, bilaterally split
- **Architecture**: Left zone holds rigid_sculpture and preserved_pattern; right zone holds fluid_form and transforming_pattern; phi_slider at center column
- **Signage**: "phi < 0 / RESIST" on left, "phi > 0 / EMBRACE" on right

## Key Elements

### Interactables
- **rigid_sculpture** — Geometric form that actively resists deformation; restoration speed scales with abs(phi); at phi=-1.0, snaps back almost instantly from any perturbation
- **preserved_pattern** — 8x8 cellular grid that heals mutations probabilistically under negative phi; the original pattern reasserts through stochastic repair
- **fluid_form** — Same initial geometry as rigid_sculpture but wandering under noise; no restoration, no memory of original position; the form becomes a process
- **transforming_pattern** — 8x8 grid where mutations propagate to neighbors under positive phi; disturbances are contagious and self-amplifying
- **phi_slider** — Range -1.0 to +1.0, default at zero; broadcasts to all four artifacts simultaneously; color gradient from cool blue (conservative) to warm magenta (becoming)

### Utilities
- **Spawn** at north, two teleporters at south: "phi_conservative" and "phi_queer"

## Learning Sequence
1. Enter at center, observe the split architecture
2. Move the phi slider negative — watch the rigid_sculpture resist deformation, the preserved_pattern heal its mutations
3. Move the phi slider positive — watch the fluid_form wander, the transforming_pattern spread mutations contagiously
4. Cross phi through zero and feel the continuous reversal of tendency
5. Understand: same lambda, different phi, radically different behavior
6. Exit through "phi_queer" (forward in sequence) or "phi_conservative" (does not advance)

## Design Intent
Phi is the term where the formula becomes ethical. Every system has a disposition toward change — conservative systems resist perturbation, return to equilibrium; adaptive systems use perturbation as material for transformation. The split architecture makes this tangible: physical orientation becomes conceptual orientation. The curriculum's thesis surfaces: creative, living systems require positive phi within the edge-of-chaos lambda band.
