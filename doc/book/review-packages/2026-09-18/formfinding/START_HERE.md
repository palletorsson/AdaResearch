## Place in the spine

forces → **formfinding** → wavefunctions

Editorial intention (a question to test against the material): Follow a body's descent or relaxation and discover how an objective, constraints and stopping rule decide what counts as an arrived-at form.

Arriving from: Move from vectors to interactions that carry bodies; distinguish a prescribed motion from motion produced by forces and constraints.

Preparing for: Let periodic rules organise movement, sound and terrain; feel how phase and superposition can alter the space a body encounters.

Declared objectives (source claims, not proof of completion):
- Measure a local gradient and distinguish three stopping conditions
- Investigate how supports, length and load constrain a cable
- Compare local relaxation rules for a connected shape
- Distinguish balance from stability
- Investigate uphill proposals and the limits of escaping local minima
- Author a model by choosing its objective, constraints and stopping rule
