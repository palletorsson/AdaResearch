## Place in the spine

wavefunctions → **randomness** → noise

Editorial intention (a question to test against the material): Ask how a draw, a distribution and a sampling rule produce different kinds of uncertainty, opportunity and difficult ground.

Arriving from: Let periodic rules organise movement, sound and terrain; feel how phase and superposition can alter the space a body encounters.

Preparing for: Discover correlated variation's power to make organic-looking forms, then question the easy answer of adding noise and the possibilities it can obscure.

Declared objectives (source claims, not proof of completion):
- Entropy as possibility space, not destruction
- PRNG vs TRNG: deterministic sequences vs physical randomness
- Distributions: uniform, Gaussian, and the shapes of chance
- Random walk: non-teleological movement, queer navigation
- Emergence: Turing morphogenesis, patterns from noise
- Shannon entropy: H = -Σ p log p. Logarithm measures disorder. Factorials count arrangements. The integral named at change (seq 4.5) reappears here as expectation — sum over a distribution becomes integral in the continuous limit.
