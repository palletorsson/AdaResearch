Rotation untethered from purpose. Each axis receives its own chaos — independent random values for X, Y, Z — demonstrating that 3D randomness is not one roll of the dice but three.

The `Random_Rotate_Random_XYZ` artifact picks three independent random angles and composes them into one orientation. `random_decay_multimesh` scales this to hundreds of instances via MultiMesh, making the population-level distribution visible. `hardware_entropy_decay` materializes entropy as physical process — radioactive decay, thermal noise, the universe's own random number generator — contrasting algorithmic PRNG with physical source. The subtlety worth catching: uniformly random Euler angles do not produce uniformly random orientations. The three-axis decomposition is a coordinate artifact, not a geometric truth. Randomness inherits the pathologies of its parameterization.
