Concept: Particles remember their best position and know the swarm's best position — from this dual memory, a population navigates a fitness landscape toward optima without gradients. An elevated observation deck over a flat valley makes the optimization topology visible: particles swooping through the valley, overshooting, converging, finding the minimum.
Sequence role: Sixth map in Swarm Intelligence (14th spine sequence, integration phase), intermediate difficulty. After ACO solved optimization through environmental memory (pheromone), PSO solves it through social memory (personal best, global best). The two maps form a pair: stigmergic optimization versus social optimization. Connects forward to SwarmIntelligence_Swarm_Intelligence_Algorithms where multiple swarm approaches are compared in a gallery; follows SwarmIntelligence_Ant_Colony_Optimization.
Technical angle: Kennedy and Eberhart's PSO: each particle has position x, velocity v, personal best p, and access to global best g. Velocity update: v = w*v + c1*r1*(p-x) + c2*r2*(g-x), where w is inertia weight, c1 and c2 are cognitive and social coefficients, r1 and r2 are random vectors. Position update: x = x + v. The fitness landscape is the valley visible from the observation deck — likely a multimodal function (Rastrigin, Ackley, or Rosenbrock) rendered as terrain height. The elevation deck provides a god's-eye view of convergence dynamics.
Critical angle: PSO replaces the environment-as-memory (ACO pheromones) with agents-as-memory — each particle carries its own history and shares only its best result. This is a different model of collective intelligence: not stigmergy but social learning. The inertia weight w controls the tension between exploration (high w, particles maintain momentum) and exploitation (low w, particles converge). PSO is also the bridge between swarm intelligence and machine learning — it optimizes any differentiable or non-differentiable function through population-based search.
Key artifacts: PSOVisualization implements particle swarm optimization over a visible fitness landscape, with the elevated observation deck providing a spatial overview of particle trajectories, personal bests, and global best convergence.
Gap: Needs a fitness landscape selector — an artifact that switches between different test functions (unimodal, multimodal, deceptive) so the learner can see how PSO's convergence behavior changes with landscape topology.
