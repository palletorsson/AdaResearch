The flow field assigns direction to space. Agents read the field and follow. No pathfinding, no goals — just local obedience to arrows. Thousands of agents streaming through invisible currents. The field is the program; the swarm is the execution.

`FlowFieldMain` renders the field as streamlines in the open channel: a central ridge splits the flow, creating saddle points and divergence zones where behavior bifurcates. Particles advect through bilinear interpolation of nearby grid vectors. The flow field makes stigmergy mathematical — the environment carries directional information, and agents need no communication beyond reading the field at their location. The patterns of convergence, divergence, and curl are also the visual grammar of fluid dynamics and meteorology. The world is full of such fields we walk without seeing.
