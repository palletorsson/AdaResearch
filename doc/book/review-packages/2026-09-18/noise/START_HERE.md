## Place in the spine

randomness → **noise** → cellularautomata

Editorial intention (a question to test against the material): Discover correlated variation's power to make organic-looking forms, then question the easy answer of adding noise and the possibilities it can obscure.

Arriving from: Ask how a draw, a distribution and a sampling rule produce different kinds of uncertainty, opportunity and difficult ground.

Preparing for: Let neighbours and local updates grow histories and spaces; test what kinds of global form can emerge from a limited rule.
