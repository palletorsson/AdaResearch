Concept: The corridor back. Every sequence ends here — not a lesson but a threshold. The dark sphere pulses in the passage between what was learned and what comes next, marking transition without teaching.

The absence of interactables is the point: after eight maps of parameter spaces and shader pipelines, the final map offers nothing to manipulate.
Critical angle: The Lab_Path refuses synthesis. It does not ask what was learned or test retention. Becoming does not announce itself. On the other side: procedural generation, morphogenesis, worlds assembled from the structured chaos this sequence defined. The proof of the sequence is not in this room but in what the learner builds after leaving it.
