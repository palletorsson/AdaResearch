# A pattern visits an address

The Life dish starts with one glider on a held 24 × 24 plane. The same present appears in a floor inset. Three gold addresses control three opal lamps, giving the moving pattern a visible consequence in the museum. The addresses and floor support remain fixed.

STEP and REPLAY allow prediction and return. BLINKER separates repetition from displacement; MEET places a block in the same automaton. LINK disconnects the lamps while Life continues. The text follows these observations into the actual B3/S23 branch and receiver call.

The study asks what persists when a pattern's participating cells change, and what additional decisions let one system affect another. The state is binary. All six original works remain; the Life dish is primary and the book hero. The next hall brings proximity into the update.
