# Color_Flashlight — What reaches the surface

Second in Colour, between Color_Context_Placed and Color_Nails. The previous room asks how a colour appears among neighbours. This room lets the visitor move an illuminating condition with their hand; the next turns toward colour addressed to a body part.

The primary encounter is `flashlight_demo`: four pickable lamps, red, green, blue and white, facing a white receiving board. Aim one beam aside and back, separate two pools, then overlap them. The board's material stays fixed. The changed appearance follows a changed relation between light and surface.

The 19 by 20 metre museum room keeps its enclosing grid walls. The exhibit root is at (9,9), at floor level, with `mount:standing`. Handles start at 1.1 metres; the six-metre-wide board rises from the floor. White starts aimed aside. A grid cube stands to the side at (13,8), leaving the central comparison clear. The old utility recess is filled for a continuous floor.

There is no lamp switch, hue selector, prism or dark sphere in this placement. The beams are RGB renderer lights. Do not promise spectral simulation, total darkness or colour-vision equivalence across viewers.
