# Grand / Petit — intent

Third in Colour, between Flashlight and Rainbow. Introduce colour as an addressable material choice after distinguishing it from illumination.

Stage the contrast as a 4.3-metre hand behind a fine manicure table, with a 32-centimetre copy on its cushion. Keep the RGB controls at human reach. The same coordinate gesture should visibly address the nails or skin at both scales.

Primary encounters remain nail_color_controller and hand_color_controller. Their receiving installation, nail_salon, is secondary. Preserve the distinction between the two display hands and any connected tracked player hand. Bottles are set dressing; no scanner or prism is part of this placement.

The question is what the same implementation permits to become bodily: small and intimate, oversized and unfamiliar, with colour choices that need not imitate skin. Let the reader test that difference before explaining the shared mesh, transform and material addresses. Carry the chosen colour toward Rainbow's ordering without claiming automatic transfer.
