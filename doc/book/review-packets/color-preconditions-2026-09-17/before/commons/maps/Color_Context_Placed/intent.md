# Colour joins the primitives

First add colour to the primitives and static transformations the visitor already knows. Begin with a few controlled comparisons, then allow combinations to become richer and taller. Keep them stacks throughout.

Palle's 17 September direction replaces the repeated four-by-two ending with more gallery DNA and lengthens the hall. The hall is now 23 × 61 m. Fifteen chroma_stack studies lead into eight color_dna_stack studies. The Kraš rainbow table DNA becomes a 6.2 m column; the final two combinations reach 7.2 and 7.4 m. The central route remains open.

The gallery is compositional material: use its actual configurations, then expose the changes of height and combination. Art references do not make these replicas. Fixed shapes do not claim to balance under gravity. Their scale makes the relation to the visitor's body available for inspection.

Book and primary order: chroma_stack, color_dna_stack. Then continue to Color_Flashlight and investigate illumination. This opening need not explain all of colour.
