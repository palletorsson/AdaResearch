# Artifact: Turing Pattern Generator

> CPU Gray-Scott simulation — du/dt = Du*laplacian(u) - u*v^2 + f*(1-u), dv/dt = Dv*laplacian(v) + u*v^2 - (k+f)*v — iterated steps_per_frame times per frame on a 2D grid

## Context

**Category:** emergence | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** turing_patterns, reaction_diffusion, morphogenesis, spots, stripes | **Themes:** commons, emergence, mixed

Generate reaction-diffusion Turing patterns in real-time — spots, stripes, and labyrinthine structures emerge from the interplay of activator and inhibitor chemicals diffusing at different rates. Alan Turing's morphogenesis made visible.

*pattern emerges from the ratio of two diffusion rates — not from a template, not from a gene, but from the mathematics of how fast activation outruns inhibition*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: generated_texture, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to hold a Turing pattern in your hands and twist the feed/kill knobs until you find the boundary where spots become stripes become mazes
- **Critical Parameter:** feed/kill ratio — the two parameters together define a 2D phase space; each preset (Spots, Stripes, Maze, Mitosis, Coral, Waves) is a point in that space
- **What Emerges:** the laplacian coupling between neighboring cells means local perturbations propagate as waves — pattern is not local but a property of the entire coupled field
- **Triggers:** VR sliders for feed, kill, and preset selection; reset button re-seeds the simulation; keyboard 1-6 for direct preset selection
