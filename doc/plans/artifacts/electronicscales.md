# Artifact: Electronic Scales

> display(t) = target + amplitude * e^(-damping*t) * sin(omega*t) — damped oscillation to final reading

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** sound, visualization, wave | **Themes:** oscillation, physics, waves

Laboratory scales demonstrating damped harmonic oscillation. When weighing, the display settles using e^(-bt) * sin(ωt)—exponential decay multiplied by sine. Adjustable damping factor and frequency. Shows how real oscillating systems lose energy over time.

*Every precise measurement is the residue of an oscillation that has almost died.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** Place objects on a scale and watch the reading oscillate before settling to true weight
- **Critical Parameter:** damping_factor — controls how fast oscillation decays to the final reading
- **What Emerges:** the drama of measurement — truth approached through diminishing oscillation
- **Triggers:** start_weighing() initiates the damped oscillation sequence; tare() resets the zero
