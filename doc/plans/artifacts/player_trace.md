# Artifact: player_trace

> path(t) — the sequence of positions your body occupies over time

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** transformation, path, trajectory, time, coordinates, trace, primitive | **Themes:** core, primitives

A recorder that turns the player's motion into a visible line in space. Every step writes another point, so translation through the room becomes geometry rather than a forgotten action. The artifact makes path and trajectory tangible, especially when compared against grids and axis-constrained motion. It turns movement into a time-indexed drawing.

*every path through space is a function of time, and you are constantly computing it*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: vertex
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner sees their own movement become geometry — the body writes in space
- **Critical Parameter:** trail_length — how many past positions are remembered and drawn
- **What Emerges:** the realization that motion is just a continuous set of points — a set of coordinates
- **Triggers:** any player movement — the LINE_STRIP grows with every step taken
