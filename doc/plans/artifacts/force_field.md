# Artifact: Force Field

> effect(player, mediator) = hazard XOR benefit -- dual-nature zone where context determines outcome

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** forces, transmutation, qfep, dual-nature, vr | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards, qfep

Dual-nature force zone: every hazard is also medicine. Fire warms or burns. Poison kills or reveals. Q-FEP: same potential, different restraint, different manifestation. Bring a mediator object into the field to transmute hazard into benefit.

*the force that burns also warms -- constraint determines outcome, not the force itself.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** a force field that burns or warms, poisons or reveals, depending on what the player brings
- **Critical Parameter:** mediator_object presence -- same field produces opposite effects based on relational context
- **What Emerges:** Q-FEP made spatial -- identical potential under different constraint produces opposite outcomes
- **Triggers:** player enters Area3D; field checks for mediator; mode determined by carried objects
