# Artifact: Deltahedron Creature

> a convex deltahedron that unfolds from its flat net — Cauchy's theorem guarantees rigidity when assembled

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** fold_system, procedural, deltahedron, net, origami, triangles, rigid | **Themes:** fold_system

A creature whose body IS its mesh net. Flat at fold_amount=0, assembled convex solid at fold_amount=1. All equilateral triangles — Cauchy's rigidity theorem guarantees structural integrity when closed. Species: tetrahedron (4), octahedron (8), icosahedron (20).

*triangles can't deform. A closed convex deltahedron can't flex. The net IS the genome.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** a creature whose genome IS its net topology — tetrahedron, octahedron, icosahedron are species
- **Critical Parameter:** fold_amount — 0.0 is flat net (vulnerable), 1.0 is assembled solid (rigid, strong)
- **What Emerges:** structural integrity increases with fold_amount; partially folded = partially vulnerable
- **Triggers:** fold_amount drives all vertex positions simultaneously — single DOF via lerp between flat and 3D
