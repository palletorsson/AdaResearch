# Artifact: Fractal Hydra

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** fractal, self-similarity, branching, hydra | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

Central body with branching heads on cylinder necks. Damage splits a head into two half-size copies — fractal branching up to depth 3. More heads means more attacks but less damage each. Cut one head, two grow back.

*the hydra is the fractal -- damage at any scale reproduces the pattern at smaller scale.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** cut one head and two grow back, each smaller, inheriting the parent's attack -- self-similarity
- **Critical Parameter:** fractal depth / head count -- cutting increases heads exponentially until max depth
- **What Emerges:** the punishment for attacking IS more attackers -- self-similarity as defense strategy
- **Triggers:** damage to a head triggers split; each new head inherits scaled attack; depth limits growth
