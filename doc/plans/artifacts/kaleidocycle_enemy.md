# Artifact: Kaleidocycle

> rotation_phase → active_face → attack_mode — topology determines combat behavior

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** color, hazard | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

Tumbling ring of tetrahedra with 4 attack modes. Rolls toward player while continuously rotating through itself. Each face color triggers different attack: Fire (red), Ice (blue), Spike (purple), Shield (gold).

*A kaleidocycle is a closed kinematic chain — every face is always present, only visibility rotates.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** a tumbling ring of tetrahedra rolls toward you, cycles to reveal fire/ice/spike/shield faces
- **Critical Parameter:** _rotation_phase — continuous rotation selects which of 4 attack modes activates
- **What Emerges:** the shield mode creates damage reduction — the kaleidocycle can randomly become temporarily invincible
- **Triggers:** _start_cycle() randomly picks AttackMode, sets target rotation to expose that face, then attacks
