# Artifact: Hardware Entropy Decay

> S_hardware = f(velocity, grip, head_rotation) — your body is the entropy source

## Context

**Category:** randomness | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** decay, shader, hardware, entropy, vr, controller, procedural, scratches, grime, weathering, interactive, immersive | **Themes:** probability, randomness

Your VR movements become entropy. Controller velocity drives scratches, grip pressure accumulates grime, head rotation feeds weathering rate. Three display surfaces (cube, sphere, cylinder) decay in real-time from your hardware inputs via a custom procedural shader.

*The observer is not separate from the system — every measurement is an intervention.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: shader_time
- **Interaction:** move your hands fast, squeeze the grip, turn your head — and watch surfaces rust and corrode because of you
- **Critical Parameter:** velocity_to_scratch — how aggressively hand speed maps to scratch intensity on the shader
- **What Emerges:** a feedback loop where curiosity (moving to look) accelerates the decay you came to observe
- **Triggers:** _sample_hardware() reads XR controller velocity, grip pressure, head angular speed every frame
