# Artifact: Tree Generator

> Proximity-based procedural tree spawner — generates parametric trees around VR camera position with distance culling

## Context

**Category:** morphogenesis | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** procedural, trees, forest, proximity, generation, culling, infinite, spatial, VR, morphogenesis | **Themes:** generation, procedural

An infinite procedural forest that grows around you. Trees spawn within a generation radius as you walk, and vanish behind you when you leave. The generation loop is the algorithm: proximity check, spacing constraint, spawn, cull. No tree exists until you approach; the forest is an illusion maintained by continuous generation. Two tree styles (simple blocks and complex clusters) with red emissive materials create an alien forest that feels both procedural and alive.

*The forest does not exist — it is generated around you, and the generation is the forest.*

## Design

- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** To populate a forest that grows around the player: trees appear ahead and vanish behind, infinite generated nature
- **Critical Parameter:** generation_distance — the radius within which new trees spawn; controls forest extent around the player
- **What Emerges:** An endless procedural forest from a simple spawn/cull loop tied to camera position
- **Triggers:** Walking forward spawns trees ahead; max_trees caps density; min_tree_spacing prevents overlap
