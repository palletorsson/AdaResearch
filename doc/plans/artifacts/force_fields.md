# Artifact: Force Fields

> Area3D.gravity_space_override = COMBINE. Three zones, three rules: directional push, radial pull, viscous drag. Same balls, different fates.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** force_field, gravity, wind, radial, vortex, particles, spatial, superposition, flow | **Themes:** fields, gravity, wind, forces

Configurable force fields — uniform gravity, directional wind, radial attraction/repulsion, and vortex fields — applied to populations of particles. Combine multiple fields to create complex flow patterns. Each field type is a spatial function mapping position to force: F(x) defines the landscape that particles navigate.

*A force field is a region with different rules. Cross the boundary and the laws change. Physics is local.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [4, 4, 4] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** To throw balls into colored zones and watch physics change — one zone pushes sideways, one sucks inward, one floats with resistance. Territory as force law.
- **Critical Parameter:** gravity and linear_damp per zone. The point attractor's gravity (20.0) vs the wind zone's damp (4.0) — pull vs resistance, two ways to trap a ball.
- **What Emerges:** Balls orbiting the point attractor. Balls floating in the wind/drag zone as buoyancy balances gravity. Balls ricocheting in the directional field.
- **Triggers:** VR grab balls → throw into zones, each zone applies its own physics automatically via Godot's Area3D system, R → reset all balls
