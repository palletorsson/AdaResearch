## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** to drag translate X/Y and rotate simultaneously — to feel that the coordinate system itself is moving, not the shape
- **Critical Parameter:** rotation angle — the single value that proves sequence is meaning (translate-then-rotate != rotate-then-translate)
- **What Emerges:** the combined display reveals that every spinning object in every game is just one matrix multiplied per frame
- **Triggers:** animate toggle sets auto_rotate, making the combined transform orbit continuously; combined panel stacks all three operations
