# Artifact: Torso Estimation Visualization

> Three animated tracking points (head + two hands) driving real-time torso orientation estimation with visual feedback

## Context

**Category:** body | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** torso, estimation, tracking, body, vr, 3point, ik | **Themes:** generation, procedural

3-point torso estimation demo — shows how head + two hand positions estimate torso orientation. Animated tracking points with visible shoulder computation and forward direction.

*The body is not given — it is the computation that fills the space between what the sensors actually see.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** To show that the VR body is inferred, not measured: three points in space produce a torso through computation
- **Critical Parameter:** head_weight — how much head direction vs hand midpoint determines torso facing
- **What Emerges:** A believable torso from three floating points — the body filling in gaps that sensors never measured
- **Triggers:** Hands moving apart widens shoulder markers; head turning reorients forward arrow; asymmetric positions tilt the torso
