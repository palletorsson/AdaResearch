# Artifact: Balance Puzzle

> stack(8 pieces) until stable for stability_time seconds → creature emerges

## Context

**Category:** puzzles | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** transformation, balance, gravity, stacking, stability, physics, puzzles | **Themes:** decoration, environment

Stack pieces until the system stops moving. Each drop translates and reorients a block, but the goal is not motion for its own sake: it is finding a configuration whose center of mass and support relations hold long enough to trigger the creature reward. The puzzle turns balance into a lived lesson about which transformations preserve stability and which topple it. It frames transformation as a negotiation with gravity.

*stability is not a property of any single piece — it emerges from the relationships between all*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** learner experiences the sublime of physics — patience and precision rewarded with life
- **Critical Parameter:** stability_time — how long the stack must hold before the reward animation fires
- **What Emerges:** the creature as reward for embodied patience; the stack as a meditation on balance under gravity
- **Triggers:** height_threshold reached AND held stable → piece count counts down → creature animation
