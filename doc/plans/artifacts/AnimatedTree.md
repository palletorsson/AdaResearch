Time-sliced L-System interpretation showing organic growth animation

*Watching a tree grow generation by generation teaches more than seeing the final form.*

## Design

- **Scale:** [1.0, 8.0, 9.0] (environment)
- **Interaction:** To grow before your eyes — each generation adding branches with tapering thickness and wind sway
- **Critical Parameter:** growth_delay — controls the pace of revelation, from instant to meditative
- **What Emerges:** Wind animation on cached segments creates living motion — the tree breathes after it finishes growing
- **Triggers:** Each generation adds depth, widens angles (angle_spread_per_gen), and adds leaves at terminal branches
