Schrödinger's cat thought experiment - superposition collapses upon observation

*observation does not reveal a pre-existing state — it creates one*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** open the box and watch the superposition collapse — the lid swings, the state resolves, then resets
- **Critical Parameter:** auto_reset_time — how long the collapsed state persists before returning to superposition
- **What Emerges:** the discomfort of genuine indeterminacy — the cat is not secretly alive or dead, it is both
- **Triggers:** click/observe collapses the wavefunction randomly; timer resets to superposition; lid animates open/close
