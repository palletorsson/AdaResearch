*The cave was never designed — it was constrained into existence, neighborhood by neighborhood.*

## Design

- **Scale:** [13.0, 3.0, 13.0] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** To grow a cave system from a small example: learn local neighborhoods and synthesize globally coherent output
- **Critical Parameter:** overlap_size — how many voxels of context must match; larger = more faithful, slower
- **What Emerges:** Caves, tunnels, and chambers that look like the exemplar but extend far beyond it
- **Triggers:** Auto-generate fills the volume; coherence search accelerates by reusing exemplar positions; debug shows internals
