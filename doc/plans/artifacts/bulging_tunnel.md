# Artifact: Bulging Tunnel

> a cylinder that refuses regularity — CSG spheres bulge the walls at intervals, making geometry breathe

## Context

**Category:** geometry | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** alternative_geometry, tunnel, distortion, non_euclidean, spatial, csg, procedural, cave, parametric | **Themes:** geometry, non_euclidean

Walk into a cylinder that refuses to stay regular. CSG spheres bulge outward at irregular intervals, warping your sense of depth — the tunnel breathes. The num_bulges and bulge_max_radius parameters control how much chaos enters the geometry: low values produce a hallway, high values produce a cave. Contrasts with Klein bottle (closed non-orientable) and Möbius strip (twisted surface); prepares for hyperbolic spaces where distortion becomes the rule.

*a corridor is just a cave with its chaos removed*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [3, 3, 10] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** to feel depth warp — the tunnel contracts and expands, confusing distance perception
- **Critical Parameter:** num_bulges — controls the lambda between hallway (F=order) and cave (E=chaos)
- **What Emerges:** at high bulge counts the tunnel becomes organic, more cave than corridor — geometry becomes landscape
- **Triggers:** changing num_bulges or bulge_max_radius rebuilds the entire tunnel with new distortion profile
