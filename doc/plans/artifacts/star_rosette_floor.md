# Artifact: Star Rosette Floor

> a floor that carries the sacred geometry of intersecting triangles

## Context

**Category:** primitives | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 2d, pattern, mosaic, floor, pompeii, hexagram, star | **Themes:** arrays, art, cultural_computation

Pompeii mosaic floor with hexagonal star rosette (hexagram) pattern. Six-pointed stars formed by overlapping equilateral triangles with small rhombi between stars. Classic Roman geometric mosaic.

*every hexagram is just two triangles that chose to coexist*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** players look down and see how two simple triangles create complex star forms
- **Critical Parameter:** stars_across — number of star cells on the short axis
- **What Emerges:** the understanding that overlap of simple forms creates emergent complexity
- **Triggers:** instantiation or apply_grid_config
