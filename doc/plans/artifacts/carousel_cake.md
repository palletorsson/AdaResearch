Watch a stack of circular layers rotate at exponentially increasing speeds. Every layer obeys the same rule, but the multiplier compounds until the sculpture reads as interference, rhythm, and spectacle rather than a simple spin. The artifact makes scaling a motion parameter feel as dramatic as changing a shape. It stages rotation as geometric progression.

*geometric progression means small multiplier differences create enormous speed ratios after many layers*
