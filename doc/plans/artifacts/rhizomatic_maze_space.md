A labyrinthine exploration of rhizomatic growth patterns, creating interconnected maze-like spaces that grow organically like fungal networks or plant root systems

*a rhizome has no beginning and no end — it is always in the middle, between things, a line of flight*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [50, 21, 50] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** to wander a non-hierarchical tunnel network where every path leads to more paths, with no center and no dead ends that feel final
- **Critical Parameter:** branch_probability (0.7) — controls how often paths fork; at 1.0 the network explodes, at 0 it is a single corridor
- **What Emerges:** merge_threshold causes independent growth paths to fuse into loops, creating the non-hierarchical topology that defines a rhizome
- **Triggers:** generate_rhizomatic_maze() builds the full system in 6 phases; regenerate_maze() creates a new seed and rebuilds everything
