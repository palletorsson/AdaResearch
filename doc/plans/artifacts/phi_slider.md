## Context

**Category:** qfep | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** interactive, slider, parameter_control, change_rate, queer_theory | **Themes:** qfep, tools

QFEP φ parameter controller. Rate of entropy change sensitivity: φ<0 (resists change) → φ=0 (neutral) → φ>0 (embraces change). Positive φ is the queer signature—valuing becoming over being.

*phi is the queer parameter — positive phi means the system does not merely tolerate change but seeks it as generative*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** grab the becoming dial and choose: resist change or embrace it
- **Critical Parameter:** phi — rate sensitivity that determines whether the system fights or welcomes transformation
- **What Emerges:** the asymmetry of the scale — negative phi feels heavy (downward particles), positive phi feels liberating (upward)
- **Triggers:** XR grab moves handle; phi_changed signal propagates globally; particles shift direction with sign
