Chair created through recursive cube subdivision

*The chair was always inside the cube — subdivision merely removed what was not chair.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [6.0, 3.0, 4.0] (environment)
- **Interaction:** To prove that a chair lives inside every cube — subdivision as revelation, not construction
- **Critical Parameter:** the 3x3x3 role assignment — which subcubes become seat vs leg vs back determines the entire form
- **What Emerges:** A recognizable chair from pure subdivision logic — no chair blueprint, only cube-cutting rules
- **Triggers:** Each step transforms role-tagged subcubes: flatten seat, stretch legs, extend back, add armrests
