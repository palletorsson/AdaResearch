# Artifact: triangle

> triangle(v0, v1, v2) — the minimal surface: 3 points that define a plane and a bounded region

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 3d | **Themes:** core, primitives

Interactive triangle mesh with draggable vertices and presets for equilateral, right-angled, and isosceles shapes.

*every surface in 3D graphics is an agreement among three points — all meshes are triangles*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 2.0, 6.0] (environment)
- **Interaction:** learner feels how a surface deforms when its boundary points move — topology through touch
- **Critical Parameter:** the double-sided mesh — front and back are different colors, making orientation visible
- **What Emerges:** the area formula as something felt — move vertices apart, area grows; collapse them, area vanishes
- **Triggers:** dragging any vertex grab sphere — mesh rebuilds instantly to reflect new positions; area displayed on drop
