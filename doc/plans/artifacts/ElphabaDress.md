# Artifact: Elphaba Dress

> r(u,v) = waist + (hem-waist)*u, modulated by sin(v*TAU + wave*u)

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** animation, wave | **Themes:** oscillation, physics, waves

Cloth simulation as wave—fabric responds to movement with ripples propagating outward. The dress becomes a visible record of motion, trailing oscillations behind every gesture.

*Fabric is a surface that remembers every wave that passes through it.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: vertex
- **Interaction:** Watch fabric ripple as sine waves propagate through a procedural dress mesh
- **Critical Parameter:** wave_intensity — transforms static garment into living oscillation
- **What Emerges:** cloth-like motion from pure trigonometric displacement of vertices
- **Triggers:** set_wave_intensity() deforms the parametric surface in real-time
