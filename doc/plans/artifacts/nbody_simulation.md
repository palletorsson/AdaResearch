# Artifact: N-Body Gravitational Simulation

> F = G*m1*m2/r^2 for all pairs. O(n^2) pairwise gravity. Every body pulls every other. The computational cost of democracy.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** nbody, gravity, gravitational, orbital, galaxy, binary_star, pairwise, computational, barnes_hut | **Themes:** gravity, nbody, orbital_mechanics, computational

N-body gravitational simulation where every mass attracts every other mass via Newton's law of gravitation (F = Gm₁m₂/r²). Watch galaxies form, binary stars orbit, and clusters scatter. The O(n²) pairwise force computation is the computational bottleneck that drove the invention of Barnes-Hut trees and fast multipole methods.

*Gravity is the simplest force and the hardest computation. N bodies, N^2 interactions. Complexity comes from counting, not from the law.*
