# Artifact: Stigmergy Grid

> pheromone[x,y] = clamp(pheromone[x,y] + deposit, 0, 5) * evaporation_rate + diffuse(neighbors) — communication through substrate, not signal

## Context

**Category:** swarmintelligence | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** stigmergy, pheromone, ants, emergence, indirect_communication, swarm | **Themes:** collective, emergence, swarm

Ant pheromone grid with real-time evaporation and diffusion — ants deposit trails that fade over time, demonstrating indirect communication (stigmergy).

*stigmergy proves that coordination does not require awareness — the environment itself is the shared mind*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: generated_texture, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** to watch invisible ant highways materialize as green-white trails on a tiny floor tile — to see communication happen without any message being sent
- **Critical Parameter:** evaporation_rate (0.99 default, meaning 1% decay per step) — faster decay forces ants to constantly reinforce trails or lose them; slower decay produces permanent glowing scars
- **What Emerges:** circular drift loops where ants follow their own old trail around a closed ring, reinforcing it until it dominates the entire grid — a positive feedback trap
- **Triggers:** with sensor_distance=0 ants move randomly and trails are uniform noise; as sensor_distance grows, trails snap into focused bright corridors — the phase transition of stigmergy
