# Artifact: Air Music Display Case

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** animation, simulation, sound, visualization, waves | **Themes:** music, oscillation, physics, waves

A 1m glass museum display case with dark walnut wood frame, containing the Air Music spatial audio system. Minimalist Scandinavian design inspired by Ferm Living. Sound is subtle and ambient—notes triggered by movement through space, phasing loops creating evolving texture.

*Framing sound as specimen transforms listening from passive reception to active examination.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** Examine a generative air music system like a museum specimen with parameter controls
- **Critical Parameter:** audio_volume_db — balances the sonic presence against the visual display
- **What Emerges:** the museum-as-instrument — display cases that you play rather than observe
- **Triggers:** slider adjustments modify the contained air music system parameters
