# Artifact: PRNG Crank Machine

> x_{n+1} = (a·x_n + c) mod m — linear congruential generator

## Context

**Category:** randomness | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** prng, lcg, deterministic, seed, algorithm, interactive | **Themes:** probability, randomness

A physical pseudo-random number generator machine. Press CRANK to step the LCG formula: state = (state * 1664525 + 1013904223) mod 2^32. Each step animates multiply, add, mod phases. Shows deterministic sequence, normalized 0-1 output, and full history.

*Determinism is not the opposite of randomness — it is randomness with a forgotten origin.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** crank the machine, watch arithmetic unfold, feel determinism wearing randomness as a mask
- **Critical Parameter:** initial_seed — same seed reproduces the entire sequence
- **What Emerges:** the history panel reveals periodicity — given enough cranks, the sequence must repeat
- **Triggers:** _crank() → 4-phase animation (multiply, add, mod, result) with color-coded transitions
