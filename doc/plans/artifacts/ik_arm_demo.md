# Artifact: IK Arm Demo

> Two procedural TwoBoneIK3D arms tracing figure-8 patterns — inverse kinematics as embodiment negotiation

## Context

**Category:** body | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** ik, inverse_kinematics, two_bone_ik, arm, body, skeleton, procedural_mesh | **Themes:** generation, procedural

Standalone TwoBoneIK3D arm demonstration — two procedural arms with animated targets tracing figure-8 patterns. Shows the same inverse kinematics solver used for VR body tracking.

*The arm does not move — the target moves, and the arm solves for how to follow.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** To show how VR arms work: skeleton, bone chain, target marker, and the solver that bends joints to reach
- **Critical Parameter:** upper_arm_length + lower_arm_length — the reach envelope; targets beyond this sum reveal the solver limits
- **What Emerges:** Fluid arm motion from a two-bone chain solver — the same algorithm that connects floating VR hands to shoulders
- **Triggers:** Figure-8 targets push arms through full range; near-limit reaches show elbow flip; symmetric motion reveals bilateral IK
