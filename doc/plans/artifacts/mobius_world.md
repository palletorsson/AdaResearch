*The Mobius strip does not have two sides — it has one side and the persistent illusion of two.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [69, 16, 72] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** To be walked — one full loop and your orientation flips, a second loop restores it, and you realize the surface has one side
- **Critical Parameter:** the u/2 in cos(u/2) — this single half-angle is the entire twist, the difference between a cylinder and a Mobius strip
- **What Emerges:** The disorientation of walking a non-orientable surface — you cannot tell which side you are on because there is only one side
- **Triggers:** Wall-walk collision layer enables lizard-feet adhesion; completing a loop flips the player's up-vector
