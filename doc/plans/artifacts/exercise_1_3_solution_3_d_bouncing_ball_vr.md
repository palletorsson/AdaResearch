A ball trapped in a box, slowly dying. Position accumulates velocity, velocity accumulates gravity, walls reverse and damp. Each bounce lower than the last as elasticity drains energy. A trail draws the ball's parabolic history as a fading line. The simplest dynamics: one force, one constraint, and the slow settling as the universe forgets.

*A bouncing ball is a clock that runs down. Each bounce measures how much the universe forgot.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: vertex
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** To trap motion in a box and watch it slowly die — each bounce lower than the last, energy leaking into the walls.
- **Critical Parameter:** _elasticity (0.9) — the fraction of speed kept per bounce. At 1.0 the ball bounces forever; below 1.0 it decays exponentially.
- **What Emerges:** The parabolic arc between bounces. The trail drawing the ball’s history as a fading line. The slow settling as elasticity drains energy.
- **Triggers:** Automatic — ball launches with initial velocity, gravity pulls down every frame, boundary collision reverses and damps per-axis
