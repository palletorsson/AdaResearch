# Artifact: Snap Tetra Puzzle

> tetrahedron = 4 vertices, 6 edges, 4 triangular faces — the minimal 3D solid

## Context

**Category:** qfep

Snap-point tetrahedron assembly puzzle. Represents constraint satisfaction and ordered structure — how Free Energy minimization drives systems toward specific geometric configurations.

*you cannot enclose volume with fewer than 4 faces — the tetrahedron is the minimum*

## Design

- **Interaction:** learner constructs the simplest possible polyhedron from 4 floating snap points
- **Critical Parameter:** 4 snap target positions — form the unique convex hull of 4 non-coplanar points
- **What Emerges:** the tetrahedron as the 3D analog of the triangle — irreducible, minimum faces for enclosure
- **Triggers:** snapping all 4 snap points to their targets → puzzle complete signal fires
