# Artifact: Distribution Sampler

> f(x|θ) — probability density function parameterized by distribution type

## Context

**Category:** emergence | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** probability, distribution, sampling, histogram, statistics | **Themes:** commons, emergence, mixed

Sample from various probability distributions — uniform, Gaussian, Poisson, exponential — and watch the histogram build in real-time. Compare theoretical curves with empirical results, experiencing the law of large numbers as shapes converge.

*Every distribution is a constraint on randomness — the shape of what remains possible.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** switch between Uniform, Gaussian, Poisson, Exponential and watch falling particles build different shapes
- **Critical Parameter:** distribution — selects which PDF governs sampling; each has fundamentally different tail behavior
- **What Emerges:** the theoretical PDF curve overlays the histogram and they converge — shape is not noise, noise has shape
- **Triggers:** _sample_distribution() dispatches to Box-Muller (Gaussian), inverse CDF (Exponential), Knuth (Poisson)
