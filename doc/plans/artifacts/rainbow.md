# Artifact: rainbow

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** color | **Themes:** algorithms, misc

Creates double rainbow arcs with atmospheric bloom and layered color bands.

*a rainbow has no location — it exists only in the geometry between sun, water, and eye*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [1.0, 21.0, 50.0] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** to stand beneath a double rainbow and feel the scale of spectral order arching overhead
- **Critical Parameter:** SECONDARY_FADE — controls how visible the second rainbow is, teaching that double rainbows exist with reversed color order
- **What Emerges:** the reversed color order of the secondary rainbow is physically accurate and usually surprises people
- **Triggers:** atmospheric particles drift with sin(time); no interaction triggers — the rainbow simply is
