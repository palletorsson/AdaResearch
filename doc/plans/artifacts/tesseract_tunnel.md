## Design

- **Interaction:** To let players walk inside a rotating tesseract and feel dimensional projection break their spatial intuition
- **Critical Parameter:** w_offset — position in the fourth dimension; animating it sweeps the projection through 4D space
- **What Emerges:** Architecture that cannot exist in 3D — nested cubes, self-intersecting edges, cells passing through each other
- **Triggers:** Changing projection_type switches views; w animation creates breathing geometry; edge colors shift with 4D depth
