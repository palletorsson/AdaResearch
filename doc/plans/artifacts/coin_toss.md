# Artifact: Coin Toss

> X ~ Bernoulli(0.5) — the atom of all probability

## Context

**Category:** randomness | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** coin, bernoulli, binary, probability, convergence, interactive, grab | **Themes:** probability, randomness

The simplest random experiment. Grab coins from a tray and toss them. A counter tracks the heads/tails ratio converging to 0.5 over many flips. Gold side = heads, silver side = tails.

*The Bernoulli trial is the hydrogen atom of randomness — everything complex is built from binary choices.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** grab a coin, toss it, watch H/T ratio crawl toward 0.5000 one flip at a time
- **Critical Parameter:** the coin's global_transform.basis.y — dot product with world UP determines heads vs tails
- **What Emerges:** the history ribbon (HHTTHHTHT...) looks random but its running average is a straight line to 0.5
- **Triggers:** _on_coin_dropped() sets thrown=true; velocity settling below threshold for 0.6s reads result
