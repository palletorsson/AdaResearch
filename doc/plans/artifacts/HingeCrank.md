A motorized crank mechanism combining three joint types into one machine. A hinge joint spins a wheel at constant velocity, a pin joint connects the wheel to a sliding rod, and a slider joint guides the rod along a rail. The result is rotary-to-linear conversion — the fundamental mechanism behind pistons, steam engines, and sewing machines. Watch how continuous rotation becomes reciprocating translation through the geometry of constraint.

*Every engine is a conversation between rotation and translation. The crank is the translator.*
