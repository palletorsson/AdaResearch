## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to turn a single entropy slider and watch a gyroid surface crumple from smooth order into noisy complexity — morphogenesis as a function of one number
- **Critical Parameter:** S (entropy) — at S=0 the gyroid is smooth and periodic, at S=1 noise disrupts the field and the surface fragments into chaotic topology
- **What Emerges:** the gyroid's triply-periodic minimal surface creates passages and chambers that open and close as entropy changes, producing architecture from pure mathematics
- **Triggers:** setting S (via set_entropy or export) recomputes frequency, noise_amp, and threshold; regenerate_at_entropy triggers marching cubes mesh rebuild
