# Artifact: Layered Membrane

> N concentric SurfaceTool cylinders with sin() + Perlin displacement -> layered translucent membrane sculpture

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** intermediate

Procedural membrane generation with layered structures

*a membrane is a boundary that is also a surface — it separates inside from outside while being neither*

## Design

- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to peer into nested translucent layers and see color shift from purple to orange as depth increases — geology made of light
- **Critical Parameter:** undulation_amount — controls the amplitude of wave displacement; at 0 the layers are smooth cylinders, at high values they fold into biological forms
- **What Emerges:** subsurface scattering + alpha transparency creates the illusion of depth even on flat screens — layers glow from within
- **Triggers:** Space key regenerates with new noise seed; rotation animation slowly reveals the structure from all angles
