A motorized drawbridge attached to a stone tower via a hinge joint. The motor rotates the bridge up and down within an 85-degree arc. Arrow keys control the motor velocity; when idle, the bridge gently oscillates with a sinusoidal drive. The hinge joint is the workhorse of mechanical constraint — one rotational degree of freedom, producing everything from doors to engines. Here it lifts a bridge, making the constraint visible as architecture.

*A hinge is the most intuitive constraint. Everything else is locked; one axis is free. Doors, bridges, jaws.*
