# Artifact: Particle Systems

> emit(position, velocity, lifetime). Each particle: born, forced, aged, dead. The system is the population, not the individual.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** particles, emitter, lifetime, force, fire, smoke, sparks, statistical, ensemble, effects | **Themes:** particles, emitters, lifetime, effects

Particle emitters spawning thousands of particles with configurable lifetimes, initial velocities, forces, and visual properties. Particles are born, live under physics, and die — creating fire, smoke, sparks, and rain from simple statistical rules. Each particle is independent, yet the ensemble produces coherent visual phenomena.

*A particle system is a statistical object. No single particle matters. The population's distribution is the phenomenon.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [12, 9, 11] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** To show four archetypal particle behaviors side by side — fountain (ballistic), fire (buoyant), sparks (explosive), snow (gentle) — all from the same GPU machinery.
- **Critical Parameter:** gravity direction and damping per system. Fountain: gravity down, low damp. Fire: gravity UP (buoyancy), high damp. These two parameters create completely different phenomena.
- **What Emerges:** Fire looking like fire despite being spheres — because upward gravity + high damping + color ramp = buoyant fading embers. The color gradient does most of the work.
- **Triggers:** Automatic — all four GPUParticles3D emit continuously. No interaction needed; the artifact is a living taxonomy.
