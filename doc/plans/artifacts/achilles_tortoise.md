# Artifact: Achilles and the Tortoise

> A_n = L*(1-1/2^n), T_n = L*(1-1/2^(n+1)) — geometric series converging to a finite limit

## Context

**Category:** primitives | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** zeno, limits, convergence, series, animation | **Themes:** core, primitives

Zeno's paradox of Achilles and the Tortoise. Watch infinite steps converge to a finite limit: A_n = L*(1-1/2^n). Each step halves the gap — infinite steps, finite distance. The paradox dissolves in the limit.

*infinite subdivision converges — Zeno was right about the steps, wrong about the sum*

## Design

- **Interaction:** learner feels the paradox in time — watching each step get smaller, knowing they converge
- **Critical Parameter:** the step reveal timing — each step marker appears sequentially, spacing halving each time
- **What Emerges:** the resolution — infinite steps sum to finite distance; the paradox dissolves in the limit
- **Triggers:** auto-advances through steps; each step places Achilles and Tortoise markers at new positions
