## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 2.0, 1.0] (environment)
- **Interaction:** learner sees the theorem not as a formula but as an area relationship — squares fitting together
- **Critical Parameter:** the right angle vertex — fixing it keeps the right angle while the learner explores leg ratios
- **What Emerges:** the theorem as a visual conservation law — the big square always equals the two small squares combined
- **Triggers:** dragging any vertex — hypotenuse square resizes to match the sum of the two leg squares
