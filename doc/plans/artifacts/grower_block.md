# Artifact: Grower Block

> scale(block) -> grow(into_player) -> pit(death) — a cube that expands and squeezes you out

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** hazard, transformation, scale, grow, breathe, pit | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

A cube that pulses between tiny and huge, physically pushing the player as it grows. When small you can pass, when large it fills the corridor and squeezes you into the pit. Color shifts from green (safe) to red (danger).

*scale is the transformation that changes everything while appearing to change nothing.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** to make scale felt as pressure — the world growing into your body, stealing your space
- **Critical Parameter:** grow_speed / max_scale — the breathing rhythm must be readable: small=safe, big=danger
- **What Emerges:** the uncanny transformation — you don't move, the world moves into you
- **Triggers:** sine-wave scale cycle; AnimatableBody3D collision pushes player as it grows
