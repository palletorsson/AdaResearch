# Artifact: Audio Catalog Tablet

> catalog[category][sound_key] -> preview(parameters) -> waveform display

## Context

**Category:** audio | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** audio, sound, vr | **Themes:** decoration, environment, music

Handheld sound library. Browse 73+ audio presets across 15 categories, preview sounds, edit parameters in VR.

*A sound catalog is a periodic table of timbres, each defined by its synthesis recipe.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [2, 2, 1] (standard)
- **Interaction:** Browse and audition a library of synthesized sounds on a VR tablet interface
- **Critical Parameter:** sound_key — selects which synthesis algorithm to preview
- **What Emerges:** a sonic encyclopedia — every sound becomes a specimen you can examine and compare
- **Triggers:** touch-selecting a sound generates audio and visualizes its waveform
