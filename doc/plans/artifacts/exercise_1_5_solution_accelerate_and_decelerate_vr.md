# Artifact: Accelerate and Decelerate

> steer = (desired - velocity).clamp(max_force). Seek + arrival. Distance to target scales desired speed.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** steering, arrival, acceleration, deceleration, seek, feedback, nature_of_code, vr | **Themes:** linear_algebra, math, vectors

A ball chases a moving target, steering with arrival behavior — accelerating toward the target and decelerating as it approaches. The target traces sinusoidal curves through 3D space. When far, the ball applies maximum force. When close, force scales down proportionally, producing smooth deceleration. Seek behavior with distance-based speed ramping.

*smooth arrival is not a decision — it is a feedback loop. The closer you get, the gentler the approach.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: vertex
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to watch a ball chase a moving target, accelerating when far and decelerating when close — smooth arrival from pure math
- **Critical Parameter:** max_force — the steering budget. High force = snappy turns. Low force = lazy curves. The tightness of the feedback loop.
- **What Emerges:** smooth deceleration zone near the target where speed ramps down proportionally to distance
- **Triggers:** automatic — target traces sinusoidal curves, ball steers toward it every frame
