Procedural pixel art floor with space invader sprites tiled in electric green on a black field. 11x8 bitmap characters march across the ground like an 8-bit arcade screen.

*a 1-bit sprite is a small 2D array — the same data structure that tiles a Roman floor*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** players look down and see 8-bit invader sprites marching across the ground
- **Critical Parameter:** tiles_short — pixel resolution on the short axis
- **What Emerges:** the understanding that bitmap sprites are just boolean arrays rendered to grids
- **Triggers:** instantiation or apply_grid_config
