# Artifact: righttriangle

> right_triangle(base, height) with 90° angle at origin — the geometric primitive of Pythagoras

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** transformation, right_triangle, geometry, invariant, reference, rotation, 2d | **Themes:** core, primitives

A right triangle presented as a clean spatial reference. Translation and rotation can move it anywhere, but the ninety-degree relation remains the defining invariant. That makes it useful for reading orientation, perpendicularity, and angular preservation against more complex scenes. It keeps the idea of the right angle available as a diagnostic form.

*the right angle is the basis of all rectangular coordinate systems — it defines what "perpendicular" means*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner has a reference form for right angles to which other geometry is compared
- **Critical Parameter:** the right angle at the origin — this defines the shape; moving it makes it non-right
- **What Emerges:** the usefulness of black — a neutral diagnostic color that does not compete with adjacent color coding
- **Triggers:** nothing — static reference object; thin (0.05 depth) for readability as a 2D form in 3D space
