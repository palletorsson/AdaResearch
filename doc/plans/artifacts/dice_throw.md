# Artifact: Dice Throw

> P(X=k) = 1/6 for k ∈ {1,...,6} — discrete uniform distribution

## Context

**Category:** randomness | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** dice, uniform_distribution, expected_value, probability, physics, interactive, grab | **Themes:** probability, randomness

A VR dice on a felt table. Grab and throw the dice — roll a 6 and 100 balls rain from the sky, roll a 1 and just 1 ball falls. Tracks roll statistics showing convergence to discrete uniform distribution with expected value 3.5.

*Fairness is not a single outcome — it is the symmetry of the generating process.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** grab a die, throw it physically, and be showered with reward balls proportional to the result
- **Critical Parameter:** balls_per_pip — multiplier that makes a 6 spectacular (96 balls) and a 1 humbling (16 balls)
- **What Emerges:** the histogram converges to E[X]=3.5 — fairness is not felt in one throw but in the accumulation
- **Triggers:** _on_dice_dropped() starts settle detection; velocity < 0.02 for 0.8s triggers _read_dice_result()
