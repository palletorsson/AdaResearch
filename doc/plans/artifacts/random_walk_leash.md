# Artifact: Random Walk Leash

> F_tug = impulse_strength · random_unit_vector — stochastic forcing on a tethered body

## Context

**Category:** randomness | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** random_walk, embodied, haptic, drift, sqrt_n, physics, interactive, grab | **Themes:** probability, randomness

A glowing orb on a leash that tugs in random directions. Grab it in VR and feel the random walk through your hand. The orb pulses with each impulse. A trail traces the walk path. Stats track drift vs sqrt(N) theoretical scaling.

*To hold randomness on a leash is to feel that unpredictability has weight.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** grab the orb and feel randomness pulling your hand — your arm is the integral of noise
- **Critical Parameter:** impulse_strength — the force per tug; too low and you feel nothing, too high and it escapes
- **What Emerges:** RMS displacement tracks sqrt(N) — the fundamental diffusion law felt through proprioception
- **Triggers:** _impulse_timer fires every impulse_interval seconds, applying a random 3D impulse to the orb
