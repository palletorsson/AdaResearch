# Artifact: Molecular Designer

> catalog(parts) + assembly(bonds, positions) -> molecular morphology

## Context

**Category:** biology | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** molecular, chemistry, constraint_satisfaction, valence, biology, interactive | **Themes:** biology, simulation

Interactive molecular construction tool — atoms snap to valence rules, bonds obey geometry, and molecular structure emerges from chemical constraints. Formalization as hands-on building.

*form is not imposed on matter — it is a conversation between catalog and constraint*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [1, 6, 8] (environment)
- **Interaction:** watch scattered atoms find each other and snap into bodies
- **Critical Parameter:** assembly_name — switches between VRBody, Chair, entirely different topologies
- **What Emerges:** the moment of recognition when disordered parts coalesce into a legible form
- **Triggers:** auto_cycle timer alternates float/assemble; JSON hot-reload rebuilds catalog live
