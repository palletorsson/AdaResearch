# Artifact: Shader Arch Gallery

> for i in total_cubes: cube[i].material = wallpaper_tile(random_group, random_palette, random_domain, random_scale) — every cube in a tunnel is a unique patchwork tile

## Context

**Category:** shaders | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** shader, gallery, arch, tunnel, surfacetool, showcase | **Themes:** art, shaders, visual

A barrel-vault arch tunnel where each section has a different GPU shader. Walk through to see 20 shaders on curved walls, ceiling, and floor.

*a patchwork is not random — it is every possible pattern given exactly one tile to exist in, and the corridor is the argument that they all deserve space*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: generated_texture, animation: static
- **Interaction:** to walk through a self-built tunnel where floor, walls, and ceiling are each a different wallpaper pattern — Art to Eat style patchwork quilted in light
- **Critical Parameter:** tunnel_length — how many cubes deep the tunnel extends; 25 cubes means hundreds of unique shader-pattern surfaces
- **What Emerges:** 10 domain generators (blocks, stripes, diagonal, cross, concentric, checker, scatter, stairs, zigzag, frame) combined with 10 palettes and 17 groups produce a tunnel that feels hand-curated but is entirely algorithmic
- **Triggers:** _ready builds the full tunnel geometry procedurally; each cube gets a random wallpaper group (0-16), palette (10 options), domain pattern (10 generators), and tile_scale
