# Artifact: Fitness Landscape Politics

> 30 PSO particles on 3 switchable fitness landscapes — convergence (corporate), spread (ecological), novelty (artistic) — same agents, different power structures

## Context

**Category:** swarmintelligence | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** pso, particles, optimization, critical_theory, fitness, swarm | **Themes:** collective, emergence, swarm, philosophy

30 PSO particles on 3 switchable fitness landscapes: convergence (corporate — minimize distance), spread (ecological — maximize diversity), novelty (artistic — seek the unvisited). Same agents, different power structures, different 'success.'

*the swarm always optimizes — the question is never whether to optimize but who wrote the objective function*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [3, 2, 3] (room_scale)
- **Interaction:** to make the learner feel the moment the swarm reorganizes when you switch landscapes, and realize that "optimal" was never neutral
- **Critical Parameter:** landscape_mode — switching it does not change the particles, only what counts as success, and the swarm obeys
- **What Emerges:** the transition animations between landscapes — particles hesitate, reorient, sometimes oscillate before committing to the new definition of success
- **Triggers:** pressing CONVERGE makes the swarm collapse to center; pressing SPREAD makes it explode outward; pressing NOVELTY makes it seek edges and gaps
