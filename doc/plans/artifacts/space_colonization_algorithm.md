space_colonization_algorithm

*a tree does not know its shape — it only knows which direction has light, and how far to reach before stopping*

## Design

- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to watch a vascular network bloom from a single root as branches compete for scattered attractor points in 3D
- **Critical Parameter:** kill_distance — the radius at which an attractor is consumed; too small and branches overshoot, too large and the tree is sparse
- **What Emerges:** natural branch tapering from thickness_decay creates tree-like hierarchy without any explicit tree data structure
- **Triggers:** regenerate rebuilds entirely; step_growth advances one iteration; toggle_animation switches between instant and animated growth
