# Artifact: CharacterRagdoll

> torso + leg + cone-twist hip. The minimal ragdoll — one joint, two bodies, biomechanical limits.

## Context

**Category:** forces | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** physics, joints, cone-twist-joint, ragdoll, character, biomechanics, hip-joint, multi-body | **Themes:** physics, simulation

A minimal ragdoll — one torso and one leg connected by a cone-twist joint at the hip. The joint limits swing to 35 degrees and twist to 25 degrees, mimicking the biomechanical range of a human hip. Press space to impulse the leg and watch how the cone-twist constraint channels the force into naturalistic swinging. This is the seed of character physics: from this single hip joint, the full ragdoll grows by adding more limbs and joints.

*A body built from constraints begins to look like it has intention. That is the uncanny valley of physics.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [1.0, 5.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** To look alive while being purely mechanical. The cone-twist hip makes the leg swing like a leg.
- **Critical Parameter:** swing_span (35°) and twist_span (25°) — mimicking human hip range of motion.
- **What Emerges:** Ragdoll-like motion from a single constrained joint. The illusion of intent from pure physics.
- **Triggers:** Space key impulses the leg; cone-twist channels the force into naturalistic swing.
