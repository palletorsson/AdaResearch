# Artifact: Fibonacci Snake

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** advanced

Triangle strip coiled in a golden spiral unfolds into a slithering sine-wave snake. Phi encodes rest, sin encodes motion. Same vertices, same faces — only positions change.

*phi encodes rest. sin encodes motion. fold_amount transitions between them.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** golden ratio geometry becoming locomotion — the spiral IS the resting pose, the wave IS the motion
- **Critical Parameter:** fold_amount — 0.0 is tight Fibonacci spiral, 1.0 is straight sine-wave snake
- **What Emerges:** the snake slithers by phase-shifting the sine wave along its body
- **Triggers:** vertices interpolate between spiral positions and sine-wave positions
