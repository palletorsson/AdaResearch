delaunay_triangulation_3d_cell

*One constraint — no point inside any circumsphere — is enough to produce the best possible triangulation.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** To show the dual of Voronoi: optimal triangulation where no point violates any circumsphere, dragging a vertex re-triangulates live
- **Critical Parameter:** randomness — how far points deviate from hierarchical placement; 0 = regular lattice, 1 = scattered cloud
- **What Emerges:** Mathematically optimal triangulation from a single geometric constraint applied everywhere
- **Triggers:** Grabbing a vertex and dragging re-triggers triangulation; low randomness produces crystalline; high produces chaotic mesh
