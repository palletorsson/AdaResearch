# Artifact: Enhanced K-Means Clustering

> assign each point to nearest centroid; move centroids to cluster means; repeat until convergence

## Context

**Category:** machinelearning | **Complexity:** intermediate

Interactive K-means clustering visualization with adjustable k, colored cluster assignments, and centroid movement animation.

*clustering does not discover structure in data — it imposes structure by choosing K*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [50, 10, 50] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** watch colored clusters crystallize from unlabeled chaos as centroids drift to their resting places
- **Critical Parameter:** cluster_count (K) — choosing K is the fundamental unsupervised learning decision
- **What Emerges:** Voronoi tessellation of space; initialization sensitivity (K-means++ vs random); the elbow in the inertia curve
- **Triggers:** iteration step reassigns points and moves centroids; convergence stops when movement < threshold
