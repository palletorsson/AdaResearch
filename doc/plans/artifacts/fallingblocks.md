# Artifact: Falling Blocks

> spawn(block) -> fall(gravity) -> stack_or_destroy -- blocks spawning and falling as hazard

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** environmental, gravity, blocks, spawner | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

Environmental hazard that spawns falling block formations. Gravity as threat — the grid raining down. Dodge, shelter, or ride the cascade.

*gravity is the simplest algorithm -- spawn, fall, accumulate. The hazard is just physics with a schedule.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [1.0, 2.0, 3.0] (room_scale)
- **Interaction:** a rain of rigid blocks that accumulate, stack, and threaten with geometric debris
- **Critical Parameter:** _spawn_timer interval -- how frequently new blocks appear; gravity determines fall speed
- **What Emerges:** Tetris without player control -- blocks fall and the landscape of danger builds itself
- **Triggers:** timer-based spawning; RigidBody3D physics drives falling; collision with player causes damage
