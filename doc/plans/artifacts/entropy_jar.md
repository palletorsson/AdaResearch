# Artifact: Entropy Jar

> dS/dt >= 0 — the second law of thermodynamics

## Context

**Category:** randomness | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** entropy, thermodynamics, irreversibility, mixing, shannon, second_law, physics, interactive, grab | **Themes:** probability, randomness

A glass jar of red and blue particles on a pedestal. Grab and shake to mix them — they never unmix. Live Shannon entropy readout tracks disorder. The second law of thermodynamics felt physically through VR interaction.

*The second law is not a prohibition — it is the statistical certainty that mixed states outnumber ordered ones.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** shake the jar and feel time's arrow in your hand — red and blue never separate again
- **Critical Parameter:** shake_threshold — the velocity at which your hand movement registers as agitation
- **What Emerges:** Shannon entropy measured across vertical bins converges to maximum and stays there
- **Triggers:** _agitate_particles() on shake detection via accelerometer-derived velocity
