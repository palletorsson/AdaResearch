# Artifact: Simple Oscillating Bridge

> y_i(t) = A_y * sin(omega * t + phi * i) + A_x * cos(omega * t + phi * i)

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 3d, animation, wave | **Themes:** music, oscillation, physics, waves

A walkable surface that breathes—rising and falling in sine wave motion. Cross it to feel oscillation in your body, the ground itself becoming unstable in a predictable rhythm.

*A traveling wave is identical oscillators displaced in phase.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** Walk across platforms that rise and fall in a sine wave beneath your feet
- **Critical Parameter:** oscillation_speed — controls how fast the wave propagates through platforms
- **What Emerges:** a walkable wave — the body learns wavelength by stepping across oscillating ground
- **Triggers:** phase offset per platform creates traveling wave illusion
