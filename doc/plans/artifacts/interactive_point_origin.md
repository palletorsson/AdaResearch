## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** primitive | **Themes:** core, primitives

Pickable point that displays its position and draws a thin line to origin (0,0,0) when held.

*a coordinate is a measurement from origin — hold the point, feel the measurement*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner feels coordinates as embodied distance from origin, not abstract numbers
- **Critical Parameter:** the live line drawn to (0,0,0) while held — distance made visible
- **What Emerges:** haptic pulse as a format-change event — the body learns before the mind
- **Triggers:** pickup cycles through 4 coordinate display formats (decimal / integer / scientific / words)
