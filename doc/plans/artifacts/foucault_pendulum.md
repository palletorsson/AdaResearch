# Artifact: Foucault Pendulum

> swing_plane(t) = initial_azimuth, earth_angle(t) += omega_earth * sin(latitude) * dt

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** advanced

A brass pendulum swings from high above, drawing rosette patterns on a cream canvas below. The swing plane slowly precesses—Foucault's elegant proof that Earth rotates beneath us. Dark ink traces accumulate into intricate spirograph-like figures over time.

*The pendulum does not rotate — the Earth rotates beneath it, and the trail is proof.*
