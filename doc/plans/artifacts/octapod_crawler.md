## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** hazards, procedural | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

Procedural 8-legged wall-crawling creature. Headcrab meets octopus. Patrols surfaces, detects player, chases and leaps. Can be stunned with fire. Future: befriend in lab, evolves through game.

*locomotion is not a feature -- it is the first argument a body makes about its space.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** eight legs finding surfaces -- walls, ceilings, floors -- body follows feet, feet follow world
- **Critical Parameter:** IK target positions per leg -- each foot plants on nearest surface; pure procedural IK
- **What Emerges:** the gait IS the geometry -- eight-leg redundancy dissolves the gait problem entirely
- **Triggers:** continuous surface sampling; foot placement via IK; body orientation follows foot normals
