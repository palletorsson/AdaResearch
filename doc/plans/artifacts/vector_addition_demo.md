# Artifact: Vector Addition

> C = A + B. Head-to-tail construction. The parallelogram law.

## Context

**Category:** mathematics | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** vectors, addition, parallelogram, interactive | **Themes:** vectors

Interactive vector addition visualizer: A + B = C. Shows parallelogram law with head-to-tail placement and ghost arrows.

*Addition is geometry, not arithmetic. Two directions become one.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [4, 2, 4] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** To let the learner grab two arrows and watch a third arrow appear — the sum — making addition tangible rather than symbolic.
- **Critical Parameter:** The angle between vector_a and vector_b. Parallel vectors add magnitudes; perpendicular vectors add by Pythagoras; opposed vectors cancel.
- **What Emerges:** The parallelogram from ghost arrows. The triangle inequality (|A+B| <= |A|+|B|) becomes visible without being stated.
- **Triggers:** Handle drag → resultant updates, preset buttons → snap to orthogonal/acute/3D configurations, ghost arrows → parallelogram appears
