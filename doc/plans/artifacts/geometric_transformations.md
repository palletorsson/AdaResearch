Animates point, line, plane, and cube primitives through rotation, scale, translation, and shear stages.

*A vertex does not know what shape it belongs to — it only knows the matrix that moves it.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [13.0, 16.0, 11.0] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** To make matrix transformations visible: watch the same four operations reshape four geometric objects in sequence
- **Critical Parameter:** stage_interval — seconds per transformation type; controls the pace of the demonstration cycle
- **What Emerges:** Understanding that all mesh deformation reduces to four matrix operations applied to vertices
- **Triggers:** Each 3-second stage applies a new transformation; the four primitives deform together showing how transforms compose
