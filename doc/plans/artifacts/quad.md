# Artifact: quad

> quad = 2 triangles sharing a diagonal — four vertices, two faces, one surface

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** primitive | **Themes:** core, primitives

Editable quad divided into pink and black triangles with draggable corners and square/rectangle/diamond presets.

*a quad face has no single correct triangulation — the software chooses, and the choice affects shading*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner discovers that every quad is secretly two triangles — and the diagonal is a choice
- **Critical Parameter:** the different front/back colors (bi-reflection) — orientation becomes unmistakably visible
- **What Emerges:** the arbitrary nature of the diagonal — quad triangulation is not unique, it is a design decision
- **Triggers:** dragging any of the 4 vertex grab spheres — both triangles rebuild instantly from shared vertices
