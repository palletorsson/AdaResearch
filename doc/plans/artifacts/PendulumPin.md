The simplest joint demo — a single sphere suspended from a fixed anchor by a pin joint. Gravity pulls the bob down; the pin constraint keeps it tethered, converting potential energy into angular motion. The bob swings freely in 3D, slowly decaying as linear damping bleeds energy. This is the atom of the joints sequence: one constraint, one body, one degree of freedom worth watching.

*A pendulum is a clock that runs down. The pin joint is the simplest promise: you may rotate, nothing else.*
