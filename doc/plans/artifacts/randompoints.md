# Artifact: randompoints

> blue_noise = dart_throwing — place points rejecting any candidate closer than min_dist to existing points, so emptiness is forbidden

## Context

**Category:** emergence | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** 3d, emergence, interactive, probability, procedural, vr, blue_noise, gaussian, distribution, dart_throwing | **Themes:** emergence, probability, randomness

Three distributions reveal three relationships with space. Uniform scatters without memory — each point ignores every other. Gaussian clusters around a center — proximity is destiny. Blue noise enforces minimum distance via dart-throwing rejection, structured randomness where emptiness is forbidden. The critical parameter is blue_noise_min_dist: the exclusion radius that converts white noise into jazz. Contrasts with randompoint (single white noise); prepares for Perlin noise (structured randomness with continuity).

*structured randomness is not less random — it is randomness with a constraint, the way jazz improvisation is constrained by harmony*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to feel the difference between fairness and freedom — blue noise is crowded rooms with social distancing, white noise is a mosh pit
- **Critical Parameter:** blue_noise_min_dist — the exclusion radius that turns white noise into structured randomness
- **What Emerges:** the packing limit — blue noise runs out of room before placing all requested points, which becomes visible as a teaching moment about density
- **Triggers:** switching DistributionType between UNIFORM/GAUSSIAN/BLUE_NOISE reveals three fundamentally different relationships with space
