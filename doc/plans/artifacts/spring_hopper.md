Coil spring creature that bounces rhythmically. Land on top when compressed to get launched upward — Hooke's law as creature.

*A spring stores energy. Compression is potential. Release is motion. F = -kx is life.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** bounce endlessly, compress and release, launch anything that lands on top
- **Critical Parameter:** _compression (0=fully extended/tall, 1=fully compressed/short)
- **What Emerges:** the launch mechanic — player times a landing on the compressed spring to get boosted
- **Triggers:** IDLE->WIND->BOUNCE->LAND->PATROL->DEAD
