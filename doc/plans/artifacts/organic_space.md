# Artifact: Organic Space

> CSG boolean(sphere, cylinder, box) + FastNoiseLite displacement -> organic cave interior with floating orbs

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** expert
**Tags:** geometry | **Themes:** geometry, non_euclidean

An algorithmic exploration of organic geometries and natural forms, creating flowing, non-Euclidean spaces that challenge traditional geometric assumptions

*an organic space is not designed from outside — it is what remains after noise has eaten away at geometry from within*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 6, 13] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** to step inside a living space where walls breathe, tunnels curve, and crystals grow from nothing — architecture without architects
- **Critical Parameter:** organic_strength — controls how much noise deforms the base geometry; at 0 it is a sphere, at 1 it is a cave
- **What Emerges:** CSG boolean subtraction of noisy spheres from the shell creates doorways and alcoves that feel intentional but are accidental
- **Triggers:** regenerate_space() creates a new random interior; interactive orbs float with tween-driven gentle oscillation
