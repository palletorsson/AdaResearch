# Artifact: Cloth Simulation

> F_spring = k * (|x2-x1| - rest) * dir. A grid of particles connected by springs. Cloth is not an object — it's a negotiation.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** physics | **Themes:** physics, soft_body

Soft body physics simulation using spring-mass systems

*Elasticity is memory. Every spring encodes where it wants to be. Cloth is a thousand memories negotiating with gravity.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [8, 12, 10] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** To drape, to ripple, to be grabbed. To show that softness emerges from many stiff connections.
- **Critical Parameter:** k (stiffness) — low k = jelly cloth, high k = rigid sheet. Wind strength shapes the drama.
- **What Emerges:** Draping from gravity + constraints. Rippling from wind + spring response. Tearing (future) from exceeding spring limits.
- **Triggers:** Wind slider → cloth billows, stiffness slider → character changes, VR grab → cloth deforms toward hand
