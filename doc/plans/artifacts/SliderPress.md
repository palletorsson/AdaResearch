# Artifact: SliderPress

> SliderJoint3D — 1 translational DOF. The opposite of a hinge. Linear motion, no spin.

## Context

**Category:** forces | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** physics, joints, slider-joint, piston, rail, motor, force, velocity, interactive, VR-controls | **Themes:** physics, joints

A piston and rail system demonstrating the slider joint — one translational degree of freedom. The piston oscillates along a Z-axis track with controllable motor speed and force via VR sliders. Real-time labels show position and velocity, making the relationship between motor parameters and motion directly readable. The slider joint is the opposite of a hinge: where hinges permit rotation and forbid translation, sliders permit translation and forbid rotation.

*Sliders translate without rotating. The opposite constraint to the hinge. Together they build machines.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [3.0, 2.0, 4.0] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** To slide. To show that constraining rotation while freeing translation builds pistons, drawers, rails.
- **Critical Parameter:** Motor speed and force — how fast and how hard the piston drives.
- **What Emerges:** Mechanical rhythm from oscillating motor. The feel of constrained linear motion.
- **Triggers:** Speed slider → piston oscillates faster/slower, force slider → piston pushes harder against resistance
