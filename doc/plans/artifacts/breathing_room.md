# Artifact: Breathing Room

> two SoftBody3D walls with sinusoidally oscillating pressure_coefficient — P(t) = (sin(t * breath_speed) + 1) * 0.5 * breath_amplitude — creating walls that inhale and exhale

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** physics | **Themes:** physics, soft_body

A corridor with soft body walls that rhythmically expand and contract.

*a room that breathes is no longer a container — it is a body, and you are inside it*

## Design

- **Scale:** [7, 2, 11] (world_scale)
- **Interaction:** to trap you inside a room that breathes — walls expand inward on the inhale and contract on the exhale, making the architecture itself feel alive
- **Critical Parameter:** breath_amplitude — low amplitude creates subtle pulsing you barely notice, high amplitude compresses the walkable space until the walls nearly touch
- **What Emerges:** the two walls breathing in sync create a bellows effect where standing between them during peak inflation feels like being squeezed by the room itself
- **Triggers:** the _process loop continuously modulates pressure_coefficient on both wall SoftBody3D instances; player collision with inflating walls creates push-back forces
