# Artifact: Mushrooms

## Context

**Category:** morphogenesis | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** procedural, mushrooms, noise, poisson-process, fairy-ring, bioluminescence, ecology, growth, spatial, morphogenesis | **Themes:** generation, procedural

A procedural mushroom meadow grown from noise. FastNoiseLite drives a spatial Poisson process — mushrooms spawn where noise exceeds a density threshold, creating natural clearings and clusters. Five species templates produce variety; a glowing variant adds bioluminescent accents. Fairy rings self-organize as perfect circles of identical species within the random field. The ground itself is procedurally deformed, and ambient lighting creates a forest-floor atmosphere. Walk through and feel how randomness filtered through ecological rules produces something that looks alive.

*A mushroom meadow is randomness that has found a niche — noise filtered through ecology.*
