# Artifact: Escher Staircase

## Context

**Category:** philosophy | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** escher, impossible, paradox, geometry, interactive | **Themes:** philosophy, qfep

Impossible staircase - locally coherent, globally impossible. Visual Gödel.

*locally coherent does not mean globally possible — this is Godel's incompleteness made architectural*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** climb "up" forever and arrive where you started — feel the paradox in your legs
- **Critical Parameter:** steps_per_side — more steps make the impossibility more convincing
- **What Emerges:** the visceral understanding that local consistency does not guarantee global consistency
- **Triggers:** climb_step() advances the highlight; completing a full loop fires paradox_completed
