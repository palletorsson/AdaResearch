# Artifact: Riemann Sphere

> C ∪ {∞} — the complex plane plus a point at infinity, wrapped onto a sphere via stereographic projection

## Context

**Category:** geometry | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** riemann, complex_analysis, stereographic, projection, sphere, alternative_geometry | **Themes:** geometry, non_euclidean

The Riemann sphere — compactified complex plane with stereographic projection lines, north pole at infinity, equator, and projection plane visible.

*infinity is not the absence of a boundary — it is a boundary that has been folded back into the space it bounds*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** see the infinite complex plane folded onto a finite sphere — the north pole IS infinity
- **Critical Parameter:** radius — the sphere that contains the entire complex plane plus its boundary
- **What Emerges:** the intuition that infinity is not "far away" — it is a point you can see and almost touch
- **Triggers:** static display; the glowing north pole and equator teach by geometry alone
