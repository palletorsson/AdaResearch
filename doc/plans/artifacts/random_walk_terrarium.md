# Artifact: Random Walk Terrarium

> E[|r|²] = n·d·σ² — mean squared displacement scales linearly with step count

## Context

**Category:** procedural | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** random_walk, terrarium, terrain, stochastic, emergence | **Themes:** commons, mixed, procedural

A contained ecosystem where random walkers create terrain — trails accumulate into landscapes, paths cross to form structures, and the terrarium slowly fills with the emergent geography of stochastic exploration.

*A random walk has no destination — yet it explores space more honestly than any planned path.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** peer into a glass box and watch colored particles wander without purpose or memory
- **Critical Parameter:** walk_mode — switches between WALK_2D (planar), WALK_3D (volumetric), LEVY_FLIGHT (heavy-tailed jumps)
- **What Emerges:** Levy flights produce dramatically different trail topology — rare giant leaps amid local clusters
- **Triggers:** mode switch buttons change step generator; each step calls _generate_step() with boundary reflection
