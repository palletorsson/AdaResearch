## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** to rotate hue offset and watch the gradient wheel spin — to see that a rainbow is just sin() applied to three channels
- **Critical Parameter:** hue_offset — shifts the entire color wheel, proving color is position on a circle not a line
- **What Emerges:** the rainbow panel reveals that spectral color is periodic — the same trigonometric oscillation that shaped gradients now paints light
- **Triggers:** saturation slider desaturates to grey; mode toggle switches HSB visualization between hue-value and hue-saturation planes
