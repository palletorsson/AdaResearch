# Artifact: Dot Product Projector

> A . B = |A||B|cos(theta). The dot product measures alignment. Positive = same direction, zero = perpendicular, negative = opposed.

## Context

**Category:** mathematics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** vectors, dot_product, projection, angle, cosine, interactive | **Themes:** vectors

Interactive dot product visualizer showing A · B = |A||B|cos(θ). Demonstrates projection and alignment measurement.

*The dot product is the question: how much do these two directions agree? The answer is a scalar — dimension collapses by design.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: vertex
- **Scale:** [2.0, 2.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** To let the learner rotate two arrows and watch a number change sign — making orthogonality a felt threshold, not a definition.
- **Critical Parameter:** The angle between vector_a and vector_b. At 90 degrees the dot product crosses zero — the phase transition of alignment.
- **What Emerges:** The perpendicular drop line from A's tip to the projection. The angle arc sweeping through the sign change at 90 degrees.
- **Triggers:** Handle drag → projection arrow slides along B, angle arc updates, result panel color-codes (green/yellow/red), preset buttons → aligned/ortho/opposed
