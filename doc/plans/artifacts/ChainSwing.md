**Category:** forces | **Complexity:** intermediate

A double-pendulum swing built from pin joints and rigid links. Two chain segments hang from an anchor beam, ending in a glowing seat that receives periodic sinusoidal force. The trail draws the seat's arc through space, revealing how constraints propagate force through a chain — each link amplifying or dampening the one above. Arrow keys let you push the swing manually, hunting for resonance where small inputs produce large arcs.

*A chain swing is a conversation between gravity and constraint. Each link votes on where the seat should go.*
