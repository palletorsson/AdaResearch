## Design

- **Interaction:** to watch 3D creatures evolve in front of you, generation by generation, toward a fitness goal no individual understands
- **Critical Parameter:** mutation_rate — too low and evolution stagnates, too high and good solutions dissolve; 0.3 is the edge of chaos
- **What Emerges:** creatures converge on similar body plans despite random initialization — convergent evolution from pure math
- **Triggers:** auto_evolve ticks generations on a timer; evolve_one_generation allows manual stepping; fitness_function selects the selection pressure
