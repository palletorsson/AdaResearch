# Artifact: Scissor Stalker

> L(extension) = Σ bar_length · f(θ_i) — Hoberman scissor linkage maps single parameter to reach

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** hazard | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

Spider-like enemy with Hoberman scissor linkage legs. Legs extend 3x their compact length for dramatic reach. Stalks with partially extended legs, pounces by rapidly extending, slams down to attack.

*A scissor linkage is amplification — small angular change becomes dramatic spatial reach.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** a crouching spider that stalks low, then explosively extends scissor legs to pounce and slam
- **Critical Parameter:** _leg_extension — 0 = compact (hiding), 1 = fully extended (3x reach for pounce)
- **What Emerges:** the walk animation arises from phase-offset sine waves on leg tilt — no IK, just linkage geometry
- **Triggers:** state machine (COMPACT→STALK→POUNCE→ATTACK→RETRACT) with leg extension ramping per state
