# Artifact: Boids Aquarium

> v_i = w_sep·separate + w_align·align + w_coh·cohere, O(n) via spatial hash grid — Reynolds (1987) in a 1m cube

## Context

**Category:** emergence | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** boids, flocking, emergence, swarm, reynolds, collective_behavior, self_organization | **Themes:** collective, emergence, swarm

A 1m glass cube containing a school of 30 boids demonstrating emergent flocking behavior. Three simple rules (separation, alignment, cohesion) create lifelike collective motion.

*the boundary of the tank is the only rule that is truly external — every other behavior is the boids talking to each other*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to peer through glass at thirty tiny creatures and realize the school has a shape, a mood, a personality — and you made it with three sliders
- **Critical Parameter:** separation_weight — below 0.5 boids collapse into a clump; above 3.0 they scatter into permanent isolation; the narrow band between is life
- **What Emerges:** rotating toroids, figure-eight loops, and sudden directional consensus events — the tank finds shapes the designer never chose
- **Triggers:** dragging SEP slider to max produces cold dispersal; dragging COH to max produces a pulsing sphere; ALIGN at max produces a synchronized arrow of motion
