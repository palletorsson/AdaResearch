# Artifact: Multimeter

> V(t) = dc_offset + ac_amplitude * sin(ac_frequency * t * TAU) + noise * randf()

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** sound, waves | **Themes:** oscillation, physics, waves

Digital multimeter with analog-style oscillating needle. Demonstrates AC voltage waveforms—the needle deflects following sin(t * frequency), showing how alternating current oscillates between positive and negative. Modes: AC voltage, DC voltage, resistance, frequency.

*Every AC measurement is reading the current value of an ongoing oscillation.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** Read voltage on a multimeter whose needle oscillates with the measured AC signal
- **Critical Parameter:** ac_frequency — determines the oscillation rate of the measured voltage
- **What Emerges:** the needle as wave visualizer — watching measurement oscillate reveals the signal nature
- **Triggers:** time drives the AC sine wave; noise_level adds measurement uncertainty
