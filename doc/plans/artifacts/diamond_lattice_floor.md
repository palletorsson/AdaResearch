# Artifact: Diamond Lattice Floor

> a floor that maps the diagonal logic of Roman reticulate masonry

## Context

**Category:** primitives | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 2d, pattern, mosaic, floor, pompeii, diamond, lattice | **Themes:** arrays, art, cultural_computation

Pompeii mosaic floor with diamond lattice pattern. Two sets of diagonal lines at 45 and 135 degrees cross to form a regular rhombus grid. Alternating dark/light diamonds create a rotated checkerboard effect.

*a rotated grid is still a grid — the math doesn't care about orientation*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** players see how a simple 45-degree rotation transforms a grid into jewelry
- **Critical Parameter:** diamonds_short — number of diamond cells on the short axis
- **What Emerges:** the insight that rotation is the simplest symmetry that changes everything
- **Triggers:** instantiation or apply_grid_config
