Interactive pyramid demo with grab handles to reshape the base and apex.

*a pyramid is just a parameterisation — move the apex and it stops being a pyramid*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: vertex
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner discovers that the pyramid shape is not fixed — every vertex is a degree of freedom
- **Critical Parameter:** apex handle — moving it changes the character from flat to tall, from pyramid to spike
- **What Emerges:** the intuition that named shapes are just default parameterisations of a more general vertex space
- **Triggers:** dragging any of the 5 grab sphere handles — mesh rebuilds instantly from new vertex positions
