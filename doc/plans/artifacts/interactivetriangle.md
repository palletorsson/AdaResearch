Standing triangle panel split into pink and black faces with grab spheres and multiple shape resets.

*a triangle is the same shape regardless of color — properties are separable from appearance*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 2.0, 1.0] (environment)
- **Interaction:** learner grabs a triangle and feels it as a physical object, not a mathematical abstraction
- **Critical Parameter:** random shader color assigned at startup — makes each instance visually distinct
- **What Emerges:** the distinction between the geometric shape and its visual representation (color is not intrinsic)
- **Triggers:** dragging any of the 3 vertex grab spheres — face deforms following hand movement
