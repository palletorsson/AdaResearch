# Artifact: Superposition Display

> |ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩; α oscillates as sin(t·speed), β = 1 - α; both states simultaneously present

## Context

**Category:** quantumalgorithms | **Complexity:** expert
**Tags:** quantum, superposition, qubit, amplitude, interactive, slider | **Themes:** algorithms, quantum

Interactive visualization of quantum superposition |psi> = alpha|0> + beta|1> with VR slider to control the amplitude ratio between basis states.

*superposition is not uncertainty about which state the system is in — it is the system genuinely being in both*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** see two basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩ breathe in and out of opacity while the superposition sphere pulses between them
- **Critical Parameter:** _speed — controls the oscillation frequency between basis states
- **What Emerges:** the visual proof that superposition is not flickering between states — it is occupying both simultaneously
- **Triggers:** VR slider adjusts oscillation speed; opacity of |0⟩ and |1⟩ spheres anti-correlate continuously
