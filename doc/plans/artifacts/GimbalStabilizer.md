# Artifact: GimbalStabilizer

> two orthogonal hinges — yaw (free) + pitch (±45°). Composed single-axis joints create multi-axis aiming.

## Context

**Category:** forces | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** physics, joints, hinge-joint, gimbal, yaw, pitch, motor, stabilizer, multi-axis, robotics | **Themes:** physics, simulation

A dual-hinge gimbal system with independent yaw and pitch control. Two motor-driven hinges are stacked orthogonally: one rotates freely around the vertical axis (yaw), the other is limited to plus-or-minus 45 degrees on the horizontal axis (pitch). Together they aim a payload in 3D space. The idle mode sweeps smoothly through a Lissajous-like pattern; arrow keys override with direct motor control. This demonstrates how composing multiple single-axis joints creates multi-axis articulation.

*A gimbal is two questions asked orthogonally: how far left, and how far up. The answer is a direction.*
