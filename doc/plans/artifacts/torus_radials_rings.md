# Artifact: Torus Radials Rings

> TorusMesh(rings, ring_segments, inner_radius, outer_radius) — a circle revolved around an axis

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** geometry | **Themes:** core, primitives

Configurable torus with rings and ring segments. Usage: torus_radials_rings:90:1:#inner:0.2#outer:0.6 or #config:16:8 for 16 rings and 8 ring segments.

*the torus is topologically distinct from the sphere — its genus is 1, meaning it has exactly one hole*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner understands the torus as a product of two circles — one for the path, one for the tube
- **Critical Parameter:** ring_segments — tube cross-section resolution; at 4 it looks square, at 32 it looks round
- **What Emerges:** that the torus has a topology distinct from a sphere — it has a hole and cannot be flattened
- **Triggers:** grid config syntax (torus_radials_rings#config:16:8) or apply_grid_config() call at runtime
