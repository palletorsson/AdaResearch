# Artifact: Windmill Floor

> a floor whose geometry captures rotational energy frozen in stone

## Context

**Category:** primitives | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 2d, pattern, mosaic, floor, roman, pinwheel, windmill, rotational_symmetry | **Themes:** arrays, art, cultural_computation

Pompeii mosaic floor with windmill/pinwheel pattern. Each unit has a small terracotta center square surrounded by 4 L-shaped blades — two dark, two light opposite — creating a spinning windmill effect. Adjacent units rotate 90 degrees for continuous flow.

*four L-shapes around a center square is one of the oldest tiling motifs*

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 0] (compact)
