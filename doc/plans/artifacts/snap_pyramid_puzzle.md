# Artifact: Snap Pyramid Puzzle

> square pyramid = 5 vertices, 8 edges, 5 faces (1 square + 4 triangles) — non-Platonic mixed solid

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner

Interactive puzzle where connecting 5 snap points forms a square pyramid that spawns a walkable prism.

*a pyramid has a free parameter — apex height — that tetrahedra and octahedra do not*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner places 5 points and observes how a square base plus apex forces triangular sides
- **Critical Parameter:** 5 snap target positions — 4 corners of a square base plus 1 apex above center
- **What Emerges:** that the apex position determines whether the pyramid is regular or skewed — height is a free variable
- **Triggers:** snapping all 5 snap points to their targets → puzzle complete signal fires
