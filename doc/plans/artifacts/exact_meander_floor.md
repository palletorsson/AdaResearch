# Artifact: Exact Meander Floor

> a faithful pixel reproduction of a specific Pompeii meander floor

## Context

**Category:** primitives | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 2d, pattern, mosaic, floor, pompeii, meander, greek_key | **Themes:** arrays, art, cultural_computation

Faithful pixel reproduction of a Pompeii meander floor from photograph. 84x66 grid with continuous Greek key meander border (3-cell dark lines on light band, 12-cell border depth), L-bend corners, and dark field with scattered white tesserae.

*the meander is a space-filling curve disguised as decoration*
