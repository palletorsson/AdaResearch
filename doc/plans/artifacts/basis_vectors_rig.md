# Artifact: Basis Vectors Rig

> P = x*i + y*j + z*k. Any point is a weighted sum of basis directions.

## Context

**Category:** mathematics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** vectors, basis, coordinate_system, linear_combination, interactive | **Themes:** vectors, linear_algebra

Interactive basis vectors (î, ĵ, k̂) showing how any 3D point is a linear combination: P = xî + yĵ + zk̂.

*Coordinates do not belong to the point. They belong to the relationship between the point and the frame.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** To decompose space into understandable parts — grab a point and see its coordinates unfold along three axes.
- **Critical Parameter:** target_point — the Vector3 being decomposed. Moving it rewrites the linear combination in real time.
- **What Emerges:** The realization that coordinates are not absolute — they depend on which basis you choose. Rotating the basis changes the numbers but not the point.
- **Triggers:** VR handle drag → coordinates update, preset buttons → snap to pure-axis or combined points, rotation presets → tilt/spin the basis itself
