# Artifact: CoordinateSystem3M

> X=red, Y=green, Z=blue axes at 3m length — the standard right-handed coordinate frame made visible

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** coordinate_system, axes, orientation, right_hand_rule, 3d, interactive, vectors | **Themes:** linear_algebra, math, vectors

Stand inside the coordinate system. Three meter-long axes — X red, Y green, Z blue — establish the right-handed frame that every 3D operation assumes. A gyroscope gadget shows your orientation relative to the axes. The arbitrary nature of axis conventions becomes visible: right-handed vs left-handed, Y-up vs Z-up are choices, not laws.

*a coordinate system is a choice of three mutually perpendicular directions with an agreed origin*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: unlit, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner stands inside the coordinate system and feels orientation as embodied direction
- **Critical Parameter:** axis_length = 3.0 — at half-scale (scale 0.5) these appear as 1.5m axes in world space
- **What Emerges:** the arbitrary nature of axis conventions — right-handed vs left-handed, Y-up vs Z-up are choices
- **Triggers:** runtime adds info panel and gyroscope gadget showing orientation relative to viewer
