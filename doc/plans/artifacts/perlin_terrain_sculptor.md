# Artifact: Perlin Terrain Sculptor

> voxel_on[pos] = noise_3d(pos × frequency) > threshold — a 24³ grid that thresholds Perlin noise, with VR sliders broadcasting changes to linked voxelnoise receivers

## Context

**Category:** procedural | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** perlin_noise, terrain, sculpting, procedural, landscape | **Themes:** commons, mixed, procedural

Sculpt terrain using Perlin noise parameters — adjust octaves, persistence, lacunarity, and scale to shape landscapes from smooth rolling hills to jagged mountain ranges. Real-time terrain generation making noise mathematics tangible.

*noise-based sculpting inverts the usual creative act — instead of adding form, you reveal it by moving the threshold through a pre-existing mathematical landscape*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: static
- **Scale:** [2.0, 2.0, 2.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to sculpt with mathematics — to move a VR slider and feel the threshold of existence rise through a landscape of cubes, watching matter emerge and dissolve at your hand
- **Critical Parameter:** threshold — the cut-plane through the noise field; at -1.0 everything is solid, at 1.0 everything is void, and interesting terrain lives between -0.3 and 0.3
- **What Emerges:** the learner discovers that sculpting with noise parameters is fundamentally different from sculpting with geometry — you are not placing matter, you are choosing which noise values count as matter
- **Triggers:** noise_scale changes restructure what frequencies create terrain features; octaves add detail without changing the overall scale; dragging sliders broadcasts payload to voxelnoise peers via signal
