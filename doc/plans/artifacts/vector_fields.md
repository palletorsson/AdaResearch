# Artifact: Vector Fields

> F(p) = sum(sources). Each point samples the superposed field of all Area3D gravity sources. Arrows point where particles would go.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** vector_field, streamlines, curl, divergence, gradient, flow, visualization, 3d, advection, topology | **Themes:** fields, vectors, flow, visualization

3D vector field visualization with arrow glyphs showing direction and magnitude at grid points. Particles advected through the field trace streamlines, revealing the flow structure — sources, sinks, saddle points, and vortices. Includes curl, divergence, and gradient field types with real-time parameter control.

*A field is not a picture of arrows. It is a promise: if you put a particle here, this is what will happen.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** To place an attractor and a repulsor and watch the field tear itself between them — arrows bending, particles caught in the tug of war.
- **Critical Parameter:** The ratio of attractor gravity (+8) to repulsor gravity (-6). It determines whether particles orbit, escape, or get trapped.
- **What Emerges:** Saddle points between attractor and repulsor where the field cancels. Particle streams forming visible flow lanes. Color gradient mapping field magnitude.
- **Triggers:** Automatic — 125 arrows orient by sampling field, 30 particles advected by Area3D gravity overrides, escaped particles respawn randomly
