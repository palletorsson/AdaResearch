## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 2.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** learner places 6 floating points and discovers the shape that emerges from this particular arrangement
- **Critical Parameter:** 6 snap target positions — form a regular octahedron at ±1 on each axis
- **What Emerges:** the dual relationship to the cube — realizing these 6 positions are the face-centers of a cube
- **Triggers:** snapping all 6 snap points to their targets → puzzle complete signal fires
