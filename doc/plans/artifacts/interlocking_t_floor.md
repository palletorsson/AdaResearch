# Artifact: Interlocking T Floor

Procedural Pompeii mosaic floor with interlocking T-shaped tetromino pattern. T-pieces (3 wide x 2 tall) tile with alternating rotations and colors, dark Ts pointing down interlock with light Ts pointing up, creating a satisfying puzzle-like woven surface.

*a T-shape and its 180-degree twin tile the plane — no gaps, no overlaps*
