# Artifact: Bias From Inside

> an embedding space experienced from the inside of its compression

## Context

**Category:** critical | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** algorithmic_bias, embedding, perspective, critical_algorithms, compression, word_vectors | **Themes:** algorithms, education, misc

Visualizes a word embedding space from the perspective of a marginalized term. Instead of the usual god's-eye view, this artifact places you INSIDE the compressed region. A slider shifts between default perspective (everything looks evenly distributed) and compressed perspective (reveals how uneven the space really is).

*the embedding space is not a fact — it is a map drawn by the powerful*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [2.0, 2.0, 2.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** players feel what it means to be algorithmically crowded into a corner
- **Critical Parameter:** perspective_blend — 0.0 = default view, 1.0 = compressed view
- **What Emerges:** the realization that "neutral" embeddings are anything but neutral
- **Triggers:** instantiation, perspective slider interaction
