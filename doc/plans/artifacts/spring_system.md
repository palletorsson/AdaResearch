# Artifact: Spring System

> F = -k(|x|-L)*x-hat. A network of masses connected by Hooke's law springs. Each spring pulls its endpoints toward rest length.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** spring, system, coupled, resonance, normal_modes, beats, energy_transfer, spectral | **Themes:** springs, coupled_oscillators, resonance, normal_modes

System-level spring behavior exploring coupled oscillators, resonance, and energy transfer between connected springs. When natural frequencies align, energy sloshes back and forth between modes in beautiful beat patterns. Demonstrates normal modes and the spectral decomposition of complex vibrations into simple harmonic components.

*A spring network is a medium. Perturbation at one point travels through connections. Structure is communication.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [8, 3, 9] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** To see a web of springs breathe — wind perturbing it, colors shifting with tension, the whole system finding equilibrium and losing it again.
- **Critical Parameter:** spring_stiffness (k=50) and damping (0.95). Higher stiffness = faster oscillation, tighter network. Lower damping = longer ringing. Together they set the network's texture.
- **What Emerges:** Tension waves propagating through the network. Color shifting from calm blue to hot pink as springs stretch. Fixed anchor points creating standing wave patterns.
- **Triggers:** Auto-interaction pulses random forces every 2 seconds, wind oscillates continuously, Space → manual random impulse, auto-rotate reveals 3D structure
