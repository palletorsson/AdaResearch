# Artifact: Spherical Harmonics

> Y_l^m(theta, phi) — position on sphere via spherical coordinates

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** 3d, geometry, sound, waves | **Themes:** music, oscillation, physics, waves

Waves on a sphere—the mathematical functions that describe electron orbitals, planetary magnetic fields, and 3D audio spatialization. These are the 3D cousins of sine waves, oscillating across spherical surfaces.

*Every function on a sphere decomposes into spherical harmonics, the way every signal decomposes into sines.*
