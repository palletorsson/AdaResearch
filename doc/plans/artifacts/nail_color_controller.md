# Artifact: nail_color_controller

> f(RGB) -> material.albedo_color on VR hand mesh surfaces — ValueMapper3D maps 3D input to nail/skin color

## Context

**Category:** unknown | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** 3d, vr | **Themes:** commons, mixed

Uses a 3D value mapper to live-adjust VR hand nail materials with a preview cube.

*choosing your nail color is the first act of digital self-authorship — the body becomes canvas before the world does*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1.0, 2.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to paint your own hands in VR, watching color flow onto nails or skin as you move sliders through RGB space
- **Critical Parameter:** target_surface — 0 selects nails (metallic, emissive), 1 selects skin (matte, no glow), completely different aesthetics
- **What Emerges:** the preview cube becomes an accidental color swatch that players use to compare colors side by side
- **Triggers:** ValueMapper3D.values_changed fires on any slider movement; GameManager persists color across scene transitions
