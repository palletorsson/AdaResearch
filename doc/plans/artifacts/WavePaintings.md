# Artifact: Wave Paintings

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** advanced
**Tags:** 2d, animation, chaos, color, interactive, visualization, vr, wave | **Themes:** art, oscillation, physics, waves

Three pendulum-painting stations—grab the bob to swing it, watch oscillation become visible trace. Each pendulum paints on a wet canvas below, translating harmonic motion into color trails. The physics of swinging becomes the brush; chaos becomes art.

*Chaos is not randomness — it is determinism so sensitive that prediction becomes impossible.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: transform
- **Interaction:** Swing pendulums by hand and watch chaotic trajectories paint on wet canvases below
- **Critical Parameter:** num_pendulums and spacing — determines how many simultaneous paintings emerge
- **What Emerges:** unique unrepeatable paintings from deterministic but chaotic double-pendulum dynamics
- **Triggers:** grabbing the bob sets initial conditions; release begins chaotic evolution
