# Artifact: Herringbone Floor

> a floor encoding the oldest structural bond pattern in Western masonry

## Context

**Category:** primitives | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** 2d, pattern, mosaic, floor, pompeii, herringbone, chevron | **Themes:** arrays, art, cultural_computation

Procedural Pompeii mosaic floor with herringbone (opus spicatum) pattern. Rectangular 2:1 bricks laid at 45-degree angles in alternating zigzag rows. Adjacent bricks point in opposite directions creating the classic V/chevron pattern found in Roman and medieval floors.

*the same brick rotated 90 degrees, row after row, becomes a chevron field*
