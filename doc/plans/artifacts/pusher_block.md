# Artifact: Pusher Block

> translation(block) -> push(player) -> pit(death) — a cube that slides and shoves you into the void

## Context

**Category:** hazards | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** hazard, transformation, translation, push, pit | **Themes:** gameplay, hazards

A red cube that translates back and forth along one axis, physically pushing the player into pits. Uses AnimatableBody3D for proper physics collision. The translation transformation made dangerous.

*translation is the simplest transformation and the most dangerous — everything that moves can push.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Interaction:** to make translation felt in the body — not a number but a force that moves you
- **Critical Parameter:** push_speed / push_distance — too slow is boring, too fast is unfair, the sweet spot teaches timing
- **What Emerges:** the player learns translation by being translated — the block does to you what you do to objects
- **Triggers:** timer-driven translation cycle; player body contact applies physics impulse
