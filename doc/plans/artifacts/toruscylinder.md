# Artifact: Torus Cylinder

> torus.rotation_y += dt*speed; cylinder.position.y = sin(t*freq)*amplitude — rotation vs oscillation

## Context

**Category:** transforms | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** transformation, rotation, oscillation, sine_wave, trail, comparison, animation, transforms, visualization | **Themes:** geometry, math, transforms

A torus rotates while a cylinder oscillates up and down, leaving a trail. Presented together, the two motions contrast continuous rotation against periodic translation without changing the scene's basic geometry. The artifact makes it easy to compare two transformation families in one glance. It teaches motion by juxtaposition.

*rotation and translation are both transformations — one preserves distance from a fixed point, the other does not*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: vertex_color, animation: vertex
- **Scale:** [5, 3, 4] (xlarge)
- **Interaction:** learner sees two fundamental animated transformations side by side: continuous rotation and periodic translation
- **Critical Parameter:** cylinder_osc_speed — the frequency of the sine wave; doubles the frequency, doubles the loops
- **What Emerges:** the sine wave as the path of a constrained oscillator — the trail makes the mathematical curve physical
- **Triggers:** _process(delta) — runs every frame; the ImmediateMesh trail grows continuously tracing the sine path
