10x10 grid of height-varying tiles driven by Perlin noise. High tiles are danger zones, low tiles are safe. Noise scrolls continuously — the landscape of randomness shifts under your feet.

*Perlin noise is not random -- it is structured variation, and structure makes the danger feel organic.*
