# Artifact: Momentum Collision

> p = mv. Total momentum conserved. Kinetic energy conserved (elastic) or lost (inelastic).

## Context

**Category:** physics

Physical momentum and collision demonstration. Objects collide with realistic mass transfer — shows conservation of momentum and the invisible forces governing motion.

*What one body loses, the other gains. Momentum is the bookkeeping of physics.*

## Design

- **Interaction:** To collide. To show that momentum always balances but energy sometimes doesn't.
- **Critical Parameter:** Coefficient of restitution — elastic (e=1, energy conserved) vs inelastic (e=0, bodies stick).
- **What Emerges:** Conservation laws from Newton's third law. The difference between momentum and energy conservation.
- **Triggers:** Launch → ball A hits ball B, elastic mode → both move, inelastic mode → they stick together, readout updates
