# Artifact: Chladni Plate

> nodal_lines where cos(m*pi*x/L)*cos(n*pi*y/L) = 0, particles migrate to nodes

## Context

**Category:** waves | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** chladni, vibration, nodes, standing waves, resonance, sand, patterns | **Themes:** music, oscillation, physics, waves

Vibrating metal plate with sand particles that migrate to nodal lines, forming standing wave patterns. Auto-cycles through modes showing different resonant geometries. Ernst Chladni's classic 18th-century experiment demonstrating that sound creates visible form.

*Vibration sorts matter into the geometry of its own nodal lines.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Scale:** [1.0, 1.0, 1.0] (compact)
- **Interaction:** Watch sand particles self-organize into Chladni figures as vibration modes change
- **Critical Parameter:** mode_pairs (m,n) — each pair defines a unique nodal pattern
- **What Emerges:** geometric order from vibration — pattern appears without any particle knowing the whole
- **Triggers:** mode cycling timer switches between vibration modes; particles drift to new nodal lines
