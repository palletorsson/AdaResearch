## Design

- **Interaction:** To be walked through — fog that has opinions about where it clumps, thinning and thickening by local rules
- **Critical Parameter:** smooth_factor and birth/survival thresholds (B5/S4) — control whether fog dissipates or aggregates
- **What Emerges:** Cloud-like formations from binary CA rules — no fluid dynamics, yet the fog moves like weather
- **Triggers:** High survival threshold → fog evaporates; low → dense permanent clouds; grid resolution → fine mist vs chunky blocks
