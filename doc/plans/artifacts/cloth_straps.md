Hanging soft body cloth straps that the player can walk through.

*a curtain is not a surface — it is an array of constraints negotiating gravity, and parting it reveals that softness is a collective property*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: shader, animation: static
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 1] (compact)
- **Interaction:** to create a curtain you walk through with your body — each strap drapes, parts, and swings back, making soft body physics tactile and intimate
- **Critical Parameter:** strap_count — more straps create a denser curtain with more inter-strap collision, fewer straps let each one swing freely
- **What Emerges:** overlapping straps tangle and bunch when disturbed simultaneously, creating knot-like configurations never coded into the system
- **Triggers:** player collision (layer 20) displaces individual straps; throwing a rigid ball (ui_accept) demonstrates mass-vs-compliance interaction
