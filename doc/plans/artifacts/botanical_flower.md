# Artifact: Botanical Flower

> Grid wrapper instantiating BotanicalFlower — petal count, length, curve, color, symmetry as procedural parameters

## Context

**Category:** proceduralgeneration | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** flower, procedural, botanical, morphology, growth | **Themes:** generation, procedural

Standalone procedural flower — configurable petal count, length, width, curve, stem height, color hue, leaf count, and symmetry. A lightweight wrapper around the BotanicalFlower generator for map placement without VR controls.

*A flower is not its petals — it is the parameters that decided how many petals to grow.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** To let players tune flower morphology through grid config, watching small parameter changes produce dramatic form shifts
- **Critical Parameter:** petal_count — the integer that most visibly transforms the flower from tulip to daisy to chrysanthemum
- **What Emerges:** Infinite flower variety from a handful of parameters forwarded to a single procedural generator
- **Triggers:** Low petal count produces simple bloom; high count produces dense rosette; bilateral symmetry produces orchid-like forms
