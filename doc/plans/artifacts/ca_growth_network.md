Network structure that grows using cellular automata rules, forming organic branching patterns through local neighbor interactions.

*Growth toward a goal, with noise, is enough to produce every vascular pattern in nature.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: particle_lifecycle
- **Interaction:** To reach — tendrils seeking invisible attractors, branching when the signal is ambiguous
- **Critical Parameter:** attractor_strength — how powerfully distant goals bend the growth direction
- **What Emerges:** Root-like, vascular networks from simple force-toward-goal logic — no branching pattern was specified
- **Triggers:** Many attractors → dense bush; few → sparse reaching arms; high branch_probability → fractal explosion
