Cellular Automata on a sphere surface.

*The same rules on a sphere produce forms no flat grid ever could — topology is a hidden variable.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** To deform a sphere three different ways — watch topology warp under CA birth/death, random drift, and gravitational attraction
- **Critical Parameter:** evolution_mode — switches between three fundamentally different deformation algorithms on the same mesh
- **What Emerges:** Organic, tumor-like growths on a sphere surface from vertex-level CA rules that know nothing about shape
- **Triggers:** Mode switch → sphere resets and begins new deformation; iteration limit → evolution halts, form is frozen
