# Artifact: Combined Forces Demo

> F_net = F1 + F2. Superposition. Forces add as vectors. The ball feels only the sum.

## Context

**Category:** vectors | **Complexity:** beginner
**Tags:** vector, addition, force, superposition, resultant, physics | **Themes:** linear_algebra, math, vectors

Vector addition as force combination — multiple forces acting on a body, visualized as arrows that add head-to-tail. The resultant force emerges from superposition.

*Nature does not apply forces one at a time. Every force acts simultaneously. The body knows only the sum.*

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: static
- **Interaction:** To let the learner drag two force arrows and watch the ball respond to their sum — not to either alone. Superposition as felt experience.
- **Critical Parameter:** The angle between F1 and F2. Parallel forces compound; perpendicular forces create diagonal motion; opposing forces cancel.
- **What Emerges:** Cancellation when forces oppose. Diagonal trajectories from perpendicular forces. The net force arrow always predicting the ball's next move.
- **Triggers:** Drag red F1 or orange F2 → yellow net force updates, ball accelerates along net force, R → reset, Space → freeze
