# Artifact: Arbitrary Angular Motion

> a += F/m, v += a, p += v, omega += random, theta += omega. Linear attraction plus independent angular motion. Two dynamics, one body.

## Context

**Category:** physics | **Complexity:** intermediate
**Tags:** angular_motion, rotation, torque, attractor, oscillation, forces, nature_of_code, vr | **Themes:** physics, simulation

Eight boxes orbit an attractor while spinning at their own rate — rotation and translation coexisting as independent freedoms. Linear attraction pulls them inward while random angular impulses accumulate, making each box spin differently. Movers wrap at boundaries creating toroidal space. Rotation does not need a force — it needs a torque, or random impulses.

## Design

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [2, 1, 1] (standard)
- **Interaction:** To show that rotation and translation are independent — boxes orbit an attractor while spinning at their own rate, two freedoms coexisting.
- **Critical Parameter:** angular_damping (0.9-0.999) — controls how quickly spin decays. Low damping = chaotic spinning. High damping = gradual alignment. The knob between chaos and order.
- **What Emerges:** Movers wrapping around boundaries and re-entering from the opposite side (toroidal space). Spin rates diverging as random angular impulses accumulate.
- **Triggers:** Automatic — 8 movers orbit attractor, each accumulating random angular velocity. VR sliders → attractor strength and angular damping.
