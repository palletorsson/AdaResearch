Drag opposes motion and scales with speed squared. Heavier objects fall faster through the same fluid. F_drag = -C * |v|^2 * v-hat. The faster you move, the harder the world pushes back.

*Drag is the medium's memory of your velocity. The faster you move, the harder the world pushes back.*

- **Visual:** material: standard, animation: physics
- **Scale:** [1, 1, 2] (standard)
- **Interaction:** To drop five balls of different masses into a blue fluid and watch the heavy ones punch through while the light ones float — mass as privilege in a resistive medium.
- **Critical Parameter:** drag_coefficient — higher C means thicker fluid. It determines terminal velocity and how quickly the fluid swallows kinetic energy.
- **What Emerges:** Terminal velocity — each mover reaches a speed where gravity and drag balance. Heavier movers reach higher terminal velocity. The cyan drag arrows grow with speed.
