Endless Museum · source review · 13 October 2026

One world.
A longer memory.

The earlier biome already knows how to accumulate forms, preserve a seed and carry objects into a growing landscape. The arena can build on that work.

Six playable stagesFull reportEditable plan · JSONSource auditClaude’s route review
25 sequences mapped. Six arena stages built. Later cards distinguish agreed directions from proposals. These are implementation scaffolds, not new screenshots or playable stages. No museum, tutorial or final.md logic changed in this review.
VocabularyBiomeGrammar follows the actual spine and accumulates what a world may be made of, do and know.
IdentityThe inheritance study retains the same panels, bodies and record as their transforms and habitat change.
Physical groundWalkgrids already build sine, random and noise meshes with collision. The task is to connect them to the existing play shell.

What to do first

  1. Make the five-stage route dependable. B20 fixes the turned Tiling slab obstruction and tests all 14 controls; see the result. B19 now exposes all nine Arrays places and builds row/column volleys: see the implementation. Motif feedback remains a separate refinement.
  2. Join the records, not the entire generators. Use current sequence keys and cumulative capabilities. Add stable surface addresses, a shared sampler and an impact receiver. Keep one floor builder active per stage.
  3. Continue from Change toward Noise. Change now has field-guided position updates and one announced turn. Next: mass and impulse → a bounded search for form → walkable sine → seeded rough ground → correlated terrain.

One important correction: Change’s current hall prescribes motion through a sampled direction field. A push that accumulates into velocity belongs in Forces. The difference should be visible in play.

Known gaps in the old scaffold

Older numeric unlock tables differ from the current spine; Tiling and Form-finding have no soft_stages entries; Shaders has no biome-vocabulary entry. A declared vocabulary word does not prove a corresponding world behaviour exists.

Some legacy layers are visual stand-ins: orbiting “swarm” shapes, a pulsing “learning” halo, static QFEP indicators and a tinted “bias” dome. The plan points to real algorithm candidates or marks the missing implementation.

Exact floor edits currently reset when changing stage. Carrying that personal state into the next biome is proposed here, separate from the already inherited abilities. The mature habitat’s attractive objects also need verified colliders before they count as cover.

The route, one addition at a time

Every card keeps earlier capacities available. Each stage foregrounds one operation; it does not run every accumulated system at full density.
