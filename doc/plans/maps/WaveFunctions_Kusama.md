# Map: Kusama's Polka Dots: Self-Obliteration

## Overview

**Dimensions:** 13 x 14 (max height: 5)
**Difficulty:** intermediate | **Time:** 5-7 minutes

Inspired by Yayoi Kusama's aesthetic of accumulation and obsession. Experience high-frequency sine wave grids, step functions creating hard polka dot edges, and UV scrolling simulating hallucinations where patterns move from canvas to wall to body to universe. Lose the burden of uniqueness—camouflaged by the pattern of the world.

## Learning Objectives

- Infinite field: high-frequency sine grids (sin(x * density) * sin(y * density))
- Step functions: thresholds for hard-edged polka dots
- UV scrolling: simulating hallucination and pattern spread
- Camouflage: losing individual contour in the pattern
- Critical: Is this madness or liberation? The pattern encompasses all, cares for none
