## Overview

**Dimensions:** 8 x 16 (max height: 2)
**Difficulty:** beginner | **Time:** 3-4 minutes

Transform unifies position, rotation, and scale into a single operational framework. By composing multiple transformations, the map reveals how complex spatial behavior emerges from simple, repeatable operations. The player encounters transformation not as a visual effect but as a layered instruction set acting on objects and bodies alike. This scene marks the transition from geometric primitives to procedural thinking.
