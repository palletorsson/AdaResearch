# Map: Harmonic Builder: The Architecture of Timbre

## Overview

**Dimensions:** 12 x 12 (max height: 4)
**Difficulty:** intermediate | **Time:** 5-10 minutes

Any complex repeating wave can be built by summing simple sine waves at integer multiples of a fundamental frequency. Construct square, sawtooth, and triangle waves by manually adjusting harmonic amplitudes. Act as a Fourier Synthesizer and discover that complexity is reducible—the strangest waveform is just a choir of sines singing in perfect ratios.

## Learning Objectives

- Harmonic series: fundamental + overtones at integer multiples (f, 2f, 3f...)
- Additive synthesis: building complex waves from simple sines
- Wave recipes: square (odd harmonics), sawtooth (all harmonics), triangle (odd 1/n²)
- Amplitude decay: 1/n for sawtooth, 1/n² for triangle
- Critical: Complexity is reducible—chaos is often undecomposed order
