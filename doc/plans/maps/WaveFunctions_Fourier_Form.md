# Map: Fourier Transform Shape: Drawing with Circles

## Overview

**Dimensions:** 13 x 14 (max height: 3)
**Difficulty:** advanced | **Time:** 5-7 minutes

Any 2D drawing can be recreated by a series of rotating circles (epicycles). Watch as complex shapes emerge from layered rotations. Learn how DFT encodes shapes into frequency spectra, and discover that your handwriting is just a list of phasors—complexity requiring no complex parts, only accumulated cycles.

## Learning Objectives

- Epicycles: nested rotating circles creating complex curves
- Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT): encoding shapes as frequency spectra
- Compression via low-pass filtering (discarding small circles)
- Phasor representation: radius, frequency, phase
- Critical: Is handwriting personal, or just a frequency spectrum?
