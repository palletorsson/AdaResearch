## Learning Objectives

- Fourier synthesis: any signal = sum of sines
- Harmonics: integer multiples of fundamental
- Natural frequency: everything has one
- Resonance: when frequencies match
- Biological oscillation: heartbeats, circadian rhythms, DNA rotation
