# Edges of Algorithm — Visual Seeds

> Moodboards per edge, drawn from a wider corpus than the four traditions in
> `arttoeat/qfep/`. The point is not "look like" — it is *cousin to*. The same
> gesture, re-encountered in a different register, so the substrate has many
> proofs of how an idea can become an image without losing precision.

For each edge (A–M):
- **Cousins** — 4–7 reference works, each with a sentence on what makes it cousin.
- **Vocabulary slice** — the catalogue rows most likely to figure in a map for this edge.
- **Mood signature** — the visual register the substrate is aiming for.

Companion docs:
- `doc/EDGES_OF_ALGORITHM.md` — the thirteen edges
- `doc/ALGORITHM_CATALOGUE.md` — the eighty algorithms in thirteen families
- `arttoeat/qfep/` — the project's seed image folder (Haeckel, Codex, floor-plan, fabric)

## A. Formal impossibility

> The algorithm ends because it provably cannot continue.

### Cousins

- **Piranesi**, *Carceri d'Invenzione* (1761). Imaginary prisons whose perspective never resolves; ropes hanging from impossible vaults; stairs that stop at walls. The plate commits to its own architecture so totally that the impossibility becomes inhabitable. *Cousin because it renders the limit by trying to exceed it.*
- **Ramon Llull**, *Ars Magna* (1305). Rotating combinatorial wheels for generating *all true theology*. He thought it would produce truth; it produced a beautiful machine. *Cousin because the diagram is the formal system reaching for, and missing, completeness.*
- **M.C. Escher**, *Drawing Hands* (1948), *Print Gallery* (1956). Self-referential closure rendered as if it could exist. *Cousin because the loop is the proof that no continuation is available.*
- **Voynich Manuscript** (15th c.). The book that has not yielded to any decoding for six hundred years. *Cousin because it stands as evidence that consistency is not sufficient for legibility.*
- **Stéphane Mallarmé**, *Un coup de dés jamais n'abolira le hasard* (1897). Words spread across the page as silent constellations. *Cousin because the typographical hole is the formal silence.*

### Vocabulary slice
`frame_in_frame_layout` (recursive nesting, no exit) · `divide_and_conquer_space` at irreducible depth · `pathfinding_bfs` terminating at dead-ends · `boolean_cuts` carving logical contradiction · `glyph` subdivision policy that *almost* parses

### Mood signature
Black ground, line-work, shadow that exceeds figure. The Carceri-cousin: depth without resolution. Light from inside the structure rather than outside. The player walks in expecting a teleporter and finds the room continues.

## B. Tractability cliff

> The algorithm ends because the runtime explodes.

### Cousins

- **Diderot**, *Encyclopédie* plates (1751–72). Workshops with all the tools laid out, each numbered, each cross-referenced — the *combinatorics* of a craft made visible at industrial scale. *Cousin because each plate is a finite enumeration; thirty plates suggest the size of the unenumerated remainder.*
- **Étienne-Jules Marey**, chronophotographs (1880s–90s). Multiple-exposure plates compositing every frame of a bird's wing-stroke into one image. *Cousin because the cost of explicit enumeration is the plate's density.*
- **Charles Babbage**, Analytical Engine notes and plans. The first imagined computing machine, drawn before it could be built, scaled past what brass would allow. *Cousin because the machine's possibility is bounded by what fits in the room.*
- **A 5-city, 8-city, 12-city travelling-salesperson set on three plates side-by-side.** A bespoke moodboard. *Cousin because the visible scaling is the argument.*
- **Hokusai**, *36 Views of Mount Fuji* (1830–32). The same mountain, thirty-six framings; the series exists because no single image is sufficient. *Cousin because completeness requires multiplicity, and multiplicity has a count.*

### Vocabulary slice
`centerpiece_flank_layout` showing N=5 / N=10 / N=15 of the same problem · `wave_function_collapse` straining at large grids · `temporal_to_spatial_strip` of growing search trees · `feather_overlay_arrays` at increasing density per plate

### Mood signature
Plates in series. Each plate readable on its own; the seventh plate is exactly twice as dense as the third. Density as a graph plotted *across* the maps of the sequence. The player walks more slowly through the larger ones not because the rooms are larger but because their attention runs out faster.

## C. Local-versus-global

> The algorithm settles on something that isn't the answer.

### Cousins

- **Topographic survey maps** with named valleys, named saddles, named summits. The walker's route from named here to named there is constrained by what they can *see* of the terrain. *Cousin because the algorithm's "stuck" is a place a body can stand.*
- **John Snow**, *Cholera Map of Soho* (1854). The point of the map is the *local cluster* around Broad Street; the rest of London recedes to give the cluster meaning. *Cousin because optimisation needs a frame to be optimisation in.*
- **Hilma af Klint**, *Paintings for the Temple — Group X* (1907). Pyramidal stages of evolution, each a flat coloured zone, each a "you have arrived" that is then exceeded by the next. *Cousin because the stages are local maxima the cosmology rises through.*
- **W.E.B. Du Bois**, *Exhibition of American Negroes* (1900) infographics. Hand-drawn data of Black-American conditions presented as the truth of *somewhere specific*. *Cousin because the local knowledge is precisely what universalist algorithms suppress.*
- **A loss-landscape rendered as physical hills**, walked. The substrate already does this if pointed at it. *Cousin because the metaphor returns to its origin.*

### Vocabulary slice
`gradient_filter_morph` (terrain shading) · `pathfinding_dijkstra` colouring by distance from spawn · `lift_and_decay` (the failed climb) · `outline_silhouette` for the ridge

### Mood signature
Soft greens and ochres. Cartographic — the map is the world abbreviated. The player can see further than they can walk. The teleporter is visible, not reachable. There is more than one teleporter, and choosing one closes the others.

## D. Representational silence

> The algorithm ends because it does not see what is there.

### Cousins

- **Andreas Vesalius**, *De Humani Corporis Fabrica* (1543). Cadavers in pastoral landscapes, every muscle named, every nerve drawn. Yet the female body figures only as the *pregnant* exception, and what was unnamed remained unnamed. *Cousin because the plate's completeness is a frame that excludes.*
- **Hooke**, *Micrographia* (1665). The microscope's gaze, made plate. The flea is rendered enormous; what was outside the field of view is outside the book. *Cousin because choice of magnification is a politics.*
- **Magritte**, *La Trahison des images* (1929). "Ceci n'est pas une pipe." *Cousin because it places the gap between sign and thing as the painting's subject.*
- **W.E.B. Du Bois**, *Exhibition of American Negroes* (1900) — counting what was uncounted. *Cousin because the diagram is what existed exactly because no one had drawn it.*
- **Anatomical plates that omit by convention** — early-modern obstetric atlases without the foetus's face, embryology without naming what cannot be named. *Cousin because conventions are shaped silences.*

### Vocabulary slice
`outline_silhouette` filter (what is the *figure* of the categories) · `boolean_cuts` carving the named from the unnamed · `flower_grammar` / `bird_grammar` showing the unnamed regions by their absence · `frame_in_frame_layout` framing what is excluded

### Mood signature
The Vesalius-cousin: a body cut open in a landscape that *contains it*. The plate is generous; the plate is specific; the plate is incomplete. The map's centrepiece is a labelled diagram with one un-labelled region. The player has time to notice.

## E. Embodiment / continuous-discrete

> The algorithm ends because it is not built for the body running it.

### Cousins

- **Leonardo**, *Vitruvian Man* (c. 1490). Body inscribed in circle and square. The geometry is the body's; the body is the geometry's. *Cousin because the proportional canon is the apparatus.*
- **Eadweard Muybridge**, *The Human Figure in Motion* (1887). The body cut into discrete frames — running, walking, climbing-stairs — with the algorithmic act of frame-spacing made visible. *Cousin because the quantising IS the gesture.*
- **Étienne-Jules Marey**, chronophotographs. The continuous body composited into one stilled diagram. *Cousin because the integral and the differential occupy the same plate.*
- **Polynesian stick charts** (rebbelib, mattang). Coconut fronds and shells encoding ocean-swell patterns; held against the navigator's chest, felt for vibration matched to remembered swells. *Cousin because the chart reads *with* the body, not for the eye.*
- **Hildegard of Bingen**, the *Liber Divinorum Operum* visions (c. 1163–73). The cosmos as a body; the body as the cosmos. *Cousin because the map is the body and the body is the world.*
- **Jain *lokapurusha***. The universe rendered as a person — three-tier cosmos as torso, head, legs. *Cousin because no other coordinate system is needed once the body is the diagram.*

### Vocabulary slice
`body` channel (the second substrate) · `temporal_to_spatial_strip` of body-poses · `morphology_dress` · `vr_hand_extension` · `human_grammar` (`bird_grammar` for non-human variants) · `scaling_binding`

### Mood signature
The body either fits the grid (and is constrained) or is plotted across it (and the grid bends). Vitruvian or Marey or chronophotographic. The map progresses when the player's gait registers; it pauses when it does not.

## F. Folding

> The algorithm ends because it becomes another algorithm.

### Cousins

- **Ernst Haeckel**, *Kunstformen der Natur* Tafel 9 — Hexacorallia. The brain coral with sulci-and-gyri folding (Turing reaction-diffusion *avant la lettre*); the radial corallites; the dendritic colony arms. *Cousin because the plate composes multiple folds — surface fold, branching fold, radial fold — into one taxonomic argument.* (See `arttoeat/qfep/20260426_145120.jpg`.)
- **Codex Seraphinianus**, the carousel page. Cyclic time made spatial — wedges around a central axis, each wedge a stage. *Cousin because it folds time into rotation.* (See `arttoeat/qfep/20260426_151943.jpg`.)
- **Maria Sibylla Merian**, *Metamorphosis Insectorum Surinamensium* (1705). Egg, larva, pupa, imago, all on the same plate, all on the same host plant. *Cousin because the lifecycle is folded onto a single botanical surface.*
- **Origami crease patterns** — flat foldings of a single sheet that produce three-dimensional birds, tessellations, parametric curvatures. *Cousin because the rule (a crease pattern) and the result (the folded form) are the same artefact.*
- **Hofstadter**, *Gödel Escher Bach* (1979) — the strange loop as folding. Bach's canons, Escher's stairs, Gödel's self-reference. *Cousin because the fold is the relation that makes a discipline.*
- **The `fold_system` documentation** in this project. *Cousin because the project has been thinking about this for a while.*

### Vocabulary slice
`folding` · `morph` · `temporal_to_spatial_circle` (carousel) and `_strip` (lifecycle) · `reaction_diffusion` · `voronoi_round` (brain coral) · `flower_grammar` × `insect_grammar` co-rendered

### Mood signature
Haeckel Tafel 9: cream-on-black, taxonomic plate, octa-symmetric corner motifs, central specimen larger than the others. The same morphology revisited at three resolutions on the same page. The map's centerpiece is mid-fold. The player walks past three smaller specimens, each a stage of the centerpiece's transformation, before reaching it.

## G. Opacity refusal

> The right not to be rendered.

### Cousins

- **Voynich Manuscript** (15th c.). Six hundred years of unyielding to decoding. *Cousin because the book commits, totally, to a visual world that will not be paraphrased.*
- **Hilma af Klint**, *Paintings for the Temple* (1906–15). She stipulated that the work be withheld for twenty years after her death; the work waited. *Cousin because the act of refusal is part of the work.*
- **Codex Seraphinianus** (1981). The whole book. *Cousin already documented.* (See `arttoeat/qfep/20260426_151819.jpg`, etc.)
- **Adolf Wölfli**, *Von der Wiege bis zum Graab* (1908–30). Edge-to-edge composition; no entry point, no figure, no ground; ornament reading as language reading as ornament. *Cousin because the density refuses the viewer's mastery.*
- **Black masking and masquerade traditions** (West African, New Orleans Mardi Gras Indians, Caribbean Carnival). The mask presents and refuses simultaneously. *Cousin because the refusal-of-recognition IS the form.*
- **Tibetan sand mandalas** at completion-and-destruction. *Cousin because the rendered cosmos is dispersed before it can be archived.*

### Vocabulary slice
`glyph` (subdivision) at high uniform density · `feather_overlay_arrays` · `pattern_from_base_rule` with rules that don't stabilise · `crackling_filter` · `pixel_decay_filter` · the `floor_plan_layers` set to a layer count the player can't fully traverse

### Mood signature
Wölfli's all-over composition. No figure. No ground. The map has no centerpiece. The walls are dense. The player either accepts the density as it is or leaves. The teleporter is at the same density as the walls.

## H. Apparatus-as-phenomenon

> The algorithm does not measure; it makes.

### Cousins

- **Hooke**, *Micrographia*. The microscope is the plate. *Cousin because what the microscope makes findable was made findable by the microscope being there.*
- **Hilma af Klint**, *The Ten Largest* (1907). Diagrams that *make* the cosmology they purport to chart. *Cousin because the apparatus and the apparatus's subject are the same artefact.*
- **Sol LeWitt**, *Wall Drawing #260* (1975) and successors. The text instruction and the executed drawing are the same work; the drafter's hand is part of the apparatus. *Cousin because the diagram runs.*
- **Ramon Llull**, *Ars Magna* combinatorial wheels. The instrument is the thought. *Cousin because the apparatus literally rotates to produce the truth.*
- **Polynesian stick charts** (again). The chart and the swell are co-determining; reading the chart and being in the boat are the same activity. *Cousin because the chart can only be read from the body the swell is moving.*
- **Marcel Duchamp**, *La Mariée mise à nu par ses célibataires, même* (the *Large Glass*, 1915–23). The work is unfinished; the *Green Box* of notes accompanies it; the apparatus and the description of the apparatus and the failure of the apparatus are all parts. *Cousin because the diagram is the diagram of itself failing.*

### Vocabulary slice
`subdivide_by_attention(viewer)` (the substrate detailing what is looked at) · `mechanism` (single-axis or complex rotation as instrument) · `frame_in_frame_layout` (the apparatus inside the apparatus) · `gradient_filter_morph` (the phenomenon's gradient)

### Mood signature
Hooke-cousin: the apparatus larger than the specimen. The player walks toward an object; the object draws itself further by being walked toward. The map's centerpiece is *being made* by the player's attention. Not a rotating exhibit; a *responsive* exhibit.

## I. Entropy debt

> The algorithm's order is paid for in disorder somewhere else.

### Cousins

- **Tibetan sand mandalas**. Built over weeks, dispersed in seconds. The dispersion *is* the teaching. *Cousin because the diagram's perfection is paid for by its dissolution.*
- **Diderot**, *Encyclopédie* workshop plates. Labour visible-but-aestheticised; the artisan numbered like a tool. *Cousin because the diagram of the workshop suppresses the workshop's heat.*
- **Robert Smithson**, *A Tour of the Monuments of Passaic, NJ* (1967), *Spiral Jetty* drawings (1970). Industrial entropy as monument. *Cousin because the work is the document of the system winding down.*
- **Vanitas painting**, c. 1620–80, Northern European. Skulls beside fresh fruit; the diagram of mortality includes the beauty it is consuming. *Cousin because the still life is a thermodynamic claim.*
- **Tibetan and Bhutanese *Wheel of Becoming* thangkas**. Cyclic existence as pie chart, with each wedge a karmic state, the whole turning. *Cousin because the cosmology's order is shown to be running on the same clock as everything else.*

### Vocabulary slice
`decay_uniform` modifier on every channel · `mould_decay` · `expanding_water_edges_decay` · `pixel_eating_decay` · `lift_and_decay` (particles) · `temporal_to_spatial_circle` (wheel of becoming as carousel) · `outline_silhouette` rendered crackling

### Mood signature
Vanitas-cousin: a centrepiece that is beautiful and visibly aging. The map renders the same scene twice — fresh and decayed — separated by a doorway. The decay is the same algorithm playing forward in time. The player can't go back.

## J. Training-fossil

> Every model is the suppressed labour of those who built it.

### Cousins

- **Diderot**, *Encyclopédie* plates again — but now read for *who is in the plate without being named*. The artisans drawn at their lathes are types, not portraits. *Cousin because the plate's general truth required a specific labour to produce.*
- **Vesalius**, *Fabrica*. The cadavers were sourced from criminals' bodies and the unclaimed dead; their identities suppressed; their anatomies preserved. *Cousin because the diagram literally fossilises the unnamed.*
- **Indigenous codices "translated" into European archives** — the Borgia Group, the Codex Mendoza. Pages annotated by colonial Spanish, the original meaning available only through the annotation. *Cousin because the artefact carries both knowledges, with one suppressing the other.*
- **Botanical plates collected by colonial expeditions** (Joseph Banks's *Florilegium*, the Bligh expedition). Plants drawn meticulously; the people who knew the plants nearly absent. *Cousin because the plate is what was extracted.*
- **Photo archives of cataloged populations** — Bertillon's anthropometric photographs, the colonial physiognomy archives. *Cousin because the catalogue is the form the labour took.*

### Vocabulary slice
`feather_overlay_arrays` (layered traces, one suppressing another) · `frame_in_frame_layout` (the plate within the catalogue) · `boolean_arrays` (named-and-numbered) · `outline_silhouette` for the not-quite-erased · `pixel_decay_filter` over a fossilised face

### Mood signature
The plate looks like Diderot. Underneath it, you can hear breath. The map's centrepiece is a beautiful diagram; in the recess behind it, the same diagram is shown with the labour visible — the engraver's elbow, the apprentice's fingers, the body that posed. The player can choose which side to walk past.

## K. Cosmotechnics

> There is no "the" algorithm.

### Cousins

- **Aztec / Mixtec / Maya codices** (Borgia, Mendoza, Madrid, Dresden). Page-as-calendar, page-as-genealogy, page-as-cosmology, read in many directions, often boustrophedon. *Cousin because each page is a complete technical system that does not reduce to Cartesian space.*
- **Tibetan thangkas**. Concentric paradises with deity-and-attribute systems holding together by visual-mathematical precision. *Cousin because the cosmography is its own logic, complete to its own readers.*
- **Yoruba ifa boards (*opon ifa*)**. Square divination tray with carved border-symbols, dust-marked with patterns generated from sixteen palm nuts. *Cousin because the apparatus, the alphabet, the corpus of poems, and the diviner's body are one technical system.*
- **Polynesian stick charts** — already a cousin of E and H, also K because they show that *a navigation technology can be entirely non-Cartesian and entirely sufficient.*
- **Aboriginal Western-Desert songline paintings** (Pintupi, Pitjantjatjara). Country rendered as iconography that is map, story, ancestor, and Law all at once. *Cousin because the painting is not a representation of country; it IS country, in the strict legal-cosmological sense.*
- **Indian Jain *lokapurusha*** — the universe-as-person. *Cousin because it shows the body and the cosmos as one cosmology, where Western science divides them.*

### Vocabulary slice
Composition variants — `temporal_to_spatial_circle` / `_strip` / `_spiral` — different cosmographies make different layouts · `plate_taxonomy_layout` per cosmography (each room is a thangka-cousin, an opon-cousin, a stick-chart-cousin) · `frame_in_frame_layout` for the Jain cosmos-as-body

### Mood signature
The map is many small rooms, each a complete world. None is the default. The player passes through each. The teleporter does not place them in the next room of *one* world; it places them in *another* world. By the third room they have stopped expecting consistency.

## L. Failure as method

> The optimum is not always the goal.

### Cousins

- **Adolf Wölfli**, **Henry Darger**, **Madge Gill**, **Howard Finster** — the outsider artists collectively. Each commits to a personal cosmology rendered with maximum density and zero compromise. None converges; none asks to. *Cousin because the work proceeds by refusing closure.*
- **Cornelius Cardew**, *Treatise* (1963–67). 193 pages of musical notation that the performer has to make their own grammar for. The score *invites* mis-readings. *Cousin because failure to converge is the work's mode.*
- **Cy Twombly**, *Blackboard Paintings* (c. 1968–71). Scribbles on blackboard ground. The mark is allowed to fail at making sense. *Cousin because the failed gesture is the gesture.*
- **Joseph Beuys**, *Lecture Blackboards* (1972–80). Diagrams drawn during lectures, photographed, kept as artefacts of thought-in-motion that did not arrive. *Cousin because the document is *what was tried* not *what was settled.*
- **Hokusai**, *Manga* sketchbooks (1814–78). Studies, repeats, exercises, occasional fully-realised drawings. The book is the practice. *Cousin because non-arrival IS the genre.*

### Vocabulary slice
`pattern_from_base_rule` with rules deliberately broken / non-stable · `crackling_filter` · `pathfinding_astar` deliberately handicapped (heuristic that mis-leads) · `morph` that doesn't complete the morph · `glyph` policies that produce noise instead of script

### Mood signature
Twombly-cousin: the wall is a blackboard. Marks accrue. None of them converges to a sentence. The map's centerpiece is *the act of marking*. The player passes through and adds. The teleporter is at a place where the wall is briefly empty.

## M. Authenticity collapse

> After generative models, the real / synthetic distinction is gone.

### Cousins

- **Codex Seraphinianus** itself. Invented authenticity is the most authentic thing the world has produced lately. *Cousin because Serafini chose to commit so totally to his invented script that the question "is this a real script" became unanswerable.*
- **Hilma af Klint** again — produced privately, indistinguishable in technique from any "discovered" cosmology. *Cousin because the work could be ancient or could be modern; only the date of paint analysis settles it.*
- **Joseph Beuys** *Blackboards*. Performance trace; the chalk dust is "real" but the lecture is not retrievable. *Cousin because the artefact is exactly the surviving fraction.*
- **Chinese landscape forgery studios** of the 19th–20th c. (Zhang Daqian's Shanghai workshop). Copies indistinguishable from originals; some are *better* than the originals; museum collections include both, undifferentiated. *Cousin because the forgery question is now categorical, not particular.*
- **The 2025–26 model-collapse / synthetic-data literature**. By 2026 most large training corpora are model-output, indistinguishable from human-output. *Cousin because we are inside the moodboard now.*

### Vocabulary slice
`glyph` policies mixing curated and noise-generated subdivisions · `feather_overlay_arrays` of mixed provenance · `frame_in_frame_layout` where the inner frame's authorship is undeterminable · `crackling_filter` simulating age regardless of actual age · the *forensic* dimension of `subdivide_by_attention(viewer)` — *what does looking-for-evidence change?*

### Mood signature
The map has five specimens. Three are visibly hand-built. Two are visibly procedural. The player can tell. The next room has five specimens; *none are visibly anything*. The map ends with the question of whether the player should care.

## How the seeds compose

Reading the cousins across edges, three patterns:

1. **Some traditions are cousin to many edges.** Hooke's *Micrographia* is cousin to D, H, and (through chronology) B. Hilma af Klint is cousin to G, H, and M. Polynesian stick charts are cousin to E, H, and K. *This is good — the multi-cousin sources are the densest visual nutrients for the substrate's grammar.*

2. **Some edges share a tradition because they ARE almost the same edge.** A and K share Llull's *Ars Magna* and the Voynich Manuscript — formal-impossibility and cosmotechnics meet at the limits-of-frame. F and M share the Codex carousel and Hilma — folding and authenticity-collapse meet at the visible mediation.

3. **The pairings already proposed in `EDGES_OF_ALGORITHM.md` (six pairs plus one solo) hold up under the seed analysis.** A↔K shares Llull / Voynich. B↔I shares the Diderot Encyclopédie. C↔L shares topographic-as-locus. D↔G/J shares Vesalius and Diderot. E↔H shares Vitruvian / Marey / Hooke. F↔M shares the carousel and Hilma. *The pairs are not artificial; they are how the corpus actually carves itself.*

## How to use this doc

### Per map design

When designing a late-spine map, name the edge it teaches. Look up its cousins here. Keep the cousins' images open while you're picking catalogue rows. Pull the substrate toward the cousins, *not* into them — the goal is not "look like Hooke" but "do what Hooke does, in cubes."

### Per auto-research iteration

The vision-critique step (file 5, still to be written) feeds *both* the captured PNGs and the named cousin images into the comparator. The score asks: *is the capture a cousin of the cousins, or is it a copy of one of them, or is it something else*. The third answer is also useful — sometimes the substrate finds an image that's neither cousin nor copy and is its own thing. Those are the moments the auto-research loop is for.

### Per sequence identity

Each sequence in the late spine commits to a *handful* of cousins as its house style. `qfeplaboratory` lives at the Codex / Haeckel / Hilma intersection. `criticalalgorithms` lives at the Wölfli / Twombly / Du Bois intersection. `searchpathfinding` lives at the Diderot / Babbage / Hokusai-series intersection. Once each sequence's cousin set is locked, the glyph system, palette, pedestal style, and pacing follow.
