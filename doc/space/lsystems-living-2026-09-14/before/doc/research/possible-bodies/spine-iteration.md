## Writing the book

Clarified with Palle, 14 September: the reader wants to understand how code makes a world and how its construction permits, supports and confines bodies. The tutorial gives this inquiry something concrete to follow. **Stay with the operation long enough for both its usefulness and its assumptions to become perceptible.**

Carry a recognisable program, object or state through the explanation. Establish what the reader already knows, what is added and what the addition makes possible. Buttons lead into understanding the underlying operation; code, diagrams and direct definitions provide footholds. Give discovery room without withholding necessary explanation. The book must also make sense away from the headset.

The Nature of Code gives us a useful model of continuity: its [bouncing ball develops through vectors](https://natureofcode.com/vectors/), and its [Ecosystem Project](https://natureofcode.com/introduction/) accumulates capabilities across chapters. Its [forces discussion](https://natureofcode.com/forces/) already makes modelling choices explicit. Ada develops the bodily consequences of such choices: what can I learn by approaching this space, where does it carry or stop me, and who can revise its rules?

Let the critical question arise through the actual consequence. A boundary may confine, support or shelter. A coarse record may omit a gesture or give it a useful steadiness. Desire and strangeness can guide the next experiment; more complexity alone cannot tell us whether a change serves this body. A question may remain with us across several halls.

Keep moving through the first pass. Strengthen tutorial continuity as we go, then return to earlier passages with knowledge of the whole journey. The detailed working guidance is in the project's `doc/book/handoffs/fable-final-discovery-voice.md`.
