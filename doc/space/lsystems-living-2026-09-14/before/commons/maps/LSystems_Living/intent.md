Concept: The world starts to grow. Grammar is no longer demonstration — it is terrain. Trees, corals, and evolved forms populate a living environment where branching is not illustration but the generative engine of an ecosystem. The L-Systems sequence culminates in grammar as world-building.
Sequence role: Sixth and final map in the L-Systems sequence (11th spine sequence, lambda_edge phase). The capstone that synthesizes everything: basic rewriting from Grammar_Lab, temporal growth from Growth, formal rigor from Grammars_And_Curves, spatial application from Architecture, and ecological interaction from Competition. Intermediate difficulty — but the conceptual weight is high, as the learner must see grammar not as tool but as ontology. Connects forward to the Procedural Generation sequence (12th spine) where rule-based world-building expands beyond grammar into noise, evolution, and stochastic generation; follows LSystems_Competition.
Technical angle: Grammar as world-building substrate — AnimatedTree provides the temporal growth engine, branching_coral extends morphogenesis into marine forms, and genetic_tree_sculptor introduces evolutionary selection pressure on grammar parameters. The ecosystem is not hand-placed but grown from interacting rule systems. Emergence operates at two levels: individual forms emerge from grammar rules, and ecosystem structure emerges from the interaction of multiple growing forms.
Critical angle: This map tests whether the learner has internalized the lambda_edge truth: a sentence can become a forest, and grammar is generative. If the earlier maps taught the mechanism, this map demonstrates the consequence — that a world doesn't need a blueprint, only a grammar. The transition from L-Systems to Procedural Generation is prepared here: if grammar can build worlds, what else can? The answer — rules and randomness — opens the next sequence.
Key artifacts: AnimatedTree provides generation-by-generation growth visualization; branching_coral extends grammar-driven morphogenesis to marine ecosystems; genetic_tree_sculptor introduces evolutionary optimization of grammar parameters, bridging toward the Procedural Generation sequence.
Gap: Needs an ecosystem metrics dashboard tracking population diversity, branching complexity, and resource distribution over time — transforming the visual experience into quantifiable ecological data.
