Concept: The vector field — a function of space that returns a direction-and-magnitude at every point. A grid of arrows for f(x,y) = (vx, vy), with presets for rotational, divergent, and wavy flow the player can dial through.
Sequence role: Sixth map of Change, opening the flow cluster. Completes the move begun in Change_Slope_Surface: from slope-at-a-point to direction-everywhere. The static field here is the instruction that Flow_Particle_Pulse then animates.
Technical angle: Evaluating a vector-valued function over a 2D grid, rendering an arrow per cell scaled and oriented by the field, and swapping the generating function between presets. The rotational/divergent/wavy set spans the canonical field types learners meet again in forces and fluid work.
Critical angle: The rule moves into the room. Earlier maps put change in a moving particle or a slider the learner pushed; here the change is the space itself — every location pre-committed to a behavior. This is the first field, the structure that forces, wavefunctions, and noise all assume without introducing.
Key artifacts: vector_field_grid renders the arrow lattice and holds the presets; science_screen shows the generating function.

Gap: vector_field_grid is a scaffolded name; it is reused in Flow_Particle_Pulse and should be built to support particle injection there. Size budget compact_only.
