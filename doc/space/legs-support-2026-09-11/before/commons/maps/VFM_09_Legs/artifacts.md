# VFM 09 Legs — Artifacts
*Vectors & Forces: From Direction to Dynamics · oscillation · 7 artifacts*

> Count the legs; the walk follows.

The map, read through what it holds — its artifacts in the order you meet them:

## One Leg
`one_leg`

One leg, one contact point — the pogo stick is locomotion reduced to its axiom. There is no gait to discover here, only the fact that a single foot cannot hold a body up without moving.

## Two Legs
`two_leg_critter`

Two legs means constant falling — every step is a crisis of balance resolved just in time. The simplest creature that has to solve balance rather than avoid it.

## Three Legs
`three_leg_critter`

Three points define a plane — the first polygon that does not need to negotiate with falling. Static stability arrives here and never has to be argued for again.

## Four Legs
`four_leg_critter`

Four legs pair diagonals because geometry demands it — trot and gallop are necessities, not choices. This is the rig the Arena's spider is built from.

## Five Legs
`five_leg_critter`

Five legs break bilateral symmetry — the gait cannot be mirrored, only invented. The odd count is the whole exhibit.

## Six Legs
`six_leg_critter`

Six legs is where stability and speed converge — two alternating tripods, always three on ground. The most common arthropod configuration, and not by accident.

## Eight Legs
`octapod_crawler`

Eight FABRIK chains on a body that patrols surfaces. Placed here with `detection:0` and `damage:0`, so it walks and does not hunt — the same animal that is a hazard elsewhere, standing still enough to be counted.
