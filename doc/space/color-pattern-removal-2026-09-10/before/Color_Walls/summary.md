# Colour becomes an office

Eight furnished cubicles occupy two sides of a 3.2 m central aisle. Forty tinted glass panes stand 2.6 m high, at zero or ninety degrees, with 1.2 m entrances. Walking changes which coloured panes overlap in the view. The original rainbow hallway, colour-space navigator and interpolator remain in their recovered arrangement.

The recovered source footprint is preserved inside a new perimeter wall. See `museum_recovery.md` for historical provenance and placement exceptions.
