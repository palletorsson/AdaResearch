From a rotating surface to a standing ride

Stand through
the turn.

The bare blade loses its usefulness as a floor when it turns through vertical. Now a small upright end-platform lets you make the journey while seeing that difference.

Actual XR rig testedMoving ride + returnHeadset comfort pending

X: forward and up.

Board the platform beside the orange blade's lower end. Wait through the docking pause. Its centre follows the blade around x, rising along an arc and finishing seven metres forward and three metres higher. The platform stays upright. Its sideways mounting keeps your body clear of the blade as the blade turns through vertical.

Each endpoint pause lasts twelve seconds; each half-turn takes twelve seconds at the current setting. The return retraces the upper arc. It does not carry you around underneath the axle through the basin.

Z: sideways and up.

Board the blue platform from its lower bank on the left. Rotation around z changes x and y while leaving z fixed. The ride therefore travels seven metres sideways and three metres up. There is no forward movement along z hidden inside it. To make this a passage, the higher landing has to receive you from another direction.

Which part turned, and which part was kept from turning?

The bare blades remain climbable during their held slopes. Riding a level end-platform is a second encounter. It exposes what the mechanism must add if the body is to stand through the turn.

What was checked.

53 actual-museum checks pass: natural boarding, outward and return travel, preserved camera orientation and room-scale offset, jump and step-off release, invalid side/below contacts, and cleanup after a rider leaves the scene. A further 1,638 standing-capsule placements around the two arcs found no contacts with the blades, rails or surrounding geometry.

The existing 42 hall checks and nine rotation-direction checks also pass. Those cover the paused blade crossings, arrays, tunnel, return wedges and retained centre geometry. Moving-rig tests use accelerated timing; human comfort at the normal pace still needs a headset visit.

Support is conditional. The platform translates the actual player body and tracked origin together only while the feet are supported; it does not rotate the player's view. Stepping off and jumping release the carry.
