## The walk

The wave becomes audible, and the room becomes a studio. The rack family carries the argument in hardware: the sine rack is the atom; the Moog rack carves harmonics away until a bass remains; the DX7 rack lets sines modulate sines into a piano that never had strings — Fourier's theorem played as an instrument; the 303 rack bends the filter itself into melody. Around them the infrastructure hums — mixer, monitor, cables you patch yourself — and the BigPipeSystem states the oldest version: pipes whose lengths *are* their pitches, the building as instrument.

## What it fixes — and confesses

Hearing is the chapter's fastest teacher; the ear does Fourier analysis without being asked. The room is dense the way real studios are dense — gear breeds — and its crowding is either the honest texture of a workshop or a jam wanting curation; that call sits on the bench. Either way, this is where the sound domain shows its face.
