# Color Context Placed — Artifacts
*Color and Composition · F_order · 21 artifacts*

> This is the room where color stops being a single lesson and becomes a crowd. Everything the rest of the sequence left out gathers here — Albers' nested squares humming on the walls, stacks that tilt complementary against complementary, a clustering lab that sorts a whole image down to its handful of governing hues. Walk it and the throughline reveals itself: color is never one thing alone. A swatch only means something next to its neighbor. The gallery puts those neighbors in the same long hall so the contrasts can argue out loud.

## Primitive Assembler
![Primitive Assembler](/scene-catalog/primitive_assembler.png)

Conveyor-fed assembly station. Primitives ride in on a directional belt and snap onto a growing stack on an illuminated pedestal, a live formula placard assembling in step (● + ■ + ▲). Loops: assemble, hold (turntable), clear, repeat. Reuses the exact DNA alphabet from primitive_stack.

`primitive_assembler`

## Primitive Combo Puzzle
![Primitive Combo Puzzle](/scene-catalog/primitive_combo_puzzle.png)

Goal plinth with the sign combo placard + a translucent ghost of the target stack; a low tray that deals real grabbable XRToolsPickable primitives (sphere/cube/cylinder/cone/disc) around its rim; a glowing centre ring marking the stacking column. Grab the pieces and stack them bottom-to-top in the target order; when the central column's shapes match and settle (stability_time), the combo solves with a ring-pulse celebration, then a new one is dealt. Reuses the DNA alphabet from primitive_stack and the project's grab_* pickables.

`primitive_combo_puzzle`
