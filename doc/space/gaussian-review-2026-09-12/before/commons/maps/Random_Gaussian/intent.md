Concept: A distribution is the shape many draws make, not a property of any one of them. The room puts a finite histogram beside the count its law expects at the same N, keeps the two on one scale and the density curve on its own, and shows what a bounded display does with the mass a law puts beyond its edges.
Sequence role: Active hall 5 in randomness. Random_Walk separated a step from the accumulated position and the stored drawing; this room gathers many draws into a picture and separates the picture from the law, the sample from the expectation, the bins from the values. It hands variation to Random_Mushrooms, where a population of forms is grown from draws like these.
Technical angle: `distribution_sampler.gd` draws at fifty a second (a bead falls and lands in a bin) up to a cap of a thousand. UNIFORM passes the draw through; GAUSS is Box-Muller with a 0.0001 offset inside the logarithm that bounds the tail at about 4.3σ; POISSON is Knuth's loop scaled by 1/(3λ); EXPON is −ln(u)/rate halved. Every raw value is folded into [0,1] by `_fit`, which counts the folds. Bars are normalised to the tallest; the density curve is drawn at its own scale. With `sample_seed` at −1 the draws come from the global stream; a non-negative seed installs a private generator that CLEAR re-seeds. The opt-in `stand:cabinet` builds a cabinet that lifts the display to 0.95 m, a shoulder carrying the keypad and a second panel (BATCH lands a hundred at once, PAUSE / RUN, NEW SEED, BINS cycles 10 · 30 · 60 by re-binning the kept values), a six-line readout under the histogram, ghost frames at the expected count per bin at the present N with the clipped mass in the edge bins, and a named seed.
Critical angle: The bell is easy to complete in language before it exists in the bins; the room asks for the sparse, lopsided picture to be described as what it is. The ghost frames are a comparison, not a target; a bar over its frame is what a sample looks like. Clipping is the display's decision, counted on the plate, so the mean of a bounded histogram is not offered as the centre of anything unbounded. The mean is a frequent region under one model; it measures no person's worth, and the edge bins hold what the model could not fit.
Key artifacts: distribution_sampler is the map's primary; the book's pearl names distribution_comparator as its hero and carries a line for the sampler, which is Astra's and Palle's to reconcile. distribution_comparator racks three laws side by side at one sample count; galton_board drops beads through pegs into bins at one and a half times bench scale; random_bell_curve is a walkable Gaussian mound in the south-west at a third of its size; gaussian_random, GaussianBlurShader, GaussianPaintSplatter and GaussianBlurCircle apply the law elsewhere; dark_sphere is a reference body.
Gap: No control varies the Gaussian's mean or deviation at the cabinet, so the visitor cannot push the tail into the edge bins on purpose; EXPON shows the fold instead. A sample-count slider for the comparator would make convergence a single gesture; it is the comparator's, not this hall's.
