## The walk

The world room takes color off the exhibits and into the architecture. The rainbow_hallway is a corridor performing the spectrum — you pass *through* the gradient rather than looking at it. The color_space_navigator makes the abstract solid walkable: RGB as a place with directions. The subpixel_display leans close to the lie itself — the three tiny lights that fake every hue you have ever seen on a screen — while the gradient_interpolator shows the in-between being manufactured, and the constellation office hangs the chapter's working palette as stars you stand under.

## What the world says

A world, in this chapter's register, is a set of surfaces that have agreed on their palette. Walls are where the agreement becomes law — paint is policy. The room lets you inhabit the color space instead of pointing at it, which is the widest the chapter knows how to open before it hands everything to the body next door.
