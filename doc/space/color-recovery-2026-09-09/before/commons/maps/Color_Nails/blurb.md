A salon for digital fingers, and then the floor opens. The hall is a dry
approach onto a lid of glass over a four-metre pool, and the hand is down
there — five times life size, filling the depth, seen through the floor you
are standing on. The controls stay up here where your own hands are: the nail
colour controller and the hand colour controller within reach of each other,
so the choice is made at arm's length and answered at the size of a room.

Colour balls scatter like polish bottles on a shelf. Choose a hue and the
thing that changes is not the object in front of you.
