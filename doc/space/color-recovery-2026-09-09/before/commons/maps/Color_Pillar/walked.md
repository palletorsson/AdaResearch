## The walk

The walk is a colonnade of ways to hold color. The pillar collection stands hues up at body height — color as column, met eye to eye rather than looked down on. The SpectrumVisualizer unrolls the physics; visual_color_mixing stages the negotiation live, channels summing to hues in front of you; color_sets_overview lays out the palettes the project actually uses, its wardrobe on hangers. The grab_rainbow_stick puts the gradient in your fist, and the persian loom drum spins the oldest color technology in the room — dye and thread agreeing on a pattern.

## What it fixes

Between the pillars you learn the chapter's working vocabulary: hue, set, mix, spectrum. Nothing is contested yet — that is the next room's job. This is the calm before context.
