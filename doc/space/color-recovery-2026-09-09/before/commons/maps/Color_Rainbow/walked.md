# Color_Rainbow — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (primitive slot, color thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

rainbow · mario_cube · pick_up_cube · dark_sphere

## The walk

The primitive is the spectrum itself: a rainbow standing in the room, the chapter's alphabet laid out in order. Beside it, the mario_cube — a pop-culture color chord everyone already knows by heart — and a plain pick_up_cube for the hand that wants to hold something while it looks. The room was recently unpacked (its bodies used to crowd; they breathe now), so the arc reads clean from either end.

## What it teaches

Every color the chapter will complicate is here first in its innocent form: a gradient with names. The rainbow is the last time the spectrum gets to look like a fact. From the next room on, context starts telling it what to be.
