## Pattern Mill
![Pattern Mill](/scene-catalog/pattern_mill.png)

Dark industrial frame with emissive accents, a glowing core and fold-order mirror rotors, a glowing warp cage, an editable touch-paint head grid, a press head on guide rods + pull lever, and a live wallpaper-group carpet feeding off a chute/tray plus a vertical proof sheet — both from one reusable texture. Touch-paint the head to edit; GROUP cycles all 17 groups and rebuilds the rotors to the new fold; SEED rerolls a procedural motif; CLEAR blanks it; PRESS punches the head down and re-stamps. DNA: group (p1..p6m), palette (bauhaus/escher/alhambra/tatami/pastel/memphis/persian/monochrome), motif_seed, density, motif_size, machine params.
