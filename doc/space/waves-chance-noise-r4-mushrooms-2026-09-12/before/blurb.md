The `mushrooms` artifact places fruiting bodies by sampling a Gaussian-or-Poisson distribution across the substrate, the apparent randomness constrained by underground rules you cannot see. `bubbles_random` provides a uniform-distribution contrast; `random_number_book_page_collection` deepens the historical thread from the map-one RAND table. Mushrooms are the visible fruiting of a hidden mycelial network. The 1955 RAND tables were institutional infrastructure — nuclear testing, Monte Carlo simulations, statistical sampling all required randomness as raw material. Randomness is never culturally neutral. It is always harvested for a purpose, and the purpose shapes the distribution that looks random enough.
