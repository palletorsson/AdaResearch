## The walk

The world room shows what the grown becomes when it is dressed for use. The mirror_cellular_texture_for_3d runs cellular automata and folds the local disorder across an axis — random texture given a spine, symmetry manufactured from a rule and a reflection, the eye reading intention where there was only fold. Beside it the rhizomatic_maze_space grows the opposite: a labyrinth with no center and no single throughline, any point reachable from any other by more than one route, Deleuze's rhizome walked as architecture.

## The world

The world register widens to the aesthetics and the politics of authorless space. Mirroring is the cheap trick every texture artist knows — reflect the grown and it reads as designed — and the room shows the seam honestly, the manufactured symmetry that lets randomness pass as intention. The rhizome is its deep counter-argument: a space that refuses the spine, that offers no correct path and no privileged view, centerless on purpose. Between them sits the whole question the chapter has been building toward — that a world without an author is not therefore a world without a *stance*. Someone chose the fold, or chose the mesh; the rule that needs no designer still encodes what kind of moving-through it wants, and the walker inherits that politics with every step.
