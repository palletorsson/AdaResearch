Concept: Random walk carves caves, algorithmic generation builds mazes. Nature versus logic — two subtractive generation strategies that hollow space rather than filling it. The cave is stochastic erosion; the maze is deterministic partitioning. Both produce navigable interiors from solid blocks, but their character is opposite.
Sequence role: Fifth map in the Procedural Generation sequence (12th spine sequence, lambda_edge phase). Shifts from additive growth (branching, evolution, colonization) to subtractive generation — carving space from solid. Intermediate difficulty — the learner contrasts random-walk cave generation with algorithmic maze generation, seeing how the same goal (navigable interior space) is achieved through opposed methods. Connects backward to LSystems_Architecture where L-Systems generated dungeon layouts, forward to PG_Sculpted_Forms where geometry is accumulated rather than carved, and laterally to the cellular automata sequence where cave-like structures emerged from rule-based erosion; follows PG_Branching_Growth.
Technical angle: The caverandomwalk artifact implements a drunkard's walk algorithm — an agent wanders randomly through a solid grid, clearing cells as it moves, producing organic, irregular caverns with natural-looking passages. The maze_generation artifact implements structured algorithms (recursive backtracking, Prim's, Kruskal's) that partition space into corridors and walls with guaranteed solvability. The random walk has no guarantee of connectivity; the maze algorithm guarantees a spanning tree of passages.
Critical angle: Caves and mazes embody the two poles of procedural generation's creative spectrum. The cave is pure randomness shaped by constraint (the walker must stay in bounds); the maze is pure rule shaped by randomness (the algorithm chooses which wall to remove). Neither has an author in the traditional sense — the cave emerges from a stochastic process, the maze from a graph algorithm. The lambda_edge truth is tested from both sides: randomness alone carves recognizable spaces, and rules alone build explorable worlds.
Key artifacts: caverandomwalk generates organic cave systems through random-walk erosion of a solid grid; maze_generation produces algorithmically perfect mazes with guaranteed connectivity through spanning-tree algorithms.
Gap: Needs a cave-maze hybrid artifact that uses random walk for large-scale cavern carving and maze algorithms for fine-scale corridor structure, producing spaces that feel both natural and navigable.
