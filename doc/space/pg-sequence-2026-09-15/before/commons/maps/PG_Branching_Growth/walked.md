> ghost-drafted from the working map (walk slot, proceduralgeneration thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

branching_growth_algorithm · organic_space

## The walk

The chapter watches structure accumulate from a single repeated choice. The branching_growth_algorithm runs a tip that splits, angles, and splits again — each new branch a smaller echo of the decision that made it — and around it grows the organic_space, chambers and passages with the irregular rightness of something that developed rather than being placed. You walk a room that was not laid out but *grew*, and your body can tell the difference before your mind names it.

## What it fixes

The walk fixes the feel of authorless form — because "organic" turns out not to be a texture you apply but a *process* you run. The branching rule is nearly nothing (split, turn, repeat), yet iterated it produces the unmistakable signature of the grown: self-similar, unrepeating, right in a way no grid is. This is the room where procedural generation shakes hands with the fractal chapter next door — both are rules eating their own output — but the procedural version insists on the *space*, on structure you can stand inside, growth you inhabit rather than observe.
