## The cast

cube_mound_scene · dome · layered_membrane

## The walk

The room sets the grown against the drawn. The cube_mound_scene accretes a landform from stacked cubes under cellular logic — deposit where the local rule says yes, and terrain rises with no landscaper. The layered_membrane drapes surface over surface, each layer a record of the order its rule fired, form wearing its own history. And between them stands the dome — a clean half-sphere cut by boolean subtraction, the authored control: this is what shape looks like when someone simply *drew* it.

## What it fixes

The second walk is a controlled comparison, and it is the chapter's most honest room. Put the grown mound beside the drawn dome and the difference is immediate and hard to name — the dome is perfect and dead, the mound imperfect and alive, and the aliveness is *information*, the accumulated trace of a thousand small conditionals the eye reads as history. The room refuses to call either better. The dome is what you want for a door; the mound is what you want for a world. Procedural generation is the art of choosing which surfaces should remember how they were made and which should pretend they were always there.
