Concept: Cube mounds pile upward, domes arc overhead, membranes fold in layers. Geometry from accumulation — not carved, not grown, but stacked, curved, and laminated. These are the most architectural of procedural forms, where simple geometric operations (piling, arcing, folding) produce complex spatial volumes.
Sequence role: Sixth map in the Procedural Generation sequence (12th spine sequence, lambda_edge phase). After subtractive carving in Caves_Mazes, this map returns to additive construction but through geometric accumulation rather than branching or evolution. Intermediate difficulty — the learner sees that procedural generation encompasses not just organic growth and algorithmic carving but also geometric composition. Connects backward to LSystems_Architecture where grammar generated built form, forward to PG_Mirrored_Patterns where symmetry operations transform generation, and laterally to the primitives and mesh sequences where geometric construction was manual; follows PG_Caves_Mazes.
Technical angle: The cube_mound_scene artifact generates terrain through voxel accumulation — cubes are placed according to height-field rules, piling into mounds and ridges. The dome artifact constructs curved surfaces through parametric arcing — meridian and parallel curves define a smooth shell from discrete elements. The layered_membrane artifact produces folded, stratified surfaces through iterative displacement of parallel sheets. Each artifact demonstrates a different geometric primitive operation (stacking, curving, laminating) as a generative strategy.
Critical angle: Sculpted forms occupy an interesting position in the procedural generation spectrum: they are algorithmic but feel crafted, generated but look designed. The mound, the dome, and the membrane are among humanity's oldest architectural forms — and here they arise from accumulation rules rather than intentional design. The lambda_edge truth applies but with a sculptural inflection: a world doesn't need an author, but the forms it produces may look authored. The gap between generation and design narrows to indistinguishability.
Key artifacts: cube_mound_scene generates terrain through voxel stacking and height-field accumulation; dome constructs curved shell geometry through parametric arc subdivision; layered_membrane produces stratified folded surfaces through iterative sheet displacement.
Gap: Needs a material-assignment system that maps structural stress or curvature to visual properties (color, texture, transparency), making the geometric logic of each form legible through its surface appearance.
