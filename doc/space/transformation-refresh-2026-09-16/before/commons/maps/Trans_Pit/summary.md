# Trans Pit — Summary

Trans_Pit is a challenge map in the Transformation sequence. After earlier maps introduced the three fundamental spatial transformations — translation, rotation, and scaling — as abstract operations, this map stages each one as a distinct lethal threat and asks the learner to navigate them. The body learns what the mind has categorised.

The space is divided into three rooms, each devoted to one transformation. The first room is a corridor lined with pusher_block artifacts: each block translates along a fixed axis at a set speed, with configurable distance and pause interval, and the corridor runs past a row of fire pits. Translation becomes displacement. Stepping in front of a pusher at the wrong time sends the learner over the edge.

The second room is a circular arena with a revolving_wall rotating at constant angular velocity around a central axis. Fire pits line the arena's outer edge. Rotation becomes sweep. The wall clears the floor in an even arc, and the learner has to move with it rather than against it.

The third room is a chamber whose centre is occupied by a grower_block expanding at a steady scale rate. Fire pits surround the chamber's perimeter. Scaling becomes pressure. As the block grows, the safe footprint shrinks toward the walls, and the learner is eventually displaced out of the chamber or into the pit.

The fire hazard is the constant across all three rooms; the transformations are the variables. The pit does not care which operation sent the learner into it. Within the sequence, Trans_Pit turns transformation from observation into stake and hands the learner forward with the three operations felt rather than only named.
