# An opening for whom?

A doorway can remain geometrically similar and change its practical meaning. Here a 0.18 by 0.24 metre opening becomes 1.8 by 2.4 metres while the visitor's body remains fixed. Uniform positive scaling does not change the topology of the room's mesh. It changes the space available to a body with particular dimensions and movement capacities. Those claims should remain distinct.

The simulation chooses its boundary: room and contents belong inside the multiplication; the museum, visitor, speed and gravity remain outside. This exclusion makes the encounter work. It also prevents the effect from being a complete physical model of shrinking an organism. The scene is a specific visual and bodily argument whose assumptions can be examined.

The crystal figure's difference precedes the visitor's new scale. Her enlargement supplies no reason to make her an enemy. Food that has opened into flowers can become a landscape without becoming fully familiar. The encounter lets the unfamiliar host movement and attention.

The serving steps are an authored accommodation. They are visible at both scales, and do not imply that the original sculpture was naturally traversable. A different body or movement system may require another route. Return to this question with sine, noise and the later discussions of terrain and existence.
