> ghost-drafted from the working map (primitive slot, cellularautomata thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

structure_growth · dark_sphere

## The walk

The sparsest room in the thread, and rightly: the primitive is the rule itself, and a rule needs almost no furniture. structure_growth accretes architecture cell by cell while you watch — no blueprint anywhere, only a local law reached again and again until something stands. The dark_sphere keeps its usual counsel at the edge.

## What it teaches

Eight bits name an elementary rule; two hundred fifty-six possible worlds. The room teaches the chapter's unit of meaning: not the cell, not the pattern, but the *rule* — the smallest thing in the project that deserves the word law. Everything the sequence later shows — gliders, chaos, computation — is already sitting inside a byte here, folded, waiting to be run.
