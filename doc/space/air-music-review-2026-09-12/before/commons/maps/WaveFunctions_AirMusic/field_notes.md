## 2026-09-11 — provisional preparation, read from source while W2's evidence was still running (Fable)

Not a development. Astra's W2 completion brief allows inspecting AirMusic's striker, bars and synthesis while the present evidence is unresolved; this records the encounter as the source describes it and what would likely have to change. Nothing here was run, placed or edited. The development handoff for AirMusic comes after W2 is returned.

**The instrument as it stands** (`algorithms/wavefunctions/resonance/`, placed once in this hall at (5,9) with no rotation or offset, and in seven other maps):

- `ResonatingMetallophone.gd` cuts a row of bars to a tuning (`pentatonic` shipped: eight bars C4–E5, spaced 0.12 m; `equal` 17, `just` 10, `harmonic` 12). Each bar's length is `BAR_LENGTH · sqrt(root / f)` — a scale you can see. Bars sit at the rig's origin height, y 0; a `GrabStick` (`commons/primitives/cubes/grab_long_stick.tscn`, an XR Tools pickable with `grab_rod.gd`) is dropped at (−0.5, 0.5, −0.3) with no rest to return to; an instruction label hangs at y −0.3 and the Hz labels at y −0.15 — below the origin, so under the floor unless the lane lifts the body by its box.
- `ResonatingBar.gd` is a `RigidBody3D` with `gravity_scale 0`, mass 0.5, `contact_monitor` on: `body_entered` from a body named or grouped "stick" (or "Hand") triggers the note. The note is pre-generated once, 5 s of `sin(2π·f·t) · exp(−t / decay) · 0.5` at 44,100 Hz, played on the bar's own `AudioStreamPlayer3D` (unit_size 3, max_distance 20, −6 dB, louder for harder hits). A second hit on the same bar restarts its player (no second voice on one bar); hits on different bars overlap because each has its own player — that is the overlap the card wants readable. The glow follows `hit_velocity · 0.5 · exp(−t/decay)` and the bar stops "resonating" below 0.001. `apply_grid_config` is `pass`.
- `CombinedWaveVisualizer.gd` sums `get_current_wave_contribution` over resonating bars into an `ImmediateMesh` LINE_STRIP at 1.5 m above the rig and a label "Resonating: N bars" / "Hit bars with stick!". A one-pixel line strip does not survive a capture (this project's standing capture rule); the label's count is the only readable trace of the voices.

**What the source suggests will need doing** (to verify at runtime before deciding):

1. A playing surface. The bars at y 0 are on the floor; the card asks for an open playable surface with a return rest for the striker. The same move as Sound's `stand` axis: a frame at hand height, opt-in by token, the shipped rig untouched in its other placements; the labels onto the frame's front, not through the instrument.
2. The striker. It is a free pickable with no rest and, being a rigid body against unfrozen bars of gravity 0, a hit will push the struck bar away from the row (mass 0.5, nothing holds it) — to confirm at runtime; if so, freeze the bars (`freeze = true`, still `contact_monitor`) so a strike rings without displacing. A cradle the stick returns to, or a tethered stick, so the second strike is possible without a search.
3. The readable comparison. One strike, two isolated notes, the same pair overlapping: a housed readout (R2) listing the active voices with their frequency and remaining amplitude, and the moment each began, so "two voices" is a line to read rather than a count; the combined trace as a ribbon (not a line strip) so a capture can show the sum. Reuse the FM rig's readout and ribbon patterns.
4. Strike path evidence. The probe should trigger through the real `body_entered` path (a stick body moved into a bar) as well as through `_trigger_resonance`, keep the two apart, and record the voices from `get_active_frequencies()` against the visualiser's count.
5. Tuning. The card keeps a second tuning optional; the `tuning` token already exists (`#tuning:equal`), and `_recut` rebuilds the row in place. A matched second instrument beside the first is the deliberate comparison if it comes; not before one resonance is understood.
6. Texts. `intent.md` describes AirMusic as Eno's phasing loops and FM synthesis (the `SystemsMusicTest` secondary), with Cage before and Sky Stairs after; the current final is the metallophone's. The book pearl's hero is `RackAmbientDrone` while the primary is `resonating_metallophone`, the same kind of mismatch Sound had — Astra's call, noted here for the handoff.

**Thread to carry** (Astra): one oscillation changes another sound (Sound) → a strike sets a response continuing after the hand leaves → two decays can overlap → the additive sum in Synthesis Lab.
