A hall built round a void: a basin of nothing in the middle with a single pillar in it, and a ring of floor you walk to get from one door to the other. On the south side, at a table, stands the primary, `resonating_metallophone`: eight metal bars cut to a pentatonic scale, the longest at the left, a striker lying across a cradle at the front. Strike a bar and lift the stick away; the bar glows and rings for seconds after the contact has ended, and the plate above the row names the voice, its hertz, its level and its age. Strike a second bar while the first still fades and the plate says two voices; the ribbon above draws their sum. Around the ring stand the rest of the room's sound: the `SystemsMusicTest` looper's FM piano over the pillar, the `RackAmbientDrone`, the `BigPipeSystem` where pitch is a length of pipe, the glass racks and lab tables, and the `air_music_display_case`, a generative system framed under glass. The thread runs from Effect Sound's one oscillation changing another to a strike that sets a response going, two responses overlapping, and on to the additive sum in the Synthesis Lab.
