# LSystems_Growth — Summary

The same axiom planted in a walled corridor and an open clearing produces two different trees. Grammar Lab built the full pipeline in a single instant — the tree arrived fully formed. Growth adds time. Each generation renders as a distinct phase, the string lengthening step by step, branches forking visibly.

The map splits asymmetrically. The left half is a walled corridor where the `AnimatedTree` grows hemmed by geometry — branches clipped at walls, sub-branches pruned where they collide. The right half is open clearing where the `ContextSensitiveTree` expands freely, its neighbor-dependent rules producing differentiation that context-free rewriting cannot. The `parametric_lsystem` at the back platform introduces continuous decay — angle shrinkage, length shortening, thickness taper — through sliders that transform rigid geometry into organic form.

Three mechanisms layer onto the Grammar Lab pipeline without replacing it. Context-sensitive rules let symbols read their neighbors, producing positional differentiation. Parametric decay attaches continuous variables to each symbol, creating the trunk-to-twig hierarchy. Environmental constraints shape growth at interpretation time, pruning branches that exceed spatial boundaries. The genome does not change. The environment does. Identity is instruction meeting constraint.

The 8x10 layout with its corridor-versus-clearing geometry is itself the argument: same rules, different space, different tree. The teleporter leads to LSystems_Grammars_And_Curves where the biological intuition developed here meets formal grammar theory.
