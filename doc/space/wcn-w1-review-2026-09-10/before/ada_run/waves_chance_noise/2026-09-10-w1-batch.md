### WaveFunctions_Pendulum

Map: `commons/maps/WaveFunctions_Pendulum/map_data.json` (structure and utilities unchanged).

Question and proposed visitor experiment: where does a movement put its past? Count the marks between two same-side crossings of the record and compare with the plate's period ÷ interval; switch FINE → COARSE and read which of the record's two limits (300 marks, 10 s) binds; STROBE, and the record of a moving bob stands still; watch the oldest mark leave.

Before → after: a pendulum against the west wall whose frame-rate-dependent trail vanished into that wall after half a metre, with no readout or control → a pendulum in a gallows frame at the head of the open south half, record receding 4.5–6 m toward the exit, cased readout 3 m down the trail, four-button panel on the walk-side post, the seismograph beside the trail as the room's second recorder.

Design decision: two limits, count and age, so the two fixed intervals run into different rules and the readout shows which; the strobe as the third policy. The record recedes toward the exit so the visitor walks its length and meets the eviction at the far end.

What changed and why: `PendulumWave.gd` rewritten (motion on `_physics_process`; fixed-time sampler; `max_history_seconds`; depth = age × 0.6 m/s; countable marks; period measured from upward crossings; FINE/COARSE/STROBE/RESET; frame, readout, panel; standalone chrome freed when embedded). ### WaveFunctions_Sine_Space

Map: `commons/maps/WaveFunctions_Sine_Space/map_data.json` (structure and utilities unchanged).

Question and proposed visitor experiment: what kinds of passage can a repeating rule make? Stand still and let a crest come (phase); freeze and walk to one (position); slide PHASE to half a turn and read the gap 2.00 m everywhere (a snake), then to zero and read it breathe (a pinch); bring AMP up and walk through the pinch — the wall does not collide.

Before → after: a corridor across the doorway's cell that a visitor entered through its north wall, unequal walls, phase creeping at 0.05 rad/s, no control → an 8 m passage along the antechamber with a west mouth off the door's row and an east mouth at the bridge head, equal walls, 0.35 rad/s, an entrance panel (AMP, PHASE, FREEZE, RESET) and a readout with the gap range; the two cases in the corners; the basin untouched.

Design decision: "animated visible surfaces around a stable walk" — collision stays off, said plainly, and made discoverable by walking into a crest; equal walls so PHASE is one variable with two exact readings.

What changed and why: `SineWallCorridor.gd` additive (panel/readout/controls/accessors behind `panel = "none"` default; floor lifted 12 mm; chrome freed when embedded); `sine_wall_corridor.tscn` (rows 8, multipliers 1.0/1.0, 0.35 rad/s, `panel = "entrance"`). Map: corridor (7,3) → (6,4); cases → (1,2) and (11,2); `museum.artifact_placement: "map"`; prose fields rewritten.

Departures from the plan: static collidable states designed (two-row trimesh) and set aside — any collider seals the corridor's whole footprint in the museum's walk map, which would sever the antechamber; on the bridge it would seal the bridge. `cut: ribs` left as a config option.
