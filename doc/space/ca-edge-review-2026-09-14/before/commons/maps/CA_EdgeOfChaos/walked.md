## What it closes

Wolfram's four classes end at the edge of chaos — the narrow regime between frozen and boiling where computation lives. The close puts you there and shows both faces at once: the same edge that self-organizes a pattern spreads a disease. Local rules, global patterns — and the closing addendum the chapter has earned: *global consequences.* Whether a rule is wonder or hazard is not in the rule. It is in what the world lets it touch.
