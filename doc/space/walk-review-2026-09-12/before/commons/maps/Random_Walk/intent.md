Concept: A random walk as a process with a position and no plan. Each step is an independent draw; the position is the running sum; the trail is a drawing the display keeps for the visitor. The room separates the three, makes the seed and the boundary rule explicit, and lets the step rule be compared apart from the dimension it is spent in.
Sequence role: Active hall 4 in randomness. Random_Definition made an arrangement repeatable from a seed; Random_Entropy showed a number keep the shares of a sample and lose its order; Random_Remove showed chance acting only within a set a rule chose first. This room turns the changing set into a path: the places a walker can reach are decided one step at a time, and the enclosure decides them again at every wall. It hands the question of many draws to Random_Gaussian.
Technical angle: `random_walk_terrarium.gd` steps five walkers thirty times a second from a timestep accumulator capped at five steps a frame. 2D draws a heading and moves a fixed 0.015 m in the middle plane; 3D draws a direction on the sphere; LEVY multiplies the step by (u + 0.01)^−0.5, capped at ten step-lengths, which the offset already bounds at exactly ten. Overshoots are folded back by the same distance; 2D pins y. The trail keeps the last 200 positions per walker. With `walk_seed` at −1 the draws come from the global stream; a non-negative seed installs a private generator that RESET re-seeds, so a run replays. The opt-in `stand:logbook` bolts a wing to the cabinet: a housed six-line readout (rule and dimension, steps and simulation time, frame clock and catch-up cap, trail kept and who is followed, the seed and RESET's policy, the glass rule), ONE / ALL to follow one walker or see five, NEW SEED, and a body round the case.
Critical angle: The trail flatters the walker with a memory it has not got. A tangle reads as hesitation or intent; the rule contains neither. The room asks the visitor to predict from a suggestive trail and to watch the prediction fail, then to find the wall behind a return, and to test an apparent crossing from a second viewpoint. Bounded by glass, these walks cannot be read as the unlimited spread of an open walk; the screen's mean squared displacement stalls at the walls, and the honest claim is about the rule, not about a law the box prevents.
Key artifacts: random_walk_terrarium is the primary and the book's hero. random_walk_128 traces a fine-grained walk on the floor; random_walk_collection racks several walk laws as grab papers on the west side; random_walk_leash puts one walk in the hand; three pixel_cloud sculptures stand in the walls; the lab_room houses a Monte Carlo workshop whose signage no longer claims Gaussian steps; dark_sphere is a reference body; catalyst_pickup, catalyst_vent and catalyst_prompter_box belong to the bracelet sequence and stay silent until the bracelet is taken.
Gap: No artifact overlays the theoretical root-N spread onto an unconfined walk; the tank's walls make that comparison impossible here by design, and the room says so rather than pretending. Random_Gaussian takes up the distribution of many draws.
