# Noise_Inside_Noise — walked

> ghost-drafted from the working map (critical slot, noise thread); Palle rules the voice.

## The cast

noisesphere · dark_sphere

## The walk

A hemisphere deformed by noise and dressed in the planet shader arches overhead — a world's skin grown from correlation, the dome as the chapter's claim to scale. Standing under the noisesphere you are inside the field twice: the dome above is noise, and the room's darkness around it is the same mathematics at lower amplitude.

## The turn (critical)

The critical question the room holds still for: if planets are noise seen from far enough away, what isn't? The chapter's power move — organic unpredictability from three lines of code — is also its imperial one: once you own a generator of the natural-looking, everything natural starts to look *generated*, and the living world gets re-read as parameters. The room does not resolve this; it puts you under the dome and lets the awe and the suspicion arrive together. Structured disorder is a gift for making worlds — and a habit of seeing that, once learned, cannot be fully unlearned.
