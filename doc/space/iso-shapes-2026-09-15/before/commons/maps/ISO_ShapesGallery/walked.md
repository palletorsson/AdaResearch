## The cast

mc_shapes_gallery · gyroid_demo · mc_torus_sculpture

## The walk

A sculpture hall where nothing was sculpted. The mc_shapes_gallery lines up spheres, blends, and unions — each one a short implicit function passed through the same fifteen-case mill. The mc_torus_sculpture stands as a single worked example: a torus that owns no polygons, only an equation whose zero-crossing the mesh testifies to. And the gyroid_demo is the hall's showpiece — sin x cos y + sin y cos z + sin z cos x = 0, a minimal surface that divides all of space into two interpenetrating labyrinths that never touch, geometry no hand would have found.

## What it fixes

The walk fixes the chapter's inversion of craft: the surface was never modeled, only *described*. Modeling here means writing a sentence in the language of fields and letting extraction do the labor. The gyroid is the proof that this is not a lesser craft — description reaches shapes that direct manipulation never would, because the equation explores space without a sculptor's habits.
