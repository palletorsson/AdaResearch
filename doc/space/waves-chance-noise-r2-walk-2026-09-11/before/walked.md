## The walk

The chapter's hero room, and fittingly it is about walking. The random_walk_terrarium keeps a wanderer under glass — one agent, coin-flip steps, going nowhere on purpose and somewhere anyway. The random_walk_128 runs the same idea at fine grain, 128×128, dense scribble near the origin and a thinning frontier; the random_walk_collection releases the plural, many walkers whose senseless individual paths pile into the square-root halo the mathematics promised. The random_walk_leash puts one on a tether so you can feel the drift fight the constraint; the pixel_cloud hangs the aggregate as weather.

## What it fixes

The walk slot walks. Its lesson is the chapter's statistical turn in miniature: a single random step is noise, but steps *accumulate*, and accumulation has laws. Nothing in the terrarium knows where it is going; the ensemble knows exactly where it will spread. Randomness has no memory, yet it leaves the most legible tracks in the room — which is why half of physics and all of diffusion is this one animal, watched patiently.
