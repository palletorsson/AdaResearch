Not all random is equal. The bell curve emerges from chaos through accumulation — the Central Limit Theorem made visible, the reason why so many things cluster around the mean.

The `galton_board` is the canonical physical demonstration: balls bounce through pegs, each collision a coin flip, and the pile at the bottom always takes the bell shape. `distribution_sampler` lets you vary mean and standard deviation interactively; `gaussian_random` visualizes the probability density directly. `GaussianBlurShader` and `GaussianPaintSplatter` apply the distribution to image processing and mark-making. The Central Limit Theorem is one of mathematics' most profound results: add enough independent contributions and the Gaussian shape is inevitable. Individual paths are unpredictable; collective shape is utterly reliable. Entropy and order are not opposites. They are partners across scale.
