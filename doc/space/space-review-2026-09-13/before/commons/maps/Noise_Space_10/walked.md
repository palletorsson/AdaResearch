## The walk

The thesis as a place. The noise_space is a whole environment driven by configurable noise, entered and walked from inside — not an exhibit about the function but an exhibit *made of* it, where changing a parameter re-decides the room around you. The largest floor in the thread, and the emptiest of furniture, because the space itself is the artifact.

## The world

The world register closes the loop the chapter opened at the white-noise wall: from samples that forget each other to an inhabitable environment made of samples that remember. Every procedural world in games, every generated dungeon and infinite terrain, is this room at industrial scale — habitability squeezed out of correlation. The lesson is the chapter's definition, now load-bearing: memory between neighbors is what makes disorder *livable*. Without it, static; with it, a place.
