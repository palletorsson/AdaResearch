# Random Game — Artifacts
*Randomness: Freedom from Pattern · E_entropy · 8 artifacts*

> An 8x8 field of falling cubes. Each one sinks and rises on its own random timer — no pattern, no warning, no rhythm to learn. Cross the grid or get hit. The floor itself is probabilistic.

The map, read through what it holds — its artifacts in the order you meet them:

## Miura Crawler
![Miura Crawler](/scene-catalog/miura_crawler.png)

Miura-ori folding enemy - flat corrugated sheet that crawls like an inchworm. Lies flat and nearly invisible, pops up when player approaches, uses zigzag locomotion. Can squeeze through gaps when flattened.

`miura_crawler`

## Scissor Stalker
![Scissor Stalker](/scene-catalog/scissor_stalker.png)

Spider-like enemy with Hoberman scissor linkage legs. Legs extend 3x their compact length for dramatic reach. Stalks with partially extended legs, pounces by rapidly extending, slams down to attack.

`scissor_stalker`

## Kaleidocycle
![Kaleidocycle](/scene-catalog/kaleidocycle_enemy.png)

Tumbling ring of tetrahedra with 4 attack modes. Rolls toward player while continuously rotating through itself. Each face color triggers different attack: Fire (red), Ice (blue), Spike (purple), Shield (gold).

`kaleidocycle_enemy`

## Kresling Spire
![Kresling Spire](/scene-catalog/kresling_spire.png)

Kresling origami sniper tower - twisting cylinder that extends from compact disc to tall tower. Rolls as disc to reposition, rises by untwisting to snipe from elevation, collapses rapidly to relocate.

`kresling_spire`

## Armadillo Eggling
![Armadillo Eggling](/scene-catalog/armadillo_eggling.png)

Small aggressive roll-form variant focused on contact pressure, tuned for swarm behavior.

`armadillo_eggling`

## Origami Droideka
![Origami Droideka](/scene-catalog/origami_droideka.png)

Magic-ball inspired fold shell using accordion pleats and braided ring bands; rolls compressed and deploys to fire.

`origami_droideka`

## cube_projectile_spawner
![cube_projectile_spawner](/scene-catalog/cube_projectile_spawner.png)

Timed hazard that spawns projectile cubes, leads the target, and fires warning effects before launch.

`cube_projectile_spawner`

## Random Cycle Cube
![Random Cycle Cube](/scene-catalog/r_c.png)

Cube that sinks out and rises back in on randomized timing, with in/out state indicator and collider gating.

`r_c`
