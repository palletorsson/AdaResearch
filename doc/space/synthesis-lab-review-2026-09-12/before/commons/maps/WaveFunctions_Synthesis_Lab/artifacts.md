# WaveFunctions Synthesis Lab — Artifacts
*Wavefunctions: Everything Oscillates · oscillation · 24 artifacts*

> Fourier's theorem: any signal decomposes into sine waves. Any wave, no matter how jagged or complex, is just simple oscillations stacked. This is the room where that principle becomes material.

The map, read through what it holds — its artifacts in the order you meet them:

## DNA Specimen
![DNA Specimen](/scene-catalog/dna_specimen.png)

A detailed 3D model of a DNA double helix displayed as a museum specimen. The iconic twisted ladder of nucleotide base pairs — adenine-thymine, guanine-cytosine — rendered as a structural biology exhibit. Information encoded in molecular geometry.

`dna_specimen`

## Holographic Display
![Holographic Display](/scene-catalog/holographicdisplay.png)

Holographic projector with sine-based effects. Scan line sweeps vertically using sin(t), content rotates and bobs, flicker effect at 15Hz creates subtle instability. Wireframe cube demonstrates 3D rotation. Projector lights pulse with phase offsets around the ring.

`holographicdisplay`

## Electronic Scales
![Electronic Scales](/scene-catalog/electronicscales.png)

Laboratory scales demonstrating damped harmonic oscillation. When weighing, the display settles using e^(-bt) * sin(ωt)—exponential decay multiplied by sine. Adjustable damping factor and frequency. Shows how real oscillating systems lose energy over time.

`electronicscales`
