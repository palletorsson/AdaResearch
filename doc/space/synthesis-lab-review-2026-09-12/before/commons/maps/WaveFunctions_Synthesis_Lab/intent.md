Concept: Fourier's theorem as the sequence's culmination — any periodic signal decomposes into sine waves, and this laboratory lets the learner take an assembled sum apart into five sines, put it back, and discover that a finite set of them can suggest a corner but not make one; the specimens around the bench (DNA rotation, worm locomotion, a seismograph's trace, a Chladni plate's nodal lines) show oscillation in other domains without the bench claiming to decompose them.
Sequence role: Twelfth and final map in Wavefunctions; the synthesis that gives the sequence its name. Every concept from the previous maps converges: sine waves (Intro/Sine_Space), pendulums (Pendulum), circular motion (Unit Circle), propagation (3D Wave), sound (Effect_Sound/AirMusic: one sinusoid under one decay per bar, two of them overlapping), form (Bernini), silence (Cage), embodied waves (Sky_Stairs/TrigWalkingPath). Additive synthesis is the unifying operation: a sum of sines on purpose; follows WaveFunctions_TrigWalkingPath in the sequence and WaveFunctions_AirMusic on the museum's route, and hands repeatability to Random_Definition.
Technical angle: Fourier synthesis f(t) = Σ Aₙ·sin(nωt), harmonics as integer multiples of the fundamental; `additive_wave_demo` sums five amplitudes in one loop over 256 samples and draws the sum and each component (`_calculate_wave_value`, `_on_harmonic_changed`, `set_preset`); under the W4 bench staging (`#stand:bench`) the display is lifted onto a backboard with the component ladder clear, the sliders stand as a console on the bench, BASELINE / H1 ALONE / HOLD are wired to the real coefficients, and a readout prints the coefficients, each row's value at a marker, and the drawn sum against the drawn total. chladni_plate for resonance as visible pattern; lissajous_curves for frequency ratios; spring_demo for the oscillator equation.
Critical angle: Fourier's decomposition is a decision about what counts as primitive (Latour): sinusoids at integer multiples. The bench frames it as a working choice — the learner does not take Fourier on faith but removes and restores contributions and reads the accumulation loop — and it also shows the choice's cost: five harmonics cannot make a sharp corner, and the label's names (sine, square, sawtooth, triangle) are recipes of amounts, not shapes. The specimens extend the thesis beyond physics as examples, not as evidence that biology is a Fourier sum.
