## The cast

## The walk

The family room. The perlin_noise station holds the 1983 original — gradients on a lattice, smoothly interpolated, randomness that flows instead of stutters, the trick every organic surface in games descends from. The simplex_noise stands beside it as the author's own second draft: fewer artifacts, better manners in high dimensions — even noise gets revised. Behind them the two terrain pieces cash the mathematics out as landscape, octaves becoming mountain ranges, persistence becoming ruggedness. A configurable_portal waits at the far end, its size and destination all parameters — even the exit is a setting.

## What it fixes

The second walk fixes provenance. Noise did not fall from the sky; it was *invented*, by a person, for a movie, and then revised by the same person twenty years later — versioning as mathematical practice. The terrain stations complete the argument by showing the invention naturalized: parameter names turned geological, a function wearing the world's clothes so convincingly that players forgot anyone made it.
