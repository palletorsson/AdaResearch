Concept: A population of recognisable bodies is an arrangement made from draws, and each draw is a separate decision. Six templates, eighty candidates gated by a noise threshold, a template, a facing and a size for each accepted one, then rings and clusters placed by their own instructions: the bed looks like one thing and is the sum of several rules. The specimen table lets a visitor find out which rule put a mushroom where it stands, replay the whole population from a named seed, and switch one rule off while the seed holds.
Sequence role: Active hall 6 of the randomness route on the waves / chance / noise programme, after Random_Gaussian (many draws under one law) and before Random_Game (a draw that decides an outcome). Random_Gaussian showed what many draws reveal together; this hall shows what several kinds of draw contribute separately to one arrangement.
Technical angle: `mushrooms.gd` routes every draw of its build through `_rf()` / `_ri()`; with `population_seed` −1 they fall through to the global stream as shipped, and under a seed a private generator makes them all, the ground's generator taking seed + 1. REGROW rebuilds in the build's order and returns every instance's template, kind, position, yaw and scale and the same ground. The opt-in `stand:specimen` raises the bed clear of the museum floor, boards it, and stands the table with six type specimens, a housed six-line plate, a highlight of flat rings under one template or one kind, and SHOW · KIND · SIZE / REGROW · NEW SEED. Lights capped at twelve. The map holds placement authority; the bed is six metres at (6,7) with its table at the north edge.
Critical angle: The meadow invites the words natural and organic and earns neither: nothing feeds, spreads, competes or dies, and a fairy ring, the emblem of self-organisation, is here a centre, a radius and a count. The hall keeps the pleasure of the population and adds the means to read its decisions, so that a visitor can say which draw made each thing they see rather than which process the picture resembles. The RAND digits on the wall remind that randomness has been an infrastructure, harvested and published, before it was a texture.
Key artifacts: `mushrooms` is the primary and the staged encounter; `reaction_diffusion_intro` is the book pearl's hero line (spots from two rates, a different way of making pattern from a random start); `bubbles_random` and `bubble_particles` keep a looser randomness in the air; `random_number_book_page_collection` carries the 1955 tables; `dark_sphere` anchors.
Gap: The bed cannot be entered; it is seen from its edge and from the table, which suits inspection and forgoes the walk among the mushrooms the shipped ten-metre meadow offered in the grid lane. The book's hero for this pearl is the reaction-diffusion display while the map's primary is the meadow; the two lines should be reconciled by the book's editor.
