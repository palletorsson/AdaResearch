## The cast

space_colonization_algorithm

## The walk

One artifact, and it grows a tree the way a tree actually grows. The space_colonization_algorithm scatters a cloud of invisible attractors — auxin, the growth hormone made spatial — and lets branch tips crawl toward the nearest ones, consuming each attractor as they reach it, splitting when pulled two ways at once. No image of the finished tree exists anywhere in the system; the form is nothing but the running negotiation between hungry tips and a cloud of demand.

## What it teaches

The primitive is authorless form at its most legible. The tree is not drawn, not stored, not planned — it is the *trace* of a simple hunger meeting a distribution, and change the cloud and you change the tree without touching a single rule. This is the chapter's engine shown clean: venation in a leaf, antlers, river deltas, the branching of lightning and lungs — all the same negotiation, and none of them designed. The room teaches you to stop asking who made a shape and start asking what it was reaching toward.
