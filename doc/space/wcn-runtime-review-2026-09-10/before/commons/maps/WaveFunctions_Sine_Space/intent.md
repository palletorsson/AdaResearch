Concept: What kinds of passage can a repeating rule make. Two facing walls, each a sum of three sines along the same seven metres, and one number between them, the phase offset, that decides whether the passage pinches or swerves. Position along the passage and phase in time both move the crests, and the room lets a body tell them apart.
Sequence role: Third active hall in Wavefunctions. The Pendulum hall laid a swing out in time as a receding record; here the same rising and falling is laid along a passage in space and read by walking. Follows WaveFunctions_Pendulum; leads to WaveFunctions_Effect_Sound, where one oscillation changes the character of another and the offset becomes audible.
Technical angle: `SineWallCorridor.gd`: per column, `offset += base_amplitude * ... * sin(base_frequency * ... * z_norm * PI + phase_shift + phase_layer)` summed over three layers (1.0/1.8/2.6 × frequency, 1.0/0.35/0.18 × amplitude); `x_pos = base_x - side * displacement`, so a positive displacement moves either wall inward; the right wall receives `phase + phase_offset_between_walls`; `phase += delta * animation_speed` while running. `base_frequency` (5.2) is cycles over the corridor length, not hertz. Placed at 8 m × 2 m × 4 m, amplitude 0.2, offset 0.6 rad, equal walls (multipliers 1.0), 0.35 rad/s, collision off, rows 8. Entrance panel: AMP (0..0.45 m) and PHASE (0..π) sliders, FREEZE and RESET buttons, a readout printing the values and the gap range. At offset 0 the gap breathes about 1.43–2.57 m; at π every sine flips sign and the gap is 2.00 m everywhere.
Critical angle: a surface that moves, a collision boundary and an available route are three different things, and this passage has only the first. The walls are drawn each frame and carry no collider; the museum's deck is what supports the body. What a wall can do to a body is not settled by how it looks, and the room makes that testable with a hand.
Key artifacts: sine_wall_corridor (primary): the passage across the antechamber, its panel and readout at the west mouth. sine_wall_explanation and sine_space_explanation (secondary): the two display cases in the antechamber's corners, each rule small enough to hold. sine_space, colorballs, dark_sphere (secondary): the older floor-wave arena in the basin below the bridge, a product of two sines under the visitor rather than beside them.

Gap: the corridor's walls do not collide (a cheap two-row trimesh collider was designed and set aside until the passage stands off the museum route); the explanation cases' facing is unverified; the runtime probe (`commons/testing/probe_wcn_sine_space.gd`) is written and not yet run; no headset walk.
