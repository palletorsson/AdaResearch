Concept: Extended neighborhoods — reaching beyond immediate adjacency to second, third, or further rings of cells. Larger neighborhoods allow richer interactions: the 3D tree grows branching structures impossible with nearest-neighbor rules, and the crossway CA demonstrates interference between overlapping influence zones.
Sequence role: Sixth map in the Cellular Automata sequence; the neighborhood radius becomes a variable rather than a constant. CA_1 through CA_5 assumed nearest neighbors; now the learner sees that extending reach changes the character of emergence entirely. The 3D tree artifact previews the full 3D automata of CA_9. The decaying bridge connects to CA_5's multi-state decay. This is the sequence's midpoint — the substrate (grid, states, neighborhoods) is now fully parameterized, and the remaining maps explore dynamics (stochasticity, continuity, dimensionality, agency); leads to CA_7.
Technical angle: Extended Moore neighborhood: radius r gives (2r+1)^2 - 1 neighbors in 2D. Larger neighborhoods smooth dynamics (more averaging) but increase rule table size exponentially. The cellular_automata_3d_tree uses extended neighborhoods in 3D to generate branching growth — each cell's fate depends on a 3D shell of neighbors. The crossway_ca demonstrates how overlapping extended neighborhoods create interference patterns. The decaying_bridge shows structural persistence under decay rules with extended reach.
Critical angle: Neighborhood radius is the cellular automaton's horizon — how far each cell can "see." Increasing it is analogous to increasing the speed of light in a CA universe: information propagates faster, correlations span greater distances, and the system's effective dimensionality increases. The philosophical question is whether local rules with large neighborhoods are still meaningfully "local," or whether locality is a spectrum rather than a binary.
Key artifacts: cellular_automata_3d_tree demonstrates branching growth under extended 3D neighborhoods; crossway_ca shows interference from overlapping influence zones; decaying_bridge combines structural persistence with multi-state decay; dark_sphere anchors the research space.
Gap: Needs a neighborhood radius slider artifact — an interactive demo where the same rule runs at radius 1, 2, 3, etc., showing how increasing reach transforms the emergent pattern from granular to smooth.
