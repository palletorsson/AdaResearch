# Trans_Rotation — two primary encounters

## One panel, three mounts

`rotation_wall_crossings` is the first court and first book encounter. Compare the y swing, x drawbridge and z sideways drawbridge. Follow the coloured hinge/axis and cross during the ninety-degree hold. Mounting and bank direction differ explicitly.

Source: `commons/artifacts/rotation_studies/wall_crossings.gd`.

## Each cube or the whole array

`rotation_array_compare` is beyond the broad landing and is the second book encounter. Five individually turning cubes face a matching row turned by one shared parent. Numbers and yellow edges make the difference observable. Cross the whole row when it aligns with its banks.

Source: `commons/artifacts/rotation_studies/array_compare.gd`.

The previous mixed cast is preserved in `doc/space/rotation-studies-2026-09-09/before`. The floor, banks and bypass aisles support these encounters; the surrounding grid does not rotate.
