# What belongs to a turn?

The surface facing a visitor can become the surface carrying them. Its dimensions have not changed. Its relation to a body and two banks has. This is the first court's precise opening for theory: passage is produced through a relation, not guaranteed by the name of a shape.

The three panels are equal in size, but their mounts and banks differ. We should keep that difference visible. An x-axis turn does not intrinsically make a bridge, nor does a y-axis turn necessarily prevent one. Here both can work because the arrangements allow them to meet a landing. A future experiment could hold the banks fixed and reverse a hinge's turn, or move its pivot. The present room invites those questions without pretending to have tested every answer.

The second court changes the unit of the operation. Five objects turning individually and a collection turning together receive the same angle, but their centres behave differently. A parent is an implemented grouping: its transform carries its children. It matters which objects are counted within that grouping and which remain outside. The visitor can inspect this boundary through the numbered cubes before reading the two different assignments in the code.

A square can conceal its own quarter turn. The yellow edge makes that difference observable. What appears unchanged can depend on which mark the observer was allowed to follow.

Rigid rotation does not change the intrinsic topology of these solids. It can change contact with banks and therefore the connections available to a walking body. Keeping these claims distinct makes the visual argument testable. Even apparent contact is not enough: collider geometry, step height and a body's width participate in whether a route exists for that body.

The six-second hold is also a condition. The probe's capsule crosses at two metres per second; that establishes one working case, not access for every visitor. The still side aisles let someone continue without taking a timed crossing. Observe how people use the pause before treating its duration as settled.

These studies leave a remainder without manufacturing mystery. The same surface can carry or obstruct; the same outline can conceal a turn; the same instruction applied at a different level can move the places of things. Each claim has a location, a visible difference and code that can be changed.
