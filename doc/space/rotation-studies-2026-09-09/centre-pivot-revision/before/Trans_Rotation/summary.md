# Trans_Rotation

Two distinct studies make rotation legible through walking.

1. **One panel, three mounts.** Equal panel dimensions, explicitly different axes, hinges and banks. The y swing, x drawbridge and z sideways drawbridge each connect their banks at ninety degrees. Look at the hinge before predicting the route.
2. **One array, two places to apply the turn.** Five marked cubes turn around their individual centres beside five cubes turning under one shared parent. Only the second row aligns with its crossing at ninety degrees.

Each cycle holds zero degrees for four seconds, turns for three, holds ninety for six and returns over three. Cross during the still interval. A broad landing separates the experiments; side aisles provide a way around them. The surrounding grid remains fixed.

Rotation preserves each solid's topology. Whether its position and orientation afford a continuous walking route is a different question, dependent on banks, collision geometry, body dimensions and timing.
