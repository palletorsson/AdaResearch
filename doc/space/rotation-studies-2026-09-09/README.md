# Trans_Rotation: two separate studies

`before/` preserves the active source map and its markdown immediately before this redesign. It includes earlier experimental material; it is not a claim that the old manuscript accurately described the old code.

The new first court compares equal-sized panels on explicit x/y/z mounts and corresponding banks. The second compares turning five cube nodes with turning one parent that carries them. A broad landing separates them. The former rotating grid, mixed transport/rotation objects and unrelated encounters are retained here as research material, rather than silently overwritten without provenance.

Both new studies are primary and are the two tags in `commons/maps/Trans_Rotation/final.md`. Current evidence: 25 endpoint/placement/centre/grid checks in `ada_run/rotation_studies_checks.json`, plus the actual museum capture `ada_run/Trans_Rotation_build_up.png`.
