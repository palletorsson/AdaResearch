## The walk

The catalyst chamber that closes the chapter, freshly unpacked from its template crowding. The becoming_catalyst waits; the targets stand ready; the science_screen renders the room's state; the proximity_spawner answers approach with presence. The chamber grammar, in the color register: a mode to grant, and surfaces waiting to receive it.

## The seed

What should germinate here is the chapter's verb: *to color*. The nails room already put the palette on the body; the chamber's job is to turn that into a power the player carries out — touch as tinting, presence as palette. The catalyst's exact grant in the color register is an open design decision; the form is fixed: the player leaves the chapter not as someone who saw color, but as someone the world now gets colored by.
