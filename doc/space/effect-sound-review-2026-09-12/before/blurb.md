Sound is oscillation made audible, and here one oscillation is put inside another's rule. The hall is a working audio bench: the racks along row five — a sine, a Moog bass, a DX7 piano, a 303 — and their controllers, the `VRAudioMonitor` drawing what plays, the `chord_tension_spring`, the `harmonic_distance_table`, the `timbre_sculptor`, the `MelodyChaser3D`, the `BigPipeSystem` stating the oldest version of pitch. Apart from them, in the south-east quarter, two reading desks hold the primary, `DualBallFMController`: a blue carrier ball and an orange modulator ball. BASELINE deals an unmodulated 440 Hz note — index zero, its envelope and tanh shaping still in place — HOLD keeps it, a lift of the orange ball changes the beginning of the next note, COMPARE plays the two back to back, and the readout names what differs. The scope above the desks draws the phase-modulation expression the rig computes, lane by lane, so the mechanism is on the table only after the ear has met its consequence.
