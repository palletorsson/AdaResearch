01 · Trans_Introduction

Give the bank a handle.

Cross on the orange plank first. Then take the side aisle to MOVE THE BANK. Its slider translates the landing up to one metre; the plank keeps its length, centre and original motion. RESET returns the landing.

The same 0.44 m-wide capsule crossed at the original setting, lost support at the far setting, and crossed all the way to the exit again after reset. The side aisle remained walkable throughout. The test held the plank aligned to isolate the bank's contribution; normal museum play keeps its timer running.

An operation that worked a moment ago keeps running exactly as before. The crossing has lost one of its agreements.

02 · Trans_Rotation

Whose difficulty is it?

Four cyan capsules try the four rotated arrays. Four wider pink capsules follow from the same starting positions. Height, speed, gravity, slope limit and forward input stay fixed. Rings keep the first stopping places; readouts include sideways drift. The trial repeats, and REPLAY starts it again.

The Z band surprised us. The narrow body stopped at a ridge. The wide body slid sideways and reached the end. Width changed the contact and therefore the route; it did not simply increase a universal difficulty.

A small research method.

Hold something steadyA bridge's motion; a body's movement rules.
Change one relationThe bank's position; the body's width.
Let the result revise the textRecord contact, loss of support and sideways drift before making a larger claim.
