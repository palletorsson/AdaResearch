## The cast

## The walk

The chapter opens by measuring its distance from the last one. The WhiteNoiseGallery hangs the baseline — pure white noise, every sample a stranger to its neighbors, the hiss that randomness sounds like before it learns anything. The NoiseColors3D stands beside it as the spectrum of temperaments: pink remembers a little, brown wanders with a heading, blue avoids itself. The randompoints piece grounds it in the simplest possible experiment — three distributions of dots, three different relationships with space — and randompoint gives you a single draggable sample to hold.

## What it opens

The threshold's move is to break "random" into a family. The last chapter ended with entropy as a method; this one begins by showing that disorder has *personalities*, distinguished by exactly one thing — how much a sample remembers its neighbors. White forgets everything and is useless for worlds. Everything the rest of the thread builds — terrain, spaces, planets — lives in the colored middle, where chance and memory negotiate.
