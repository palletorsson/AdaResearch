There was a connection in the geometry. There was not yet a route for every body.

The last shape has an inhabitant.

NEXT, BACK and RESET refuse a solid rebuild while a visitor is inside. Leaving does not execute a delayed change; another press is needed. GHOST and FRAME remain available because they do not alter collision.

This carries the discussion of entropic morality into one concrete dependency: someone at a control desk can propose a revision while another body relies on the current arrangement. The interlock preserves that visitor's present route out. It is a limited implemented condition, not a claim that the installation has resolved every question of authority or access.
