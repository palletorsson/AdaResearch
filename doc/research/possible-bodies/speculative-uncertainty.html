What remains when the name is withheld?

A computational body can carry several descriptions at once. The possibility field keeps them visible before a selector turns one into the official state.

Walk around the cloud and compare its competing contours. Change the threshold that counts a point as present. The shape shifts, but no single contour is the body by itself. It is a reading produced by a rule.

Try naming the same region by density, distance, and history. Each name makes a different surface easier to see. The model has not become arbitrary; its ontology has become conditional.

The useful uncertainty is not ignorance. It is the time in which more than one continuation can still be tested. The next hall carries this unresolved field into a rhizomatic network of relations.

Uncertainty as a duration

The possibility cloud is the sole artifact in this deliberately small room. Its threshold, seed, and classifications should remain visible so an undecided sample is not mistaken for an empty one.
