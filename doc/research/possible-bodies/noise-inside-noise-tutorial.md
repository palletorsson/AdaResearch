# Noise Inside Noise — find the borrowed address

1. Enter through a side opening and stand inside the noise sphere. Look overhead, then compare the two smaller spheres in SKIN mode and press ZERO. The two sampled fields agree while the same palette and round support remain.
2. Press WARP. Look up as the enclosing field changes while the room stays in place. Follow the yellow point on each body and image. It still names p. The pink point on the left names q, the address from which the right-hand display obtains its answer.
3. Compare the cyan original grid and pink displaced grid. The selected yellow line follows p to q. SAMPLE chooses another witness without rebuilding or changing the fields.
4. Read address_at and value_at in final.md: q = p + amount × displacement(p), then evaluate base at q. The offset uses two components from fixed Perlin fields; the base is a fixed Value field.
5. Cycle WARP through 0, .35, .8 and 1.4. The numbers measure displacement in field-coordinate units, not metres walked by the player. ZERO is a direct return, not a random reseed.
6. Press RELIEF while retaining the warp amount. The plate images keep their values. Both small sphere meshes now use radius .94 + .16 × value metres. This is a separate, post-sampling application. The enclosing sphere's geometry and collision stay fixed.
7. Press ZERO in RELIEF and compare the two silhouettes. Then return to SKIN and change WARP again. Input-coordinate displacement and output mesh displacement can be varied separately.
8. Walk around the pair. Sphere direction x and z supply the 2D coordinates; y is omitted. Look for the resulting relationship between upper and lower parts. A coordinate chart is part of the work.
9. Watch the dark orb. The study has no automatic clock, while the orb continues. The comparison remains available without claiming that the game has paused.

Current controls do not edit seeds, iteration count, frequency or time. The two small sphere surfaces have no collision; the enclosing wall, ceiling, circular floor and plinths do. Walk out through the opposite opening. Headset reach and comfort remain for the deferred headset session.
