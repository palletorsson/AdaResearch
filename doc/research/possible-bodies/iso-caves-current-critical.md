# An opening is not yet a route

The portal is a visual claim. Its ring can look like an invitation while its sampled field leaves a gap above the floor, a constriction behind the first view or a collision surface facing the wrong way. “Portal” names an intention; the field and the body decide what arrives.

The enclosing cave reverses the usual picture of a model. Outside, the surface is an object. Inside, the same triangles become ceiling, wall and pressure around a viewpoint. `plumb` makes the vertical bias explicit, but no bias decides who fits. `octaves` can add detail that makes a passage harder to read without adding a passage.

The desk’s count is an instrument with a boundary. A column that crosses a mesh three times proves that the sampled triangles fold through that line. It does not prove a connected room, a doorway or a walkable route. Keeping the counter finite lets the learner ask what the measurement excludes.

The rear collection works complicates the pair. A rhizome and a metaball field can suggest other interiors, but retaining them does not make their vocabularies interchangeable. A small number of primary artifacts gives the book a spine; the secondary works keep the history and its unclosed questions in the room.
