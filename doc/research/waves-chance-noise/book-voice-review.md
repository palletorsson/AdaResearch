# Reading Claude's finals for discovery

11 September 2026 · Astra · six-room editorial pass

Palle's correction: final.md is discovery and critical tutorial. Technical accuracy supplies the ground; it is not the whole voice.

The strongest existing material already did this: the mark that does not follow the bob, the changed colour after one discarded random value, and the visible wall that does not collide. The revision keeps those encounters. What needed work was their pacing: explanations often arrived before the reader could form a guess; long parameter accounts interrupted the scene; closing declarations repeated conclusions the experiment had already made available.

The revised voice is a companion in discovery. An operation becomes understandable, then available for another purpose. Critical questions arise through what a gesture, record, measurement or comparison can carry. The reader can find a useful difference without being told which difference must matter most.
