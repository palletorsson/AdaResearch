A control can work while hiding what it changes. A number can be correct and too small to read. These images ask what the visitor can discover from the place where they stand.

12 rooms · 86 original captures · 12 rooms freshly captured. All six wavefunction halls and six randomness halls with Claude work or a survey. Unworked rooms and extensions are outside this set. Featured views were inspected; further views are available for comparison.

Each card gives one consequential improvement, what to preserve, and evidence to return. Full-size PNGs are unchanged. These are desktop museum renders; images cannot establish headset reach or what a person hears. Failed checks and unsuccessful exits remain visible.
