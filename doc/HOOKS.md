# Hooks

Seed sentences for the Ada curriculum. Each hook is a compressed claim — a line that can open a map, justify an artifact, or close a blog post. They're organised by domain. Tags mark the register: **L** logical (a structural observation), **P** poetic (a sensory or rhythmic image), **C** critical (a political or ethical framing).

The hooks are not theorems. They are places to start.

## How to build from these

- **Open a map's critical.md with a hook.** One line is enough. The rest of the file is the hook's defence.
- **Close a map's tutorial.md with a hook.** The last sentence of a tutorial is where the hook earns its place — the learner has just done the work the hook describes.
- **Stack two hooks for a blurb.** Pair a logical hook with a poetic one. The logic grounds; the poetry invites.
- **Pair a hook with an artifact.** If the artifact does not enact the hook, either the artifact is missing something or the hook is wrong for this map.
- **Use hooks to diff maps.** Two maps that want the same hook are either a duplicate or a sequence. Two maps with no shared hook are probably in different sequences for a reason.
