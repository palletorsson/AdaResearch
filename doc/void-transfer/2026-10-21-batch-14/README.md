# Colour carried into the Void

Ten original placements from Color_Nails and Color_Rainbow. Import the existing
PackedScenes with their settings. Check their connections and controls, export
their geometry to the editor, and verify that saved positions reach the native
game. Only repair a concrete integration failure. Do not redesign the encounter
or rewrite its texts during this transfer.

The shared tower stays 42 x 32 metres with six-metre storeys. Fit limitations
belong in the handoff so the later curation can address individual installations.
